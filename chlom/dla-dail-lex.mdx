# DLA, DAIL, and LEX

Phase 2 reconciles historical naming conflicts so the automation and documentation layers use one meaning for each term.

## DLA — Dynamic Licensing Asset

A DLA is the governed license object or licensing asset that represents a permission package and its lifecycle.

A DLA may reference:

- asset or property
- licensor / rights controller
- licensee
- rights granted
- restrictions
- territory
- term
- usage caps
- revenue rules
- evidence references
- approvals
- status
- remedy state

Historical materials sometimes used DLA to mean **Decentralized Licensing Authority**. That authority function is now described as **Licensing Stewardship / Issuer Authority** so DLA can retain one machine-readable meaning.

## DAIL — Decentralized Autonomous Information Ledger

DAIL is the event and information-ledger concept used to preserve governed state changes and lineage.

DAIL should record or reference events such as:

- asset registered
- rights reviewed
- license proposed
- license approved
- license activated
- payment confirmed
- evidence added
- correction issued
- hold created
- license suspended
- license revoked
- appeal filed
- restoration approved

In the current baseline, DAIL is an event/audit architecture. A later cryptographic implementation may anchor or settle selected events without changing the business meaning of the record.

## LEX — Licensing Exchange

LEX is the planned marketplace/exchange layer for governed licensing opportunities.

Its intended role is to connect approved rights inventory with buyers while preserving provenance, policy, permissions, economics, and auditability.

LEX is not the same as an ordinary product catalog. A listing should only become transactably active when the underlying rights and operational gates are satisfied.

## Separation of responsibilities

```text
Rights owner / steward
        ↓
CHLOM rights + rules
        ↓
DLA license object
        ↓
LEX discovery / transaction surface
        ↓
Payment + entitlement + agreement
        ↓
DAIL event record
        ↓
Revenue + evidence + remedies
```

## Current status

DLA, DAIL, and LEX are documented as institutional architecture. Their decentralized or autonomous implementations remain gated until production evidence exists.
