# CHLOM asset, rights and provenance model

> **State:** Phase 2 / 2.99 public-safe institutional doctrine. This page describes the evidence and decision model. It does not prove that a particular asset is owned, cleared, licensed, registered, commercially available or certified.

CHLOM separates **asset identity**, **source lineage**, **rights evidence**, **permissions** and **current operational state** so that one kind of evidence is never silently promoted into another.

## Core separation

A CrownThrive record may have several independent identities and states:

1. **Institutional asset/work ID** — the durable semantic identity of the asset or work.
2. **Source record** — the exact source row, file, page, repository object, provider record or other evidence object from which a claim was recovered.
3. **Fingerprint ID** — a deterministic digest over a versioned record; useful for integrity and lineage, not ownership proof.
4. **Rights state** — the current evidence-backed position on ownership, control, license-in, assignment, public-domain status, uncertainty or another governed rights classification.
5. **Permission state** — the specific uses that are permitted, prohibited, restricted, conditional or still unresolved.
6. **Operational state** — whether the asset is merely recovered, current, packaged, published, licensed, entitled, active, retired or otherwise dispositioned.

These fields must remain distinct. A recovered title, domain, file, source row, hash, payment record or public page can prove that an object existed or was represented; it does not by itself prove title, current authority, production state or commercial clearance.

## Minimum governed asset record

A material CHLOM asset/work record should be able to reference:

- stable asset/work ID;
- canonical title/name and known aliases;
- source lineage and version history;
- creator and contributor identities where supported;
- creation/publication dates where supported;
- editable/source masters and derivative relationships where applicable;
- ownership or control basis;
- assignments, releases, inbound licenses and other rights instruments;
- human-authorship contribution and AI/model/provider provenance where material;
- third-party or embedded material;
- excluded or reserved rights;
- territory, media, term, quantity, exclusivity and sublicensing constraints where applicable;
- registration or filing state only when independently evidenced;
- confidentiality / exposure class;
- evidence references and integrity digests;
- dispute, hold, correction and remedy state;
- public-safe wording approved for projection.

Private contracts, restricted evidence, customer/partner identity records, credentials, protected economic schedules and trade-secret implementation remain outside ordinary public documentation.

## Rights-state vocabulary

Rights language must never outrun evidence. The governing standards distinguish states such as:

| Rights state | Public-safe meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `owned_documented` | CrownThrive ownership/control is supported by governed records. |
| `licensed_in` | Use is supported by an inbound third-party license. |
| `assigned` | A written assignment is recorded. |
| `work_made_for_hire_claimed` | Applicable agreement language exists; legal sufficiency may still require specialist review. |
| `common_law_mark` | Use of a mark is evidenced without claiming a verified registration. |
| `filed_pending` | A filing receipt/serial record is verified; registration is not implied. |
| `registered` | A current registration is verified. |
| `trade_secret_candidate` | Valuable confidential information is being evaluated for protection controls. |
| `trade_secret_controlled` | Secrecy/access controls and supporting evidence are in force. |
| `public_domain_or_unowned` | CrownThrive does not claim exclusive ownership in the underlying material. |
| `rights_uncertain` | Evidence is incomplete, conflicting or not yet dispositioned. |

The `™` symbol is a brand claim, not proof of federal registration. Historical “patent pending” or registration language does not become current merely because it appears in an older source.

## Provenance and chain of title

CHLOM treats provenance as an evidence graph rather than a single text field.

A chain-of-title path can include:

```text
source / creation evidence
        ↓
creator / contributor identity
        ↓
assignment / license / release / ownership basis
        ↓
current rights controller
        ↓
permitted and excluded uses
        ↓
license / entitlement / distribution decision
        ↓
DAIL evidence and correction history
```

If any required link is missing or contradictory, the affected use remains HOLD rather than being filled by inference.

## Fingerprints do not create rights

A Fingerprint ID proves only the integrity relationship defined by its versioned canonicalization/digest contract.

It does **not** prove:

- ownership;
- authorship;
- chain of title;
- current deployment;
- legal registration;
- license authority;
- commercial availability;
- identity control;
- certification.

The same rule applies to source-record fingerprint cohorts: source fingerprinting preserves an exact evidence row and lineage, but canonical asset binding occurs only after object type and stable identity are reconciled.

## Permission model

Rights and permissions are related but not identical. A rights controller may authorize some uses while withholding others.

A governed permission decision may need to resolve:

- asset/work covered;
- licensor/rightsholder authority;
- licensee or permitted actor;
- specific right/use;
- media/channel;
- territory;
- effective term;
- quantity, audience, seat or usage cap;
- exclusivity/non-exclusivity;
- modification/derivative authority;
- sublicensing or transferability;
- attribution/credit requirements;
- revenue/split obligations;
- consent, privacy, cultural/canon and brand-safety constraints;
- renewal, revocation, suspension and remedy conditions.

Access to one CrownThrive asset never implies access to adjacent brands, characters, works, source files, methods, datasets, APIs or other ecosystem IP.

## Current vs historical evidence

Historical papers, Help Center pages, product catalogs and archived plans are evidence of design/history within their scope. They do not silently establish current rights, registration, production, licensing, pricing or availability.

When historical material conflicts with stronger current evidence:

- preserve the historical record;
- mark the current conclusion separately;
- record the evidence and effective date;
- append correction/supersession lineage;
- reopen specialist review when the change is material.

SimpleBase remains retired historical recovery provenance only. Missing historical bodies are never fabricated to create a rights or policy record.

## #131 publication boundary

Public CHLOM documentation may explain the rights model, vocabulary, lifecycle, decision reasons, safe interfaces and public digests.

The public projection must not reproduce:

- private contracts or privileged communications;
- private DAIL/evidence bodies;
- customer or partner records;
- restricted contributor/identity data;
- production credentials, keys or secret references that enable access;
- protected key-management or security design;
- proprietary calibration, private eval corpora or defensive implementation;
- private economic schedules or negotiated terms;
- trade-secret implementation merely because a public interface exists.

If classification, chain of title or third-party rights are unresolved, publication stays HOLD.

## Licensing relationship

A CHLOM rights record can support a **DLA — Dynamic Licensing Asset** only after the relevant asset, rights controller, rights granted, restrictions, approvals and evidence references are sufficiently defined.

LEX or another transaction surface may discover or route an opportunity, but a listing or payment object does not make the underlying rights transactably active. Rights, license, agreement, entitlement, payment and evidence remain separate governed states.

## Research-candidate boundary

Research-created asset/work/license records remain `RESEARCH_CANDIDATE` until deliberately promoted. Research registry growth does not automatically create Phase-2.99 hard-exit debt and must not be presented as certified production inventory.

The same applies to historical blockchain/token/DAO/ZK/oracle concepts: source recovery preserves their lineage; it does not reactivate them.

## Phase relationship

- **Phase 2 / 2.99:** recover and reconcile asset identity, source lineage, rights vocabulary, public/private classification and evidence gaps.
- **Phase 3:** seed the executable rights/IP registry, evidence graph, approvals and internal service contracts.
- **Phase 4:** federate rights/permission decisions across platform adapters.
- **Phase 5+:** activate separately governed licensing, entitlements and economic routing only when rights, legal, security, fulfillment and commerce gates are satisfied.

No phase advancement, rights grant, license issuance or commercial activation is created by this documentation page.

## Operating rule

**Preserve first. Classify second. Authorize only from evidence.**

CHLOM should make it easier to answer *what is this asset, where did it come from, who can prove authority over it, what uses are allowed, what remains uncertain, and what evidence supports the conclusion* without exposing the protected evidence itself.
