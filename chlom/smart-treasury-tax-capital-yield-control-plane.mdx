# Smart Treasury, Tax, Capital and Yield — Economic Control Plane

Historical CHLOM sources describe a **Smart Treasury** capable of enforcing treasury policy, routing fees and royalties, maintaining reserves, supporting grants/incentives, producing continuous audit trails and—in later concepts—optimizing eligible capital/yield positions. The Grand Prospectus extends this family into **Smart Tax, Smart Capital and Smart Yield**.

Current CHLOM preserves the economic-control logic while imposing a critical boundary: **calculation, recommendation, authorization, provider execution and institutional settlement are separate states**.

## Historical treasury purpose

Recovered sources describe treasury capabilities for:

- receiving license/network/marketplace fees;
- routing royalties and stakeholder splits;
- maintaining operational and reserve balances;
- grants/development/community allocations;
- validator/oracle/contributor incentives;
- tax withholding/routing concepts;
- governance budgets;
- policy-driven asset allocation;
- AI-assisted yield monitoring/rebalancing;
- continuous auditing/reporting;
- role/multisig controls.

These are design concepts, not evidence of current autonomous investment or tax execution.

## Security controls

Treasury implementation should include:

- separation of duties;
- transaction limits;
- approved destination controls;
- secrets/key isolation;
- dual control for sensitive actions;
- idempotency;
- reconciliation;
- anomaly detection;
- recovery/compensation paths;
- immutable evidence of approvals;
- provider-exit/continuity procedures.

## Failure conditions

HOLD or DENY when:

- no current economic schedule exists;
- recipient identity/authority is unresolved;
- provider amount/currency differs from institutional expectation;
- rights or entitlement state is disputed;
- approval threshold is not satisfied;
- tax treatment is required but unresolved;
- execution provider is unavailable and no safe fallback exists;
- a recommendation tries to become execution without authority;
- reconciliation cannot match the event to an authorized obligation.
