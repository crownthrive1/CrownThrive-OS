## Purpose

P-03 is the current CHLOM capability container for answering a fundamental institutional question before commerce, distribution, access or transfer occurs:

> **What rights exist, who can exercise them, under what evidence, scope, conditions, duration and remedies?**

P-03 owns the rights-before-license and rights-before-revenue control plane. It registers source assets/evidence, models rights as a graph, issues/manages Dynamic Licensing Assets, governs LEX eligibility, binds agreements and entitlements, and routes disputes/remedies without manufacturing ownership from provider activity or payment.

```text
SOURCE ASSET / WORK / RIGHT
          |
          v
EVIDENCE + CHAIN OF TITLE
          |
          v
RIGHTS GRAPH
          |
          v
LICENSABLE SCOPE
          |
          v
DLA  = Dynamic Licensing Asset
          |
          +--------> agreement / entitlement
          |
          +--------> LEX candidate
          |
          v
USAGE / TRANSFER / RENEWAL
          |
          v
DAIL + RECONCILIATION + REMEDIES
```

The asset/evidence registry identifies the subject of rights without claiming more than the evidence proves.

Possible asset classes include:

- copyrighted work;
- trademark/brand asset;

- publication;
- software/code asset;
- dataset;
- license/permit record;
- physical-space/right-of-use record;
- franchise/operator rights;
- curriculum;
- image/likeness/publicity-right object;
- commercial inventory;
- contract-derived right;
- other evidence-backed institutional asset.

Evidence can include:

- source files;

## Rights graph

Rights are modeled as relationships, not one Boolean ownership field.

```text
                [SOURCE RIGHT]
                      |
        +-------------+-------------+
        |                           |
   [OWNER A]                    [OWNER B]
        |
     license
        |
   [LICENSEE]
        |
   sublicense if permitted
        |
   [SUBLICENSEE]
```

Each edge can carry:

```text
scope
territory
media/channel
field of use
duration
exclusivity
transferability
sublicensing
revocability
payment/economic terms reference
conditions
source evidence
```

## Rights conservation invariant

A downstream license cannot legitimately expand beyond its source authority.

```text
CHILD_RIGHTS <= PARENT_RIGHTS <= SOURCE_RIGHTS
```

For set-based modeling:

```text
R_child subseteq R_parent subseteq R_source
```

A transfer or sublicense that requires rights outside the parent set is invalid/held even if a provider technically accepts the transaction.

## Chain of title

Chain of title is a lineage graph of how current claimed authority derives from prior rights/evidence.

```text
SOURCE CREATION
      |
      v
INITIAL OWNERSHIP / CLAIM
      |
      v
ASSIGNMENT / LICENSE / SUCCESSION
      |
      v
CURRENT LICENSOR AUTHORITY
      |
      v
DLA / AGREEMENT / ENTITLEMENT
```

Missing links do not get inferred simply to make the chain complete.

## Dynamic Licensing Asset

A DLA is a machine-readable representation of a license/permission state. It is not the underlying intellectual property or automatically a transferable token.

### DLA record

```yaml
dla_id: stable ID
asset_id: source asset
issuer_authority_ref: required
holder_or_beneficiary_ref: subject
rights_scope: structured rights set
territory: structured
channels_or_media: []
field_of_use: []
effective_from: timestamp
expires_at: nullable
transferability: prohibited|conditional|allowed
sublicensing: prohibited|conditional|allowed
status: draft|pending|active|suspended|expired|revoked|terminated|disputed
agreement_ref: nullable
policy_refs: []
evidence_refs: []
version: integer
```

## DLA lifecycle

```text
DRAFT
  |
EVIDENCE_PENDING
  |
RIGHTS_VERIFIED
  |
APPROVAL_PENDING
  |
ACTIVE
  |
  +--> AMENDED --> ACTIVE(new version)
  +--> SUSPENDED --> RESTORED / REVOKED
  +--> DISPUTED --> RESOLVED / MODIFIED / REVOKED
  +--> EXPIRED --> RENEWED / CLOSED
  +--> TERMINATED
```

State transitions require policy/authority/evidence and preserve prior versions.

## Agreements

An agreement is not the same object as a DLA.

```text
AGREEMENT
 legal/commercial terms + signatures + obligations

DLA
 machine-readable current permission state

ENTITLEMENT
 operational access/use benefit resolved from agreement/license
```

One agreement can produce multiple DLAs or entitlements when the rights/evidence support that structure.

## Entitlements

An entitlement is a current operational permission such as access, download, use, seat, booking right, distribution permission or platform capability.

Payment can be a precondition for an entitlement but is not the entitlement itself.

```text
PAYMENT SUCCESS
     |
     v
AGREEMENT / OFFER / POLICY RECONCILIATION
     |
     v
ENTITLEMENT CREATED / UPDATED
```

## LEX eligibility

A listing/transmission decision can require:

- licensor authority;
- current DLA state;
- transfer/sublicense rights;
- counterparty eligibility;
- jurisdiction/sector rules;
- pricing/economic schedule authority;
- required agreement template/version;
- dispute/escrow requirements;
- provider capability/readiness.

## Revocation

Revocation terminates or invalidates a permission state under authorized conditions. It does not erase evidence that the license previously existed.

### Revocation propagation

Potential downstream effects may include:

- stop new use;
- suspend entitlement;
- remove/hold LEX listing;
- block sublicense creation;
- trigger economic reconciliation;
- preserve completed lawful historical uses;
- notify dependent systems;
- update public-safe status if appropriate.

## Transfer and sublicensing

A transfer must verify that the transferor can transfer the exact rights set.

```text
requested_rights
      subseteq
transferable_parent_rights
      ?
 yes -> continue eligibility
 no  -> DENY
```

Sublicensing creates a child relationship and never destroys the parent source chain.

## Versioning

DLA versions preserve effective periods.

```text
DLA v1  2026-01-01 -> 2026-03-31
DLA v2  2026-04-01 -> current
```

Historical usage is evaluated against the version that governed the event, not automatically against today's terms.

## Operating procedures

### Asset onboarding

1. assign stable asset ID;
2. preserve source evidence;
3. record claims without overstatement;
4. reconcile ownership/licensor authority;
5. build rights graph;
6. classify restrictions;
7. mark unresolved conflicts;
8. create licensable scope only when supported.

### DLA issuance

1. confirm source asset;
2. confirm issuer authority;
3. confirm rights scope;
4. evaluate policy/eligibility;
5. bind agreement where required;
6. create DLA candidate;
7. obtain approval;
8. activate exact version;
9. emit/read back DAIL evidence.

### Transfer

1. resolve active DLA;
2. calculate allowed transfer scope;
3. verify recipient eligibility;
4. verify economic/escrow conditions;
5. execute authorized state changes;
6. read back provider/operational state;
7. reconcile rights graph and entitlement;
8. emit evidence.

### Dispute

1. freeze mutable evidence;
2. create case;
3. apply bounded hold if authorized;
4. review evidence/authority;
5. issue disposition/remedy;
6. propagate correction;
7. retain original lineage.

## Failure modes

### Provider says user owns asset

**Response:** provider evidence is one source; do not convert to institutional ownership without supporting rights evidence.

### Payment succeeded before entitlement

**Response:** reconcile payment, rights, agreement and entitlement; compensate/refund/hold as policy requires.

### Child license exceeds parent

**Response:** DENY creation or transfer.

### Missing chain-of-title link

**Response:** HOLD high-risk rights activation; preserve unresolved claim.

### Revoked DLA remains listed externally

**Response:** hold listing/use, initiate provider reconciliation and record mismatch.

### Expired agreement but active cached entitlement

**Response:** fail closed where current entitlement requires current agreement; reconcile cache/provider.
