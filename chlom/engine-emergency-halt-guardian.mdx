## Purpose

The Emergency Halt / Guardian Engine is CHLOM's **bounded circuit breaker**. It lets authorized guardians stop a dangerous or unresolved operation quickly without granting permanent or universal administrative power.

```text
NORMAL OPERATION
      |
critical trigger / credible evidence
      |
      v
HALT REQUEST
      |
guardian + scope + authority
      |
      v
PAUSE / HOLD
      |
containment / investigation / remediation
      |
      v
RESTORE | NARROW | REVOKE | COMPENSATE
      |
      v
DAIL
```

## Trigger sources

Potential trigger classes include:

- confirmed/credible credential compromise;
- critical policy mismatch;
- stale sanctions/oracle evidence where current evidence is mandatory;
- rights/license dispute requiring temporary containment;
- duplicate/unauthorized economic execution;
- provider breach/outage producing unsafe state;
- bridge/proof integrity issue;
- model/automation runaway;
- public/private data leak;
- governance/runtime exploit.

Protected thresholds/detection logic remain restricted.

## Scope minimization

```text
pause only the smallest scope that safely contains the risk
```

If one asset is disputed, institution-wide halt is not justified unless evidence/policy supports broader scope.

## HOLD relationship

`HOLD` is the decision state; `halt/pause` is an operational control.

```text
HOLD can trigger PAUSE
PAUSE can remain while HOLD is reviewed
```

## Provider propagation

A local halt must be propagated to relevant providers and read back where side effects exist.

```text
INSTITUTIONAL HALT
   |
   +--> provider pause/disable
   +--> queue suppression
   +--> storefront/listing hold
   +--> payout hold
   +--> access restriction
   |
readback / mismatch
```

Provider success does not mean every dependent surface is contained; reconciliation remains necessary.

## Restoration

Restoration requires evidence, authority and exact scope.

```text
halted
  |
remediation evidence
  |
revalidation
  |
restore? -- no --> remain held
  |
 yes
  v
lift exact scope
  |
provider readback + DAIL
```

## Compensation

If a halt interrupted a partially executed workflow, restoration may require compensation rather than naive rollback:

- refund;
- entitlement restoration;
- reschedule;
- reversal/clawback;
- provider reconciliation;
- corrected public status;
- re-issued license/action.

## Failure modes

### Guardian authority expired

DENY new halt/lift action; emergency alternative requires separately valid authority.

### Provider refuses pause

Keep institutional state halted, escalate provider incident, use alternate containment/exit path where available.

### Halt remains past review/expiry

Escalate stale halt; do not silently keep indefinite emergency power.

### Restore provider before institutional review

Provider state becomes mismatch; institutional halt remains until authorized lift/readback.

## Security threats

- stolen guardian credential;
- abuse/censorship through overbroad pause;
- hidden permanent emergency authority;
- provider bypass;
- unauthorized lift;
- race between execution and halt;
- incomplete propagation.
