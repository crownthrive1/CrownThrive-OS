## Purpose

P-01 is CHLOM's current capability container for establishing **who or what is acting, under which organizational context, with what verified attributes, delegated scope, consent state and access rights**.

Identity is deliberately separated from authority. A person, organization, agent, service, wallet, provider account or device can be identified without automatically receiving permission to act.

```text
IDENTITY
   |
   v
VERIFICATION
   |
   v
ROLE / RELATIONSHIP
   |
   v
DELEGATION / AUTHORITY
   |
   v
CONSENT / PURPOSE
   |
   v
ACCESS DECISION
   |
 PASS | HOLD | DENY
```

## CHLOM Six Functions mapping

| Function | P-01 responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| Rights | Establish which subject may hold or exercise an entitlement |
| Rules | Apply authentication, verification, consent and access policies |
| Roles | Canonical role, organization, delegation and actor relationships |
| Revenue | Bind economic actions to an accountable actor without creating economic authority |
| Records | Identity, verification, consent, delegation and access evidence |
| Remedies | Suspend, revoke, recover, rotate, correct or appeal access/identity state |

## Scope

P-01 covers the current cloud/API identity-and-access plane. It may map to historical DID/Fingerprint/SBT concepts, but it is not defined by a requirement to deploy a public blockchain.

### In scope

- CrownThrive participant identity;
- organization and tenant identity;
- platform/account aliases;
- OIDC and SSO integration;
- authentication state;
- role and group membership;
- verification tiers;
- delegated authority records;
- consent and purpose bindings;
- access-control decisions;
- identity recovery and key/account rotation;
- machine/service/agent identity;
- provider identity aliases;
- public-safe identity projection;
- DAIL evidence for material identity changes.

### Out of scope unless separately delegated

- granting legal/professional licensure merely from a verified account;
- financial authorization merely from identity;
- rights ownership merely from possession of an account;
- autonomous expansion of an agent's authority;
- publication of private identity evidence;
- raw secret or credential storage in documentation.

## Source-component interpretation

### ThriveApps

Within P-01, ThriveApps represents a consumer of shared identity/access capabilities and a source of account/application identity surfaces. Its inclusion in the build-pack pallet does not imply every ThriveApps deployment currently uses every P-01 interface.

### BrandCards

BrandCards can consume participant, brand, organization, public-profile and verification-state projections. Public BrandCards must receive only the identity fields authorized for public projection.

### Organization identity

Organization identity is distinct from individual identity.

```text
PERSON ----member_of----> ORGANIZATION
  |                           |
  |                           +--> tenant scope
  +--> role                   +--> policies
  +--> delegation             +--> provider aliases
```

An actor can have different authority in different organizations.

### OIDC / SSO

OIDC/SSO provides authentication and identity federation. It does not make the external identity provider the sovereign source for every CrownThrive role, right or entitlement.

```text
External IdP
    |
 authenticate
    |
    v
CrownThrive Identity Resolver
    |
    +--> map stable subject
    +--> resolve tenant
    +--> resolve local roles
    +--> resolve verification
    +--> resolve consent
    |
    v
Authorization decision
```

### Roles

Roles describe institutional relationships. They are versioned and scoped.

### Verification

Verification states are evidence-backed assertions, not permanent universal trust scores.

### Consent

Consent is purpose-specific, time-aware and revocable where applicable. Authentication is not consent.

## Authentication and authorization separation

```text
AUTHENTICATION QUESTION
"Are you the subject represented by this session?"

            !=

AUTHORIZATION QUESTION
"May this subject perform this action on this object now?"
```

P-01 must never treat successful login as sufficient permission for rights, economic, security or governance actions.

## Access decision inputs

A material authorization decision can include:

- actor identity;
- organization/tenant;
- role;
- delegation;
- verification requirements;
- consent/purpose;
- target resource;
- requested capability;
- current policy versions;
- time window;
- risk/security state;
- applicable HOLD/deny controls.

## PASS / HOLD / DENY

```text
identity resolved? -------- no --------> DENY
      |
     yes
      v
required verification current? -- no/unknown --> HOLD or DENY
      |
     yes
      v
role/delegation permits action? --- no -------> DENY
      |
     yes
      v
consent/purpose satisfied? ---- no/unknown ---> HOLD or DENY
      |
     yes
      v
higher-order policy/security gate clear? -- no --> HOLD/DENY
      |
     yes
      v
PASS
```

The precise distinction between HOLD and DENY is policy-controlled.

## Session model

A session is temporary evidence of authenticated continuity.

A session should bind:

```text
session ID
subject ID
tenant/organization
authentication method
issued time
expiry
risk/security context
scopes or claims
revocation state
```

A stale session cannot override a newly revoked role, delegation or verification.

## Agent identity

Agents require stable institutional identity separate from the human who created them.

```text
FOUNDER / PRINCIPAL
       |
    delegates
       |
       v
   AGENT ID
       |
  capability scope
       |
  authority ceiling
       |
  evidence obligation
```

An agent may not self-issue higher authority, self-certify protected verification or become vote-eligible merely because it can call an API.

## Operating procedures

### Onboarding

1. create/resume stable subject;
2. attach tenant/organization context;
3. authenticate through approved method;
4. gather only required verification;
5. assign roles through authorized process;
6. record consent by purpose;
7. create delegations if explicitly authorized;
8. emit evidence and read back final state.

### Role change

Role change requires authority, effective time, prior-state preservation and DAIL evidence.

### Revocation

Revocation must propagate to relevant access decisions. A provider-side removal that is not reconciled institutionally remains incomplete.

### Recovery

Recovery restores control without silently restoring revoked authority.

### Offboarding

Offboarding terminates current relationships/delegations while retaining required historical evidence.

## Failure modes

### Identity collision

Two provider accounts appear to represent one subject or one provider account appears mapped to multiple institutional subjects.

**Response:** HOLD identity-sensitive actions until reconciled.

### Stale role

External provider says `admin`, institutional role was revoked.

**Response:** institutional role state controls; provider mismatch becomes reconciliation evidence.

### Verification unavailable

Required verification provider is unavailable or evidence is stale.

**Response:** fail closed for operations that require that verification.

### Consent ambiguity

No purpose-specific consent can be resolved.

**Response:** do not infer consent from account existence or prior unrelated activity.

### Agent scope drift

Agent attempts capability outside its delegation.

**Response:** DENY and emit security/governance evidence.

## Security controls

Public documentation does not expose:

- authentication secrets;
- OAuth client secrets;
- recovery factors;
- private identity evidence;
- anti-fraud fingerprint composition;
- private verification-provider credentials;
- account-takeover detection thresholds.

Those belong in the secret store or restricted companion artifacts as applicable.

## Invariants

```text
identity != authority
authentication != authorization
role != delegation
verification != permission
account != person
provider ID != institutional ID
consent for purpose A != consent for purpose B
agent capability != founder authority
recovery != authority restoration

```
