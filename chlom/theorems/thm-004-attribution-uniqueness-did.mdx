## Recovered guarantee

The Gold Paper's attribution theorem treats public DID/identifier collision as negligible under its collision-resistant construction assumptions, allowing governed events to resolve to a stable identity record.

## Critical distinction

Cryptographic identifier uniqueness is not the same as proving one natural person cannot create multiple identities. Sybil resistance, credential validation, alias resolution and the private CHLOM Fingerprint layer are separate mechanisms.

## Protected boundary

The private Fingerprint ID and proprietary identity-resolution/deduplication signals remain restricted. Public documentation may expose DID identity, key rotation, credential relationships and safe attribution receipts only.

## Current mapping

Use stable canonical IDs, tenant-safe alias resolution, key rotation/revocation, credential lineage and append-only corrections. Provider/usernames remain aliases, not identity truth.

## Verification targets
