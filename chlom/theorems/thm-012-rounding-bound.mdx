## Recovered statement

The Gold Paper summarizes the historical rounding guarantee as a bounded payout residual, with a deterministic residual sink and full conservation under the source model. The source states a bound of the form:

```text
residual <= m - 1
```

for `m` modeled allocation recipients under its integer-floor construction.

## Meaning

Finite-precision integer allocation can leave a remainder. The theorem bounds that remainder and requires deterministic handling so rounding does not become untracked value leakage.

## Protected boundary

The exact splitter construction, proof details and internal implementation/calibration remain restricted. Public CHLOM guidance exposes only deterministic rounding, conservation and explicit residue treatment.

## Current mapping

FORM-032 governs public rounding discipline. A production economic engine must prove or test its own bound under its actual denominator, currency/token precision, recipient count and rounding method before citing THM-012 as implemented.

## Verification targets

Property-test recipient counts and random split vectors; verify conservation, worst-case residual, deterministic sink, negative adjustment handling, batching and large integer limits.

## Limitation

Changing from source integer-floor arithmetic to another rounding mode changes the applicable formal bound and requires a new proof/model.
