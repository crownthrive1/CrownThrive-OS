## Recovered guarantee

The restricted source states that a valid Splitter conserves its governed payout basis: the complete set of allocations and deterministic finite-precision residue reconciles to the original basis rather than creating or losing value.

Public-safe form:

```text
sum(allocated_value) + deterministic_residue = payout_basis
```

The source's exact integer allocation construction and proof remain protected.

## Assumptions

A valid split vector/schedule, authorized recipients, explicit basis, exact or bounded finite-precision arithmetic, deterministic residue handling, no duplicated/dropped event and valid state transition.

## Current mapping

Cloud-first CHLOM maps this theorem to versioned allocation schedules, fixed-point arithmetic, deterministic rounding, calculation records and settlement reconciliation. A current implementation cannot claim this theorem until the exact production calculation path is property-tested or formally mapped against the source assumptions.

## Verification targets

Conservation across random schedules, residue bounds, refunds/reversals, batched versus unbatched execution, maximum integer/precision cases, and DAIL-backed calculation/settlement replay.

## Boundary

Historical theorem lineage is preserved; it is not proof that the present deployment is formally verified.
