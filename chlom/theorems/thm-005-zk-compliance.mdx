## Meaning

A verified proof can establish the exact predicate encoded by an approved circuit without disclosing the witness. It cannot establish a different legal/policy proposition that the circuit did not encode.

## Preconditions

Correct circuit and verification key, approved version, public-input/context binding, current witness source, no relevant revocation and a policy that actually accepts that proof type.

## Boundary

Cryptographic validity is not current proof usability and not execution authority. The circuit/proof body and protected templates remain private under the vault binding.

## Current mapping and tests

Use circuit/key registries, replay/domain separation, witness currentness, proof receipts and DAIL lineage. Test malformed proofs, wrong keys/circuits, stale or revoked witnesses, context replay and privacy leakage.
