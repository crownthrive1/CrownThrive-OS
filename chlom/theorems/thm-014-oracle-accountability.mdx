## Recovered statement

The Gold Paper's historical oracle accountability condition is summarized as:

```text
p_det * s_V * S_V >= benefit_false
```

where the modeled expected penalty from detection and slashing is at least as large as the benefit of submitting false oracle information.

## Meaning

A bonded oracle should face enough expected economic downside that false reporting is not rational under the simplified source assumptions.

## Assumptions

Detection probability is meaningful and enforceable, bonded stake/value can actually be penalized under valid authority, false-report benefit can be bounded, the oracle identity is attributable, and collusion/correlation risks are separately modeled.

## Protected boundary

Role-specific parameters, oracle reputation/calibration, detection-stack design and validator/AI co-training details remain restricted.

## Current mapping

Modern CHLOM can apply the concept to assurance deposits, provider reliability and multi-source evidence. Current cloud systems should not claim a live slashing oracle economy unless technically and legally activated.
