## Recovered guarantee

The Gold Paper states that when all required accepted proof predicates are valid for the same governed context, their encoded compliance statements can be composed conjunctively:

```text
Verify(pi_i) = true for all required i
=> AND C_i(context) = true
```

## Assumptions

Every relevant predicate is actually included; circuits/rules are correct; proof contexts match; witnesses remain usable; and no higher-priority policy conflict has been omitted.

## Critical limitation

Proving every included predicate does not prove an omitted requirement. Applicability, policy-pack stacking, rights resolution and authority remain separate controls.

## Current mapping

CHLOM composes identity, rights, DLA validity, sanctions/eligibility, economic prerequisites and other controls through three-valued PASS/HOLD/DENY evaluation rather than reducing everything to one opaque score.

## Verification targets

All-pass, one failure, one stale proof, missing mandatory predicate, incompatible contexts, duplicated predicate and conflicting policy packs.
