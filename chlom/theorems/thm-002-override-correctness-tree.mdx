## Recovered statement

The source describes override-tree payout correctness as an ancestor receiving a path-dependent share of a payout basis, with the complete tree preserving total value. Public-safe notation is:

```text
ancestor_share = B * product(path_rates)
```

and the tree-level allocation remains conservative under the source assumptions.

## Meaning

A hierarchical override structure can route value upstream through parent/child relationships without inventing additional value. The path and rate configuration determines the ancestor share; the complete distribution remains bounded by the original payout basis.

## Assumptions

Valid acyclic tree/graph structure, authorized edge rates, bounded rate domains, deterministic traversal, stable payout basis, valid recipient identities and source-defined conservation rules.
