## Recovered statement

The restricted source states that after a valid subtree revocation, **future flow to the revoked subtree is zero while the modeled distribution remains conservative**.

## Meaning

Revoking a node or branch in a hierarchical license/override structure must stop future benefits/permissions allocated to that branch without causing the remaining system to lose or manufacture value. The post-revocation destination of previously routed value follows the governing schedule/remedy rules.

## Assumptions

Valid tree/graph structure, unambiguous revocation scope, effective-time semantics, deterministic redistribution/residual policy, no stale cached entitlement bypass and correct descendant traversal.

## Current mapping

Modern CHLOM can apply the principle to sublicenses, commissions, referrals, entitlements and delegated permissions. Revocation becomes an append-only lifecycle event; affected descendants are recomputed and future execution checks current state.

## Protected boundary

Exact override-tree algorithms and protected redistribution mechanics remain in private custody.
