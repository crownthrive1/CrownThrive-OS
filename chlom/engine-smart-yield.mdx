## Source basis

The recovered Help Center describes **Smart Yield/Farming** as an automated allocation concept intended to use staking/yield strategies to help offset recurring costs such as taxes or insurance. It separately names the `Smart Yield & Compliance Farming Protocol (SYFP) Whitepaper`. Historical Smart Treasury/DeFi material provides the broader strategy-routing lineage.

This article preserves the concept as research. It does not promise yield, activate farming strategies or make tax/insurance payments from investment returns.

## Non-purpose

Smart Yield does not:

- guarantee positive returns;
- justify risking mandatory tax/insurance reserves;
- create investment advisory authority;
- allow unreviewed yield strategies;
- defer obligations because expected yield has not arrived;
- imply a live CHLOM farming/staking product;
- make historical token mechanics current.

## Liability-first invariant

A core current-safe interpretation is:

```text
mandatory obligation funding
   takes precedence over
optional yield optimization
```

If a tax/insurance/other liability is due, the system must not depend on speculative future returns unless a separately authorized and professionally reviewed plan permits it.

## Funding hierarchy

A public-safe model:

```text
cash/book balance
  - mandatory reserves
  - committed obligations
  - near-term liquidity needs
  = optional eligible surplus
```

Only optional eligible surplus can enter a candidate optimization flow unless another adopted policy says otherwise.

## Yield mathematics

A simple research identity is:

```text
net_return = gross_return - fees - losses - taxes/other costs
```

and liability coverage can be expressed descriptively as:

```text
coverage_ratio = reconciled_available_value / required_liability_value
```

No target ratio, APY or strategy return is adopted by this article.

## Reinvestment versus liability funding

Yield/revenue must be classified before routing:

```text
reconciled proceeds
   +--> liability reserve
   +--> reinvestment
   +--> general treasury
   +--> other adopted allocation
```

The allocation schedule must be explicit and current.

## Smart Treasury relationship

Smart Treasury owns treasury policy/recommendations; Smart Yield is a specialized historical strategy family. Smart Treasury may consume a yield candidate, but Smart Yield cannot self-authorize treasury movement.

## Smart DeFi relationship

Smart DeFi can provide candidate protocols/strategies. Smart Yield adds a **purpose-driven allocation concept**, e.g. funding/offsetting a recurring liability. The two remain separately governed.

## Smart Tax relationship

Smart Tax defines/records tax obligations under professional authority. Smart Yield cannot calculate tax liability merely because it is intended to help fund one.

## Automation boundary

Historical text suggests automated allocation. Current-safe workflow:

```text
strategy signal
 -> candidate recommendation
 -> current risk/liquidity/professional checks
 -> authorization
 -> provider execution
 -> readback/reconciliation
```

No auto-invest/auto-unwind rule is activated by documentation alone.

## Failure modes

- liability amount/due date unresolved -\> HOLD optimization;
- reserve coverage insufficient -\> no optional yield allocation;
- strategy review expired -\> HOLD;
- provider yield estimate changes -\> recompute, never guarantee;
- strategy loss -\> update reconciliation/risk, do not hide loss;
- unwind fails -\> incident/escalation;
- provider position cannot be read back -\> unresolved;
- professional gate missing -\> no real-money activation.

## Threat model

- misleading APY/yield marketing;
- strategy exploit/insolvency;
- loss of mandatory reserves;
- liquidity lockup;
- provider substitution;
- oracle/price manipulation;
- hidden leverage;
- fee drag;
- tax/accounting misclassification;
- automated risk-taking without authority.

## Test vectors

1. mandatory reserves not fully funded -\> no optional allocation;
2. strategy candidate \+ no professional approval -\> HOLD;
3. simulated synthetic strategy -\> permitted in research environment;
4. provider reports yield but position balance mismatch -\> unreconciled;
5. loss occurs -\> recorded; no smoothing/false APY;
6. liability due before strategy unlock -\> strategy ineligible for that purpose;
7. yield proceeds -\> allocation schedule before liability/reserve routing;
8. duplicate provider event -\> idempotent reconciliation;
9. historical APY/example -\> never current promise;
10. strategy retired -\> no new allocations.

## Current mapping

Smart Yield remains a historical/current-research economic optimization concept under Smart Treasury/P-11. Any live investment/yield/farming/staking implementation requires legally/professionally reviewed provider contracts, risk controls and production evidence.
