## Historical purpose

The Risk/Scoring Pallet was the runtime-facing location for normalized risk information used by CHLOM's compliance gates. Off-chain AI/oracle components could produce risk evidence and the runtime could use approved risk state when deciding whether a transaction/action was acceptable.

```text
OBSERVATIONS / AI / ORACLE DATA
             |
             v
        RISK MODEL
             |
             v
      RISK SCORE / BAND
             |
             v
  HISTORICAL RISK PALLET
             |
             v
 POLICY / COMPLIANCE PRE-CHECK
```

## Risk is evidence, not verdict

```text
risk score
   !=
violation
   !=
authority to punish
```

Risk can influence policy evaluation, escalation or HOLD behavior; it cannot manufacture prohibited facts.

Exact historical storage names/scales are not recovered and are not invented.

## Historical quantitative lineage

Recovered CHLOM material includes example risk formulas such as weighted combinations of amount deviation, peer risk and KYC/verification level. Those examples are historical modeling lineage—not current production calibration.

A generic current-safe form is:

```text
risk = f(versioned_inputs, model_version)
```

The protected definition of `f`, thresholds and calibration belongs in the restricted model registry when proprietary.

## Freshness

Risk is time-dependent.

```text
current? -> usable according to policy
stale?   -> HOLD/recalculate where required
unknown? -> HOLD/deny according to operation
```

A cached low-risk score cannot override a new sanctions, identity, rights or security state.

## Risk bands

If used, risk bands must be versioned policy/model outputs.

```text
LOW
MEDIUM
HIGH
CRITICAL
```

No public threshold values are institutionalized by this page.

## Current AIE mapping

AIE is the current/historical intelligence lineage for anomaly/risk signals. Risk computation belongs in the intelligence layer; policy consequences belong in ACE/P-02.

```text
AIE / analytics
     |
 risk evidence
     |
     v
P-02 / ACE policy
     |
PASS | HOLD | DENY / REVIEW
```

## Model governance

A risk model requires:

- model ID/version;
- documented inputs;
- training/evaluation provenance where applicable;
- bias/risk assessment;
- threshold/config version;
- authorized deployment state;
- monitoring/drift;
- rollback;
- human-review triggers.

## Historical operation families

- write/update risk record from authorized source;
- query risk;
- check risk threshold/band;
- invalidate/stale a record;
- use risk in compliance pre-check.

Exact historical extrinsic names are not recovered.

## Human review

High uncertainty or protected decision classes can route to human review.

```text
risk high / uncertainty high
        |
        v
HOLD
        |
review evidence
        |
new decision under authority
```

The reviewer decides under current policy; the score itself is not a final adjudication.

## Security / abuse risks

- score manipulation;
- poisoned input data;
- model drift;
- threshold leakage/gaming;
- discriminatory proxy features;
- stale score replay;
- provider/oracle spoofing;
- self-reinforcing false positives;
- model version mismatch.

Protected thresholds, feature logic and anti-evasion rules remain restricted.

## Public / restricted split

Public-safe:

- model governance architecture;
- risk-evidence semantics;
- freshness/versioning;
- current mapping.

Restricted:

- model weights;
- feature engineering;
- thresholds;
- training/eval data;
- adversarial tests;
- provider/oracle detail;
- fraud-detection rules.

## Invariants

```text
risk score != violation
risk score != legal conclusion
model output != authority
stale risk != current evidence
higher score != automatic punishment
AIE recommendation != ACE/P-02 decision
historical risk formula != current production model
runtime pallet recovered != deployed runtime
```
