# CHLOM Across the CrownThrive Ecosystem

CHLOM is a shared governance layer. It does not replace the product, audience, commerce, media, education, analytics, community or cultural-governance systems it serves.

[**PentaGreen™**](/commerce/pentagreen) (legacy alias: ThriveEvergreen™) is CHLOM's cross-pallet commerce and economic-execution subprotocol. It converts CHLOM-governed rights and authority into exact product, SKU, offer, price, advertising, payment, entitlement, fulfillment, settlement and reconciliation state without becoming the source of ownership itself.

The division of authority is explicit:

- **CHLOM** governs rights, authority, policy, lineage, evidence and remedies;
- **Cultural Imprint Engine (CIE)** governs cultural meaning, narrative continuity, imprint identity, representation, aesthetics, audience/canon constraints and responsible reuse;
- **ThriveEvergreen** governs commercial activation and the complete economic lifecycle;
- **AdLuxe Network / Studio / Places** govern specialist advertising creation, inventory, delivery and measurement while ThriveEvergreen controls their commercial/economic layer;
- **CrownLytics / CrownPulse / ThrivePush** measure, signal and communicate approved state;
- individual CrownThrive platforms own their domain experience and fulfillment surfaces.

## Cultural Imprint Engine interoperability

CHLOM is interoperable with **Cultural Imprint Engine** through the versioned contract `ct.contract.cie-chlom.interop.v1`.

There is one CIE engine identity: **Cultural Imprint Engine** (`ct.framework.cultural-imprint-engine`). Public-safe and internal-governed CIE usage are profiles of that same engine. CHLOM must not create or accept a second engine identity from stale `Cultural Impact Engine` wording.

### CIE → CHLOM

CIE supplies a typed envelope containing object/use identity, CIE state, conditions, audience class, canon state, source references, usage profile, correlation ID and digest.

CHLOM remains authoritative for:

- ownership and rights state;
- license state;
- permission state;
- evidence state;
- governed records and remedies.

A CIE `PASS` or `CONDITIONAL_PASS` never becomes CHLOM `ALLOW` by inheritance.

### CHLOM → CIE

CHLOM returns a typed receipt containing CHLOM record identity, rights state, license state, permission state, evidence state, correlation ID and digest.

That receipt never creates or rewrites CIE cultural alignment. CHLOM `ALLOW` never becomes CIE `PASS` by inheritance.

### Composed readiness

The two states may be deterministically composed into bounded outcomes including cultural/rights denial, CIE/CHLOM/evidence HOLD states and `COMPOSED_READY_NON_EXECUTING`.

`COMPOSED_READY_NON_EXECUTING` means the two governance layers are sufficiently resolved for the evaluated use. It does **not** authorize provider writes, production deployment, ThriveEvergreen ECAC, checkout, customer entitlement, D3 action or sovereign approval.

See [CIE ↔ CHLOM Interoperability](/doctrine/cie-chlom-interoperability).

## Virality Music

CHLOM governs or can govern:

- song, master, stem, lyric, artwork, character, book, voice, remix, adaptation, and AI-use rights;
- Virality Vault versus Distribution Catalog status;
- participatory-rights settings;
- licensing eligibility;
- provenance and human-authorship records;
- corrections and rights holds.

CIE remains responsible for applicable story-world, cultural, canon, representation, audience and cross-imprint constraints.

## Good Shit Only

GSO is the preferred governed rights and licensing commerce surface when a transaction grants broader commercial, institutional, derivative, or exploitation permissions.

CHLOM supplies the rights state and audit logic beneath the listing. CIE may independently constrain cultural/brand reuse where relevant.

## Stripe Direct

Stripe Direct is appropriate for selected simple finished digital goods. CHLOM still defines the rights boundary so payment for a personal-use file is not mistaken for a broader IP grant. CIE state does not itself authorize the economic transaction.

## KJV Visualized and The Sermon Toolkit

CHLOM can govern edition rights, digital-license classes, public-display permissions, church/school/maker use, entitlement state, and correction history while the product platform handles the customer experience. CIE governs cultural/narrative and audience-context requirements that apply to the use.

## AdLuxe Network

CHLOM can govern creative ownership, campaign rights, publisher permissions, sponsor use, distribution scope, and rights/policy requirements. CIE governs applicable cultural-context and representation constraints. ThriveEvergreen separately governs economic activation.

## CrownThrive Studios and media corridors

CHLOM can connect source rights to production, adaptation, public performance, distribution, attribution, release windows, and downstream revenue obligations. CIE preserves identity, canon, narrative, representation and audience continuity.

## CrownThriveU / Impact Institute

CHLOM can define educational-use permissions, cohort access, credential rights, curriculum licensing, public-display rules, and institutional usage. CIE can independently govern culturally specific curriculum framing and responsible reuse.

## Analytics and communications

CrownLytics, CrownPulse, ThrivePush, and related systems may consume approved status and event data. They should not receive restricted evidence or private rights documents merely because they can consume an event.

Public-safe CIE/CHLOM events must not include private evidence references. Internal-governed references require receiving-system authorization.

## Integration principle

Each platform owns its domain experience. CIE owns the governed cultural/narrative meaning of the evaluated use. CHLOM owns the governed meaning of permissions, authority, lineage, evidence and remedies. ThriveEvergreen owns commercial/economic activation. Interoperability connects those states without collapsing them.
