# CHLOM Across the CrownThrive Ecosystem

CHLOM is a shared governance layer. It does not replace the product, audience, commerce, media, education, analytics, or community platforms it serves.

## Shared operating pattern

```text
CrownThrive ID
      ↓
CrownThrive IO / MCP
      ↓
CHLOM
      ↓
OpsOasis + Collab Portal
      ↓
Support + CrownInsights
      ↓
CrownLytics + CrownPulse + ThrivePush
      ↓
CrownRewards + partner systems
      ↓
Portfolio platforms and corridors
```

## Virality Music

CHLOM governs or can govern:

- song, master, stem, lyric, artwork, character, book, voice, remix, adaptation, and AI-use rights
- Virality Vault versus Distribution Catalog status
- participatory-rights settings
- licensing eligibility
- provenance and human-authorship records
- corrections and rights holds

## Good Shit Only

GSO is the preferred governed rights and licensing commerce surface when a transaction grants broader commercial, institutional, derivative, or exploitation permissions.

CHLOM supplies the rights state and audit logic beneath the listing.

## Stripe Direct

Stripe Direct is appropriate for selected simple finished digital goods. CHLOM still defines the rights boundary so payment for a personal-use file is not mistaken for a broader IP grant.

## KJV Visualized and The Sermon Toolkit

CHLOM can govern edition rights, digital-license classes, public-display permissions, church/school/maker use, entitlement state, and correction history while the product platform handles the customer experience.

## AdLuxe Network

CHLOM can govern creative ownership, campaign rights, publisher permissions, sponsor use, distribution scope, and brand-safety or policy requirements.

## CrownThrive Studios and media corridors

CHLOM can connect source rights to production, adaptation, public performance, distribution, attribution, release windows, and downstream revenue obligations.

## CrownThriveU / Impact Institute

CHLOM can define educational-use permissions, cohort access, credential rights, curriculum licensing, public-display rules, and institutional usage.

## Analytics and communications

CrownLytics, CrownPulse, ThrivePush, and related systems may consume approved status and event data. They should not receive restricted evidence or private rights documents merely because they can consume an event.

## Integration principle

Each platform owns its domain experience. CHLOM owns the governed meaning of permissions, authority, lineage, evidence, economics, and remedies that must remain consistent across platforms.
