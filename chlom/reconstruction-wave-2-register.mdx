## Continuity inherited from Wave 1

Each page identifies historical meaning, current meaning, implementation boundary, evidence/authority separation, founder-adjudication status and correction behavior. No source-era token, DAO, blockchain, royalty rate, legal classification, API or provider integration is promoted merely because it existed in a paper.

## Key reconciliations advanced

### DLA

The authority/object ambiguity is decomposed into:

```text
historical Decentralized Licensing Authority
→ current Licensing Stewardship / Issuer Authority
+ current Dynamic Licensing Asset
```

### DAIL / DAL

Current DAIL remains **Decentralized Autonomous Information Ledger**. Historical DAL meanings remain source-period records for attestation, adjudication and distribution-related concepts.

### LEX

LEX becomes an explicit rights-gated lifecycle rather than a generic marketplace assumption. Listing, payment, DLA, entitlement, fulfillment and settlement remain distinct states.

### Service lineage

The two historical TLaaS expansions are preserved, CaaS remains Compliance-as-a-Service, and dS-CaaS is retained as a distinct current label whose exact expansion/relationship stays source/ADR controlled where contradictory.

### Cases and overrides

Historical adjudication/guardianship concepts map to cases, holds, appeals, remedies and bounded override authority. An override never erases the control/evidence it overrides.

### Economics

Royalty/split calculation is separated from obligation, provider execution and institutional settlement. Historical percentages are not activated by reconstruction.

## Accounting rule

Wave counts increase only when substantive bodies exist. A nav route, recovered title or historical mention is not counted as a reconstructed article by itself.

## Founder review mode

A founder ruling may be issued after publication in ordinary language. The ruling becomes a dated adjudication/correction, updates current meaning, and propagates to terminology, source, platform, policy, article, changelog and machine records while retaining the earlier source lineage.

## Phase effect

Wave 2 expands institutional knowledge and engineering specification. It does not by itself satisfy the Phase 2.99 hard exit, authorize Phase 3, activate decentralized/token infrastructure, grant rights, move money or certify legal/professional compliance.
