# ALG-009 — Economic Allocation

## Institutional identity

ALG-009 converts a validated economic event into a deterministic allocation plan. It is designed for royalties, commissions, platform fees, reserves, approved deductions and other multi-party obligations while keeping every cent/unit accounted for.

## Core invariants

```text
provider evidence != approved deduction
historical percentage != current schedule
sum of authorized destinations + holds + residue = validated economic basis
```

No allocation is valid without a versioned schedule and a defined basis.

## Procedure

```text
function allocate(event, schedule):
    gross = validated_gross(event)
    deductions = authorized_deductions(event, schedule)
    net = gross - sum(deductions)

    allocations = []
    for recipient in schedule.recipients:
        raw = net * recipient.bps / 10000
        allocations.append(raw)

    allocations = deterministic_round(allocations, schedule.rounding)

    assert gross == sum(deductions) + sum(allocations) + holds + residue
    return allocation_plan
```

## Schedule contract

A schedule should declare gross or net basis, recipient stable IDs, basis points or fixed amounts, priority/order, floors/caps if adopted, reserve treatment, recoupment if applicable, effective period, currency precision, rounding/residue policy and authority/source evidence.

## Deductions

Only deductions authorized by the governing agreement/policy are allowed. A payment processor's fee may be observed in provider evidence, but whether it is borne by a seller, shared, reimbursed or excluded from distributable basis depends on the adopted schedule.

## Deterministic rounding

Rounding must define mode, precision, ordering/tie handling and residue destination. The same input snapshot and schedule version must produce the same allocation result across implementations.

The allocation plan is an obligation/calculation record. Provider payout execution and final settlement are separate stages.

## Safety and authority

ALG-009 does not decide who deserves a share. It computes an already-authorized schedule. AI may simulate alternative allocations but cannot change the active schedule through this algorithm.

## DAIL evidence

Store the calculation inputs, schedule version, evidence refs, exact arithmetic result, rounding result, conservation assertion and downstream payout/settlement references. Corrections create reversing or superseding records rather than deleting the original calculation.

## Tests

Test full 10,000 bps distribution, partial distribution with reserve, exact decimals, one-cent residue, zero gross, refunds/negative adjustments, duplicate recipients, share total over 10,000, unsupported currency precision, schedule version change and reversal.

## Versioning

Arithmetic rules, rounding or schedule interpretation changes require a new algorithm version. Existing allocations remain tied to the version that generated them.
