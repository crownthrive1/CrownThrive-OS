# ALG-025 — Audit Finding Severity

## Institutional identity

```yaml
algorithm_id: ALG-025
name: Audit Finding Severity
classification: reconstructed_from_requirements
visibility: public_safe_pattern
source_family: audit/certification; ACE controls; case management
version: 1.0.0
current_authority: specification_only
```

ALG-025 classifies audit/control findings into operational severity bands. The public algorithm intentionally avoids a universal proprietary numeric score because severity depends on control domain, exposure, materiality and adopted policy.

## Public-safe decision pattern

```text
if control_failure is prohibited hard condition:
    CRITICAL
elif material impact and active exposure:
    HIGH
elif control weakness without confirmed material event:
    MEDIUM
else:
    LOW / ADVISORY
```

## Inputs

```yaml

  failed_control_ref
  control_class

  evidence_refs[]

```

## Severity semantics

**CRITICAL** means a prohibited or catastrophic control condition requiring immediate governed response under the applicable policy. **HIGH** represents material active exposure or a significant failure requiring urgent remediation. **MEDIUM** represents a meaningful weakness without confirmed material impact at the time of assessment. **LOW/ADVISORY** represents limited-impact gaps, opportunities or informational deviations.

These labels are not legal conclusions and should not be presented as external certifications unless an authorized assurance process supports that claim.

## Deterministic factors

The policy can define hard mappings such as a known revoked production credential still in use, public exposure of a restricted secret, unauthorized rights grant, or execution beyond authority. Other domains may require contextual review.

## Human review

Where materiality or impact is uncertain, the system should preserve the algorithm's preliminary class and route to a qualified reviewer rather than forcing a false precision score.

## Remediation linkage

Severity can determine SLA, escalation and temporary safeguards, but remediation authority remains governed. A CRITICAL finding may trigger an emergency hold if the adopted policy allows it; the severity label itself is not the pause authority.

## DAIL evidence

Record finding, evidence, policy version, preliminary severity, reviewer adjustments and final accepted severity. If a reviewer changes the class, append the change with reason rather than overwriting the preliminary evaluation.

## Tests

Test hard-prohibited condition, active material exposure, weakness without incident, advisory item, uncertain impact, competing control classes, reviewer downgrade/upgrade and policy-severity mapping change.

## Versioning

Severity definitions and mappings are versioned by domain. Avoid changing the historical meaning of prior labels without a migration/adjudication record.
