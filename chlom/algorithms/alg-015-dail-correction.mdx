# ALG-015 — DAIL Correction and Supersession

## Institutional identity

```yaml
algorithm_id: ALG-015
name: DAIL Correction and Supersession
classification: reconstructed_from_requirements
visibility: public_safe_pattern
source_family: DAIL current architecture; founder adjudication; evidence correction lineage
version: 1.0.0
current_authority: specification_only
```

DAIL corrections are additive. ALG-015 ensures institutional memory can be corrected without deleting the evidence of what was originally recorded, who corrected it, why, and what evidence justified the new effective interpretation.

## Constitutional rule

```text
correct != erase
supersede != delete
new truth claim must preserve prior lineage
```

## Procedure

```text
function correct_event(original_id, correction):
    original = get(original_id)
    require correction.authority
    require correction.reason
    require correction.evidence_refs

    correction.supersedes = original_id
    append_event(correction)

    recompute_effective_projection(original.subject)
```

## Correction classes

A correction may clarify metadata, correct a factual error, supersede an interpretation, record a revocation, reverse an economic event, replace an outdated provider mapping, or append a founder/governance adjudication. The event type must state which semantics apply.

## Authority

Not every actor who can observe an error can change the effective institutional state. The correction authority must match the subject and correction class. A provider can correct its observation; it cannot unilaterally change CrownThrive rights or governance authority unless the institutional rules grant that power.

## Evidence

The correction should identify why the earlier event is no longer the effective interpretation and point to the evidence supporting the correction. A bare 'fixed' entry is inadequate for high-consequence state.

## Effective projection

Consumers may read an effective state derived from the correction chain, but must be able to inspect the chain where authorized:

```text
E0 original
  |
  v
E1 correction of E0
  |
  v
E2 supersession of E1
```

The reducer selects the current effective representation according to valid event and authority semantics; it does not physically replace E0.

## Conflict handling

Competing corrections with unresolved authority or evidence should create a conflict/HOLD state. The reducer must not choose whichever event arrived last if authority precedence is unresolved.

## DAIL self-reference

The correction itself is appended through ALG-014 and becomes part of DAIL. Corrections to corrections are allowed through the same mechanism, producing a complete audit chain.

## Tests

Test simple factual correction, revocation, two corrections in sequence, unauthorized correction, correction without evidence, conflicting corrections, correction to restricted evidence, projection rebuild, historical readback and founder adjudication propagation.

## Versioning

Reducer/correction semantics are versioned. A later algorithm upgrade cannot rewrite historical event bytes; it may recompute an effective projection with explicit version lineage.
