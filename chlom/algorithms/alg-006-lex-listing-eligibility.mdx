LEX Listing Eligibility determines whether a proposed license, permission, brand right or other governed rights-bearing asset is eligible to enter the CHLOM License Exchange workflow.

## Core invariant

```text
marketability does not create rights
payment capability does not create rights
listing creation does not activate a license
```

A listing is a governed offer projection of already-resolved rights.

## Procedure

```text
function listing_eligibility(asset, dla, offer):
    require asset.registered
    require rights_clear(asset)
    require dla.active
    require offer.scope ⊆ dla.rights
    require economic_schedule_valid(offer)
    require listing_policy_passes(offer)

    if any required fact unknown:
        return HOLD

    return PASS
```

## Rights checks

The listing must trace to source rights and an active DLA/version. The offered scope must not exceed territory, duration, channels, exclusivity, transferability or derivative-use limits. If the DLA allows use but prohibits transfer, the asset cannot be projected as freely tradable.

## Seller/issuer checks

The actor creating the listing must have authority to offer the exact right. A platform administrator may have technical ability to create a record without being legally or institutionally authorized to offer the right.

## Economic checks

Economic validation covers the existence and version of an approved schedule, supported currency/rail, required royalties or commissions, fee handling, tax/professional gates where applicable, refund/escrow expectations and deterministic rounding rules. It does not treat historical tokenomics or sample percentages as current commercial terms.

## Policy checks

Applicable Compliance Packs can impose eligibility rules, disclosure requirements, prohibited categories, transfer constraints, required evidence, jurisdiction restrictions or review gates. Unknown required policy facts route to HOLD.

PASS means eligible to proceed into a listing-creation action, not that a later buyer transaction is approved.

## Lifecycle interaction

Eligibility must be rechecked when a listing is renewed, materially edited, or executed after relevant rights/policy state has changed. Revocation or suspension of the underlying DLA should prevent further execution and may deactivate the marketplace projection.

## DAIL evidence

Record the eligibility snapshot and the exact DLA, source-right, policy and economic versions used. Provider marketplace IDs are aliases that may be attached after creation.

## Tests

Test unregistered asset, disputed rights, inactive DLA, scope overrun, transfer-prohibited DLA, invalid economic schedule, unsupported currency, missing disclosure, stale evidence, listing edit after approval, DLA revoked after listing, and exact-scope valid listing.

## Versioning

Material changes to listability rules require a new version; historical listings retain the rule version under which they were assessed.
