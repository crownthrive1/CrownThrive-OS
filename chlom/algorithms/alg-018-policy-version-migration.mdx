# ALG-018 — Policy Version Migration

## Institutional identity

```yaml
algorithm_id: ALG-018
name: Policy Version Migration
classification: reconstructed_from_requirements
visibility: public_safe_pattern
source_family: Compliance Pack versioning; Policy Engine; governance/change control
version: 1.0.0
current_authority: specification_only
```

ALG-018 governs the transition from one approved CHLOM policy version to another. It exists to make policy evolution testable and reversible and to prevent a generated, simulated or merely edited rule from becoming institutional policy automatically.

## Constitutional invariant

```text
simulation does not self-adopt
AI-generated policy != adopted policy
```

## Procedure

```text
function adopt_policy(new_version):
    validate_schema(new_version)
    run_regression_suite(new_version)
    simulate_against_historical_events(new_version)
    compare_deltas(current_version, new_version)
    require_governed_approval()
    schedule_effective_time()
    append_dail_event("POLICY_ADOPTED")
```

## Candidate validation

Validate syntax, schema, referenced controls, dependencies, effective dates, allowed values and forbidden self-references. Unsupported references must fail before simulation.

## Regression testing

The suite should test explicit requirements and known edge cases. A policy version that makes tests pass by deleting a required control is not acceptable; test coverage is itself governed.

## Historical replay and delta analysis

Replay the candidate against representative historical events and compare outcomes to the current version:

```yaml
PASS_to_PASS:
PASS_to_HOLD:
PASS_to_DENY:
HOLD_to_PASS:
HOLD_to_DENY:
DENY_to_PASS:
```

High-impact deltas require review and explanation. Replay is an analysis tool, not retroactive rewriting of historical decisions.

## Approval

Approval must be performed by the authority required for that policy class: founder/reserved authority, governance body, compliance/legal reviewer, platform owner or another explicitly delegated role. AI can prepare evidence but cannot satisfy a human approval requirement by asserting approval.

## Effective-time scheduling

A new version can be approved before it becomes effective. At activation, the system should revalidate that the approval remains valid, dependencies are available and no emergency HOLD blocks deployment.

## DAIL evidence

Append candidate registration, test result, simulation, approval, activation and rollback events separately. Do not collapse them into one mutable status row without event lineage.

## Tests

Test compatible change, breaking schema, missing dependency, unexpected PASS-to-DENY spike, absent approval, approval revoked before effective time, rollback, overlapping effective windows and concurrent candidate versions.

## Versioning

ALG-018 itself is versioned independently from the policies it migrates.
