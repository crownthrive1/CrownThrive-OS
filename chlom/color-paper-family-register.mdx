## Why color matters

A paper's color is not decorative metadata. In the recovered corpus, paper families separated different kinds of institutional thinking.

```text
                  CHLOM PAPER TAXONOMY
                         |
      +------------------+------------------+
      |                  |                  |
      v                  v                  v
  EXPLANATION        DEEP DESIGN        CONTROLLED STUDY
      |                  |                  |
   WHITE              BLACK / GOLD      BLUE / RED / GREEN
                                              |
                                              v
                                  exact historical semantics
                                  require source recovery
```

Only the Blackpaper family has an explicit surviving Help Center definition: **a Blackpaper is not a whitepaper; it is a declaration of structure, sovereignty and enforcement across economic layers.**

The exact original semantic distinctions for Blue, Red and Green are not sufficiently recovered to fabricate definitions today.

## White Papers

### Confirmed state

`confirmed_family`

### Strongest recovered examples

- CHLOM: AI-Driven Decentralized Compliance and Licensing Whitepaper;
- Technical White Paper — Protocol, Cryptography, Economics and Verification;
- CHLOM Developer Whitepaper;
- module-specific whitepaper families;
- sovereign-industry licensing whitepapers/studies;
- macroeconomic / financial architecture whitepaper lineage;
- institutional synthesis and feasibility whitepaper lineage.

### Functional interpretation

White Papers are the strongest recovered family for explaining a problem, architecture, technical approach, market/industry implications and implementation thesis.

### Authority boundary

A whitepaper may describe intended architecture. It does not prove deployment, legal approval, token activation or current provider state.

## Blackpapers

### Confirmed state

`confirmed_family`

### Explicit recovered definition

Blackpapers are distinguished from whitepapers and framed around **structure, sovereignty and enforcement across economic layers**.

### Recovered Blackpaper-style topics

Current recovery records include lineage around:

- sovereignty and enforcement;
- governance paths;
- formal proof;
- DAO architecture;
- Substrate/runtime design;
- Fingerprint ID;
- ledger/attribution;
- onboarding;
- enforcement structures;
- deep implementation reasoning.

### Functional interpretation

Blackpapers preserve deep architecture, enforcement assumptions, constitutional structure, invariants and system mechanics. They may be more sensitive than ordinary explanatory papers.

### Security rule

A historical Blackpaper reference is not permission to disclose restricted algorithms, credentials, security configurations or trade-secret implementation detail.

## Gold Papers

### Confirmed state

`confirmed_family`

### Recovered lineage

The Help Center and recovery register preserve references to:

- CrownThrive Gold Paper;
- CHLOM Gold Paper;
- Internal Gold Paper;
- Master Technical Document variants associated with deep internal design.

### Current treatment

Gold Papers are treated as **high-value internal design/IP lineage** until each exact source body is recovered and classified.

Recovered surrounding references associate this family with identity, license lifecycle, splitters, override trees, treasury, governance, verifiers, oracles, bridges, indexing, proofs and formal invariants.

That association is a recovery map, not a claim that every listed topic appears in every Gold Paper.

## Blue Papers

### Confirmed state

`family_confirmed_individual_bodies_partial`

### What is known

Blue Papers exist as a distinct CHLOM Help Center family.

### What is not yet source-proven

The exact historical scope, color semantics, individual titles and original bodies are not sufficiently recovered in the presently available source set.

### Reconstruction rule

Do not assign Blue a made-up meaning such as "technical specification" or "policy paper" unless an original Blue Paper, Help Center definition or founder adjudication establishes it.

## Paper color does not establish authority

```text
WHITE != public
BLACK != secret
GOLD  != canonical
BLUE  != approved
RED   != rejected
GREEN != current
```

Authority comes from source, date, version, classification, ADR/founder adjudication and verified current state.
