# CIE Cultural Governance Pallet

**Pallet ID:** `ct.chlom.pallet.cie-cultural-governance`  
**State:** candidate, non-operational

The pallet defines a fail-closed sequence:

1. CIE context and cultural-alignment preflight.
2. CHLOM identity and chain-of-title verification.
3. CHLOM permission, license, and policy checks.
4. CHLOM evidence-provenance and audit receipt.
5. Human review, remedy, and appeal for material or consequential uses.

CIE cannot grant rights. CHLOM cannot substitute for cultural alignment. Missing evidence, unresolved rights, material canon conflict, public/restricted-data leakage, digest mismatch, or unavailable human remedy returns HOLD or BLOCK.

No wallet, on-chain, token, settlement, checkout, entitlement, or money-movement state is activated by this pallet.
