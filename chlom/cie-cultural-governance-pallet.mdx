# CIE Cultural Governance Pallet

`ct.chlom.pallet.cie-cultural-governance` packages the Cultural Imprint Engine into a CHLOM-compatible capability surface. It primarily maps to P-02 Governance & Compliance and secondarily to P-04 Media & Intellectual Property.

## Purpose

CIE determines whether a proposed use is culturally and narratively aligned. CHLOM separately determines whether the use is owned, permitted, licensed, recorded and remediable. A CIE PASS cannot manufacture rights; a CHLOM rights PASS cannot erase a CIE cultural hard block.

## Service interfaces

Cloud-first target interfaces:

- `POST /v1/cie/scans` — submit normalized evidence packet;
- `GET /v1/cie/scans/{id}` — retrieve score and findings;
- `POST /v1/cie/scans/{id}/corrections` — attach remediation evidence;
- `POST /v1/cie/scans/{id}/appeals` — request governed reconsideration;
- `GET /v1/cie/policies/current` — retrieve public-safe scoring contract;
- MCP targets: `cie.scan`, `cie.explain`, `cie.corrections`, `cie.appeal`, `cie.docs.open_delta`.

These are Phase-3 targets, not current production claims.

## CHLOM six-function mapping

- **Rights:** CIE routes rights-sensitive findings to CHLOM/DLA; it does not grant rights.
- **Rules:** five canonical dimensions, evidence rules, score bands and hard blocks.
- **Roles:** CIE parent, five dimension subagents, remediation/escalation subagent, Agent D and human authority.
- **Revenue:** candidate paid scans, continuous monitoring, enterprise policy packs and managed governance.
- **Records:** append-oriented scan, finding, score, evidence, correction and appeal records.
- **Remedies:** correction, hold, rescan, appeal, assignment, escalation and reversible release block.

## AI/ML boundary

The first implementation is deterministic and evidence-scored. Future ML may prioritize patterns or propose candidate findings only after labeled datasets, privacy review, bias testing, TEVV and human override are proven. ML may never determine a person's cultural authenticity.
