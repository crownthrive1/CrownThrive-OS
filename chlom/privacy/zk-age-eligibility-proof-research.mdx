## Source boundary

## Purpose

A ZK age proof allows a subject to prove an age-related predicate such as:

```text
age_at(reference_date) >= required_threshold
```

without necessarily disclosing full date of birth, identity document number or underlying credential body.

The proof family can also support bounded eligibility predicates derived from age where an adopted policy requires them.

## Public statement model

The private witness can include birth-date/credential data according to the approved circuit and custody model. That witness is not written into public DAIL or frontend payloads.

## Predicate semantics

The proof must define whether age is computed at:

- current date;
- transaction/effective date;
- event date;
- another explicit policy reference date.

A generic predicate is:

```text
DOB + threshold_years <= reference_date
```

Calendar arithmetic must define leap-day and jurisdiction-specific interpretation where relevant. The article does not set a legal age threshold.

## Credential source

The witness should derive from a registered credential/evidence source acceptable to the policy. A self-asserted birth date and a verified government/authorized credential are different evidence classes.

Issuer authority, revocation, expiration and currentness are checked independently from cryptographic proof verification.

## Privacy objective

The verifier should learn only the approved public statement, such as `threshold satisfied`, plus the minimum metadata needed to determine proof usability. It should not learn full birth date unless the policy explicitly requires disclosure outside the ZK flow.

## Proof usability

FORM-025 / ALG-013 apply:

```text
proof cryptographically valid
AND circuit version allowed
AND witness credential current/not revoked
AND context matches
AND policy accepts proof type
= proof usable candidate
```

A usable proof still does not grant action authority.

## Reuse and replay

Where a proof is action-specific, bind it to domain/context, subject or pseudonymous commitment, policy, nonce/challenge and effective time. A proof generated for one service or jurisdiction should not automatically be replayable into another.

## Minors and sensitive data

Age proofs can concern minors and other sensitive contexts. Public-facing widgets should expose the threshold result, not underlying private details. Access logs, evidence custody and retention require the applicable privacy/safety policy.

## Failure states

- invalid proof;
- unsupported/retired circuit;
- revoked/expired source credential;
- stale reference date or proof;
- wrong threshold/policy context;
- wrong subject binding;
- nonce/replay failure;
- credential source not accepted;
- privacy projection contains prohibited fields.

## DAIL evidence

A public-safe proof receipt can preserve proof ID/digest, circuit/version, public statement, issuer/source class, verification result, currentness checks, context, evaluated time and later revocation/supersession references. Private witness stays outside the event.

## Tests

Test below/at/above threshold, leap-day date handling, wrong reference date, invalid credential, revoked credential, retired circuit, wrong nonce/context, cross-tenant replay, stale proof and public-payload privacy scan.
