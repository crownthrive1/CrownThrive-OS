## Purpose

A ZK proof-of-license should prove a narrowly defined statement about an active license/DLA while minimizing disclosure of confidential license terms, identity details and source-right evidence.

A representative public predicate is:

```text
subject holds a current valid permission
for requested_use u
under DLA/license version v
within term/territory/channel constraints
```

without necessarily disclosing the entire agreement or private commercial terms.

## Core predicate relationship

The proof should correspond to the effective DLA validity and rights constraints rather than a simple `license_exists` flag.

```text
license_exists
AND source_rights_valid
AND holder_binding_valid
AND requested_use_in_scope
AND term_valid
AND not_revoked_or_suspended
AND required_policy/credential conditions represented by circuit are satisfied
```

The exact predicate encoded by a circuit must be explicit and versioned.

## Relationship to DLA

The circuit does not create or extend a DLA. It proves a predicate about a DLA/license state already grounded in source rights and institutional lifecycle records.

```text
proof of license != license issuance
proof of license != renewal
proof of license != transfer authority
```

## Rights-scope proof

A useful proof can show that a requested use belongs to an allowed scope without revealing the entire scope set. The underlying model must preserve:

```text
requested_use ∈ R_effective
```

where `R_effective` follows CHLOM's rights intersection and conservation rules.

## Holder binding

The proof must bind the permission to the correct holder/licensee identity or approved pseudonymous commitment. A valid license held by another party cannot be reused by the prover.

## Revocation and currentness

A proof generated while a license was active can become unusable after revocation, suspension, expiration, parent-right loss or policy-required evidence change. The proof receipt therefore must be evaluated against current lifecycle/revocation state when the action requires current validity.

## Private terms

The circuit may be designed to prove facts such as:

- permission covers a use category;
- territory includes a requested region;
- term includes a requested date;
- sublicensing is allowed;
- a capacity threshold remains;
- a required credential/economic condition is satisfied;

without revealing unrelated rates, parties, business terms or private evidence.

Whether a particular predicate is implemented is determined by the actual adopted circuit, not this conceptual list.

## Proof usability

ALG-013/FORM-025 apply. Cryptographic validity is only the first gate. Circuit version, DLA/currentness, witness source, requested use and policy context must all match.

## Embeddable verification

A public license-status widget can display a public-safe verification receipt or canonical proof reference without exposing the private witness. Static screenshots or copied markup should not be treated as current proof.

## Replay protection

Where the proof authorizes a specific action, bind it to tenant/domain/action, object, nonce/challenge and time. A proof created for one transaction should not automatically authorize a different transaction or broader scope.

## Threat model

- proof for wrong license/subject;
- proof for broader use than encoded;
- stale proof after revocation;
- circuit accepts incomplete DLA predicate;
- private agreement leakage;
- replay/cross-tenant use;
- malicious/retired verification key;
- parent license revoked but child proof remains accepted;
- provider alias mistaken for canonical license identity.

## DAIL evidence

A public-safe receipt can store proof digest/ref, circuit/version, DLA/asset commitment refs, requested-use class, reference/evaluation time, verification result, currentness check and correction/revocation lineage. It must not contain the private license body.

## Tests

Test valid in-scope license, requested scope outside license, expired DLA, revoked parent, suspended license, wrong holder, wrong nonce/context, retired circuit, stale proof and public-payload privacy scan.
