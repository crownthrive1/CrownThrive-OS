## Source-discipline decisions

Wave 2C deliberately does **not** pretend every recovered title has a surviving original body.

Two categories remain explicitly partial:

### Scribes

Confirmed source lineage exists for:

- Oracle & Scribe Network research;
- Scribes;
- Cognitive Scribe Network & human-layer research.

The complete original standalone body has not been recovered in the current corpus, so the page reconstructs a conservative bounded-review architecture rather than claiming verbatim recovery.

### Sovereign-industry studies

The paper registry confirms studies for music, film/streaming, real estate, education, gaming/simulation, healthcare, finance/capital markets, government/public sector, football/sports and literary creators.

The full original body for every individual study is not currently recovered. Wave 2C therefore reconstructs the shared blueprint framework and retains each sector for later source-by-source articleization.

## Rights hierarchy advanced

Wave 2C makes the rights-conservation invariant explicit:

```text
CHILD RIGHTS <= PARENT RIGHTS <= SOURCE RIGHTS
```

It adds parent/child DLA lineage, chain of title, derivative rights, assignments versus sublicenses, exclusivity collisions, downstream invalidation and rights-graph test vectors.

## Human assurance advanced

The Scribe/verifier model separates:

```text
scribe -> organize/contextualize
verifier -> bounded evidence check
authority -> institutional decision
```

The article also adds competency, conflict-of-interest, quorum, specialist-routing, AI-assisted Scribe and separation-of-duties rules.

## AI/risk layer advanced

The AIE/ACE article recovers the Genesis distinction:

```text
AIE -> intelligence / score / forecast / simulate
ACE -> deterministic policy evaluation
Authority Gateway -> permission to act
```

It adds shadow mode, drift monitoring, fairness evaluation, policy simulation, explanations and temporary reversible-hold controls.
