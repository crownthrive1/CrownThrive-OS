## O. Pallet packaging architecture

The July 2026 CHLOM build pack groups platform capabilities into 12 governed pallets:

1. P-01 Identity & Access
2. P-02 Governance & Compliance
3. P-03 Rights & Licensing
4. P-04 Media & Intellectual Property
5. P-05 Growth, Advertising & Measurement
6. P-06 Commerce, Booking & Loyalty
7. P-07 Community, Directory & Events
8. P-08 Education, Incubator & Impact
9. P-09 Physical Suites & Place-Based
10. P-10 Operations & Institutional Memory
11. P-11 Capital, Revenue & Settlement
12. P-12 Sustainability & Impact Evidence

These capability pallets are a **cloud/API product-packaging model** and should not be confused with historical Substrate runtime pallets. Both meanings are preserved through namespace/version metadata.

## Build status fields

Each component must carry:

`source-recovered → reconciled → specified → prototype → test → staged → production → maintained → superseded`.

Additional flags identify `cloud-service`, `runtime-pallet`, `protocol-research`, `UI-component`, `agent-tool`, `vendor-adapter`, `restricted-IP`, and `legal-review-required`.

## Phase 3 requirement

Sprint 0 should seed this atlas as machine-readable component records so Phase 3 can select what to build without losing hundreds of historical concepts or accidentally shipping them all at once.
