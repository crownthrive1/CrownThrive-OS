# CHLOM Compliance-as-a-Service

**Platform ID:** `ct.platform.chlom-caas`  
**CSS framework:** `ct.framework.crownthrive-services-stack`  
**State:** controlled test / no professional certification claim

CHLOM Compliance-as-a-Service converts shared-service governance into reusable controls, evidence requests, gap states, remediation sequencing, independent verification and immutable receipts.

CaaS is the governance and evidence layer above the CrownThrive Services Stack. It does not replace qualified legal, accounting, tax, accessibility, cybersecurity, insurance or regulatory professionals.

## CaaS outputs

For every CSS lane, CaaS can produce:

- service and provider inventory;
- exact contract and version state;
- authority and data-class boundaries;
- mapped controls;
- evidence requirements;
- deterministic readiness and coverage scores;
- unknowns and stale-evidence flags;
- prioritized remediation;
- specialist-review triggers;
- independent certification receipts;
- rollback, compensation and readback requirements;
- public-safe trust evidence and restricted operating evidence.

## Twenty-eight foundational controls

Each of the fourteen service lanes begins with two mandatory controls. The catalog covers identity continuity, public/private identity separation, authentication assurance, credential/session lifecycle, least privilege, separation of duties, billing reconciliation, provider/economic-truth separation, license scope, revocation/renewal, least-data analytics, metric freshness, notification consent, delivery deduplication, CRM tenant boundaries, CRM lifecycle events, ticket evidence, escalation/SLA, search lineage, access-aware filtering, commerce-state separation, refund/dispute compensation, exact route contracts, failover/rollback/readback, rewards truth, reward reversal/expiry, documentation source/readback, and public/private retention.

## Readiness is not certification

A CaaS status such as PASS, HOLD, BLOCKED or READY_FOR_SPECIALIST_REVIEW is an institutional evidence state. It is not a legal conclusion or formal certification.

An automated score cannot convert:

- missing evidence into proof;
- provider marketing into verified capability;
- a technical canary into professional approval;
- one successful write into generalized mutation authority;
- a payment response into CrownThrive economic truth;
- a package candidate into a live commercial offer.

## Control lifecycle

`planned -> mapped -> evidence requested -> evidence received -> verifying -> verified | hold | blocked -> superseded | retired`

Each material control binds an owner and a different verifier. The control body and private evidence remain restricted; public-safe outputs expose stable IDs, version, objective, evidence classes, lifecycle state, reason classes and digests.

## Protected algorithms

CaaS uses four Vault-bound algorithms:

- CSS Service Readiness Score;
- CSS Control Coverage Score;
- CSS Provider Portability Score;
- CSS Route and Provider Selection Score.

Weights, thresholds and routing calibration remain restricted. Scores evaluate systems and evidence, never people, and cannot manufacture authority.

## Commercialization boundary

CaaS may become a licensed internal, managed, enterprise or customer-facing service only after rights, pricing, fulfillment, support, security, privacy, tax/accounting and professional-authority boundaries are accepted. Checkout and entitlements remain separately gated.
