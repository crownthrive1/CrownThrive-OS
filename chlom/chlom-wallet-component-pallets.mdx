# CHLOM WalletKit component pallets

**Catalog state:** `RESEARCH_CANDIDATE`  
**Effective offers:** none  
**Public prices:** none  
**Stripe Product/Price objects:** none  
**Checkout:** disabled

CHLOM WalletKit is organized as modular pallets rather than one monolithic wallet. Each pallet preserves a bounded capability, evidence contract, portability path, and independent release gate. The customer can receive a simple experience while CHLOM retains explicit separation among Money, Rights, Rewards, Impact, and Proof.

## Twelve component candidates

| Pallet | Strategic role | Current state |
| --- | --- | --- |
| CHLOM Wallet Core | Stable identity, policy bindings, and append-only lineage | Controlled test |
| Money Evidence Adapter | Stripe/provider verification and Money translation | Test mode only |
| Rights Wallet | Entitlements, terms, expiration, and revocation | Controlled test |
| Unified Value Receipt | Human-readable five-lane receipt and digest | Reference UI |
| Split & Obligation Ledger | Conserved allocations and obligation evidence | No settlement |
| ThriveFund Evidence Router | Impact obligation and external-settlement evidence | No disbursement |
| Proof Anchor | Hash/root preparation and proof state | No broadcast |
| Passkey Identity | WebAuthn registration, assertion, persistence, and recovery | No production credential |
| Smart-Account Preflight | ERC-4337/7579 source, codehash, and predeployment gates | HOLD predeployment |

## Candidate bundles

### Builder

Wallet Core, Unified Value Receipt, and SDK/API Pack for builders who need CHLOM semantics without a full enterprise control plane.

**State:** `PRICE_REVIEW`

### Commerce & Rights

Wallet Core, Money Evidence Adapter, Rights Wallet, Unified Value Receipt, and Split & Obligation Ledger for digital products, licensing, education, media, memberships, and creator platforms.

**State:** `PRICE_REVIEW`

### Impact & Proof

## Commercial moat

Generic wallet creation is replaceable infrastructure. CHLOM WalletKit’s defensible layer is the convergence of:

- stable CrownThrive identity above providers and chains;
- rights-aware payment reconciliation;
- Unified Value Receipts;
- versioned allocations and ThriveFund obligation evidence;
- proof anchoring without protected-data disclosure;
- passkey recovery and account-continuity contracts;
- provider portability and migration drills;
- SDK/API/MCP and component packaging;
- incident, rollback, and governed-release evidence.

This creates a high-quality walled garden through convergence, usability, trust, and institutional continuity rather than forced dependency on a proprietary coin or one external provider.

## Rights and standards boundary

CrownThrive’s proprietary layer is the CHLOM convergence architecture, algorithms, policy/evidence contracts, UX composition, product packaging, and institutional implementation developed for WalletKit, subject to current rights records.

WebAuthn, CAIP, ERC-4337, ERC-7579, Stripe, and other external standards or providers remain third-party dependencies. They are not relabeled as CrownThrive intellectual property. Distribution requires license review, attribution, and exact source/dependency provenance.

## Independent commercialization gates

4. legal, tax, and accounting scope;
5. fulfillment and support readiness;
6. tenant isolation and authorization;
7. quotas, overages, and abuse controls;
8. provider credential continuity;
9. package provenance and release evidence;
