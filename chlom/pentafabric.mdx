## CrownThrive Pentafabric™

CrownThrive Pentafabric™ is the five-layer architecture used to explain and engineer the convergent CrownThrive operating model without collapsing identity, rights, evidence, execution, and economics into one undifferentiated system.

CHLOM spans the five layers as a **metaprotocol**: it coordinates Rights, Rules, Roles, Revenue, Records, and Remedies across heterogeneous software, providers, databases, websites, agents, organizations, optional chains, and human authorities.

## Layer 1 — Identity & Imprint

Resolves **who or what exists and is acting**.

Core domains include:

- CrownThrive stable IDs and institutional identity;
- DIDs or equivalent decentralized identity references where justified;
- CHLOM Fingerprint public verification references;
- provenance, custody, source and version identity;
- authorship and ownership-evidence references;
- organization, service, agent, repository, asset and product identity;
- Cultural Imprint Engine context and imprint classifications.

Private derivation logic, keys, protected mappings and sensitive identity evidence remain vault-bounded.

## Layer 2 — Rights & Conditions

Resolves **what is allowed, required, restricted, delegated, licensed or remediable**.

Core domains include:

- Rights, Rules and Roles;
- Dynamic Licensing Assets (DLAs);
- Licensing Stewardship / Issuer Authority;
- contracts and conditions;
- eligibility, approvals, restrictions and revocation;
- territory, term and field of use;
- hybrid licensing models connecting human agreements, software controls and optional decentralized settlement;
- remedies and post-termination obligations.

A DLA is an evidence-bound licensing object. It does not manufacture ownership or replace chain of title.

## Layer 3 — Ledger & Assurance

Resolves **what happened, what evidence supports it, and whether institutional confidence is sufficient**.

Core domains include:

- DAIL — Decentralized Autonomous Information Ledger;
- evidence references, hashes, signatures and attestations;
- approvals, decisions, corrections and version history;
- compliance oracles and policy evaluators;
- independent verification and certification gates;
- disputes, holds, appeals and restoration;
- audit and reconciliation.

DAIL is the current canonical information-ledger identity. Historical DAL meanings remain lineage only.

## Layer 4 — Execution & Interoperability

Resolves **how governed intent becomes software, automation and usable surfaces**.

Core domains include:

- pallets, modules, containers and components;
- scaffolds, wireframes, portals, dashboards and widgets;
- frameworks, plugins, skills, algorithms, formulas and scripts;
- APIs, MCP servers/tools/resources, SDKs, webhooks and event contracts;
- agents, sub-agents, factories, builders, verifiers and schedulers;
- PentaDocs and website-mesh projection;
- cloud, database, provider and optional chain adapters.

Execution is bounded: builders create candidates and do not certify their own work.

## Layer 5 — Economy & Distribution

Resolves **how already-authorized capability becomes access, entitlement, product, revenue or distribution**.

Core domains include:

- PentaGreen and ECAC;
- exclusive CrownThrive SKU issuance through PentaGreen;
- pricing, subscriptions and usage access;
- entitlements, fulfillment, refunds and settlement;
- PentaCredits and approved payment rails;
- royalties, commissions, holds and reconciliation;
- developer, business, enterprise, institutional, partner, research and OEM licensing;
- affiliates, ambassadors, media, marketplaces and ecosystem distribution.

Economics are downstream of identity, rights, conditions and evidence.

## Five-layer flow

```text
Identity & Imprint
        ↓
Rights & Conditions
        ↓
Ledger & Assurance
        ↓
Execution & Interoperability
        ↓
Economy & Distribution
        ↺
DAIL receives outcomes, evidence, corrections and lifecycle state
```

## Core invariant

**Execution may optimize within authority. It may never manufacture authority.**

A model, agent, provider, repository, API, MCP tool, payment rail, chain or workflow may provide evidence or perform a bounded action. None can independently create ownership, licensing, certification, legal authority, entitlement or settlement truth.

## Relationship to CrownThrive OS, CIE and PentaGreen

- **CrownThrive OS** coordinates institutional state, platforms, PentaDocs, agents, providers and operational controls.
- **CIE** contributes cultural, narrative, lineage and imprint context, primarily to Layer 1 and governed policy interpretation.
- **CHLOM** provides the cross-layer metaprotocol for rights, licensing, evidence, authority and remedies.
- **DAIL** provides evidence-bearing record and correction lineage.
- **Factories and interfaces** materialize Layer 4 capabilities.
- **PentaGreen / ECAC** controls Layer 5 economic activation.
