CHLOM has evolved across whitepapers, technical compendia, prospectuses, Help Center eras and implementation plans. **Acronyms are versioned concepts, not timeless strings.** When the same acronym was reused, the older meaning remains attached to its source period and the current meaning is explicitly identified.

## DLA lineage

### Historical

**DLA = Decentralized Licensing Authority** in the early whitepaper, Genesis Volume, Technical Blueprint, Building & Understanding paper and Grand Prospectus.

Historical role: issue, verify, renew, suspend and revoke licenses; manage issuer authority; enforce prerequisites; integrate identity, AI/oracles and governance.

### Current

**DLA = Dynamic Licensing Asset** — the governed license object/permission package. The former authority function is represented separately as licensing stewardship / issuer authority.

## DAIL / DAL lineage

### Current DAIL

**DAIL = Decentralized Autonomous Information Ledger** — current event/evidence/audit lineage.

### Historical DAL meanings recovered

- **Decentralized Attestation Ledger** — Genesis Volume evidence/proof memory layer.
- **Decentralized Adjudication Layer** — Grand Prospectus dispute/arbitration layer.
- **Distributions Authority / Layer** — recovered later module/Help Center lineage.

No historical DAL is silently rewritten into DAIL; source-period meaning remains visible.

## ACE lineage

The strongest recovered expansion is **Adaptive Compliance Engine**, used in the Genesis Volume for deterministic + AI-assisted policy/risk enforcement. Later descriptive phrases such as automated compliance evaluator/engine remain source-specific unless explicitly adopted as a rename.

## AIE lineage

- **AI Intelligence Engine** — explicit Genesis Volume meaning; broad ML/risk/predictive intelligence feeding ACE.
- **Anomaly Intelligence Engine** — later CrownThrive reuse for anomaly/outlier monitoring.

Status: `founder_adjudication_available`.

## ADE lineage

- **Autonomous Decision Engine** — explicit Genesis Volume meaning; actuator for eligible policy/governance decisions.
- **Attribution & Distribution Engine** — later CrownThrive reuse for attribution, revenue splits and distribution/settlement.

Status: `founder_adjudication_available`.

## TLaaS lineage

The corpus contains two explicit expansions:

- **Tokenized Licensing as a Service** — Technical Blueprint / Building & Understanding lineage.
- **Trust Layer as a Service** — Grand Prospectus integration/API service lineage.

They are related but not identical. Both remain preserved until an ADR or founder adjudication establishes the current relationship.

## CHLOM Coin / CHM / CHLM

- **CHLOM Coin** — historical utility/network-fuel token for fees, licenses, marketplace, staking and incentives.
- **CHM** — historical governance/compliance token for voting, bonds and governance participation.
- **CHLM** — historical shorthand/native-token notation appearing in portions of the Grand Prospectus; not automatically a third live token.

Current token/coin/public-sale/DAO economics remain research/gated unless separately adopted and legally/technically activated.

## Fingerprint ID

Historical identity anchor joining actors, credentials, licenses, revenue, evidence, reputation and governance. Current stable institutional identity can preserve the function without requiring historical biometric/device fingerprinting assumptions.

## Compliance Pack

Historical modular policy bundle containing rules, applicability, versions, override requirements and enforcement logic. Current mapping: versioned policy/rule packages and compliance-as-code artifacts.

## Smart Treasury family

Historical terms include **Smart Treasury, Smart Tax, Smart Capital and Smart Yield**. Current economic implementation separates policy, provider evidence, tax/finance authority, allocation, settlement and money movement.

## Oracle Lattice / Oracle Hub

Historical **Oracle Lattice** describes multiple regulatory/identity/market/AI data providers cross-validating external reality. Modern **Oracle Hub / External Evidence Gateway** preserves source identity, freshness, disagreement, authority and remedy controls.

## Scribes / Cognitive Scribes

Recovered research family for expert/reviewer/verifier networks. A historical Scribe title does not automatically confer current institutional authority.

## Legacy governance terms

Historical sources include Master DAO, Sectoral DAO, Local/Sub-DAO, Council, Technical Committee, Treasury, regulator-observer roles, token-holder referenda and conviction voting. These are historical governance architecture, not automatic current corporate authority.

## Adjudication rule

Any acronym/system may be reclassified after publication through founder adjudication. Corrections preserve original expansion, source family, effective period, later expansion, current status, alias/predecessor/successor relationship, changelog and downstream propagation.

Recovery is not permission to erase terminology evolution.
