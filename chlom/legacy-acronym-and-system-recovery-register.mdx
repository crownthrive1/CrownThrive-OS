# CHLOM Legacy Acronym and System Recovery Register

This register preserves the acronym and system-name meanings recovered from CHLOM whitepapers, prospectuses, technical blueprints, Help Center-era material, founder adjudications, and later institutional build records.

The rule is simple: **acronyms are versioned concepts, not timeless strings**. When the same acronym was reused, the older meaning remains attached to its source period and the current meaning is explicitly identified.

## DLA lineage

### Historical

**DLA = Decentralized Licensing Authority** in the early whitepaper, Genesis Volume, Technical Blueprint, Building & Understanding paper, and Grand Prospectus.

Historical role: issue, verify, renew, suspend and revoke licenses; manage issuer authority; enforce prerequisites; integrate identity, AI/oracles and governance.

### Current

**DLA = Dynamic Licensing Asset**.

Current role: the governed license object/permission package. The former authority function is represented separately as licensing stewardship / issuer authority.

## DAIL / DAL lineage

### Current DAIL

**DAIL = Decentralized Autonomous Information Ledger**.

Current role: durable event/evidence/audit lineage. The implementation can be cloud/event-ledger first and later receive cryptographic anchoring without changing institutional meaning.

### Historical DAL meanings recovered

The corpus contains multiple incompatible DAL expansions:

- **Decentralized Attestation Ledger** — Genesis Volume evidence/proof memory layer.
- **Decentralized Adjudication Layer** — Grand Prospectus dispute/arbitration layer.
- **Distributions Authority / Layer** — recovered module/Help Center lineage referenced in the full component atlas.
- additional data/audit/distribution variants may exist in unrecovered Help Center bodies.

No historical DAL should be silently normalized into DAIL. The source-period meaning must be retained.

## AIE lineage

### Historical Genesis meaning

**AIE = AI Intelligence Engine**.

Role: machine-learning core feeding ACE with risk scoring, anomaly detection, predictive analysis, regulatory-text processing and model improvement.

### Later/current reuse

**AIE = Anomaly Intelligence Engine** appears in later CrownThrive documentation for anomaly/monitoring functions.

Status: `founder_adjudication_available`.

Both concepts remain distinct until the Founder chooses whether to retain the acronym reuse, rename one family, or designate predecessor/successor semantics.

## ADE lineage

### Historical Genesis meaning

**ADE = Autonomous Decision Engine**.

Role: actuator executing eligible policy/governance decisions, bounded enforcement, state changes, suspensions and automated actions.

### Later/current reuse

**ADE = Attribution & Distribution Engine**.

Role: attribution, split calculation, revenue routing and settlement/distribution logic.

Status: `founder_adjudication_available`.

## ACE lineage

The strongest recovered expansion is **Adaptive Compliance Engine** in the Genesis Volume. It is the policy/risk/compliance guardian integrating deterministic rules, AIE intelligence, pre-checks, anomaly handling, audits and reporting.

Some later prose uses variations such as automated compliance evaluator/engine. These should be preserved as source-specific descriptions rather than used to overwrite Adaptive Compliance Engine without a decision.

## TLaaS lineage

The corpus contains at least two direct expansions:

### Tokenized Licensing as a Service

Used in the Technical Blueprint and Building & Understanding sources for reusable issuance/management of tokenized license rights.

### Trust Layer as a Service

Used in the Grand Prospectus for the API/SDK/integration service that abstracts CHLOM complexity for external platforms.

These are related but not identical concepts. Current documentation keeps TLaaS as historical/research terminology until an ADR or founder adjudication establishes the canonical relationship.

## CHLOM Coin / CHM / CHLM

### CHLOM Coin

Historical utility/fuel token for fees, license payments, LEX activity, staking and incentives.

### CHM

Historical governance/compliance token for voting, governance bonds, issuer/oracle staking and governance incentives.

### CHLM

The Grand Prospectus uses **CHLM** in some sections as shorthand/native-token notation. It must not be automatically treated as a third live token. It is historical notation pending exact source-context classification.

Current state: token/coin/public-sale/DAO economics are research/gated unless separately adopted and legally/technically activated.

## Fingerprint ID

Historical CHLOM identity anchor joining actors, credentials, licenses, revenue, evidence, reputation and governance.

Current interpretation: stable institutional identity and future DID/VC/cryptographic adapters may implement the same function. A historical Fingerprint algorithm does not by itself prove current biometric/device fingerprinting or production identity infrastructure.

## Compliance Pack

Historical modular policy bundle containing rule sets, applicability, versions, override requirements and enforcement logic.

Current mapping: policy/rule packages, governance contracts and compliance-as-code artifacts.

## Smart Treasury / Smart Tax / Smart Capital / Smart Yield

Historical economic subsystem family covering automated fund routing, protocol fees, taxation concepts, reserves, grants, staking rewards, investments and yield strategies.

Current mapping: treasury/allocation/economic schedules remain separated from payment-provider evidence and require independent finance/tax/governance authority.

## Oracle Lattice / Oracle Hub

Historical **Oracle Lattice**: network of multiple external data/AI/regulatory oracles intended to cross-validate external truth.

Modern **Oracle Hub / External Evidence Gateway**: provider-independent evidence intake with source identity, freshness, authorization, disagreement and remedy controls.

## Scribes / Cognitive Scribes

Recovered research family for expert/reviewer/verifier networks supporting CHLOM analysis, attestations and review. Modern mapping is the reviewer/expert network; no historical Scribe title automatically confers institutional authority.

## CHLOM ML / AI/ML

Historical papers use CHLOM ML and AI/ML broadly for risk intelligence, fraud detection, scoring, predictive compliance and model governance. Current model records must identify provider/model/version/purpose/evaluation/authority rather than treating 'CHLOM AI' as one timeless model.

Licensable/brand-imprint lineage recovered from XENThrive-era materials; not the same as the retired ThriveCafe platform.

## Adjudication rule

Any acronym or system in this register may be reclassified after publication through founder adjudication. The correction must preserve:

- original expansion;
- source family;
- effective period;
- later expansion;
- current canonical status;
- alias/predecessor/successor relationship;
- changelog and downstream propagation.

Recovery is not a license to erase terminology evolution.
