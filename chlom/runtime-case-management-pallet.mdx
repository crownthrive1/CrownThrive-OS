## Historical purpose

The Case Management Pallet gave CHLOM a protocol-level object for matters that could not be resolved as an immediate binary transaction check: disputes, suspected violations, appeals, evidence conflicts, revocation reviews and other adjudication workflows.

```text
TRIGGER / COMPLAINT / CONFLICT
          |
          v
        CASE
          |
      EVIDENCE
          |
   REVIEW / HOLD
          |
      DISPOSITION
          |
   REMEDY / APPEAL
          |
       CLOSURE
```

## Case versus event

A case is a persistent process object. An event is evidence/state transition within the case.

```text
case opened
case evidence added
case hold activated
case disposition issued
case appealed
case closed
```

all remain in lineage.

## Evidence preservation

Evidence is append-only/correctable in lineage.

```text
original evidence
    |
correction / challenge
    |
review finding
```

A case must not delete inconvenient evidence to produce a clean narrative.

## Reviewer assignment

Reviewers require:

- identity;
- role/authority;
- scope/domain competence where required;
- conflict-of-interest handling;
- assignment effective period;
- evidence access appropriate to classification.

## HOLD relationship

A case can activate a bounded hold on an asset/license/account/action when current authority permits.

```text
case open
   !=
automatic system-wide suspension
```

Hold scope must be explicit.

## Appeal

Appeal creates a new review path linked to the prior decision.

```text
ORIGINAL DISPOSITION
        |
      APPEAL
        |
NEW REVIEW / EVIDENCE
        |
AFFIRM | MODIFY | REVERSE | REMAND
```

The original disposition remains in history.

## Due-process / authority boundary

CHLOM case workflows organize institutional decisions. They do not replace courts, regulators, arbitrators, licensed professionals or statutory procedures when those bodies have legal authority.

## Failure cases

### Provider suspends account before institutional review

Record provider action as evidence; reconcile its effect without treating provider decision as institution-wide adjudication.

### Reviewer conflict identified mid-case

Reassign/handle according to policy; preserve prior activity and conflict evidence.

### Case closed, new material evidence appears

Reopen/new linked case or correction path under policy; do not rewrite closed history.

### Appeal filed after remedy already executed

Policy determines hold/restoration/compensation; appeal does not automatically erase executed state.

## Security/privacy

Case records can contain sensitive legal, identity, financial, security and personnel information. Public pages expose only authorized dispositions/summaries when appropriate.

## Public / restricted split

Public-safe:

- case architecture;
- published precedents/decisions where approved;
- appeal/remedy semantics.

Restricted:

- evidence;
- parties' private data;
- deliberation;
- security/legal material;
- professional reviews;
- confidential settlement terms.

## Invariants

```text
case opened != guilt/violation
provider action != institutional adjudication
HOLD != final disposition
appeal != erasure
disposition != legal judgment beyond authority
case closed != evidence deleted
DAIL != adjudicator
historical case pallet != current chain deployment
```
