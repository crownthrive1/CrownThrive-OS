## Common institutional contract

Every service must support or integrate with:

- stable CrownThrive IDs and aliases;
- tenant/organization/actor context;
- role, relationship and delegated authority;
- purpose and data classification;
- version/effective-period resolution;
- source/evidence references;
- policy/rights/approval decision;
- request/correlation/causation IDs;
- idempotency and concurrency protection;
- standardized errors;
- DAIL event emission;
- correction/supersession/rollback references;
- observability and environment/version metadata.

## State and immutability rules

- accepted agreements, licenses, rights decisions and material evidence records are immutable;
- amendments and corrections create linked records;
- deletion is governed by retention/legal hold and does not erase required audit history;
- current state is computed from valid events/versions, not by overwriting historical truth;
- one service may not silently change another service's aggregate;
- cross-service state changes occur through APIs/events/workflows with reconciliation.

## Authorization contract

Authorization considers:

- actor/service identity and authentication assurance;
- tenant/organization;
- role and relationship to the resource;
- delegated scope and expiration;
- policy/rights/license/entitlement state;
- purpose, jurisdiction and environment;
- data classification;
- autonomy/action risk class;
- required approvals and separation of duties;
- holds, sanctions or incident state.

Possessing a valid token is necessary but not sufficient.

## Source and evidence contract

Material decisions store source/evidence references, authority tier, confidence/evidence level and review status. Restricted evidence is returned by reference or approved proof rather than broad raw payload. Conflicting or stale evidence creates a hold/decision queue.

## DAIL contract

Each material event records ID/type/schema, tenant, actor/service, subject, timestamp/environment, correlation/causation, state transition/versions, authority/approval, evidence references, classification/retention and integrity anchor/signature where applicable.

## Approval contract

Approval requests include action, resources, risk/autonomy class, source/rights/policy context, proposed mutation, alternatives, approvers, separation-of-duties rules, expiration, conditions and decision evidence.

## Rights and DLA contract

A DLA cannot grant more than the linked rights authority. Rights changes trigger license impact analysis. DLA/offer/license/entitlement/usage states are separate but reconciled. Active verification fails on expiry, revocation, suspension, unmet eligibility or unresolved authority.

## Rules and Legal Depot contract

Policies have IDs, versions, scope, effective dates, precedence, applicability, tests and required acceptance/approval. Historical terms remain resolvable for prior transactions. A current page does not prove acceptance.

## Economic contract

Prices, fees, commissions, royalties, splits, rewards, reserves, impact allocations and taxes/withholding classifications are versioned schedules. Calculations must identify inputs, rounding, currency, period, source events and reconciliation. Agents cannot mutate ownership or money destination to force a balance.

## Remedy contract

Material disputes and failures support case creation, evidence, hold, notice, review, decision, appeal, correction, revocation, refund/chargeback handling, reinstatement and final outcome. Remedies propagate to affected platforms and preserve prior events.

## Identity contract

External provider subjects map to stable CrownThrive actors through verified account links. CrownThrive ID records roles, organizations, consent, eligibility and assurance; provider accounts remain replaceable. Universal SSO is a target state, not a current blanket claim.

## API and MCP contract

- public, authenticated, developer, operator and restricted capabilities remain separated;
- tools state side effects, data class and autonomy/approval class;
- base URLs/environments/docs/auth/scopes are registered and verified;
- webhooks are signed, idempotent/replayable and monitored;
- SDKs/docs derive from authoritative contracts;
- private operations tools are not exposed as public developer tools;
- vendor drift and deprecation create controlled migration.

## Agent contract

The orchestrator retrieves current records, compiles run packets, invokes registered agents/tools, enforces authority, routes approvals, verifies outputs, executes through scoped APIs/MCPs, emits DAIL and updates the next-run baseline. Agents do not self-authorize A3/A4 actions.

## Service acceptance tests

- cross-tenant and unauthorized relationship access denied;
- stale/conflicting source held;
- expired/revoked DLA/entitlement rejected;
- duplicate request/webhook safe;
- optimistic concurrency conflict detected;
- correction preserves previous version;
- restricted evidence excluded from public result;
- approval expiration/condition enforced;
- revenue arithmetic/reconciliation passes;
- case hold propagates;
- event/audit record complete;
- backup/restore and rollback verified;
- model/agent/tool failure degrades safely;
- API/MCP schema/version/deprecation behavior validated.

## End-to-end definition of success

CrownThrive can:

1. register an organization and governed asset;
2. preserve chain-of-title evidence privately;
3. resolve roles, rights and applicable policies;
4. create an approved DLA/license offer;
5. verify partner/customer eligibility and acceptance;
6. receive payment or other required consideration through an approved rail;
7. issue a verifiable entitlement;
8. accept usage/fulfillment events;
9. calculate and reconcile economic obligations;
10. manage a dispute/correction/remedy;
11. expose safe API/MCP actions to connected platforms;
12. prove the full versioned DAIL history.

## Deferred adapters

Dedicated network, public-chain settlement, tokens, DAOs, bridges, staking, production ZK and autonomous legal/compliance authority remain gated adapters/research. The cloud service contract must work first and remain the canonical institutional meaning.
