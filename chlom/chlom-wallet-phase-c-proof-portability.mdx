# CHLOM Wallet Phase C — proof, settlement planning and portability

**State:** `CONTROLLED_TEST`

**Production activation:** false

Phase C turns three proprietary CHLOM algorithm families into executable, testable and machine-addressable wallet primitives:

1. **HARP v1** packages sanitized commitments into deterministic proof capsules.
2. **EASOR v1** compiles allocation and reversal obligation plans without settling them.
3. **Portable Wallet Capsule v1** preserves wallet identity and event-chain commitments above replaceable providers.

These primitives extend the existing CHLOM Wallet lanes—Money, Rights, Rewards, Impact and Proof—without collapsing their legal or economic meanings.

## Why these primitives exist

A closed CrownThrive wallet experience must feel simple without becoming technically or institutionally opaque. Phase C therefore separates three concerns that ordinary wallets commonly blur:

- **proof**: what can be verified without exposing the underlying protected evidence;
- **economic planning**: what amounts and obligations a governed schedule produces before any provider action;
- **continuity**: what remains CrownThrive-owned when a payment, chain or wallet provider changes.

The resulting lock-in is the quality of the CHLOM policy/evidence experience, not dependence on a single provider, token or blockchain.

## HARP v1 — Hash Anchor Root Packager

HARP consumes only stable identifiers, record classes, payload digests, optional policy digests, occurrence metadata and sanitized public metadata. It produces:

- domain-separated leaf commitments;
- a deterministic Merkle root;
- a deterministic manifest digest;
- record-level inclusion proofs;
- a candidate anchor packet with broadcast disabled.

HARP explicitly excludes raw evidence bodies, provider credentials, signing material and private records.

## EASOR v1 — Economic Allocation Schedule & Obligation Resolver

EASOR compiles a versioned basis-point schedule into exact minor-unit obligations using a deterministic largest-remainder algorithm. The compiler:

- requires the schedule to total exactly `10,000` basis points;
- sorts rules by stable leg code;
- uses remainder-descending, leg-code-ascending tie breaking;
- conserves the exact gross amount;
- compiles proportional reversal previews capped by the original leg amounts;
- keeps rights, rewards and ThriveFund/Impact obligations separate from Money.

EASOR outputs `PREVIEW_HOLD`. It does not call Stripe, a bank, a chain or another provider.

## Portable Wallet Capsule v1

The portable capsule exports CrownThrive-owned continuity above external infrastructure. It preserves:

- the stable CHLOM wallet ID;
- issuer DID reference;
- append-only event sequence and chain head;
- provider-neutral adapter aliases;
- entitlement commitments;
- proof-capsule references.

It excludes raw provider references, API credentials, webhook payload bodies, idempotency secrets and signing material. Import/remap output is always `HOLD` until separately reviewed.

- controlled provider dual-read/import drill;
- operational rollback and correction workflow;
- authenticated customer projection with least-privilege access;
- testnet/mainnet authority, signer and code-identity evidence for any future broadcast;
- separate pricing and live-commerce authorization.

Phase C is therefore an executable institutional substrate, not a production settlement or blockchain launch.
