## Purpose

P-06 is CHLOM's current capability container for **commercial objects and customer-facing economic workflows**: products, SKUs, offers, storefronts, orders, subscriptions, bookings, loyalty, rewards, payment evidence, fulfillment and entitlements.

It is not the authority to manufacture prices, rights or payouts. Current CrownThrive economic activation is governed through ThriveEvergreen where applicable, with CHLOM rights/policy/evidence controls surrounding execution.

```text
ASSET / SERVICE / CAPABILITY
          |
          v
RIGHTS + POLICY + COMMERCIAL OBJECT
          |
          v
SKU / OFFER / PRICE / BOOKING RULE
          |
          v
ECAC | HOLD | DENY
          |
          v
ORDER / BOOKING / SUBSCRIPTION
          |
          v
PAYMENT PROVIDER
          |
          v
FULFILLMENT / ENTITLEMENT
          |
          v
RECONCILIATION + DAIL
```

## Six Functions mapping

| Function | P-06 responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| Rights | Check that the commercial object can be sold/booked/redeemed and that resulting entitlement is valid |
| Rules | Offer, inventory, booking, subscription, refund, reward and fulfillment rules |
| Roles | Customer, merchant, operator, service provider, staff, partner, fulfiller |
| Revenue | Orders, bookings, payments, credits, fees and commercial activation evidence |
| Records | SKU, offer, order, booking, payment, loyalty, fulfillment and entitlement records |
| Remedies | Refund, cancellation, reschedule, reversal, credit correction, dispute and compensation |

## Source-component interpretation

### ThriveSeat

ThriveSeat contributes booking/service management: service listings, staff/resource calendars, appointments, check-in and booking state. P-06 governs shared booking semantics without claiming every provider capability is currently integrated.

### ThriveKiosk

ThriveKiosk contributes local/in-person discovery, check-in, commerce or activation surfaces where configured. Kiosk identity/place relationships can depend on P-09.

### Melanin Magic commerce

Melanin Magic contributes product catalog, wholesale/retail/professional commerce use cases. Product/provider catalogs are mapped into canonical commercial objects rather than treated as the CHLOM source of rights/economic truth.

### CrownRewards

CrownRewards contributes loyalty points, tiers, rewards, redemption, referrals and partner/member relationships. Reward balances and redemption rights must be evidence-backed and versioned.

### Payments

Payment processors are replaceable execution providers. A payment-provider success response is payment evidence—not the complete institutional order, entitlement, settlement or earnings state.

### Booking invariant

```text
PAYMENT != BOOKING CONFIRMATION
BOOKING CONFIRMATION != SERVICE COMPLETION
```

Both may be related through policy, but evidence must remain distinct.

## Capacity and inventory

P-06 must prevent overselling where a capacity constraint is authoritative.

```text
available = capacity - committed - governed_holds
```

The exact concurrency/locking algorithm can be provider-specific but the institutional result must be idempotent and reconciled.

## Provider independence

Commerce storefronts, booking systems, payment processors and loyalty software are adapters.

```text
CANONICAL ORDER
   |
   +--> Storefront adapter
   +--> Payment adapter
   +--> Booking adapter
   +--> Fulfillment adapter
   +--> Loyalty adapter
```

Provider replacement should not require new institutional identities for orders, SKUs or entitlements.

## Operating procedures

### Product/SKU onboarding

1. establish product/service asset;
2. verify rights/authority where required;
3. assign canonical product ID;
4. issue CrownThrive SKU;
5. define fulfillment type;
6. create governed offer/price candidate;
7. obtain ECAC/economic activation where applicable;
8. project to storefront/provider;
9. read back;
10. emit evidence.

### Booking flow

1. resolve service/resource/capacity;
2. resolve customer/provider identity;
3. calculate eligibility;
4. create hold/request;
5. execute payment if required;
6. confirm capacity and booking atomically/idempotently;
7. notify parties;
8. read back and record evidence.

### Loyalty earn

1. receive reconciled qualifying event;
2. resolve exact program/rule version;
3. compute candidate units;
4. run duplicate/fraud controls;
5. post immutable ledger entry;
6. update balance projection;
7. emit evidence.

### Refund

1. resolve original order/payment;
2. validate refund authority/policy;
3. execute provider action;
4. read back;
5. reconcile order, entitlement, loyalty and settlement;
6. emit correction/compensation evidence.

## Failure modes

### Duplicate payment webhook

**Response:** idempotency prevents duplicate institutional payment state.

### Booking provider confirms after capacity lost

**Response:** HOLD/reconcile; compensate rather than silently overbook.

### Payment captured, digital entitlement failed

**Response:** retry/repair entitlement within authority or refund/compensate; do not call order fulfilled.

### Reward rule changed after earning event

**Response:** evaluate historical earning under the effective rule version unless a separately adopted correction applies.

### Retired SKU reused by provider

**Response:** provider ID must map to new/current canonical SKU; retired CrownThrive SKU remains retired.

### Subscription provider active, institutional entitlement revoked

**Response:** resolve cause and reconcile; provider billing state does not override rights/policy.
