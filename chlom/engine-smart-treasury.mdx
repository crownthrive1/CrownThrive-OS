## Source basis

Historical CHLOM describes a Smart Treasury that can route fees/royalties, enforce budget/reserve policy, produce audit trails and—in later research—rebalance or allocate idle funds. Genesis includes treasury rebalancing pseudocode with example portfolio targets and tolerance bands. The Help Center separately names Treasury Pallet, Treasury Routing Pallet, Smart Treasury Engine, Smart DeFi, Smart Capital and Smart Yield.

Those components remain separate. This article is the **treasury orchestration/control engine**.

## Non-purpose

The Smart Treasury Engine does not:

- manufacture spending authority;
- authorize investment/yield merely because an algorithm recommends it;
- make historical crypto strategies current;
- replace accounting/tax/legal/professional review;
- treat provider balance as canonical book state;
- self-change its own risk/reserve policy;
- imply current autonomous money movement.

## Input state

## Historical rebalancing lineage

Genesis includes a research example with buckets such as stable, yield and volatile positions and tolerance-band rebalancing. That pseudocode is preserved as historical algorithm lineage.

The current-safe abstraction is:

```text
target_i = adopted target for bucket i
deviation_i = current_i - target_i
```

If an adopted policy defines tolerance `τ_i`, a candidate rebalance can be considered when:

```text
|deviation_i| > τ_i
```

But the actual target/tolerance/strategy and action remain governed private parameters, not public defaults.

## Reserve logic

Before any optional allocation:

```text
available_after_reserves
= canonical_book_balance
- mandatory_reserves
- committed_obligations
- other_locked_amounts
```

No allocation may use value reserved for an obligation outside adopted authority.

## Routing

Treasury Routing and Fee Router components can generate deterministic destinations after an economic schedule is authorized. Smart Treasury may coordinate those components but must preserve separate calculation and settlement receipts.

## AI/ML relationship

Historical sources contemplated AI-triggered treasury suggestions. Modern rule:

```text
AI/ML recommendation != money-movement authority
```

AI can surface risk, forecast liquidity, suggest rebalancing or detect anomalies. Execution remains bounded by adopted economic authority, approvals and provider readback.

## Strategy allowlist

Any external investment/yield strategy must have an explicit profile:

- provider/strategy identity;
- legal/economic review state;
- allowed assets/currencies;
- exposure limits;
- liquidity/unwind conditions;
- security/provider risk;
- accounting/tax treatment;
- monitoring/readback;
- status/expiry.

Historical Smart DeFi/Yield concepts are not implicitly allowlisted.

## Failure modes

- book/provider mismatch -\> hold/reconcile;
- reserve data stale -\> HOLD;
- economic authority missing -\> no execution;
- provider unavailable -\> hold/retry;
- provider confirms wrong amount -\> unresolved correction;
- risk/FX source stale -\> hold strategy-dependent action;
- strategy becomes unapproved -\> block new actions, follow unwind/hold plan;
- model recommends out-of-policy action -\> reject;
- duplicate execution -\> idempotency prevents second movement.

## Threat model

- treasury key/account compromise;
- provider alias substitution;
- manipulated price/FX/oracle;
- hidden reserve reduction;
- AI/algorithm authority bypass;
- double payment/investment;
- strategy rug/provider failure;
- accounting mismatch;
- stale balance;
- unauthorized economic schedule.

## Test vectors

1. recommendation within policy \+ required approval -\> executable;
2. recommendation without authority -\> no execution;
3. reserve breach -\> hold/deny;
4. stale FX -\> hold;
5. provider balance mismatch -\> reconcile before new optimization;
6. duplicate instruction -\> no duplicate side effect;
7. strategy removed from allowlist -\> new allocation blocked;
8. provider success but reconciliation mismatch -\> not settled;
9. historical yield/crypto pseudocode -\> not active production rule;
10. correction -\> append correction, no erasure.
