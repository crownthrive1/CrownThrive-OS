## Institutional custody

The factory uses a strict private/public split:

- **THIVEBASE** holds stable identities, classifications, lifecycle states, authority ceilings, public-safe digests, validation receipts, work queues, commercialization state, and custody evidence.
- **Supabase Vault** holds separate recovery encryption material for each generation. Raw secret return is prohibited.
- **Encrypted Google Drive custody** holds each private generation package. Upload and exact-byte ciphertext readback are required before custody may be marked verified.
- **DAIL** records generation completion, fleet expansion, custody, and agent-fleet activation through restricted digest-only events.
- **Mintlify and GitHub** receive only contract-level, count-level, state-level, and governance-level projections.

All four generation packages have verified encrypted Drive custody. Internal monthly trade-secret archives remain a separate continuity series and must not be conflated with package-level Drive verification.
