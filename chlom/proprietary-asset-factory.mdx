## Runtime and authority boundary

The 5,000 algorithms are not unrestricted production algorithms. They remain disabled pending independent verification and governed allowlisting. Each future runtime cohort must require:

1. authenticated and authorized execution;
2. versioned input validation and evidence fingerprinting;
3. source-authority, recency, contradiction, rights, security, privacy, and budget checks;
4. protected-parameter execution without body exposure;
5. sanitized output with uncertainty, reason codes, and rollback references;
6. DAIL receipt emission;
7. negative authorization and disclosure testing; and
8. independent certification before activation.

No score, algorithm, prompt, script, agent, repository branch, tag, DAIL event, or documentation page creates sovereign authority. **D3 decisions, money movement, final legal or rights decisions, checkout activation, and human signatures remain human-reserved.**
