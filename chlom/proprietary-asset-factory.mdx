## CHLOM Proprietary Asset Factory

The **CHLOM Proprietary Asset Factory** is CrownThrive's governed system for creating, classifying, testing, pinning, encrypting, preserving, recovering, packaging, and preparing reusable institutional intelligence for controlled commercialization.

Founder authority from **Kavonte Jones Sr. on August 22, 2026** authorizes candidate-only expansion under ThriveBase, DAIL, Supabase Vault, encrypted Google Drive custody, independent verification, and human-reserved D3 controls. A later bounded founder directive authorized the first two exact-scope professional-service offers without opening the protected asset estate or production algorithm runtime.

<Warning>
  This is a public-safe projection. It does not expose protected formulas, prompt bodies, executable script bodies, secret material, private identity mappings, recovery material, or restricted implementation evidence.
</Warning>

The active policy is `ct.policy.chlom-proprietary-factory-100k-plus`. The next candidate range is **G49-G60**.
