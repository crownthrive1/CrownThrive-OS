## Institutional custody

The corpus uses a private/public split:

- **THIVEBASE** holds stable identities, classifications, lifecycle states, authority ceilings, public-safe digests, DAIL receipts, commercialization state, and custody evidence.
- **Supabase Vault** holds the recovery encryption material. Raw secret return is prohibited.
- **Encrypted Google Drive custody** holds the private package. Upload and exact-byte readback were independently verified.
- **DAIL** records package registration and external-custody verification using restricted digest-only evidence.
- **Mintlify and GitHub** receive only this contract-level public projection.

A later internal August archive revision records the expanded trade-secret estate and preserves predecessor-chain continuity. External custody claims require actual encrypted upload and exact readback; an internal archive record alone is not sufficient.

## Runtime and authority boundary

The new 1,000 algorithms are **not unrestricted production algorithms**. They are registered as disabled pending independent verification. Each contract requires:

1. authenticated and authorized execution;
2. versioned input validation and evidence fingerprinting;
3. source-authority, recency, contradiction, rights, security, and budget checks;
4. protected-parameter execution without body exposure;
5. sanitized result, uncertainty, reason codes, and rollback references;
6. DAIL receipt emission; and
7. independent verification before any PASS or activation.

No score, algorithm, prompt, script, agent, tag, or DAIL event creates sovereign authority. **D3 decisions, money movement, final legal/rights decisions, checkout activation, and human signatures remain human-reserved.**

This validates the reusable execution contract. It does not certify every algorithm for production use.
