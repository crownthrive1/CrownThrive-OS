# License Lifecycle and Remedies

CHLOM treats licensing as a governed lifecycle rather than a one-time file or payment.

## Representative lifecycle

```text
Draft
→ Review
→ Approved
→ Offered
→ Accepted
→ Paid / Effective
→ Active
→ Amended / Renewed
→ Expired / Completed
```

A license may also enter exceptional states:

```text
Hold
Suspended
Disputed
Revoked
Refunded
Terminated
Corrected
Appealed
Restored
```

## Approval before activation

A DLA should not become active merely because a checkout succeeds. Activation may require different combinations of:

- rights approval
- identity or authority validation
- agreement acceptance
- payment confirmation
- policy validation
- compliance review
- delivery or entitlement readiness

The exact gate depends on the product and license class.

## Remedies

CHLOM remedies are designed to correct the governed state without erasing history.

### Hold

Pauses further action while facts or rights are reviewed.

### Suspension

Temporarily disables an active right or entitlement subject to defined conditions.

### Revocation

Ends a right or permission according to the governing agreement or policy.

### Correction

Fixes an attribution, metadata, scope, identity, status, or record while preserving the prior state and correction event.

### Refund or economic reversal

Reconciles payment without pretending the original payment event never occurred.

### Appeal

Creates a reviewable challenge to a decision with a defined authority and outcome record.

### Restoration

Reactivates an eligible state after the remedy condition is resolved.

## Downstream reconciliation

A remedy is incomplete until affected systems are reconciled.

Examples include:

- product listing
- entitlement
- GSO rights listing
- Stripe state
- public page
- distribution catalog
- partner delivery
- royalty record
- search index
- evidence record

CHLOM therefore treats remedies as workflows, not just status labels.
