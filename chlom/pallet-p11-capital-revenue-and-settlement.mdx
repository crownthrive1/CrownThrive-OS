## Purpose

P-11 is CHLOM's current capability container for **economic calculation, obligations, allocations, commissions, royalties, authorized provider execution, reversals/clawbacks, reconciliation, settlement state and financial reporting**.

It enforces the distinction between a commercial event and final institutional economic truth.

## Source-component interpretation

### ThriveFund

ThriveFund contributes governed fund/grant/capital-allocation concepts. Current institutional documentation must not infer nonprofit status, investment-product status, securities treatment or guaranteed returns from the name or historical source material. Every live capital action requires its own authority/legal/economic basis.

### Fee routing

Fee routing converts an authorized fee schedule into explicit obligations/allocations. It never creates a fee merely because a historical paper proposed one.

### Commissions

Commission obligations derive from current agreements/program rules and verified qualifying events.

### Royalties

Royalty obligations derive from verified rights, agreements and current schedule versions. Historical rates are lineage, not active schedules.

### Clawbacks

Clawbacks/reversals are explicit corrective economic events; they do not erase the original obligation or provider transaction.

### Finance reporting

Reporting is a projection of reconciled economic records with defined cutoffs/as-of dates. Provider dashboards and bank/payment processor reports are evidence sources, not the only canonical ledger.

## Schedule model

An economic schedule is a versioned rule set.

```yaml
schedule_id: stable ID

source_authority_ref: required

```

Rates/percentages live in the exact authorized schedule—not in generic pallet prose.

## Deterministic allocation

For basis-point schedules, a public-safe reference model is:

```text
allocation_i = floor(B * bps_i / 10,000)
```

where `B` is the governed basis amount in minor units. Remainder handling must be deterministic and versioned.

This formula is a calculation pattern; it does not define any current percentage.

## Conservation invariant

For a closed allocation plan:

```text
sum(allocations) + remainder = governed_basis
```

If fees/taxes/reserves are outside the basis, the schedule must define that explicitly. P-11 must not silently mix gross and net bases.

## Gross/net waterfall

```text
GROSS QUALIFYING AMOUNT
          |
          +--> authorized adjustments / refunds
          |
          v
GOVERNED BASIS
          |
          +--> fee obligations
          +--> royalty obligations
          +--> commission obligations
          +--> reserve / other authorized allocations
          |
          v
NET / RESIDUAL according to schedule
```

Each step needs a versioned basis definition.

## Settlement model

Settlement means institutional reconciliation determined that the obligation and execution evidence satisfy the applicable contract/policy state.

```yaml
settlement_id: stable ID
obligation_ref: required

```

## Reconciliation equation

A basic variance model is:

```text
variance = expected_obligation - reconciled_provider_amount
```

Interpretation requires sign convention, currency and timing definitions. `variance = 0` does not prove the underlying rights/policy were valid; it proves only amount agreement under the reconciliation scope.

## Commission model

Commission calculation requires:

- current program/agreement;
- qualifying event definition;
- attribution rule/version;
- eligible participant;
- governed basis;
- current commission schedule;
- reversal/refund rules;
- settlement evidence.

```text
referral click != qualifying conversion
qualifying conversion != final commission
final commission != provider payout
provider payout != settled commission until reconciled
```

       |

## Operating procedures

### Schedule adoption

1. identify source authority/agreement;
2. define basis, units, rates/rules, rounding;
3. define effective period;
4. create test vectors;
5. legal/economic review where required;
6. adopt exact version;
7. publish public-safe semantics if allowed;
8. store restricted commercial details appropriately;
9. emit DAIL event.

### Obligation generation

1. receive qualifying event;
2. resolve rights/agreement/attribution;
3. select exact schedule version;
4. compute deterministic candidate;
5. validate conservation/limits;
6. create obligation(s);
7. route approval/execution.

### Provider payout/execution

1. resolve authorized obligation;
2. bind idempotency key;
3. execute through certified provider;
4. record provider receipt;
5. read back status;
6. reconcile amount/currency/counterparty/time;
7. mark settled/partial/held;
8. emit evidence.

### Reversal/clawback

1. preserve original obligation/settlement;
2. capture reversal trigger;
3. evaluate current/historical agreement rule;
4. calculate corrective amount;
5. authorize remedy;
6. execute or offset;
7. reconcile;
8. update reporting and DAIL.

### Period close/report

1. freeze `as_of` boundary;
2. collect relevant reconciled records;
3. identify pending/unreconciled items;
4. apply correction versions;
5. generate statements/projections;
6. approve/public-project appropriate figures;
7. preserve reproducible report inputs.

## Failure modes

### Provider payout succeeds twice

**Response:** idempotency and obligation reconciliation identify duplicate execution; create corrective action without deleting original receipts.

### Rights revoked after sale but before payout

**Response:** policy/agreement determines hold/remedy; do not automatically pay solely because provider captured funds.

### Commission attribution changes after settlement

**Response:** historical settlement remains tied to original version unless a governed correction/reversal applies.

### Currency mismatch

**Response:** HOLD reconciliation until governed FX/source and currency identities are resolved.

### Partial provider data

**Response:** report as partial/unreconciled through exact `as_of`; do not claim definitive total.

### Historical royalty percentage found in old paper

**Response:** preserve as historical source rate; do not activate.

### Tax rule unresolved

**Response:** HOLD tax-sensitive automation and route professional/legal review; do not guess.
