# Wireframe: Evidence, Audit & Case Review Console

## Wireframe identity

```yaml
wireframe_id: WIRE-ASSURE-001
source_family: recovered evidence/audit/case review surface family
classification: source_grounded_functional_reconstruction
version: 1.0.0
```

## Purpose

This console is the canonical assurance workspace for reviewing evidence packages, continuous audits, compliance cases, disputes and appeals. It composes evidence, reviewer, control and remedy state without treating case creation or algorithmic severity as a final adjudication.

## Work queue

Display assigned audits/cases, priority/severity, SLA/deadline, subject/object, evidence completeness, current stage, active HOLDs and assigned reviewers. Queue eligibility follows reviewer assignment/conflict rules.

## Evidence pane

Compose Evidence Checklist, Evidence Reference Panel, source applicability/currentness, attestation verification, correction history and protected evidence access. Evidence bodies remain in custody; the console uses stable references and role-scoped views.

## Control/audit pane

List applicable controls, individual PASS/HOLD/DENY results, required evidence, evaluator version, finding severity, remediation tasks and retest state. Coverage percentage cannot hide a failed hard control.

## Case pane

Show Case Summary, parties/subject, issue statement, procedural stage, notices, evidence deadlines, assigned reviewers and active safeguards. Opening a case is procedural and not a finding of fault.

## Reviewer pane

Show reviewer identity/credential, independence/conflict status, assignment, completed review, quorum state and recommendation/decision authority. Recusal is explicit and preserved in DAIL.

## Findings and remediation

Findings link to control/evidence, severity, owner, due date and Compliance Tasks. Completing remediation routes the affected controls for re-evaluation rather than directly clearing the case.

## Appeal/review history

Compose Review/Appeal Timeline, earlier decisions, appeal basis, new evidence and current effective outcome. Prior decisions remain visible according to access policy.

## Remedy panel

Authorized remedies—correction, reinstatement, suspension, refund/compensation, license amendment or other case-specific actions—are shown separately from decision and execution state.

## Layout

```text
QUEUE / FILTERS
CASE OR AUDIT HEADER
EVIDENCE             CONTROLS / FINDINGS
REVIEWERS             REMEDIATION TASKS
APPEAL HISTORY        REMEDY / EXECUTION
DAIL TIMELINE         ACCESS AUDIT
```

## Data dependencies

Cases, audit scheduler, policy engine, evidence custody, DAIL, reviewer network, identity/credentials, remedies, economic/DLA services and authority gateway.

## Accessibility/security

Evidence access is least-privilege and auditable. Tables/graphs have text equivalents. Restricted evidence and personal data cannot leak through search snippets, previews or client caches.

## Tests

Test insufficient evidence, conflicted reviewer, hard DENY, stale evidence, appeal, reopened case, restricted evidence, remedy execution failure and cross-tenant isolation.

## Versioning

Case/audit/control/reviewer schemas remain independently versioned; this wireframe composes exact versions rather than becoming a parallel source of truth.
