## Source basis

The recovered Help Center describes the **Compliance Case Engine** as an investigation lifecycle of `open → investigate → appeal → resolve` with evidence archives, SLAs and reversible actions. It is distinct from the historical Case Management Pallet, which stores case records/states, and DREP/DAP, which focus on disputes/arbitration.

## Purpose

The engine orchestrates compliance/institutional cases that require more than a single synchronous decision.


## Case classes

Public-safe classes can include:

- compliance review;
- rights/ownership conflict;
- credential/identity review;
- license/DLA suspension or renewal issue;
- provider mismatch;
- audit finding;
- economic reconciliation dispute;
- security/abuse incident;
- governance/process exception;
- data/provenance correction.

A case class determines its authority, evidence, SLA and remedy profile.

## Triage

Triage determines:

- whether a case should exist;
- duplicate/correlation handling;
- severity/priority;
- appropriate owner/reviewer;
- initial holds needed for safety;
- evidence preservation requirements;
- external professional/legal/security routing.

Triage must not convert allegations into findings.

## Evidence model

All substantive findings should reference evidence objects rather than copied narrative alone.


## Assignments and conflicts

Assignment requires current role/delegation and conflict checks. High-impact cases may require multiple reviewers or a professional lane.

```text
case owner != final decision authority automatically
review assignment != authority expansion
```

## Temporary/reversible actions

The engine can orchestrate temporary safeguards when pre-authorized:

- HOLD a transaction/listing/license;
- pause a capability;
- require re-verification;
- prevent provider retries;
- preserve disputed funds/state where authority exists.

Temporary action must have scope, reason, expiry/review, rollback/restoration path and DAIL evidence.

## Decision/remedy separation

```text
finding != remedy
remedy candidate != authorized remedy
```

The engine routes a remedy to the correct authority/executor.

## Appeals

Appeal creates a new review stage with preserved prior decision and evidence lineage. Appeal grounds, deadline and reviewer independence follow the adopted profile.

## Provider evidence

A provider flag, suspension, chargeback, content removal or account state may open a case but is not automatically the institutional finding.

## Automation and AI

AI can:

- cluster related evidence;
- summarize timelines;
- flag missing documents;
- suggest case class/priority;
- identify policy references;
- surface contradictions.

AI must not make reserved D3/professional/legal conclusions without the required human/professional gate.

## Failure modes

- duplicate case -\> correlate/merge reference without deleting originals;
- wrong authority -\> hold/reassign;
- evidence inaccessible/corrupt -\> HOLD and preservation response;
- reviewer conflict -\> recuse/reassign;
- SLA missed -\> escalate operationally;
- provider state changes during case -\> record new evidence, don't rewrite history;
- remedy fails at provider -\> case remains unresolved/remedy-pending;
- appeal deadline ambiguity -\> HOLD/adjudicate rather than silently deny.

## Threat model

- retaliatory/fraudulent case creation;
- evidence deletion/tampering;
- biased reviewer;
- secret adverse action;
- case-status manipulation;
- leaking restricted case data;
- SLA gaming;
- AI hallucination promoted as finding;
- provider result substituted for investigation;
- closing case before remedy readback.

## Test vectors

1. valid report -\> case created with source linkage;
2. duplicate signal -\> correlated, no duplicate irreversible action;
3. allegation only -\> no finding yet;
4. missing critical evidence -\> HOLD;
5. conflicted reviewer -\> recusal;
6. temporary hold expires without extension -\> review/release according to policy;
7. finding \+ unauthorized remedy -\> remedy not executed;
8. appeal -\> old decision retained, new review path;
9. provider remedy fails -\> case remains open/pending;
10. restricted case -\> public projection reveals only permitted status.
