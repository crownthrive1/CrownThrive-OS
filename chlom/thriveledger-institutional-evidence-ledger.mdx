# ThriveLedger™ Institutional Evidence Ledger

ThriveLedger™ is CrownThrive's internal institutional evidence-ledger subsystem inside ThriveBase. It is designed to become a high-integrity evidence substrate for later CHLOM and ThriveEvergreen reconciliation without pretending that a provider response, bank feed, payment event or wallet event is economic truth by itself.

> **Constitutional rule:** ThriveLedger records and reconciles evidence; it never manufactures economic truth.

## What ThriveLedger does

ThriveLedger provides six governed capabilities:

1. sanitized status;
2. digest-first evidence ingestion;
3. evidence-backed balance observation;
4. deterministic reconciliation;
5. independent integrity audit;
6. digest-only CHLOM evidence export.

Its accounting core uses double-entry journal structures, explicit evidence links, legal-entity boundaries, append-only evidence chains, reversal-based corrections and signed/hash-linked assertion receipts.

## Source model

The v1 source registry separates evidence origin from institutional truth. Current source classes include:

- CrownThrive-only business-finance evidence;
- CrownThrive Stripe read evidence;
- verified credit-commerce Stripe webhook receipts;
- CHLOM Wallet provider receipts;
- CHLOM Wallet cryptographic ledger events;
- CrownThrive internal-commerce evidence;
- a disabled government-authoritative adapter class reserved for later exact-endpoint certification.

The government source class is deliberately fail-closed. ThriveLedger does not infer tax treatment, filing status, legal sufficiency or regulatory conclusions merely because a government endpoint may exist.

## Real-time and near-real-time evidence flow

Verified CHLOM Wallet provider receipts, CHLOM ledger events and terminal verified CrownThrive credit-commerce webhook receipts are mirrored into ThriveLedger through internal event triggers. External financial-account and Stripe account-state observations use bounded read-only evidence refreshes.

Provider event references are hashed before ThriveLedger stores them where possible. Raw credentials, routing information, bank account numbers and private provider account identifiers are prohibited from the ledger's institutional evidence payloads.

## Legal-entity separation

ThriveLedger v1 seeds only CrownThrive LLC's accounting entity. Connected evidence belonging to an individual or a different legal entity is not posted into CrownThrive books merely because a connector can see it.

Cross-entity journal lines are denied by database control. A related-party or intercompany event requires explicit evidence and a governed classification before it can enter CrownThrive accounting state.

## Evidence versus accounting truth

An evidence event can be valid without being journal-ready. ThriveLedger therefore separates:

- **evidence observed** — a source emitted or returned something;
- **evidence verified** — source integrity/authentication checks passed;
- **mapping certified** — an approved accounting rule identifies the accounts and treatment;
- **journal posted** — a balanced entry exists and is linked to eligible evidence;
- **reconciliation PASS** — expected evidence and accounting relationships reconcile;
- **institutional export** — only bounded digests/receipts are eligible to inform CHLOM.

Ambiguous evidence enters HOLD rather than being auto-posted to make the books appear complete.

## Vault and IP boundary

ThriveLedger's custody anchor was generated inside PostgreSQL Vault. Documentation and registries store only its governed alias/reference; secret material is not placed in source history or documentation.

Protected implementations include the reconciliation algorithm, audit algorithm, entity-boundary algorithm, schema, evidence-ingest skill, CHLOM-export skill and private MCP contract. Public documentation may describe their contracts and safeguards but not their protected bodies.

Drive archive custody for the protected ThriveLedger package remains **pending**. A Vault-bound asset does not become Drive-certified merely because its registry says Drive custody is required.

## CHLOM relationship

ThriveLedger is an internal precursor/evidence substrate, not a replacement for CHLOM. Its export surface returns assertion receipts, source-chain heads and integrity digests without raw financial bodies or credentials.

CHLOM and ThriveEvergreen may later consume those receipts as evidence. They must still apply their own rights, authority, commerce, settlement, remedy and governance rules. A ThriveLedger PASS proves the stated ledger controls passed for the stated scope; it does not independently create payment, earning, payout, entitlement, tax, legal or licensing truth.
