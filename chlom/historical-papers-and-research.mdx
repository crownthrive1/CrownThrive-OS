# Historical Papers and Research

CHLOM’s institutional history includes multiple generations of technical, economic, governance, and launch design. Phase 2.5 preserves those sources as part of the knowledge base while labeling their authority correctly.

## Principal retained source families

### CHLOM Master / institutional compendia

Purpose: consolidate terminology, architecture, history, operating model, recovered Help Center content, and current reconstruction decisions.

Current use: high-value source for Phase 2 reconstruction, subject to current ADRs and verified production evidence.

### White Paper

Purpose: describe CHLOM as a decentralized licensing and compliance MetaProtocol, including TLaaS, tokenized licensing, identity, AI compliance, LEX, governance, treasury, and zero-knowledge concepts.

Current use: historical architecture and strategic target specification.

### BlackPaper

Purpose: deeper design and implementation reasoning around the protocol and its operating assumptions.

Current use: historical technical source and target architecture.

### Internal Gold Paper

Purpose: deeper internal design for identity, license lifecycle, splitters, override trees, treasury, governance, verifiers, oracles, bridges, indexing, proofs, and formal invariants.

Current use: target architecture and restricted-design reference. Public documentation must not expose private Fingerprint implementation or protected internals.

### Genesis Volume

Purpose: long-range dedicated-network and protocol-engine vision.

Current use: historical strategic and technical roadmap.

### Technical White Paper and Extended Appendices

Purpose: define component architecture, proving, gas/economic assumptions, threat models, cross-chain ideas, test vectors, settlement, formal verification, and operations.

Current use: detailed target implementation specification and future engineering reference.

### LEX concept materials

Purpose: define a licensing and brand exchange for programmable IP, verified ownership, automated licensing, royalties, and governed transactions.

Current use: product and market architecture for the future LEX layer.

### Dual-token / launch / testbed materials

Purpose: explore token models and staged deployment before a dedicated CHLOM network.

Current use: historical economic and experimentation record. No token model is canonical for production unless a new ADR explicitly approves it.

### Formal models / TLA\+ research

Purpose: express invariants and state-machine properties for later verification.

Current use: engineering research and future assurance work.

## Phase 2.95 paper-family recovery

The recovered Help Center explicitly confirms CHLOM **White Papers, Blackpapers, Gold Papers, Blue Papers, Red Papers, and Green Papers**, plus a separate Thrive Flywheel paper family containing Whitepapers, Blackpapers, Green Papers, Blue Papers and Red Papers.

The Founder additionally directed recovery of **Purple, Silver, Orange and Yellow Papers**. Dedicated Drive/source searches during Phase 2.95 did not surface authoritative paper files or legacy Help Center categories using those exact names. They are now reserved as `recovery-target` families in the Paper Suite and IP Library rather than being fabricated.

The Phase 2.95 Paper Suite page also records extensive module-specific whitepapers, sovereign-industry licensing papers, developer papers, LEX/DLA/TLaaS/DAL research, Fingerprint/DID work, formal proof material and internal Gold Paper variants.

## Preservation rule

Do not delete a historical paper because the current architecture differs.

Instead record:

- source date
- original purpose
- original terminology
- current authority class
- superseded concepts
- still-valid concepts
- unresolved decisions

## Public claim rule

A historical design is evidence that CrownThrive designed or considered a capability. It is not sufficient evidence that the capability is deployed, audited, legally approved, or commercially available.
