# Recovered CHLOM Paper and Technical Document Title Census

The lost Help Center preserves a large **title-level CHLOM paper and technical-document corpus**. This census makes those exact recovered titles durable even when the original article body or source file has not yet been recovered.

## Counting rule

```text
TITLE RECOVERED
      !=
BODY RECOVERED
      !=
SOURCE VERIFIED
      !=
CURRENT AUTHORITY
      !=
IMPLEMENTATION COMPLETE
```

Every title below is a **reconstruction target or recovered source record**, not automatic proof that its historical claims are current.

## Status codes

```yaml
BODY_RECOVERED: substantive source body located
PARTIAL_SOURCE: meaningful source fragments/related body located
TITLE_RECOVERED: exact Help Center title recovered; body not yet established
RECONSTRUCTED: current source-grounded article has been authored
RESTRICTED_SOURCE: body located but protected/internal
SOURCE_NOT_LOCATED: reserved target without source proof
```
