## Purpose

P-02 is the current CHLOM capability container that turns institutional rules into **versioned, explainable, reviewable decisions**. It binds governance authority, policy applicability, eligibility, approvals, compliance evidence, audit, holds, denials, corrections and escalation without allowing automation to manufacture authority.

```text
SOURCE AUTHORITY
      |
      v
POLICY / GOVERNANCE RECORD
      |
      v
APPLICABILITY + ELIGIBILITY
      |
      v
EVIDENCE SUFFICIENCY
      |
      v
DECISION
 PASS | HOLD | DENY
      |
      v
ACTION / ESCALATION
      |
      v
DAIL + AUDIT + CORRECTION
```

## Six Functions mapping

| Function | P-02 responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| Rights | Prevent actions that lack required rights or eligibility |
| Rules | Canonical policy, rule, approval and governance execution |
| Roles | Resolve decision makers, reviewers, councils and delegated approvers |
| Revenue | Gate economic activation against policy and authority |
| Records | Policies, decisions, approvals, audits, exceptions, holds and corrections |
| Remedies | Review, appeal, override, compensation, suspension and restoration paths |

## Source-component interpretation

### CHLOM Core

CHLOM Core is the shared governance/compliance operating spine represented inside P-02. It includes the logic required to apply CHLOM's Six Functions consistently across pallets without forcing each platform to reimplement policy, authority and evidence behavior independently.

### CIE

The Cultural Imprint Engine is included where cultural-imprint decisions, brand/corridor rules, partner/operator boundaries and imprint-specific approvals require CHLOM-governed policy and evidence. CIE does not become synonymous with all CHLOM governance.

### Policy packs

Policy packs are versioned bundles of rules, tests, evidence requirements and decision outcomes. They may be jurisdictional, contractual, institutional, product-specific or corridor-specific.

### Eligibility

Eligibility is a computed/adjudicated result based on current criteria and evidence. It is not a permanent property of a person or organization.

### Approvals

Approvals are explicit authority-bearing decisions with scope, effective period and evidence.

### dS-CaaS events

P-02 may expose or consume dS-CaaS governance/compliance events. The exact expansion/relationship of dS-CaaS remains governed by source/ADR lineage where contradictory; this pallet treats it functionally as a current governance/runtime service label rather than rewriting historical acronym meaning.

### Audit

Audit is an evidence and assurance process. Audit PASS does not automatically create legal certification or professional opinion.

## Three-valued decision algebra

CHLOM deliberately treats unknown/unresolved state differently from explicit denial.

```text
PASS = requirements satisfied and action authorized
HOLD = insufficient, stale, conflicting, unresolved or pending evidence/authority
DENY = explicit prohibition or failed requirement under current policy
```

### Combination rule

A safe default for multiple mandatory gates is:

```text
if any mandatory gate == DENY:
    result = DENY
else if any mandatory gate == HOLD:
    result = HOLD
else:
    result = PASS
```

This public-safe abstraction does not expose protected policy weighting or fraud thresholds.

## Policy conflict behavior

Conflicts are first-class states.

```text
POLICY A says PASS
POLICY B says DENY
        |
        v
CONFLICT DETECTED
        |
        +--> deterministic precedence available? -- yes --> apply + record
        |
        +--> no --> HOLD + adjudication queue
```

No silent last-write-wins rule is allowed for material governance.

## Founder adjudication

Founder adjudication is an explicit current governance capability where reserved authority applies. It is recorded as a dated decision and propagates through affected policies/articles/machine records while retaining prior lineage.

```text

     |
     v

     |
     v

     |

     +--> policy updates
     +--> docs updates
```

Founder adjudication is not a mechanism for silently erasing historical source meaning.

## AI boundary

AI may:

- classify evidence;
- identify possible applicability;
- detect anomalies;
- draft policy mappings;
- simulate impact;
- recommend review;
- summarize conflicts.

AI may not, merely because it produced a score:

- create authority;
- grant protected rights;
- make reserved professional decisions;
- move money outside approved authority;
- self-adopt a policy;
- bypass a human-reserved D3 decision.

```text

   |
   v
EVIDENCE / RECOMMENDATION
   |
   v
POLICY + AUTHORITY GATE
   |
   v
DECISION
```

## Override model

Overrides are exceptional authority-bearing actions.

An override requires:

- explicit authority;
- bounded scope;
- reason/evidence;
- prior state preservation;
- effective time;
- expiry or review point when appropriate;
- rollback/compensation path;
- DAIL receipt.

```text
POLICY BLOCK
    |
OVERRIDE REQUEST
    |
AUTHORITY VALID?
  no -> DENY
  yes
    |
SCOPE / SAFEGUARDS VALID?
  no -> HOLD/DENY
  yes
    |
EXECUTE OVERRIDE
    |
DAIL + REVIEW
```

An override does not rewrite the original policy result.

## Operating procedures

### Policy adoption

1. identify source authority;
2. record exact source/version;
3. classify scope;
4. decompose requirements;
5. author machine representation;
6. validate against source;
7. run tests/simulation;
8. obtain adoption authority;
9. set effective period;
10. publish public/restricted projections;
11. emit DAIL lineage.

### Policy update

Updates create a new version; prior versions remain tied to historical decisions.

### Emergency hold

A material uncertainty can trigger HOLD without pretending the underlying rule is DENY.

### Audit remediation

Findings become tracked remediation objects with owners, due conditions and closure evidence.

### Appeal/review

Review creates a new decision path; it does not destructively edit the original decision.

## Failure modes

### Conflicting policy versions

**Result:** HOLD until precedence/currentness is resolved.

### Missing authority

**Result:** HOLD or DENY; do not infer approval from prior behavior.

### Stale external regulation evidence

**Result:** fail closed for actions requiring current evidence; do not claim regulatory compliance from stale feeds.

### AI model drift

**Result:** model output can be downgraded/held without rewriting policy authority.

### Provider says success, policy says no

**Result:** provider action is evidence; institutional state remains unreconciled/held or compensated.

### Audit scope ambiguity

**Result:** audit cannot be represented as comprehensive certification beyond its defined scope.
