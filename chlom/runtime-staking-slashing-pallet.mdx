## Historical purpose

The Staking & Slashing Pallet was designed to align historical CHLOM validator, issuer, oracle or participant behavior through bonded economic incentives: participants could stake value to obtain/maintain roles and lose some stake under governed misconduct conditions.

```text
PARTICIPANT
   |
BOND / STAKE
   |
ROLE / ELIGIBILITY
   |
GOOD PERFORMANCE ------------> rewards / continued eligibility
   |
MISCONDUCT / PROVEN BREACH ---> slashing / suspension / removal
```

## Slashing

Slashing should require evidence and an authorized adjudication path.

```text
MISCONDUCT SIGNAL
   |
EVIDENCE / CASE
   |
POLICY + AUTHORITY
   |
PROVEN CONDITION?
   |
   +--> no -> no slash / case close
   +--> hold -> HOLD
   +--> yes -> bounded slash / role remedy
```

A risk score or AI anomaly alone should not be equivalent to proven slashable misconduct.

## Unbonding / timelock

Stakes historically could require an unbonding delay so a participant could not immediately exit before pending misconduct/economic obligations were resolved.

