## CHLOM Grand Prospectus — Consolidated Historical Source Guide

The **CHLOM™ Grand Prospectus** consolidates multiple CHLOM prospectuses, module studies, operating concepts and companion systems into one book/part/chapter structure. It is a major recovery source because many later Help Center articles can be decomposed from it.

## Core Four historical model

The Grand Prospectus organizes CHLOM around:

1. **Identity & Fingerprint** — DIDs, Fingerprint IDs, credentials, attribution, reputation and accountability.
2. **Governance & Tokenomics** — DAO/council governance, economics, overrides, treasury and policy.
3. **ZK Privacy & Security** — selective disclosure, encrypted storage and privacy-preserving proofs.
4. **Compliance & Policy Enforcement** — Compliance Packs, policy registries, smart-rule execution, monitoring and sanctions.

The current Six Functions model — Rights, Rules, Roles, Revenue, Records, Remedies — is a later operating decomposition and does not erase the Core Four history.

## Historical lifecycle

The Prospectus describes identity onboarding → license issuance → transaction/policy enforcement → governance/override → audit/dispute resolution → reward/update.

Current CHLOM preserves that lifecycle but separates each state transition into explicit evidence, authority and implementation records.

## Fingerprint ID

The source treats **CHLOM Fingerprint ID** as an identity anchor connecting actors to credentials, roles, licenses, reputation, governance and transactions. Historical sybil-resistance and device/behavior pseudocode remains design history, not proof of deployed identity algorithms.

## Compliance Packs

A **Compliance Pack** is a modular, versioned ruleset for requirements such as privacy, KYC/AML, IP licensing, industry standards or override policy. Current policy/rule registries and dS-CaaS preserve this idea: rules should be versioned, scoped, composable, testable and traceable to authority.

## DLA

The Prospectus uses **DLA = Decentralized Licensing Authority** for the historical license-issuance authority. Current machine records reserve **DLA = Dynamic Licensing Asset** for the license object; authority is modeled separately.

## TLaaS

The Grand Prospectus uses **TLaaS = Trust Layer as a Service**: APIs, SDKs, plug-ins and service workflows that expose CHLOM capabilities to external products.

This differs from the Technical Blueprint's **Tokenized Licensing as a Service** usage. Both remain preserved.

## LEX

LEX is the licensing exchange for governed sale, leasing, sublicensing, auctions, fractional licensing and secondary-market activity. A LEX listing should be grounded in recognized rights and transfer permissions rather than unrestricted marketplace assumptions.

## DAL

The Grand Prospectus uses **DAL = Decentralized Adjudication Layer** for disputes, mediation, arbitration, verdicts, enforcement and appeals.

This conflicts with the Genesis Volume's **Decentralized Attestation Ledger** meaning. Modern **DAIL** is reserved for the current information/event/evidence/audit layer; cases and remedies remain separate services.

## Smart Treasury, Smart Tax, Smart Capital and Smart Yield

The Prospectus explores automated fees, allocations, reserves, grants, incentives, staking and possible investment/yield strategies. These are historical economic architecture. Current money movement requires adopted economic schedules, appropriate finance/tax authority and provider reconciliation.

## AI, audit, validators and oracles

The Prospectus contains substantial design material on AI/risk systems, continuous audits, code/security audits, case management, validators, oracle registries, regulatory-feed oracles, sanctions/identity/data oracles and network oversight.

These are inputs for later standalone reconstructed articles.

## Privacy and ZK

Historical privacy architecture includes zero-knowledge attribute proofs, selective disclosure, encrypted off-chain data vaults, private credential verification and proof-based compliance. Current architecture retains these as gated privacy/cryptographic targets.

## Why this source matters to the 795-article recovery

The Grand Prospectus supports article families across identity, governance, privacy, Compliance Packs, licensing, LEX, arbitration, treasury, AI/risk, oracles, validators, integrations, industry use cases, sustainability, developer tooling and adoption.

## Current interpretation

The Prospectus is evidence of **historical CHLOM design intent and terminology**, not automatic evidence of current production systems, prices, legal status, deployed tokens, DAO governance or live chain infrastructure.
