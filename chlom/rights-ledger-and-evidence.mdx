# Rights Ledger and Evidence Model

CHLOM separates **the claim**, **the evidence supporting the claim**, and **the permission to expose that evidence**.

This page is the current public-safe evidence/audit successor for recovered CHLOM evidence-oriented material. It defines how evidence is referenced, reviewed, corrected, and projected. It does not convert an evidence reference into a right, license, legal conclusion, payment entitlement, valuation, provider authority, or public claim merely because supporting material exists.

## Rights Ledger

A governed rights record should identify, where applicable:

- stable asset ID
- title / canonical name
- owner or controlling party
- contributor or creator roles
- source of authority
- rights categories
- territories
- channels
- term
- exclusivity
- restrictions
- derivative permissions
- commercial-use status
- institutional-use status
- public-display status
- AI-training status
- AI-voice status
- remix / stem / sampling status
- adaptation status
- governing agreement reference
- reviewer
- effective date
- superseded record
- correction history

The ledger is one governed registry family within the [CHLOM Registry Model](/chlom/registry-model). Stable identities and relationships should survive vendor, route, repository, or provider changes so the evidence trail is not lost when an implementation changes.

## Evidence references

Evidence is private by default.

A public record may say that supporting evidence exists without reproducing restricted source material. Evidence references should preserve enough metadata to audit the decision:

- evidence ID
- source type
- source title
- classification
- custodian
- received or created date
- hash or integrity marker where appropriate
- governing claim
- review state
- access policy
- supersession or correction relationship
- dependent records affected by the evidence

The [Source Reconciliation and Authority](/chlom/source-reconciliation) contract determines how conflicting or differently aged sources are compared. The [Restricted Source Register](/knowledge/restricted-source-register) determines when source existence may be referenced publicly while the underlying artifact remains restricted.

## Evidence is not economic truth

Provider receipts, delivery logs, payment objects, analytics counters, webhook success, platform status pages, user assertions, screenshots, and historical documentation are evidence inputs. None should directly manufacture institutional truth outside its authority domain.

For example, successful delivery does not by itself establish rights ownership; a payment-provider object does not by itself establish final institutional settlement; a provider account does not by itself establish CrownThrive role authority; and a historical paper does not by itself establish current production state. Evidence must be reconciled against the governing record before it is projected as current meaning.

## Auditability and integrity

Where technically appropriate, evidence references should carry integrity markers such as checksums, signed receipts, immutable event IDs, or DAIL-linked state transitions. An integrity marker proves that a referenced representation can be compared for change; it does not prove that the represented claim is legally or operationally correct.

Audit records should be sufficient to answer:

- what claim was being evaluated;
- which evidence was available at that time;
- who or what performed the review;
- which authority basis applied;
- what decision or hold resulted;
- which downstream objects depended on that result;
- whether later evidence corrected or superseded the decision.

The [Documentation Source of Truth & Autonomous Governance](/standards/documentation-source-of-truth-and-autonomous-governance) applies the same principle to documentation: source evidence and approved institutional meaning are linked, not silently collapsed into one record.

## Provenance for AI-assisted creative work

For AI-assisted music and media, the rights ledger may include:

- generator and model/version
- generation date
- account/subscription tier at generation
- human-authored lyrics
- human arrangement/editing
- human production contribution
- source audio
- voice provenance
- synthetic vocal status
- similarity-review status
- distributor
- DSP disclosure
- copyright-claim status
- PRO status
- ISRC
- master/stem archive reference

The existence of AI assistance does not, by itself, resolve copyright, contract, publicity, voice, or platform questions.

## Public, operator, and restricted projections

The same evidence object may support different projections. A public page may expose a stable evidence reference and review state; an operator view may expose additional metadata needed for reconciliation; a restricted governance surface may expose source details to authorized reviewers. These are projections of one evidence relationship, not competing facts.

Sensitive identity verification, credentials, private agreements, personal data, security artifacts, secret material, and other restricted evidence remain fail-closed. See [Security & Privacy Continuity](/technology/security-privacy-continuity) for the surrounding least-privilege and exposure boundary.

## Correction doctrine

A correction must preserve lineage.

Do not silently replace:

```text
old attribution → new attribution
```

Instead record:

```text
original record
→ correction event
→ corrected record
→ affected downstream objects
→ remediation status
```

This allows CrownThrive to prove what was known, when it was known, what changed, and how affected products or licenses were reconciled.

## Phase and execution boundary

A substantive evidence model is necessary for machine-readable governance, but it is not sufficient to unlock broad execution. The [Phase 3 Readiness Gate](/technology/phase-3-readiness-gate) independently governs whether schemas, identities, authority checks, rollback, evidence, and operational controls are ready for broader automation.

Until those gates pass, this page should be used as the canonical evidence/audit contract for reconstruction and controlled-test qualification without implying production activation, rights activation, provider-write authority, money movement, or terminal article disposition acceptance.
