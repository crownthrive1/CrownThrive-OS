# CHLOM Unified Value Receipt

The **CHLOM Unified Value Receipt (UVR)** is the wallet's user-facing explanation layer for a governed CrownThrive transaction or value event.

The design goal is simple:

> A user should be able to understand what they paid, what they received, what they earned, what impact was allocated, and whether the underlying evidence is verified — without learning the payment, rights, rewards, impact and blockchain architecture underneath it.

## The receipt card

A completed wallet experience can render one card with four independently governed legs:

```text
CHLOM VALUE RECEIPT

Money
Paid / refunded / disputed / pending

Rights
What access, license or entitlement exists

Rewards
What approved loyalty/reward value was earned

Impact
What ThriveFund/CII obligation was calculated,
approved or externally settled

Proof
CHLOM receipt fingerprint + optional anchor status
```

The card is a **unified view**, not a semantic merger. A provider payment receipt remains a provider payment receipt. A CHLOM license remains a rights record. A CrownRewards entry remains a reward. A ThriveFund obligation remains an impact/economic record.

## Core invariants

1. A successful Stripe/provider payment cannot make the Rights leg active by itself.
2. A calculated ThriveFund obligation cannot display as externally settled without settlement evidence.
3. Rewards cannot be displayed as cash, equity or transferable crypto unless a separately governed record explicitly defines that class.
4. The UVR points to authoritative records rather than replacing them.
5. Corrections and reversals create new lineage; historical receipts are not silently rewritten.
6. The receipt fingerprint is deterministic for the exact underlying state/version.
7. Public proof views exclude private agreements, credentials and sensitive evidence bodies.

## Why this is strategically different

Most payment products stop at **payment status**. Most crypto wallets stop at **assets and transactions**. Most rights systems stop at **licenses or entitlements**.

The UVR is designed around the CrownThrive/CHLOM convergence problem: one customer action can have payment, rights, reward, affiliate, partner, impact and evidence consequences across multiple ecosystem corridors.

A unified receipt can therefore become a reusable product primitive for:

- CrownThrive digital products and subscriptions;
- Virality Music licenses and publishing products;
- KJV/Sermon Toolkit entitlements;
- Crown Affiliates and Ambassador attribution;
- CrownRewards progression;
- MM Suites operator and license flows;
- AdLuxe campaign/economic records;
- CrownThriveU credentials and licensed curriculum;
- CHLOMLex / external licensing transactions;
- approved ThriveFund/CII evidence paths.

Each corridor keeps its specialized system. CHLOM produces one understandable proof surface.

## API and product implications

The UVR becomes a natural commercial primitive for:

- CHLOM Proof Receipt API;
- CHLOM Rights Wallet API;
- CHLOM WalletKit;
- enterprise white-label wallet experiences;
- support/dispute evidence packages;
- machine-readable audit/export packets.

The wallet should render a human-readable card while APIs expose a structured receipt and verification endpoint.

## Controlled-test implementation

A restricted controlled-test implementation now includes an append-only Unified Value Receipt record and deterministic receipt-digest builder. A synthetic test proved:

- four-leg receipt construction;
- deterministic SHA-256 receipt fingerprinting;
- linkage to an append-only wallet event;
- fail-closed rejection of in-place receipt mutation.

The synthetic receipt was rolled back after validation.

This is controlled-test evidence only. It does not certify live payment processing, a rights grant, a current reward rule, a current ThriveFund allocation, a financial statement, tax treatment or a blockchain anchor.
