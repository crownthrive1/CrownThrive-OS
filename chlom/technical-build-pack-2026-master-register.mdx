## 1. CHLOM-TECH-001 — Master Technical Implementation Blueprint

### Purpose

From proprietary governance framework to production rights, licensing and compliance infrastructure.

### Core decision

Build CHLOM as two things at the same time:

1. CrownThrive's governing operating system for rights, permissions, revenue, records and remedies;
2. a licensable multi-tenant software platform that may later serve external partners under controlled agreements.

### MVP boundary

The first production release centers on:

- verified identity;
- asset/evidence registry;
- rights graph;
- Dynamic Licensing Assets;
- licensing transactions;
- entitlements;
- settlement records;
- audit trails;
- disputes/remedies.

Explicitly **not** the MVP:

- a blockchain network;
- custodial wallet;
- token sale;
- legal ownership oracle;
- fully autonomous compliance system.

### Governing hierarchy

```text

        |
        v
Cultural Imprint Engine
        |
        v
Thrive Flywheel
        |
        v
CHLOM
Rights | Rules | Roles | Revenue | Records | Remedies
```

The hierarchy is institutional, not a claim that every software integration is already complete.

## 3. CHLOM-ARCH-003 — Identity, Rights, Licensing and Governance Architecture

### Purpose

Canonical records, trust model, DLA/LEX lifecycle, policy governance and remedies.

### Trust-separation invariant

```text
identity claim
    != verification
    != role
    != claim
    != evidence
    != review status
    != entitlement
    != legal effect
```

### Recovered trust model

- an identity assertion is not verification;
- verification does not make later fraud impossible;
- a role grants scoped system authority, not ownership;
- a claim is not a legal determination;
- evidence supports or contradicts a claim, not automatic truth;
- review status is process evidence, not universal validity;
- entitlement records permission under a specific agreement/policy;
- a fingerprint/attestation proves a record state existed, not that the underlying legal claim is correct.
