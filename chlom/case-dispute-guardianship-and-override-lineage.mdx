# Cases, Disputes, Guardianship and Override Lineage

Historical CHLOM materials include dispute, arbitration, adjudication, guardianship, emergency pause and override concepts. Modern CHLOM preserves those functions as explicit **remedy and authority workflows** rather than assuming a historical DAO or protocol pallet is live.

## Historical adjudication

The Grand Prospectus uses **DAL = Decentralized Adjudication Layer** for disputes, mediation, arbitration, verdicts, enforcement and appeals. Other papers describe dispute/arbitration pallets and governance mechanisms for reviewing contested license or compliance state.

Current CHLOM separates adjudication from DAIL: cases and remedies are operational services, while DAIL preserves the event/evidence history.

## Case lifecycle

```text
issue / complaint / conflicting evidence
→ case opened
→ evidence preserved
→ affected state held where required
→ authority and reviewer assignment
→ review / response / hearing workflow
→ disposition
→ remedy / correction
→ appeal where eligible
→ restoration / closure
→ DAIL lineage
```

A case may affect a DLA, entitlement, listing, payment/settlement, platform privilege, claim or other governed state, but the case record should identify exactly what is held and why.

## Holds and pause controls

A **HOLD** is not a failure state to hide. It is an explicit safety disposition used when evidence, authority, dependency or review is incomplete.

Historical pause/guardianship ideas map to modern emergency controls such as temporary suspension, read-only/degraded mode, provider quarantine, credential/capability freeze, commerce hold or release stop.

## Guardianship

Historical guardianship terminology refers to protective oversight capable of pausing or restricting unsafe activity. Current implementation should define guardians by stable role/authority rather than allowing any agent or platform administrator to assume universal emergency power.

## Overrides

An override should never erase the condition it overrides. A governed override requires identifiable authority, scope, reason/evidence, effective/expiration period, affected controls, rollback/compensation where applicable and a DAIL receipt.

```text
blocked/held condition
→ authorized override request
→ authority/evidence check
→ approved / denied / held
→ bounded temporary effect
→ expiry/review
```

Reserved D3 or professional decisions remain outside autonomous override.

## Appeals

An appeal is a new review path, not silent mutation of the original decision. The original decision, evidence and policy version remain preserved; the appeal produces its own record and may affirm, modify, reverse or remand the outcome according to authority.

## Remedies

Remedies may include correction, takedown, suspension, revocation, refund/reversal, compensation, access restriction, reinstatement, re-review, public correction or other domain-specific action. A remedy must respect the authority of the underlying domain—case resolution cannot manufacture ownership, money-movement authority or professional/legal conclusions.

## Separation of duties

The originator of a consequential action should not become the only reviewer of the resulting dispute. Current CHLOM governance uses independent verification/specialist review and human-reserved authority where required.

## Provider disputes

Provider chargebacks, platform abuse reports or external enforcement notices enter CHLOM as evidence. They may trigger a case or hold, but the provider is not automatically the institution's final adjudicator for unrelated rights or governance questions.
