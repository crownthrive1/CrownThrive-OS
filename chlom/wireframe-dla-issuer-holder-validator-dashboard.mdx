## Purpose

This dashboard is the role-composed working surface for a **Dynamic Licensing Asset** across its principal participants: issuer/licensor, holder/licensee/operator, and governed validator/reviewer. It is distinct from the generic Licenses Overview and License Detail wireframes because it organizes DLA work by role and outstanding action.

## Shared header

The top context bar contains DLA stable ID/version, asset, current lifecycle status, source-rights state, issuer/licensee identities, term, territory/channel summary, current policy/evidence freshness and any active HOLD/suspension.

## Issuer view

Primary panels:

- assets/right sources available for licensing;
- DLA candidates awaiting activation;
- active grants and upcoming expirations;
- sublicense/transfer requests;
- rights conflicts or evidence-refresh tasks;
- approved LEX projections/offers;
- royalty/settlement exception summary;
- amendment/revocation workflows.

Issuer actions are constrained to the exact rights and authority actually controlled. The UI cannot grant scope absent from the source-right graph.

## Holder / licensee view

Primary panels:

- active permissions and exact permitted uses;
- term/territory/channel/cap status;
- required credentials/evidence tasks;
- usage and remaining capacity where finite;
- renewal/amendment requests;
- payment/economic obligations and reconciliation;
- support/dispute/appeal links;
- provider fulfillment/entitlement status.

Unknown scope is never rendered as unlimited.

## Validator / reviewer view

Primary panels:

- assigned DLA review queue;
- rights-graph/source evidence;
- issuer authority;
- licensee eligibility/credential state;
- Compliance Pack results;
- conflicts, stale evidence and model/rule explanations;
- reviewer independence/conflict status;
- recommendation/approval packet;
- DAIL review receipt.

A reviewer recommendation is separated from the authority needed to execute activation, suspension or revocation.

## Role switching

A user holding multiple roles must see a clear active-role context. The interface cannot combine issuer and reviewer powers in a way that bypasses conflict-of-interest or independence rules.

## State model

`DRAFT`, `PENDING_RIGHTS`, `PENDING_ELIGIBILITY`, `PENDING_REVIEW`, `PENDING_APPROVAL`, `ACTIVE`, `HOLD`, `SUSPENDED`, `REVOKED`, `EXPIRED`, `SUPERSEDED`.

## Mobile/accessibility

Role work queues become stacked sections; rights graphs require a textual tree alternative; statuses and actions use explicit labels, not color alone.

## Data/API dependencies

DLA registry, rights graph, identity/credential service, policy engine, evidence/DAIL service, case/review service, economic schedules, entitlement service and provider-neutral adapters.

## Acceptance tests

Test issuer overscope attempt, holder cap/expiry, reviewer conflict, stale source evidence, active DLA with provider outage, payment success while policy is HOLD, role switch, amendment and revocation propagation.

## Versioning

This wireframe documents the role composition; the underlying DLA schema, policy and component versions remain independently versioned.
