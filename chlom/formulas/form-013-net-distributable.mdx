# FORM-013 — Net Distributable Calculation

## Formula identity

```yaml
formula_id: FORM-013
classification: derived_model
origin: Smart Treasury, royalty and settlement requirements
adopted_deduction_policy: false
```

## Expression

Let:

```text
gross_receipt = G
approved_refunds = R
approved_taxes_or_withholding = T
provider_fees = F
contractual_reserve = V
```

Then a candidate net-distributable model is:

```text
D_net = G - R - T - F - V
```

## Critical authority rule

The existence of a provider charge, tax estimate, reserve preference or refund request does not automatically make it an approved deduction. Every subtraction must resolve to the contract, policy, law/professional determination, or other authority governing that economic event.

## Gross definition

`G` must specify whether it is authorized amount, captured amount, settled amount, invoice amount, provider-reported gross, or another basis. These are not interchangeable.

## Refunds

Only approved and semantically linked refunds/reversals should reduce the relevant distribution basis. A refund from a prior period may require a compensating adjustment rather than rewriting the original distribution.

## Taxes and withholding

`T` is an abstraction. Applicable tax calculation, collection, remittance and legal treatment require jurisdiction-specific authority and professional/legal configuration where appropriate. FORM-013 does not determine tax liability.

## Provider fees

Provider evidence may show actual execution fees. The economic schedule decides whether those fees reduce the distributable basis, are absorbed by a platform, passed through, capped, or treated differently.

## Contractual reserve

`V` represents only a reserve explicitly authorized for the event/schedule. Reserve release later creates its own economic event or state transition.

## Non-negativity

If policy does not allow negative distributable balances for the event class:

```text
D_net >= 0
```

Otherwise, negative values must represent typed obligations/recoupments rather than accidental arithmetic underflow.

## Calculation record

Store all components, evidence refs, authority refs, currency/unit, schedule version, rounding and resulting `D_net`.

## Tests

Test no deductions, each deduction individually, all deductions, deductions exceeding gross, prior-period refund, provider-fee disagreement, reserve release and multi-currency input rejection without conversion.

## Versioning

The formula can remain stable while deduction policies change. Every economic result must therefore bind both FORM-013 and the applicable schedule/policy versions.
