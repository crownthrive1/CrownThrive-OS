# FORM-025 — Proof Freshness and Usability Predicate

## Formula identity

```yaml
formula_id: FORM-025
classification: derived_boolean_predicate

adopted_as_reconstruction_contract: yes
related_algorithm: ALG-013
```

## Predicate

A proof is usable for a current CHLOM decision only when:

```text
proof_usable =
    cryptographically_valid
AND circuit_version_allowed
AND verification_key_current
AND witness_source_current
AND revocation_state_current
AND policy_context_matches
```

Therefore:

```text
proof_valid != proof_usable
proof_usable != action_authorized
```

## Cryptographic validity

The proof verifies under its declared scheme, public inputs and verification key. This is necessary but does not answer whether the proof remains approved for current policy.

## Circuit/key currentness

The circuit and verification key must be active under the current registry. A retired or compromised key can make an otherwise valid proof unusable.

## Witness-source currentness

If the proof was generated from a credential, license, sanctions state, solvency snapshot or other mutable source, that source must satisfy the policy's currentness requirement.

## Revocation

Known revocation/supersession can invalidate reuse even before ordinary expiry/freshness limits.

## Policy context

The statement proven must match the action and scope. A proof for one subject, tenant, jurisdiction or purpose cannot be assumed reusable elsewhere.

## Tests

Test current proof, invalid proof, retired circuit, revoked key, stale witness, revoked credential, wrong policy context, cross-tenant replay and proof generated immediately before revocation.

## DAIL evidence

Record proof receipt/digest, circuit/key versions, witness evidence refs, currentness checks and result without storing private witness data.

## Versioning

Changes to usability predicates require explicit versioning independent of cryptographic verification libraries.
