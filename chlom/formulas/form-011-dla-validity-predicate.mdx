# FORM-011 — DLA Validity Predicate

## Formula identity

```yaml
formula_id: FORM-011
classification: derived_formal_predicate
origin: DLA lifecycle and activation requirements
adopted_as_reconstruction_contract: yes
```

## Predicate

A Dynamic Licensing Asset is valid for a given evaluation context only when all required conditions are true:

```text
VALID(DLA) =
    rights_valid
AND issuer_authorized
AND licensee_eligible
AND effective_time_valid
AND not_revoked
AND not_suspended
AND required_credentials_current
AND required_policy_passed
AND required_economic_conditions_met
```

## Three-valued extension

Real CHLOM state includes unresolved facts. Therefore production evaluation should use PASS/HOLD/DENY semantics rather than ordinary two-valued Boolean coercion. If any mandatory predicate is unknown because evidence is missing/stale, validity is **HOLD**, not true.

## Predicate components

### rights\_valid

Source rights and parent chain support the requested scope under FORM-009/FORM-010.

### issuer\_authorized

The actor granting the DLA possesses current authority over the relevant rights.

### licensee\_eligible

Identity, credentials, jurisdiction and other eligibility requirements pass.

### effective\_time\_valid

Current/requested time falls within the license's effective term and any activation/timelock constraints.

### not\_revoked / not\_suspended

The lifecycle record contains no currently effective revocation or suspension.

### required\_credentials\_current

Credential attestations remain valid, unrevoked and fresh enough for policy.

### required\_policy\_passed

Applicable Compliance Packs resolve according to ALG-001/ALG-017.

### required\_economic\_conditions\_met

Only conditions that are actually contractual/policy prerequisites belong here. Payment provider success alone is not automatically sufficient; it must reconcile to the defined obligation.

## Use-specific validity

A DLA can be valid generally yet not authorize every requested use. The predicate should be evaluated with requested scope/context, not just as a global `active=true` flag.

## Tests

Individually falsify/unknown each predicate, test all true, revoked after prior PASS, stale credential, economic condition reversed, scope not covered and policy version change.

## DAIL evidence

Record the exact DLA version, source-rights snapshot, predicate evidence and result for consequential uses.

## Versioning

Adding/removing a mandatory predicate is a material contract change and requires a new formula/policy version.
