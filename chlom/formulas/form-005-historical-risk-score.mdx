# FORM-005 — Historical Risk-Score Expression

## Recovered historical expression

```text
risk_score = α * (transaction_amount / std_dev)
           + β * peer_risk_avg
           + γ * KYC_level
```

The source states that actual models would be more complex and likely machine-learned. This equation therefore illustrates feature combination rather than a production specification.

## Feature interpretation

- `transaction_amount / std_dev` is an anomaly/scale-like term, though the exact baseline and standard deviation are not fully specified by the expression alone;
- `peer_risk_avg` captures contextual risk from a peer/reference group;
- `KYC_level` represents identity verification state, but the source does not define whether larger values mean stronger verification or greater risk.

## Directionality caveat

If a higher KYC level represents **stronger verification**, a positive `+ γ*KYC_level` would increase risk and may be directionally counterintuitive. The institutional record must preserve that historical expression rather than silently change it, while explicitly refusing to treat the sign as a production rule.

## Derived modern candidate

A safer normalized reconstruction is:

```text
R = σ(
      w1 * amount_zscore
    + w2 * peer_risk
    + w3 * anomaly_score
    + w4 * sanctions_risk
    - w5 * verified_identity_strength
    + w6 * rights_uncertainty
    + b0
)
```

where `σ` is a bounded transform such as logistic. This is a **derived candidate**, not adopted CHLOM model logic.

## Model governance

Before any risk equation can be consequential, CHLOM requires explicit feature semantics, units, missing-value behavior, calibration data, approved weights/model version, uncertainty handling, performance evaluation, bias/fairness review where relevant, adversarial analysis and threshold governance.

## Authority boundary

Risk is evidence for decisioning, not a legal conclusion:

```text
risk score != violation
risk score != authority
```

A high score can route to HOLD/review or a governed policy outcome; it does not independently revoke rights or execute penalties.

## Test requirements

Check feature directionality, scaling, outliers, missing values, standard-deviation near zero, peer-group leakage, protected/sensitive attributes, monotonic expectations, uncertainty and threshold behavior.

## DAIL lineage

Any model evaluation should reference model ID/version, feature snapshot, evidence inputs, result, uncertainty, threshold policy and final authority decision.

## Versioning

The recovered expression remains FORM-005 historical lineage. Derived and future production models must receive independent model IDs/versions.
