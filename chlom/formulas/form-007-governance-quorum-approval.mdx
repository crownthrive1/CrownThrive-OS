# FORM-007 — Governance Quorum and Approval Ratios

## Formula identity

```yaml
formula_id: FORM-007

adopted_thresholds: false
```

## Core ratios

```text
quorum_ratio = participating_weight / total_eligible_weight

approval_ratio = yes_weight / (yes_weight + no_weight)
```

Recovered historical examples discuss thresholds such as:

```text
quorum_ratio >= 15%
approval_ratio > 50%
constitutional_threshold ≈ 2/3
```

These numbers are **historical illustrative parameters**, not current CrownThrive/CHLOM governance rules.

## Variable semantics

`participating_weight` must define whether abstention counts. `total_eligible_weight` requires a frozen eligibility snapshot or deterministic method for determining eligible voting weight. `yes_weight` and `no_weight` can be equal-person or policy-defined weighted votes.

## Generic governed condition

A reconstructed current form is:

```text
proposal_passes =
    quorum_ratio >= q_min
AND approval_ratio >= θ
AND authority_class_allows(proposal_type)
AND timelock_satisfied
AND no_reserved_human_veto
```

The added authority conditions are not part of the simple historical ratios; they express current governance safety boundaries.

## Abstention and recusal

Policy must define whether abstentions count toward participation/quorum and whether recused/conflicted voters are removed from eligible weight. Failing to define the denominator can materially change the result.

## Snapshot discipline

Governance calculations should bind to an eligibility/voting-weight snapshot or explicit live-weight rule. Otherwise moving balances/roles during a vote can create ambiguous results.

## Authority boundary

Mathematical quorum cannot grant authority that the governing legal/organizational framework does not possess. A token or weighted vote cannot silently replace reserved founder, board, legal or professional authority.

## Calculation record

Record eligible-weight snapshot, participation set, exclusions/recusals, weights, threshold version, tally, rounding and result.

## Tests

Test zero eligible weight, exact quorum, below quorum, exact approval threshold, abstention variants, recusal, weighted voters, supermajority and denominator changes.

## Versioning

Thresholds and voting semantics are versioned policy. Historical examples remain intact and are never silently promoted to current governance.
