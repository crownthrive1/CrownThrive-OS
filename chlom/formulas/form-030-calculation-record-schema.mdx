# FORM-030 — Governed Calculation Record Schema

## Formula identity

```yaml
formula_id: FORM-030
classification: quantitative_record_schema
origin: CHLOM model governance and calculation lineage requirements
adopted_as_documentation_contract: yes
```

FORM-030 is not an arithmetic equation. It is the minimum institutional record needed for a machine-significant CHLOM calculation to be reproducible, reviewable and governable.

## Canonical schema

```yaml
calculation_id:
formula_id:
formula_version:
inputs:
input_evidence_refs:
units:
rounding_policy:
result:
result_unit:
executed_by:
calculated_at:
policy_version:
authority_context:
review_state:
DAIL_event_ref:
```

## Required semantics

### calculation\_id

Stable unique ID for this execution/result, not the formula definition itself.

### formula\_id and formula\_version

Bind the calculation to the exact mathematical contract used. Human-readable formula names are not sufficient for machine replay.

### inputs and evidence

Store normalized inputs plus evidence references showing where consequential values came from. Sensitive evidence can remain in protected custody with only a reference/digest in the calculation record.

### units

Every numeric input/result must have explicit units or a declared dimensionless type. Percent and basis points are distinct units.

### rounding policy

Economic and finite-precision calculations require deterministic rounding mode and precision.

### executed\_by

Identify the human, agent, service or job that performed the calculation. This is not the same as approval authority.

### policy and authority context

A calculation may be mathematically correct but not applicable to the decision. Bind the policy/economic schedule and authority context that makes the result institutionally meaningful.

### review state

Typical values may include `UNREVIEWED`, `MACHINE_VERIFIED`, `HUMAN_REVIEWED`, `APPROVED_FOR_USE`, `SUPERSEDED`, with exact meanings governed by schema/policy.

## Immutability/correction

If an input or formula was wrong, append a corrected calculation referencing the earlier one. Do not silently change the historical result.

## Tests

Reject missing formula version, unitless consequential inputs, unresolved evidence refs, unsupported rounding policy, duplicate calculation IDs and cross-tenant evidence leakage.

## DAIL

The calculation record either is a DAIL event/projection or is referenced from one. Its digest should be reproducible where classification permits.

## Versioning

Schema evolution follows backward-compatible or explicitly migrated versions; old calculation records remain readable.
