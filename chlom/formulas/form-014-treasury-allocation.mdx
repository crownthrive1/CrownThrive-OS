# FORM-014 — Treasury Allocation Mathematics

## Formula identity

```yaml
formula_id: FORM-014
classification: derived_public_safe_model
origin: CHLOM Smart Treasury lineage
adopted_weights: false
```

## Weighted allocation

For treasury inflow `I` and approved category weights `w_i`:

```text
allocation_i = I * w_i
```

with:

```text
0 <= w_i <= 1
Σ w_i = 1
```

if the entire governed inflow is allocated in that cycle.

## Reserve-target extension

For a reserve target `R_target`, current reserve balance `R_balance`, and policy share `w_reserve`:

```text
reserve_gap = max(0, R_target - R_balance)
reserve_contribution = min(w_reserve * I, reserve_gap)
overflow = w_reserve * I - reserve_contribution
```

The overflow destination must be explicit in policy; it cannot disappear or be redirected by implementation convenience.

## Authority boundary

The formula computes a schedule; it does not decide treasury policy. Category definitions, weights, reserve targets, caps, eligible expenditures and approval rights remain governed objects.

## Inflow classification

`I` must be a defined economic basis such as settled treasury revenue, designated contribution, fee allocation or another adopted class. Pending provider balances and expected receivables should not be mixed with settled funds without explicit accounting rules.

## Conditional allocations

Policies may add priority rules such as funding a reserve before growth allocation, capping a category, carrying unused amounts forward or holding funds pending review. Each condition must be deterministic and versioned.

## Reconciliation

Treasury allocation plans remain separate from provider/bank execution. Expected category allocations should reconcile to actual movements under ALG-010.

## Tests

Test weights summing exactly 1, under/over 1, reserve below target, reserve at target, reserve contribution larger than gap, zero inflow, negative/invalid weights, and currency precision.

## DAIL evidence

Record inflow basis, schedule/weight version, target/balance evidence, calculation, approvals and execution/reconciliation references.

## Versioning

Every adopted weight/target schedule has its own version and effective window. FORM-014 remains the reusable mathematical model.
