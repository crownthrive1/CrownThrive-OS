# FORM-008 — Conviction Voting Abstraction

## Formula identity

```yaml
formula_id: FORM-008
classification: historical_concept_plus_derived_model
origin: CHLOM historical governance/token research
adopted: false
```

## Expression

Historical CHLOM materials discuss conviction-style voting, where longer commitment/locking can increase governance weight. A generic reconstruction is:

```text
effective_vote_weight = base_weight * conviction_multiplier(lock_duration)
```

No multiplier function or schedule is adopted by this documentation.

## Components

- `base_weight` is the voter's eligible weight under the underlying governance system;
- `lock_duration` is the committed time period measured in an explicit unit;
- `conviction_multiplier(d)` is a governed function mapping duration to weight amplification.

## Design purpose

The historical rationale is to give participants with longer-term commitment greater influence and to reduce rapid vote cycling. This is an economic/governance design choice, not inherently more democratic or legally authoritative.

## Required constraints for any future use

A multiplier function should be bounded, monotonic if that is the intended policy, resistant to overflow/manipulation, and transparent before votes are cast. Maximum multiplier, minimum lock, unlock behavior, early exit/penalty, delegation interaction and snapshot rules must all be defined.

## Example family, not adopted

A future policy might choose stepwise or smooth multipliers, but no example schedule on this page is current. The formula only institutionalizes the abstraction so the concept is not lost.

## Authority boundary

Conviction weight can affect a CHLOM governance mechanism only if that mechanism is validly activated and authorized. It cannot manufacture authority over CrownThrive legal/corporate matters reserved elsewhere.

## Risk considerations

Long-lock multipliers can concentrate power among capital-rich actors, create illiquidity, enable vote markets or entrench old stakeholders. Governance design therefore needs concentration, delegation and capture analysis.

## Calculation record

Record base weight source, lock start/end, duration unit, multiplier function/version, effective weight, snapshot time and any exclusions.

## Tests

Test zero duration, minimum duration, maximum duration, boundary transitions, very large base weight, early unlock, concurrent votes, delegation and multiplier schedule change during a vote.

## Versioning

The concept remains historical/derived. Any activated multiplier schedule gets its own policy/formula version.
