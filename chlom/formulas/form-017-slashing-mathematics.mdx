# FORM-017 — Slashing Mathematics

## Formula identity

```yaml
formula_id: FORM-017
classification: historical_concept_plus_derived_arithmetic
origin: CHLOM validator/oracle economic-security research
adopted: false
```

## Basic arithmetic

For stake `S` and slash fraction `s`:

```text
slash_amount = S * s
stake_after = S * (1 - s)
```

with the normal domain:

```text
S >= 0
0 <= s <= 1
```

Historical CHLOM sources discuss example slashing ranges, including roughly `1%–10%` for certain infractions. Those ranges are research examples and not current penalties.

## Economic meaning

Slashing is modeled as an economic consequence for defined misconduct by validators, oracles or other bonded roles. The formula only calculates an amount after an authorized slash fraction is resolved.

## Due-process boundary

No AI score, anomaly flag or provider assertion can directly call FORM-017 and confiscate value. Any future implementation requires evidence standards, violation taxonomy, authority, notice/review/appeal behavior where applicable, emergency restrictions, reversal/compensation rules and legal/economic activation.

## Partial and capped slashing

Policies can define fixed fractions, severity bands, maximum slash caps or cumulative limits. Those are schedule parameters and must be versioned. The generic arithmetic remains the same.

## Repeated events

Sequential slashes can be calculated against original stake or remaining stake depending on policy. These produce different outcomes and must never be left implicit.

## Accounting destination

Slashed value might be burned, transferred to a treasury/protection pool, or otherwise handled under an adopted rule. FORM-017 does not determine that destination.

## Related protected calibration

The restricted Gold Paper contains deterrence/calibration relationships connecting stake, gain, detection probability and slash severity. Those restricted implementation/calibration details are bound in the private vault and are not expanded here.

## Tests

Test no slash, full slash, fractional slash, fraction out of range, sequential slash under both policies, insufficient stake, rounding at token precision and reversal/appeal simulation.

## DAIL evidence

A consequential slash calculation must bind violation/evidence refs, authority, stake snapshot, fraction/schedule version, amount, destination, execution receipt and appeal/correction lineage.

## Versioning

Formula arithmetic is stable; severity schedules and legal/governance activation are separate versioned objects.
