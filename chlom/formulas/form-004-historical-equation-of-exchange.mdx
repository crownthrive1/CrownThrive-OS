# FORM-004 — Historical Modified Equation-of-Exchange Concept

```yaml
formula_id: FORM-004
```

## Expression

```text
P * T = k
```

Recovered Genesis material uses a simplified relationship in which:

- `P` represents CHLOM coin price;
- `T` represents transaction volume or a network-usage proxy;
- `k` is a scaling constant described as being influenced by supply, burn and inflation factors.

## Correct interpretation

This expression is a **conceptual feedback model** used to reason about possible network economics. It is not presented here as an empirically proven law, a price prediction equation, or a current CHLOM production mechanism.

## Why preserve it

The formula shows that historical CHLOM research considered interaction between utility demand, network activity and monetary policy rather than treating token supply in isolation. That intellectual lineage remains useful even though current implementation sequencing is cloud-first and no live token economics are inferred.

## Dimensional caution

Unless `k` is assigned compatible units, `P*T=k` is schematic rather than a fully dimensioned physical/economic equation. Any simulation must state whether `T` means count of transactions, monetary transaction volume, velocity proxy or another metric.

## Sensitivity

If `k` were held constant by assumption, higher `T` mathematically corresponds to lower `P`, and vice versa. But the historical text also treats `k` as influenced by other economic variables, so a constant-`k` interpretation should not be overextended.

## Missing factors

Market price can be influenced by supply, liquidity, speculation, exchange availability, demand, regulation, macro conditions and other factors not represented here. Network transaction count can also change independently of token value.

## Authority boundary

FORM-004 is retained solely as historical CHLOM economic research. It does not authorize token issuance, pricing, trading, stabilization, buybacks or public economic claims.

## Simulation requirements

Any use should define `P`, `T`, `k`, observation window, data source, assumptions about supply/burn/inflation, whether `k` is fixed or dynamic, and intended purpose of the simulation.

## Tests

Validate arithmetic under declared units, reject undefined `T`, test constant and dynamic `k` scenarios, and include sensitivity analysis rather than one-point forecasting.

## Versioning

The source expression is immutable historical lineage. More complete economic models get distinct IDs.
