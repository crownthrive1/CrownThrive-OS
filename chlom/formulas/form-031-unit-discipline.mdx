# FORM-031 — Quantitative Unit Discipline

## Formula identity

```yaml
formula_id: FORM-031
classification: quantitative_safety_invariant
origin: CHLOM calculation governance
adopted_as_documentation_contract: yes
```

FORM-031 institutionalizes unit discipline as a first-class control. Many financial, risk and temporal failures are not caused by difficult mathematics; they are caused by combining numbers that look compatible but represent different units or semantic bases.

## Forbidden silent mixes

CHLOM calculations must not silently mix:

- percentages and basis points;
- annual and monthly rates;
- nominal and real values;
- gross and net receipts;
- token units and fiat units;
- integer minor units and decimal major units;
- seconds and milliseconds;
- event time and effective time;
- current and historical supply;
- confidence and probability without definition;
- amount and count;
- settled value and pending/provider-authorized value.

## Typed value contract

A consequential numeric value should carry:

```yaml
value:
unit:
scale_or_precision:
basis:
observation_time:
source_ref:
```

For rates, also record numerator/denominator units and annualization/measurement period.

## Percent versus basis points

```text
1% = 100 bps
10,000 bps = 100%
```

The unit should be encoded, not inferred from magnitude.

## Currency conversion

Never add or compare unlike currencies without an explicit conversion event/model containing source rate, timestamp, pair direction, fees/spread policy and rounding.

## Time rates

A monthly rate cannot be multiplied by 12 and called an equivalent annual rate without defining whether compounding is ignored. The calculation must state nominal/effective assumptions.

## Gross versus net

Economic schedules must bind to a named basis. A 20% royalty on gross is economically different from 20% of net distributable.

## Validation

Schema validators should reject incompatible units where machine-detectable. Human-readable documents should include unit annotations on every table/formula where ambiguity could change meaning.

## Tests

Test percentage/bps conversion, cents/dollars, seconds/milliseconds, USD/token, gross/net mismatch, annual/monthly rate, timestamp unit and dimensionless score with undefined scale.

## DAIL evidence

Calculation records under FORM-030 include units and bases so later auditors can replay without guessing.

## Versioning

Unit vocabulary/schema can evolve, but aliases must resolve deterministically and historical records remain interpretable.
