# FORM-015 — Historical Insurance-Pool Micro-Fee

## Formula identity

```yaml
formula_id: FORM-015
classification: historical_source_example

adopted: false
```

## Expression

Recovered historical CHLOM material gives an illustrative concept in which a transaction can contribute a small percentage to a protection/insurance pool:

```text
insurance_contribution = transaction_value * insurance_rate
```

A source example discusses a rate such as `0.01%` in a securities-trade context. That rate is preserved as historical illustration only.

## Purpose

The design concept is to capitalize a shared protection reserve as economic activity occurs, rather than funding a reserve only after a loss. Possible historical use cases include investor protection, dispute coverage or ecosystem risk reserves.

## Units

If `transaction_value` is denominated in currency/unit `U` and `insurance_rate` is a dimensionless fraction, contribution is in `U`. Multi-currency systems need explicit conversion and accounting rules rather than pooling unlike units directly.

## Authority boundary

An insurance/protection pool can implicate regulated insurance, securities, fiduciary, tax and consumer-protection issues. This formula does not establish a live insurance product or authorize collecting such a fee.

## Economic schedule requirements

Any future activation would require an approved rate, covered transaction classes, beneficiary rules, reserve custody, claims/remedy process, caps, refunds/reversals, accounting treatment and applicable legal/professional review.

## Reversal handling

If the originating transaction is refunded or reversed, the policy must define whether the contribution is reversed, retained, netted against future activity or handled through another reserve rule. Do not infer this from the simple multiplication.

## Tests

Test zero transaction value, zero rate, historical 0.01% scenario, rate bounds, refund/reversal, currency mismatch and large-volume accumulation.

## Versioning

The historical expression and example remain immutable. Any current protection-fund design must receive its own adopted schedule and legal/governance lineage.
