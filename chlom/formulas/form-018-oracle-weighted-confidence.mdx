# FORM-018 — Oracle Weighted-Confidence Model

## Generic weighted model

For observation `i`:

```text
weight_i = authority_i * reliability_i * freshness_i
```

A normalized numeric aggregation can be expressed as:

```text
C = Σ(weight_i * normalized_claim_i) / Σ(weight_i)
```

For boolean/attestation claims, a support ratio can be expressed as:

```text
support_ratio = supporting_trusted_weight / total_trusted_weight
```

For adversarial or outlier-prone numeric feeds, a weighted median may be preferable to a weighted mean.

## Variable semantics

`authority_i` represents how authoritative a source is for the claim domain, not organizational prestige. `reliability_i` reflects evidence-backed source performance under a versioned methodology. `freshness_i` reflects currentness under the applicable evidence TTL. All three must use explicit scales.

## Independence

Weights do not replace independence checks. Ten observations ultimately derived from one source should not automatically carry ten times the evidentiary weight. ALG-011 handles source quorum and independence before or alongside weighted aggregation.

## Confidence versus truth

`C` is an evidence confidence/aggregation score under a chosen model. It is not ontological truth, legal certainty or authority.

```text
high confidence != authority to act
```

## Missing/zero weight

If `Σ weight_i = 0`, no numeric result is valid. The system should return no-result/HOLD rather than divide by zero or assume a neutral confidence.

## Contradictory evidence

Material contradictions should be surfaced explicitly. A high average must not hide a split between two equally authoritative sources on a binary legal/identity fact.

## Governance

Weight scales, reliability windows, source domains and minimum quorum are policy/model parameters. AI may estimate source reliability, but it cannot silently rewrite active authority weights.

## Tests

Test one source, multiple agreeing sources, outlier, all zero weights, stale source, high-reliability but unauthorized source, correlated sources, contradictory equal-weight evidence and weighted-median behavior.

## DAIL evidence

Record source refs, component weights, model/version, aggregation method, result and contradiction metadata.

## Versioning

Each adopted weighting methodology receives its own model version; old calculations retain the prior weight definitions.
