# UI-CASE-004 — Review / Appeal Timeline

## Component identity

```yaml
surface_id: UI-CASE-004
component: Review / Appeal Timeline
classification: role_scoped_component_contract
owner_capabilities: Cases; Review; Governance; DAIL
```

## Purpose

This timeline shows the procedural history of reviews and appeals: assignment, evidence closure, recommendation, decision, notice, appeal request, appeal decision, remand/reopen and final closure.

## Data contract

Stage/event ID, stage type, reviewer/authority class, effective/recorded time, public-safe outcome, evidence/decision refs, notice/appeal deadlines, supersedes/remands relationship and current procedural state.

## States

`INITIAL_REVIEW`, `DECIDED`, `NOTICE_ISSUED`, `APPEAL_WINDOW`, `APPEALED`, `REMANDED`, `APPEAL_DECIDED`, `FINAL`, `REOPENED`, `REDACTED`.

## Semantics

A later appeal result supplements/supersedes the effective outcome according to policy but does not delete the initial decision. Deadlines come from the governing case policy, not presentation defaults.

## Interactions

Open decision/evidence, inspect reviewer eligibility, submit or view appeal where authorized, track deadline and navigate to current effective remedy.

## Accessibility/tests

Provide chronological list alternative, explicit dates/state labels and accessible deadline alerts. Test remand, late appeal, reopened case, reviewer recusal, protected decision body and multiple appeal levels.

## Versioning

Review and appeal stage schemas are versioned by case type/policy.
