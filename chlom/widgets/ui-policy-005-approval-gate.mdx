# UI-POLICY-005 — Approval Gate

## Component identity

```yaml
surface_id: UI-POLICY-005
component: Approval Gate
classification: public_safe_component_contract
owner_capabilities: Authority; Governance; Policy
```

## Purpose

The Approval Gate makes human/governance prerequisites visible before a consequential action. It prevents the interface from presenting a technically executable operation as available when founder, reviewer, council, cosigner, legal or other required approval is still missing.

## Data contract

Action ID/version/hash, required approval classes, received approvals, pending approvals, authority-validity state, quorum/cosign requirement, timelock/effective window, evidence freshness and final gate disposition.

## States

`OPEN_FOR_EXECUTION`, `AWAITING_APPROVAL`, `AWAITING_COSIGN`, `QUORUM_NOT_MET`, `TIMELOCKED`, `APPROVAL_EXPIRED`, `AUTHORITY_INVALID`, `HOLD_CHANGED_SNAPSHOT`, `DENIED`.

## Semantics

Approval is bound to the exact action/version and scope. A prior approval cannot silently authorize a materially changed payload. Green policy results or provider readiness do not bypass this gate.

## Actions

Authorized users can view approval requirements, provide/sign approval, recuse, request additional evidence or inspect the approval/DAIL trail. Execution remains a separate idempotent action after the gate opens.

## Accessibility

List missing approvals and timing in text; show disabled-action reasons programmatically.

## Tests

Test exact-action approval, changed version, expired delegation, missing cosigner, quorum boundary, timelock, approval revocation and stale evidence after approval.

## Versioning

Approval policies and authority schemas are versioned; UI renders effective requirements from canonical policy rather than hard-coded role names.
