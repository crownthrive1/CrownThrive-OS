# UI-RIGHTS-004 — DLA Status Card

## Component identity

```yaml
surface_id: UI-RIGHTS-004
component: DLA Status Card
classification: public_safe_component_contract
owner_capabilities: DLA; Rights; Policy; Evidence
```

## Purpose

The DLA Status Card is the canonical compact view of a Dynamic Licensing Asset. It shows whether the exact license/permission version is active and usable for a context without exposing private agreement bodies or reverting to the historical Decentralized Licensing Authority meaning.

## Data contract

DLA stable ID/version, asset, issuer/licensee public-safe identities, status, effective term, territory/channel summary, transfer/sublicense flags, rights-evidence state, credential/policy gate summary, amendment/revocation indicator and last evaluated time.

## States

`DRAFT`, `PENDING_GATES`, `ACTIVE`, `SUSPENDED`, `REVOKED`, `EXPIRED`, `SUPERSEDED`, `HOLD_RIGHTS`, `HOLD_CREDENTIAL`, `HOLD_POLICY`, `ERROR`.

## Semantics

The card is a projection of FORM-011/ALG-003 style validity. An `ACTIVE` record does not authorize a use outside its scope; consuming actions still evaluate requested use/context.

## Actions

View full license detail, rights graph, evidence, amendments, sublicenses or request a governed lifecycle action. The component itself cannot activate, revoke or amend a DLA.

## Accessibility and tests

Expose status, term and restriction textually. Test every lifecycle state, superseded versions, revoked parent, hidden confidential terms, stale evaluation and mobile/table embeddings.

## Versioning

DLA terminology and lifecycle schema are current canon; historical Authority-era records must be clearly labeled lineage-only.
