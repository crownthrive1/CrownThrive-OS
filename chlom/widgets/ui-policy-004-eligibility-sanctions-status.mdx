# UI-POLICY-004 — Eligibility / Sanctions Status

## Component identity

```yaml
surface_id: UI-POLICY-004
component: Eligibility / Sanctions Status
classification: public_safe_component_contract
owner_capabilities: Eligibility; Sanctions; Oracle; Policy
```

## Purpose

This component summarizes whether a subject currently satisfies the eligibility controls relevant to an action, including sanctions/restricted-party evidence where applicable.

## Data contract

Eligibility state, required credential summary, jurisdiction status, sanctions source/freshness summary, possible/confirmed-match state, policy version, evaluated-at time, recheck deadline and review requirement. Sensitive identity match attributes remain protected.

## States

`ELIGIBLE`, `INELIGIBLE`, `POSSIBLE_MATCH_REVIEW`, `CONFIRMED_RESTRICTED`, `HOLD_STALE_SOURCE`, `HOLD_MISSING_CREDENTIAL`, `NOT_APPLICABLE`, `ERROR`.

## Semantics

A fuzzy/name similarity is not displayed as a confirmed sanctions match. Stale mandatory sources cannot render as eligible. The final action can have additional policy/authority gates even when this component shows eligible.

## Interactions

Open public-safe check summary, refresh/reverify, submit supporting evidence or route a possible match to authorized review.

## Privacy

Do not expose dates of birth, government identifiers or other matching attributes in default surfaces. False-positive resolution evidence is protected.

## Tests

Test exact match, possible match, stale source, unavailable source, de-listed subject, missing credential, jurisdiction mismatch and false-positive reviewer resolution.

## Versioning

Source applicability/matching policies and state vocabulary are versioned independently.
