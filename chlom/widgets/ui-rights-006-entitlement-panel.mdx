# UI-RIGHTS-006 — Entitlement Panel

## Component identity

```yaml
surface_id: UI-RIGHTS-006
component: Entitlement Panel
classification: public_safe_component_contract
owner_capabilities: Rights; DLA; Commerce; DAIL
```

## Purpose

The Entitlement Panel shows the benefits or access actually available to a subject after the governing DLA, rights, policy and economic prerequisites are satisfied. It separates **entitlement truth** from provider delivery state.

## Data contract

Entitlement ID/version, subject, source DLA/agreement, entitlement type, effective scope, valid term, consumption/cap state, status, fulfillment/provider aliases, last reconciled time and evidence/DAIL reference.

## States

`PENDING`, `ACTIVE`, `PARTIALLY_FULFILLED`, `CONSUMED`, `SUSPENDED`, `REVOKED`, `EXPIRED`, `HOLD_RECONCILIATION`, `ERROR`.

## Semantics

Provider access or file delivery is not itself the canonical entitlement. Likewise, a payment success event does not create entitlement unless the rights/policy/economic gates authorize it.

## Actions

View source license, inspect fulfillment evidence, refresh reconciliation, use/download/access where permitted, or initiate a governed support/remedy workflow. No UI action may bypass cap or revocation checks.

## Privacy

Only show entitlement details appropriate to the viewer; hidden licensed assets and private deal terms remain protected.

## Tests

Test payment success without entitlement, active entitlement with provider outage, revoked DLA, cap consumed, partial delivery, expired access, duplicate fulfillment and entitlement correction.

## Versioning

Entitlement state is versioned independently of provider IDs and storefront implementation.
