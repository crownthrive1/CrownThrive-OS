# UI-RIGHTS-001 — Asset Record Card

## Component identity

```yaml
surface_id: UI-RIGHTS-001
component: Asset Record Card
classification: public_safe_component_contract
owner_capabilities: Rights; DLA; DAIL
```

## Purpose

The Asset Record Card is the compact canonical presentation of a governed asset: creative work, brand, IP, platform object, agreement-linked asset or other rights-bearing subject. It prevents provider catalog IDs from replacing CHLOM stable asset identity.

## Data contract

Stable asset ID, public title/label, asset class, permitted owner/controller summary, source-right status, DLA/license status/count summary, provenance indicator, lifecycle state, public provider aliases and last material update.

## States

`REGISTERED`, `RIGHTS_VERIFIED`, `RIGHTS_HOLD`, `DISPUTED`, `REVOKED_OR_RETIRED`, `ARCHIVED`, `REDACTED`, `ERROR`.

## Semantics

Possession of a file, listing, payment or provider account is not displayed as ownership. Rights state comes from canonical evidence and rights-graph evaluation.

## Actions

Open rights graph, provenance timeline, DLA records or authorized review/edit workflows. Writes remain authority-gated.

## Tests

Test aliases, disputed rights, multiple permissions, retired asset, restricted source, missing label and cross-tenant collision.

## Versioning

Asset states and disclosure schema are versioned; stable asset identity persists across provider changes.
