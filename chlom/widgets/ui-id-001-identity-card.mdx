# UI-ID-001 — Identity Card

## Component identity

```yaml
surface_id: UI-ID-001
component: Identity Card
classification: public_safe_component_contract
owner_capabilities: Identity; DAIL
```

## Purpose

The Identity Card is the canonical reusable presentation of a person, organization, service or governed identity across CHLOM. It anchors every view to a stable canonical identity while keeping provider aliases and restricted Fingerprint evidence separate.

## Data contract

Stable identity ID/DID, permitted display name, entity type, lifecycle/verification state, public credentials or badges, relevant role summary, source/currentness indicator, public aliases and last material verification time.

## Private boundary

Private Fingerprint ID, device/behavioral signals, raw identity documents, KYC payloads and deduplication heuristics are never rendered in the public/default card. Restricted operators use separate protected evidence references under least privilege.

## States

`ACTIVE`, `UNVERIFIED`, `HOLD_REVIEW`, `SUSPENDED`, `REVOKED`, `REDACTED`, `ERROR`.

## Actions

Open authorized identity profile, credential summary or evidence lineage; request reverification through governed workflow. The card cannot self-grant roles or authority.

## Tests

Test role-based redaction, revoked status, missing display name, alias collision, cross-tenant identity, screen-reader ordering and mobile rendering.

## Versioning

Identity-state/disclosure schemas are centrally versioned; stable IDs survive UI redesign.
