# ROLE-SURF-006 — MM Suites / Suite Pro Operator Surface

## Purpose

This surface translates CHLOM's rights, rules, roles, revenue, records and remedies into the daily operating context of a Melanin Magic Suites / Suite Pro operator: credentials, suite/location status, booking eligibility, compliance work, local economic activity, training and governed ecosystem integrations.

## Operator identity and credentials

Compose Identity Card, Credential/Verification Badge, Role/Authority Badge and Consent State. Professional/operator credentials and location-specific approvals remain versioned, currentness-aware records rather than static profile fields.

## Suite/location state

Show canonical suite/location ID, assigned operator, facility/operating status, required evidence, applicable Compliance Packs, provider aliases, incident/hold state and last assurance review. A provider location ID is an alias, not the CHLOM location identity.

## Booking and service eligibility

Bookings/services can proceed only when the relevant operator, location, service rights/permissions, policy and commercial conditions pass. Payment or marketplace availability does not override a credential or safety HOLD.

## Compliance scorecard

Compose Policy Result Badges, Compliance Task List, Evidence Checklist and audit/certification state. The scorecard must show individual control dispositions and not reduce legal/safety status to an opaque percentage.

## Local revenue and economic state

Display authorized local revenue/commission/royalty statements, economic schedule versions, payment evidence and reconciliation. ThriveEvergreen/economic authority remains separate from payment-provider execution.

## Campaigns and ecosystem participation

Authorized offers/campaigns can connect the operator to CrownRewards, CrownFluence, Crown Ambassadors, affiliates, AdLuxe and other ecosystem programs according to policy. Participation in one program does not create authority in another.

## Training and advancement

Show required/optional CrownThriveU training, certificates, renewal dates and evidence. Training completion becomes a credential/evidence event where governance requires it, not merely a badge.

## Protected data boundary

Customer personal data, payment credentials, confidential operator files, internal risk models and provider secrets remain protected. Dashboards use minimum authorized projections.

## Tests

Test expired credential, location hold, booking while provider is healthy but policy is HOLD, training renewal, multi-location operator, revenue mismatch, revoked integration and cross-location data isolation.

## Versioning

Operator, suite/location, booking, policy, economic and training records are versioned independently and connected by stable IDs/DAIL lineage.
