# ROLE-SURF-008 — Publisher / Advertiser Surface

## Institutional identity

```yaml
surface_id: ROLE-SURF-008
role_family: Publisher / Advertiser
classification: governed_role_composition
version: 1.0.0
```

## Purpose

This surface governs publisher, advertiser and AdLuxe-related workflows from property/inventory registration through creative-rights checks, campaign approval, delivery evidence, measurement, billing and settlement.

## Property and inventory

Show canonical property/site/channel identity, owner/operator, inventory classes, audience/content restrictions, ad-supported/member experience rules, provider aliases, integration health and current eligibility. Provider ad-server IDs remain aliases.

## Creative and rights clearance

Every creative/campaign asset links to source-rights and usage permissions appropriate to placement. Logo, music, image, likeness, copy and other rights can be independently constrained. Uploading creative does not prove rights clearance.

## Campaign policy gates

Compose Policy Result Badge, applicable evidence, Compliance Tasks and Approval Gate. Campaigns can be held for prohibited claims, missing rights, unsafe targeting, expired approvals, unavailable required evidence or other governed controls.

## Delivery and measurement

Ad-server/provider events are normalized as observations. Show impressions, clicks, conversions, spend/revenue and quality metrics with source/currentness and deduplication. CrownLytics, CrownPulse and other analytics surfaces consume governed projections; they do not rewrite canonical campaign or economic records.

## Billing and economic reconciliation

Use Fee Rule Version, statements and Settlement/Reconciliation Status to compare agreed campaign economics with provider billing/delivery. Successful provider delivery does not create a final invoice, earning or entitlement without institutional reconciliation.

## Brand safety and privacy

Targeting, audience and content controls respect applicable policy and consent. Personally identifiable or sensitive evidence is minimized; restricted targeting logic, provider credentials and proprietary fraud-detection models remain protected.

## Disputes and remedies

Campaign delivery disputes, invalid-traffic concerns, billing mismatches, rights complaints, takedowns or refunds route to governed cases and remedy records with evidence lineage.

## Tests

Test creative without rights, disallowed property, expired campaign approval, provider duplicate delivery, invalid-traffic hold, measurement-source conflict, invoice mismatch, refund and cross-property data isolation.

## Versioning

Property, creative, campaign, policy, measurement and economic schedules are versioned; historical reporting remains tied to the exact versions effective at delivery.
