## Purpose

This surface is the canonical working environment for governed stewards, Scribes, verifiers, auditors and other authorized reviewers. It composes the evidence and decision tools required to evaluate rights, policies, credentials, cases and assurance packages without granting reviewers powers outside their assignment.

## Work queues

The primary queue organizes assigned reviews, open Compliance Tasks, evidence-refresh work, disputes/cases, audit findings, policy migrations and requests awaiting review. Queue membership comes from governed assignment/eligibility logic rather than manual dashboard visibility alone.

## Evidence workspace

Compose the Evidence Reference Panel, Source Reliability/Applicability Badge, DAIL Event Timeline, Correction/Supersession History and Evidence Checklist. Restricted evidence opens only under the viewer's assigned purpose/scope and access is auditable.

## Rights and licensing review

Use Asset Record Card, Rights Graph Viewer, DLA Status Card, Scope Summary and Entitlement Panel to determine whether the requested permission is supported by the source-right chain and current lifecycle state.

## Policy and risk review

Use Policy Result Badge, Risk/Rule Explanation Panel, Eligibility/Sanctions Status and applicable Compliance Pack/version references. The reviewer can validate or challenge evidence/model interpretation according to policy; a model score is never treated as a conclusion by itself.

## Human-assurance controls

Reviewer assignment, independence/conflict state and quorum are visible. Recusal and replacement preserve DAIL history. Review completion produces a signed/attributed review result, not execution authority unless a separate authority class explicitly says otherwise.

## Cases, appeals and remedies

Use Case Summary, Hold/Suspension State, Review/Appeal Timeline and Remedy/Action Panel. Reviewers can recommend/decide only within the authority defined for that case class. Appeals preserve the initial record.

## Output packet

A completed review packet includes object/version, reviewer identity and eligibility, evidence set, applicable policies/models, findings/reason codes, decision/recommendation, uncertainty or unresolved conflicts, timestamp and DAIL receipt.

## Security boundary

Reviewers cannot browse unrelated tenant/private evidence, reveal protected algorithms, access vault secrets by implication, or self-assign to conflicted cases. Export actions are separately scoped and logged.

## Tests

Test conflicted reviewer, expired credential, insufficient quorum, stale evidence, restricted evidence, rights conflict, model drift, appeal, reviewer replacement and cross-tenant isolation.

## Versioning

Role composition and review schemas are versioned; historical review decisions remain bound to the exact policy/evidence/assignment snapshot used.
