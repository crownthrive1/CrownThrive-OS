# ROLE-SURF-007 — Affiliate / Ambassador Surface

## Institutional identity

```yaml
surface_id: ROLE-SURF-007
role_family: Affiliate / Ambassador
classification: governed_role_composition
version: 1.0.0
```

## Purpose

This surface governs how affiliates, Crown Ambassadors, CrownFluence participants and similar approved promoters discover eligible offers, create tracked referrals, satisfy disclosure/training conditions, inspect conversions and reconcile commissions without treating tracking-provider data as final economic truth.

## Identity and program standing

Compose Identity Card, Credential/Verification Badge, program role, agreement/version, training/disclosure status, current eligibility and any hold/suspension. Enrollment in one program is not blanket permission to promote every CrownThrive product or brand.

## Approved offer catalog

Display only offers the participant is authorized to promote, including brand/product, allowed channels, geography/audience restrictions, disclosure language requirement, approved assets, effective window, commission schedule version and prohibited claims.

## Tracking and attribution

Referral links/codes resolve to stable participant and offer IDs. Click/conversion provider events are observations that must be deduplicated and reconciled. Attribution corrections remain append-only and can be disputed through cases.

## Disclosure and training

Required disclosure, brand-safety or campaign training is shown as current credential/task state. Missing or expired requirements can HOLD new promotion/commission eligibility according to program rules.

## Commissions and payouts

Compose Fee Rule Version, Royalty/Commission Statement, Settlement/Reconciliation Status and Refund/Clawback State. A tracked conversion does not automatically equal an earned/settled commission until eligibility, attribution, refund window and economic schedule conditions reconcile.

## Content/brand controls

Approved creative assets and usage rights are linked to rights/DLA records. Participants cannot alter marks, licensing scope or advertising claims beyond the approved offer/campaign contract.

## Disputes and remedies

Provide cases for attribution disputes, rejected conversions, disclosure violations, commission mismatches and suspension/appeal. Holds and findings remain scoped and reviewable.

## Privacy boundary

Participants see only the customer/order information necessary for authorized attribution/support; raw personal/payment data and other affiliates' performance/economics remain protected.

## Tests

Test expired agreement, unauthorized offer, missing disclosure training, duplicate conversion, refund after commission calculation, attribution conflict, suspended participant, wrong channel/territory and payout mismatch.

## Versioning

Offer, campaign, commission, agreement, disclosure and tracking schemas are versioned; historical conversions remain tied to the versions effective when they occurred.
