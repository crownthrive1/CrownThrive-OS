# ROLE-SURF-004 — Licensee / Operator Surface

## Institutional identity

```yaml
surface_id: ROLE-SURF-004
role_family: Licensee / Operator
classification: governed_role_composition
version: 1.0.0
```

## Purpose

This surface gives a licensee or operating user an exact view of what CHLOM currently permits, what conditions remain, what evidence is required, what economic obligations exist and what remedies/support paths are available.

## Permission view

Compose DLA Status Card, Usage/Territory/Term Summary and Entitlement Panel. The surface must answer what is allowed, where, when, through which channel and under what usage/cap constraints. Unknown scope never appears as unlimited.

## Conditions and compliance tasks

Show current Policy Result Badges, Eligibility/Credential status, required Compliance Tasks and evidence deadlines. A HOLD includes a remediation/reverification path where allowed. Completing a task triggers re-evaluation rather than locally flipping a result to PASS.

## Usage and capacity

Show governed consumption/remaining capacity, renewal or expiration state and any explicit unlimited scope. Historical usage is DAIL-backed where consequential. Concurrent usage must respect capacity-safety/idempotency controls.

## Economic obligations

Show applicable fee/economic schedule version, invoices/obligations, payment evidence, refunds and reconciliation state. Payment-provider success does not activate permission if rights/policy conditions no longer pass.

## Renewal and amendment

Renewal is a new governed evaluation of source rights, issuer authority, licensee eligibility, current credentials, policy and economic terms. The surface can compare old/new versions but cannot assume renewal because a payment method remains valid.

## Support, disputes and appeals

Users can open/view cases, submit evidence references, track review/appeal timelines and see holds/remedies. Complaint/dispute state is procedural and should not be framed as a final adverse finding before decision.

## Privacy and provider separation

Provider IDs, storefront accounts and delivery services remain aliases. Restricted licensor contracts, internal model details and other tenants' evidence are not exposed.

## Tests

Test active permission, expired term, territory/channel mismatch, cap reached, explicit unlimited, stale credential, payment matched with rights revoked, renewal changed terms, appeal and provider outage.

## Versioning

Every displayed permission and obligation is tied to the exact DLA, policy, economic-schedule and entitlement version.
