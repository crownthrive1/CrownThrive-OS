## Purpose

This is the canonical CHLOM command surface for founder/reserved-authority and holdings-level oversight. It composes portfolio, authority, risk, economic, incident and system-health views without turning the dashboard itself into authority.

## Core information zones

### Portfolio and ecosystem state

Show governed platforms, pallets, critical assets, licensing/rights exposures, current certification states, unresolved convergence dependencies and major provider/deployment health. Each item resolves to stable registry IDs rather than brand-name-only rows.

### Reserved authority and approvals

Compose `UI-ID-003 Role / Authority Badge`, `UI-POLICY-005 Approval Gate`, pending adjudications, timelocked actions and exact-version approval requests. The surface distinguishes `FOUNDER ACTION REQUIRED` from internal-agent work that can continue without human action.

### Risk and assurance

Show consequential HOLD/DENY states, audit findings, evidence freshness, model drift, unresolved rights conflicts, security incidents and certification blockers. Risk model output is separated from institutional authority.

### Capital and economic state

Show authorized high-level revenue/obligation/settlement summaries, treasury allocations, reserves/holds, CrownThrive economic control-plane status and reconciliation exceptions. Provider balances remain evidence, not institutional economic truth.

### Incident and continuity

Show P0/P1 incidents, emergency holds, continuity failures, dependency outages, recovery state and restricted override existence/status. Secret kill-switch material is never rendered.

## Write actions

Permitted actions can include adjudication, approval, ratification, bounded override/reversal, policy adoption and other founder-reserved functions only when the exact action passes ALG-002 and any required quorum/timelock. Dashboard presence never grants authority.

## Public/private boundary

Private vault materials, credentials, personal data, proprietary Fingerprint internals, model weights, trade-secret algorithms and restricted deal economics appear only as protected references/status. The surface must never hydrate protected content into the client unless the viewing role, purpose and object scope authorize it.

## Evidence and DAIL

Every consequential decision launched from this surface records the exact object/action version, authority, approvals, evidence snapshot, policy/model versions and resulting DAIL receipt.

## Accessibility and resilience

Critical states have textual labels, sortable tables and non-chart equivalents. Provider outages or stale evidence must display explicit degraded/HOLD state rather than old green metrics.

## Tests

Test founder role with and without specific action authority; approval for wrong snapshot; stale evidence; provider outage; restricted-data redaction; emergency hold; competing adjudications; mobile layout; and exact action-needed labeling.

## Versioning

Surface layout can evolve independently, but authority semantics and component contracts remain versioned institutional dependencies.
