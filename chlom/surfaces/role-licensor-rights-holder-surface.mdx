# ROLE-SURF-003 — Licensor / Rights Holder Surface

## Institutional identity

```yaml
surface_id: ROLE-SURF-003
role_family: Licensor / Rights Holder
classification: governed_role_composition
version: 1.0.0
```

## Purpose

This surface gives creators, brand owners, licensors and other authorized rights controllers one governed place to register assets, evidence controlled rights, create/manage Dynamic Licensing Assets, project eligible offers to LEX, inspect downstream use and reconcile royalties or disputes.

## Asset and rights workspace

Use Asset Record Cards, Rights Graph Viewer and Provenance Timeline to display source-right evidence, agreements, transfers, restrictions and conflicts. Uploading or listing an asset does not itself prove ownership; rights state remains evidence-driven.

## DLA workspace

Rights holders can prepare DLA candidates, define requested permission scope, term, territory, channels, transfer/sublicense rules and economic schedule references. Activation follows ALG-003 and cannot bypass issuer-authority, policy or licensee-eligibility gates.

## LEX offer management

Eligible DLA scopes can be projected into LEX offers after ALG-006. The surface shows offer version, available scope, listing state and transaction status without converting market demand or payment success into rights truth.

## Usage and downstream licensing

Show active licensees, sublicenses/derivative grants, usage caps, current entitlement state and revocation/suspension lineage. Rights Graph conservation prevents descendants from regaining rights removed upstream.

## Economics

Compose Split Tree, Royalty/Commission Statement, Fee Rule Version, Settlement/Reconciliation Status and Refund/Clawback State. Calculated allocations remain distinct from settled provider payouts.

## Disputes and remedies

Rights holders can open/view governed cases, submit evidence references, track holds/appeals and see authorized remedies. The surface does not allow unilateral deletion of conflicting claims or adverse evidence.

## Protected IP boundary

Unpublished works, master assets, trade-secret deal terms and confidential contracts stay in protected custody. Public LEX projections expose only the metadata and rights/economic terms approved for disclosure.

## Tests

Test verified/disputed source rights, expired issuer authority, DLA amendment, attempted overscope sublicense, listing after revocation, royalty mismatch, confidential terms, duplicate provider aliases and cross-tenant asset isolation.

## Versioning

Asset, rights, DLA, offer and schedule versions are first-class. Historical licenses and settlements remain reproducible after terms change.
