## Purpose

The Agreement & Entitlement Layer separates two institutional facts that are often incorrectly collapsed:

1. **an agreement exists and was accepted under a specific version**, and
2. **a subject currently possesses a usable entitlement created by that agreement plus valid rights, policy and economic conditions**.

```text
AGREEMENT ACCEPTED
      !=
ENTITLEMENT ACTIVE
      !=
PROVIDER ACCESS DELIVERED
```

This separation is necessary across DLA licensing, LEX transactions, subscriptions, downloads, bookings, member access, API plans and other CrownThrive rights-bearing experiences.

## Agreement object responsibilities

The layer records or references:

- agreement stable ID/version;
- parties and their canonical identities;
- DLA or source-right references;
- agreement type;
- accepted terms/version;
- acceptance/assent evidence;
- effective period;
- amendment/supersession state;
- jurisdiction/governing terms where applicable;
- economic schedule references;
- transfer/sublicensing restrictions;
- required approvals;
- privacy/classification;
- DAIL event references.

The agreement body can remain in protected legal/evidence custody. Public/default projections should expose only permitted metadata.

## Entitlement responsibilities

An entitlement represents the current permission or benefit the subject can actually exercise. A canonical entitlement should include:


## Activation rule

A conservative entitlement predicate is:

```text
ENTITLEMENT_ACTIVE =
  agreement_effective
AND DLA_or_rights_valid
AND subject_eligible
AND required_policy_passed
AND required_economic_conditions_reconciled
AND not_revoked_or_suspended
AND within_term
AND capacity_remaining_or_explicitly_unlimited
```

Unknown required state routes to HOLD.

## Agreement acceptance

Acceptance evidence must identify the exact version accepted, subject identity, time, method and relevant agreement context. A checkbox, provider account or payment can be acceptance evidence only when the governing legal/contract flow defines it that way; documentation must not infer assent from mere usage.

## Entitlement creation

Entitlement creation is a governed state transition. It may follow DLA activation, LEX fulfillment, subscription/booking confirmation or another authorized workflow. Provider fulfillment should attach as an alias/evidence record after or alongside canonical entitlement creation, not replace it.

## Provider separation

Examples of provider state that may attach to an entitlement:

- storage/download delivery ID;
- streaming/account permission ID;
- booking-provider confirmation;
- payment/subscription ID;
- external LMS/course enrollment;
- API plan/client alias.

These are execution aliases. If the provider changes, the entitlement identity and historical agreement lineage remain stable.

## Revocation and suspension

Revocation/suspension can originate from DLA lifecycle, agreement termination, case/remedy, credential/policy state, refund/reversal rules or other adopted authority. The entitlement history is append-only; current provider access must reconcile to the effective institutional state.

## Capacity and usage

Finite entitlements can track seats, downloads, uses, API calls, entries or other quantified permissions. Capacity follows the source license/agreement and cannot be increased by the fulfillment provider. Explicit unlimited semantics must be represented intentionally; unknown is not unlimited.

## Renewal and amendment

A renewal or amendment creates a new agreement/DLA/entitlement version or an explicit extension event according to the governing contract. The layer must preserve which terms controlled each historical period/use.

## UI projections

Primary reusable surfaces:

- DLA Status Card;
- Usage/Territory/Term Summary;
- Entitlement Panel;
- Consent/assent evidence where applicable;
- Fee Rule Version;
- Settlement/Reconciliation Status;
- Correction/Supersession History.

## Security/privacy

- protect confidential agreement bodies;
- enforce subject/object/tenant access;
- never expose payment credentials;
- prevent provider alias from being used as entitlement authority;
- validate revocation/currentness on consequential use;
- make access changes idempotent and auditable.

## Failure states

Agreement version mismatch, missing assent evidence, DLA revoked, policy HOLD, payment mismatch, entitlement/provider drift, usage cap reached, term expired, provider access missing despite active entitlement, provider access remaining after entitlement revocation.

## Tests

Test accepted agreement with entitlement HOLD; active entitlement with provider outage; provider access without canonical entitlement; amendment; renewal; refund-triggered suspension; cap exhaustion; explicit unlimited; cross-tenant entitlement lookup; and provider migration.

## Versioning

Agreement, DLA, entitlement and provider fulfillment versions are separate institutional objects. The layer never rewrites prior terms to match current provider or policy state.
