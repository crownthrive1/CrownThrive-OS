## Smart Treasury, Oracle Lattice and External Evidence Lineage

Historical CHLOM treats treasury automation and external evidence as core infrastructure. The **Smart Treasury** manages economic obligations and allocation; the **Oracle Lattice** connects protocol decisions to external reality.

Modern CHLOM preserves those functions while separating provider evidence, financial authority, policy decisions and actual settlement.

## Historical Smart Treasury

Recovered papers describe a treasury capable of receiving license/network/marketplace fees, routing royalties and stakeholder splits, collecting/reserving taxes, funding grants/development, maintaining reserves/insurance, paying validators/oracles/contributors, implementing governance budgets, and potentially allocating eligible assets to approved yield strategies.

The Grand Prospectus extends the family into **Smart Tax, Smart Capital and Smart Yield** concepts.

## Modern treasury principle

Current economic truth is not created by provider success alone.

```text
provider payment/delivery event
→ evidence
→ rights/economic schedule check
→ policy/commerce reconciliation
→ allocation/obligation record
→ settlement authorization
→ settlement evidence
→ correction/reversal if necessary
```

A payment, ad-delivery report, marketplace event or booking receipt cannot independently create a royalty, commission, entitlement, payout, invoice or recognized settlement unless the governing rules allow it.

## Historical Smart Tax

The papers explore automated tax withholding/routing. Current boundary: tax classification and remittance are jurisdictional/professional matters. Historical pseudocode or example percentages are not current tax advice or tax rates.

## Historical Smart Capital and Smart Yield

Smart Capital describes programmatic allocation into operations, reserves, incentives, grants, growth and strategic funding. Smart Yield explores eligible treasury assets in approved yield opportunities.

Current state: these are research/design concepts unless separately activated. No source paper authorizes live DeFi investment, autonomous rebalancing, custody or money movement.

## Oracle Lattice — historical concept

The Genesis Volume uses **Oracle Lattice** for a network of external data providers whose information can be cross-checked rather than trusting one source.

Historical oracle domains include sanctions/watchlists, regulatory changes, identity/KYC, corporate registries, financial/market data, exchange rates, content/IP fingerprints, IoT/supply-chain sensors, court/regulator orders, AI/ML risk signals and audit/solvency evidence.

## Why a lattice instead of one oracle

Historical CHLOM contemplates multiple sources, source weighting/reputation, corroboration, disagreement handling, staking/slashing, batched commitments, proof references and governance-approved source lists.

Modern CHLOM retains the same institutional goal using an **External Evidence / Oracle Gateway**.

## Modern external-evidence contract

Every external observation should identify source/provider, environment/account where applicable, observation time, freshness/expiry, subject/object, claim type, evidence reference/digest, confidence/source quality where meaningful, authorization scope, contradiction state, downstream use allowed and correction/revocation behavior.

Provider evidence is evidence — not sovereign truth.

## AI and regulatory-feed oracles

AI may classify or summarize evidence and produce bounded risk/advisory outputs, but it cannot fabricate observations, refresh stale evidence, or upgrade HOLD/DENY to PASS by confidence alone.

Regulatory-feed oracles should open a policy delta for review rather than automatically rewriting institutional law.

## Oracle-to-DAIL flow

```text
external source
→ authenticated observation
→ normalization
→ source/freshness check
→ contradiction check
→ evidence reference
→ DAIL event
→ policy/rights/eligibility use
→ decision receipt
```

Later cryptographic anchoring may add proof without changing the evidence semantics.
