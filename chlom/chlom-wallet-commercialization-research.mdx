# CHLOM Wallet commercialization research

**State:** `RESEARCH / PRICE_REVIEW`  
**Live offer:** none created by this packet  
**Stripe Product/Price:** none created by this packet  
**Independent price certification:** pending

CHLOM Wallet should not compete as another commodity embedded-wallet wrapper. Public market pricing shows that basic wallet creation and authentication are increasingly inexpensive. The premium CrownThrive product therefore has to be the governed convergence layer: rights, policy, evidence, Unified Value Receipts, impact routing, provider portability and cross-corridor integration.

## Current public benchmarks

As of August 22, 2026:

| Provider | Public developer pricing signal | Strategic implication |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Privy | Free through 499 MAU; Core $299/month for 500–2,499 MAU; Scale $499/month for 2,500–9,999 MAU; enterprise custom | Wallet/auth alone cannot justify arbitrary premium pricing |
| Crossmint | 1,000 monthly active wallets free; overages start at $0.05 per active wallet; advanced/enterprise features custom | Basic wallets are becoming a low-cost infrastructure layer |

Sources:

- `https://www.privy.io/pricing`
- `https://www.crossmint.com/pricing`

These are dated public market observations, not quotes guaranteed for CrownThrive and not evidence of equivalent feature scope.

## CHLOM differentiation stack

A CHLOM commercial package earns premium positioning only when it bundles material CrownThrive-specific value such as:

1. rights and entitlement state rather than payment status alone;
2. Unified Value Receipts spanning Money, Rights, Rewards and Impact;
3. versioned economic-schedule and obligation evidence;
4. ThriveFund obligation/evidence routing;
5. append-only reconciliation and proof receipts;
6. provider/chain portability above vendor-native identifiers;
7. CHLOM policy and DAIL integration;
8. API/MCP integration with CrownThrive's governed agent model;
9. enterprise white-label policy and rights workflows;
10. support/dispute/recovery evidence packages.

## Internal price-review boundary

Restricted research candidates now exist for Sandbox, Builder, Pro, Institutional and Enterprise tiers. Exact candidate figures remain an internal pricing experiment until the independent CrownThrive Price Certifier reviews value, costs, margins, market comparables, support burden, usage assumptions and legal/tax implications.

Public documentation therefore does not convert the internal research figures into an offer.

## Recommended commercial architecture

The current pricing hypothesis should use a hybrid model:

- **free/sandbox** to make integration friction low;
- **flat recurring access** for predictable WalletKit developer tiers;
- **usage components later** for expensive proof, signature, provider or high-volume operations only after metering evidence exists;
- **enterprise license + service** for white-label rights/policy/impact implementations;
- **provider pass-through costs** separated from CrownThrive software value where applicable;
- **no hidden token purchase requirement**.

This structure keeps the end-user experience simple while making infrastructure economics auditable.

## Certification criteria

A price should not move from `price_review` to an effective offer until the independent certifier has evidence for:

- target customer and buyer value;
- competitor/comparable set and as-of dates;
- direct provider and infrastructure cost;
- support and compliance burden;
- gross-margin target;
- usage assumptions and abuse controls;
- annual discount logic;
- taxes and geographic restrictions where applicable;
- included quotas/overages;
- entitlement and fulfillment cost;
- cancellation/refund terms;
- release and checkout authority.

Market positioning is now sufficiently defined for continued product engineering, but price publication and Stripe object creation remain separate gates.
