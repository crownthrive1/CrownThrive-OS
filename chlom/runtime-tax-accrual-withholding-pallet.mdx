## Professional boundary

Tax is a high-stakes legal/accounting domain. Historical automation design does not authorize current automated tax conclusions.

```text
historical Smart Tax design
      !=
current tax advice
      !=
automatic filing/remittance authority
```

Current P-11 may store and execute tax/withholding records only where applicable rules, professional/legal review and authority are resolved.

## Rule versioning

```text
transaction at T
   |
use jurisdiction/rule version effective at T
```

Later tax-rule changes do not silently rewrite historical records unless a governed correction applies.

## Withholding versus tax

Withholding is an amount retained/remitted under a rule; it is not identical to the recipient's final tax liability.

```text
withheld amount != final tax owed
```

## Failure cases

### Jurisdiction unresolved

HOLD tax-sensitive automation; do not guess.

### Rule version stale

HOLD/review until current/effective rule is verified.

### Provider withholds amount different from expected

Record provider evidence and reconcile; do not silently overwrite expected obligation.

### Historical tax rate found in paper

Preserve historical source only; do not apply to current transactions.

## Security/privacy

Tax records can contain highly sensitive personal/financial data. Public documentation exposes architecture only. Restricted data is limited to authorized finance/legal workflows.
