# CHLOM Terminology and Acronym Lineage

CHLOM has evolved across multiple whitepapers, technical compendia, Help Center eras, implementation plans, and reconstructed institutional records. Phase 2.7 makes terminology drift explicit.

## DLA

Historical CHLOM materials use **DLA** for **Decentralized Licensing Authority**. The current reconstructed CrownThrive operating model uses **DLA** for **Dynamic Licensing Asset**.

These are not interchangeable meanings.

- Historical meaning should remain attached to its original papers and architecture period.
- Current machine-readable records should use Dynamic Licensing Asset unless an ADR states otherwise.
- Agents must resolve context and effective date before interpreting the acronym.

## DAIL / DAL

Legacy source families contain variations around data/audit/information-ledger terminology. The current institutional architecture reserves **DAIL** for the durable event/evidence/audit-ledger function used by Phase 3.

Any historical DAL or variant term should be preserved as an alias or predecessor, not silently rewritten in the original source.

## LEX

LEX remains associated with the licensing exchange / marketplace concept. Historical decentralized or token-oriented implementation assumptions should be treated as target/research architecture unless current implementation evidence exists.

## ACE

The strongest recovered historical expansion is **Adaptive Compliance Engine**. The Genesis Volume uses ACE for the rule/compliance guardian that consumes intelligence, applies policy, flags or quarantines risk, and coordinates enforcement. Later descriptive phrases such as automated compliance evaluator/engine remain source-specific language unless separately adopted as a rename.

## AIE

The source corpus contains a material acronym reuse:

- **AI Intelligence Engine** — explicit Genesis Volume meaning for the broader ML/risk/predictive intelligence layer feeding ACE.
- **Anomaly Intelligence Engine** — later CrownThrive/CHLOM reuse for anomaly and outlier-monitoring capabilities.

Both meanings remain recoverable and machine-distinct. A later founder adjudication may retain both with namespaced IDs, rename one family, or establish a predecessor/child relationship. Until then, agents must resolve source family and effective period before interpreting `AIE`.

## ADE

The source corpus also contains a material ADE reuse:

- **Autonomous Decision Engine** — explicit Genesis Volume meaning for the actuator that executes eligible policy/governance outcomes.
- **Attribution & Distribution Engine** — later CrownThrive/CHLOM reuse for attribution, split calculation, revenue routing and distribution/settlement logic.

These concepts are not interchangeable. Their histories are preserved pending founder adjudication.

## TLaaS and related research

Two explicit historical expansions are now recovered:

- **Tokenized Licensing as a Service** — Technical Blueprint / Building and Understanding source family, focused on reusable issuance and management of tokenized licenses.
- **Trust Layer as a Service** — Grand Prospectus source family, focused on exposing CHLOM trust/compliance/licensing capabilities through APIs, SDKs, plug-ins and service workflows.

They are related but not identical concepts. Both remain in the historical/research register unless an ADR or founder adjudication establishes their current relationship.

## Canonical acronym record

Every important acronym should eventually carry:

- acronym
- current expansion
- historical expansions
- effective dates
- source family
- stable concept ID
- predecessor/successor links
- current status
- implementation evidence
- public-safe definition

## CaaS / dS-CaaS

Public CrownThrive material has used **Compliance-as-a-Service (CaaS)** in connection with CHLOM, while the current institutional documentation also preserves **dS-CaaS** as a distinct governance/framework label. Until an ADR establishes the exact relationship and expansion, agents must not assume these terms are synonyms.

## MVP namespace collision

**MVP** may refer to **Melanated Voices Platform** in the CrownThrive media ecosystem, while ordinary software language uses MVP for **minimum viable product**. Machine-readable records must namespace the term so media assets and software maturity states cannot collide.

## Token and economic notation lineage

Historical CHLOM papers describe **CHLOM Coin** as a utility/network-fuel token and **CHM** as a governance/compliance token. Portions of the Grand Prospectus also use **CHLM** shorthand/native-token notation. No third current token is inferred from that notation drift, and no historical token supply, sale, staking, burn, dividend or governance mechanic is a current production claim without separate adoption and legal/technical activation.

## Studio and app naming drift

Terms such as **ThriveStudio / CrownThrive Studios / AdLuxe Studio** and **ThriveApps / CrownApps** must be resolved through the Legacy, Alias and Successor Register rather than by string similarity. They may represent brands, service engines, predecessors or separate assets.

## Rule

Terminology changes are corrections or evolutions in the institutional record. They are not permission to erase older meanings from source history. A later founder adjudication changes the effective current interpretation through an append-only correction/supersession record; it does not rewrite the original paper or erase an older acronym expansion.
