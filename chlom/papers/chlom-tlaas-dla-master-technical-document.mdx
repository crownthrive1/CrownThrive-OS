## Recovery statement

This title is recovered exactly from the historical corpus. The title itself does not spell out `TLAAS`, so this record does not pretend the lost body is available. Related Technical Blueprint material explicitly defines **Tokenized Licensing as a Service** around DLA smart contracts/APIs, while the Grand Prospectus separately defines **Trust Layer as a Service**. The DLA qualifier makes the tokenized-licensing lineage the strongest related source mapping, but the acronym collision remains documented rather than silently erased.

## Historical subsystem decomposition

A plausible source-supported decomposition includes:

1. **Identity adapter** — DID/Fingerprint/credential resolution.
2. **Rights/issuer resolution** — determine whether licensing authority exists.
3. **DLA lifecycle service** — apply, approve, issue, renew, suspend, revoke.
4. **License registry** — maintain canonical or source-era license status/metadata.
5. **Token/representation adapter** — optional NFT/SBT or chain representation.
6. **Compliance pre-check** — apply rules, risk and eligibility conditions.
7. **Oracle adapter** — ingest required off-chain evidence.
8. **LEX connector** — expose eligible licenses to controlled exchange functions.
9. **Economic router** — route fees/royalties/settlement according to adopted schedules.
10. **Audit/event recorder** — preserve evidence and lifecycle history.

## Master object boundaries

### Rights object

Represents source rights and their evidence.

### Provider representation

## LEX adapter

LEX can only expose licenses/rights that are eligible for transfer/listing.

```text

+ transferability permits
+ issuer/holder authority resolved
```
