## DID versus identity

```text
DID != person
DID != organization automatically
DID control != institutional authority
key != identity
credential != role
```

A DID is a portable cryptographic identifier representation linked to a stable institutional identity.

## Fingerprint ID relationship

Historical CHLOM links DIDs to Fingerprint identity concepts. Current mapping keeps the distinction:

```text

```

## Credential relationship

A DID can identify an issuer or subject, but does not make a credential trustworthy by itself. Current verification requires issuer authority, signature/proof, status/currentness and subject binding.

## Recovery and rotation

Current-safe recovery requires explicit authorization and evidence. Key loss or compromise should rotate/revoke cryptographic controls without erasing the underlying institutional identity or historical events.

## Privacy
