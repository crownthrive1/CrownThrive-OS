## Recovery statement

The recovered title proves that CHLOM documented a dedicated TLaaS/LEX technical artifact. Related sources explicitly describe LEX as a marketplace for tokenized licenses and rights, with compliance checks, sublicensing, escrow/settlement, royalties and identity eligibility. The exact historical body is not authenticated, so this article reconstructs the source-supported system contract rather than pretending to reproduce the lost text.

## Rights-first invariant

```text
LEX can transact only what the licensor/holder is authorized to transact.
```

A marketplace listing does not create rights and cannot make a non-transferable license transferable.

## Listing eligibility

A listing candidate should resolve:

- canonical DLA/right reference;
- current owner/holder or authorized listing actor;
- transferability/sublicensing permission;
- territory and term constraints;
- exclusivity/conflict state;
- counterparty requirements;
- economic schedule;
- required agreement/consent;
- evidence freshness;
- applicable policy.

## Offer and agreement

An accepted offer is not sufficient by itself. The system must also establish the required agreement/assent and the exact rights being conveyed.

## Escrow / atomicity lineage

Historical sources describe trustless/atomic settlement where payment and license transfer complete together or revert.

```text

 + validate final rights state
 + execute institutional transfer/sublicense
 + activate entitlement
 + capture/release consideration
 + reconcile provider records
```

When true atomicity is unavailable across providers, the system needs compensation/rollback logic.

## Failure modes

- DLA inactive -> listing HOLD/DENY;
- no transfer rights -> DENY;
- seller not authorized -> DENY;
- buyer qualification stale -> HOLD;
- payment succeeds, transfer fails -> compensate/reconcile;
- provider token transfer succeeds, canonical DLA fails -> institutional transfer incomplete;
- overlapping exclusive right -> HOLD;
- sublicense exceeds parent scope -> DENY;
- economic schedule missing -> HOLD;
- provider listing exists after DLA revocation -> adapter must delist/hold and reconcile.
