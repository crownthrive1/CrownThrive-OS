## Three-valued compliance

Current CHLOM makes uncertainty explicit:

```text
PASS = mandatory resolved conditions satisfied
HOLD = required evidence/authority/policy unresolved or stale
DENY = authoritative prohibition/failure established
```

This current semantic model is not claimed as the paper's exact historical formalism unless later source confirms it.

## Adaptive behavior

`Adaptive` can describe policy selection or response changing with context such as jurisdiction, role, asset, risk, current evidence or environment. It does **not** mean ACE may rewrite policy or self-expand authority.

```text
adaptive evaluation != self-legislating policy
```

## Policy source boundary

ACE consumes source-authorized rules from Compliance Packs / Rules Registry / governing records.

```text
AI/model output != policy authority
provider flag != institutional rule automatically
```

## Evidence relationship

ACE can evaluate credentials, DLA state, oracle observations, sanctions/risk evidence, ZK proofs, provider readback and professional review. Evidence missing from a mandatory control should not silently evaluate as PASS.

## Risk/AIE relationship

AIE/risk models may supply intelligence or anomaly evidence. A risk score is not itself a finding or prohibition unless the adopted policy gives it that effect.

## Human/professional gate
