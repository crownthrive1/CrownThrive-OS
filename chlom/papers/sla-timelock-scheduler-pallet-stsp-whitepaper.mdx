## Timelock boundary

A timelock delays execution; it does not create authority.

```text
approval + valid authority + timelock elapsed
   -> execution candidate
```

If authority, policy, rights or evidence changes during the delay, execution-time revalidation may invalidate the prior authorization.

## SLA boundary

SLA metrics measure agreed service conditions. They do not automatically create contractual penalties or remediation unless the adopted agreement/profile says so.

```text
measured breach != remedy authority automatically
```
