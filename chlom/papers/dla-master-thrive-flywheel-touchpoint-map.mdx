## Touchpoint classes

### Media & cultural content

Potential governed objects include content licenses, distribution windows, performance/display rights, syndication terms, creator/partner permissions and derivative-use restrictions.

### Commerce & products

Licensing touchpoints may include brand/IP usage, reseller/wholesale permissions, product/campaign rights and marketplace eligibility. Economic activation is separate from rights authorization.

### Booking & professional services

The license layer may record professional/brand/service permissions where source evidence supports them. CHLOM does not substitute for government-issued professional licensing.

### Advertising & distribution

Ad inventory, campaign assets, publisher relationships and brand-use rights can carry DLA terms; provider ad-serving success does not create those rights.

### Community / affiliate / ambassador

Referral, promotional, content-use or program permissions can be modeled as governed rights with term/scope rather than informal access assumptions.

### Education & training

Course content, instructor rights, certificates and redistribution permissions can be represented through explicit rights/credential objects.

### Physical / place-based operations

MM Suites and other place-based corridors can use CHLOM records for brand/license/operating permissions while keeping leases, professional credentials and legal obligations source-controlled.

## Rights-first invariant

```text
MONETIZATION <= AUTHORIZED USE <= LICENSED RIGHTS <= SOURCE RIGHTS
```

A revenue opportunity cannot enlarge the rights grant.

## Platform/provider boundary

Each CrownThrive brand/platform can use native providers, but provider records are execution evidence only.

```text
provider says "active"
        !=
institutional DLA active
        !=
source rights proven
```

The relevant states must reconcile.

## ThriveEvergreen boundary

When a licensed action becomes an economic action, current CrownThrive doctrine routes economic authorization through ThriveEvergreen where applicable. ThriveEvergreen may optimize execution inside authority; it may not manufacture licensing authority.

## CIE relationship

For media/cultural assets, the Cultural Imprint Engine can classify/canonicalize narrative and imprint treatment. CIE classification does not replace source rights or DLA permission.

```text
CIE says what/where it belongs
CHLOM says what rights/authority apply
ThriveEvergreen governs economic activation
DAIL preserves institutional evidence
```

## DAIL relationship

Current DAIL records material licensing lifecycle, approval, correction, transfer and evidence events. Historical DAL labels are never substituted for current DAIL.
