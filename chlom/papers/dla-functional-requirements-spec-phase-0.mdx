## Identity requirements

**DLA-FR-ID-01** — The applicant/holder must resolve to an institutional identity.

**DLA-FR-ID-02** — Required credentials must be verifiable without assuming that account possession equals identity.

**DLA-FR-ID-03** — Identity state must support revocation/rotation/change without silently changing license ownership semantics.

## Authority requirements

**DLA-FR-AUTH-01** — Issuance requires source-backed authority.

**DLA-FR-AUTH-02** — Authority must be scoped to the asset/catalog, rights, territory, actions and term actually delegated.

**DLA-FR-AUTH-03** — Delegation cannot expand the principal's authority.

Current normalization:

```text
ISSUER_AUTHORITY <= LICENSABLE_SOURCE_RIGHTS <= SOURCE_RIGHTS
```

## Rights requirements

**DLA-FR-RGT-01** — Permissions and restrictions must be explicit.

**DLA-FR-RGT-02** — Transferability and sublicensing are independent fields/decisions, not implied rights.

**DLA-FR-RGT-03** — Exclusive rights require collision/overlap analysis.

**DLA-FR-RGT-04** — Child/sub-license rights may not exceed parent/source rights.

## Eligibility requirements

Historical sources support credential, AI/risk, oracle and policy prechecks. Current treatment requires three-valued outcomes where evidence is unresolved:

```text
PASS -> may continue
HOLD -> requires resolution/review
DENY -> may not continue
```

A missing fact is not a PASS.

## Issuance requirements

An approved license record should include enough information to determine:

```text
who
what asset/right
which permissions
which restrictions
where
when
which transfer/sublicense rules
which evidence/policies
which issuer authority
which version/status
```

Historical NFT/SBT representation is preserved as an implementation pattern, not a current mandatory form.

## Lifecycle requirements

```text
candidate
 -> review
 -> active
 -> renewed|amended
 -> held|suspended
 -> reactivated
 -> expired|revoked|terminated|superseded
```

Every transition must have an attributable reason/evidence reference and authorized actor/service.

## Transfer requirements

**DLA-FR-XFER-01** — Transfer must be allowed by the active license.

**DLA-FR-XFER-02** — The recipient must satisfy holder/eligibility conditions.

**DLA-FR-XFER-03** — Transfer may require approval where governing terms require it.

**DLA-FR-XFER-04** — Economic settlement success cannot bypass rights/eligibility checks.

## Sublicensing requirements

```text
requested_subrights
  <= parent_license_sublicensable_rights
  <= issuer/source_rights
```

Term and territory intersections must also remain within the parent grant.

## Compliance requirements

Historical DLA descriptions integrate AI risk checking, sanctions/external data and automated compliance. Current CHLOM separates:

- Rules Registry;
- Approval Service;
- evidence/oracles;
- risk intelligence;
- human/governed review where required.

No model is itself the legal authority.

## Governance requirements

Exceptional action must be bounded, attributable, reviewable and non-self-escalating. Historical broad DAO/council descriptions are treated as design lineage unless current governance evidence activates them.

## Failure modes

The implementation must not silently:

- issue with unresolved rights;
- treat provider IDs as licenses;
- reactivate revoked authority without evidence;
- convert a failed/unknown check into PASS;
- duplicate a license without version/supersession logic;
- let payment settlement create entitlement where license activation failed.
