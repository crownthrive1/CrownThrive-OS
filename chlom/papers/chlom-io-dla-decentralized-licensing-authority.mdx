## What the recovered title proves

The lost Help Center contained a dedicated CHLOM.io-facing article for the historical **Decentralized Licensing Authority**. The title proves a documented product/surface concept existed. It does not prove that every described service was deployed publicly or remains available at CHLOM.io today.

## Historical product intent

Related CHLOM sources describe the DLA as the user/system-facing authority for:

- applying for a license or credential;
- validating prerequisites;
- issuing a smart license/token;
- recording ownership/holder state;
- checking license validity;
- renewing or amending licenses;
- suspending/revoking invalid or noncompliant licenses;
- supporting sublicensing/transfer where permitted;
- exposing an integration point to applications and LEX.

## Historical verification contract

A safe reconstruction of the historical verification question is:

```text
Is this license authentic,
active,
within term,
within scope,
held by the expected subject,
and usable for the requested action?
```

## Current correction

The current system identity is materially different:

```text
CURRENT DLA
= Dynamic Licensing Asset

CURRENT AUTHORITY
= Licensing Stewardship / Issuer Authority
```

The current CHLOM.io architecture should therefore present the license **object** separately from who has authority to create or manage it.

## Required current-safe UI disclosures

Any surface reviving the historical title should make clear:

- `DLA` now means Dynamic Licensing Asset;
- historical Decentralized Licensing Authority is lineage, not current naming;
- a license record does not manufacture legal rights;
- automated decisions operate only inside evidenced authority;
- external provider success is not institutional licensing truth;
- unresolved source-rights/authority questions fail closed.
