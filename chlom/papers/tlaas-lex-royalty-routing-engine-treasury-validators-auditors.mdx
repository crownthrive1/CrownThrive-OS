## Provider execution

Stripe, crypto rails, wallets or other providers are execution systems, not economic truth.

```text

 != institutional settlement until reconciled
```

## Partial failure

If one recipient payment fails:

- preserve the authorized schedule;
- do not recalculate recipients silently;
- place failed leg in HOLD/retry;
- reconcile successful legs;
- compensate only under adopted rules.

## Refunds / clawbacks

Refunds, disputes or reversals should generate explicit inverse/correction records rather than deleting the original distribution.
