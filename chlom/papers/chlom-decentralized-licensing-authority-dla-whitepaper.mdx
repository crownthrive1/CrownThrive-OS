## Historical doctrine recovered from related sources

Historical CHLOM described the Decentralized Licensing Authority as an on-chain licensing authority composed of smart-contract/governance mechanisms for:

- license application and eligibility checking;
- issuance of tokenized license/credential objects;
- immutable or tamper-evident license records;
- lifecycle state such as active, suspended, expired and revoked;
- identity/credential prerequisites;
- AI/compliance prechecks;
- hierarchical parent-child licensing and sublicensing;
- transfer constraints;
- enforcement hooks;
- governance/override pathways;
- integration with LEX, oracles and economic routing.

Related source bodies explicitly describe DLA issuance, revocation, smart-license terms, chain-of-title/ownership updates, Fingerprint-linked identity and lifecycle automation.
