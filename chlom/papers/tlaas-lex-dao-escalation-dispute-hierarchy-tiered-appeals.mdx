## Independence

A reviewer that made the original material decision should not automatically be the sole final appeal authority. Conflicts of interest must be resolved under the active governance profile.

## Historical DAO boundary

```text
historical DAO escalation

```

Current authority comes from the institutional governance contract effective for the case.

## Evidence standard

An appeal should reference the exact evidence used below, any new evidence, currentness, and which finding/rule is challenged.

## HOLD during appeal

A disputed DLA/listing may remain active, be temporarily held, or be restricted depending on the adopted remedy profile and risk. Appeal itself does not automatically stay every action.

## LEX propagation

If a dispute affects transferability, entitlement or listing validity:

```text
case decision
 -> canonical DLA/right state
 -> LEX listing/transaction state
 -> entitlement/provider state
 -> reconciliation
```

## Remedies

Possible profile-controlled remedies include:

- no action;
- warning/correction;
- temporary HOLD;
- suspension;
- revocation/termination;
- listing cancellation;
- entitlement correction;
- economic compensation/reversal where authorized.

## Correction

Reversed decisions are superseded, not erased. The original decision and appeal remain part of institutional history.

## Failure modes

- conflicted reviewer participates improperly -> HOLD/review;
- appeal window ambiguous -> HOLD until profile resolved;
- historical DAO rule used as current authority -> reject;
- provider asset delisted before institutional remedy authority -> reconcile;
- corrected case leaves stale LEX/entitlement state -> propagation incident.
