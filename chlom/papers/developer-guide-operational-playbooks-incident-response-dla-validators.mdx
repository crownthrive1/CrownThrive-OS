## Purpose

This article preserves the operational problem represented by the recovered title: if validators or assurance nodes participate in DLA/cross-chain verification, the system needs repeatable procedures for faults, suspected compromise, conflicting observations, downtime and evidence correction.

## Incident classes

High-level classes include:

- validator unavailable;
- invalid or expired signer;
- equivocation/conflicting signed observations;
- suspected key compromise;
- source-network finality/reorg problem;
- stale oracle/source data;
- quorum degradation;
- replay/duplicate claim;
- bridge mismatch;
- policy/configuration drift;
- evidence omission;
- contaminated downstream license/entitlement projection.

## Detection

Signals can come from observability, cross-validator disagreement, signature verification, freshness checks, downstream reconciliation failures or human reports.

## Triage

Assess:

```yaml
triage:
  scope: single_validator|validator_set|source_network|bridge|downstream_state
  impact: informational|degraded|material|critical
  active_exploitation_suspected: boolean
  affected_claims: []
  affected_dla_or_entitlements: []
  safe_containment_available: boolean
```

Exact severity thresholds belong to adopted operational policy.

## Containment

Prefer bounded containment over destructive action:

- stop accepting the affected validator's new observations;
- place affected claims/actions in HOLD;
- pause a specific bridge/import path;
- rotate/revoke compromised credentials when authorized;
- preserve unaffected services where safe.

Do not grant new authority during emergency response.

## Evidence preservation

Preserve signed messages, source refs, timestamps, config versions, logs, policy versions and all institutional decisions needed to reconstruct what occurred. Restricted logs remain private; public records can carry safe hashes/refs.

## Recovery

Potential recovery steps include replacing/rotating a validator identity, restoring a known-good configuration, re-reading canonical source state, rebuilding projections and re-running affected approval decisions.

## Reconciliation

Every potentially contaminated downstream state must be enumerated and checked rather than assuming validator recovery automatically repairs prior results.

```text
incident window
   |
affected observations
   |
claims
   |
DLA / entitlement / LEX / evidence projections
   |
re-evaluate
   |
keep | hold | correct | revoke/supersede
```

## Correction

Institutional records are corrected through explicit supersession. Prior evidence is retained; a correction records why the old state is no longer current.

## Post-incident review

Review root cause, detection latency, containment effectiveness, missed dependencies, policy defects, required tests and whether authority boundaries held.

## Communication boundary

Public incident disclosure must not expose credentials, exploitable details or protected partner data. Founder/human action labels should be used only when human action is actually required.
