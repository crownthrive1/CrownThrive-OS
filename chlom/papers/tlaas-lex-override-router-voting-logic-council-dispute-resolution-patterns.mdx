## Override router purpose

An override router is a controlled dispatcher for exceptional actions that need higher authority than ordinary lifecycle automation.

Potential action classes include:

- temporary hold;
- license suspension;
- revocation request;
- transfer block;
- marketplace delisting/hold;
- evidence quarantine;
- emergency provider pause;
- dispute escalation;
- correction/restoration.

## Emergency actions

A bounded emergency action should be:

- narrow in scope;
- time-limited where feasible;
- independently evidenced;
- logged immediately;
- non-self-escalating;
- subject to required after-the-fact review/ratification;
- reversible or compensable where technically possible.

```text
emergency authority != permanent authority expansion
```

## SAFE_HOLD pattern

When facts are materially unresolved but continuation could cause harm:

```text
unresolved high-risk condition
 -> SAFE_HOLD
 -> evidence/review
 -> approve, deny, correct, restore, or expire hold
```

HOLD does not adjudicate guilt or ownership by itself.

## Dispute hierarchy

A source-compatible dispute path can be normalized as:

```text
incident / contested action
  |
case intake
  |
evidence preservation
  |
initial review
  |
remedy / HOLD / no-action
  |
appeal or governed escalation where permitted
  |
final institutional disposition
  |
DAIL correction/remedy events
```

## Conflict of interest

A voter/reviewer with a material conflict must be excluded or handled according to the applicable governance profile. Participation rights are not presumed merely from role title or token possession.

## Timelock

Non-emergency material decisions can include a timelock so affected systems can validate, challenge or prepare safe execution.

```text
approved_at + timelock <= executable_at
```

## Execution separation

```text
governance decision
 -> signed/authorized execution instruction
 -> bounded executor
 -> readback
 -> DAIL receipt
```

The voting/router layer should not directly obtain unlimited provider credentials simply because it can authorize a decision.

## Rollback and correction

If an override is later found improper:

- preserve the original override event;
- restore/correct affected state where possible;
- record the correction authority and basis;
- reconcile downstream LEX, entitlement and provider state;
- compensate economic effects when required by the adopted authority/remedy framework.

## LEX-specific use

An override can affect a listing or transaction only within authorized scope. Delisting a provider item does not revoke underlying source rights automatically; revoking a DLA does require downstream listing/entitlement propagation.

## AI relationship

AI can surface anomaly/risk evidence, prioritize review or recommend HOLD within adopted policy. It cannot cast itself as the deciding authority.

## Failure modes

- quorum formed from ineligible voters -> invalid/HOLD;
- emergency hold exceeds scope/term -> correction/escalation;
- provider pause succeeds but institutional decision fails -> provider state must reconcile;
- duplicate execution -> idempotent/no double remedy;
- evidence altered after vote -> revalidation/correction;
- AI signal treated as final authority -> governance violation.
