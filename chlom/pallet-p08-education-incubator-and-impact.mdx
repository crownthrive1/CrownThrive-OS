## Purpose

P-08 is CHLOM's capability container for **learning programs, curriculum objects, cohorts, instructor/operator roles, credentials, incubator pathways, alumni relationships and evidence-backed impact outcomes**.

The pallet distinguishes education, training and internal certification from regulated professional licensure. It also separates a curriculum's intellectual-property rights from the learner's participation/completion record.

```text
CURRICULUM / PROGRAM
       |
       v
RIGHTS + VERSION + APPROVAL
       |
       v
INSTRUCTOR / OPERATOR ELIGIBILITY
       |
       v
```

## Six Functions mapping

| Function | P-08 responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| Rights | Curriculum use, teaching, distribution and credential-issuance permissions |
| Rules | Enrollment, instructor, assessment, completion, conduct and program rules |
| Roles | Learner, instructor, mentor, cohort lead, reviewer, incubator participant, alumni |
| Revenue | Course/program/subscription/grant economics handed to authorized commerce/economic systems |
| Records | Curriculum versions, enrollments, assessments, completions, credentials, alumni and impact evidence |
| Remedies | Retake, correction, credential revocation, course removal, appeal, suspension, program exit |

## Source-component interpretation

### CrownThriveU

CrownThriveU contributes courses, cohorts, instructor-led programs, learning records and credentials. P-08 provides shared institutional semantics; it does not imply every CrownThriveU course has the same accreditation or legal effect.

### Impact Institute

Impact Institute contributes impact-oriented learning/research/program structures where verified. The pallet does not infer nonprofit, accreditation or governmental status from the word `Institute`.

### ThriveAlumni

ThriveAlumni contributes post-program community, continuing engagement, networking and governed participation pathways. Historical governance concepts remain lineage and do not automatically become current authority.

### Curriculum licensing

Curriculum is an IP asset. P-03 governs source rights/license scope; P-08 governs educational deployment, versioning and learning usage.

### Cohorts

Cohorts are versioned/time-bounded learner groups with defined program, instructors, sessions and completion criteria.

## Operating procedures

### Curriculum onboarding

1. assign curriculum ID;
2. preserve source/master content;
3. resolve P-03 ownership/license rights;
4. version curriculum;
5. define objectives and assessments;
6. classify instructor requirements;
7. review/approve exact version;
8. create delivery course/program;
9. publish public-safe metadata;
10. emit DAIL evidence.

### Instructor onboarding

1. resolve identity;
2. collect required evidence;
3. evaluate program-specific eligibility;
4. assign bounded role;
5. issue access/teaching scope;
6. monitor/review;
7. revoke/end assignment when applicable.

### Completion/credential

1. resolve enrollment/course version;
2. verify required evidence;
3. evaluate completion criteria;
4. require human review where reserved;
5. record completion;
6. issue credential under issuer authority;
7. publish verification projection if approved;
8. emit DAIL evidence.

### Credential correction/revocation

Retain original issuance, record reason/authority, update current status and propagate verification-state changes.

### Program retirement

Stop new enrollment, preserve learner histories and credential validity rules, archive/supersede curriculum and retain source rights/evidence.

## Failure modes

### Course provider says completed, assessment evidence missing

**Response:** provider completion is evidence; institutional credential can remain HOLD.

### Instructor self-claims professional license

**Response:** do not represent it as verified without authoritative evidence.

### Curriculum updated mid-cohort

**Response:** preserve cohort's governing version or apply an explicit version-transition rule.

### Payment active, enrollment suspended for policy reason

**Response:** reconcile P-06 subscription/payment with P-08 entitlement/remedy rather than forcing access from billing state.

### Credential verification endpoint fails

**Response:** do not mark credential revoked solely because projection is unavailable; distinguish service health from credential status.

### Impact metric lacks attribution basis

**Response:** report observed output only; do not make causal impact claim.

## Current implementation effect

P-08 is a **Pilot** pallet in the July 2026 Technical Build Pack. CrownThriveU, Impact Institute, ThriveAlumni, curriculum licensing and cohort systems remain subject to their own implementation and authority evidence; P-08 defines the common institutional contract.
