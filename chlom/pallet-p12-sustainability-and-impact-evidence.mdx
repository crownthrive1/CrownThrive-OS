## Purpose

P-12 is CHLOM's current capability container for **environmental/social-impact activity records, contribution evidence, project/provider evidence, impact calculations, reporting and public-claim governance**.

Its job is not to turn a receipt, badge or provider marketing claim into unqualified institutional impact truth. P-12 preserves the chain from action to evidence to attributed claim and requires explicit methodology, units, time windows and confidence/verification state.

```text
AUTHORIZED ACTIVITY / CONTRIBUTION
             |
             v
PROVIDER / PROJECT EVIDENCE
             |
             v
NORMALIZED IMPACT RECORD
             |
             v
METHODOLOGY + ATTRIBUTION + UNIT
             |
             v
CLAIM CANDIDATE
             |
             v
VERIFY / REVIEW
             |
        PASS | HOLD | DENY
             |
             v
PUBLIC / INTERNAL REPORT + DAIL
```

## Six Functions mapping

| Function | P-12 responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| Rights | Authority to use project data, badges, evidence and public claims |
| Rules | Methodology, evidence, reporting, claim, privacy and attribution rules |
| Roles | Contributor, project/provider, verifier, reviewer, beneficiary, reporting steward |
| Revenue | Link authorized purchases/contributions/funding to impact evidence without manufacturing impact |
| Records | Projects, contributions, provider evidence, units, methodologies, claims and corrections |
| Remedies | Correct, withdraw, qualify, supersede or dispute unsupported impact claims |

## Source-component interpretation

### EcoDrive

EcoDrive contributes sustainability project/provider use cases such as traceable tree planting, kelp/ocean/food/water and related impact evidence where supported by provider records. P-12 does not assume a provider's unit definitions or permanence claims without recording the applicable methodology/evidence.

### Stripe Climate

Stripe Climate contributes payments-linked climate-contribution evidence and reporting where configured. A payment-provider contribution receipt proves a payment/contribution event within its scope; it does not by itself prove physical carbon removal or a specific environmental outcome beyond the provider's supported evidence.

### Impact evidence

Impact evidence can be direct, provider-reported, independently verified, modeled or derived. The evidence class must be explicit.

### Reporting

Reports are reproducible projections over defined evidence and methodology versions.

### Claims controls

Public claims require evidence, scope and language appropriate to the underlying assurance level.

## Evidence classes

P-12 can classify evidence as:

```text
E0 — unsupported claim / source not located
E1 — self-attested internal evidence
E2 — provider receipt/report
E3 — corroborated multi-source evidence
E4 — independent verification/audit within defined scope
```

This scale is an institutional modeling example and must be formally adopted/versioned before being used as a production scoring system.

## Attribution model

P-12 distinguishes contribution attribution from causal attribution.

### Contribution attribution

```text
"CrownThrive funded/supported X under provider/project record Y"
```

can be supported by economic/provider evidence.

### Causal outcome attribution

```text
"CrownThrive caused environmental/social outcome Z"
```

requires substantially stronger methodology/evidence. P-12 must not infer it merely from contribution.

## Carbon/climate boundary

Carbon-related claims can involve concepts such as funding, removal, avoidance, offsets, credits, retirement, permanence and estimated impact. These terms are not interchangeable.

```text
climate contribution
    !=
verified carbon removal owned by CrownThrive
    !=
retired offset
    !=
net-zero status
```

unless evidence and methodology support the exact statement.

## Calculation model

A generic derived quantity can be represented as:

```text
reported_impact = f(input_evidence, methodology_version, attribution_rules)
```

P-12 does not invent `f`. The actual function must come from an adopted methodology and receive its own formula/model ID under CHLOM's quantitative registry.

## Operating procedures

### New provider/project

1. assign provider/project canonical IDs;
2. preserve provider source documentation;
3. classify project type;
4. record methodology/version;
5. determine rights to use/publicize data;
6. define evidence ingestion/readback;
7. create test records;
8. approve/hold integration;
9. publish public-safe project info where authorized;
10. emit DAIL evidence.

### Contribution reconciliation

1. receive payment/activity evidence;
2. resolve contributor/program/project;
3. deduplicate/idempotently post contribution;
4. reconcile provider confirmation;
5. update contribution status;
6. do not create quantified impact until methodology/evidence supports it.

### Impact measurement

1. freeze evidence period/as-of;
2. select exact methodology version;
3. validate inputs/units;
4. calculate or ingest supported measurement;
5. classify evidence/uncertainty;
6. review/verify as required;
7. record measurement and DAIL lineage.

### Public claim publication

1. create exact claim text;
2. bind evidence/methodology/scope;
3. verify rights/disclosure language;
4. approve exact version;
5. publish public projection;
6. read back;
7. monitor expiry/provider changes;
8. correct/withdraw when evidence changes.

### Correction/withdrawal

Preserve the original claim/report, publish the correction/supersession, propagate to dependent surfaces and retain DAIL lineage.

## Failure modes

### Provider receipt exists, project outcome unavailable

**Response:** report contribution only; do not infer outcome.

### Provider changes methodology

**Response:** create new methodology version and protect historical reproducibility.

### Different providers use same unit name differently

**Response:** keep methodology/unit namespaces separate until a validated conversion exists.

### Public badge implies stronger certification than evidence

**Response:** revise/withdraw badge projection and document exact criteria.

### Reversed/refunded contribution

**Response:** reconcile contribution state and any dependent claims under current methodology/correction rules.

### Evidence is stale

**Response:** HOLD time-sensitive claims rather than presenting old evidence as current.

## Current implementation effect

P-12 is a **Later pilot** pallet in the July 2026 Technical Build Pack. EcoDrive, Stripe Climate and other project/provider relationships retain their own current evidence/methodology state; P-12 defines the shared institutional impact-evidence and claims-control contract.
