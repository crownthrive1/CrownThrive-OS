## Non-purpose

Smart Capital does not:

- make CHLOM a lender, broker-dealer, investment adviser or securities issuer by documentation alone;
- create collateral rights not granted by the owner/agreement;
- allow AI risk scores to approve regulated financing autonomously;
- promise financing, returns or repayment;
- activate historical tokenized lending;
- allow one license to be pledged beyond its permitted rights.

## Rights/asset eligibility

Before an asset/license can support financing:

- ownership/rights must be evidenced;
- transfer/assignment/encumbrance rights must permit the contemplated use;
- existing liens/obligations must be known as required;
- DLA/licensing terms must not prohibit it;
- valuation cannot exceed source evidence without qualification;
- exclusive capacity cannot be double-pledged.

```text
capital_rights <= source/contract rights
```

## Underwriting evidence

Potential evidence classes may include:

- verified revenue history;
- contractual cash-flow rights;
- payment/settlement history;
- asset valuation evidence;
- business/operating metrics;
- identity/credential evidence;
- risk/fraud indicators;
- existing obligations;
- provider/source reliability;
- market/sector conditions.

Exact underwriting rules are proprietary/professional and not published here.

## AI/ML relationship

AIE/ML can assist with anomaly detection, forecasting or risk classification. CHLOM AI can gather evidence/explain decisions. But:

```text
model score != credit decision authority
```

Regulated/high-impact decisions require the appropriate authorized human/professional entity and explainable/adverse-action/remedy processes where applicable.

## Repayment/revenue-share routing

If an agreement validly authorizes automatic allocation from a revenue stream:

```text
reconciled revenue event
 -> authorized repayment schedule
 -> repayment obligation
 -> provider execution
 -> reconciliation
 -> remaining distributable revenue
```

Repayment logic must not redirect unrelated revenue or exceed the agreement.

## Valuation

Valuation may use source-backed methods but should preserve:

- valuation date;
- methodology;
- assumptions;
- source data;
- range/uncertainty;
- reviewer/provider identity;
- whether the value is internal, third-party, appraisal or model estimate.

```text
model valuation != guaranteed liquidation value
```

## Default/remedies

Any default/remedy process must follow the agreement and applicable authority. Possible system states include notice, cure window, hold, revised plan, collection/provider process, collateral enforcement candidate or dispute—but CHLOM does not create extra-contractual remedy rights.

## Failure modes

- rights/ownership unclear -\> HOLD;
- asset already fully encumbered -\> deny/hold;
- underwriting evidence stale -\> refresh/HOLD;
- required professional/legal review missing -\> HOLD;
- provider funding success but canonical terms mismatch -\> reconcile/escalate;
- repayment provider fails -\> obligation remains open;
- revenue lower than forecast -\> contract/remedy path, not model rewrite;
- duplicate revenue event -\> idempotency prevents double repayment.

## Threat model

- forged rights/collateral;
- double pledge;
- manipulated valuation/revenue;
- discriminatory/opaque underwriting;
- provider identity substitution;
- unauthorized revenue interception;
- hidden fees/terms;
- AI overreach;
- securities/lending classification errors;
- privacy leakage.

## Test vectors

1. asset ownership unresolved -\> HOLD;
2. license prohibits assignment/encumbrance -\> not eligible;
3. model predicts eligibility but professional gate absent -\> no offer activation;
4. duplicate collateral pledge beyond rights -\> reject;
5. funding provider success but amount mismatch -\> unreconciled;
6. revenue-share allocation exceeds contract -\> reject;
7. repayment event replay -\> idempotent;
8. valuation stale -\> refresh/hold;
9. dispute over rights -\> hold/remedy path;
10. historical Smart Capital concept -\> no live lending claim.

Current use can support internal evidence gathering, rights eligibility, modeling and reconciliation, but any lending, investment, securities or credit activity requires properly authorized providers and professional/legal controls.
