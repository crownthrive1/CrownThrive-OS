# CHLOM Overview

CHLOM is CrownThrive’s governance spine for intellectual property, licensing, attribution, evidence, revenue allocation, corrections, and remedies.

Its current operating definition is **Compliance Hybrid Licensing and Ownership Model**.

## Six canonical functions

CHLOM is organized around six functions:

1. **Rights** — who owns or controls an asset and what permissions exist.
2. **Rules** — conditions, restrictions, jurisdictions, windows, caps, and policies.
3. **Roles** — actors, authorities, responsibilities, and least-privilege permissions.
4. **Revenue** — fees, splits, royalties, commissions, reserves, and reconciliation.
5. **Records** — sources, evidence, approvals, events, versions, receipts, and corrections.
6. **Remedies** — holds, suspensions, revocations, corrections, disputes, refunds, appeals, and restoration.

## Current implementation posture

Phase 2 and Phase 2.5 establish CHLOM as a **cloud-first, API-first, event-driven, multi-tenant, human-governed core**.

Historical CHLOM papers describe a broader decentralized architecture including programmable licenses, decentralized identity, zero-knowledge verification, AI compliance, treasury logic, public licensing exchange, token models, and chain-based settlement. Those designs are preserved as target architecture and research. They are not treated as evidence that those systems are live today.

## Institutional role

CHLOM does not replace CrownThrive brands. It governs the rights and operating relationships around them.

The intended pattern is:

```text
CrownThrive Holdings / stewardship
        ↓
CrownThrive operating ecosystem
        ↓
CHLOM rights + rules + records
        ↓
CrownThrive IO / APIs / MCP
        ↓
Platforms, products, services, licenses, evidence, and revenue events
```

## Governing doctrine

- Evidence is private by default.
- Corrections append; they do not silently erase history.
- Public claims must be supportable.
- Human approval remains mandatory for legal, rights, compliance, enforcement, security, money movement, and irreversible actions.
- A concept appearing in a historical paper does not make it production-ready.
- Stable identifiers and lineage survive implementation changes.

CHLOM should be useful, auditable, and governable before it becomes decentralized.
