## Purpose

P-05 is CHLOM's current capability container for **authorized audience growth, advertising inventory, campaign execution, creator/influencer activation, notification delivery, analytics and evidence-backed measurement**.

Its governing principle is that growth systems may optimize delivery and measurement only within valid identity, consent, rights, economic and policy boundaries.

```text
AUTHORIZED OFFER / MESSAGE / INVENTORY
              |
              v
AUDIENCE + PURPOSE + CONSENT
              |
              v
CAMPAIGN / DELIVERY PLAN
              |
              v
PROVIDER EXECUTION
              |
              v
DELIVERY / VIEW / CLICK / CONVERSION EVIDENCE
              |
              v
ATTRIBUTION + RECONCILIATION
              |
              v
DAIL / CROWNLYTICS / ECONOMIC HANDOFF
```

## Six Functions mapping

| Function | P-05 responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| Rights | Verify authority to use creative, audience, placement and campaign assets |
| Rules | Consent, targeting, disclosure, campaign, inventory and measurement rules |
| Roles | Advertiser, publisher, creator, partner, analyst, operator, recipient |
| Revenue | Advertising/creator inventory and attribution evidence for authorized economics |
| Records | Campaigns, placements, deliveries, conversions, suppression and provider receipts |
| Remedies | Pause, suppress, correct attribution, compensate, refund/escalate where authorized |

## Source-component interpretation

### AdLuxe Studio

Creative/campaign preparation and managed advertising services. P-05 binds creative approval, campaign configuration and evidence without assuming a particular ad-delivery provider is permanent.

### AdLuxe Network

Advertising network capabilities: advertiser/publisher relationships, inventory, campaign delivery, reporting and reconciliation.

### AdLuxe Places

Place/location-based advertising inventory and local activation. Place identity and physical inventory may depend on P-09.

### CrownPulse

Social-proof/FOMO-style signals and related growth surfaces. CrownPulse events must be truthful, purpose-appropriate and privacy-safe; fabricated activity is incompatible with CHLOM evidence rules.

### CrownLytics

Measurement/analytics layer for event collection, funnels, campaign tagging and performance evidence. Analytics data does not independently create conversion, earnings or attribution truth.

### ThrivePush

Notification/campaign execution across authorized audiences. Consent and suppression are first-class controls.

### CrownFluence

Creator/influencer campaign relationships, briefs, approvals, deliverables, performance evidence and authorized payout handoff.

## Targeting boundary

Targeting logic must be governed by applicable purpose, consent, privacy and platform policies. The pallet does not institutionalize discriminatory or prohibited targeting merely because a provider supports a field.

## Budget/pacing boundary

Campaign budget configuration is an economic instruction, not a blank check.

Budget authority may require:

- campaign approval;
- exact budget ceiling;
- time period;
- provider account scope;
- spend cap;
- rollback/pause authority;
- reconciliation.

## Operating procedures

### Campaign creation

1. assign campaign ID;
2. define purpose/objective;
3. resolve offer/creative rights;
4. resolve audience/consent;
5. select authorized inventory/channels;
6. bind economic ceiling/schedule;
7. validate disclosures/policies;
8. approve exact version;
9. create provider projection;
10. read back provider state;
11. activate and record DAIL evidence.

### Campaign monitoring

Monitor delivery, spend/economic evidence, audience complaints, provider health and quality signals. Optimization must remain inside approved authority.

### Pause

Pause can be triggered by policy, security, rights, budget, quality, provider or founder/governance conditions. Provider pause must be read back.

### Opt-out/complaint

Process promptly into suppression/consent state and propagate to relevant sending systems.

### Attribution correction

Preserve original attribution, create correction reason/evidence, recalculate only under adopted rules, and reconcile dependent economics.

## Failure modes

### Provider sends to suppressed recipient

**Response:** record incident, suppress locally, stop/repair campaign/provider state, reconcile exposure and review controls.

### Provider reports conversion without institutional match

**Response:** keep as candidate evidence, not settled conversion.

### Creative rights expire mid-campaign

**Response:** HOLD/pause affected delivery and reconcile provider state.

### Budget exceeds authorized ceiling

**Response:** pause/deny additional spend, preserve evidence and route reconciliation/remedy.

### Analytics duplication

**Response:** deduplicate through defined correlation/idempotency logic; do not silently inflate public metrics.

### Provider outage

**Response:** fail over only through certified adapters and applicable authority; do not duplicate sends/spend.

