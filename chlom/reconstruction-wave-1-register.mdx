# CHLOM Reconstruction Wave 1 Register

Wave 1 converts the first recovered CHLOM source corpus into durable PentaDocs article bodies while establishing the continuity and founder-adjudication rules later waves inherit.

## Program objective

The full recovery target remains the **795-title Help Center census**. A recovered title alone is not considered a recovered body. Each title moves through source discovery, reconstruction, conflict marking, adjudication where needed, documentation QA and continuity registration.

Wave 1 focuses on foundational CHLOM papers and terminology because later articles depend on these definitions.

## Continuity contract for later waves

Every later wave must:

1. identify the target title set;
2. find authoritative source bodies;
3. preserve source-era meaning before modernization;
4. classify historical versus current claims;
5. mark contradictions rather than silently normalize them;
6. write a substantive body, not a placeholder;
7. add founder-adjudication status where material;
8. link source/current-state pages;
9. update the wave register/changelog;
10. retain superseded/history records instead of deleting them.

## Founder review mode

The Founder may review pages after publication and issue decisions in plain language. A founder decision is converted into a dated adjudication/correction record and propagated through affected pages and machine records.

This lets recovery move at speed without sacrificing institutional truth.
