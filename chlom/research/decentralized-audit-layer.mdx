## Independence and corroboration

The historical decentralized framing implies resistance to single-source assurance. Current implementations can achieve bounded independence through multiple source classes, independent reviewers, source registries, separation of duties and immutable correction history without claiming blockchain decentralization.

## Audit versus certification

```text
audit executed != certification
control coverage != compliance guarantee
attestation != legal conclusion
public proof != disclosure of private evidence
```

Certification requires the exact assurance authority and package defined for the object/domain.

## Evidence privacy

An externally verifiable audit design must not require publishing protected evidence. Public-safe attestations, digests, inclusion proofs or status receipts can verify selected properties while restricted evidence remains in governed custody.
