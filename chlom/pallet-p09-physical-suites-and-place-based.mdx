## Purpose

P-09 is CHLOM's current capability container for **physical places, suites, locations, operator relationships, territory/use rights, place-bound services, bookings, kiosks and local advertising inventory**.

It exists because physical operations create a distinct evidence problem: a digital record can say a suite, territory, operator or inventory unit exists while the real-world site may be unavailable, transferred, closed, out of compliance or governed by a different agreement. P-09 keeps physical state, contractual rights, operator authority and provider projections synchronized without treating any one provider as sovereign truth.

## Source-component interpretation

### MM Suites

Melanin Magic Suites contributes the strongest place-based operating model: suite/service locations, Suite Pro/operator roles, booking, regional/franchise/licensing lineage and physical-service delivery. Historical prospectuses and agreements must be reconciled to current legal/operating status before public claims of franchise, territory or investment rights.

### Suite Pros

Suite Pro is an operator/professional role within the MM Suites model. It is not another name for ThriveSeat, and it is not automatically equivalent to a franchisee, regional licensee or regulated professional credential.

### BrandCards

BrandCards can represent public-safe place/operator/business identity and activation surfaces. A BrandCard is a projection, not the source of place ownership, operator authority or licensure.

### ThriveKiosks

ThriveKiosks represent place-bound digital interaction/commerce/discovery surfaces where configured. Kiosk state must resolve to a canonical place and operator context.

### Booking

Bookings use P-06 commercial/booking semantics while P-09 supplies the authoritative place/resource/capacity relationship.

### Local advertising

Local/place-based advertising inventory maps to P-05. P-09 supplies the physical location, screen/placement identity and controlling operator/rights evidence.

## Physical-state evidence

Physical operational state may require evidence beyond digital-provider state:

- site inspection/readiness record;
- address/location verification;
- operator confirmation;
- occupancy/control evidence;
- equipment/resource status;
- booking availability;
- provider check-in evidence;
- incident/maintenance record;
- applicable permit/license evidence when required and verified.

No article should claim a place is licensed by government merely because CHLOM marks it operational.

## Local compliance boundary

Physical operations may be subject to jurisdiction-specific permits, professional licenses, occupancy, safety, employment, tax or health requirements. CHLOM can track verified evidence/currentness but does not issue governmental clearance unless a verified authority relationship specifically supports that function.

```text
CHLOM ELIGIBILITY PASS
       !=
GOVERNMENT PERMIT
```

## Operating procedures

### New place onboarding

1. assign place ID;
2. preserve source/address/control evidence;
3. resolve P-03 rights/agreements;
4. resolve operator identity/role;
5. create resources/capacity;
6. verify required operating evidence;
7. bind booking/commerce/ad adapters;
8. certify public projection;
9. activate/read back;
10. emit DAIL evidence.

### Operator change

1. preserve outgoing assignment;
2. validate incoming operator authority;
3. update effective periods;
4. rotate access/configuration where needed;
5. reconcile booking/kiosk/provider aliases;
6. emit evidence.

### Maintenance hold

Set exact resource/place hold, stop new bookings/inventory if required, propagate to providers, read back and restore only through authorized closure of the hold.

### Place closure

1. stop new commercial/booking activity;
2. preserve customer obligations;
3. reconcile refunds/reschedules;
4. remove/hold public availability;
5. preserve rights/contract history;
6. archive or transfer controlled assets;
7. record DAIL closure.

## Failure modes

### Booking provider shows open while physical site closed

**Response:** P-09 physical hold controls; reconcile provider and affected bookings.

### Operator account active after agreement ended

**Response:** revoke/suspend institutional authority and provider access; preserve audit evidence.

### Territory overlap

**Response:** HOLD affected exclusivity claims/activations and open rights/case review.

### Kiosk online at retired location

**Response:** disable/hold device surface under security authority and reconcile place mapping.

### Ad provider serves retired physical inventory

**Response:** stop delivery, reconcile P-05 campaign/provider state and economic consequences.

### Public location address stale

**Response:** correct public projection after verified source update; preserve prior version.
