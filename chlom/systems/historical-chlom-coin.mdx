## Source basis

Historical CHLOM sources describe **CHLOM Coin** as a utility/economic token associated with fees, staking, rewards, licensing/marketplace activity, treasury mechanics and burn/inflation models. The Genesis, Grand Prospectus and later tokenomics research preserve multiple quantitative examples.

This article isolates CHLOM Coin from the separate historical **CHM governance token** so the two-token design is no longer buried inside one combined tokenomics page.
