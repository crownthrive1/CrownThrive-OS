## Source basis

Historical CHLOM sources describe a dual-token architecture in which **CHM** serves a governance role distinct from the economic/utility role assigned to **CHLOM Coin**. Source-era materials connect CHM to proposals, voting, quorum, staking and protocol-governance mechanics.

This standalone record prevents CHM from being collapsed into CHLOM Coin or treated as a current CrownThrive governance instrument.

## What CHM is not currently

- CHM is minted or transferable today;

- historical DAO smart contracts are deployed;

## Delegation

Token-governance systems can support delegation, but current-safe interpretation requires:

```text
delegated token vote
   != delegated institutional authority outside the proposal system
```

A delegation must remain scoped to the governance mechanism that recognizes it.

```text
protocol governance != corporate legal governance automatically
```

Any future governance token activation would need explicit legal/entity/charter treatment.

## Rights boundary
