# Royalty, Splits, Allocation and Settlement Lineage

Historical CHLOM papers treat automated attribution, royalties, distributions and treasury routing as a core benefit of programmable licensing. Later documentation uses **ADE = Attribution & Distribution Engine** for this family. Modern CHLOM preserves the economic logic while separating **calculation, obligation, provider payment and final institutional settlement**.

## Historical economic model

Recovered source families explore automatic royalty calculation, licensor/licensee distributions, creator/stakeholder splits, recurring/streaming royalties, marketplace fees, treasury allocations, taxes/reserves and programmable settlement.

Some sources use smart contracts or tokens as the execution layer. Those are historical implementation choices; the economic meaning can be implemented conventionally.

## Current economic schedule

A current royalty/split rule should be versioned separately from ownership or license identity. It may identify gross basis, eligible deductions, recipient roles, percentages/basis points, caps/floors, effective period, currency/unit, tax/withholding handling, reserve/hold rules, reversal behavior and approval authority.

No historical percentage becomes current merely because it appeared in a paper or old offer.

## Calculation versus money movement

```text
economic schedule
→ allocation calculation
→ obligation/accrual record
→ payment/settlement authorization
→ provider execution
→ provider evidence
→ institutional reconciliation
→ settled / corrected / reversed / held
```

An allocation plan can be mathematically valid without authorizing a transfer. Likewise, a payment-provider success can occur without proving the correct institutional allocation.

## Attribution

Attribution answers who/what contributed to an economic event and which rule applies. It may depend on license/DLA, affiliate/campaign attribution, creator participation, rights class, product/SKU, territory, event date or other governed context.

Attribution must be evidence-backed; a provider referral ID or campaign tag is not automatically conclusive if contradicted by institutional records.

## Distribution

Distribution converts an accepted allocation into obligations or settlement instructions. The historical ADE concept maps to current allocation/obligation/settlement services rather than unrestricted autonomous transfers.

## Reversals and disputes

Refunds, chargebacks, rights revocation, duplicate events, provider errors or corrected attribution may require reversal or compensating actions. The original allocation remains preserved; a correction references the original plan and records what changed.

## DLA and LEX relationship

Royalty/split obligations may be referenced by a DLA and activated by an eligible LEX or commerce event. The economic layer cannot expand the license scope or manufacture rights.

## ThriveEvergreen relationship

For current CrownThrive economic activation, provider evidence must reconcile through the accepted commerce/economic authority. Delivery, payment or ad-performance evidence cannot directly create earnings/payouts if the governing economic/rights state does not support them.

## DAIL evidence

DAIL should preserve schedule/version identity, triggering event, calculation/plan digest, obligation records, provider receipts, reconciliation outcome and later corrections/reversals without exposing restricted financial payloads publicly.

## Historical token settlement

CHLOM Coin/CHM, staking and smart-contract settlement remain research/historical until separately activated. Current architecture can use fiat/payment providers or internal ledgers without losing the provider-independent economic contract.
