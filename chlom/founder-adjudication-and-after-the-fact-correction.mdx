# Founder Adjudication and After-the-Fact Correction

CHLOM reconstruction proceeds on a **publish-with-lineage, correct-without-erasure** model.

The purpose of this model is to prevent the Help Center recovery from stalling merely because every historical contradiction has not yet been personally adjudicated, while also preventing reconstructed text from silently becoming irreversible truth.

## Operating rule

A source-grounded article may be reconstructed and published when the available evidence is sufficient to explain the historical concept, current interpretation, unresolved conflicts, implementation state, and source basis.

Publication does **not** waive the Founder's reserved authority to later adjudicate:

- canonical names and acronyms;
- platform/framework identity;
- predecessor/successor relationships;
- historical-versus-current operating meaning;
- economic schedules;
- ownership or rights claims;
- strategic disposition;
- product/platform status;
- implementation priority;
- public/private classification.

Founder adjudication changes the **effective institutional interpretation**. It does not erase the source history that preceded it.

## Reconstruction states

- `reconstructed_source_grounded` — body rebuilt from one or more identified source families;
- `reconstructed_with_conflict` — body rebuilt, but one or more material contradictions remain explicit;
- `founder_adjudication_pending` — a founder decision is required for canonical resolution;
- `founder_adjudicated` — a dated founder decision resolves the current institutional meaning;
- `historical_preserved` — retained to preserve an older architecture, claim, acronym or operating period;
- `superseded_currently` — earlier interpretation remains available but is no longer the current default;
- `restricted_source_only` — source exists but body or operative detail is not suitable for public documentation;
- `source_not_located` — title/reference exists, but adequate source content has not yet been recovered.

## Required article envelope

A reconstructed article should identify, where applicable:

1. **Current meaning** — present institutional interpretation.
2. **Historical meaning** — what earlier papers or Help Center eras said.
3. **Source basis** — exact source families used.
4. **Implementation status** — historical, specified, prototype, controlled test, pilot, production, or another governed state.
5. **Known conflicts** — terminology, status, economic, identity or authority conflicts.
6. **Founder adjudication status** — whether a later founder ruling can alter current interpretation.
7. **Correction behavior** — how a later ruling is appended and propagated.

## Correction pattern

```text
historical source
    ↓
reconstructed article vN
    ↓
founder adjudication
    ↓
correction / supersession record
    ↓
article vN+1
    ↓
linked contradiction + alias + source + changelog updates
```

The prior version, source references, and contradiction record remain part of institutional history.

## Known examples that may be adjudicated later

- AIE as historical **AI Intelligence Engine** versus later **Anomaly Intelligence Engine** reuse;
- ADE as historical **Autonomous Decision Engine** versus later **Attribution & Distribution Engine** reuse;
- DAL as historical **Decentralized Attestation Ledger**, **Decentralized Adjudication Layer**, or distribution-related uses versus current DAIL;
- DLA as historical **Decentralized Licensing Authority** versus current **Dynamic Licensing Asset**;
- TLaaS as **Tokenized Licensing as a Service** in one source family and **Trust Layer as a Service** in another;
- XENThrive as historical commerce/brand lineage rather than a current primary CHLOM platform.

These conflicts are preserved now and can be adjudicated later without blocking recovery.

## What reconstruction cannot invent

Reconstruction must not manufacture legal ownership, patent status, securities/token classification, current prices, current royalty rates, active provider integration, production deployment, credentials, current authority from historical governance metaphors, or current platform status merely because a legacy article existed.

If evidence does not support the operative claim, the article must say so.

## Propagation requirement

A founder adjudication affecting a canonical term or system must propagate to materially affected records, including terminology/acronym lineage, alias/successor register, contradiction ledger, source register, platform/framework registries, CHLOM module/component catalogs, affected Help Center articles, changelog, and machine manifests/validators where the term is machine-significant.

## Continuity rule

New source discovery, a revived platform, a newly recovered paper, or a founder ruling does not restart the recovery program. It enters the same append-only correction and propagation system.

The objective is institutional memory that can improve over time without losing where it came from.
