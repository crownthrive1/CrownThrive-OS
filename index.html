INSTITUTIONAL LIFECYCLE

PENTA is the operating grammar.

Five functions. One continuous loop. The current institution-wide generation is Execute, while every component can continuously traverse all five functions.

01
DISCOVER

Find what is real.

Providers, assets, identities, versions, rights signals, dependencies, risks, opportunities, and gaps.

02
GOVERN

Define what is allowed.

Rights, rules, roles, credentials, cultural constraints, evidence requirements, licensing, and authority ceilings.

03
EXECUTE · CURRENT

Perform authorized work.

Durable software, routes, factories, queues, publishers, media systems, commerce, automation, APIs, and MCPs.

04
VERIFY

Prove what happened.

Readback, receipts, hashes, tests, canaries, reconciliation, signatures, evidence, and independent assurance.

05
PRESERVE

Protect what must outlive us.

Versions, provenance, archives, rights context, recovery packages, succession, portability, and institutional memory.
