## Penta Families™ // Institutional Family Portals

Penta Family™ remains CrownThrive's production umbrella control plane. **Penta Families™** is the governed topology directly beneath it: fifteen operating families that organize the full Penta estate by mission while preserving every child's independent maturity, authority, provider, rights, economic and evidence state.

<Info>
  Family assignment creates **context and accountability**, not authority. A `specified` Penta remains `specified` after it receives a family and portal.
</Info>

## Why families

The Penta estate now spans OS architecture, routes, primitives, agentic workflows, factories, security, resilience, observability, data/knowledge, governance, workforce, research, communications, media and commerce. A flat list is no longer enough to explain the system.

The family layer answers:

- where each Penta belongs;
- which mission it primarily serves;
- which other families it hands work to;
- where operators should enter the system;
- which portal tells the family story;
- and whether a newly named Penta has actually been institutionalized.

## Exhaustive discovery

The verifier does not trust one registry. It reconciles:

1. the machine Penta Family and all `data/penta/systems*.json` catalogs;
2. the PentaOS technical component registry and PentaRoute primitive census; and
3. the broader `PENTA-FAMILY-REGISTRY.md` institutional identity/portal registry.

Names are normalized across display forms such as `Penta Federation` and `PentaFederation`. Genuinely distinct identities remain distinct.

If a Penta exists in any canonical source but has no unambiguous primary family, CI fails closed. This makes family/portal institutionalization a mandatory part of future Penta growth.

## Primary family vs. cross-family role

Every Penta has exactly one primary family. That family owns its family-story/portal context.

A Penta may serve other families without receiving a second primary owner. For example, PentaStatus is a Resilience & Continuity system that also serves Observability and Communications; PentaAssure belongs to Build/Certification/Release while supporting Security and Governance.

## Portal content contract

Every family portal carries:

**Story · Mission · Member Census · Member Status · Responsibilities · Inputs/Outputs · Authority Boundary · Cross-Family Handoffs · Operations · SOPs/SLAs · Evidence · API/MCP · Incidents/Recovery · Releases/Changelog · Roadmap · Support**

The machine runtime renders these sections dynamically against the current census.

## Runtime verification

```bash
python runtime/penta_families.py --root . --portal-index
python runtime/penta_families.py --root . --family security-trust
python tests/test_penta_families.py
```

A future registry addition that is not classified returns `hold_fail_closed`.

## Authority boundary

```text
FAMILY ≠ AUTHORITY
FAMILY ≠ MATURITY
PORTAL ≠ DEPLOYMENT
INTEROPERABILITY ≠ PERMISSION
READINESS ≠ PROVIDER-WRITE AUTHORITY
DOCUMENTATION ≠ CERTIFICATION
```

Consequential work still resolves through child maturity, PentaRoute/PentaMCP, PentaOD readiness when applicable, CHLOM/DAIL authority, PentaHybrid human gates, PentaCredentials, PentaCertify/PentaAssure, provider certification and exact readback.
