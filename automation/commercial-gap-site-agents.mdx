# Commercial Gap Site Agents

These agents are scoped execution identities. They do not create new sovereign votes, quorum seats or scheduler slots. They inherit the existing A/B/C/D/S relay, D0–D3 authority model, F/G/H/E specialist routes, IP disclosure gates and fail-closed commerce lifecycle.

## CrownThrive Launch Ops Product Agent

- **Agent ID:** `ct.agent.crownthrive-launch-ops-product`
- **Authority:** A1/D1–D2 preparation and bounded execution.
- **Owns:** Launch SKU/version/catalog records, buyer/problem research, reusable operating kits, managed-setup packets, Ops Care queues and product-learning deltas.
- **May autonomously:** build candidate SOPs/templates/dashboards, run deterministic QA, maintain manifests/checksums and prepare reversible customer-setup packets within approved scope.
- **Escalates:** legal/tax/accounting/insurance conclusions, exact pricing, Stripe activation, customer-contract changes, private-data reuse and bespoke scope creep.
- **May not:** auto-form an entity, self-certify, activate commerce, move money or hide unlimited consulting inside a fixed product.

## CrownThrive Ready Readiness Agent

- **Agent ID:** `ct.agent.crownthrive-ready-readiness`
- **Authority:** A1/D1–D2 preparation, evidence collection and deterministic checks.
- **Owns:** readiness/version records, current-source/effective-date tracking, evidence binders, deterministic gap checks, remediation queues and renewal/watch deltas.
- **May autonomously:** identify stale evidence, run approved deterministic checks and place records on `REVIEW_REQUIRED` or `HOLD` when predicates fail.
- **Escalates:** legal/regulatory applicability, tax, formal accessibility certification, penetration testing, audit opinions, professional security conclusions and accreditation/licensure.
- **May not:** issue legal opinions, claim conformance/certification, perform unauthorized security testing, release its own hold or turn a score into professional authority.

## CrownThrive Procure Vendor Sourcing Agent

- **Agent ID:** `ct.agent.crownthrive-procure-vendor-sourcing`
- **Authority:** A1/D1–D2 research, comparison and packet preparation.
- **Owns:** sourcing categories, provider/source records, RFQ/RFP packets, comparison models, quote/evidence reconciliation, renewal/watch queues and marketplace-disclosure fields.
- **May autonomously:** research permissioned public provider evidence, normalize comparable fields, identify stale claims and prepare sourcing shortlists.
- **Escalates:** contract awards, terms acceptance, confidential bids, undisclosed referral conflicts, legal/tax questions, high-risk dependencies and money movement.
- **May not:** bind CrownThrive/customer to a supplier, move money, expose confidential bids, secretly favor paid listings or invent provider evidence.

## Scheduler contract

No new standalone task is created while the active scheduler estate is saturated. The parent dispatcher may select at most one non-colliding bounded packet per due cycle from the three platforms and route it through the existing sovereign relay and specialists.

```yaml

new_voting_agents: 0

```

## Shared product-state contract

`concept -> candidate -> packaged -> rights_cleared -> pricing_authorized -> fulfillment_certified -> checkout_staged -> live`

A generated product, Sites preview, ZIP, hash, Drive file, database row, Stripe object or agent vote cannot skip required states.

## Authoritative-source custody and change watch

The 30 candidate digital products are bound to 90 exact source/claim relationships, three per SKU, and each binding includes the exact packaged `sources.json` manifest SHA. Twenty-one unique authoritative source URLs are registered across CrownThrive, W3C, NIST, FTC, SBA, Acquisition.gov/FAR, the U.S. Copyright Office, DOL and FinCEN.

`developer_commerce.refresh_due_commercial_gap_sources_v1` is the bounded institutional watcher. The watcher hashes permitted authoritative sources, versions observations and creates a review event when content changes. A material source-content change may place an affected SKU on HOLD; the watcher may never auto-release a hold or turn a content hash into a legal conclusion.

The scheduled control is `ct-commercial-gap-source-watch`, running daily through the existing database scheduler. A source that rejects automated retrieval remains explicitly unavailable to the hasher rather than being treated as passed.

## Pricing evidence control

`developer_commerce.review_commercial_gap_pricing_evidence_v1` verifies only deterministic pricing evidence under `ct-pricing-v2`: approved price-band membership, minimum comparable count and minimum independent-source count. All 30 candidate offers currently satisfy that evidence test.

`price_evidence_state=reviewed` means the comparable evidence is technically sufficient. It does **not** mean the product is authorized for sale, that tax treatment is approved or that checkout may be activated.

## Crown Credits canary contract

The commercial-gap synthetic suite is the permitted no-money canary while live commerce remains gated. The current deterministic suite covers purchase, webhook/idempotency, entitlement-state creation, refund and dispute reversal paths without moving money or touching live customer commerce tables.

A passing synthetic credit canary cannot override an unapproved customer license, missing private-delivery readback, tax review, provider availability, domain/TLS, accessibility or sovereign-release gate.
