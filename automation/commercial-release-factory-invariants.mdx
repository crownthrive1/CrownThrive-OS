# Commercial Release Factory Invariants

The following invariants are mandatory for the CrownThrive Commercial Release Factory:

- Every exact product version has all nine hard release dimensions.
- Missing, stale, contradictory or originator-only evidence resolves to HOLD.
- The originator and its child workers are non-voting and cannot self-approve.
- CrownThrive Launch, Ready and Procure remain Crown Credits-only at the product layer.
- Per-SKU Stripe Product/Price objects are not the checkout mechanism for these three surfaces.
- Credit debit evidence is not a license or entitlement.
- Entitlement must bind the exact approved asset version and license state.
- Fulfillment must require entitlement and remain private until authorized delivery.
- Refunds, disputes and reversals preserve append-only evidence and cannot silently erase prior state.
- Automated accessibility checks do not create a formal conformance opinion.
- CrownThrive Ready never creates a professional audit, legal, security, accessibility or compliance certification.
- CrownThrive Procure never allows a commercial relationship to create supplier merit, endorsement or award authority.
- Custom-domain intent does not prove DNS, TLS or canonical routing.
- Provider hosting health is distinct from anonymous public application accessibility.
- Publication requires an accepted exact release, certified route, read-after-write and rollback.
- A provider acknowledgement without public readback is incomplete evidence.
- Secret values, private algorithm bodies, private evidence and credential locators never enter public projection.
- Agent replication increases bounded capacity only and never creates vote, D3 or credential authority.
