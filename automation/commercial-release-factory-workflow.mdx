# Commercial Release Factory Workflow

## Hourly materialization

An hourly THIVEBASE condition watch at minute 27 resolves the current 30-product cohort from `commercial_sites_candidate_4`. The prior source key remains an accepted input alias. The job deterministically generates or refreshes one v2 package per exact SKU and asset version.

Repeated execution is idempotent. The materializer cannot create a sovereign vote, offer, entitlement, public price, Stripe object, fulfillment path, or direct provider write. Missing or contradictory evidence is recorded as a typed HOLD rather than converted into a synthetic PASS.

## Current required gates

1. package integrity;
2. rights provenance;
3. Crown Credits pricing and tax;
4. private fulfillment;
5. credit checkout and webhook;
6. entitlement and license issuance;
7. refund, dispute, remedy, and rollback;
8. accessibility and device review;
9. DNS, TLS, canonical route, and application readback; and
10. governed acceptance and publication.

CrownThrive Ready adds an eleventh qualified-professional review gate. Its products may organize readiness evidence but cannot issue legal, security, privacy, tax, accessibility, or regulatory conclusions.

Publication is queued automatically only after the accepted policy approves the exact package and the destination is certified. A later non-PASS transition quarantines the release and invokes rollback handling.

## Evidence states

- **PASS** requires exact-version evidence and an authorized, independent reviewer where applicable.
- **HOLD** records missing, stale, contradictory, outside-authority, or professional/founder-dependent evidence.
- **FAIL** records a proven exact negative result.
- **NOT APPLICABLE** requires positive evidence and cannot be used as a shortcut.

## Drift and rollback

Changes to source bytes, asset hash, package hash, rights, price evidence, tax profile, fulfillment object, webhook contract, entitlement rule, remedy sequence, accessibility evidence, domain state, destination consumer, or reviewer authority create a new review boundary. Earlier acceptance cannot silently float to the changed artifact.
