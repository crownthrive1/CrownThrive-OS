## Governance Observability Penta Capability

Governance observability is a bounded Phase-3 capability distributed across the current Penta operating environment. It **does not manufacture authority** and is not a replacement sovereign agent.

## Ownership

- **PentaNurture™** owns drift detection, evidence-health assessment, remediation routing, and continuity follow-up.
- **PentaStatus™** owns current-state projection and machine readback.
- **PentaMerge™** consumes exact-head evidence only through its own merge contract.
- **PentaCloser™** consumes supersession, staleness, and representation evidence when disposing of obsolete work.
- **PentaCertify™** validates the exact implementation and tests without granting broader policy or execution authority.
- **CHLOM** remains the policy/evidence governance layer.

## Supersession rule

Historical references to `ct.agent.governance-observability` or an existing CrownThrive Services Stack scheduler are preserved as source-era lineage only. They do not establish a current worker lease, current scheduler binding, current vote, or current authority.

## Runtime boundary

The encrypted archive format and resumable checkpoint semantics remain valid design controls. The recorded August-23 v21 archive measurements remain dated evidence. Current archive progress and current scheduler/subroute binding require live readback before continuation.

## Prohibited effects

This capability cannot merge, delete branches, vote, issue policies, issue contracts, activate archive runtime, move money, mutate rights, disclose credentials/plaintext archive data, perform provider writes, or make D3 decisions.
