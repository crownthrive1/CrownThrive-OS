# Penta Family™ // Institutional Controls

This guide covers the v1.2 institutional-controls extension of the Penta Family production control plane. The family umbrella is production, while every member below remains independently maturity-gated. Registration, portal availability and documentation do **not** create execution authority.

## Shared operating rule

All nine systems follow the same institutional lifecycle:

**Discover → Govern → Execute → Verify → Preserve**

Every consequential action must resolve:

1. the exact registered Penta member;
2. evidence-backed member maturity;
3. CHLOM/DAIL authority and accountable owner;
4. PentaHybrid human review/approval when policy requires it;
5. certified provider/credential route for provider effects;
6. readback and preserved receipts.

Unknown identity, ambiguous role, missing policy source, missing provider certification, unresolved human gate, missing readback or contradictory evidence yields `hold_fail_closed`.

## PentaCompliance

**Portal:** `/penta/compliance`

Owns compliance obligation inventories, applicability matrices, control mappings, evidence calendars, attestations, exceptions and remediation routing. It coordinates with PentaPolicy, PentaLegal, PentaRisk, PentaAudit, PentaAssure and PentaOFAC.

It does not invent regulatory requirements, waive obligations, declare unsupported compliance or file binding attestations without authorized signatory authority.

## PentaPrivacy

**Portal:** `/penta/privacy`

Owns privacy inventories, purpose/consent boundaries, minimization, retention, privacy-impact reviews, data-subject rights and privacy incident routing.

It does not fabricate consent, broaden processing purposes silently, waive rights or disclose protected data. Binding legal interpretations route through PentaLegal and accountable authority.

## PentaIdentity

**Portal:** `/penta/identity`

Owns human, agent, service and organizational identity lifecycle, role bindings, access-review context and separation-of-duties relationships.

PentaIdentity is deliberately separate from **PentaCredentials**. Identity answers *who or what is acting and what governed roles are bound to it*. PentaCredentials manages secret/provider credential references and health. Authentication or possession of a credential never grants a role or governance authority by itself.

## PentaData

**Portal:** `/penta/data`

Owns data classification, ownership, stewardship, schema governance, lineage, quality contracts, sharing boundaries and data-product lifecycle.

PentaData supplies governed semantics and lineage to PentaAnalytics and PentaInstitute. It cannot silently redefine source truth, disclose restricted data or infer a new permitted use merely because data is technically accessible.

## PentaRecords

**Portal:** `/penta/records`

Owns record classes, authoritative-copy designation, retention schedules, administrative/legal holds, disposition eligibility and evidentiary record integrity.

It complements PentaDocs and PentaGeneration. It cannot destroy held records, rewrite historical evidence or perform disposition without an authorized retention/disposition instrument.

## PentaProcure

**Portal:** `/penta/procure`

Owns procurement intake, requirements, sourcing/comparison, requisitions, approval packets, purchasing workflow, receiving and procurement receipts.

It can prepare and route a purchase but cannot commit CrownThrive funds, place an order or accept vendor terms without the exact spend, contract, payment and provider authority required through PentaCost, PentaPay, PentaCapital, PentaContracts and accountable approvers.

`PentaProcurement` is a recognized alias; the canonical machine/system name is **PentaProcure**.

## PentaVendor

**Portal:** `/penta/vendor`

Owns vendor/provider onboarding, due diligence, ownership, concentration, risk, security posture, service levels, performance, renewals and offboarding.

It does not sign agreements, approve spend, create provider credentials or self-certify adapters. Those responsibilities remain with PentaContracts/PentaLegal, PentaCapital/PentaCost/PentaPay, PentaCredentials and PentaCertify respectively.

## PentaContracts

**Portal:** `/penta/contracts`

Owns contract lifecycle mechanics: intake, templates, clause matrices, review packets, approvals, signature workflow, obligations, milestones, renewals, amendments and termination tracking.

It is not legal counsel and cannot bind CrownThrive. Binding effects require PentaLegal and authorized human/signatory authority.

## PentaQuality

**Portal:** `/penta/quality`

Owns quality plans, acceptance criteria, nonconformance tracking, root-cause analysis, corrective/preventive actions and effectiveness evidence.

It cannot lower criteria after failure, erase a nonconformance or self-certify consequential work. PentaAssure and PentaAudit remain independent verification surfaces.

## Cross-plane composition

The family v1.2 registry exposes three explicit composition planes:

| Plane | Members | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `data_identity_privacy` | PentaData, PentaPrivacy, PentaIdentity, PentaRecords, PentaSecurity, PentaAudit | Govern data, identity, privacy and record integrity |
| `procurement_vendor_contracts` | PentaProcure, PentaVendor, PentaContracts, PentaCapital, PentaCost, PentaPay, PentaLegal, PentaRisk | Govern sourcing through contract/spend/provider relationship lifecycle |
| `quality_compliance_assurance` | PentaQuality, PentaCompliance, PentaAssure, PentaAudit, PentaRisk, PentaPolicy, PentaSecurity | Govern requirements, control quality, independent evidence and remediation |

These planes are topology, not sovereign authority.

## Maturity and promotion

The nine v1.2 members begin as `specified` unless stronger implementation evidence exists. Promotion follows the normal family maturity sequence:

```text
specified → implemented → certified → production
```

`hold` may interrupt any promotion path. `retired` preserves historical identity while removing current eligibility.

Promotion to `implemented` requires actual executable assets/configuration and tests. Promotion to `certified` requires independent PentaCertify/PentaAssure evidence for the exact capability. Promotion to `production` requires verified live execution/readback in the authorized scope. A family-level production label never shortcuts this sequence.
