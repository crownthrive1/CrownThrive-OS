# Penta Family™ // Institutional Controls

This guide covers the v1.2 institutional-controls extension of the Penta Family production control plane. The family umbrella is production, while every child remains independently maturity-gated. These nine systems are now `implemented` at the source/runtime layer and remain non-execution-eligible through the Penta Family gate until independently certified or promoted with exact capability evidence.

## Shared operating rule

All nine systems follow **Discover → Govern → Execute → Verify → Preserve**. Every consequential action must resolve the exact registered member, evidence-backed maturity, CHLOM/DAIL authority and accountable owner, required PentaHybrid human review, certified provider/credential path for provider effects, and defined readback/preserved receipts.

Forbidden authority-manufacturing actions always return `hold_fail_closed`. Incomplete but potentially valid consequential requests return `governance_required`.

## PentaCompliance

**Portal:** `/penta/compliance`

Owns obligation inventories, applicability matrices, control mappings, evidence calendars, attestations, exceptions and remediation routing. It does not invent regulatory requirements, waive obligations or make unsupported/binding attestations.

## PentaPrivacy

**Portal:** `/penta/privacy`

Owns privacy inventories, purpose/consent boundaries, minimization, retention, privacy-impact reviews, data-subject rights and privacy incident routing. It does not fabricate consent, silently broaden processing purposes, waive rights or disclose protected data.

## PentaIdentity

**Portal:** `/penta/identity`

Owns human, agent, service and organizational identity lifecycle, role bindings, access-review context and separation-of-duties relationships. PentaIdentity is distinct from **PentaCredentials**: authentication or possession of a credential never grants a role or governance authority.

## PentaData

**Portal:** `/penta/data`

Owns data classification, ownership, stewardship, schema governance, lineage, quality contracts, sharing boundaries and data-product lifecycle. It cannot silently redefine source truth or bypass privacy/security classification.

## PentaRecords

**Portal:** `/penta/records`

Owns record classes, authoritative-copy designation, retention schedules, administrative/legal holds, disposition eligibility and evidentiary integrity. Held records cannot be destroyed, and historical evidence cannot be rewritten.

## PentaProcure

**Portal:** `/penta/procure`

Owns procurement intake, sourcing/comparison, requisitions, approval packets, purchasing workflow, receiving and procurement receipts. Procurement capability does not create spend authority. `PentaProcurement` is a recognized alias; **PentaProcure** is canonical.

## PentaVendor

**Portal:** `/penta/vendor`

Owns vendor/provider onboarding, due diligence, ownership, concentration, risk, security posture, SLAs, performance, renewals and offboarding. It cannot sign agreements, approve spend, create provider credentials or self-certify adapters.

## PentaContracts

**Portal:** `/penta/contracts`

Owns contract lifecycle mechanics: intake, templates, clause matrices, review packets, approvals, signature workflow, obligations, milestones, renewals, amendments and termination tracking. It is not legal counsel and cannot bind CrownThrive.

## PentaQuality

**Portal:** `/penta/quality`

Owns quality plans, acceptance criteria, nonconformance tracking, root-cause analysis, corrective/preventive actions and effectiveness evidence. It cannot lower criteria after failure, erase findings or self-certify consequential work.

## Runtime dispositions

`runtime/penta_institutional_controls.py` returns bounded governance states rather than performing provider effects:

```text
advisory_ready
workflow_ready
governance_required
execution_ready
hold_fail_closed
```

At current `implemented` maturity, all nine systems may analyze, validate and prepare governed work, but Penta Family rejects attempted execution. `execution_ready` requires supplied `certified` or `production` maturity plus all exact authority, human, separation-of-duties, provider and readback requirements.

## Cross-plane composition

| Plane | Members | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `data_identity_privacy` | PentaData, PentaPrivacy, PentaIdentity, PentaRecords, PentaSecurity, PentaAudit | Govern data, identity, privacy and record integrity |
| `procurement_vendor_contracts` | PentaProcure, PentaVendor, PentaContracts, PentaCapital, PentaCost, PentaPay, PentaLegal, PentaRisk | Govern sourcing through contract/spend/provider relationship lifecycle |
| `quality_compliance_assurance` | PentaQuality, PentaCompliance, PentaAssure, PentaAudit, PentaRisk, PentaPolicy, PentaSecurity | Govern requirements, quality, evidence and remediation |

These planes are topology, not sovereign authority.

## Maturity and promotion

The nine v1.2 members are now:

```text
implemented → certified → production
```

Promotion to `certified` requires independent PentaCertify/PentaAssure evidence for an exact capability. Promotion to `production` requires verified live execution/readback through the authorized provider scope. `hold` can interrupt any promotion path; the family-level production label never shortcuts member maturity.
