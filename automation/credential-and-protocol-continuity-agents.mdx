# Credential & Protocol Continuity Agents

## Registered non-voting agents

| Agent | Role | Ceiling | State |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `ct.ops.agent.credential-continuity` | credential custody, dual-reference continuity, presence/equality/version drift and bounded certification routing | D2; initial secret insertion remains D3 founder-authorized | active |
| `ct.ops.agent.protocol-foundry` | repeated-procedure discovery, proprietary protocol compilation, Vault-first candidate registration and verifier routing | D2 | test |
| `ct.ops.agent.protocol-commercialization` | commercial candidate creation, pricing/rights/fulfillment evidence and continuity | D2 | test |

All are non-voting, no-self-approval identities. Each has a provisional `did:chlom:` identity and private fingerprint commitment. The public projection may expose only the DID candidate and approved public digest—not private fingerprint records, salts, commitments or subject mappings.

### Credential binding skill

- preflight registry/lifecycle state;
- founder-authorized initial bind through the Vault Broker;
- primary and continuity-recovery reference creation;
- immutable version archive and active binding;
- server-side equality/HMAC verification;
- D0 continuity refresh;
- approved D1 custody and missing-recovery repairs;
- D2 mismatch/public-exposure/source-precedence escalation;
- provider-write authority kept separate.

### Protocol foundry skill

- observe repeated institutional procedures;
- classify novelty, risk, rights and secrecy;
- compile stable protocol candidates;
- register private body Vault-first;
- expose contract digest only;
- assign separate owner/verifier;
- append DAIL and archive evidence;
- open commercial candidate without activation.

### Protocol commercialization skill

- build offer candidates from verified/public-safe contracts;
- gather market, cost, tax, legal, security and delivery evidence;
- propose license and fulfillment structures;
- preserve version/entitlement/revocation continuity;
- keep checkout, prices and Stripe objects inactive until approved.

## Recurring schedules

### Credential Binding and Alias Integrity Watch

Hourly condition-watch subroute. It checks registry/audit parity, primary/recovery presence, same-material HMAC, active immutable version, custody coverage, stale consumers and Founder Directive v3 budget semantics.

Allowed self-healing is limited to D0 refresh and approved D1 deterministic custody/recovery repair. Credential mismatches, unregistered rotations, source-precedence defects and public-exposure findings route to independent D2 review. Initial secret insertion remains D3 founder-authorized.

### Continuous Protocol Foundry

Daily condition-watch subroute. It uses stable protocol ID \+ semantic version deduplication, Vault-first custody, public-contract-only projection, independent verification and automatic commercial-candidate creation. It does not create a new external scheduler slot.

### Pricing and Commercialization Review

The existing monthly pricing route now consumes protocol candidates. It may gather evidence and update candidate state but cannot activate checkout or create payment objects.

## MCP/runtime tools

The controlled-test CHLOM Vault Broker exposes:

- `chlom.credential.envelope.create` — founder/admin/service-role only; creates a five-minute one-time public-key envelope; D3;
- `chlom.credential.envelope.bind` — founder/admin/service-role only; accepts client-side encrypted ciphertext and returns a sanitized binding/version/custody receipt; D3;
- `chlom.protocol.register` — Vault-first protocol candidate registration; D1.

The earlier MCP input surface that accepted raw credential material is disabled. The active broker exposes no generic secret-get operation, returns no raw credential or proprietary protocol body, and keeps provider writes outside the custody operation.

## Institutional safeguards

- reuse existing agents before creating new ones;
- owner/verifier separation;
- no self-certification;
- no raw secret or restricted body export;
- no provider mutation from custody operations;
- no automatic checkout, price, license grant or entitlement;
- append or supersede, never silently delete;
- D3 remains human-reserved.
