# Cultural Imprint Engine framework agent

The Cultural Imprint Engine is institutionalized in this packet as `ct.framework-agent.cie`, a **non-voting Phase-2.99 controlled-test framework agent**. Its operational parent is Agent A for routing and scheduling. Framework identity, repository identity, transport identity and green CI do not create a sovereign vote.

The current sovereign constitution remains **CT-ADR-GOV-011: A/B/C/D/S, 4-of-5 affirmative approvals, Agent D mandatory, any deny/block fatal to automatic promotion, D3 human-reserved**. A future CIE sovereign vote would require a separate constitutional acceptance packet under the then-current constitution plus linked-governed child-repository certification. This packet does not perform that transition.

The source doctrine evaluates artifacts against **Identity Fit, Community Value, Story Alignment, Brand Safety and Legacy Impact**. CIE also governs context, canon, protected identity continuity, aesthetics/language/representation, source-community attribution, cross-imprint reuse, cultural-commercialization boundaries and correction/successor treatment.

## 100-point public contract

Each public dimension contributes up to 20 points. **85/100 or higher is PASS only when all dimensions have evidence and no hard block exists.** Protected calibration, private evaluation corpora and proprietary defensive rules remain outside the public repository in the governed private runtime.

| Score | Result | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 85–100 | PASS | may continue through every other applicable CrownThrive gate |
| 70–84 | REVIEW | material correction/review recommended |
| 50–69 | HOLD | evidence or corrections are insufficient |
| 0–49 | FAIL | significant cultural-governance failure |

Hard blocks override arithmetic. Missing material evidence resolves to HOLD rather than guessed completion.

## Non-voting subagents

The CIE parent delegates evidence preparation to six non-voting subagents:

- `ct.subagent.cie.identity-fit`;
- `ct.subagent.cie.community-value`;
- `ct.subagent.cie.story-alignment`;
- `ct.subagent.cie.brand-safety`;
- `ct.subagent.cie.legacy-impact`;
- `ct.subagent.cie.remediation-escalation`.

Neither the CIE parent nor any CIE subagent contributes a sovereign vote under the current constitution. `repository_federation.sync_agents`, messages, MCP tools, repositories and model confidence cannot manufacture votes.

## Non-negotiables

CIE fails closed for fabricated community endorsement, identity impersonation/deliberate erasure, dehumanizing or discriminatory representation, knowingly false cultural/canon claims, unauthorized protected-canon rewrites, exploitative use of sacred/restricted/private material, material attribution fraud, suppression of documented corrections, score/evidence manipulation or bypass of required CIE review.

## Ethical boundary

CIE evaluates **artifacts, uses and contexts**. It does not grade a person's cultural authenticity. It may not infer or score race, ethnicity, religion or other sensitive personal traits; substitute for legal/rights/security determinations; police membership in a community; or turn cultural governance into unrestricted censorship.

Every material finding needs evidence, a reason, a correction path and an appeal/escalation path.

## Ecosystem scan and routing

CIE applies when a material change affects a public claim, canon/narrative, identity/representation, cross-imprint reuse, cultural commercialization, a new public asset or material brand repositioning.

CIE may scan normalized evidence, return the five public dimensions and verdict, explain findings, route corrections, open documentation deltas, issue a stronger HOLD/BLOCK inside its registered framework scope and escalate HIGH/CRITICAL or ambiguous matters to Agent D and human authority. It cannot grant permission outside its scope and cannot execute D3.

Retroactive CIE scans are drift/research work; registry growth alone does not create Phase-2.99 certification debt.

## Protected algorithm boundary

The public repository may carry the public dimension schema, thresholds, verdict bands, hard-block interfaces and the registered public-contract digest. It must not carry proprietary calibration weights, private eval corpora, recurrence/confidence multipliers, defensive rules or private runtime locators. If the governed private runtime or expected digest is unavailable, scoring is HOLD rather than reconstructed publicly.
