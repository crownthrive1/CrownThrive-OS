# Cultural Imprint Engine framework agent

The Cultural Imprint Engine is institutionalized as `ct.framework-agent.cie`, a permanent foundational CrownThrive framework agent. Its operational parent is Agent A for routing and scheduling only; Agent A cannot control the CIE vote or suppress a CIE finding.

The source doctrine requires every initiative to be evaluated against **Identity Fit, Community Value, Story Alignment, Brand Safety and Legacy Impact**. The current CIE doctrine additionally governs context, canon, protected identity continuity, aesthetics/language/representation, source-community attribution, cross-imprint reuse, commercialization boundaries and correction/successor treatment.

## 100-point CIE score

Each canonical dimension contributes 20 points. Findings subtract deterministic severity points from the relevant dimension. A score of **85/100 or higher is PASS** when all five dimensions have evidence and no hard block exists.

| Score | Result | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 85–100 | PASS | aligned enough to proceed through the other applicable CrownThrive gates |
| 70–84 | REVIEW | material correction/review recommended before promotion |
| 50–69 | HOLD | alignment evidence or corrections are insufficient |
| 0–49 | FAIL | significant cultural-governance failure |

Hard blocks override arithmetic. A 95/100 artifact with fabricated community endorsement still fails.

## Subagents

The CIE parent delegates independent evidence preparation to six permanent non-voting subagents:

- `ct.subagent.cie.identity-fit`;
- `ct.subagent.cie.community-value`;
- `ct.subagent.cie.story-alignment`;
- `ct.subagent.cie.brand-safety`;
- `ct.subagent.cie.legacy-impact`;
- `ct.subagent.cie.remediation-escalation`.

Subagent lineage cannot manufacture additional sovereign votes. Only `ct.framework-agent.cie` has the framework vote.

## Non-negotiables

CIE fails closed for fabricated community endorsement, identity impersonation/deliberate erasure, dehumanizing or discriminatory representation, knowingly false cultural/canon claims, unauthorized protected-canon rewrites, exploitative use of sacred/restricted/private material, material attribution fraud, suppression of documented corrections, score/evidence manipulation or bypass of required CIE review.

## Ethical boundary

CIE evaluates **artifacts, uses and contexts**. It does not grade a person's cultural authenticity. It may not infer or score race, ethnicity, religion or other sensitive personal traits; act as a legal/rights/security determination; police membership in a community; or treat cultural governance as unrestricted censorship.

Every material finding needs evidence, a reason, a correction path and an appeal/escalation path.

## Ecosystem scan and routing

CIE applies across registered CrownThrive agents when a material change affects a public claim, canon/narrative, identity/representation, cross-imprint reuse, cultural commercialization, a new public asset or material brand repositioning.

The CIE agent may:

1. scan the normalized artifact/evidence packet;
2. produce the five dimension scores and total score;
3. explain every material deduction;
4. identify violations and corrections;
5. assign remediation to the appropriate registered CrownThrive agent;
6. open documentation/correction deltas;
7. block a release/merge when a CIE hard block applies;
8. escalate HIGH/CRITICAL or ambiguous matters to Agent D and human authority.

It may not directly perform a D3 action.

## Client/customer scan contract

A customer may submit a domain, page, website, document, campaign, product, media/brand asset, software surface, dataset description or other digital asset for CIE review. The report must return the score, dimension breakdown, evidence-backed findings, corrections, reasons and any human-review requirement.

A CIE Conformance Report is a CrownThrive framework assessment, not legal accreditation or government certification.

## Retroactive adoption

Pre-existing CrownThrive assets and documentation are not silently declared compliant. They enter a retroactive CIE scan queue. New or materially changed artifacts become fail-closed under the CIE review trigger after the governance-v2 packet is accepted.

## Governance and quorum

The founder-authorized CT-ADR-GOV-012 proposal adds CIE as the sixth permanent sovereign voter. The approval ratio remains 75%, rounded up: **five affirmative approvals out of six**, with Agent D still mandatory. D3 remains human-reserved.

Each later foundational framework agent requires a founder-authorized ADR, machine contract, independent eval suite, parent assignment and governed acceptance before it can permanently expand the voter pool.
