# PentaGeneration™

PentaGeneration is CrownThrive’s seven-generation continuity subsystem. It exists so institutional truth, rights, cultural meaning, operating knowledge, recoverability, and succession context survive leadership, platform, provider, market, and technology change.

<Info>
  **Service contract:** `ct.pentageneration.v1`<br />**Version:** `1.0.0`<br />**Owner:** CrownThrive, LLC<br />**Runtime:** ThriveBase \+ authenticated `penta-generation` service<br />**Authority:** bounded; continuity evidence never manufactures legal, founder-reserved, economic, security, rights, or destructive authority.
</Info>

## The seven-generation horizon

| Generation | Code | Focus |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | `G1-ORIGIN` | Origin & Founder Intent — preserve purpose, authority, canon, provenance, and operating truth. |
| 2 | `G2-TRANSFER` | Transfer Readiness — make knowledge, runbooks, roles, and decision context inheritable. |
| 3 | `G3-RESILIENCE` | Operational Resilience — survive founder absence, provider failure, staff turnover, and platform drift. |
| 4 | `G4-CULTURE` | Cultural Continuity — preserve Cultural Imprint Engine meaning, community obligations, and narrative context. |
| 5 | `G5-ASSETS` | Rights, Capital & Asset Stewardship — preserve IP provenance, licensing, archives, financial rails, and custody. |
| 6 | `G6-ADAPTATION` | Adaptive Governance — evolve technology, markets, and leadership without severing lineage. |
| 7 | `G7-LEGACY` | Legacy & Regeneration — enable future stewards to prove what changed, why, under whose authority, and what remained invariant. |

## What PentaGeneration preserves

The v1 continuity registry covers eight primary asset classes:

- canon and doctrine;
- source code and release history;
- runtime state and recovery evidence;
- rights and intellectual-property lineage;
- Cultural Imprint Engine context;
- institutional documentation and archives;
- PentaBooks publishing canon and edition lineage;
- provider and integration bindings.

Protected evidence, private records, credentials, secret implementation details, restricted rights evidence, and unreleased assets remain outside public documentation.

## Runtime model

ThriveBase materializes PentaGeneration through seven governed record families:

- `penta_generation_system_state`
- `penta_generation_horizons`
- `penta_generation_assets`
- `penta_generation_bindings`
- `penta_generation_events`
- `penta_generation_handoffs`
- `penta_generation_proofs`

Two bounded internal functions provide machine-readable evaluation and status:

- `penta_generation_evaluate_v1(jsonb)`
- `penta_generation_status_v1()`

The authenticated Edge Function `penta-generation` exposes the current service contract with JWT verification enabled.

## Continuity evaluation

PentaGeneration scores seven independent dimensions from 0–100:

1. governance
2. archives
3. succession
4. rights
5. operations
6. culture
7. recovery

Classification is deterministic:

| Score | State |
| --- | --- |
| 90–100 | `continuity_ready` |
| 75–89.99 | `bounded_ready` |
| 60–74.99 | `at_risk` |
| below 60 | `continuity_failure` |

A continuity score is a readiness signal, not an ownership instrument, legal determination, license, approval, or authorization for destructive change.

## System bindings

PentaGeneration is an interoperability layer, not a replacement for existing systems:

- **ThriveBase** stores runtime state, continuity ledgers, proofs, and handoff records.
- **CHLOM** governs rights, evidence, provenance, licensing, roles, rules, revenue, records, and remedies.
- **Cultural Imprint Engine (CIE)** preserves cultural meaning, community obligations, protected context, and interpretation lineage.
- **PentaBooks** preserves manuscript, source, edition, and publishing-canon inheritance.
- **PentaFactory** supplies software-production continuity when its runtime binding is independently certified.
- **PentaFramework Factory** supplies framework/version inheritance when independently certified.
- **PentaDocs** preserves public institutional knowledge, current-state documentation, and superseded historical versions.
- **GitHub repository family** preserves versioned source, review, release, and recovery history.

`declared`, `registered`, and `verified` are deliberately separate states. Naming a binding does not prove it is live.

## Generational handoff contract

A handoff must preserve the applicable entity authority, ownership, licensing, rights, governance, and founder-reserved constraints then in force. A continuity handoff does **not** imply a transfer of CrownThrive ownership.

Minimum handoff evidence includes:

- canonical-source proof;
- authority map;
- asset and rights lineage;
- operating and recovery runbooks;
- archive-integrity evidence;
- dependency/provider map;
- continuity evaluation;
- unresolved risks and holds;
- correction and rollback paths.

## Continuity invariants

PentaGeneration enforces six institutional invariants:

1. Preserve truth.
2. Preserve rights.
3. Preserve culture.
4. Preserve recoverability.
5. Make succession testable.
6. Never silently rewrite canon.

Corrections append. Superseded material remains discoverable as history. Future stewards must be able to determine which record controlled at a given point in time.

## Completion standard

A continuity transition is complete only when the receiving generation can independently locate governing records, distinguish current from historical state, identify authority and rights boundaries, execute the documented recovery path, reproduce required verification, and understand unresolved holds without relying on undocumented personal memory.
