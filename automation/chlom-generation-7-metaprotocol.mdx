# CHLOM Generation 7 Metaprotocol

**State:** CONTROLLED TEST / GOVERNED HOLD.  
**Phase effect:** none. Phase 2 / 2.99 remains current and Phase 3 remains blocked by the existing hard-exit contract.

CHLOM means **Compliance Hybrid Licensing and Ownership Model**. DAIL means **Decentralized Autonomous Information Ledger**. Generation 7 extends the Gen-6 bounded portfolio model with deterministic evidence scoring, protected-capability brokerage, provisional CHLOM identity/DID references, website-remediation scoring and commercialization candidates.

## Resource-budget semantics

The canonical local monthly request-budget sentinel is:

- `-1` — no CrownThrive local monthly ceiling;
- `0` — disabled;
- positive integer — CrownThrive local monthly ceiling.

This sentinel never overrides provider throttles, quotas, billing units, plan capacity or legal/contractual restrictions. A provider can remain billable or rate-limited even when CrownThrive's local monthly ceiling is `-1`.

## Deterministic scoring family

Generation 7 defines six public contracts with restricted implementations:

| ID | Public name | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `ct.alg.gen7.eggs` | Evidence-Grounded Governance Score | Measure evidence sufficiency/quality before a governance recommendation. |
| `ct.alg.gen7.bbis` | Bottleneck Burn-Down Impact Score | Prioritize work by dependency unlock, blocker severity and expected closure value. |
| `ct.alg.gen7.seorps` | SEO Remediation Priority Score | Rank crawlability, accessibility and SEO remediation packets from dated evidence. |
| `ct.alg.gen7.gds` | Governance Decision Sufficiency Score | Decide whether evidence is sufficient for internal resolution, independent verification, governed review or human adjudication. |
| `ct.alg.gen7.licv` | Licensing Value Score | Support candidate licensing/value research without creating a live price or operative license. |
| `ct.alg.gen7.acu` | Agent Capacity Utilization Score | Measure WIP utilization, completion, blocker burn-down, review latency and retry efficiency. |

Exact weights, score thresholds, calibration, private features and evaluation material remain in restricted Vault custody. Public consumers receive only the contract, version, score/band where authorized, reason classes and immutable digests.

A score is evidence processing, not truth. It cannot manufacture missing evidence, rights, authority, legal compliance, accessibility conformance or certification.

## Decision sufficiency

The current controlled-test decision path is:

`evidence -> deterministic score -> immutable receipt -> authority class -> candidate disposition -> independent verification / governance / human boundary`

D0/D1 high-evidence work may resolve internally only when the underlying operation is already permitted by policy. D2 remains independently verified. D3 and qualified-professional/reserved-human questions remain human-adjudicated regardless of score.

## Gen-7 support mesh

Generation 7 adds no external scheduler slots and no new sovereign voters.

- **Agent Q — Evidence Governor:** evidence scoring, contradiction sufficiency and agent-efficiency receipts.
- **Agent R — Remediation Optimizer:** bottleneck scoring, SEO priority and bounded remediation planning.
- **Agent T — Value & Licensing Analyst:** market evidence, licensing-value scoring and commercialization candidates.

All three are A2/D2 maximum, non-voting, parent-subroute identities. A/B/C/D/S remain the sovereign voter pool and Agent D remains mandatory under the current constitution.

## Vault-first implementation boundary

Novel algorithms, canons, moats, business methods, scheduling methods, protected scripts, credentials, private policy bodies and comparable institutional IP are classified before use. When protected, their bodies remain encrypted/private and are consumed through allowlisted server-side capability adapters or opaque handles. Models, public docs and ordinary clients do not receive raw credentials, private keys, protected algorithm bodies or private identity IDs.

## Hybrid identity and provisional DIDs

Generation 7 preserves stable public fingerprints and restricted non-correlatable private mappings. Provisional `did:chlom:` records are internal/test bindings for portability and later anchoring. They are **not** a claim that a public blockchain, DID method registry, verification key or verifiable credential has been production-certified.

Fingerprints establish bounded record/version integrity only. They do not establish ownership, authorship, legality, deployment, certification or biometric identity.

## Website-remediation example

Fresh dated crawl evidence can be transformed into a bounded remediation packet rather than manually reinterpreted by every agent. Current controlled-test examples include crawlability repair and accessibility/alternative-text remediation. Public documentation exposes only sanitized state and scores; provider project identifiers and raw audit evidence remain restricted.

## Commercialization boundary

CHLOM Gen-7 licensing tiers and prices may be researched as **candidate economics**. A candidate price does not create a live offer. Checkout, Stripe objects, operative license terms, customer entitlements and production distribution remain separately gated by rights, pricing authorization, counsel/tax review where required, fulfillment, security and activation evidence.

The protected kernel is not transferred merely because access to a licensed capability is sold.

## History and archives

Generation 7 is additive. Superseded logic remains queryable in Git/DAIL/THIVEBASE/Drive history. Monthly encrypted custody archives create immutable revisions; the current month can advance to a newer revision but prior revisions are never silently deleted.
