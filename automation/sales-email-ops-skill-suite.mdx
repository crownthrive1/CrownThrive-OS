# Sales & Email Operations Skill Suite

The Sales & Email Operations V2 build is decomposed into six reusable governed skills. The skills are operational contracts, not independent sovereign agents and not a grant of unrestricted email, legal, financial, rights, or credential authority.

## Skills

| Skill | Primary job | Autonomy | Ceiling |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sales Inbox Triage | classify reason, priority, lifecycle and P0 escalation | A2 | D1 |
| Lead Legitimacy & Scam Vetting | research identity and risk before engagement | A1 | D1 |
| Inbound Lead Nurture & Close | qualify and advance routine opportunities within adopted terms | A2 | D2 |
| Locticians Claimable-Profile Outreach | research, personalize and conditionally send claim outreach | A2 | D2 |
| Follow-Up, Suppression & Opt-Out | bounded cadence, duplicate prevention and hard-stop enforcement | A2 | D1 |
| Sales Continuity & Evidence | reconcile mailbox, restricted CRM, Collab, Drive and documentation state | A2 | D1 |

## Shared rules

All skills must:

- determine the reason for contact before acting;
- use evidence-backed personalization and never invent business facts;
- preserve P0 founder escalation for court/legal, material financial/transaction, personal/sensitive, credential/security, binding-contract, exclusivity and exceptional-rights matters;
- stop on suppression, opt-out, legal complaint, unresolved identity or material risk hold;
- preserve raw correspondence and private prospect data in restricted systems rather than public documentation;
- verify provider outcomes after consequential actions;
- remain nonvoting and unable to self-approve reserved actions.

## Cold-outreach gate

Locticians cold outreach is fail-closed. Sending requires the governed outbound configuration to confirm commercial outreach is enabled, a valid physical postal address is configured, the recipient is not suppressed, the contact source is verified, and the profile remains claimable. If any predicate fails, the skill may research and queue only.

## Source contract

Machine-readable suite state is defined in `developers/manifests/sales-email-ops-skill-suite.v2.json`. Individual reusable instructions are under `skills/sales-email-ops-v2/*/SKILL.md`.

## Public / restricted boundary

This page documents public-safe behavior only. Raw emails, private contacts, provider message IDs, suppression records, lead research, legal/sensitive escalations, credentials, and restricted commercial evidence remain outside the public repository.
