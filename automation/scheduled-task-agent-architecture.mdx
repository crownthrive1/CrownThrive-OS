## Purpose

CrownThrive does not treat a scheduler entry as an anonymous reminder. Every durable scheduled or conditional workflow should resolve to a stable governed agent identity with an explicit role, authority ceiling, tool/data boundary, schedule, evidence contract, escalation path, and retirement/supersession state.

The scheduler is an execution trigger. It is **not** the institutional source of authority.

## Current production topology

As of August 24, 2026, the canonical scheduler topology is `ct.scheduler-topology.production.v1` and is **production-certified**. The former one-agent-one-external-ChatGPT-clock model is superseded where a compatible internal or folded route exists.

```text
external failure-domain clocks
├─ CrownThrive External Evidence Relay
├─ CrownThrive Communications Watch
├─ CrownThrive Daily Backup & Continuity
└─ CrownThrive Maintenance Coordinator   # temporary while targeted maintenance is open

internal execution fabric
├─ stable CHLOM / ThriveBase agent identities
├─ owner/verifier work packets and internal subroutes
├─ pg_cron for database-native recurrence
└─ Mintlify workflows for documentation recurrence
```

The normal post-maintenance external-clock target is **three**: evidence relay, communications, and backup/continuity. The maintenance coordinator is a temporary distinct recovery failure domain.

The production stress suite passed with zero retired clocks enabled, zero pending scheduler dispositions, zero unreconciled schedules, zero enabled D3 external clocks, and deterministic repeatability. Targeted maintenance currently suspends the external evidence relay without changing the topology's production state.

See [Agent Scheduler Topology Production Convergence](/developers/certification/agent-scheduler-topology-production-convergence-2026-08-24) and the machine contract `developers/contracts/agent-scheduler-topology-production.v1.json`.

        ↓
CHLOM Orchestrator
        ↓

        ↓

        ↓
Permission + risk + suppression + evidence gates
        ↓
Provider task executes
        ↓
Readback / outcome / DAIL evidence
        ↓
GitHub + private registry + documentation reconciliation

## Canonical layers

### 1. Reserved human authority

Founder, legal, finance, security, rights and other qualified-human decisions remain explicit. D3/A4 actions cannot be manufactured by a schedule, model output, provider task, workflow success, or agent quorum.

### 2. CHLOM orchestration

`ct.chlom.agent.orchestrator` is the operating parent for governed machine execution. It routes bounded packets to the institutional relay, specialist agents, operational watches, sales agents, content/governance agents, and reusable skill packages.

### 3. Sovereign relay identities

The five sovereign relay identities remain the governed voter pool, but they no longer require five independent external ChatGPT scheduler slots. Their logical cadence, packet ownership, verifier identity and voting rules are preserved inside the institutional execution fabric.

| Agent | Role | Historical external cadence | Current scheduling treatment | Vote |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `ct.relay.agent-a` | Orchestrator & Integrator | hourly `:00` | folded/internal execution identity | yes |
| `ct.relay.agent-b` | Evidence & Reconciliation Auditor | hourly `:15` | folded/internal execution identity | yes |
| `ct.relay.agent-c` | Build & Documentation Executor | hourly `:30` | folded/internal execution identity | yes |
| `ct.relay.agent-d` | Independent Gatekeeper & Handoff Steward | hourly `:45` | folded/internal execution identity | yes, mandatory |
| `ct.relay.agent-s` | Security & Resilience Sentinel | hourly `:55` | folded/internal execution identity | yes |

Their former standalone external clocks are retained only as disabled historical scaffolding and are dispositioned as `RETIRED_SCHEDULING_SCAFFOLDING`. A shared external evidence-relay clock does not proxy, combine or manufacture their votes.

Automatic D0-D2 promotion still requires the applicable sovereign approvals, mandatory Agent D participation where required, no deny/block, applicable specialist evidence, rollback, and current-head validation. D3 remains human-reserved.

### 4. Non-voting specialist layer

The standing specialist lane is:

- `ct.specialist.agent-e` — API/MCP and federation engineering;
- `ct.specialist.agent-f` — source reconciliation, articleization and public-safe projection;
- `ct.specialist.agent-g` — domain, engine and provider current-state certification;
- `ct.specialist.agent-h` — webhook, provider-delivery and federation-path certification.

Specialists contribute evidence and bounded execution. They do not create sovereign votes.

### 5. Operational and governance watches

Operational identities such as provider/engine watch, ThriveBase integration health, credential continuity, engine/infrastructure health, homepage reconciliation, Virality canon drift and provider-delivery review remain stable institutional agents. Connector-dependent recurrence is now serviced through the **CrownThrive External Evidence Relay** when due; database-native recurrence stays on `pg_cron`; documentation recurrence stays on Mintlify workflows.

Artifact continuity is bound to the registered PentaGeneration identity `ct.agent.pentageneration-continuity`, successor task contract `ct.task.asset-registry-hourly.v5`, and the [Asset Continuity & Institutional Artifact Operations](/crownthrive-os/runbooks/asset-continuity) runbook. Its **hourly** incremental scan is a due logical subroute of **CrownThrive External Evidence Relay**; its 23:00 `America/New_York` deep convergence is a due logical subroute of **CrownThrive Daily Backup & Continuity**. Both routes share one private task binding, one durable cursor and one receipt lineage. The preserved v4 standalone-task evidence remains only for provider migration and recovery; after the v5 subroute produces a verified receipt, any redundant standalone clock must be retired with exact provider readback. This creates no external clock and does not expand the agent's A1/D1 default authority.

These agents may observe, prepare, reconcile, and perform bounded reversible actions according to their individual A/D limits. They do not inherit sovereign vote authority from the relay clock or from being scheduled. A compatible duty must not create a new external recurring task merely because it has a distinct logical agent identity.

### 6. Sales & growth agents

Sales & Email Operations V2 still separates sales work into reusable specialist identities and skills:

- Sales Inbox Triage;
- Lead Legitimacy & Scam Vetting;
- Inbound Lead Nurture & Close;
- Locticians Claimable-Profile Outreach;
- Follow-Up, Suppression & Opt-Out;
- Sales Continuity & Evidence.

Their external recurrence is consolidated into **CrownThrive Communications Watch** rather than maintaining separate Sales Desk and Locticians scheduler slots. During targeted maintenance this failure domain is read-only for founder attention and urgent inbound alerts. After the governed commercial/outbound reactivation wave, it may route eligible sales and outreach work under the original specialist identities and compliance gates.

Sensitive, legal, financial, credential, binding-contract and exceptional-rights matters remain at the appropriate human boundary; consolidation of the clock does not consolidate authority.

Provider task identifiers and private routing details are restricted operational evidence and are not required in the public documentation projection.

The August 21, 2026 reconciliation recorded a historical snapshot of **20 active provider-task bindings**. That count is preserved as lineage only and no longer describes the production scheduler surface. Current runtime state resolves from `chlom_runtime.scheduler_topology_v1`, `chlom_runtime.automation_agent_bindings`, `chlom_runtime.agent_schedule_definitions`, the active external task readback, `pg_cron`, and Mintlify workflows. ## Task lifecycle

```text
REQUESTED
→ AGENT_BOUND
→ SKILL_BOUND
→ SCHEDULED / CONDITION_WATCH
→ ACTIVE
→ HEARTBEAT / RUN EVIDENCE
→ VERIFIED / WATCH / HOLD / ACTION_REQUIRED
→ PAUSED / SUPERSEDED / RETIRED
```

A disabled or completed provider task remains historical evidence; it does not remain an active agent execution surface.

## Identity and continuity

Stable agent identity is independent of scheduler implementation. Agent identity may include UUID, candidate DID, fingerprint commitment, repository binding, authority ceiling, heartbeat and evidence references. Those identity dimensions are not interchangeable:

- UUID identifies the agent record;
- DID state addresses decentralized identity readiness;
- fingerprint commitment addresses integrity/version binding;
- repository binding addresses execution/source custody;
- heartbeat addresses liveness;
- vote eligibility addresses governance authority.

A valid heartbeat does not prove authority. A candidate DID does not prove certification. A scheduled task does not create a voter.

## Agent-to-skill relationship

A reusable skill defines how a capability operates. An agent owns or invokes one or more skills. A task invokes an agent on a cadence or condition.

```text
Task → Agent → Skill(s) → Tools / Data → Evidence → Review
```

This prevents large scheduler prompts from becoming the only copy of institutional behavior. Material scheduler logic should be decomposed into versioned skills, public-safe operating contracts, machine manifests, and private runtime records.

## Sales example

The Locticians claim route demonstrates the production model:

```text
CrownThrive Communications Watch
→ due logical Locticians outreach subroute
→ ct.chlom.agent.locticians-claim-outreach
→ ct.skill.sales.locticians-claim-outreach.v2
→ research + claim-state + suppression gates
→ governed offer lookup
→ personalized outreach candidate
→ compliance / commercial-reactivation gate
→ provider send or HOLD
→ follow-up/suppression agent
→ sales continuity/evidence agent
```

The shared communications clock does not become the sales originator and does not bypass the specialist identity. A coupon, prospect or email body is not embedded as public institutional state. The public contract describes the behavior; the private CRM stores the current offer, prospect, delivery and suppression evidence.

## Hard boundaries

- No task creates authority by existing.
- No subagent or specialist becomes a voter through scheduling.
- No agent self-approves a material action.
- D3 remains human-reserved.
- Secrets, credentials, raw email bodies, private recipients, customer records and restricted evidence stay private.
- Commercial claims resolve from current governed price/offer records rather than stale prompts.
- Suppression and opt-out are checked at execution time, not only when a campaign is created.
- Provider capability and configured scheduler state do not prove successful execution; outcome/readback evidence is required.
- Paused, completed and superseded tasks remain lineage records rather than being silently deleted.
- Compatible recurring duties fold into the nearest certified internal or external failure domain; a new external task requires a materially distinct cadence, authority, data-access, security or failure-domain need.
- `RETIRED_SCHEDULING_SCAFFOLDING` retires a clock, not the underlying institutional agent identity.

## Completion standard

A scheduled workflow is institutionally complete only when the current provider task is bound to a stable agent, its governing skill and authority are known, private/public boundaries are classified, execution evidence can be recovered, failures have an escalation/rollback route, and the next run can inherit exact state without rediscovering the workflow from chat.
