## Penta Family™ Interoperability Fabric

**Status:** Production protocol control plane  
**Protocol:** `1.0.0`  
**Runtime:** `runtime/penta_interop.py`  
**Registry:** `data/penta/interoperability.registry.json`  
**Envelope:** `schemas/penta/interoperability-envelope.schema.json`  
**Certification:** `.github/workflows/penta-interoperability-production.yml`

The Penta Family interoperability fabric is the common machine contract that makes every registered Penta addressable without collapsing the responsibility, maturity, authority, provider, or human-governance boundaries between systems.

Interoperability is not permission. A route, tool, credential, successful test, production family label, or valid message never creates authority.

## Production invariant

Every registered Penta participates in the same bounded lifecycle:

**Discover → Govern → Execute → Verify → Preserve**

The common envelope carries the exact source and target machine keys, operation, requested effect, risk class, trace/correlation identifiers, idempotency key, evidence references, authority trace, PentaHybrid gate state, provider-binding reference, readback strategy, metadata, and SHA-256 receipt.

Unknown members fail closed. Duplicate machine identities fail closed. Provider effects without a certified binding and readback fail closed. Consequential execution without CHLOM/DAIL authority and an accountable owner fails closed.

## All-Penta rule

Every family member has a protocol address. That includes members at `specified`, `implemented`, `certified`, `production`, `hold`, and `retired` maturity.

Addressability does **not** mean execution eligibility:

- `specified` and `implemented` members may participate in bounded analyze/prepare/route/verify/preserve handoffs where their own domain rules allow.
- `certified` and `production` are the only maturity states that may be considered for `execute`.
- `hold` and `retired` members fail closed.
- Execution requires both source and target to be execution-eligible.
- D2/D3 work requires the applicable PentaHybrid human gate.
- Provider work requires PentaCredentials/PentaCertify-backed provider binding plus exact readback.
- Child maturity is never promoted by the interoperability fabric.

## Institutional-control integration

PentaCompliance and PentaLicense are `production` for their bounded deterministic internal control-plane scope. PentaPrivacy, PentaIdentity, PentaData, PentaRecords, PentaProcure, PentaVendor, PentaContracts, and PentaQuality remain `implemented`.

All are addressable through the production fabric. The two production controls may execute only inside their declared internal scope; legal, provider, economic, and D3 effects retain independent authority, human, binding, and readback gates. Implemented controls remain ineligible for execution until independently certified or promoted.

## Certification

The production workflow validates:

1. family and protocol registries;
2. machine schemas;
3. Python compilation across the shared Penta runtime;
4. Penta Family census/portal tests;
5. institutional-service and institutional-control tests;
6. PentaMail/runtime-suite tests;
7. Penta observability tests and self-test;
8. provider-control-plane contracts;
9. exact equality between runtime Penta inventory and Penta Family census;
10. complete Penta dependency resolution;
11. required interoperability and observability spines;
12. zero protocol blockers.

A failing, missing, stale, or contradictory exact-head result is `HOLD`. Documentation, a mergeable PR, or a production label cannot substitute for the certification receipt.

## Evidence and continuity

Certification emits `ct.penta.interoperability-certification.v1` with:

- family member count;
- runtime inventory member count;
- addressable member count;
- execution-eligible member count;
- per-member coverage;
- missing-spine findings;
- dependency gaps;
- provider contract errors;
- family contract errors;
- final `PASS` or `HOLD`.

Receipts are deterministic SHA-256 evidence. PentaTrace carries execution lineage, PentaLogger preserves structured evidence, PentaError normalizes failures, PentaMetric records runtime measurements, PentaStatus projects current state, PentaScribe preserves institutional terminology/change lineage, PentaGeneration preserves long-horizon continuity, and PentaDocs projects the governed record.

## Constitutional boundary

**Penta Family interoperability may make every Penta reachable. It may never make every Penta powerful.**

Power remains bounded by exact maturity, authority, role, capability, provider certification, human gates, and verified readback.
