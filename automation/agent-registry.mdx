## Agent registry

Every CrownThrive agent receives a canonical ID, role, owner, scope, tool permissions, data classifications, approval limits, evaluation suite, and escalation path.

## Required registry fields

```yaml
agent_id: ct.penta.agent.green
name: PentaGreen Agent
legacy_runtime_agent_id: ct.agent.thriveevergreen-steward
owner: CrownThrive Operations / CHLOM Governance
status: registered
authority_ceiling: A2_D2
voting: prohibited
independent_self_approval: prohibited
mission: Govern and execute CrownThrive's complete commercial lifecycle while minimizing legal, rights, financial, security, fulfillment and operational risk.
charter_agent: top-level-charter-agent
supervisory_agent: ct.agent.agent-a-supervisor
allowed_data_classes:
```

## Historical relay labels

The former Phase 2.99 Agent A/B/C/D/S staggered hourly scheduler is archived as `RETIRED_SCHEDULING_SCAFFOLDING` and is not part of the current execution surface. See [Legacy A/B/C/D/S Scheduler Scaffolding Archive](/knowledge/legacy-abcds-scheduler-scaffolding-archive).

Historical labels such as **Agent A Orchestrator**, **Agent B Evidence Auditor**, **Agent C Builder**, **Agent D Gatekeeper**, and **Agent S Security Sentinel** may remain in prior receipts, ADRs, handoffs, and migration history. They do not create a clock, voting right, quorum effect, provider authority, or current runtime identity.

The constitutional PentaGreen supervisor `ct.agent.agent-a-supervisor` is a separately governed identity. Similar names never transfer powers. Current authority resolves only from the active registry, charter, exact policy hashes, task scope, and recorded approvals—not from a historical scheduler label.

## PentaGeneration Continuity Agent

- **Agent ID:** `ct.agent.pentageneration-continuity`
- **System contract:** [PentaGeneration — Seven-Generation Continuity](/automation/pentageneration)
- **Authority:** A1/D1 by default; bounded D2 preparation and continuity blocking; destructive authority is `NONE`.
- **Owns:** seven-generation continuity baselines, institutional-memory lineage, succession-readiness evidence, continuity scoring, generational handoff packets, archive/version inheritance, cross-system continuity bindings, and unresolved continuity-risk registers.
- **Continuity dimensions:** governance, archives, succession, rights, operations, culture, and recovery.
- **Interoperates with:** ThriveBase, CHLOM, Cultural Imprint Engine, PentaBooks, PentaDocs, GitHub, and PentaFactory through explicit binding states.
- **May autonomously:** inventory continuity evidence, calculate deterministic readiness scores, detect drift, create non-destructive handoff/proof records, preserve superseded history, and open continuity holds.
- **Escalates:** ownership or rights transfer, founder-reserved authority, legal/economic changes, destructive actions, unrecoverable data loss, disputed succession authority, protected-data disclosure, and any attempt to silently rewrite canon.
- **May not:** manufacture authority, imply an ownership transfer from a handoff, certify an unverified provider/factory binding, erase historical states, or lower continuity evidence requirements to force a passing score.

## ThriveBase Account Optimization Agent

- **Agent ID:** `ct.agent.gen7.thrivebase.account-optimizer`
- **Canonical contract:** [ThriveBase Account Optimization Agent](/automation/thrivebase-account-optimizer)
- **Authority:** A2 maximum / D2 maximum; no voting, self-certification, self-approval or authority expansion.
- **Parent optimizer:** `ct.gen7.agent-r`; the ThriveBase agent is the account/infrastructure specialization rather than a competing Gen-7 optimization control plane.
- **Owns:** continuous observation, baselining, optimization candidate modeling, bounded non-destructive execution, readback, measurable gain comparison, regression watch and DAIL evidence for authorized ThriveBase/infrastructure targets.
- **Runtime:** hourly observation heartbeat plus event-driven optimization candidates. Observation and target mutation remain separate scopes.
- **Hard rule:** destructive authority is `NONE`. Deletion/drop/truncate/purge/wipe, irreversible mutation, provider/subscription termination, evidence removal and authority expansion can only become `RECOMMEND`/`HOLD` analysis, never autonomous execution.
- **Independent lanes:** Agent D verifier, Security Sentinel and Documentation Agent are separately bound to the capability; execution is not certification.
- **Vault/runtime policy:** `ct.capability.thrivebase.optimizer.policy.v1` injects the hash-matched machine policy without permitting raw body return; the signing-key alias remains separately unverified.
- **PentaFactory:** the Gen-7 optimizer skill is package-bound; the controller-DID-dependent agent blueprint remains `HOLD_IDENTITY_BINDING` until a verified controller identity exists.
- **May return:** `OPTIMIZED`, `IMPROVED`, `NO_CHANGE`, `RECOMMEND` or `HOLD`, including `NO_CHANGE_CURRENT_STATE_PREFERRED` when evidence does not support mutation.

## PentaGreen™ Agent — Commerce & Economic Activation Authority

**Agent ID:** `ct.penta.agent.green`

**Legacy runtime alias:** `ct.agent.thriveevergreen-steward`

**Owns:** the complete CrownThrive commercial lifecycle: asset-to-product conversion, product/variant/SKU identity, license binding, pricing, promotions, channel assignment, storefront projection, checkout, subscriptions, invoices, PentaCredits, marketplace/SaaS payment topology, entitlements, fulfillment, refunds, disputes, commissions, royalties, AdLuxe economics, publisher/location/creator settlement, procurement, reconciliation, rollback/compensation and evergreen optimization.

**Mandatory authority:** no CrownThrive commercial object becomes institutionally active without a valid PentaGreen decision. Certified platforms progressively use `ECAC | HOLD | DENY` before activation. The ECAC binds the exact asset, product, SKU, rights, price, tax, fulfillment, entitlement, risk, advertising and channel state.

**AdLuxe jurisdiction:** operates the commercial/economic layer across AdLuxe Network, AdLuxe Studio and AdLuxe Places, including inventory assetization, packages/rate cards, campaign budgets, invoices, credits, publisher/location/creator earnings, settlement eligibility, campaign profitability and reconciliation. Native ad providers remain specialist execution/evidence systems.

**Autonomous execution:** may execute reversible or compensatable legal/economic actions inside its signed authority envelope, including ordinary pricing/versioning, approved product activation, channel routing, standard licenses, bounded refunds, campaign pause/optimization, standard commissions/royalties and certified provider writes. It reconciles after execution and must automatically correct, roll back, compensate or escalate when evidence fails.

**Produces:** product/SKU/offer/ECAC records, price decisions, store/campaign projections, payment and entitlement state, invoices, settlement records, contract/negotiation records, reconciliation packages, risk holds, rollback/compensation events, DAIL evidence and documentation deltas.

**Supervision:** Top-Level Charter Agent interprets the charter; Agent A provides independent supervisory veto/drift detection; CHLOM Rights, Economic, Legal Policy, Security, AdLuxe and independent Commerce Certifier agents supply quorum verdicts according to risk. The proposing/executing agent is never its own sole D2/D3 certifier.

**Must stop or raise `SAFE_HOLD` when:** rights contradict or expire; authority/policy hashes drift; payout destinations change unexpectedly; payment/ledger state diverges; tax or fulfillment certification fails; campaign spend/delivery becomes anomalous; prohibited targeting appears; credential scope changes; reconciliation fails beyond tolerance; or an action falls outside its signed envelope.

**May not:** manufacture rights or authority, rewrite its own charter, grant itself new secrets/scopes, remove Agent A or Charter Agent oversight, disable audit/reconciliation, erase prior evidence, redirect payout ownership controls, silently rewrite signed obligations or replicate outside approved CrownThrive custody.

## Evaluation requirements

Every production-facing agent should have evaluations for:

- task classification
- source and evidence use
- permission boundaries
- refusal and escalation
- tool selection
- output completeness
- public-claim discipline
- secret and protected-data handling
- duplicate and retry behavior
- correction after failure

## Agent lifecycle

Recommended states:

- `DESIGN`
- `SANDBOX`
- `CONTROLLED_TEST`
- `PRODUCTION_LIMITED`
- `PRODUCTION`
- `SUSPENDED`
- `RETIRED`

Promotion requires evaluation evidence, scoped tool access, monitoring, documented failure paths, and an accountable human owner.

<Warning>
  An agent title does not create authority. Authority comes from the registry, current permissions, task scope, and recorded approvals.
</Warning>
