## PentaCompliance + PentaLicense

PentaCompliance and PentaLicense are production internal control-plane software under Penta Family v1.4. Their public identity and internal decision runtimes are active; binding legal and provider actions retain their own gates.

## PentaCompliance

PentaCompliance registers adopted obligations with exact source references, applicability scope, effective dates, accountable owners, controls and evidence requirements. Its machine result is one of:

- `PASS_EVIDENCE_SATISFIED`
- `HOLD_EVIDENCE_GAP`
- `HOLD_SOURCE_INVALID`

The pass is deliberately scoped to the adopted evidence set. It is not a universal representation of legal compliance and cannot replace counsel or an authorized attestation.

## PentaLicense

PentaLicense owns exact license eligibility and lifecycle records. It requires the asset/version/hash, rights-control evidence, requested rights, territory, media, dates, licensee, terms, acceptance, compliance receipt and CHLOM authority. D2/D3 and reviewed lanes require PentaHybrid evidence. Provider effects additionally require a certified binding and explicit readback.

An eligible request creates an immutable internal grant. Amendment, renewal, suspension and revocation actions append hash-bound events. Public verification excludes private party, terms, approval and authority data.

## Command Center

The authenticated Command Center is the operating surface for obligations, evidence evaluations, asset rights profiles, license requests, decisions, internal grants and lifecycle events. Writes are same-origin, role-gated and evidence-chained. Public routes never expose private licensee or governance detail.

## Software contract

Every Penta member can submit a typed software gap through PentaRFA. PentaFactory builds the candidate; tests include acceptance, negative and stress coverage; PentaCertify/PentaAssure remain independent; PentaRelease and provider adapters require exact-head/readback evidence. No member self-promotes.
