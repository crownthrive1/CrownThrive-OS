# Rotation 003 Production Protocol

Rotation 003 produces in format-fit waves rather than mechanically adapting every property into every medium.

## Wave sequence

1. Lock exact source, continuity field, protected material, and rights controller.
2. Draft the public-safe and sealed source projections separately.
3. Complete the primary work before derivative support products.
4. Run editorial, cultural, accessibility, safety, and format-specific review.
5. Generate the independent package and exact hashes.
6. Complete dual custody and equality readback.
7. Converge PentaBooks, PentaDocs, PentaGreen, evidence, route, and recovery registries.
8. Exercise provider, entitlement, reader, checkout, and recovery canaries.
9. Certify only the exact SKU and edition that passed.
10. Back-apply newly proven stronger controls to earlier rotations through additive audits.

## Priority order

The first production subjects are the four full-length works, because they establish the strongest source spine for audio, curriculum, live kits, merchandise, sponsor inventory, and licensing. Support products may develop in parallel but cannot invent canon or rights authority ahead of their source work.
