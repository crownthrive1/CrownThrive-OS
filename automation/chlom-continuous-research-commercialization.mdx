# CHLOM Continuous Research and Commercialization

CHLOM is not treated as a one-time documentation project. It is an evolving metaprotocol with a continuous governed lifecycle.

## Lifecycle

```text
observe / recover sources
        ↓
reconcile current canon
        ↓
R&D + identity + rights + compliance analysis
        ↓
agent-factory candidate intake
        ↓
Execution Builder / PentaFactory
        ↓
independent verification
        ↓
canonical parent package
        ↓
public child projection PR
        ↓
commercial packaging candidate
        ↓
PentaGreen™ / ECAC for economic activation
        ↓
DAIL evidence, outcomes, corrections and next cycle
```

## Continuous lanes

The standing lanes are:

- canon and source reconciliation;
- R&D and experimental candidate recovery;
- DID, fingerprint, identity, provenance and ownership-evidence modeling;
- DLA and hybrid licensing design;
- compliance-oracle and condition evaluation;
- pallets, modules, components, frameworks, plugins, skills and factory packages;
- APIs, MCPs, SDKs, webhooks and agent contracts;
- portals, dashboards, widgets, wireframes and documentation;
- parent/child repository projection;
- website mesh observation and fallback readiness;
- commercial packaging and PentaGreen™ handoff.

## Source-of-truth discipline

Automation must distinguish:

- current founder adjudication;
- current machine-verified institutional state;
- current canonical documentation;
- recovered primary source evidence;
- validated public registries and schemas;
- historical, superseded, experimental, or research material.

Research may be preserved without becoming current canon. New evidence may version or correct an existing article or record rather than erase its lineage.

## Automation boundary

The system may autonomously perform bounded research, comparison, classification, documentation drafting, schema generation, testing, candidate packaging, public-safe projection, and repair proposals within accepted contracts.

It may not autonomously manufacture ownership, legal authority, licensing rights, certification, price, checkout, entitlement, payment, settlement, or D3 authority.

## Documentation loop

The CHLOM Documentation & Projection Steward should continuously check for:

- terminology drift;
- stale repository relationships;
- public/private leakage;
- unversioned corrections;
- missing articleization for important pallets, modules, frameworks, wireframes, portals, contracts, agents, plugins, APIs, MCPs and services;
- divergence between the canonical parent managed package and public child;
- historical claims accidentally presented as current.

## Commercial loop

The CHLOM Commercial Packager can transform a verified capability into a candidate product or service package containing:

- product identity and version;
- intended customer and use case;
- deliverables;
- license class;
- machine-access requirements;
- support level;
- implementation dependencies;
- evidence and restrictions;
- SKU candidate;
- pricing-input request;
- PentaGreen™ handoff.

Only PentaGreen™ / ECAC may convert that candidate into current CrownThrive economic state.

## Public projection cadence

The managed public child projection is designed for scheduled continuity checks plus event-driven projection when canonical managed files change. Child updates remain pull-request based and exact-parent-SHA evidenced.
