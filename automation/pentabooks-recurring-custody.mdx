# PentaBooks Recurring Custody and Recovery

## Objective

The recurrence closes the gap between content production and durable, independently verifiable commercial readiness. It does not treat registration, model output, a local path, a Drive file ID, or a workflow-success badge as equivalent to a complete and deliverable product.

## Cadence

- Primary cycle: `0 * * * *`
- Cold-recovery cycle: `7 * * * *`
- Timezone contract: `America/New_York`
- Idempotency: `rotation_id + product_id + sha256`

GitHub Actions schedules are UTC-based and may be delayed by the provider; the evidence payload must preserve both the scheduled expression and actual observed time. Business-time interpretation remains `America/New_York`.

## Primary cycle

1. Discover newly generated or changed candidate artifacts.
2. Resolve the exact rotation, SKU, edition, source lineage, and rights controller.
3. Classify each object as public, controlled, sealed, secret alias, or prohibited.
4. Compute byte size and SHA-256 before transport.
5. Verify the complete independent package contract.
6. Preserve producer/Library custody.
7. Write to the exact Google Drive rotation and product lane.
8. Download the Drive object and prove byte-for-byte equality.
9. Append immutable custody evidence.
10. Reconcile Master Registry, PentaDocs, PentaGreen, package, rights, route, QA, and recovery projections.
11. Hand only eligible records to PentaGreen.
12. Emit a bounded cycle report and alert only on actionable drift.

## Cold recovery

The cold route retries only missed or degraded subjects. It recovers absent product payloads, stale provider readbacks, dead-lettered registry writes, expired leases, failed alerts, and projection drift. It never duplicates a previously accepted subject/hash pair.

## Fail-closed conditions

The cycle must use `RECOVERY_REQUIRED` or `QUARANTINED` when:

- the exact source or artifact hash is unknown;
- the delivery ZIP is named but the binary is absent;
- the Drive write cannot be downloaded and compared;
- public and sealed material are mixed;
- a rights or identity ambiguity exists;
- provider credentials are absent or unverified;
- registry rows conflict on SKU, edition, price, rights, or custody;
- entitlement, expiration, refund, recovery, or checkout canaries fail;
- a public route exposes unreleased or protected content.

## Runtime secret aliases

The implementation recognizes only secret names, never secret values:

- `PENTABOOKS_DRIVE_CREDENTIALS_JSON`
- `PENTABOOKS_THRIVEBASE_URL`
- `PENTABOOKS_THRIVEBASE_SERVICE_ROLE_KEY`
- `PENTABOOKS_ALERT_WEBHOOK`

Their mere presence does not prove authority or a successful provider call. Every mutation still requires exact provider readback.

## Rotation 002 recovery subject

Rotation 002 has verified control custody and an open 32-SKU product-payload recovery queue. The recurrence may close a SKU only after recovering or rebuilding the exact complete-product ZIP from governed source, writing it to both custody lanes, downloading it, matching size and SHA-256, and reconciling the registries. Source novels cannot be relabeled as adaptation packages.

## Evidence boundary

A successful guard run proves that the guard executed. A successful Drive read proves only that Drive predicate. A successful database append proves only that event mutation. A successful provider product query proves only that provider predicate. Public release requires the full current gate set, not a composite optimistic status.
