## ThriveInstitutionalize™ control skill

The fleet uses `ct.skill.factory.thrive-institutionalize.v1` as its cross-cutting institutionalization contract. It requires every material generation to reconcile:

- exact identities and counts;
- fingerprints and immutable digests;
- Vault recovery custody;
- encrypted Drive upload and exact readback;
- DAIL lineage and integrity;
- independent review;
- public-safe documentation impact;
- recovery and supersession;
- scheduler bindings; and
- candidate commercialization state.

The skill creates no D3 authority, votes, production invocation, publication, checkout, Stripe objects, money movement, rights grants, or entitlements.

## Schedule model

The verified topology includes native internal build, verification, documentation, rights/commercialization, archive/continuity, and 30-minute orchestration schedules, plus existing external relay bindings.

The active 20K-plus policy records ten completed generations and allows only one candidate generation at a time. Future generations G11 through G15 remain candidate-only, separated by a registered 30-day minimum cadence and prior-generation acceptance dependencies.

```text
G10 complete
→ G11 institutional market networks
→ G12 rights-economy operating systems
→ G13 adaptive cultural infrastructure
→ G14 global enterprise federation
→ G15 generational sovereign continuity
```

The orchestrator may enqueue a candidate only when:

- the previous generation has validated counts and package integrity;
- Vault and encrypted Drive custody are complete;
- exact ciphertext readback is verified;
- DAIL integrity is clean or explicitly held;
- no unresolved collision or duplicate identity exists;
- security and rights preflight is complete;
- protected-body publication remains prohibited; and
- production, checkout and Stripe activation remain false.

## Per-generation contract

Each future candidate generation targets:

- 1,000 proprietary algorithm specifications;
- 300 reusable agent skills;
- 500 reusable prompts;
- 300 reusable scripts; and
- 2,100 protected assets total.

Counts are not authority. Any generation that fails uniqueness, validation, custody, rights, security, independent review, Drive readback, or DAIL requirements remains blocked and must not be represented as institutionally complete.

## Cryptographic custody bonds

Each completed generation receives a verified institutional bond receipt connecting package identity, root digest, encrypted archive digest, Vault recovery custody, Drive ciphertext readback, DAIL evidence, runtime-disabled state, and commercialization hold. A separately encrypted fleet-closure bond aggregates all ten generation roots and custody receipts without exposing protected bodies.

These are institutional cryptographic custody bonds. They are not financial surety bonds, securities, customer guarantees, or production-activation receipts.

## Self-healing boundary

The fleet may perform bounded deterministic repair of manifests, indexes, read-only checks, packaging attempts, stale evidence pointers, schedule metadata and non-authoritative documentation projections when tested rollback is available.

It may not silently:

- alter rights or signed terms;
- change public prices or customer entitlements;
- reconstruct missing source code from deployment artifacts;
- publish trade-secret bodies;
- widen credentials, MCP tools or provider writes;
- invent proof, reviews or votes;
- delete institutional history; or
- promote an asset to production or live commerce.

## Commercialization boundary

Commercialization agents may prepare candidate packs, pricing hypotheses, implementation models, support plans, licensing templates, market-fit studies and value evidence. They may not enable checkout or create Stripe objects until Rights, Security, Privacy, Tax, Pricing, Fulfillment, Support, Accessibility and exact-artifact governance all pass.

The objective is durable enterprise value and measurable usefulness. No automated agent may claim a guaranteed valuation, guaranteed revenue, guaranteed customer demand, or guaranteed return.
