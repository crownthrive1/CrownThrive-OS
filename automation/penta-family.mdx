# Penta Family™ // Production Control Plane

**Status: `production` — scope: `institutional_control_plane`.**

Penta Family™ is CrownThrive's canonical umbrella for the PENTA institutional software family. It is a production control plane, not a blanket production label for every Penta member. The family composes registered PENTA catalogs, validates their state, exposes maturity and portals, and fails closed before a member can proceed toward consequential execution.

<Info>
  **Canonical invariant:** Penta Family can be production while an individual Penta member is not. No child inherits production status, certification, credentials, authority, provider access, legal standing, economic permissions, or rights merely because it belongs to the family.
</Info>

## What the family controls

The production family contract provides:

- one canonical family identity and registry;
- a deterministic composition of required PENTA catalogs;
- canonical maturity semantics across members;
- duplicate-key and missing-catalog rejection;
- validation that every Penta named in a control plane actually resolves to the family census;
- a fail-closed member eligibility gate;
- production snapshots and maturity counts;
- execution-eligible and held-member visibility;
- a mandatory per-member portal and guide contract;
- bounded control-plane topology across governance, routing, build, security, knowledge, media, economics, workforce, service delivery, intelligence and institutional services; and
- evidence/readback requirements before downstream side effects.

The family runtime does not directly execute provider actions. It establishes whether a registered member is eligible to proceed to its own downstream gates and can render the canonical portal payload for each system.

## Maturity is independent per member

| Maturity | Family dispatch posture |
| --- | --- |
| `specified` | Held — architecture/contract exists but execution is not eligible. |
| `implemented` | Held — software exists but certification/production evidence is not yet sufficient. |
| `certified` | Eligible to proceed to downstream authority/provider gates. |
| `production` | Eligible to proceed to downstream authority/provider gates within its certified production scope. |
| `hold` | Explicitly blocked. |
| `retired` | Historical/non-executable. |

Only `certified` and `production` pass the Penta Family eligibility gate. Passing that gate is not final authorization.

## Production dispatch contract

A member can pass the family eligibility gate only when:

1. the machine key is registered in a required family catalog;
2. all required family catalogs are present and valid;
3. no duplicate machine key exists;
4. every Penta reference in the family control-plane topology resolves unambiguously to a registered member;
5. the member maturity is `certified` or `production`; and
6. the family itself composes without an invariant failure.

Consequential execution still requires the applicable CHLOM/DAIL authority trace, accountable owner, PentaHybrid/human gate, provider adapter certification, PentaCredentials binding, legal/rights/security/spend controls, release policy, and provider readback.

<Warning>
  **Fail closed.** Unknown members, unresolved control-plane names, duplicate machine keys, missing required catalogs, invalid maturity, `specified`, `implemented`, `hold`, and `retired` states are not execution eligible through the family control plane.
</Warning>

## Portals and comprehensive system guides

Every registered Penta now has a canonical portal contract at:

```text
/penta/{machine_key_suffix}
```

The portal is required to expose overview, status, responsibilities, inputs/outputs, authority boundaries, dependencies, SOPs/SLAs, runbooks, guides, evidence, API/MCP state, changelog and support context. Portal state is independent from execution maturity.


## Canonical catalogs

The production family composes four required catalogs:

- `data/penta/systems.registry.json` — core PENTA institutional catalog;
- `data/penta/systems.extensions.pentascribe-marketer.json` — PentaScribe/PentaMarketer extension;
- `data/penta/institutional-services.registry.json` — institutional operating-fabric services; and
- `data/penta/systems.extensions.operations-workforce.json` — software delivery, credentials, maintenance, mail/concierge/status, economic controls, workforce, HR, board/compliance and agent-suite systems.

Catalog membership is explicit and change-controlled. A new `Penta*` name does not become executable merely by appearing in documentation.

## Runtime and verification

The canonical family runtime is `runtime/penta_family.py`. It validates the production contract, composes a deterministic family snapshot, rejects unresolved control-plane references, exposes member maturity, generates the portal index, renders per-member portal payloads, and returns a fail-closed dispatch disposition.

The CI lane validates all required PENTA catalogs and schemas, compiles the runtimes, verifies the family census, renders the portal index and a representative PentaMail portal, runs institutional authority-boundary tests, and runs dedicated Penta Family regression tests. Those tests prove that production members may pass the family eligibility gate while `specified`, `implemented`, and unknown members remain held.

## Production boundary

Penta Family is designed to make the PENTA estate more executable **without making CrownThrive less governed**.

```text
FAMILY PRODUCTION ≠ UNIVERSAL CHILD PRODUCTION
PORTAL CONTRACTED ≠ FRONTEND DEPLOYED
MEMBER ELIGIBLE ≠ FINAL AUTHORIZATION
CREDENTIAL PRESENT ≠ PERMISSION GRANTED
PROVIDER CAPABLE ≠ PROVIDER CERTIFIED
DOCUMENTED ≠ DEPLOYED
SUCCESSFUL CALL ≠ UNBOUNDED AUTHORITY
```

The operating rule remains: **build the capability, certify the capability, bind the capability, execute within authority, preserve evidence, maintain it, and carry it forward.**

## Source of truth

The machine-readable source of truth is `data/penta/family.registry.json`; the schema is `schemas/penta/family-registry.schema.json`; the executable verifier and portal renderer is `runtime/penta_family.py`; the full portal/census guide is `automation/penta-family-portals.mdx`; and the long-form institutional contract is `docs/phase3/PENTA_FAMILY_PRODUCTION_CONTRACT_v1.0.md`.

Changes to family membership, portal requirements, status semantics, execution-eligible maturity, authority invariants, required catalogs, control-plane references, or fail-closed behavior are production control-plane changes and must pass CrownThrive's governed source, review, CI, merge, release, and evidence pathways.
