## Penta Family™ // Production Control Plane

**Status: `production` — scope: `institutional_control_plane`.**

Penta Family™ is CrownThrive's canonical umbrella for the PENTA institutional software family. It is a production control plane, not a blanket production label for every Penta member. The family composes registered PENTA catalogs, validates their state, exposes maturity and portals, and fails closed before a member can proceed toward consequential execution.

<Info>
  **Canonical invariant:** Penta Family can be production while an individual Penta member is not. No child inherits production status, certification, credentials, authority, provider access, legal standing, economic permissions, or rights merely because it belongs to the family.
</Info>

## What the family controls

The production family contract provides:

- one canonical family identity and registry;
- a deterministic composition of required PENTA catalogs;
- canonical maturity semantics across members;
- duplicate-key and missing-catalog rejection;
- validation that every Penta named in a control plane actually resolves to the family census;
- a fail-closed member eligibility gate;
- production snapshots and maturity counts;
- execution-eligible and held-member visibility;
- HOT/WARM/SLEEP/HOLD/DEGRADED/RETIRED readiness through PentaOD and PentaHeartbeat;
- a mandatory per-member portal and guide contract;
- bounded control-plane topology across governance, routing, readiness, build, security, knowledge, media, economics, workforce, service delivery, intelligence and institutional services; and
- evidence/readback requirements before downstream side effects.

The family runtime does not directly execute provider actions. It establishes whether a registered member is eligible to proceed to its own downstream gates and can render the canonical portal payload for each system.

## Maturity is independent per member

| Maturity | Family dispatch posture |
| --- | --- |
| `specified` | Held — architecture/contract exists but execution is not eligible. |
| `implemented` | Held — software exists but certification/production evidence is not yet sufficient. |
| `certified` | Eligible to proceed to downstream authority/provider gates. |
| `production` | Eligible to proceed to downstream authority/provider gates within its certified production scope. |
| `hold` | Explicitly blocked. |
| `retired` | Historical/non-executable. |

Only `certified` and `production` pass the Penta Family eligibility gate. Passing that gate is not final authorization.

## PentaOD, PentaHeartbeat and PentaBeata

The family now uses three distinct readiness layers rather than treating every service as permanently executing:

- **PentaOD™** is the on-demand readiness controller. It classifies registered services and exact certified operations as `HOT`, `WARM`, `SLEEP`, `HOLD`, `DEGRADED`, or `RETIRED`; wakes eligible components on real demand; idles wake-on-demand components; and appends wake/dispatch receipts.
- **PentaHeartbeat™** is the whole-system readiness aggregate. It combines PentaOD state, PentaMesh/Fabric health and the canonical ThriveBase heartbeat into a five-minute institutional readiness signal.
- **PentaBeata™** is the public-safe liveness primitive. It reports sanitized PentaRoute plus readiness state without issuing provider writes or exposing secrets.

`HOT` means **ready inside authority already earned**. It does not mean unrestricted permissions.

A certified-write-on-demand operation is dispatch-ready only when its exact PentaCertify record is `verified_write`, risk is D0–D2, credential state is verified, idempotency is verified or explicitly not applicable, rollback/compensation is verified or explicitly not applicable, read-after-write is verified, certification has not expired, and the PentaOD heartbeat is fresh. D3, blocked, held and retired operations do not auto-wake.

For Partnero attribution, `customers.create` and `transactions.create` remain separate exact operations. When PentaCertify marks either exact operation `verified_write`, certification triggers immediately synchronize the Partnero surface flags and the PentaOD component. A genuine signup or transaction event then wakes that exact component on demand and dispatches only after PentaOD confirms fresh readiness. Coupons, deletes, balances, partner lifecycle administration and unrelated Partnero mutations remain closed.

## Production dispatch contract

A member can pass the family eligibility gate only when:

1. the machine key is registered in a required family catalog;
2. all required family catalogs are present and valid;
3. no duplicate machine key exists;
4. every Penta reference in the family control-plane topology resolves unambiguously to a registered member;
5. the member maturity is `certified` or `production`; and
6. the family itself composes without an invariant failure.

Every `certified` or `production` member must also carry at least one registered bounded execution adapter in `data/penta/execution-adapters.registry.json`, or family validation fails closed. All 15 current production members carry tested adapters (15/15 coverage). Adapters declare no provider effect; they do not grant provider access, auto-promotion, or authority beyond the gates below.

Consequential execution still requires the applicable CHLOM/DAIL authority trace, accountable owner, PentaHybrid/human gate, provider adapter certification, PentaCredentials binding, legal/rights/security/spend controls, release policy, PentaOD readiness when the capability is on-demand, and provider readback.

<Warning>
  **Fail closed.** Unknown members, unresolved control-plane names, duplicate machine keys, missing required catalogs, invalid maturity, `specified`, `implemented`, `hold`, `retired`, stale heartbeat, missing credential, or unverified exact-write certification are not execution eligible through the family control plane.
</Warning>

## Canonical catalogs

The production family composes the eight required child-member catalogs declared by `data/penta/family.registry.json`: core systems, PentaScribe/PentaMarketer, institutional services, operations/workforce, institutional controls, observability, readiness, and the bounded PentaImmune/PentaEVIBuilder maintenance-and-evidence extension.

The Error/Logger/Trace/Metric observability spine is now a required Penta Family catalog. Its members keep independent maturity and authority even though the catalog participates directly in family composition and convergence certification.

Catalog membership is explicit and change-controlled. A new `Penta*` name does not become executable merely by appearing in documentation.

## Runtime and verification

The canonical family runtime is `runtime/penta_family.py`. It validates the production contract, composes a deterministic family snapshot, rejects unresolved control-plane references, exposes member maturity, generates the portal index, renders per-member portal payloads, and returns a fail-closed dispatch disposition.

Live readiness is separately carried by the ThriveBase PentaOD/PentaHeartbeat runtime. PentaOD reconciliation runs every two minutes, PentaHeartbeat records a system readiness heartbeat every five minutes, and on-demand idle reconciliation runs every fifteen minutes. Certification changes are event-driven and do not wait for the periodic sweep. PentaRoute Autonomy supervises all three schedules and performs bounded recovery when a scheduler is absent, inactive, stale, or failed.

The runtime suite carries the family-registered Error/Logger/Trace/Metric observability spine. The convergence certifier evaluates it as part of the complete eight-catalog family while preserving each member's independent maturity.

The CI lane validates all required PENTA catalogs and schemas, compiles the runtimes, verifies the family census, renders the portal index and a representative PentaMail portal, runs institutional authority-boundary tests, and runs dedicated Penta Family regression tests. Those tests prove that production members may pass the family eligibility gate while `specified`, `implemented`, and unknown members remain held.

## Production boundary

Penta Family is designed to make the PENTA estate more executable **without making CrownThrive less governed**.

```text
FAMILY PRODUCTION ≠ UNIVERSAL CHILD PRODUCTION
HOT ≠ UNBOUNDED AUTHORITY
WAKE ≠ CERTIFICATION
PORTAL CONTRACTED ≠ FRONTEND DEPLOYED
MEMBER ELIGIBLE ≠ FINAL AUTHORIZATION
CREDENTIAL PRESENT ≠ PERMISSION GRANTED
PROVIDER CAPABLE ≠ PROVIDER CERTIFIED
DOCUMENTED ≠ DEPLOYED
SUCCESSFUL CALL ≠ UNBOUNDED AUTHORITY
```

The operating rule remains: **build the capability, certify the capability, bind the capability, keep it ready, wake it on demand, execute within authority, verify the result, preserve evidence, maintain it, and carry it forward.**

## Source of truth

The machine-readable family source of truth is `data/penta/family.registry.json`; the schema is `schemas/penta/family-registry.schema.json`; the executable verifier and portal renderer is `runtime/penta_family.py`; the full portal/census guide is `automation/penta-family-portals.mdx`; the required observability catalog is `data/penta/systems.extensions.observability.json`; the family readiness/liveness member definitions are `data/penta/systems.extensions.readiness.json`; and the current long-form institutional contract is `docs/phase3/PENTA_FAMILY_PRODUCTION_CONTRACT_v1.1.md`.

Changes to family membership, portal requirements, status semantics, readiness semantics, execution-eligible maturity, authority invariants, required catalogs, control-plane references, or fail-closed behavior are production control-plane changes and must pass CrownThrive's governed source, review, CI, merge, release, and evidence pathways.
