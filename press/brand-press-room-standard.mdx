# Brand Press Room & Media Kit Standard

Every public-facing CrownThrive identity should have an intentional media/press disposition. That can be a dedicated press room, a portfolio-level press block, or an archive/history profile.

## Lifecycle-aware press treatment

| Identity state | Press treatment |
| --- | --- |
| Current public / production | Current media kit and fact sheet. |
| Development / controlled test | Clearly labeled development profile; no production claims. |
| Reserve / future build | Concept/portfolio profile, not launch copy. |
| Research | Research/innovation profile; architecture remains non-production. |
| Predecessor / merged | Historical profile with successor link. |
| Sunset / archived | Archive/history profile; no current-product CTA unless separately authorized. |
| Restricted | Public existence/role only where safe; no confidential details. |

## Boilerplate rules

A brand boilerplate should answer:

- what the identity is;
- who stewards it;
- what problem or audience it serves;
- how it relates to CrownThrive;
- current lifecycle/disposition;
- where the public can verify it.

Avoid unsupported superlatives, evergreen counts, unverified market leadership, guaranteed outcomes, unstated legal status or claims that every planned capability is deployed.

## Public domains

Use only public-safe domains from the governed domain register. If a brand has multiple domains, identify the role of each when known:

- primary;
- alternate/defensive;
- predecessor;
- redirect/migration;
- reserve/future;
- product/campaign property.

Domain custody does not reactivate a sunset brand or prove deployment.

## Powered-by and ecosystem relationships

Approved relationship language may include:

- Powered by CrownThrive;
- Powered by the CrownThrive Ecosystem;
- a CrownThrive brand/platform/imprint;
- aligned to the Thrive Flywheel;
- governed by the Cultural Imprint Engine where actually applicable;
- supported by named CrownThrive platforms where the relationship is currently supported.

Do not infer technical integration, entitlement, data sharing, SSO, financial settlement or rights transfer from relationship copy alone.

## Approved media assets

Media kits may route to:

- approved logos;
- public product/service screenshots;
- approved founder/leadership imagery;
- public campaign artwork;
- approved brand photography;
- public trailers/sizzle reels;
- approved PDFs/fact sheets.

Do not expose:

- source masters intended to remain restricted;
- private dashboards/admin screenshots;
- secret URLs or credentials;
- raw customer/licensee records;
- unpublished contracts;
- confidential partner pricing;
- trade-secret diagrams/code;
- unreleased media/IP.

## Metrics

Every metric must carry an evidence class and as-of date. Current facts should be refreshed from the system of record when feasible.

Scenario, estimate, aspiration and management valuation are labeled as such; they never become audited fact through repetition.

## Corrections and version history

Nothing material is silently overwritten. Each press page maintains a compact changelog and preserves material prior claims through version/correction lineage.

## Internal linking

Every brand press page links to:

- the public portfolio entry;
- the primary platform/brand documentation;
- domain record(s);
- alias/successor record where relevant;
- current public/legal/support route where applicable;
- relevant changelog;
- master [CrownThrive Press Room](/press/index).

## Governance

D0/D1 factual routing/formatting can use bounded automation. Material claims involving legal status, rights, money, privacy/security, regulated services, current production capability, economic performance or high-consequence metrics require the applicable specialist review and institutional acceptance before publication.
