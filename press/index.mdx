## Approved institutional boilerplate

**CrownThrive, LLC** is a Gretna, Virginia-based convergent ecosystem that develops and governs a portfolio of brands, platforms, media properties, operating frameworks, education/community programs, commerce systems and institutional infrastructure. CrownThrive's model emphasizes ownership, interoperability, documentation, cultural integrity, rights governance, measurable operating systems and long-term continuity.

This boilerplate describes institutional direction. It should not be used to imply that every historical, reserve or developing platform is currently in production.

## Portfolio and brand facts

Media, analysts and partners should use the current [Public Brand & Platform Portfolio](/portfolio/public-brand-platform-portfolio) rather than copying an old website list or undated platform count.

A portfolio entry should distinguish:

- canonical identity;
- historical names/aliases;
- current disposition;
- public domain(s);
- current public description;
- source/evidence date;
- related platform/framework;
- press/media-kit state.

## "Powered by CrownThrive" relationship language

Public first-party pages may describe a brand or platform as **Powered by CrownThrive**, **Powered by the CrownThrive Ecosystem**, backed by the Convergent Ecosystem, or aligned to the Thrive Flywheel/Cultural Imprint Engine.

Those statements are useful public relationship evidence. They do not by themselves establish:

- current backend integration;
- universal SSO;
- API entitlement;
- legal ownership of third-party content;
- active provider configuration;
- current revenue or performance;
- current rights clearance.

The institutional registry controls those separate claims.

## Media assets and logos

Brand logos, approved photography, screenshots, artwork, product imagery, speaker/headshot assets and other media should be routed through the relevant brand's governed media-kit record. Do not lift unreleased masters, restricted screenshots, private dashboard images or internal architecture diagrams from operational records.

## Metrics and claims

Every public metric should be dated and evidence-backed. Older numbers remain historical rather than being silently replaced.

Use:

```yaml
metric_name:
value:
unit:
as_of:
method:
evidence_ref:
status: verified | management_claim | historical | scenario
```

Do not collapse visitors, accounts, profiles, members, customers, active users, listings or subscribers into the same metric.

## Corrections and historical statements

CrownThrive follows a permanent **no silent overwrite** rule. If a prior public statement becomes stale:

1. preserve the prior version or evidence;
2. publish or route to the current statement;
3. identify the effective date where material;
4. record the correction/supersession relationship.

See [Public Correction Backlog](/knowledge/public-correction-backlog).
