# Self-Funding Contract Suite

The Self-Funding Business Engine requires contracts to make the economic routing enforceable outside the software. The software records and enforces the active commercial rule; it does not create contractual rights by itself.

## Master Provider Economics Agreement

**Purpose:** establish the provider relationship, eligibility, service/product responsibilities, 80% provider allocation, settlement timing, refunds, disputes, rights, compliance, taxes, records and termination.

### Required provisions

1. Parties and authority.
2. Covered products/services and approved channels.
3. Provider eligibility and onboarding requirements.
4. Active economic schedule and effective date.
5. Provider share: **80% of the qualified transaction allocation basis**, subject to the executed agreement.
6. HQ growth and technology allocation: 10%.
7. Regional leadership allocation: 5% where applicable.
8. National advertising allocation: 3%.
9. ThriveFund allocation: 2%.
10. Processing-fee treatment and who bears each fee.
11. Refund, chargeback, dispute and reversal mechanics.
12. Settlement timing and reserve/hold mechanics.
13. Rights and licensing representations.
14. Data, attribution and analytics permissions.
15. Confidentiality, security and privacy obligations.
16. Independent contractor/business relationship terms where applicable.
17. Tax responsibility.
18. Audit and reconciliation rights.
19. Suspension and termination.
20. Governing law, dispute resolution and notices.

## Regional Leadership / License Agreement

Defines territory, authority, responsibilities, service standards, economic share, approved downstream agreements, reporting, territory conflicts, suspension and revocation.

The 5% regional allocation is **not automatic**. It requires an active regional authorization and an applicable economic schedule.

## National Advertising Fund Schedule

Defines the 3% allocation, eligible advertising uses, budget ownership, campaign approval, measurement, reporting, fraud controls, unused-fund treatment and audit rights.

## ThriveFund Allocation Schedule

Defines the 2% allocation, eligible community projects, qualification, milestone evidence, reporting, clawback/rerouting, conflicts and public-impact claims.

## Affiliate / Ambassador Agreement

Affiliate and ambassador compensation is a separate acquisition-cost lane unless an offer explicitly states that compensation is transaction-funded. The contract must identify:

- offer/version;
- commission rate;
- attribution window;
- eligible event;
- disclosure requirements;
- reversal/refund rules;
- payout threshold and timing;
- prohibited conduct;
- creative/IP rights;
- termination;
- audit and correction rules.

## Suite / Place-Based Agreement

MM Suites and other place-based corridors can add rent, suite, booking, membership, upsell and local advertising economics. These streams should be separately contracted and reconciled unless an agreement expressly incorporates them into the transaction schedule.

## Signature and registry rule

The institutional sequence is:

```text
Template
  ↓
Commercial review
  ↓
Rights/compliance review
  ↓
Counterparty negotiation
  ↓
Signature
  ↓
Executed document + checksum
  ↓
Contract registry activation
  ↓
Policy binding
  ↓
Production eligibility
```

A template is not an entitlement. A signed agreement is not automatically production-ready until provider credentials, rights, payment capability, reconciliation and certification gates also pass.

## Economic schedule versioning

Every material percentage, fee, commission, hold, payout window or reversal rule must be versioned. Historical examples remain historical. The active contract and effective economic schedule control current execution.
