## Acquisition and Distribution Engines

CrownThrive's distribution advantage comes from owning or controlling multiple entry points. The institution should not market every asset equally; it should route qualified audiences through a small set of proven acquisition engines.

## Primary engines

| Engine | Primary audience | Cross-corridor objective |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Locticians | Beauty/wellness professionals and consumers | Melanin Magic, ThriveSeat, education, media, rewards, advertising and MM Suites validation |
| Virality Music | Music, story-world and IP audiences | Licensing, publishing, books, GSO, Go Flipbooks, media and creator services |
| The Mane Experience / media | Culture, contributors, creators, readers/viewers | Audience growth, AdLuxe inventory, products, education, media and creator pathways |
| Crown Affiliates / Ambassadors | Performance and advocacy distribution | Qualified referrals, progression, education and reporting across eligible offers |
| AdLuxe | Advertisers, publishers, creators and locations | Monetize owned/partner inventory while acquiring commercial partners |
| Search / tools / education | High-intent utility and learning audiences | Use free value to qualify and route users into paid tools, education, services and products |
| Events / phygital | Local, community and experiential audiences | Tickets, Gather, kiosks, screens, memberships and offline-to-online conversion |

## Campaign doctrine

Campaigns require a defined audience, offer, source, attribution model, landing path, conversion event, economics, consent basis and support owner. Acquisition activity should be measurable through normalized CrownLytics events.

## Distribution compounding

Successful content or products can be distributed through CrownThrive media, AdLuxe, affiliates, ambassadors, ThrivePush, CrownRewards, partner embeds, Go Flipbooks, social channels, physical screens and events—but only where rights and audience relevance permit.

## Anti-fragmentation rule

A new audience channel should not automatically become a new standalone platform. First test whether it can operate as a channel, collection, campaign, imprint, product family, or integration within an existing corridor.
