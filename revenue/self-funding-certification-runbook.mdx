# Self-Funding Certification Runbook

## Objective

Move the Self-Funding Business Engine from implemented/gated to production-capable only when evidence proves that the software, provider, contracts, rights, settlement and reversal paths all work together.

## Gate 1 — PentaBuild

Build and package:

- allocation engine;
- transaction state machine;
- provider adapter;
- contract registry bindings;
- event/audit ledger;
- reconciliation worker;
- refund/dispute handlers;
- dashboard/reporting surfaces;
- automated test suite.

Required evidence: build artifact, version, checksum, test report and deployment manifest.

## Gate 2 — PentaCredentials

Bind provider credentials through reference-only secret handling.

Required evidence:

- credential exists;
- correct environment is selected;
- minimum required scope;
- secret not present in source/database payloads;
- rotation path documented;
- provider account ownership confirmed.

## Gate 3 — Provider readiness

For each connected provider:

- onboarding complete;
- transfer capability enabled;
- required business information verified;
- account status monitored;
- payout destination verified;
- provider-specific requirements current.

## Gate 4 — Contract readiness

No provider allocation may activate without an executed applicable agreement.

Required:

- counterparty;
- covered products;
- economic schedule;
- effective date;
- refund/dispute terms;
- tax treatment;
- rights/compliance representations;
- termination;
- signature evidence;
- contract checksum.

## Gate 5 — CHLOM / PentaGreen authorization

CHLOM validates rights, eligibility and evidence. PentaGreen activates authorized commerce economics.

Constitutional rule:

> **PentaGreen may optimize within authority. It may never manufacture authority.**

## Gate 6 — PentaCertify

Certification must cover:

1. read verification;
2. write canary in a controlled environment;
3. readback verification;
4. idempotency/retry test;
5. refund test;
6. dispute/chargeback test;
7. partial reversal test;
8. failed-transfer test;
9. reconciliation test;
10. rollback/compensation test;
11. audit evidence test;
12. security/RLS test.

## Gate 7 — Production authorization

Only after all prior gates pass can the provider adapter move from `fail_closed_unbound` toward production-enabled state.

Production authorization must identify:

- exact adapter version;
- exact provider account;
- exact policy version;
- exact contract versions;
- approved transaction types;
- approved currencies/geographies;
- risk ceiling;
- rollback owner;
- monitoring owner;
- authorization timestamp.

## Gate 8 — Canary

Production should begin with a constrained canary rather than unrestricted money movement.

Monitor:

- allocation accuracy;
- processor settlement variance;
- transfer success rate;
- reversal success rate;
- dispute rate;
- duplicate-event rate;
- provider capability failures;
- reconciliation latency;
- unexplained balance variance.

Any critical mismatch places the engine in `HOLD`.

## Gate 9 — Institutional release

After canary success:

```text
PentaBuild
   ↓
PentaCredentials
   ↓
CHLOM
   ↓
PentaGreen
   ↓
PentaCertify
   ↓
Canary
   ↓
PentaRelease
   ↓
Production
```

The release record must preserve the exact evidence set. A later policy or contract change creates a new version and does not silently mutate historical transaction economics.

## Current state

The Self-Funding Business Engine is **implemented and bound at the control-plane level, with live money movement intentionally held**. The Stripe provider adapter is fail-closed/unverified for live transfer operations. This is the correct institutional state until the production gates above are independently satisfied.
