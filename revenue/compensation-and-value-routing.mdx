## Compensation and Value Routing

The current CrownThrive framework set includes a **Compensation Structure** focused on commission/payout design, equity or ownership alignment, and long-term guardrails. Compensation should be modeled by economic relationship rather than one global percentage.

## Compensation classes

Potential classes include:

- employee or contractor compensation;
- creator royalties and revenue shares;
- licensor / rights-holder splits;
- affiliate commissions;
- ambassador rewards;
- creator-campaign fees;
- marketplace or seller payouts;
- instructor, mentor and consultant earnings;
- referral rewards;
- partner/platform revenue shares;
- Stripe Connect transfers where applicable;
- CrownRewards points and other closed-loop benefits;
- Holdings/intercompany allocations;
- impact-designated routing.

## Required record

Every compensation rule should identify actor/role, triggering event, eligible product or revenue base, percentage or amount, exclusions, refunds/chargebacks, tax/reporting treatment, payment rail, approval authority, effective dates, cap/floor, dispute path and source agreement.

## Ownership is different

Equity, membership interests, profit interests, revenue shares, commissions, royalties and loyalty points are not interchangeable. The system should never infer legal ownership because a participant receives compensation.

## Automation boundary

Agents may calculate expected amounts, reconcile records and prepare payout batches. Live movement of money, changes to economic terms, ownership decisions and exceptions require explicit authorization consistent with policy and account controls.

## Phase 3 implication

The Revenue Registry needs versioned economic rules tied to rights, contracts, roles, products, orders and events. DAIL should preserve the rule version used for each calculation.
