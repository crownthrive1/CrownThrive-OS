# Protocol Commercialization & Licensing Continuity

## Governing protocol

```yaml
protocol_id: ct.protocol.protocol-commercialization-continuity
version: 1.0.0
commercial_default: candidate_hold
checkout_enabled: false
stripe_objects_created: false
public_body_allowed: false
activation_requires_independent_approval: true
```

Every verified or promising CrownThrive protocol should produce a **commercial candidate**, not an automatically activated offer. Candidate creation preserves commercial optionality while keeping pricing, rights, delivery, tax, consumer-protection, security and entitlement gates separate.

## Offer families

A proprietary protocol may support one or more offer families:

- licensed public-contract/template package;
- governed audit and certification service;
- managed implementation;
- API/MCP capability pack;
- institutional training and certification;
- enterprise integration;
- continuity, recovery and archive service;
- ongoing provider or policy drift-watch subscription.

The restricted protocol body, private implementation, credentials, internal security evidence and trade-secret topology are not included in public packages unless a separate license explicitly authorizes controlled delivery.

## Commercial state model

```text
candidate_hold
→ research
→ pricing_review
→ counsel_review
→ approved_not_live
→ live
→ superseded | retired
```

A protocol may remain commercially valuable while staying in HOLD. `approved_not_live` still means no public checkout or entitlement exists.

## Required gates before activation

A live protocol offer requires current evidence for:

- CrownThrive ownership or lawful commercialization rights;
- public-safe contract and restricted implementation custody;
- security/privacy and IP-disclosure review;
- accessible documentation and customer instructions;
- market and unit-economic pricing evidence;
- tax, consumer-protection and contract review;
- fulfillment, support and service-level boundaries;
- entitlement, suspension, revocation and refund behavior;
- version, archive, provider-exit and continuity controls;
- independent release approval.

## Initial commercial candidates

The private control plane currently carries candidate records for:

- Credential Continuity Audit;
- Secure Provider Binding Implementation;
- Metaprotocol Protocol Foundry License;
- Provider Adapter Certification;
- Protected Asset Archive Continuity Service.

Each remains `research` or `candidate_hold`, with no binding price, checkout, Stripe object or entitlement.

## Agent responsibilities

The non-voting Protocol Commercialization Continuity Agent may:

- create and deduplicate offer candidates;
- collect market and cost evidence;
- propose license scope and delivery model;
- define support, fulfillment and entitlement requirements;
- route legal, tax, rights, security and pricing reviews;
- preserve version and archive lineage.

It may not set binding economics, create live payment objects, publish the restricted body, grant rights, activate entitlement or represent a candidate as available for purchase.

## Continuity rule

Commercial records are effective-dated and versioned. A new offer version supersedes rather than silently overwrites the prior one. Licenses remain tied to the exact protocol/version, scope, term, territory, customer, delivery class and effective terms.
