# Customer Journey, Seat Model and Gateways

CrownThrive should market a small number of outcomes rather than forcing users to understand the full internal portfolio. The **Seat Model** and **Entry \+ Gateways** concepts describe how one person or organization can enter through a relevant lane and progressively access adjacent capabilities.

## Journey

```text
Discover → Engage → Receive Value → Activate → Retain → Expand → Contribute / Earn / Operate
```

A typical institutional journey is:

1. **Discover** through search, media, professional discovery, referral, affiliate, event, ad, partner, or physical endpoint.
2. **Enter** through the relevant platform and, over time, a CrownThrive ID with explicit role and consent.
3. **Activate** through one useful action: purchase, booking, course, referral, submission, support request, campaign, license, event, or content experience.
4. **Retain** through reliable delivery, CrownRewards, saved libraries, useful communications, community, support, recurring workflows and visible progress.
5. **Expand** only when an adjacent corridor solves a demonstrated need.
6. **Progress** into creator, affiliate, ambassador, mentor, instructor, seller, partner, incubatee, licensee, operator or governance pathways when qualified.

## One Seat, Multiple Industries

This is the integration goal: a governed identity and role can unlock multiple eligible experiences without duplicative onboarding. It must not be documented as fully implemented until SSO, role portability and platform adapters are actually working.

## Gateways

Gateways may include CrownThrive.com, Locticians, Virality Music, The Mane Experience, CrownThriveU, AdLuxe, GSO, direct partner links, ThriveTickets, search/tools, media channels and phygital endpoints.

## Consent and relevance

Cross-corridor routing must respect user expectation, purpose limitation, permissioned communications, rights, eligibility and brand fit. The Flywheel is a relevance engine, not a blanket-marketing permission.
