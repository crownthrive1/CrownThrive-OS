## Crown Affiliates and the Thrive Flywheel

**Crown Affiliates is not merely a coupon-link program.** It is a distribution lane for turning CrownThrive's growing portfolio into repeatable, attributable circulation across products, services, media, education, events, licensing and partner opportunities.

## Flywheel role

```text
Platform / brand creates value
        ↓
Approved affiliate offer + creative
        ↓
Crown Affiliate distributes
        ↓
Audience enters relevant gateway
        ↓
Conversion / activation event
        ↓
Attribution + commission record
        ↓
CrownRewards / progression / proof
        ↓
Adjacent relevant CrownThrive lane
        ↓
Repeat circulation
```

## Institutional integrations

- **CrownThriveU Affiliate Academy** — onboarding, FTC/disclosure education, product knowledge and future certifications.
- **CHLOM** — offer permissions, territory/brand rules, attribution evidence, commission rules, disputes and correction history.
- **CrownLytics** — conversion evidence, cohort performance and approved metrics.
- **CrownPulse** — approved social-proof signals.
- **ThrivePush** — permissioned campaign/re-engagement communication.
- **AdLuxe Network** — paid distribution and partner inventory.
- **CrownRewards** — retention and progression incentives distinct from cash commissions.
- **Virality Music / media / The Mane Experience** — content-led discovery and affiliate-ready products/offers.
- **Melanin Magic / GSO / commerce** — product distribution.
- **CrownThriveU / experts / services** — education and professional-service referrals.
- **ThriveTickets / events** — event acquisition and attendance loops.
- **MM Suites / Locticians** — local professional, booking, product and operator pathways.

## Developer/event requirements

The affiliate API/event model should eventually support:

- affiliate identity and status;
- approved campaigns/offers;
- referral links/codes;
- click/lead/conversion events;
- attribution window/version;
- refund/dispute reversals;
- commission accrual;
- hold/approval state;
- payout state;
- disclosure/compliance state;
- product/platform relationship;
- campaign creative rights;
- training/certification state.

## Economics rule

The [Self-Funding Business Engine](/revenue/self-funding-business-engine) treats affiliate and ambassador economics as governed acquisition costs that are distinct from the core 80/10/5/3/2 transaction target unless a specific commercial agreement makes them transaction-funded.

Historical affiliate pages contain differing commission percentages, windows, holds and payout thresholds. Those values must be **versioned offer/economic schedules**, not hard-coded institution-wide assumptions. The live affiliate property currently advertises a specific program offer, but Phase 3 should retrieve the applicable rule by offer/version and effective date.

## Affiliate progression

A participant may progress from promoter to trained affiliate, ambassador, creator, instructor, partner, operator or other role where eligibility allows. Progression is not automatic ownership or equity.

## Phase 3 priority

Affiliate events are high-value early DAIL/event-taxonomy candidates because they demonstrate how identity, platform attribution, rules, money, corrections, analytics and the Thrive Flywheel connect in one traceable flow.
