# Measurement, KPIs and Evidence

CrownThrive should distinguish **measurement**, **management claims**, **forecasts**, and **verified external proof**. Public figures must be supportable from dated evidence and should not become permanent constants copied across the ecosystem.

## Executive KPI families

### Portfolio

- P0 availability and delivery readiness;
- percentage of active assets with complete owner/domain/revenue/support/rights/event records;
- duplicate or overlapping public promises;
- vendor/domain/license renewal risk;
- roadmap throughput and blocked work.

### Commercial

- revenue by lane, platform and product family;
- gross and contribution margin;
- MRR/ARR where applicable;
- average order value;
- refunds, disputes and failed payments;
- customer acquisition cost and payback;
- repeat purchase, retention and expansion.

### Product / Platform

- activation and key-action completion;
- uptime and error rates;
- deployment frequency and rollback rate;
- support contacts per active user;
- conversion and abandonment by funnel.

### Rights / CHLOM

- assets with chain-of-title records;
- active licenses and renewal windows;
- unresolved holds/disputes;
- provenance completion;
- rights corrections and response time.

### Media / Culture

- qualified audience, consumption and completion;
- creator supply and approval rate;
- content reuse and licensing;
- direct-commerce conversion;
- advertising fill/yield where real inventory exists.

### Impact

- allocations, disbursements, completed outputs and outcome evidence;
- community participation and progression;
- public-accountability status.

## Evidence classes

Each metric should state its source and confidence class, for example:

- transaction-ledger actual;
- production-platform actual;
- analytics estimate;
- source-file registry count;
- management-reported claim;
- forecast/scenario;
- third-party verified.

## Current public-claim conflict

CrownThrive public pages currently contain multiple ecosystem counts and phase descriptions from different implementation eras. The Master Narrative Ledger should reconcile these rather than treating search-engine snippets as current truth.

## Phase 3 implication

CrownLytics should consume normalized events and produce traceable calculations. Public dashboards should link each material number to its source period, methodology and last refresh.
