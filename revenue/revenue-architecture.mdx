## Revenue Architecture

CrownThrive's model is intentionally diversified. The institution should measure revenue by economic stream and accountable operating corridor rather than attaching unsupported valuations to every platform.

## Eight revenue lanes

1. **Platform subscriptions and memberships** — recurring access, SaaS, premium accounts and memberships.
2. **Incubator, enterprise and professional delivery** — scoped builds, strategy, support, consulting, managed services and studio work.
3. **Media, advertising and sponsorships** — AdLuxe inventory, sponsorships, creator campaigns, production and channel monetization.
4. **Education, experts and certifications** — courses, cohorts, credentials, instructor/mentor activity, workshops and expert services.
5. **Commerce, wholesale and product margin** — Melanin Magic, GSO, digital products, wholesale, selected POD/private label and controlled physical products.
6. **Marketplace and transaction fees** — tickets, bookings, campaigns, rentals, seller fees, commissions and eligible processing/service fees.
7. **Licensing, IP and brand commercialization** — CHLOM/CHLOMLex rights to brands, domains, content, templates, software use, media, products and adaptations.
8. **Events, phygital and hardware** — tickets, virtual/physical experiences, kiosks, TapStations, signage, installation, maintenance, lease and rental.

## Value-routing rule

Shared infrastructure can serve many brands, but each material fee, royalty, commission, contribution, partner share, impact allocation, or intercompany charge should have a documented economic basis and an auditable record.

CHLOM governs permitted rights, eligibility, splits and evidence. The proposed Holdings layer governs capital allocation. CII/ThriveFund governs designated impact flows under its adopted structure.

## Self-funding transaction engine

The [Self-Funding Business Engine](/revenue/self-funding-business-engine) defines the founder-directed transaction-to-growth flywheel: qualified commerce can route an authorized 80% provider share plus designated HQ growth/technology, regional leadership, national advertising and ThriveFund allocations, then feed attribution, analytics, loyalty, engagement, cross-sell and network expansion.

The allocation is a target policy for the engine, not a claim that every current CrownThrive transaction uses the same schedule. Active contracts, payment rails, CHLOM controls, PentaGreen authority and reconciliation remain controlling.

## Unit-economic discipline

Track acquisition cost, activation, retention, support burden, gross margin and lifetime value by platform and lane. Free products should have an explicit acquisition, qualification, education, data, community or partner purpose. Physical inventory should not be scaled ahead of validated demand and cash-flow protection.

## Planning figures

Five-year figures in the Holdings plan are management assumptions, not audited forecasts or valuations. Phase 3 should support actual-ledger ingestion and separate **historical actual**, **current run-rate**, **budget**, **forecast**, and **scenario** data.
