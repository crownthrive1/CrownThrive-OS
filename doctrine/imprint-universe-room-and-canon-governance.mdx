# Imprint, Universe, Room and Canon Governance

CrownThrive's intellectual-property architecture includes more than platforms and products. Imprints, universes, rooms, series, collections, characters, studies and operating formats each require stable identity and rules.

## Object types

### Imprint

A governed cultural/editorial/commercial identity under CrownThrive. An imprint may own tone, audience, design language, canon rules, rights practices and product families without being a separate legal entity.

### Universe / world

A narrative/cultural property containing works, characters, chronology, locations, rooms, products, sound and adaptation rights.

### Room

A structured experience or operating surface organized around a purpose, audience and rules. Rooms may be physical metaphors, online destinations, ministry/study spaces, listening environments, creator spaces or gated operational interfaces.

### Series / collection

A grouping of works governed by sequence or theme without necessarily sharing full canon.

### Canon record

An approved statement about an identity, relationship, timeline, world rule or content boundary.

## Presentation counts versus governed object counts

A public navigation shelf may present selected doors without defining the complete universe registry. A public room count may exceed the number of currently recovered canonical names. Preserve separate metrics and records:

- featured/public door;
- top-level universe;
- nested series/collection;
- room/experience;
- declared count;
- directly named count;
- live-verified count;
- unresolved stable placeholder.

Current examples:

- Virality's nine featured homepage doors are a dated curated shelf, not the total governed-universe count;
- The Moon Knows Our Names is a featured door/first cycle within Black Baby Universe, not automatically a competing parent universe;
- KJV Visualized declares sixteen Ministry \+ Study Rooms while fourteen canonical names are recovered and two stable room records remain unresolved.

Agents may not promote a door, cycle, alias, historical concept or similarly named cross-corridor room merely to make counts agree.

```text
CrownThrive LLC
  └── imprint

              ├── work / room / character / study

              ├── rights profile
              ├── audience/content class
              └── platform distribution routes
```

## Canon state

`proposed → reviewed → canonical → corrected → superseded → alternate_continuity → archived`

Rumor, disputed account, unreliable narrator, historical uncertainty and fictional evidence must be modeled as content states rather than collapsed into canon truth.

## Crossovers and handoffs

A crossover requires:

- explicit parent identities;
- canon effect on each side;
- rights and contributor authority;
- audience compatibility;
- cultural/editorial review;
- data and product implications;
- licensing/adaptation scope;
- effective version;
- rollback or decanonization path where applicable.

A marketing link is not a canon crossover.

## Audience controls

Each universe/room should declare whether it is general, family, children/youth, ministry, adult, 18\+, 21\+, professional, developer, partner or internal. Age gates do not replace legal compliance, platform policies or content labeling.

## Room governance

A room record identifies:

- purpose and intended transformation;
- audience and access;
- host platform;
- privacy/data class;
- source/canon rules;
- products, events, media and curriculum;
- moderators/reviewers;
- AI use;
- commerce/licensing;
- related rooms;
- metrics;
- current state.

## Cultural Imprint Engine relationship

The Cultural Imprint Engine governs context, cultural specificity, representation, aesthetics, naming, voice, historical responsibility and cross-imprint reuse. It does not create ownership by itself; CHLOM records the applicable rights, authority, evidence and remedies.

## Machine-discovery rule

Search, recommendations and agents must retrieve relationships through stable records. They must not infer that similarly named characters, rooms, editions, brands or imprints are the same entity.

## Commercialization

Licensing may cover an entire imprint, a universe, character, room format, product family, visual style package, curriculum or defined adaptation right. The grant must be explicit and cannot silently include unrelated CrownThrive properties.
