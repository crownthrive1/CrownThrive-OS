# Founder Doctrine and Code

Phase 2.7 restores founder doctrine as a first-class institutional source family rather than reducing it to business-plan narrative.

<Warning>
  Historical doctrine is preserved as lineage. It does not automatically override current legal authority, adopted policy, current registries, production evidence, or later founder decisions.
</Warning>

## Institutional purpose

The recovered Founder Handbook / Playbook / Activation Package treats CrownThrive as a **convergent ecosystem** whose shared infrastructure, knowledge, culture, governance, technology, media, commerce, education, community, licensing, and impact functions must remain intelligible across leadership transitions.

A recurring doctrine is that documentation is **institutional and generational insurance**. CrownThrive should be reconstructable from governed records instead of founder memory, a single SaaS, or scattered chats.

## Knowledge preservation doctrine

CrownThrive does **not silently destroy institutional knowledge**. History, source lineage, canon, doctrine, prior decisions, superseded states, failed experiments and correction records are part of the institutional foundation.

Permanent rules:

- no material knowledge is silently overwritten;
- a newer state **supersedes** an older state through explicit version/correction lineage rather than erasing it;
- historical material may be archived, restricted, redirected, merged or marked retired, but its existence and provenance remain recoverable;
- corrections append the corrected state and reason while preserving the prior state;
- deletion is reserved for governed legal/privacy/security obligations or non-substantive duplicate/transport cleanup, and even then the institutional record retains the disposition and reason where legally permitted;
- machine registries, docs, canon and doctrine must be exportable off-provider.

This rule applies across Phases 1–20 and to future roadmap generations.

## Chat, founder-memory and idea capture

Founder conversations, ideas, decisions and recollections are legitimate **source inputs** when available to an authorized agent. Material CrownThrive content surfaced in chats should be reconciled into governed institutional records rather than remaining conversation-only.

Chat-derived material must be labeled by evidence class:

- a direct founder decision or attestation may establish founder-controlled policy, intent or personally observed non-secret administrative facts within scope;
- a recollection is preserved as founder recollection until corroborated where independent proof is required;
- brainstorming and proposed ideas remain proposed/research until adopted;
- runtime, cryptographic, payment, legal-rights or provider-behavior claims still require their applicable evidence/specialist controls.

An agent may use accessible prior conversations and institutional memory to discover material, but inaccessible chats must never be assumed recovered. Material chat-derived knowledge is promoted into Mintlify/GitHub/source registries with date, source context, disposition, version and correction lineage.

## Canon and doctrine as first-class knowledge

`canon` and `doctrine` are first-class institutional knowledge classes. Each material canon/doctrine record should carry a stable ID, provenance, version/effective period, owner/authority, current/historical/research state, supersession/correction lineage, cross-platform relationships, machine-ingestible projection and off-provider recovery path.

Canon constrains what is accepted as true/authorized within its defined creative, cultural, institutional or product domain. Doctrine records why and how CrownThrive intends to operate. Neither silently overrides stronger current legal, transactional, runtime or later founder evidence outside its scope.

## Founder culture pillars

The handbook source family repeatedly centers:

- Clarity
- Execution
- Integrity
- Legacy
- Discipline
- Community First
- Accountability

Related ThriveAlumni cultural language includes **Service, Honor, Study, and Example**. These are cultural/leadership values, not substitutes for legal authority or role permissions.

## Founder Code themes

Recovered themes include:

1. Protect institutional knowledge and CrownThrive intellectual property.
2. Do not misuse access, confidential information, credentials, customer data, or ecosystem authority.
3. Do not undermine the institution for short-term personal advantage.
4. Do not trade safety, legal integrity, data integrity, cultural trust, or rights integrity for revenue.
5. Surface failures and material risks early rather than hiding them.
6. Preserve source lineage and correction history.
7. Train successors and reduce key-person dependency.
8. Treat every platform as part of a governed institutional system, not an isolated side project.

## Leadership continuity

The source family preserves concepts including apprenticeship, succession, a Capstone Governance Thesis, long-horizon stewardship, knowledge transfer, and structured activation. Phase 3 should convert these into explicit role, approval, succession, and continuity records rather than personality-dependent practice.

## Historical incubation doctrine

Older material uses staged incubation language roughly equivalent to Phase 0 through Phase 4 progression. Preserve that lineage, but map current portfolio lifecycle states independently so historical stage names do not create false current status.

## Operating cadence

Recovered doctrine supports a recurring institutional rhythm spanning daily, weekly, monthly, quarterly, and annual review. Each cadence should eventually bind to owners, evidence, KPIs, risks, decisions, and DAIL events.

## Canonicalization rule

Founder doctrine answers **why and how CrownThrive intends to operate**. It does not by itself prove that a specific product is active, an entity exists, a patent was filed, a payment promise is enforceable, or a technical feature is deployed.
