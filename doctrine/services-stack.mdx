# Services Stack

The **Services Stack** is the institutional layer that turns CrownThrive infrastructure into repeatable customer and partner outcomes. Public CrownThrive currently presents service families such as business/system operations, advertising/distribution, digital products/platform development, media production/publishing, events/experiences, and monetization/financial strategy.

## Service families

The exact public catalog can evolve, but each service should resolve to governed internal capabilities rather than a custom founder-only process.

Representative families include:

- strategy, formation and operating-system design;
- platform, portal and digital-product development;
- automation and agent workflows;
- advertising, creator, affiliate and distribution programs;
- media production, publishing and syndication;
- commerce, product, licensing and monetization design;
- education, cohort and expert-delivery programs;
- event, ticketing and experience operations;
- support, knowledge, analytics and optimization;
- rights, provenance and licensing support through CHLOM where applicable.

## Service record

Every service should have a stable ID, public promise, target customer, scope boundaries, deliverables, prerequisites, pricing model, owner, SLA/support path, required tools, data/rights requirements, acceptance criteria, evidence, renewal/retainer behavior and cross-sell rules.

## Delivery pattern

```text
Discovery + Alignment
→ Scope + Authority
→ Execution Roadmap
→ Delivery
→ Acceptance / Evidence
→ Support
→ Measurement
→ Optimization / Expansion
```

## Anti-fragmentation rule

A service does not require a new public platform. Reusable internal engines can support many offers. The public service catalog should remain understandable even when the underlying ecosystem is complex.

## Phase 3 implication

The Orchestrator should be able to resolve a service ID into approved workflows, agent roles, platform capabilities, rights checks, deliverables and evidence requirements.
