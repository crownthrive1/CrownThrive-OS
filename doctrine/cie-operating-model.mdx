# CIE Operating Model

The **Cultural Imprint Engine operating model** converts CrownThrive cultural doctrine into a repeatable, evidence-backed decision process. It is the public-safe contract for how an imprint, narrative, visual system, crossover, campaign, channel, adaptation or public cultural claim enters review and exits with a bounded state.

This operating model does not expose protected scoring, private calibration or restricted evaluation material.

## Intake classes

A CIE review begins by classifying the object being evaluated.

| Intake class | Typical examples |
| --- | --- |
| `IMPRINT_IDENTITY` | new imprint, channel, brand family, cultural program |
| `NARRATIVE_CANON` | universe rule, character continuity, origin story, series canon |
| `REPRESENTATION` | imagery, language, community portrayal, historical framing |
| `VISUAL_AESTHETIC` | logo, visual system, photography, motion, symbolism |
| `CROSS_IMPRINT_REUSE` | adaptation, collaboration, crossover, syndicated reuse |
| `PUBLIC_NARRATIVE` | press language, launch statement, public description, crisis response |
| `PARTNERSHIP_CULTURAL_FIT` | co-brand, creator, operator, distributor or external partner use |
| `AUDIENCE_BOUNDARY` | family, children/youth, ministry, adult, 18\+, 21\+, professional |
| `CORRECTION_CONTINUITY` | rename, correction, retirement, successor, archived identity |

An intake can carry more than one class. The classes are dimensions of review, not licenses.

## Required intake envelope

Every material CIE request should carry enough evidence to identify the object and the proposed use.

```yaml
cie_request:
  request_id: stable-id
  object_id: stable-object-id
  object_type: imprint|universe|room|campaign|asset|channel|claim|partnership
  proposed_use: human-readable purpose
  audience_class: declared
  source_refs: []
  rights_state: known|unknown|disputed|not_applicable
  canon_state: proposed|reviewed|canonical|corrected|superseded|archived|not_applicable
  public_exposure: none|internal|partner|public
  commercial_effect: none|candidate|material
  requested_action: review|classify|reuse|publish|correct|retire
```

Missing material identity, source, audience or rights context should produce HOLD rather than invented assumptions.

## Review sequence

```text
1. IDENTIFY
2. SOURCE
3. CLASSIFY
4. CONTEXT
5. RIGHTS BOUNDARY
6. CULTURAL/NARRATIVE REVIEW
7. AUDIENCE + PUBLIC-CLAIM REVIEW
8. DECISION
9. HANDOFF
10. EVIDENCE + CONTINUITY
```

### 1. Identify

Resolve stable IDs, canonical names, aliases, parents, successors and current lifecycle state.

### 2. Source

Determine what the requested claim or creative direction is based on. Preserve source-derived statements as source evidence until higher-authority current evidence adopts or supersedes them.

### 3. Classify

Determine imprint family, object type, audience/content class and affected corridors/platforms.

### 4. Context

Identify the cultural, historical, community and narrative context that would be lost if the asset were treated as generic content.

### 5. Rights boundary

CIE may inspect declared rights state because rights affect whether a proposed use can proceed, but CIE does not create the right. Unknown or disputed material rights route to CHLOM/legal review.

### 6. Cultural and narrative review

Evaluate continuity, representation, context, provenance, narrative consistency, visual/aesthetic fit and responsible reuse.

### 7. Audience and public-claim review

Check whether the use crosses family/adult/ministry/professional boundaries or makes unsupported claims about ownership, status, endorsement, availability or commercial authority.

### 8. Decision

Return one of the bounded CIE states.

| State | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `PASS` | CIE scope is satisfied for the evaluated use. No downstream authority is created. |
| `CONDITIONAL_PASS` | CIE scope can pass if named conditions are satisfied before downstream execution. |
| `HOLD` | evidence, rights, context, audience, authority or another prerequisite is unresolved. |
| `DENY` | the proposed use conflicts with a CIE hard boundary and should not proceed in the submitted form. |
| `CORRECT` | an already-published or recorded surface requires a controlled correction while preserving lineage. |

### 9. Handoff

Route the result to the next authority without collapsing layers.

- CHLOM for rights, licensing, permission, evidence and remedies;
- CSS for reusable platform/service execution;
- ThriveEvergreen for governed economic activation;
- platform/runtime governance for deployment;
- human governance for D3/high-impact decisions;
- Master Narrative Ledger for canon, naming and public wording continuity.

### 10. Evidence and continuity

Record what was evaluated, under which source/version, who or what reviewed it, the decision state, conditions, handoffs and reopen triggers.

## CIE hard blocks

A public-safe implementation should fail closed when the request attempts to:

- infer cultural identity or sensitive traits about a person as a score;
- manufacture provenance, source-community authority or ownership;
- merge separate imprints, universes or communities without evidence;
- cross a declared audience boundary without required controls;
- publish restricted/private evidence or protected implementation material;
- treat a source contract as proof that a current offer, price or entitlement is active;
- convert CIE PASS into a CHLOM license, checkout, payout, provider write, production deployment or sovereign vote;
- bypass required human review for material public-cultural or D3 decisions.

## Correction protocol

CIE uses correction rather than historical erasure.

```text
detect drift
→ preserve old source/version
→ identify canonical replacement
→ record reason and authority
→ update public/current projection
→ retain predecessor link
→ re-run affected downstream validations
```

This is especially important for renamed frameworks, stale public descriptions, retired imprints and source-era commercial terms.

## Reopen triggers

A prior CIE outcome should reopen when material inputs change, including:

- source or rights evidence changes;
- audience classification changes;
- canon or parent identity changes;
- a new distribution territory or format changes context;
- commercial use changes from candidate to material;
- legal/privacy/security controls change;
- public wording materially changes;
- protected-runtime digest or required evidence becomes unavailable;
- a correction or contradiction is discovered.

## Machine role

The current CIE framework agent may validate envelopes, produce public-safe review artifacts, prepare handoffs and preserve evidence inside its D2 ceiling. It is non-voting, cannot self-certify, and must HOLD when protected runtime or required authority is missing.

## Human role

Human review remains mandatory where cultural consequence, legal rights, public narrative, children/family safety, sensitive representation or D3 authority is material. Automation is used for consistency and evidence, not to eliminate accountable judgment.
