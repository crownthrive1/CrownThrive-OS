# CIE Framework Reconciliation Contract

This contract is the current public-safe substantive successor for recovered Cultural Imprint Engine framework material whose legacy titles describe channel operations, implementation models, analytics, network structures, or integrations across CrownThrive properties. It preserves the institutional meaning of those records without treating a recovered framework title as proof of current deployment, production activation, economic authority, rights authority, or a completed implementation.

The governing invariant is: **a framework defines reusable structure and decision boundaries; it does not manufacture execution authority or prove runtime state**.

## Framework identity and lineage

CIE is the cultural-governance layer that protects meaning, representation, narrative continuity, imprint identity, audience context, and responsible reuse across CrownThrive. The canonical current doctrine is defined in [Cultural Imprint Engine](/doctrine/cultural-imprint-engine). Recovered documents may describe channels, networks, production models, analytics practices, imprint operations, studio relationships, or platform-specific implementations. Those descriptions remain useful lineage, but they must be reconciled against the current CIE identity before reuse.

A current framework record should therefore distinguish:

```text
recovered framework title
→ stable CIE framework identity
→ source and alias lineage
→ current capability boundary
→ affected imprints / channels / surfaces
→ implementation state
→ evidence and review state
→ governed handoffs
```

Historical names, provider assumptions, platform assignments, or deployment models remain source facts until current evidence or an authoritative decision promotes them.

## Public and internal operating profiles

CIE has one institutional identity with different governed exposure profiles. [CIE Public & Internal Usage](/doctrine/cie-public-internal-usage) defines how public-safe doctrine and internal governed operating detail can coexist without creating two separate engines. A recovered framework may contain material appropriate only for internal implementation, restricted evidence, or a historical operating context. Publication therefore requires projection rules rather than copying every source detail into public documentation.

The public projection may explain purpose, role boundaries, interoperability, correction semantics, and approved operating concepts. Restricted implementation details, private analytics, credentials, internal review artifacts, confidential partner information, or source material with unresolved authority remain outside the public projection.

## Channels, networks, and implementation state

A channel or network described by a recovered CIE framework can be institutionally meaningful without being currently deployed. Each current record should separate at least these dimensions:

- **framework identity** — the reusable CIE rule or structure;
- **imprint or channel relationship** — which cultural/media identity the framework governs;
- **surface relationship** — which website, media channel, studio, room, application, or provider may implement it;
- **implementation state** — historical, specified, controlled-test, staged, production, superseded, or unresolved;
- **evidence state** — what current evidence supports the implementation claim;
- **authority state** — which actor or policy can approve changes or activation;
- **continuity state** — how the relationship survives provider, platform, domain, or organizational changes.

This prevents a detailed framework document from being mistaken for a live service map. It also prevents implementation drift from silently redefining cultural governance.

## Analytics and measurement boundary

Recovered channel-operations material may include analytics, performance indicators, audience segmentation, content measurement, or optimization concepts. CIE can define which cultural and narrative questions should be measured, but a metric does not become institutional truth merely because a dashboard reports it. Current measurement still depends on approved source definitions, evidence quality, privacy constraints, and the CrownThrive metrics model.

Analytics used by CIE must remain compatible with the source, evidence, and claims controls documented in [Evidence, Claims and Proof Standard](/standards/evidence-claims-and-proof-standard). CIE may inform interpretation and cultural context; it does not bypass evidence requirements or convert a provider metric into a public claim without reconciliation.

## Cross-platform handoffs

The current integration boundary is defined in [CIE Integration and Handoffs](/doctrine/cie-integration-handoffs). Framework material that references studios, media platforms, MM Suites, channels, partner imprints, CHLOM, or other CrownThrive systems must resolve through typed handoffs rather than collapsing those systems into CIE.

A safe handoff preserves:

1. the source asset or request;
2. the CIE cultural/context review state;
3. the stable institutional identity involved;
4. the receiving platform or governance subsystem;
5. the authority ceiling for the next action;
6. supporting evidence and correction lineage;
7. the result, HOLD, or DENY state.

CIE can supply cultural constraints, canon context, audience rules, imprint meaning, and review outcomes. It does not automatically grant licensing rights, payment authority, provider-write permission, production deployment, or institutional approval outside its charter.

## Framework versus implementation

The recovered framework family must keep the following non-equivalences machine-visible:

```text
framework definition ≠ deployed service
channel blueprint ≠ active channel
analytics model ≠ verified metric
integration diagram ≠ live interoperability
provider capability ≠ CrownThrive authority
historical assignment ≠ current platform mapping
CIE review ≠ rights or economic approval
```

Those distinctions are especially important when old material names providers, product surfaces, studios, channels, or proposed network structures that may have changed since the source was written.

## Correction and supersession

When a current CIE framework conflicts with older source material, CrownThrive preserves both lineage and the governing current interpretation. The old source is not silently rewritten. Instead, the current record identifies what changed, which source or decision controls, what implementation assumptions are superseded, and which dependent documents or systems require reconciliation.

The [Master Narrative Ledger](/doctrine/master-narrative-ledger) provides the terminology and continuity context for renamed or superseded institutional concepts. Material changes should also propagate through the documentation governance process so public and internal projections remain synchronized.

## Authority and safety boundary

This contract operates within bounded D2 documentation and architecture authority. It may classify, reconcile, link, specify, and prepare CIE framework records for parent review. It does not create founder authority, legal-policy effect, rights or license grants, economic authority, investment or securities claims, patent status, franchise or regional-license activation, identity credentials, provider-write expansion, secret publication, money movement, or production activation.

Where recovered material intersects those domains, the relevant specialist governance lane remains controlling. CIE framework reconciliation cannot be used as a semantic shortcut around CHLOM, ThriveEvergreen, security, legal, evidence, or other institutional gates.

## Phase relationship

This substantive successor improves the documentation and machine-addressable substrate required for later implementation. It does not unlock Phase 3. The [Phase 3 Readiness Gate](/technology/phase-3-readiness-gate) independently governs stable schemas, authorization, provider boundaries, evidence, rollback, security, observability, and operational readiness.

Phase 3 therefore remains `blocked_pending_phase_2_99_hard_exit`. Phases 11–20 remain `reserved_definition_required` until authoritative definitions exist.
