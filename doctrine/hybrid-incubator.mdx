# Hybrid Incubator

The **Hybrid Incubator** is CrownThrive's venture and capability-development model. It combines digital infrastructure, education, community, media, commerce, governance, and operating support so ideas can be tested and activated inside a larger institutional system.

## What it incubates

The model can support:

- founder and entrepreneur ventures;
- CrownThrive-owned portfolio assets;
- partner brands and co-branded programs;
- creators, instructors, affiliates, ambassadors, and experts;
- physical and phygital concepts;
- licensed cultural IP and imprint operators.

## Progression

Incubation should move through evidence gates rather than hype:

```text
Concept → Register → Validate → Build → Pilot → Monetize → Measure → Grow / License / Reserve / Sunset
```

Not every idea becomes a standalone company or public platform. Some become products, imprints, content properties, licensed concepts, internal engines, or reserved assets.

## Shared resources

Incubated work can draw from CrownThrive ID, CrownThrive IO/MCP, CHLOM, CrownThrive Support, CrownLytics, CrownRewards, Crown Affiliates, AdLuxe, CrownThriveU, OpsOasis, Collab Portal, media distribution, and selected commerce rails.

## Evidence gates

Before scaling, an incubated asset should establish customer need, accountable ownership, rights clarity, delivery capability, support, economics, analytics, legal fit, and operational capacity. Capital-intensive or regulated concepts require stronger review and may require separate entities, insurance, permits, professional counsel, or partner-funded pilots.

## Relationship to Holdings

The proposed Holdings layer governs portfolio lifecycle and capital allocation. CrownThrive, LLC operates the incubator. CHLOM governs rights, permissions, licensing, provenance, and related audit evidence.
