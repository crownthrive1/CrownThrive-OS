## Cultural Imprint Engine

The **Cultural Imprint Engine (CIE)** is CrownThrive's institutional framework for governing cultural meaning, narrative continuity, representation, imprint identity, aesthetics, canon and responsible reuse across the Convergent Ecosystem.

CIE exists so CrownThrive can scale across media, beauty, wellness, technology, education, commerce, licensing, community and physical corridors **without flattening culture into generic branding or letting operational growth outrun contextual responsibility**.

CIE is a governance framework and decision input. It is not a substitute for legal rights, CHLOM licensing, platform security, human judgment or source evidence.

## Institutional mission

CIE protects five things at the same time:

1. **Meaning** — why a work, brand, universe, campaign or experience exists.
2. **Continuity** — what is canon, what is historical, what is corrected and what is successor state.
3. **Representation** — how communities, identities, histories and cultural material are portrayed.
4. **Imprint integrity** — how names, voice, aesthetics, symbols, audience, channels and crossovers remain coherent.
5. **Responsible scale** — how a culturally specific asset can move through distribution, licensing, commerce, AI and cross-platform reuse without losing provenance or authority.

## Ten CIE governance domains

The recovered Founders Handbook establishes a broader CIE doctrine than a conventional style guide. CrownThrive carries that doctrine forward as ten connected governance domains.

| Domain | CIE responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| Institutional identity | Define the role, meaning and cultural position of an imprint, corridor, platform, universe or program. |
| Narrative doctrine | Govern story logic, origin, voice, narrative anchors, public framing and continuity. |
| Imprint classification | Classify institutional, corridor, platform, media/channel, brand/campaign and related imprint families. |
| Cultural governance | Review representation, historical context, cultural-use boundaries, partnership alignment and red lines. |
| Visual and aesthetic governance | Govern visual identity, symbolism, typography, imagery, motion and cross-surface aesthetic continuity. |
| Imprint architecture | Define parent/child identity, structural tier, scope, format, hierarchy and multi-layer imprint stacks. |
| Cross-imprint integration | Govern how corridors, platforms, products and channels collaborate without erasing distinct identity. |
| Continuity and permanence | Preserve archives, version history, legacy meaning, stewardship and drift-prevention controls. |
| Public perception and cultural diplomacy | Govern major external positioning, partnership narratives, crisis framing and culturally sensitive public communication. |
| Final cultural accountability | Require evidence, correction, escalation and stewardship when cultural impact is material. |

These domains are interoperable. They do not create independent ownership or licensing rights.

## Narrative doctrine

CIE evaluates material narratives against enduring CrownThrive anchors:

- heritage and origin;
- sovereignty and ownership;
- development and discipline;
- legacy and continuity;
- community and reciprocity.

A reusable story pattern recovered from CrownThrive doctrine is:

```text
Context → Constraint → Design → Discipline → Transition → Legacy
```

The pattern is a narrative tool, not a mandatory formula for every creative work. CIE uses it to test whether institutional messaging preserves origin, structure and long-horizon meaning.

## Imprint and canon model

CIE works with the [Imprint, Universe, Room and Canon Governance](/doctrine/imprint-universe-room-and-canon-governance) model.

At minimum, an imprint record should resolve:

- stable identity and aliases;
- owner/steward and legal relationship;
- imprint family and parent identity;
- purpose, audience and cultural context;
- narrative and visual profile;
- canon or continuity state where applicable;
- rights and licensing evidence;
- platform and distribution relationships;
- commerce boundaries;
- source masters and version history;
- current lifecycle and implementation state;
- correction, rollback and retirement path.

A similarly named asset, historical alias, public navigation door or marketing crossover does not become the same entity by inference.

## CIE decision states

CIE decisions should resolve to explicit, reviewable states rather than vague approval language.

```text
PROPOSED
→ CIE_SCORE
→ PASS | REVIEW | HOLD | FAIL | FAIL_HARD_BLOCK
→ PASS_WITH_CIE_WAIVER when a bounded, culturally legitimate exception is independently evidenced and accepted by the governed CIE waiver contract
→ PUBLISHED / APPLIED only through the separately authorized downstream system
→ CORRECTED | SUPERSEDED | RETIRED when state changes
```

For every **eligible CIE subject**, the scoring algorithm fires and returns the real numerical cultural-alignment score whenever the protected scorer itself is available. Missing dimension evidence or malformed/non-actionable findings are reported as input gaps; they do not replace the CIE score with `HOLD_INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE` and they do not manufacture deductions. Runtime unavailability, protected-bundle integrity failure, or a subject that is not eligible for CIE scoring remains a separate fail-closed condition.

The raw CIE score is immutable decision evidence. A waiver never edits, inflates or replaces it. When a raw score does not reach the normal pass threshold, has no non-waivable hard block, and a legitimate cultural/contextual reason exists, the governed CIE waiver contract may issue `PASS_WITH_CIE_WAIVER`. The waiver must bind the original score, raw verdict, reason code, substantive rationale, evidence references, exact scope, algorithm/runtime version, decision digest, actor/authority, DAIL score event, DAIL waiver event and any superseded waiver. Invalid, convenience-only or unsupported exception claims remain rejected.

`PASS` or `PASS_WITH_CIE_WAIVER` means the evaluated cultural/narrative use was accepted by the CIE contract presented to it. Neither state by itself creates a CHLOM license, legal approval, commercial entitlement, provider write, technical certification, production deployment or sovereign authority.

## Hard cultural controls

CIE may require correction, HOLD or escalation when a proposed use:

- removes provenance or falsely manufactures cultural origin;
- collapses distinct communities, imprints, universes or historical periods into one generic identity;
- uses caricature, dehumanizing stereotype, trauma spectacle or exploitative representation;
- materially distorts source history or current institutional state;
- uses culturally specific symbols, language or aesthetics without adequate context or authority;
- creates a misleading public claim about ownership, licensing, status or endorsement;
- crosses an audience boundary, including family/children, ministry, adult, 18\+ or 21\+ classifications;
- reuses protected narrative, character, visual or cultural IP outside the granted scope;
- bypasses required CHLOM, legal, privacy, security or human review.

CIE is designed to correct and preserve where possible, not to erase source history.

## Relationship to CHLOM

CIE and CHLOM are complementary and intentionally separate.

| Question | Primary authority |
| --- | --- |
| Is the use culturally, narratively and contextually aligned? | CIE |
| Is the asset owned, licensed, permitted, attributable and auditable? | CHLOM |
| Is the current public claim supported by evidence? | Source hierarchy \+ Evidence Standard |
| Can the platform technically execute it? | Platform/CSS/runtime controls |
| Can money, entitlement or commercial state change? | PentaGreen \+ finance/commerce governance |

**CIE does not manufacture rights. CHLOM does not manufacture cultural alignment.** A use may pass one layer and fail another.

## Relationship to the CrownThrive Services Stack (CSS)

The **CrownThrive Services Stack (CSS)** is the shared service architecture used to avoid rebuilding identity, authentication, authorization, billing, licensing, analytics, notifications, CRM, ticketing, search, commerce, routing, rewards and documentation separately for every platform.

CIE supplies cultural and narrative policy inputs to CSS-backed experiences. CSS supplies reusable execution capability. A CSS service cannot override a CIE hard block, and a CIE pass does not grant CSS authority that the caller does not already possess.

## Relationship to the Thrive Flywheel

CIE is a quality and integrity gate inside the Thrive Flywheel, not a drag on it.

```text
Discovery → Engagement → Value Delivery → Community Reinforcement
→ Amplification → Growth → Reinforcement → Discovery
```

CIE protects the meaning of what enters and recirculates through those stages. CrownLytics/CrownPulse and related evidence systems can surface performance or drift; CIE determines the cultural/narrative implications of that evidence within its authority.

## Relationship to the Hybrid Incubator

Every venture leaving the Hybrid Incubator should have enough CIE identity to answer:

- What is this?
- Who is it for?
- What culture, history or community does it touch?
- What is its narrative and visual identity?
- What is its audience/content class?
- What may it be confused with?
- What rights and permissions are required?
- What must remain true as it scales?

A venture can be commercially promising and still fail the CIE identity or cultural-integrity gate.

## Source and evidence discipline

CIE must preserve the difference between:

- source-derived doctrine;
- current adopted doctrine;
- current machine/runtime state;
- historical or superseded language;
- contractual source terms;
- public-safe projection;
- private/protected implementation.

A source file proves that a statement existed in that source. It does not automatically prove that the statement is the current operational, legal, financial or technical state.

## Public/private implementation boundary

The current CIE child package is public-safe by design. It may expose schemas, dimensions, hard-block interfaces, deterministic validators, stable IDs, digests, eval shapes and governed handoff contracts.

Protected calibration, private evaluation corpora, defensive rules, restricted evidence, credentials and protected economic/policy logic remain in approved private runtime/Vault custody. If the protected scoring runtime or its sealed policy bundle is unavailable or fails integrity verification, scoring fails closed as a runtime HOLD. For an otherwise eligible subject, ordinary evidence gaps are surfaced in the score packet and DAIL record but do not suppress the numerical CIE score. Technical/evidence certification remains a separate PentaCertifier concern and may independently HOLD even when CIE returns or waives a cultural-alignment score.

## Human-reserved decisions

High-impact cultural, rights, legal, public-narrative or D3 decisions remain human-reserved. The framework agent is non-voting and cannot self-certify, self-activate, alter quorum, remove Agent D, create a sovereign vote or convert a framework evaluation into production authority.

## First-wave institutionalization

Wave 1 establishes the durable doctrine and machine-addressable information plane:

- expanded CIE doctrine;
- explicit CIE ↔ CHLOM ↔ CSS ↔ Thrive Flywheel ↔ Hybrid Incubator handoffs;
- Kulture House/Kulture Radio and Bonged Out! institutional source records;
- source-lineage and current-state separation;

Runtime expansion, protected algorithm changes, production activation and new commercial authority are intentionally outside this wave and remain governed by their existing gates.
