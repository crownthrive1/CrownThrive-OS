# Cultural Imprint Engine

The **Cultural Imprint Engine (CIE)** is CrownThrive's cultural-governance framework. It exists to prevent the ecosystem from scaling operationally while losing context, authorship, cultural specificity, narrative continuity, or community accountability.

## Governing domains

CIE should classify and review:

- cultural context and audience;
- narrative and canon;
- protected identities and character continuity;
- imagery, aesthetics, language, and representation;
- source-community attribution;
- cross-imprint and cross-platform reuse;
- commercialization boundaries;
- corrections, retirements, and successor descriptions.

## Public contract and protected runtime

CIE uses a split architecture. Public-safe material may define bounded artifact/use/context schemas, the five public dimensions, hard-block interfaces, evidence requirements and public contract digests. Proprietary calibration, weights, private evaluation corpora, defensive rules, restricted evidence and protected reasoning stay in the governed Vault/private runtime. If the required private runtime or accepted digest is unavailable, material evaluation must HOLD rather than reproduce protected logic publicly.

The framework package is a non-voting D2 agentic machine. Package health or precheck validation is not operational certification, sovereign approval or permission to invoke the protected runtime. D3 remains human-reserved and Agent D remains mandatory for parent certification.

## Relationship to CHLOM

CIE and CHLOM are complementary, not duplicates. **CIE answers whether use is culturally and narratively aligned. CHLOM answers whether use is owned, permitted, licensed, eligible, recorded, and auditable.** A use can pass one layer and fail the other.

The interoperable handoff therefore carries only sanitized receipt/verdict metadata. A CIE PASS never grants ownership, permission, license, entitlement, certification or legal authority; CHLOM performs its own independent rights and governance gates.

## Continuity to the Convergent Ecosystem

The next Framework Factory target is the **Convergent Ecosystem**. CIE may define a public-safe candidate handoff for cultural-governance receipts, but Convergent Ecosystem implementation remains research-only until CIE has governed framework acceptance and linked-governed parent certification. Generating a schema or agent identity does not activate the next framework or create a vote.

## Commercialization boundary

Public-safe reference contracts, implementation kits, managed-runtime integration scopes, training/operator enablement and conformance-assessment designs may be packaged as commercialization candidates. Exact pricing, Stripe Product/Price objects, checkout, license grants, certification status and customer entitlements require separate authority and rights clearance.

## Imprints

Recovered institutional sources identify imprint-oriented structures such as Kulture House, BongedOut!, Luxperiences Network, ThriveStudio Productions, media channels, and other culturally governed operating concepts. Their historical existence does not automatically establish current production status. Each imprint needs a stable record, status, rights relationship, and current owner.

## Phase 3 implication

CIE should become a policy input to the Approval Service and Rules Registry, with explicit human review for sensitive cultural, identity, or public-narrative decisions. Phase 3 remains blocked until the Phase 2.99 hard exit and full documentation reconciliation are accepted.
