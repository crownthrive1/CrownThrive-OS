# Cultural Imprint Engine

The **Cultural Imprint Engine (CIE)** is CrownThrive's cultural-governance framework for evaluating an artifact, proposed use, narrative context, source-community relationship, canon, brand safety, and legacy impact without scoring people or inferring sensitive traits.

CIE remains **`CONTROLLED_TEST / PROVISIONED_UNLINKED`**, non-operational, non-voting, and pending Agent-D parent certification. Its physical child repository exists, but a candidate contract is not `linked_governed` merely because files, tests, OIDC-compatible workflows, transport messages, or repository identities exist.

## Public decision contract

The public-safe model contains five equal dimensions:

| Dimension | Maximum |
|---|---:|
| Identity Fit | 20 |
| Community Value | 20 |
| Story Alignment | 20 |
| Brand Safety | 20 |
| Legacy Impact | 20 |

The public pass threshold is **85 of 100**, but hard blocks override any score. Missing material evidence returns **HOLD**. Person scoring, sensitive-trait inference, unverified rights, material canon conflict, source-community harm without a governed remedy, public disclosure of restricted material, digest mismatch, sovereign-vote creation, and D3 escalation are hard blocks.

The dimensions, threshold, schemas, state machine, hard-block interface, non-voting agent contract, and public digests may be published. Calibration, weights, private eval corpora, defensive rules, runtime locators, credentials, fingerprints, private evidence, and private policy or economic calibration remain in protected custody.

## Interoperability contract

CIE interoperability uses:

- versioned JSON/REST contracts;
- MCP tool contracts that remain disabled until certification;
- idempotency keys and bounded payloads;
- signed, replay-resistant event envelopes;
- deterministic SHA-256 request and decision receipts;
- public-safe portable JSON exports;
- least-data defaults and private evidence by default;
- explicit human review, appeal, remedy, and audit paths.

Provider credentials never belong in a browser or public contract. Unsigned events, replay, digest mismatch, protected-field publication, and non-idempotent material requests fail closed.

## Relationship to CHLOM

CIE and CHLOM are complementary, not duplicates.

1. **CIE** evaluates cultural alignment, source context, canon, narrative continuity, community value, brand safety, and legacy impact.
2. **CHLOM** verifies identity, chain of title, rights, permission, license, evidence provenance, policy, remedies, and auditability.
3. A use can pass one layer and fail the other.
4. CIE never grants legal rights, and CHLOM never substitutes for cultural alignment.
5. Material or consequential decisions require human review and a governed appeal/remedy path.

The candidate CHLOM pallet is `ct.chlom.pallet.cie-cultural-governance`. Wallet, blockchain, token, settlement, or money-movement activation is not implied.
