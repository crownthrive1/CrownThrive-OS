# Cultural Imprint Engine

The **Cultural Imprint Engine (CIE)** is CrownThrive's cultural-governance framework. It exists to prevent the ecosystem from scaling operationally while losing context, authorship, cultural specificity, narrative continuity, or community accountability.

## Governing domains

CIE should classify and review:

- cultural context and audience;
- narrative and canon;
- protected identities and character continuity;
- imagery, aesthetics, language, and representation;
- source-community attribution;
- cross-imprint and cross-platform reuse;
- commercialization boundaries;
- corrections, retirements, and successor descriptions.

## Public contract and protected runtime

CIE uses a split architecture. Public-safe material may define bounded artifact/use/context schemas, the five public dimensions, hard-block interfaces, evidence requirements and public contract digests. Proprietary calibration, weights, private evaluation corpora, defensive rules, restricted evidence and protected reasoning stay in the governed Vault/private runtime. If the required private runtime or accepted digest is unavailable, material evaluation must HOLD rather than reproduce protected logic publicly.

The framework package is a non-voting D2 agentic machine. Package health or precheck validation is not operational certification, sovereign approval or permission to invoke the protected runtime. D3 remains human-reserved and Agent D remains mandatory for parent certification.

## Agent-machine contract

The public CIE agent-machine contract names one non-voting parent agent and six bounded, non-voting builder/transport subagents for closed-envelope validation, protected-runtime request preparation, sanitized CHLOM record preparation, plan-only retroactive scanning, research-only Convergent handoff preparation, and public-safe evidence packaging.

Every identity remains `PREPARED_NOT_ACTIVATED` or `PRECERT_CONTRACT_VALIDATION_ONLY`. Delegated children cannot independently verify Agent-C-originated work, certify a parent, cast a sovereign vote, create a vote receipt, change quorum, remove Agent D, exercise D3, activate a framework, or invoke `sync_agents`.

The agent machine may prepare deterministic, unexecuted request or planning capsules. Protected-runtime dispatch, CHLOM writes, provider reads/writes, database writes, customer writes, rights grants, certification, commerce, and framework activation remain disabled.

## Relationship to CHLOM

CIE and CHLOM are complementary, not duplicates. **CIE answers whether use is culturally and narratively aligned. CHLOM answers whether use is owned, permitted, licensed, eligible, recorded, and auditable.** A use can pass one layer and fail the other.

The interoperable handoff therefore carries only sanitized receipt/verdict metadata. A CIE PASS never grants ownership, permission, license, entitlement, certification or legal authority; CHLOM performs its own independent rights and governance gates.

The public CHLOM pallet record schema covers six functions: rights, rules, roles, revenue, records, and remedies. It excludes private evidence bytes, credentials, private fingerprints, protected algorithm bodies, and private runtime topology. The record schema is not a write authorization.

## Retroactive ecosystem scan

CIE includes a bounded, plan-only retroactive scan contract for public-safe artifact and platform-content surfaces. Person, employee, customer, user-profile, individual-sensitive-profile, or sensitive-trait scoring is prohibited.

A valid scan plan does not authorize a provider read, database query, provider mutation, CHLOM write, or customer action. New findings remain `RESEARCH_CANDIDATE`; registry growth alone is not certification debt. Execution requires a separately accepted packet, authorized read adapter and scope, applicable specialists, current-main reconciliation, Agent-D exact-head certification, and tested rollback.

## Continuity to the Convergent Ecosystem

The next Framework Factory target is the **Convergent Ecosystem**. CIE may define a public-safe candidate handoff for cultural-governance receipts, but Convergent Ecosystem implementation remains research-only until CIE has governed framework acceptance and linked-governed parent certification. Generating a schema or agent identity does not activate the next framework or create a vote.

The retroactive scan contract may surface research candidates for a future Convergent packet. It cannot automatically promote, implement, activate, or certify that framework.

## Institutionalization and commercialization boundary

The institutional package includes public-safe contracts, validators, skills, documentation, changelog, asset classification, API/MCP mapping, CHLOM integration design, rollback/reopen rules, and fulfillment candidate scopes.

Commercialization candidates include a public-safe reference pack, governed managed-runtime service, API/MCP integration, CHLOM pallet integration, Convergent readiness assessment, operator training, implementation service, and conformance assessment. Protected runtime source, calibration, weights, private evaluation corpora, defensive rules, private evidence, credentials, fingerprints, and private topology are not customer deliverables.

Exact pricing, Stripe Product/Price objects, checkout, payment links, license grants, certification status, customer entitlements, and automatic fulfillment require separate governed authority and rights clearance.

## Imprints

Recovered institutional sources identify imprint-oriented structures such as Kulture House, BongedOut!, Luxperiences Network, ThriveStudio Productions, media channels, and other culturally governed operating concepts. Their historical existence does not automatically establish current production status. Each imprint needs a stable record, status, rights relationship, and current owner.

## Phase 3 implication

CIE should become a policy input to the Approval Service and Rules Registry, with explicit human review for sensitive cultural, identity, or public-narrative decisions. Phase 3 remains blocked until the Phase 2.99 hard exit and full documentation reconciliation are accepted.
