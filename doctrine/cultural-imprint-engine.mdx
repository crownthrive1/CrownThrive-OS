# Cultural Imprint Engine

The **Cultural Imprint Engine (CIE)** is CrownThrive's cultural-governance framework. It exists to prevent the ecosystem from scaling operationally while losing context, authorship, cultural specificity, narrative continuity, or community accountability.

## Governing domains

CIE should classify and review:

- cultural context and audience;
- narrative and canon;
- protected identities and character continuity;
- imagery, aesthetics, language, and representation;
- source-community attribution;
- cross-imprint and cross-platform reuse;
- commercialization boundaries;
- corrections, retirements, and successor descriptions.

## Relationship to CHLOM

CIE and CHLOM are complementary, not duplicates. **CIE answers whether use is culturally and narratively aligned. CHLOM answers whether use is owned, permitted, licensed, eligible, recorded, and auditable.** A use can pass one layer and fail the other.

The current interoperability candidate defines a CHLOM pallet interface across six functions: **rights, rules, roles, revenue, records, and remedies**. It is contract-validation-only. It does not grant database, provider, customer, or public-client write authority. Any future write path requires an accepted CHLOM contract, service-only authority, independent security/privacy review, read-before-write, read-after-write, and tested rollback.

## Framework agent and authority

The package identity is `ct.framework-package.cie`; the framework agent identity is `ct.framework-agent.cie`. The framework agent and its delegated subagents are non-voting. Package files, workflows, OIDC receipts, transport messages, tests, registrations, or successful evaluations do not create a sovereign vote. A/B/C/D/S remain the sovereign voter pool, Agent D remains mandatory for parent certification, and D3 remains human-reserved.

## Public-safe interoperability boundary

The public package may expose a closed envelope, safe subject types, hard-block states, deterministic validation, public contract digests, and fail-closed interfaces. CIE evaluates artifacts and uses, not people. Person, user-profile, employee-profile, customer-profile, or sensitive-authenticity scoring is prohibited.

The current public-safe shell can validate an envelope and prepare non-executing HOLD results. It cannot invoke the protected decision runtime, mutate a provider or database, issue rights clearance, activate a customer entitlement, or create sovereign authority.

## Protected runtime boundary

Protected CIE calibration, weights, defensive rules, private evaluation corpora, restricted evidence, credentials, private fingerprints, and private policy or economic logic remain outside public and customer outputs. They stay in governed Vault/private-runtime custody. If the required runtime or its registered digest is unavailable or mismatched, the required result is `HOLD_PROTECTED_RUNTIME_UNAVAILABLE`.

## Convergent Ecosystem handoff

CIE is the predecessor gate for the next Framework Factory packet. The current CIE-to-Convergent contract is intentionally non-executable and returns `HOLD_TARGET_FRAMEWORK_NOT_ACCEPTED`. Convergent Ecosystem remains a research candidate until CIE is linked/governed and accepted, Agent D certifies the current head, Phase 2.99 hard-exit gates pass, and the Convergent source receives a separate governed promotion packet.

## Imprints

Recovered institutional sources identify imprint-oriented structures such as Kulture House, BongedOut!, Luxperiences Network, ThriveStudio Productions, media channels, and other culturally governed operating concepts. Their historical existence does not automatically establish current production status. Each imprint needs a stable record, status, rights relationship, and current owner.

## Commercialization boundary

Public-safe reference packs, implementation scopes, managed-runtime designs, API/MCP integration, operator training, Convergent readiness assessment, and CHLOM pallet integration design may be prepared as candidates. No exact price, Stripe Product or Price, checkout, license grant, certification status, fulfillment activation, or customer entitlement is authorized by the framework packet.

## Phase 3 implication

CIE should become a policy input to the Approval Service and Rules Registry, with explicit human review for sensitive cultural, identity, or public-narrative decisions. The current candidate does not close Phase 2.99, activate Phase 3, or certify CIE.
