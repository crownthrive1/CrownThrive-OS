# Revival Doctrine

<Warning>
  Recovery is not activation. A historical name, domain, engine, article, architecture paper, prototype or business plan remains institutional history until its current disposition is governed.
</Warning>

## Why this doctrine exists

CrownThrive has accumulated a large multi-era estate of brands, platforms, frameworks, research, operating bibles, product concepts, technical architecture and Help Center specifications. The institution should preserve the useful invention without rebuilding obsolete sprawl.

The Founder Handbook itself treats documentation as institutional memory and requires the ecosystem roster to evolve without losing the lineage that future stewards need. The current policy therefore separates **knowledge preservation** from **operational activation**.

## Permanent rule

Every material historical asset receives exactly one governing disposition:

`ACTIVATE | REVIVE | MERGE | MIGRATE | RESEARCH | RESERVE | LICENSE | ARCHIVE | SUPERSEDE | SUNSET`

A disposition is not merely a label. It determines the asset's current public treatment, build authority, risk review, successor relationship, documentation state and reopening trigger.

## Requirements archaeology

Recovered Help Center articles and historical plans can function as a **requirements archaeology dataset**.

A historical source may reveal:

- a user problem CrownThrive intended to solve;
- a workflow or operating capability;
- a policy or governance invariant;
- an integration pattern;
- a monetization concept;
- a content or production format;
- a reusable name, brand, role, template or technical component.

The current team may preserve the requirement while rejecting the old implementation. A source description never becomes runtime proof merely because it is detailed.

## Source-to-current transformation

```text
historical source
→ source classification and effective era
→ reusable requirement/IP extraction
→ current collateral and evidence search
→ current best-use-case research
→ successor/overlap analysis
→ disposition proposal
→ risk and specialist review
→ independent governance/quorum
→ current artifact or explicit reserve/archive state
→ versioned changelog and reopening trigger
```

A reconstructed current artifact is labeled as a new current artifact. It is never represented as the lost or superseded historical original.

Preserve and disposition the Founder Handbook, Foundational Volume, Core Operational Triad, compensation/attribution studies, Legal Depot/SOP estate, market/defensibility studies and the CHLOM paper/technical corpus.

Advanced CHLOM blockchain, dedicated L1/Substrate, tokenomics, ZK proof networks, decentralized oracles, DAOs, staking/DeFi and cross-chain designs remain **RESEARCH** unless a later governed phase promotes them with current technical and legal evidence.

## Merge instead of relaunch

A reusable capability does not require its old standalone product to return.

Examples under current review include:

- SocialAIly functions into Thrive AI Studio / AdLuxe Studio / ThrivePush;
- CrownApps / ThriveApps into one canonical asset after naming governance;
- CrownThrive Music lineage under the current Virality Music system unless a later ADR changes the relationship;
- duplicate studio identities into the accepted Studios hierarchy;
- duplicate booking/service-operation names into the current ThriveSeat lane;
- old Cafe naming preserved while Melanin Magic Cafe remains distinct;
- overlapping intelligence functions split cleanly among CrownInsights, CrownLytics and CrownPulse.

## Evidence boundaries

- domain custody ≠ active application;
- purchased or historical engine ≠ installed/current deployment;
- provider documentation ≠ CrownThrive entitlement;
- historical price or commission ≠ current economic authority;
- historical blockchain/token claim ≠ deployed network;
- recovered article title/body ≠ current policy;
- public reachability ≠ rights, security, payment or technical certification.

## History never disappears

CrownThrive does not silently destroy or overwrite institutional knowledge. `MERGE`, `MIGRATE`, `SUPERSEDE` and `SUNSET` preserve the predecessor, source files, prior role, effective era, decision reason and successor relationship.

## Governance

Material `ACTIVATE`, `REVIVE`, `MIGRATE` and `LICENSE` decisions require the applicable risk-derived specialists and independent governance. CI, an agent's own tag or fluent reconstructed prose is evidence, not quorum.

## Phase inheritance

- **Phase 1:** preserve origin, identities and doctrine.
- **Phase 2 / 2.99:** complete recovery, disposition, current-state reconciliation and documentation acceptance.
- **Phase 3:** instantiate accepted assets through the CHLOM \+ THIVEBASE Institutional Core, then CrownThrive ID, then one bounded adapter pilot.
- **Phases 4–10:** federate, monetize, license, physicalize, scale and prove continuity as gates permit.
- **Phases 11–20:** replication through perpetual institutional continuity inherit the same source-lineage and no-destruction rules.

## Phase 3 boundary

Historical contradictions may remain as explicit lineage where they do not affect present authority or behavior. Material current-truth contradictions affecting authority, rights, privacy, security, money, production/runtime state or public representation must be closed before Phase 3.
