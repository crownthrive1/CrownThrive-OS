# Core Operating Spine

The **Core Operating Spine** is the institutional structure that keeps CrownThrive operating as one body instead of a collection of disconnected platforms.

## Canonical sequence

```text
CrownThrive ID
      ↓
CrownThrive IO / MCP
      ↓
CHLOM
      ↓
OpsOasis + Collab Portal
      ↓
CrownThrive Support + CrownInsights
      ↓
CrownLytics + CrownPulse + ThrivePush
      ↓
CrownRewards + partner / affiliate distribution
      ↓
Operating corridors and portfolio assets
```

The sequence describes institutional responsibility, not proof that every integration is already technically complete.

## Spine responsibilities

### Governance and authority

Who may decide, approve, publish, spend, license, change access, or alter an institutional record.

### Identity

Who or what is acting, which roles apply, and which consents or eligibility claims exist.

### Interoperability

How platforms call approved tools, exchange normalized events, route work and handle failures without sharing unrestricted credentials.

### Rights and rules

What CrownThrive owns or controls, what others may do with it, which policies apply, and what evidence supports the decision.

### Operations

How work becomes assigned, versioned, accepted and audited through OpsOasis, Collab Portal, GitHub/Codex and specialized systems.

### Support and knowledge

How people and agents retrieve current institutional guidance, resolve issues and convert repeated failures into updated knowledge.

### Measurement and engagement

How CrownLytics, CrownPulse, ThrivePush and related systems convert normalized events into analysis, proof, communication and re-engagement.

### Retention and distribution

How CrownRewards, affiliates, ambassadors, partners, media and other channels create ethical repeat activity and expansion.

## Phase 3 implication

Phase 3 implements the machine-readable center of this spine—DAIL, approvals, rights, DLA, rules, roles, revenue, remedies and IO/MCP—without requiring every downstream platform to be rebuilt first.
