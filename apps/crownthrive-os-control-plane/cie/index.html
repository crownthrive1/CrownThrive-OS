Failure is visible

A legitimate engine has to know when to stop.

The public conformance engine can detect unsupported claims, missing attribution, missing rights context, sponsor override, explicit forbidden language, tone spikes and irreversible release attempts with open findings.

HOLD

Stop release, retain failure evidence, route to identity or rights review, and generate remediation.

WARN

Keep draft-only, mark review required, retain warning evidence, and generate remediation.

PASS

Allow the next separately authorized stage, attach imprint context, and emit conformance evidence.
