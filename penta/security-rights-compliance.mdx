# Security \+ Rights \+ Compliance Pentas

This lane protects CrownThrive's execution, assets, rights and provider relationships. These systems are designed to fail closed where required evidence, authority or compliance state is missing.

## PentaOFAC™

**Mission:** sanctions/compliance screening and fail-closed risk-control authority. **Portal:** `/io/pentas/ofac`. Maintains screening inputs, provider/source evidence, disposition, review requirements and auditable escalation without claiming legal determinations beyond its certified scope.

## PentaSecure™

**Mission:** security prevention, detection, response, remediation packaging and security-readiness governance. **Portal:** `/io/pentas/secure`. Tracks findings, severity, affected assets, control state, remediation, retesting, release impact and residual risk.

## PentaVault™

**Mission:** governed custody for secret references, proprietary implementation material, valuable assets and lineage. **Portal:** `/io/pentas/vault`. Documentation and status surfaces may expose references and health metadata, never secret material itself.

## PentaIP™

**Mission:** intellectual-property, marks, rights metadata, provenance, ownership/permission state and governance. **Portal:** `/io/pentas/ip`. Maintains CrownThrive marks, chain-of-title, licensing relationships, usage boundaries, source provenance and rights-related escalations.

## Required operating pack

Security/compliance/right systems require source provenance, exact asset/identity scope, policy/version identity, access controls, tamper-evident audit, incident handling, evidence retention, review/appeal/escalation where appropriate, provider/readback state, release impact and explicit human-reserved boundaries for decisions that cannot be delegated.
