# Continuity \+ Economics Pentas

This lane preserves CrownThrive across time while coordinating economic execution under explicit authority boundaries.

## PentaGeneration™

**Mission:** seven-generation continuity, succession, lineage, handoff logic, continuity proofs, archive/version inheritance and long-horizon stewardship. **Portal:** `/io/pentas/generation`. Ensures institutional memory and authority lineage survive personnel, provider, platform and generation changes.

## PentaGreen™

**Mission:** commerce and economic activation authority within the CHLOM metaprotocol. **Portal:** `/io/pentas/green`. Constitutional rule: **PentaGreen may optimize within authority. It may never manufacture authority.**

## PentaCredits™

**Mission:** CrownThrive credits/economic-value subsystem governed through PentaGreen and applicable CHLOM controls. **Portal:** `/io/pentas/credits`. Credits require explicit issuance, redemption, expiration, transfer and reconciliation semantics and must not be represented as external currency absent an applicable authorized framework.

## PentaCost™

**Mission:** cost, resource-consumption, abuse-prevention and economic-control visibility. **Portal:** `/io/pentas/cost`. Tracks spend, provider consumption, budget pressure, misuse patterns and action thresholds without independently authorizing payment.

## PentaPay™

**Mission:** governed compensation/payment workflow coordination subject to existing financial authority. **Portal:** `/io/pentas/pay`. Coordinates payment instructions, evidence, approvals, reconciliation and status; it does not create funds or bypass provider/account authority.

## Operating requirements

Continuity records must preserve source authority, successor/predecessor relationships, version inheritance and recovery. Economic systems require explicit financial authority, transaction evidence, reconciliation, auditability, failure handling and clear separation between internal accounting constructs and provider-settled money.
