# Media \+ Creative Pentas

These Pentas operate creative-production infrastructure while preserving the identities, rights, providers and systems of record of the media and publishing brands they support.

## PentaMedia™

**Mission:** media asset binding, operations, scheduling, routing, maintenance and media-provider health. **Portal:** `/io/pentas/media`. Covers live, linear, on-demand, syndication, HLS/RTMP, EPG, advertising markers, monetization bindings and distribution-health evidence where applicable. PentaMedia does not replace Melanated TV, MVP, Virality Music, Locticians media or other distinct media brands.

## PentaStudios™

**Mission:** studio assets, provider bindings, recording/production routing and studio operations. **Portal:** `/io/pentas/studios`. Maintains recording/production providers, sessions, destinations, handoffs, source custody, publication readiness and operational status.

## PentaBooks™

**Mission:** governed manuscript, book and edition production, packaging, QA, canon continuity, adaptation readiness and publishing workflows. **Portal:** `/io/pentas/books`. Maintains protected masters, edition/version lineage, release assets, rights metadata, quality gates and publishing evidence.

## Required operating pack

Each media/creative Penta requires an asset registry, rights/provenance record, provider bindings, production and distribution runbooks, release/version lineage, status/readback contract, monetization boundaries where relevant, incident/recovery procedures, archive/master custody and public-claims rules.
