# Penta Intelligence, Research & Impact Family

**Family ID:** `intelligence-research`  
**Portal:** `/io/pentas/families/intelligence-research`

## Story

This family helps CrownThrive understand what is happening, why it matters, what may happen next and whether institutional actions are producing intended impact. It keeps observation, analysis, hypothesis and recommendation separate from authority.

PentaSignal senses weak/internal/external signals; PentaAnalytics performs governed analysis; PentaInstitute runs research, scenario and decision-science work; PentaImpact evaluates impact, effectiveness and outcome evidence.

## Primary members

PentaSignal · PentaAnalytics · PentaInstitute · PentaImpact

## Responsibilities

- early-warning and signal sensing;
- quantitative/qualitative analytics;
- research and source synthesis;
- scenarios, foresight and red-team analysis;
- impact/effectiveness measurement;
- confidence/uncertainty and hypothesis tracking.

## Operating flow

```text
approved data/signals
→ PentaSignal/PentaAnalytics
→ research question or anomaly
→ PentaInstitute study/scenario
→ PentaImpact outcome lens
→ evidence-backed recommendation
→ accountable governing/operating family
→ actual outcome fed back into analysis
```

## Cross-family handoffs

Knowledge/Data provides governed sources. Governance/Legal receives policy/strategic analysis. Observability supplies internal telemetry. Commerce receives economic recommendations only after authority/evidence review.

## Authority boundary

A model, forecast, scenario, impact score or research conclusion is not execution permission, legal advice, rights clearance, financial authority or production certification. Confidence must remain visible and contradicted evidence must be preserved.

## Incidents and recovery

Bad source lineage, stale data, model drift or contradictory evidence degrades confidence and can force HOLD on recommendations. Research bodies are versioned; corrections preserve prior findings/effective periods.

## Releases and roadmap

The family should progressively add reproducible methods, evaluation sets, source freshness, bias/uncertainty reporting and decision-outcome feedback without converting analytics into final authority.
