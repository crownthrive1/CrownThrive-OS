# Penta Knowledge, Semantics & Data Family

**Family ID:** `knowledge-semantics-data`  
**Portal:** `/io/pentas/families/knowledge-semantics-data`

## Story

This family preserves what CrownThrive knows, what its words mean, which records are authoritative, how datasets and assets relate, and how institutional memory survives changes in vendors, people, systems and public projections.

PentaDocs maintains governed documentation and knowledge projection. PentaScribe maintains terminology, glossaries and institutional language. PentaData governs schemas, classification and data lineage. PentaRecords governs authoritative records, holds and retention. PentaNotes captures feedback and lessons. PentaSets groups governed assets/corpora. PentaMaps renders operational/system relationships. PentaStars preserves formal capability/contract semantics. PentaIP preserves intellectual-property and provenance metadata.

## Primary members

PentaDocs · PentaScribe · PentaData · PentaRecords · PentaNotes · PentaSets · PentaMaps · PentaStars · PentaIP

## Responsibilities

- institutional documentation and source lineage;
- terminology, glossary and semantic governance;
- data schemas, classification, stewardship and lineage;
- records retention/hold/disposition;
- feedback, lessons and ramifications;
- governed set/corpus identity;
- system/relationship maps;
- formal contract/capability semantics;
- IP/provenance and mark metadata.

## Operating flow

```text
source/evidence/change
→ classification + stable identity
→ Data/Records/Scribe reconciliation
→ Docs/Maps/Sets projection
→ applicable governance/rights review
→ validation/readback
→ Serialized/Generation preservation
```

## Cross-family handoffs

Governance/Legal supplies policy/rights decisions; Intelligence consumes governed data; Media/Creative consumes canon/asset lineage; Resilience preserves version/archive continuity.

## Authority boundary

A document, dataset, map, record or metadata entry can preserve truth or policy but cannot manufacture runtime deployment, legal sufficiency, ownership, rights clearance, provider authority, payment or production certification.

## Incidents and recovery

Contradictions, stale documents, broken lineage and retention violations become explicit correction/reconciliation work. Historical records are preserved and superseded rather than silently rewritten.

## Releases and roadmap

Every family/member should eventually expose machine-ingestible structured records plus audience-tailored documentation while resolving to the same stable institutional identity and source hierarchy.
