# Penta Governance, Legal & Institutional Controls Family

**Family ID:** `governance-legal`  
**Portal:** `/io/pentas/families/governance-legal`

## Story

This family converts governing authority into administrable institutional workflows without pretending software is the source of that authority. CHLOM/DAIL, adopted entity authority, binding agreements and human-reserved decisions remain upstream.

PentaBoard coordinates bounded oversight; PentaPolicy maintains adopted policy; PentaLegal manages legal-operations workflows; PentaContracts manages contract lifecycle; PentaLicense coordinates license operations; PentaEthics manages ethics review; PentaCapital manages capital-decision packets; PentaProcure manages procurement and PentaVendor manages provider/vendor governance.

## Primary members

PentaBoard · PentaPolicy · PentaLegal · PentaContracts · PentaLicense · PentaEthics · PentaCapital · PentaProcure · PentaVendor

## Responsibilities

- institutional policy and exception workflows;
- legal operations and professional escalation;
- contract lifecycle and obligations;
- licensing administration;
- ethics review and conflicts;
- capital decision packets;
- procurement/sourcing;
- vendor/provider due diligence and lifecycle.

## Operating flow

```text
institutional matter
→ authority/source identification
→ Policy/Legal/Contracts/Ethics as applicable
→ human/signatory/professional gate
→ Procure/Vendor/Capital execution packet if needed
→ certified execution path
→ Records/Audit evidence
```

## Cross-family handoffs

Security/Trust supplies compliance/privacy/security evidence. Workforce supplies accountable roles. Commerce receives only authorized economic actions. Knowledge/Data preserves contracts, decisions and policy lineage.

## Authority boundary

This family administers governance mechanics. It cannot practice law, sign for CrownThrive, certify regulatory compliance, waive obligations, commit capital, accept provider terms or manufacture a board/fiduciary/legal authority that does not exist outside the software.

## Incidents and recovery

Conflicting policy, expired authority, contract ambiguity or missing professional evidence results in HOLD/escalation. Historical decisions remain preserved through amendments/supersession rather than silent overwrite.

## Releases and roadmap

Policies, clause libraries, approval matrices and decision models are independently versioned. Automation may expand only where the underlying authority and professional-control boundary are explicit.
