# Penta Media, Studio & Publishing Family

**Family ID:** `media-creative`  
**Portal:** `/io/pentas/families/media-creative`

## Story

This family operates CrownThrive's creative-production and media-distribution estate while keeping creative existence separate from canon, rights, licensing, provider deployment and commerce authority.

PentaMedia governs channels, media assets, schedules and delivery-provider health. PentaStudios governs studio/recording/production assets and provider bindings. PentaBooks governs manuscripts, editions, illustration, QA, canon continuity, packaging and publishing workflows.

## Primary members

PentaMedia · PentaStudios · PentaBooks

## Responsibilities

- media asset/channel inventory;
- recording/studio production routing;
- schedules, playout and media-provider state;
- manuscripts, editions and publishing packages;
- canon/version lineage;
- creative QA and delivery evidence;
- rights/provider/commerce handoffs.

## Operating flow

```text
creative source/master
→ identity + canon/provenance
→ studio/media/book production
→ rights/cultural/editorial review
→ QA + package/readback
→ PentaGreen/commercial gate if applicable
→ certified distribution/provider path
→ audience/delivery evidence
→ archive/version preservation
```

## Cross-family handoffs

Knowledge/Data preserves canon and source lineage; Commerce/Economy handles authorized monetization; Security/Trust governs provider/data controls; Build/Release produces software/automation used by creative operations.

## Authority boundary

A master, manuscript, channel, cover, stream, product card or provider capability does not automatically create copyright ownership, licensing permission, canon approval, public-release authority, checkout eligibility or revenue recognition.

## Incidents and recovery

Missing masters, broken delivery, rights disputes, provider drift, edition mismatch or channel outage remain asset-specific unless evidence proves wider impact. Recovery preserves exact edition/master/provider lineage and avoids replacing protected source with a weaker derivative.

## Releases and roadmap

Every release should resolve to stable asset/content identity, contributor/provenance, rights/canon state, provider version, delivery evidence and archive/rollback. Family expansion should support media, books, studio and adaptation pipelines without collapsing them into one rights model.
