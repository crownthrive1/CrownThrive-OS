# Penta Automation & Agentic Family

**Family ID:** `automation-agentic`  
**Portal:** `/io/pentas/families/automation-agentic`

## Story

This family converts already-governed intent into typed workflows, agents, model-assisted tasks and reusable institutional capabilities. It is where CrownThrive defines what machines may do, how they are decomposed into skills/tools/scripts, how orchestration works and when human review must interrupt automation.

## Primary members

PentaOrchestrator · PentaMation · PentaFlows · PentaAgents · PentaLLM · PentaMCL · PentaBoxes · PentaSkills · PentaTools · PentaRithms · PentaScripts · PentaSuite · PentaRFA

## Responsibilities

- workflow and state-machine definitions;
- bounded agent lifecycle and delegation;
- model/provider governance context;
- skills/tools/plugin packaging;
- algorithm/script provenance;
- RFA intake and temporary agent laboratories;
- retries, compensation and escalation paths.

## Operating flow

```text
typed request
→ PentaRFA / PentaOrchestrator
→ PentaMation + PentaFlows
→ registered agent/skill/tool/script
→ routing + authority + human gate
→ bounded execution
→ independent verification
→ evidence/lessons preserved
```

## Cross-family handoffs

Routing & Interoperability supplies exact paths; Build/Release produces missing software; Security/Trust enforces identity/credential/policy boundaries; Workforce/People governs roles and accountable human handoffs.

## Authority boundary

Agents, LLMs and algorithms are not sovereign. Automation may not self-create D3 authority, self-certify consequential work, grant rights, move money, invent provider credentials or convert model confidence into institutional truth.

## Status, incidents and recovery

PentaStatus/PentaHeartbeat report runtime state. PentaTriage classifies incidents. Failed automated work must retain exact workflow version, owner, inputs, evidence and rollback/compensation path.

## Releases and roadmap

New skills/tools/agents must enter through stable identity, contract, tests, authority ceiling, portal/documentation and release evidence. Generated candidates inherit no production status from PentaFactory or the family itself.
