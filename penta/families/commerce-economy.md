# Penta Commerce & Economy Family

**Family ID:** `commerce-economy`  
**Portal:** `/io/pentas/families/commerce-economy`

## Story

This family coordinates CrownThrive's authorized economic execution. It exists to turn already-approved offers, pricing, credits, compensation and cost constraints into controlled economic operations without letting a provider, checkout screen or optimization algorithm manufacture financial authority.

PentaGreen is the Commerce & Economic Activation Authority. PentaCredits governs internal credits/value representations. PentaPay coordinates authorized payment/compensation workflows. PentaCost governs cost/resource consumption and economic-control visibility.

## Primary members

PentaGreen · PentaCredits · PentaPay · PentaCost

## Constitutional rule

> **PentaGreen may optimize within authority. It may never manufacture authority.**

## Responsibilities

- economic opportunity/activation analysis;
- offer/SKU/price readiness handoff;
- credits issuance/use/expiry controls;
- compensation/payment workflow coordination;
- cost/resource visibility and control;
- provider/economic readback and reconciliation;
- attribution to approved institutional objects.

## Operating flow

```text
approved product/service/economic object
→ CHLOM/DAIL + rights/policy authority
→ PentaGreen eligibility/optimization
→ Cost/Credits/Pay controls as applicable
→ certified provider execution path
→ settlement/entitlement/readback evidence
→ reconciliation + audit + PentaStatus
```

## Cross-family handoffs

Governance/Legal supplies authority/contract constraints; Security/Trust supplies credentials/compliance/risk controls; Communications markets only approved offers; Media/Creative supplies cleared assets/products; Intelligence may recommend but cannot execute.

## Authority boundary

Provider capability, a configured price, checkout UI, payment attempt, credit balance, optimization recommendation, product card or ECAC candidate is not enough to move money, issue rights, create entitlement, recognize revenue or bind a customer. Exact economic authority and provider readback remain required.

## Incidents and recovery

Payment/credit/cost incidents preserve previous/new balances, provider object identity, idempotency, entitlement state, refund/compensation path and audit evidence. Failed provider calls never silently create institutional economic truth.

## Releases and roadmap

Economic services should converge on stable value classes, explicit ledgers/receipts, provider-neutral institutional identities, bounded spend/value ceilings, reconciliation and reversible/compensating operations while preserving legal/tax/rights gates.
