# Penta Workforce & People Family

**Family ID:** `workforce-people`  
**Portal:** `/io/pentas/families/workforce-people`

## Story

This family governs how CrownThrive's people, agents and hybrid teams are organized, supervised, developed and handed work. It separates role membership and operational assignment from sovereign decision authority.

PentaDirectors issues bounded supervisory standards; PentaManagers manages assignments/capacity; PentaWorkforce OS is the digital-workforce environment; PentaCohorts groups workers; PentaAccelerator develops capabilities; PentaTriage routes needs/incidents; PentaHR governs lifecycle/policy; PentaBenefits coordinates benefits/entitlements; PentaAlumni preserves stewardship/community continuity; PentaHybrid handles human + AI review and escalation.

## Primary members

PentaDirectors · PentaManagers · PentaWorkforce OS · PentaCohorts · PentaAccelerator · PentaTriage · PentaHR · PentaBenefits · PentaAlumni · PentaHybrid

## Responsibilities

- role and assignment lifecycle;
- workforce capacity and supervision;
- cohort organization;
- development/readiness pathways;
- triage and escalation;
- HR policy/lifecycle records;
- benefits coordination;
- alumni/stewardship participation;
- human-in/on-the-loop decision handoffs.

## Operating flow

```text
work need / capability gap
→ PentaTriage
→ Directors/Managers role and capacity check
→ cohort/agent/human assignment
→ Hybrid review when required
→ work execution through applicable family
→ outcome/performance/lessons
→ HR/Notes/Generation preservation
```

## Cross-family handoffs

Governance/Legal defines charters/policy; Communications handles service messaging; Automation handles digital workers; Intelligence supplies evidence for development and capacity decisions.

## Authority boundary

Employment, membership, cohort placement, benefits status, management role or agent contract never creates sovereign/D3 authority. PentaHybrid records and routes valid human authority; it does not fabricate a human role, quorum or approval.

## Incidents and recovery

Role conflict, stale assignment, capacity overload, policy violations and unresolved human gates remain visible. Reassignment preserves prior owners, evidence and accountability rather than erasing lineage.

## Releases and roadmap

Workforce contracts should converge on stable identity, role/capability matrices, capacity/SLA evidence and succession paths while preserving privacy and human-reserved decisions.
