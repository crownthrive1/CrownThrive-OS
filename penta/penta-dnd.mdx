# PentaDND

PentaDND is CrownThrive COS's first-class scoped do-not-disturb and continuity capability. Its canonical current identity is `penta.dnd`. The identifier `ct.penta.dnd.v1` is retained as a historical/compatibility contract alias; it is not a second current Penta identity and does not make PentaDND a child of PentaSuper.

## Operating contract

PentaDND provides exact-scope TTL isolation, bounded lease open/renew/release behavior, continuity/topology coordination, HOT/WARM/dual-COLD redundancy readback, and virtual routing support. It is not a global maintenance mode. Unrelated work continues outside the exact DND scope.

PentaDND does not create runtime, provider, credential, financial, deployment, certification, dispatch, voting, quorum, or D3 authority. D3 remains human-reserved. The canonical Identity Fabric gate remains `pm_execution_eligible=false`; visibility and job registration do not change that execution boundary.

## Current interfaces

The current public interface includes `public.penta_dnd_status_v1`, `public.penta_dnd_preflight_v1`, and the governed hourly orchestrator. The native runtime exposes `penta_dnd.open_lease_v2`, `penta_dnd.renew_lease_v2`, `penta_dnd.close_lease_v1`, `penta_dnd.preflight_v1`, `penta_dnd.ensure_topology_v1`, `penta_dnd.activate_redundancy_v1`, `penta_dnd.append_receipt_v1`, and `penta_dnd.status_v1`.

## Safety semantics

An active DND scope permits read-only work, permits only explicitly allowed scoped mutations, rejects unrelated mutation inside that exact scope, and leaves unrelated scopes unaffected. Duplicate acquisition by the same owner is idempotent; another active owner cannot silently preempt the lease. TTL is bounded, renewal is owner-checked, terminal leases are preserved as evidence, and deletion is rejected by the DND evidence model.

## Continuity and evidence

The topology binds HOT live packet/routing state, WARM PentaWire/PentaRoute provider coordination, COLD-A immutable DAIL checkpoint custody, and COLD-B independent backup/recovery custody. DND lease and continuity events append DAIL lineage. CHLOM subjects, DID bindings and package fingerprints provide provenance without granting new authority.
