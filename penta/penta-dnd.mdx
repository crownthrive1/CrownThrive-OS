## PentaDND

PentaDND is CrownThrive COS's first-class exact-resource do-not-disturb and continuity capability. Its singular canonical current identity is `penta.dnd`. The identifier `ct.penta.dnd.v1` is preserved as a historical/compatibility predecessor and runtime-contract alias; it is not a second current Penta identity and does not make PentaDND a child of PentaSuper.

## Identity and execution boundary

| Field | Current contract |

| Canonical Family | `SYSTEM_ARCHITECTURE` / Penta System Architecture Family |

| Compatibility predecessor | `ct.penta.dnd.v1` |

Visibility, identity registration, routing discoverability, or job registration never manufactures execution authority. PentaDND does not create provider, credential, financial, deployment, certification, dispatch, voting, quorum, or D3 authority.

## Exact-scope operating contract

The canonical scope catalog is `ct.penta.dnd.scope-registry.v1`, read through `penta_dnd.scope_status_v1()`. Matching is exact by registered `scope_kind` plus exact `scope_ref`. Unknown kinds HOLD as `HOLD_DND_SCOPE_KIND_UNREGISTERED`; empty refs HOLD; D3 always HOLD. A conflict holds only the matching resource. Unrelated work continues.

Activation and continuity work uses registered kinds rather than inventing ad hoc scope strings. Important current scope contracts include:

- PentaDND runtime: `penta_runtime:penta.dnd`.
- Canonical identity, alias, or Family: `penta_identity:penta.dnd`.
- DND contracts: `penta_contract:<exact contract id>`.
- Hard-repair surgery: `hard_repair_case:<case id>`.
- Scheduler identity: `cron_job:<job>`.
- Provider/runtime integration: `provider_resource:<provider/resource>`.
- GitHub source: `github_branch:<exact branch ref>` or `pull_request:<exact PR ref>`.
- Database migration: `supabase_migration:<exact migration ref>`.
- Deployment: `deployment:<exact deployment ref>`.
- Certification: `certification_route`, `certification_handoff`, or `cos_release_certification` with the exact applicable ref.

Registered legacy aliases normalize to their governed canonical kind while preserving lineage. `system_quiescence` and `workstream` never imply a blanket global shutdown.

## Runtime interfaces

Public status and orchestration are exposed through `public.penta_dnd_status_v1`, `public.penta_dnd_preflight_v1`, and the governed hourly orchestrator. The native runtime exposes `penta_dnd.open_lease_v2`, `penta_dnd.renew_lease_v2`, `penta_dnd.close_lease_v1`, `penta_dnd.preflight_v1`, `penta_dnd.scope_status_v1`, `penta_dnd.ensure_topology_v1`, `penta_dnd.activate_redundancy_v1`, `penta_dnd.append_receipt_v1`, and `penta_dnd.status_v1`.

## Lease and isolation semantics

PentaDND provides bounded-TTL exact-resource isolation. Duplicate acquisition by the same owner is idempotent. A competing active owner cannot silently preempt a lease. Renewal is owner-checked, release is explicit, stale ownership is bounded by TTL/recovery semantics, terminal leases remain evidence, and the DND evidence model rejects deletion.

An active DND scope permits read-only work and only explicitly allowed scoped mutations inside the exact resource. It does not stop unrelated work. There is no global-maintenance requirement and no scheduler-created authority.

## Continuity and redundancy

The production topology coordinates HOT live packet/routing state, WARM PentaWire/PentaRoute provider coordination, COLD-A immutable DAIL checkpoint custody, and COLD-B independent backup/recovery custody. Virtual-node and routing support are continuity mechanisms, not new authority.

The native/internal DND recurrence remains canonical when healthy. A second external DND clock is prohibited.

## Evidence and governance

PentaDND lease, scope, topology, redundancy, and continuity events preserve append-only evidence and DAIL lineage. CHLOM subjects, DID bindings, fingerprints, packages, PentaContext, PentaCensus, Pentas nodes/cookies/routes, and PentaWire discoverability may prove identity and provenance but do not expand authority.

Independent technical and security certification belongs to PentaCertifier/PentaCertify and PentaSecurity. PentaDND must not self-certify. Source changes remain subject to exact-head PR governance, provider readback, and independent certification before merge or promotion.
