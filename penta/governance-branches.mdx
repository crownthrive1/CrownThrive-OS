# Governance \+ Institutional Branch Pentas

This lane formalizes governance, supervision, checks and balances, participation, appeals and bounded workforce direction. It remains subordinate to applicable CrownThrive/CHLOM constitutional authority and human-reserved D3 decisions.

## PentaBoard™

**Mission:** highest Penta directive/oversight body beneath applicable CrownThrive and CHLOM authority. **Portal:** `/io/pentas/board`. Issues institutional directives, resolves escalated conflicts and maintains oversight evidence.

## PentaExecutive™

**Mission:** governed executive implementation branch. **Portal:** `/io/pentas/executive`. Executes ratified directives and policies through bounded operational systems; it cannot self-legislate new authority.

## PentaLegislative™

**Mission:** policy/rule proposal, deliberation and institutional rulemaking branch within delegated scope. **Portal:** `/io/pentas/legislative`. Maintains proposal lineage, votes, quorum evidence, enactment status and supersession.

## PentaJudicial™

**Mission:** governed interpretation, dispute, appeal and remedy branch for Penta institutional decisions. **Portal:** `/io/pentas/judicial`. Preserves separation from originating decision-makers where required and maintains case/evidence/remedy lineage.

## PentaDemocracy™

**Mission:** voting, participation, representation and institutional democratic process layer. **Portal:** `/io/pentas/democracy`. Maintains eligibility, ballot/proposal identity, quorum, vote integrity, outcome evidence and escalation to human ratification where required.

## PentaDirectors™

**Mission:** supervisory policy, SOP, SLA, standards and organizational-direction layer. **Portal:** `/io/pentas/directors`. Converts higher directives into controlled operating policy for managers and systems.

## PentaManagers™

**Mission:** agent/workforce management and bounded contract/assignment issuance. **Portal:** `/io/pentas/managers`. Assigns work, tracks performance/obligations and escalates without exceeding issued authority.

## Governance chain

```text
CrownThrive / CHLOM reserved authority
        ↓
PentaBoard + institutional branches
        ↓
PentaDirectors
        ↓
PentaManagers
        ↓
Cohorts / workers / agents / execution systems
```

## Controls

Originators must not manufacture their own approvals. Material governance requires identifiable authority, quorum/ratification when applicable, evidence, conflict-of-interest handling, appeal/remedy pathways, versioned rules and auditable effective dates. Human-reserved decisions remain human-reserved.
