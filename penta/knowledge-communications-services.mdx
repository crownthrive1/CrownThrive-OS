# Knowledge \+ Communications \+ Services Pentas

This lane turns institutional truth into maintained documentation, controlled communications, service delivery and owner-visible operational state.

## PentaDocs™

**Mission:** institutional documentation, knowledge, navigation, glossary and documentation governance. **Portal:** `/io/pentas/docs`. PentaDocs is the CrownThrive system; Mintlify is the current documentation provider. PentaDocs maintains current-state, historical-context and public/private boundaries without allowing historical text to authorize present execution.

## PentaScribe™

**Mission:** maintain terms, glossaries, indexes, FAQs, dictionary, marks, nomenclature, institutional records and reconciliation lineage. **Portal:** `/io/pentas/scribe`. PentaScribe prevents vocabulary drift and preserves canonical/superseded aliases.

## PentaMarketer™

**Mission:** governed marketing of CrownThrive outputs using canonical institutional vocabulary, approved claims, rights and current product/platform truth. **Portal:** `/io/pentas/marketer`. PentaMarketer consumes PentaScribe/PentaDocs truth and cannot invent production, legal, performance or rights claims.

## PentaMail™

**Mission:** canonical email communications rail covering delivery, identities, templates, queues, retries, bounce/complaint handling and communications audit. **Portal:** `/io/pentas/mail`. PentaMail transports approved messages; it does not create underlying operational truth or authority.

## PentaStatus™

**Mission:** self-status/introspection and owner-reporting authority for Penta/system health, readiness, incidents, dependencies, data freshness, documentation freshness, resource/cost signals and action items. **Portal:** `/io/pentas/status`. PentaStatus owns the semantics and aggregation of health reports.

Default reporting classes are **immediate** for critical/security/compliance/fail-closed events; **daily** for changes, degradation, blockers and material actions/costs; **weekly** for family health/readiness; and **monthly** for lifecycle, access, documentation, dependency and release posture.

## PentaConcierge™

**Mission:** concierge service intake, triage, routing, fulfillment, escalation, follow-up and service-history orchestration. **Portal:** `/io/pentas/concierge`. PentaConcierge coordinates service work but hands material execution to the Penta/platform that actually owns the necessary authority.

## Shared operating contract

Truth originates from authoritative system records and provider readback; PentaDocs/Scribe maintain institutional representation; PentaStatus reports state; PentaMail transports communications; PentaConcierge routes requests; PentaMarketer projects approved value and narratives. Each system retains separate audit, access and lifecycle records.
