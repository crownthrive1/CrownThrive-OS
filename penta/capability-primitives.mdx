# Capability Primitive Pentas

These are reusable institutional primitives. When independently executable they inherit the full Penta identity, authority, status, audit and release contract; when embedded, their caller must preserve equivalent evidence and policy controls.

## PentaQuery™

**Mission:** governed structured query capability. **Portal family:** `/io/pentas/primitives`. Requires bounded sources, filters, access policy, provenance and query evidence.

## PentaSearch™

**Mission:** governed discovery/search capability. Requires source scope, ranking/selection transparency appropriate to use, freshness state and result provenance.

## PentaRead™

**Mission:** bounded retrieval/read capability. Read authority is source-specific and does not imply mutation rights.

## PentaList™

**Mission:** governed enumeration/listing capability. Preserves pagination, scope and completeness signals so partial lists are not represented as complete inventories.

## PentaParse™

**Mission:** structured parsing and normalization. Tracks source format, parser/version, failures, confidence/validation and lossiness.

## PentaResolve™

**Mission:** identity/reference/relationship resolution. Maintains ambiguity handling, chosen canonical identity and supporting evidence.

## PentaTransform™

**Mission:** governed transformation of data/assets between authorized forms. Requires provenance, versioning and reversible/source-retention rules appropriate to the asset.

## PentaValidate™

**Mission:** schema, policy, evidence and contract validation. Validation outputs must name the rule/version and cannot certify outside their defined scope.

## PentaCache™

**Mission:** governed cache/storage acceleration. Requires TTL/freshness, invalidation, privacy and source-of-truth rules.

## PentaSync™

**Mission:** governed state synchronization. Requires conflict strategy, directionality, checkpoints, retries, reconciliation and authoritative-source designation.

## PentaIngest™

**Mission:** governed intake/ingestion of external or internal records/assets. Requires provenance, validation, classification, rights/privacy handling and rejection/quarantine states.

## Primitive contract

Every primitive must identify caller, source/target, exact capability, policy/version, data classification, success/failure semantics, evidence, status and terminal state. A successful primitive operation proves only that bounded operation.
