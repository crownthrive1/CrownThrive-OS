# Architecture \+ Convergence Pentas

These Pentas define how CrownThrive systems fit together without collapsing their responsibilities. Each follows the universal Penta portal/documentation/status/audit contract.

## PentaOS™

**Mission:** coordinate the Penta operating-system layer. **Portal:** `/io/pentas/os`. Owns family-wide OS composition and operating boundaries; it does not replace CHLOM governance or platform systems of record.

## PentaVergence™

**Mission:** converge CrownThrive systems, lanes and execution surfaces. **Portal:** `/io/pentas/vergence`. Tracks what should interoperate, what must remain distinct, and the evidence required before convergence is treated as active.

## PentaTechture™

**Mission:** technical and architectural structure governance. **Portal:** `/io/pentas/techture`. Maintains architectural decisions, patterns, constraints, versioning and structural integrity.

## PentaPology™

**Mission:** topology, relationship, dependency and system-graph authority. **Portal:** `/io/pentas/pology`. Maintains canonical nodes, edges, dependency state and graph-level impact visibility.

## PentaPlanes™

**Mission:** define and enforce control, data, execution, governance and related plane boundaries. **Portal:** `/io/pentas/planes`.

## PentaFabric™

**Mission:** institutional/runtime fabric connecting governed Penta capabilities. **Portal:** `/io/pentas/fabric`. Carries orchestration, evidence, lifecycle and handoff contracts across bounded systems.

## PentaMesh™

**Mission:** interoperable service and execution mesh. **Portal:** `/io/pentas/mesh`. Resolves governed routes, redundancy and cross-system connectivity without bypassing authority.

## Penta Federation™

**Mission:** federation bindings, events, proofs and cross-domain state. **Portal:** `/io/pentas/federation`. Governs federation identity and state exchange across participating domains.

## PentaInterOps™

**Mission:** interoperability contracts across Penta and CrownThrive systems. **Portal:** `/io/pentas/interops`. Owns interface compatibility, handoff semantics and integration evidence.

## PentaBase™

**Mission:** canonical Penta interface to ThriveBase/runtime data services. **Portal:** `/io/pentas/base`. It references and governs runtime/data access; ThriveBase remains the underlying institutional substrate.

## PentaControl™

**Mission:** control-runtime and governed execution authority. **Portal:** `/io/pentas/control`. It executes only within already-granted authority and fails closed when required evidence is absent.

## PentaOrchestrator™

**Mission:** cross-system, dependency-aware orchestration. **Portal:** `/io/pentas/orchestrator`. Coordinates order, prerequisites, retries, handoffs and terminal states.

## PentaMation™

**Mission:** governed automation layer. **Portal:** `/io/pentas/mation`. Turns repeatable institutional work into bounded automation with status, rollback, TTL and audit requirements.

## PentaFlows™

**Mission:** workflow definitions, state machines, transitions and evidence. **Portal:** `/io/pentas/flows`. Defines allowed transitions and prevents undocumented workflow state from becoming institutional truth.

## Required operating pack

Every system above requires: architecture charter, topology/dependency inventory, access model, API/event contracts, status adapter, audit trail, incident/rollback procedure, release lineage and current-state evidence.
