# Workforce \+ Human-System Operations Pentas

This lane provides the living institutional environment around CrownThrive's human and agent workforce while preserving privacy, role boundaries and human-reserved authority.

## PentaWorkforce OS™

**Mission:** institutional digital-workforce operating environment. **Portal:** `/io/pentas/workforce`. Integrates roles, contracts, policies, assignments, lifecycle state, services, evidence, feedback and escalation for governed workers and agents.

## PentaCohorts™

**Mission:** workforce/agent cohort grouping and lifecycle. **Portal:** `/io/pentas/cohorts`. Maintains cohort purpose, membership, duration, outcomes and handoffs.

## PentaAccelerator™

**Mission:** bounded development and acceleration lane for workers, agents and capabilities. **Portal:** `/io/pentas/accelerator`. Tracks goals, curriculum/tasks, progress, evidence and graduation/exit criteria.

## PentaNotes™

**Mission:** agentic feedback, voting, ramifications, lessons and institutional feedback record. **Portal:** `/io/pentas/notes`. Captures structured observations without allowing informal notes to silently become policy.

## PentaTriage™

**Mission:** intake severity/priority classification and routing for workforce/system needs. **Portal:** `/io/pentas/triage`. Determines urgency and ownership, not final domain authority.

## PentaHealth™

**Mission:** workforce/system health and operational-wellness coordination. **Portal:** `/io/pentas/health`. Maintains operational health signals and routes appropriate support while respecting privacy and professional-boundary requirements.

## PentaHR™

**Mission:** workforce lifecycle, role, policy, personnel and institutional HR coordination. **Portal:** `/io/pentas/hr`. Maintains onboarding, role changes, policy acknowledgments, assignment/contract state and offboarding evidence.

## PentaBenefits™

**Mission:** governed workforce benefit/entitlement coordination. **Portal:** `/io/pentas/benefits`. Tracks eligibility, enrollment/entitlement state, provider dependencies and changes within approved policy.

## PentaLegal™

**Mission:** institutional legal workflow, policy review, rights/compliance escalation coordination. **Portal:** `/io/pentas/legal`. Coordinates legal records and escalation but does not represent automated output as professional legal judgment absent authorized human/provider review.

## PentaSELF™

**Mission:** bounded self-observation, self-model and readback for governed system awareness. **Portal:** `/io/pentas/self`. Supports introspection and remediation proposals without allowing a system to rewrite its own authority, governance or protected boundaries.

## Shared controls

Workforce systems require role identity, least-privilege access, privacy boundaries, retention rules, conflict/appeal routes, human oversight where required, auditability, lifecycle state and clear separation between operational coordination and licensed/professional determinations.
