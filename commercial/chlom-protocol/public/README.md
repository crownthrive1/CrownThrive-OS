# CHLOM™ — Commercial Metaprotocol, Rights Infrastructure & Integration Surface

**CHLOM — Compliance Hybrid Licensing and Ownership Model** is CrownThrive's metaprotocol for **Rights, Rules, Roles, Revenue, Records, and Remedies** across software, media, commerce, licensing, agents, organizations, creators, platforms, and other governed assets.

CHLOM is built to coordinate heterogeneous systems without pretending they are one system. It can sit above databases, websites, APIs, MCPs, agents, providers, optional chains, contracts, and human governance while preserving identity, rights, evidence, correction, and economic boundaries.

> **Repository role:** `crownthrive/chlom-protocol` is the public commercial child of the CHLOM repository family. The canonical public-safe governance parent is `crownthrive1/CrownThrive-OS`. Both repositories are public. Restricted institutional state, secrets, private contracts, protected evidence, private economic logic, and confidential implementation details belong outside public Git.

## Why CHLOM exists

Digital and hybrid ecosystems break when ownership, permission, evidence, automation, and money are treated as separate afterthoughts. CHLOM provides a common metaprotocol so systems can answer six institutional questions consistently:

1. **Rights** — what is owned, licensed, permitted, restricted, or revocable?
2. **Rules** — what policies, conditions, eligibility, risk, or approvals apply?
3. **Roles** — who or what may act, under what identity and delegated authority?
4. **Revenue** — how are authorized fees, royalties, commissions, holds, adjustments, and value routed?
5. **Records** — what evidence, versions, approvals, agreements, and events must be preserved?
6. **Remedies** — how are disputes, holds, corrections, suspensions, revocations, appeals, and restoration handled?

## CrownThrive Pentafabric™

CHLOM operates across the **CrownThrive Pentafabric™**, a five-layer convergent architecture:

1. **Identity & Imprint** — stable IDs, DIDs, Fingerprint references, provenance, custody, ownership/authorship evidence, and Cultural Imprint Engine context.
2. **Rights & Conditions** — permissions, rules, roles, DLAs, issuer authority, contracts, restrictions, approvals, fields of use, territories, revocation, and remedies.
3. **Ledger & Assurance** — DAIL, evidence, hashes, signatures, attestations, compliance oracles, audit, verification, corrections, and certification gates.
4. **Execution & Interoperability** — pallets, modules, frameworks, plugins, skills, APIs, MCPs, agents, factories, portals, dashboards, wireframes, website mesh, and provider adapters.
5. **Economy & Distribution** — ThriveEvergreen / ECAC, SKUs, access, pricing, entitlements, payments, Crown Credits, royalties, commissions, enterprise licensing, partner distribution, and reconciliation.

See [`docs/architecture/PENTAFABRIC.md`](docs/architecture/PENTAFABRIC.md).

## Canonical terminology

- **DAIL** = **Decentralized Autonomous Information Ledger**.
- **DLA** = **Dynamic Licensing Asset**.
- Historical “Decentralized Licensing Authority” responsibilities are represented by **Licensing Stewardship / Issuer Authority**.
- Historical DAL meanings remain lineage and are not the current canonical DAIL identity.

## What the public repository is for

This repository is designed to become a high-trust commercial and technical front door for CHLOM. Public-safe surfaces can include:

- architecture and research;
- public registries, schemas, contracts, and event models;
- DAIL-compatible public verification patterns;
- integration guidance and machine-access terms;
- public-safe agent, plugin, framework, pallet, module, and skill specifications;
- developer evaluation and compatibility assets;
- licensing, enterprise, research, and partner pathways;
- support, correction, contribution, and security routing.

The repository is **not** a license to commercially clone CHLOM or reconstruct protected CrownThrive implementation methods.
