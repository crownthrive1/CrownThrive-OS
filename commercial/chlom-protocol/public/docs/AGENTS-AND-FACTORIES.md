# CHLOM Agents, Oracles & Factories

CHLOM is designed for continuous governed development. Automation is decomposed into bounded agents and factories so research, documentation, identity, compliance, integration, and commercialization can progress without allowing one agent to manufacture authority or certify its own work.

## Public-safe candidate agent pack

| Agent ID | Mission | Pentafabric layer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `ct.agent.chlom-rd-researcher` | Recover, compare, test, and propose CHLOM R&D candidates | All / research |
| `ct.agent.chlom-rights-identity-steward` | Reconcile identities, DIDs, fingerprints, provenance, ownership evidence, and DLA references | 1–2 |
| `ct.agent.chlom-compliance-oracle` | Evaluate conditions, policy, evidence sufficiency, and compliance candidates | 2–3 |
| `ct.agent.chlom-docs-projection-steward` | Keep parent and child documentation aligned to current canon and public/private boundaries | 3–4 |
| `ct.agent.chlom-commercial-packager` | Prepare licensable packages, access classes, SKU candidates, and ThriveEvergreen handoffs | 4–5 |
| `ct.agent.chlom-mesh-reliability` | Observe public discovery routes, repository projection, interface health, and fallback readiness | 4 |

These are bounded candidate identities. A manifest or skill definition does not prove that a persistent production worker is currently executing under that identity.

## Core rules

Every CHLOM automation follows these rules:

1. **Source before assertion.** Recovered or new claims are tied to a source, version, or explicit founder adjudication.
2. **No authority manufacture.** Agents may prepare, analyze, reconcile, build, and recommend within contract; they may not invent ownership, rights, legal authority, certification, price, entitlement, or settlement.
3. **Builder and verifier separation.** A builder receipt is not certification. High-consequence promotion requires an independent verifier or an exact authorized human action.
4. **Public-safe projection.** Public repositories receive allowlisted material only. Restricted bodies, secrets, private evidence, private economics, and protected implementation detail stay outside public Git.
5. **Evidence-bearing outputs.** Candidate artifacts should carry stable IDs, versions, source references, hashes where appropriate, authority scope, and machine-readable state.
6. **Rollback and correction.** Automated mutation must be reversible where technically possible, and corrections must preserve lineage rather than silently rewriting history.

## Trigger classes

Agents may be invoked from:

- source or canon changes;
- repository drift;
- scheduled continuity checks;
- a new DLA, identity, fingerprint, ownership, or provenance candidate;
- new compliance conditions or external standards;
- API/MCP/plugin integration requests;
- public documentation gaps;
- licensing and packaging requests;
- mesh or provider availability changes;
- founder-directed R&D or commercialization work.

## Monetized skills and services

Public skill definitions can describe the capability and integration contract. The protected execution method, private prompts, restricted data, private scoring, private pricing, customer context, and confidential implementation can remain vaulted.

CHLOM-related professional and machine-delivered services may be packaged for rights mapping, provenance, licensing design, integration, policy controls, compliance automation, repository and registry setup, DAIL-compatible evidence architecture, API/MCP integration, training, migration, and enterprise deployment under separately governed commercial terms.
