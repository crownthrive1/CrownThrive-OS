# CrownThrive Pentafabric™

**CrownThrive Pentafabric™** is the five-layer convergent metaprotocol architecture used to explain how CrownThrive OS, CHLOM, the Cultural Imprint Engine, DAIL, agent/factory execution, and ThriveEvergreen fit together without collapsing governance, implementation, evidence, and economics into one system.

CHLOM operates across all five layers as a metaprotocol: it defines interoperable identity, rights, conditions, evidence, execution, and economic contracts that can coordinate multiple platforms, providers, chains, databases, agents, websites, and human authorities without requiring them to share one runtime.

## Layer 1 — Identity & Imprint

**Question:** Who or what is acting, and what is it?

This layer establishes subject, asset, organization, platform, agent, repository, work, version, and cultural-context identity.

Public-safe capabilities include:

- CrownThrive IDs and stable IDs;
- DIDs or equivalent decentralized identity references where implemented;
- CHLOM Fingerprint references and public verification records;
- authorship, ownership, provenance, source, version, and custody references;
- Cultural Imprint Engine context and imprint classifications;
- organization, actor, service, agent, asset, product, and repository identities.

Private derivation logic, keys, protected mappings, sensitive evidence, and restricted identity data stay outside the public repository.

## Layer 2 — Rights & Conditions

**Question:** What is allowed, required, prohibited, or licensed?

This layer is the policy and licensing plane.

It includes:

- Rights and permissions;
- Rules and eligibility;
- roles and delegated authority;
- Dynamic Licensing Assets (DLAs);
- Licensing Stewardship / Issuer Authority;
- contracts, conditions, approvals, restrictions, territories, fields of use, terms, revocation, and remedies;
- hybrid licensing models that can coordinate human agreements, software controls, registries, and optional decentralized settlement.

A DLA is an evidence-bound licensing object, not a substitute for chain of title or the underlying agreement.

## Layer 3 — Ledger & Assurance

**Question:** What happened, what evidence supports it, and can it be trusted?

This layer records and evaluates institutional state.

It includes:

- DAIL — Decentralized Autonomous Information Ledger;
- evidence references, hashes, signatures, attestations, approvals, and version history;
- audit trails, correction records, dispute state, holds, revocation, appeals, and restoration;
- compliance oracles and policy evaluators;
- independent verification, certification gates, reconciliation, and exception handling.

DAIL is the canonical information-ledger identity. Historical DAL meanings remain lineage only.

## Layer 4 — Execution & Interoperability

**Question:** How is governed intent turned into software, workflows, interfaces, and experiences?

This layer materializes the system through:

- pallets, modules, containers, components, scaffolds, wireframes, portals, dashboards, and widgets;
- frameworks, plugins, skills, adapters, and SDKs;
- APIs, MCP servers/tools/resources, webhooks, feeds, and event contracts;
- agents, sub-agents, agent contracts, factories, builders, verifiers, and schedulers;
- website mesh projection, public documentation, developer surfaces, and failover routing;
- cloud, database, optional chain, and provider adapters.

Execution never manufactures authority. Builders create candidates; independent controls determine promotion.

## Layer 5 — Economy & Distribution

**Question:** How does authorized value become a product, entitlement, transaction, license, or distribution relationship?

This layer connects CHLOM to CrownThrive commerce and growth.

It includes:

- ThriveEvergreen economic activation and ECAC decisions;
- CrownThrive SKUs and commercial packaging;
- pricing, access tiers, subscriptions, usage rights, entitlements, fulfillment, refunds, and settlement;
- developer, business, enterprise, institutional, partner, OEM, white-label, and research licensing;
- Crown Credits and approved payment/provider rails;
- partner, affiliate, ambassador, marketplace, media, and ecosystem distribution;
- revenue attribution, royalties, commissions, holds, adjustments, reconciliation, and reporting.

Economic execution is downstream of identity, rights, conditions, and evidence. Provider success cannot independently create economic truth.

## Why it is a metaprotocol

Pentafabric is not a single blockchain, database, API, contract format, or application. It is a coordination architecture that lets heterogeneous systems participate under common identity, rights, evidence, execution, and economic contracts.

That is the operating distinction:

- CrownThrive OS coordinates the ecosystem;
- CIE provides cultural and imprint context;
- CHLOM provides the metaprotocol for ownership, licensing, compliance, evidence, and remedies;
- DAIL preserves governed information state;
- factories and interfaces materialize bounded capabilities;
- ThriveEvergreen activates economic state only after authority exists.
