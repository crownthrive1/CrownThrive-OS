# CHLOM Licensing & Access Pathways

CHLOM is commercialized through explicit access and licensing pathways rather than by treating a public GitHub repository as a blanket license.

## Public / Community

Designed for public understanding, issue reporting, registry correction, public-safe research orientation, compatibility discovery, and evaluation of published documentation.

Public access does not include production commercial implementation, white-label rights, certification rights, private data access, protected implementation logic, or permission to train commercial models on the protected corpus.

## Developer / Integration

Designed for developers evaluating schemas, event models, public contracts, SDK/interface patterns, API/MCP compatibility, and sandbox integration.

Production machine access may require credentials, a developer agreement, usage terms, rate/cost controls, environment restrictions, and a commercial plan.

## Business / Commercial

Designed for organizations using CHLOM-controlled capabilities in paid products, operations, customer workflows, licensing programs, compliance systems, rights workflows, or commercial services.

Terms may cover seats, environments, call volume, permitted products, field of use, data, support, branding, audit, economics, and distribution.

## Enterprise / Institutional

Designed for larger organizations, multi-brand systems, regulated or high-assurance environments, universities, institutions, enterprise software, federated deployments, and custom governance integrations.

Terms may include dedicated integration, private deployment, SSO/RBAC, audit exports, custom policy packs, DAIL-compatible evidence architecture, service levels, migration, training, and negotiated rights.

## Ecosystem / Partner

Designed for CrownThrive ecosystem interoperability, approved partners, resellers, implementation partners, embedded/OEM arrangements, white-label deployments, channel distribution, and strategic integrations when expressly authorized.

A partner relationship does not automatically include sublicensing, trademark, certification, data, model, or redistribution rights.

## Research / Education

Research and educational use can be separately licensed when use extends beyond ordinary lawful public reference. Agreements can define corpus, methods, publication, attribution, confidentiality, model/data use, derivative outputs, and commercialization boundaries.

## Service packages

CHLOM can support paid professional or managed services around:

- rights and ownership mapping;
- licensing architecture and DLA design;
- identity, DID, fingerprint, and provenance architecture;
- DAIL-compatible evidence and audit systems;
- compliance condition modeling and oracle integration;
- API, MCP, SDK, webhook, and agent integration;
- registry, schema, and contract deployment;
- repository, documentation, and public/private boundary design;
- migration, implementation, training, and enterprise enablement.

## Economics

Prices are resolved from the separately governed CrownThrive commercial catalog and ThriveEvergreen economic authority. This repository does not freeze or invent current pricing.

Historical or experimental prices found in old pages, papers, screenshots, source files, or prior repository versions are not current merely because they remain discoverable.
