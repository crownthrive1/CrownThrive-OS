## CrownThrive OS current operational state

**Effective snapshot:** August 27, 2026<br>
**Institutional phase:** Phase 3 — Execute<br>
**OS release:** CrownThrive OS `3.13.0.1` on the `3.x` compatibility line; provider publication verified `2026-08-26T23:46:15Z`<br>
**Release hold:** generated `v3.14.0.0` is an unpublished candidate and does not supersede `v3.13.0.1`<br>
**Operating posture:** Operational by subsystem and evidence scope; no blanket production promotion

CrownThrive is operating in Phase 3. The institution is converting governed knowledge, authority, workflows, provider integrations, factories, documentation, commerce controls, and continuity mechanisms into durable software. The active institutional lifecycle is **Discover → Govern → Execute → Verify → Preserve**.

Phase 3 is an execution generation, not a universal certification. A component is operational only within the exact scope supported by its current state and evidence. `HOLD`, `CONTROLLED_TEST`, `BUILT_UNDEPLOYED`, `CANDIDATE`, `HISTORICAL`, and `SUPERSEDED` remain meaningful fail-closed states.

## Current authority chain

1. CrownThrive OS canonical records establish institution-wide current state.
2. CHLOM governs rights, rules, roles, authority, evidence, and remedies within its scope.
3. CIE supplies cultural and contextual constraints where applicable.
4. ThriveBase and other OS-bound records preserve durable operational state.
5. Factories, workflows, agents, and adapters may execute only within explicit authority.
6. Provider mutations require bounded certification and readback.
7. DAIL, release, attestation, rollback, and reconciliation evidence preserve what happened.
8. PentaDocs, websites, storefronts, and other public surfaces are downstream projections and may not override the OS.

## Hard boundaries still in force

- Provider capability does not create provider-wide write authority.
- A successful workflow does not prove live deployment or business effect without readback.
- Self-hosted runner production use remains held until separate exact-head runner attestation is verified and the runtime policy is deliberately promoted.
- D3 and sovereign human authority remain human-reserved.
- Phase 3 does not silently rename independent v1/v2 component contracts.
- Historical Phase 2.x, ten-phase, and twenty-phase roadmap records remain evidence, not current operating instruction.
