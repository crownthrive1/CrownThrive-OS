# Operating principles

These rules apply across CrownThrive platforms, products, publishing, commerce, media, automation, support, and partnerships.

## Source of truth before presentation

Every public number, product status, character identity, price, file, edition, licensing claim, call to action, and dashboard state must come from a governed record.

## No pretend availability

Do not call a product available because a card exists, a filename exists, or a checkout button can be rendered.

A product may be marked available only when its required source, rights, price, checkout, fulfillment, entitlement, support, and policy states are complete and tested.

## Preserve useful history

New editions do not silently erase older editions. Existing work may be classified as current, legacy, superseded, collector, archive, development, or rights-only.

Corrections append to the record. They do not overwrite history without a change log.

## Rights are explicit

A public page, stream, preview, download, performance, or purchase does not grant adaptation, character, merchandise, voice, music, training, source-file, broadcast, or endorsement rights unless an approved agreement expressly says so.

## Culture and community are infrastructure

Cultural specificity is not decoration. The Cultural Imprint Engine governs meaning, representation, continuity, context, and community alignment.

## Privacy is not erasure

Protected people, children, descendants, customers, creators, and partners may be acknowledged without surrendering private records, likenesses, identities, or biographies.

## Evidence before adjectives

CrownThrive separates:

- fact
- meaning
- permission
- protection
- verified public evidence
- first-party measurements
- governed internal inventory
- development targets
- projections

Do not let a compelling description replace a supportable claim.

## Automation remains accountable

Agents may research, classify, draft, test, reconcile, and prepare changes within their permissions. They may not bypass approval gates for money, rights, publishing, security, protected identities, or irreversible actions.

## Failure must remain visible

Errors, failed payments, broken routes, rejected releases, revoked entitlements, rights holds, and incomplete work must receive explicit states. Do not conceal a failure behind a success message.

## Done means verifiable

A task is complete only when:

- the governed output exists
- required tests pass
- ownership and status are recorded
- affected systems are reconciled
- approvals are captured
- public claims match reality
- rollback or correction paths exist

<Warning>
  A convincing interface is not evidence that the underlying system works. CrownThrive measures completion at the record, transaction, entitlement, rights, and operational levels.
</Warning>
