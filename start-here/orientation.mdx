# New here: what this site is

This site is dense on purpose. If you landed here from a search result or a link and are wondering what you're looking at, start on this page. It answers the questions first-time visitors ask most, in plain language, and then points you to the deeper pages.

## What is this site?

**CrownThrive Operating System (CrownThrive OS)** is the public-safe documentation of how the CrownThrive ecosystem is organized and governed. It covers:

- what each CrownThrive platform is responsible for;
- the doctrine, policies, and operating rules that apply across platforms;
- the licensing, rights, and commerce model (CHLOM);
- the developer surface (APIs, MCP, adapters, events);
- the institutional knowledge base — recovered history, decisions, source records, and reconciliation registers.

You are reading a **projection** of the institutional record. The record itself lives in a governed Git repository. Mintlify renders and searches it. Off-provider exports back it up. See [Help Center Architecture](/support/help-center-architecture) for how those pieces fit together.

## Why does it exist?

Two reasons drive the scope and the strictness:

1. **A prior Help Center was lost.** Institutional knowledge was erased. This system exists so that a third-party outage, an account closure, or a vendor change cannot delete CrownThrive's memory again.
2. **One governed source of truth.** Platforms, teams, partners, and AI agents need a single place to look up what is real, what is approved, what changed, and what is still unresolved. Without that, drift and contradictions compound.

## Why is it so complex?

The complexity is not decoration. It reflects three things at once:

- **Multiple audiences share one source.** Founders, operators, developers, rights/licensing reviewers, growth teams, and automation agents all read from the same governed record. See [Enter by role](/index#enter-by-role-not-by-filesystem).
- **Public and private are separated.** Restricted evidence (contracts, credentials, customer data, unreleased masters) is deliberately not on this site. See the [Public/Private Boundary](/ecosystem/public-private-boundary).
- **Sources have different authority.** A verified production record does not carry the same weight as a historical plan or a chat suggestion. See the [Institutional Source Authority Hierarchy](/knowledge/source-authority-hierarchy).

## When was this built?

CrownThrive OS is a working reconstruction of institutional knowledge, not a document written in a single day. It is authored and reconciled continuously through a phased program.

- **August 17, 2026** — the Founder resolved a large adjudication queue; those decisions became canonical instructions unless later superseded. See the [Adjudication Queue](/knowledge/adjudication-queue) and [Founder Adjudications — 2026-08-17](/knowledge/founder-adjudications-2026-08-17).
- **Phase 2.99 — Hard Exit** is the current program state. Phase 3 entry is deliberately blocked until the full hard-exit criteria pass. See [Phase 2.99 Plan](/changelog/phase-2-99-plan) and the [Phase 3 Readiness Gate](/technology/phase-3-readiness-gate).
- **Ten-phase roadmap** — the current planning horizon runs through Phase 10, with reserved definitions through Phase 20. See [Phase and Maturity Namespaces](/doctrine/phase-and-maturity-namespaces).

Individual pages carry their own version notes when the underlying record has changed.

## How was it built?

- **Mintlify** renders the site and provides search.
- **Git** (this repository) is the versioned source of every page, registry, and decision record.
- **Off-provider exports** preserve the record independently of any single vendor.
- **AI agents** assist with retrieval, gap detection, articleization, and drafting, under [bounded authority](/automation/agentic-operating-model) — they do not bypass approval gates.
- **Governed pull requests** are the only path by which content changes. Direct edits to the deploy branch are not permitted.

## Where does the content come from?

Content is reconciled from several source classes, each with a defined authority:

- founder-approved decisions and adopted governance;
- verified production evidence from the systems of record;
- current adopted contracts, policies, and licenses;
- current CrownThrive public statements;
- historical plans, prospectuses, Help Center recoveries, and research;
- conversation history and model memory, used only as retrieval aids.

The precedence rules are in the [Institutional Source Authority Hierarchy](/knowledge/source-authority-hierarchy). Nothing is silently promoted from a lower class to a higher one.

## What is "institutional knowledge"?

In CrownThrive OS, **institutional knowledge** is the accumulated, versioned body of decisions, policies, plans, evidence, and definitions that belong to the institution rather than to any one person, chat log, or vendor account. It has four traits:

- **Governed** — every material change goes through an approval gate.
- **Sourced** — every claim points back to an evidence class.
- **Versioned** — corrections append; history is preserved rather than overwritten.
- **Recoverable** — a provider outage cannot erase it.

For the full definition and knowledge layers (public, gated, restricted, machine-readable), see [Help Center Architecture](/support/help-center-architecture).

## What is the point?

CrownThrive OS is the **single governed source of truth** for the CrownThrive ecosystem. Its point is that the entire organization — platforms, teams, partners, and AI agents — always has one reliable, versioned, auditable place to look up what is real, what is approved, and what happened.
