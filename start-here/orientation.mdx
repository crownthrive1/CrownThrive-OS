CrownThrive OS is a production institutional operating environment for the CrownThrive ecosystem. **PentaDocs** is its governed documentation and institutional-knowledge system. This page gives first-time readers the shortest path into the architecture without requiring them to understand every subsystem first.

<Info>
  **PentaDocs and Mintlify are not the same thing.** PentaDocs is CrownThrive's governed documentation and institutional-knowledge system. Mintlify is the current third-party editor, renderer, search, navigation, and hosting provider that displays approved PentaDocs content. A provider feature, preview, or successful render does not by itself create CrownThrive authority or change the institutional record.
</Info>

## What is this site?

PentaDocs is the public-safe projection of CrownThrive's institutional record. It explains:

- what CrownThrive platforms, frameworks, brands, and systems do;
- how CHLOM governs rights, licensing, evidence, and authority;
- how the Cultural Imprint Engine, Thrive Flywheel, Convergent Ecosystem, Hybrid Incubator, and related frameworks connect;
- how Penta systems coordinate software, providers, governance, media, documentation, continuity, and operations;
- how APIs, MCP surfaces, adapters, jobs, agents, evidence, releases, and production changes are governed;
- what is current production truth, what is historical context, and what remains gated or unresolved.

Mintlify is the current rendering/search/hosting provider for PentaDocs. It is not the institutional system itself. See [Help Center Architecture](/support/help-center-architecture).

## How to read current-state language

PentaDocs separates institutional truth by evidence and authority so readers do not mistake a plan, artifact, or provider feature for a completed production capability.

| Label | What it means | What it does not mean |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Current** | Supported by the controlling adopted record or current evidence identified for that claim. | Every connected provider or downstream capability is automatically certified. |
| **Production baseline** | The institutional operating posture is active within its stated scope. | Every payment, rights, credential, publication, or destructive action is authorized. |
| **Certified** | A named capability or path passed its defined tests and evidence requirements. | Unrelated providers, credentials, versions, or execution paths also passed. |
| **Candidate or planned** | The item is proposed, designed, queued, or being evaluated. | The capability exists in production. |
| **Historical or superseded** | The item is retained for lineage, recovery, or context. | It independently controls the current system. |

<Warning>
  Treat provider availability, a configured credential, generated software, a green preview, or an old roadmap as evidence about that specific fact only. Material provider writes, money movement, rights grants, legal actions, publication, and destructive operations still require their own current authority and readback.
</Warning>

## Why does it exist?

CrownThrive spans multiple industries, platforms, providers, content systems, rights structures, automation layers, and communities. A durable institutional record prevents those pieces from drifting into contradictory versions of the truth.

The system also exists because provider loss must never equal institutional-memory loss. Git history, governed evidence, registries, and off-provider continuity preserve the record independently of the rendering vendor.

## Why is it dense?

The same institutional record must support several audiences at once: founders and operators, developers, media and commerce teams, rights/licensing reviewers, partners, community-facing teams, and bounded AI agents.

The density also reflects deliberate separation between:

- public knowledge and restricted evidence;
- historical context and current authority;
- software existence and provider certification;
- technical PASS evidence and governance authority;
- plans, candidates, production state, and archived lineage.

See the [Institutional Source Authority Hierarchy](/knowledge/source-authority-hierarchy) and [Public/Private Boundary](/ecosystem/public-private-boundary).

## What is the current operating state?

As of **August 26, 2026, CrownThrive is operating in Phase 3 production and convergence**. Earlier Phase 2.x and Phase 2.99 materials remain important historical and lineage records, but they do not override current production truth.

The production hierarchy includes CHLOM governance and the Penta control planes. PentaFabric orchestrates institutional execution, PentaSELF provides bounded self-awareness and self-healing, and PentaMesh carries governed interoperability and routing. Provider execution remains evidence-based and fail-closed: PentaBuild can build what is missing, PentaCertify must prove provider capability, PentaCredentials governs credential readiness, and PentaNurture maintains the runtime.

Phase 3 production does **not** mean every provider action is automatically authorized. D3 remains human-reserved, and unresolved rights, credentials, provider canaries, money movement, or other authority gates remain explicit.

## How is it built and maintained?

- **PentaDocs** owns governed institutional documentation and approved meaning.
- **Git** provides version history, correction lineage, and source control.
- **Mintlify** renders and searches the current approved documentation surface.
- **ThriveBase** and connected production systems provide runtime evidence.
- **PentaSELF and related Penta systems** detect drift, reconcile bounded state, and route software/remediation work.
- **Governed pull requests and production receipts** preserve material changes instead of silently overwriting history.
- **Off-provider continuity** protects institutional memory from vendor failure.

## Where does the content come from?

Content is reconciled from founder directives and adopted governance, verified production evidence, current contracts and policies, public CrownThrive statements, versioned repository records, recovered historical material, research, and conversation history used as contextual retrieval evidence.

Those sources do not have equal authority. Historical or conversational material can inform recovery and lineage but cannot independently manufacture current production authority.

## What is institutional knowledge?

In CrownThrive OS, **institutional knowledge** is the governed, sourced, versioned, and recoverable body of decisions, definitions, policies, evidence, architecture, operating rules, and history that belongs to the institution rather than to one person, one chat, or one provider account.

It is:

- **Governed** — material changes have explicit authority and review boundaries.
- **Sourced** — claims retain evidence and provenance.
- **Versioned** — correction and supersession preserve lineage instead of erasing history.
- **Recoverable** — provider loss cannot erase institutional memory.
- **Executable where appropriate** — machine-readable registries and contracts can drive bounded software behavior without turning documentation into unrestricted authority.

## What is the point?

The goal is one coherent institutional operating truth across CrownThrive's ecosystem: software can act on it, people can audit it, communities and partners can understand the public-safe portions, and future generations can inherit the history without reconstructing the institution from scattered accounts.
