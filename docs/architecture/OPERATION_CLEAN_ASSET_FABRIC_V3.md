# Operation C.L.E.A.N. v3 — Asset Governance & Commercialization Fabric

**Canonical program ID:** `ct.operation.clean.asset-fabric.v3`  
**Owner:** CrownThrive, LLC  
**Status:** institutionalization candidate  
**Effective:** 2026-08-31

## Purpose

Operation C.L.E.A.N. is CrownThrive’s cross-asset governance and commercialization program. It expands the original Virality Music catalog-cleanup doctrine into a reusable lifecycle for CrownThrive-created, acquired, purchased, licensed, imported, rebuilt, published, distributed, and customer-authorized assets.

The existing audio-protection program remains a separate bounded sublane and is not replaced by this contract.

C.L.E.A.N. is **not** a deletion engine and does not manufacture rights, canon, publication status, commercial authority, or production evidence.

## Framework

### C — Capture & Custody

Discover and preserve material source assets before cleanup or commercialization. Register stable identity, source/provider reference, custody, version, origin/acquisition basis, recovery location, and fingerprints where applicable.

Deletion authority is `NONE` by default. Exact duplicates remain evidence-bearing source objects until the governed retention/removal contract explicitly permits destructive action.

### L — Label & Link

Assign canonical identity and connect each asset to its brand/corridor, universe, work/composition family, aliases, versions, creators/contributors, rights/provenance records, derivatives, products, providers, dependencies, and evidence lineage.

Titles may change. Canonical identity must not.

### E — Evaluate & Evidence

Evaluate the asset for provenance, chain of title, canon/continuity, CIE alignment, CHLOM authority/rights/consent/provenance, technical quality, accessibility, security/privacy, audience evidence, creative value, licensing utility, merchandising potential, distribution fitness, commercial readiness, and rollback/recovery.

Provider presence, payment, a fingerprint/DID, a Drive file, or a database row is evidence; none alone proves ownership or licensing/release authority.

### A — Assign & Activate

Assign one primary lifecycle disposition and zero or more governed secondary commercial routes.

Canonical primary dispositions include:

- `ACTIVE_RELEASE`
- `LICENSING_CANDIDATE`
- `DIRECT_COMMERCE`
- `GO_FLIPBOOKS`
- `BACKROAD_PROGRAMMING`
- `SOUNDCLOUD_TEST`
- `MERCH_CANDIDATE`
- `ADLUXE_CAMPAIGN`
- `EDUCATION_CANDIDATE`
- `REPAIR`
- `DEVELOPMENT`
- `VAULT`
- `ARCHIVE`
- `RIGHTS_HOLD`
- `CANON_HOLD`
- `SECURITY_HOLD`
- `REMOVAL_QUARANTINE`

Activation is route-specific. One work may be digitally sale-ready while print, licensing, public adaptation, or merchandise remains held.

### N — Normalize, Nurture & Monetize

Normalize metadata, artwork, previews, accessibility, fulfillment packages, pricing basis, license classes, manifests, analytics identity, internal links, campaigns, and derivative-rights records. After release, route approved assets through CrownLytics/CrownPulse measurement and the applicable Thrive Flywheel distribution/retention corridors.

## Asset classes

The program may govern music masters/stems/renders; books/manuscripts/PDF/EPUB; plays/poems/scripts; artwork/portraits/photos/video; merchandise designs; software/source ZIPs/purchased scripts; websites/domains; datasets; contracts/licenses; product files; advertising inventory; service templates; campaigns; analytics evidence; and institutional documentation.

## Source precedence

1. Exact current runtime/provider/production readback.
2. CrownThrive OS / ThriveBase / CHLOM / CIE governed state and DAIL evidence.
3. Canonical Drive registries and approved source masters.
4. Approved books, universe bibles, canon sheets, release manifests, and change logs.
5. Exact GitHub source and governed PR state.
6. Public-safe PentaDocs projection.
7. Conversation/project notes as discovery evidence until reconciled into durable custody.

Corrections append/supersede; they do not silently overwrite history.

## Governance routing

```text
intake/discovery
→ custody + canonical asset identity
→ master asset registry + CLEAN execution queue
→ CHLOM identity/rights/consent/provenance
→ CIE canon/cultural review when applicable
→ PentaSecurity when security/privacy relevant

→ provider/runtime readback
→ DAIL institutional evidence
→ PentaDocs public-safe projection

```

The originator may not independently certify its own D2 release candidate.

## Commercial routes

Current architecture must not depend on Melanated Voices. Historical documentation that names Melanated Voices as a licensing/commerce dependency is preserved as history and must be marked superseded where current architecture differs.

- Official music distribution: deliberately selected release rail (currently DistroKid where applicable).
- Rights/licensing: CHLOM-governed Good Shit Only (GSO) path.
- First-party direct commerce: approved CrownThrive/Virality commerce path.
- Hosted reader/publishing: Go Flipbooks.
- Discovery/testing: SoundCloud and Backroad FM where applicable.
- Promotion: AdLuxe only after product/release eligibility.

Payment never substitutes for a rights grant.

## Canon controls

Canon/identity drift is an operational defect. The program must preserve and surface unresolved variants rather than collapse them for convenience.

Current public-safe doctrines include:

- Fact / Meaning / Permission / Protection.
- Sequence before opinion.
- Context before extraction.
- Consent before expansion.
- Name before nickname.
- Attribution before interpretation.
- Correction without erasure.

Public Willie/Maybelle family identity remains **Jenkins**; Carter/Jenkens/Jenkins ancestral spelling variants remain governed genealogy/evidence fields and do not authorize public renaming.

Protected identities remain protected. Missing identity fields are not permission to invent a name, face, biography, relationship, branch, or chronology.

Every HOLD must identify the smallest missing predicate, current owner, evidence required, and next executable action.

## Autonomous authority boundaries

D0/D1 automation may discover, fingerprint, classify duplicate candidates, normalize metadata, update governed registries/queues, prepare manifests, perform non-destructive organization with readback, detect canon/version drift, run tests, draft public-safe docs, prepare product/release candidates, and reconcile analytics.

D2 actions follow current independent security/governance/certification requirements before governed release.

Human/D3 or separately reserved authority remains required for material money movement, credential creation/rotation, final legal conclusions, final rights/license grants, exclusivity, binding contracts, destructive deletion, sovereign authority changes, and other current human-reserved actions.

## Definition of CLEAN

An asset is `CLEAN` only when its required identity, custody, provenance, version/canon state, rights state, technical state, commercial disposition, evidence references, and next lifecycle state are explicit and read back from the relevant canonical systems.

`CLEAN` does **not** automatically mean public, purchasable, licensable, distributed, or deletable. Those remain separate governed states.

## Operating doctrine

> Capture before cleanup.  
> Identity before interpretation.  
> Canon before expansion.  
> Rights before licensing.  
> Evidence before claims.  
> Package before promotion.  
> Readback before PASS.  
> Correction without erasure.  
> Create broadly; commercialize narrowly.  
> Preserve everything valuable; activate only what earns activation.
