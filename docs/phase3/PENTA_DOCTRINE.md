# PENTA Doctrine — Discover → Govern → Execute → Verify → Preserve

**PENTA** is CrownThrive's five-stage institutional execution doctrine:

> **Discover → Govern → Execute → Verify → Preserve**

The name is derived from Greek *pénte* — **five** — and is used deliberately as the common operating grammar for CrownThrive's PENTA systems.

PENTA is not merely a prefix. A PENTA participant must be understandable by people, addressable by software, governed by CHLOM, observable through ThriveBase, verifiable through evidence/readback, and preservable for continuity.

## Five-stage contract

1. **Discover** — identify providers, assets, routes, endpoints, dependencies, contracts, capabilities, versions, state, and relevant institutional context. Discovery does not create authority.
2. **Govern** — resolve identity, rights, credentials, risk class, capability, certification requirements, and policy through CHLOM and related governance controls.
3. **Execute** — perform the authorized operation through PentaRoute, PentaTun, a provider-native adapter, queue, workflow, factory, runtime, or other registered execution surface.
4. **Verify** — prove the intended result through read-after-write, receipts, tests, hashes, canaries, heartbeats, reconciliation, signatures, or provider readback.
5. **Preserve** — retain versions, evidence, receipts, bindings, provenance, history, continuity context, and enough information for future participants to continue the work.

## Institutional relationship

- **CrownThrive** is the institutional/ecosystem umbrella.
- **CIE** governs cultural meaning, narrative continuity, representation, imprint identity, aesthetics, audience/canon constraints, and responsible reuse.
- **CHLOM** governs authority, rights, capabilities, policy, evidence, vault custody, certification, and remedies.
- **ThriveBase** is the primary execution/state/evidence control plane.
- **Phase 3** is the current convergence era of autonomous discovery, bounded-write certification, provider-adapter completion, runtime proof, and institutional self-operation.
- **PentaRoute** is the governed routing/execution family.
- **Penta Federation** binds PENTA participants across systems and repositories.
- **PentaDocs** is the human + machine institutional knowledge layer.

## Extension rule

PENTA is intentionally extensible. A future PENTA participant may be added when it expresses a genuinely distinct institutional responsibility. It must document what it discovers, what governs it, what it executes, how it verifies the result, what it preserves, its dependencies, risk ceiling, and authority boundary.

The canonical rule is:

> **If CrownThrive cannot discover it, govern it, execute it, verify it, and preserve it, the institutional loop is incomplete.**
