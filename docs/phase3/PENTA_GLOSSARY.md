# PENTA Institutional Glossary

Canonical terminology for the Phase 3 PENTA architecture. The machine-readable system registry is `data/penta/systems.registry.json`; this file is the human-oriented institutional companion.

## Core doctrine

- **PENTA** — Discover → Govern → Execute → Verify → Preserve.
- **Discover** — establish evidence about providers, assets, endpoints, contracts, dependencies, versions, state, capabilities, and participants without inventing authority.
- **Govern** — resolve policy, capability, identity, rights, credential, risk, certification, and approval requirements.
- **Execute** — perform an authorized operation through a registered runtime, provider adapter, queue, workflow, compiler, or factory.
- **Verify** — prove the result with readback, receipts, hashes, canaries, tests, heartbeats, signatures, or reconciliation.
- **Preserve** — retain evidence, versions, bindings, provenance, history, continuity context, and handoff state.

## Institutional PENTA systems

- **Penta Control** — consolidated portfolio-level PENTA command, coordination, intent decomposition, and convergence surface.
- **Penta MCP** — machine/tool interoperability and capability invocation surface.
- **PentaDocs** — CrownThrive institutional documentation, evidence projection, knowledge, versioning, and continuity system. Mintlify may be a provider/implementation surface; it is not the product name.
- **PentaRoute** — governed exact-routing/execution family across provider adapters, runtimes, queues, tools, and bounded capabilities.
- **PentaTime** — canonical temporal-governance layer for schedules, clocks, recurrence, windows, expiry, scheduler topology, and time semantics.
- **PentaMation** — canonical automation/orchestration layer for events, schedules, jobs, queues, dependencies, retries, compensation, and workflow convergence. PentaMation automates existing authority; it does not create authority. It delegates time semantics to PentaTime, routing to PentaRoute, and consequential human gates to PentaHybrid.
- **PentaHybrid** — human + AI integration layer for human-in/on-the-loop controls, handoff, review, approval, override, escalation, quorum, separation of duties, and accountable decision lineage.
- **PentaAlumni** — machine-addressable ThriveAlumni human stewardship/governance layer for councils, committees, appointments, mentoring, succession participation, institutional memory, and bounded human review. Membership alone creates no binding authority.
- **PentaInstitute** — CrownThrive institutional think tank and decision-science layer for research, strategic studies, scenario planning, foresight, policy analysis, red teaming, evidence synthesis, and recommendations. It may use major think-tank operating patterns but has no implied affiliation with RAND Corporation or any other external institution.
- **PentaSignal** — strategic sensing, weak-signal detection, anomaly/early-warning, watch, and hypothesis layer. Signals remain observations until corroborated.
- **PentaAssure** — independent assurance/certification layer aggregating evidence sufficiency, tests, audits, policy conformance, red-team checks, and release/capability readiness.
- **PentaFederation** — canonical name for the cross-system, cross-repository, and cross-provider binding/interoperability layer. **Penta Federation** remains a recognized historical/display alias.
- **PentaFactory** — canonical name for the governed software/framework production system: specification, generation, compilation, testing, packaging, release, and provider-native deployment. **PentaFramework Factory** is retained as a superseded alias for the same system.
- **PentaMedia** — governed media operations, asset bindings, maintenance, receipts, and continuity.
- **PentaBooks** — governed book/manuscript/edition/QA/publication/adaptation lifecycle.
- **PentaGeneration** — seven-generation continuity, horizons, handoff, proof, succession, archive/version inheritance, and institutional memory.
- **PentaStudios** — production/studio assets, providers, routes, control surfaces, livestream/recording operations, and MCP interoperability.

## Relationship to non-PENTA institutional owners

PENTA naming does not erase CrownThrive's existing institutional ownership boundaries:

- **CIE** owns cultural meaning, canon, representation, narrative continuity, cultural imprint and responsible reuse.
- **CHLOM** owns authority, rights, policy, permissions/capabilities, evidence obligations, certification requirements and remedies.
- **DAIL** owns durable evidence/audit lineage.
- **ThriveBase** is a primary operational state/evidence control plane where bound.
- **CrownLytics / CrownPulse** remain analytics/intelligence/measurement systems; PentaSignal consumes and interprets signals rather than replacing them.
- **CII / ThriveFund** retain impact/reinvestment responsibilities; no redundant PentaImpact is created by default.
- **CrownThrive Holdings** retains portfolio stewardship/capital-allocation responsibilities; no redundant PentaCapital is created by default.
- **OpsOasis / Penta Control** retain operations/control responsibilities; no decorative PentaOps layer is needed merely to expand the namespace.

## Governance and evidence vocabulary

- **Bounded-write certification** — exact-operation write certification instead of provider-wide write authority.
- **Read-after-write** — independently read a resource after mutation to prove the intended state.
- **Canary** — synthetic bounded test designed for minimal blast radius and strong evidence.
- **Receipt** — machine-readable execution evidence containing identity, time, target, version, hash, status, and result.
- **Evidence hash** — cryptographic digest used to detect drift and support reproducibility.
- **Fail-closed** — refuse execution when required proof, authority, binding, credential, quorum, or human gate is absent.
- **Human gate** — a PentaHybrid requirement that prevents consequential automation from continuing until the specified accountable human review/approval/quorum exists.
- **Institutional dependency** — a relationship between systems needed to fulfill responsibility. It is not automatically an executable workflow DAG.
- **Hardwired** — persistent registered binding with health monitoring, vault-bound credentials where applicable, explicit capability contracts, reconnect/retry behavior, and evidence. It does not mean bypassing provider controls.
- **Institutionalize** — convert a concept or asset into a named, registered, governed, documented, versioned, observable, and continuity-aware capability.
- **Canonical** — current authoritative identity/definition/source.
- **Current truth** — best verified representation of present production state, distinguished from plans, targets, drafts, and history.
- **Historical version** — preserved superseded state retained for provenance, auditability, continuity, or reproducibility.
- **Machine-readable** — structured so software can resolve identity, role, state, version, capabilities, dependencies, evidence, and policy.
- **Human-readable** — explained sufficiently for operators, developers, partners, auditors, researchers, councils, and future stewards.

## Extension rule

A future PENTA name should represent a distinct institutional responsibility, fit the five-stage doctrine, carry a machine key and human name, declare authority/risk boundaries, document dependencies, and bind into the appropriate PentaRoute/Phase 3/CHLOM/ThriveBase/PentaFederation corridor. Duplicate decorative names should not be created merely to expand the catalog.

Before a new top-level system is admitted, ask three questions:

1. Does an existing CrownThrive/PENTA system already own the responsibility?
2. Does the proposed system have a distinct machine contract, authority boundary, verification method, and preservation target?
3. Would removing the new name create a real architectural gap rather than just a branding gap?
