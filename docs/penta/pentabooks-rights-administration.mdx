# PentaBooks Rights Administration

PentaBooks supports separately controlled corridors for staged readings, amateur/community/church/professional/touring productions, livestream and archival recording, scripted audio, podcast and broadcast excerpts, audiobook development, film and television evaluation and optioning, vertical drama, translation, curriculum, library and institutional use, exhibition, artwork and quotation, merchandise manufacturing, wholesale and retail distribution, approved character/world/mark use, structured research, and exact AI/model-use requests.

Each corridor has its own scope, controller, approvals, compensation, reporting, delivery, security, termination, and evidence requirements. Permission in one corridor never implies permission in another.
