## Continuity model

PentaBooks uses five fields:

1. **Fixed Core** — established names, relationships, dates, creator decisions, corrections, and governing facts.
2. **Near Field** — new scenes or products that arise naturally from an existing person, room, object, event, emotional consequence, or open question.
3. **Expansion Field** — credible corridors that widen medium, audience, territory, or commercial use.
4. **Experimental Field** — alternative staging, interactive form, visual treatment, or noncanonical test labeled before release.
5. **Sealed Field** — protected identities, medical material, children, confidential customer information, sealed testimony, and facts lacking publication authority.
