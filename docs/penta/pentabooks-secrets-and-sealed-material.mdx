# PentaBooks Secrets and Sealed Material

PentaBooks separates content by authority and exposure rather than treating every source as publishable.

## Public

Approved descriptions, product facts, public routes, non-secret identifiers, declared status, approved excerpts, and public-safe evidence projections.

## Controlled

Unreleased manuscripts, production packages, full-resolution artwork, editable source, QA records, hashes, draft forms, and provider handoff data. Controlled does not mean public or licensed.

## Sealed

Protected identities, medical material, children, confidential customer or family information, sealed testimony, disputed facts without publication authority, and source bodies restricted by creator decision, contract, privilege, or safety.

## Secret aliases

Credentials, OAuth tokens, service-role keys, private signing material, webhook secrets, private prompt grammar, and similar operational secrets remain in an approved vault. Repositories, Drive control packages, PentaDocs, products, logs, and ledgers may contain only the approved alias, classification, hash, rotation/SKU scope, and retrieval reference.

A secret’s presence at runtime does not prove its authority or a successful provider action. Exact provider readback and immutable evidence remain mandatory.
