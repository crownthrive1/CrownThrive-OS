# PentaBooks Licensing Boundary

PentaBooks licensing templates are model forms, not legal advice, not self-executing agreements, and not evidence that any license exists.

Every operational grant must resolve the exact parties, property and edition, approved materials and hashes, rights controller, medium, use, territory, language, term, exclusivity, compensation, royalty and reporting, approvals, credits, accessibility, security, archive and deletion obligations, change control, termination, signatures, and evidence receipt.

AI/ML training, fine-tuning, retrieval ingestion, embeddings, synthetic voice, identity synthesis, and model-use rights are prohibited by default. Any exception must be an exact CHLOM-controlled grant naming files, hashes, model purpose, retention, downstream access, output rights, deletion obligations, security, compensation, audit, and termination conditions.
