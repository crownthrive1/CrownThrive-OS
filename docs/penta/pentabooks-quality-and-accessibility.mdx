# PentaBooks Quality and Accessibility

Production volume is never a substitute for editorial quality, visual integrity, cultural specificity, accessibility, rights authority, or delivery reliability.

Each format receives appropriate checks: manuscript and continuity review; PDF preflight and visual inspection; DOCX structure and accessibility; EPUB3 validation; responsive reader behavior; meaningful alt text or decorative marking; captions and transcripts; color-independent status; large-print or low-vision production where appropriate; live-event accessibility riders; package inventory, manifest, hashes, and extraction; and bounded recovery tests.

A zero-warning tool result is one predicate, not a complete editorial or accessibility judgment. Independent dramaturgical, editorial, legal, cultural, and production review remain separately identified where required.
