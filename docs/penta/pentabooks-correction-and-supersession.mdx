# PentaBooks Correction and Supersession

A correction appends a new evidence generation and names the affected fact, prior value, corrected value, authority, reason, timestamp, and dependent projections. Supersession never erases the earlier edition or rewrites its hash.

A stronger edition must retain predecessor lineage, disclose material changes, re-run applicable quality and rights gates, generate new hashes, establish new custody, and update public and commercial projections only after the exact new edition passes.

The rule enables continuous improvement without canon drift, hidden replacement, or broken customer entitlements.
