# PentaBooks Merchandise Boundary

A PentaBooks artwork or merchandise-design package may support controlled development for apparel, journals, mugs, tumblers, art prints, posters, work shirts, aprons, patches, backdrops, café products, programs, digital promotion, and approved wholesale or retail applications.

The package does not itself grant manufacturing, resale, wholesale, retail, derivative, public-display, mark, character, or territory rights. Those rights remain with the controller unless an exact executed license states otherwise. Product samples, manufacturer proofs, inventory, quality checks, provider listings, and royalty/reporting obligations remain separate evidence predicates.
