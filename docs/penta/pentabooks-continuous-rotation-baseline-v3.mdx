# PentaBooks Continuous Rotation Baseline v3

**Baseline ID:** `PB-BASELINE-003`  
**Effective:** September 3, 2026  
**Applies to:** Rotation 001 backfill, Rotation 002 completion, Rotation 003, and every later rotation.

Rotation 002 is the minimum historical reference, not a permanent ceiling. Every new cycle must improve the production system and append those improvements to prior rotations without silently replacing editions, correction history, provenance, or evidence.

## Eight hard gates

1. **Source lock** — exact source edition, immutable subject, creator decisions, correction history, and lineage.
2. **Independent SKU package** — each SKU owns its reading edition, editable source, EPUB3 where applicable, reader, landing page, plain text, Markdown, metadata, JSON-LD, rights boundary, PentaGreen handoff, manifest, hashes, cover assets, and delivery package.
3. **Dual custody** — producer or Library custody plus Google Drive custody with file ID, byte size, SHA-256, download, and equality readback.
4. **Quality assurance** — editorial, PDF, DOCX, EPUB, accessibility, visual, manifest, hash, and packaging checks appropriate to the format.
5. **Rights, sealed material, and secrets separation** — public-safe metadata is projected separately from protected source, identities, customer information, credentials, and trade-secret material.
6. **Registry convergence** — Master Registry, PentaDocs, PentaGreen, package index, evidence, route, and recovery projections agree on the exact subject and current state.
7. **Provider activation** — exact product, price, tax, fulfillment, entitlement, reader, expiration, download, refund, and recovery predicates are exercised and read back where applicable.
8. **Public release** — public route, checkout, transaction, delivery, analytics, and post-sale readback pass. No preceding gate implies this one.

## Recurrence

PentaBooks runs two deterministic cycles in `America/New_York`:

| Cycle | Cron | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Primary | `0 * * * *` | Discover, classify, hash, reconcile, custody-check, register, and hand off new artifacts |
| Cold recovery | `7 * * * *` | Retry missed files, stale readbacks, dead-lettered handoffs, and provider drift |

The idempotency key is `rotation_id + product_id + sha256`. Retries append evidence and update projections only after immutable source evidence exists. An hourly run proves only what it actually read back; it cannot manufacture upload, database, commerce, or release success.

## Failure containment

A failing SKU is quarantined independently unless a shared dependency is proven unsafe. Working systems are not rolled back for novelty. Accepted artifacts remain intact, and recovery appends a new event rather than rewriting history.

## Secret and protected-material rule

No credential, OAuth token, service key, private prompt grammar, protected customer record, sealed identity, unpublished legal/medical evidence, or sealed raw source body may enter PentaDocs, public repositories, ordinary Drive control folders, product packages, analytics payloads, or logs. Only approved secret aliases, classification, hashes, and retrieval references may be serialized outside the vault.

## Prior-rotation upgrade rule

When a new rotation establishes a stronger invariant, PentaBooks creates an additive backfill audit for every earlier rotation. Prior works retain their original generation and receive explicit supersession, correction, and custody links. Unknown or missing evidence is recorded as `RECONCILIATION_REQUIRED`, never assumed to pass.

## Rotation 003 minimum

Rotation 003 — **Performance, Learning, and Licensing Deployment** — begins with 36 independent products: four full-length stage or screen works, eight scripted-audio episodes, eight education/facilitator products, four live-experience kits, 30 merchandise designs organized into four product systems, 14 editable licensing forms organized into four rights kits, and four infrastructure/control products.

Rotation 003 adds source-separated public and sealed editions, accessibility riders before layout, consent and safety packets, territory/language/term/royalty matrices, ethical sponsor inventory, a CrownLytics event dictionary, and a successful recovery drill before release certification.
