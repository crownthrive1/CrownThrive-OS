# PentaBooks

PentaBooks is CrownThrive’s governed canon, manuscript, edition, adaptation, packaging, QA, correction, custody, and product-lineage engine. It works with PentaGreen for commercialization, Go Flipbooks for controlled readers, CrownThrive Studios for production, Backroad FM and Melanated Studios for audio, Melanated TV and Melanated Voices TV for screen and cultural programming, CrownThriveU for learning, GSO Rights Desk for licensing, and the wider Thrive Flywheel for measured activation.
