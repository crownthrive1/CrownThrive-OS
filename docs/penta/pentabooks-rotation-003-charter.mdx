# PentaBooks Rotation 003

## Performance, Learning, and Licensing Deployment

**Rotation:** `PB-ROT-003`  
**Baseline:** `PB-BASELINE-003`  
**State:** `AUTHORIZED_AND_REGISTERED__CONTENT_PRODUCTION_NOT_YET_CERTIFIED`

Rotation 003 converts the Transmedia Foundation into deployable performance, learning, live-experience, sponsor, measurement, and rights-administration corridors. It opens with 36 independent candidate products and stronger safeguards than Rotation 002 from source intake forward.

## Production minimum

| Output class | Minimum |
|---|---:|
| Independent products | 36 |

| Scripted-audio episodes | 8 |

| Live-experience kits | 4 |
| Merchandise designs | 30 |
| Editable licensing forms | 14 |
| Dual-custody completion | 36 of 36 |

## Full-length development slate

1. **What Really Happened — The Hearing** — full-length documentary theatre.
2. **Five-Forty Coffee** — full-length ensemble stage work.
3. **TrapOpera — Episode 102: The Ledger Refuses to Close** — one-hour teleplay.
4. **The Moon Knows Our Names — The Night the Three Worlds Touched** — half-hour animated family pilot.

These are authorized production subjects, not completed-work claims.

## Eight scripted-audio episodes

The audio lane contains four new **Porch Records** episodes and four **Receipt Night Depositions** episodes. Every episode requires a complete script, sound map, cast sheet, transcript, clip map, sponsor-safe inventory, and exact rights/voice boundary.

## Eight learning products

The education lane covers archive ethics, adaptation without canon drift, Southern comedy performance, story-world licensing, audio-drama production, screenwriting from living archives, merchandise rights and approvals, and the Living Archive interview method. Each product requires facilitator and learner editions, activities, assessment, accessibility notes, and an institutional-use boundary.

## Four live-experience kits

Rotation 003 develops a Ticketed Porch Interview kit, Community Staged Reading kit, Receipt Night Listening Event kit, and Five-Forty Coffee Archive Salon kit. Each must include run of show, staffing, venue, ticketing, sponsorship, accessibility, recording, rights, safety, and post-event evidence.

## Rights, merchandise, and infrastructure

Four merchandise systems contain 30 governed designs. Four licensing kits contain 14 editable forms across performance/touring/recording; education/library/institutional use; sponsor/broadcast/media integration; and translation/territory/approved use. Four infrastructure products establish analytics, ethical sponsor inventory, website/reader activation, and release/recovery evidence.

## Stronger-than-Rotation-002 controls

- Source-separated public and sealed editions.
- Accessibility riders before layout and production capture.
- Consent and safety packets for interviews, live events, voices, likenesses, children, and protected facts.
- Territory, language, term, compensation, royalty, reporting, and termination matrices.
- Ethical sponsor inventory and exclusion categories.
- A CrownLytics event dictionary covering discovery, preview, inquiry, checkout, entitlement, refund, recovery, engagement, and license reporting.
- A successful recovery drill before release certification.
- Exact per-SKU delivery and provider readback before any release-ready state.

## Commercial and truth boundary

Every product remains independently purchasable and independently licensable. PentaGreen must establish exact rights, pricing, tax, fulfillment, entitlement, and provider evidence before activation. This charter authorizes and registers production; it does not claim completed manuscripts, customer-delivery ZIPs, provider products, public readers, checkouts, sales, or executed licenses.
