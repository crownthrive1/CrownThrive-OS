# Billing, Payments, and Transaction Support

Use this guide for an unrecognized charge, duplicate transaction, failed payment, incorrect amount, missing receipt, refund-status question, or other payment concern tied to a CrownThrive property.

## Identify the transaction first

Before contacting support, gather the minimum information needed to locate the transaction:

- CrownThrive platform, product, or URL;
- transaction or order date;
- amount and currency;
- order, invoice, receipt, or transaction reference ID;
- account email or username;
- last four digits of the payment method only when needed to distinguish transactions;
- a redacted screenshot when it helps show the issue.

Never send a full card number, CVV, bank password, one-time code, API key, or payment-provider password.

## Unrecognized charge

Confirm the descriptor, date, amount, and whether another authorized user could have made the purchase. If you believe the charge is unauthorized, secure the associated CrownThrive account and contact your card issuer or payment provider promptly using its official fraud process. Also report the transaction to ThriveSupport so the related account or order can be investigated.

## Duplicate charge

Check whether one entry is pending and another is posted. A pending authorization can look like a duplicate before settlement. If two completed charges remain for the same intended purchase, provide both references and timestamps.

## Failed or declined payment

Do not repeatedly submit payment when the interface shows a pending attempt. Verify billing information, available payment methods, and any error returned by the payment processor. Some declines can only be resolved by the issuer or payment provider.

## Refund or reversal status

Refund eligibility and timing depend on the specific product, agreement, transaction state, and payment method. Provide the original order/reference and any refund confirmation already received. ThriveSupport should not promise a refund or settlement date unless the governing system or policy provides one.

## Incorrect price or amount

Capture the product/offer page, checkout amount, final charged amount, applicable discount or credit, and transaction reference. Do not edit screenshots in a way that removes the relevant price or timestamp.
