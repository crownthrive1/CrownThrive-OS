# Welcome to CrownThrive Support

ThriveSupport is the central help entry point for CrownThrive’s connected ecosystem. CrownThrive spans business infrastructure, media, technology, commerce, community, education, analytics, advertising, creator tools, and governance. You do not need to understand the entire ecosystem before asking for help. Start with the problem you are trying to solve and we will route it to the correct support lane.

## Choose the support lane that matches your issue

### Account or access
Use the account lane when you cannot sign in, your recovery method is not working, your profile is locked, you are unsure which account belongs to a platform, or your access changed unexpectedly.

### Platform or feature help
Use platform help when you can sign in but need instructions, a feature is not behaving as expected, or you are unsure which CrownThrive platform should handle a task.

### Crown Credits, rewards, billing, or payments
Use the transaction lane for questions about supported Crown Credits activity, rewards, charges, duplicate transactions, failed payments, receipts, payment status, or other account-level transaction concerns.

### Creator, partner, affiliate, or collaborator help
Authenticated creators and partners may have additional support documentation based on their assigned role and program access. Sign in before opening protected creator/partner help.

### Privacy or security
Use the privacy/security lane for suspected account compromise, phishing, exposed credentials, unauthorized access, privacy requests, or potentially sensitive data exposure.

### Technical incident
Use technical support when a CrownThrive property is unavailable, returning errors, failing to complete a workflow, or behaving differently across devices or browsers.

## What to include when you contact support

A strong support request lets the team reproduce and route the issue without making you repeat yourself. Include:

1. The CrownThrive platform, product, or URL involved.
2. The email address or username associated with your account, when relevant.
3. What you were trying to do.
4. What happened instead.
5. The exact error message, if one appeared.
6. The approximate date and time, including your time zone.
7. A transaction, order, ticket, or reference ID when applicable.
8. A redacted screenshot when it materially shows the problem.

## Never send these through a normal support message

Do not send your password, one-time authentication code, recovery code, API key, private key, full payment-card number, bank login, or other secret credential. CrownThrive support documentation and staff workflows are designed around minimum necessary information.

## What happens after you submit an issue

Your issue may be routed to a platform-specific support lane, a billing or security workflow, a creator/partner queue, or an internal operator depending on the problem. Some CrownThrive properties are natively connected while others use distinct underlying systems, so the correct resolution path can vary by property.
