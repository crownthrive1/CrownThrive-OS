# Crown Credits Overview

Crown Credits are used on supported CrownThrive surfaces as an ecosystem credit or rewards mechanism. Availability, earning rules, redemption options, expiration treatment, transferability, and any purchase restrictions are governed by the specific participating product or program shown at the point of use.

## Before reporting a Crown Credits problem

Confirm the CrownThrive property and account where the activity occurred. Then check the balance/activity history available in that property.

For missing or incorrect credit activity, collect:

1. The platform or URL.
2. The date and approximate time.
3. The action, order, campaign, reward, or purchase connected to the credit activity.
4. Any transaction/reference ID.
5. The expected credit amount and the amount actually shown.
6. A redacted screenshot of the activity or balance when useful.

## Common situations

**Credits have not appeared yet.** Confirm that the qualifying action completed successfully and that you are viewing the same account used for the action. Some programs may record a pending state before final crediting.

**A balance changed unexpectedly.** Review the activity history for redemptions, reversals, refunds, corrections, expiration entries, or program adjustments before opening a ticket.

**A redemption failed.** Record the exact error and do not repeatedly submit the same redemption if the interface shows a pending transaction.

**A refund or reversal is involved.** Credits connected to refunded, reversed, disputed, or canceled transactions may be adjusted according to the governing product/program rules.

## Important limitations

Do not assume that Crown Credits are cash, withdrawable funds, transferable value, or universally usable across every CrownThrive property. The participating program’s current terms control those questions.

Never send a password, one-time code, payment credential, API key, or other secret to prove a Crown Credits issue.
