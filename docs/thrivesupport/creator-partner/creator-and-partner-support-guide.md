# Creator and Partner Support Guide

This article is for authenticated CrownThrive creators, partners, affiliates, collaborators, and other approved operators with `creator_partner` access or higher.

## Use this lane for

- onboarding or program-access issues;
- creator/partner profile and identity problems;
- campaign, referral, affiliate, or attribution questions;
- collaboration or Collab Portal workflow issues;
- approved media, publishing, licensing, or distribution support;
- access to creator tools, dashboards, inventories, or assigned programs;
- transaction or reward questions tied to a creator/partner relationship.

## Before opening a case

Identify the CrownThrive property and your relationship to it. Include the program/campaign/project name, your account identity, the affected URL or asset, relevant reference IDs, the expected result, the actual result, and a short chronology.

For attribution or referral issues, include the campaign/referral identifier and evidence of the intended route when available. For content or licensing issues, identify the asset and governing agreement/workflow without attaching restricted source material unless the case specifically requires it.

## Rights and access

Creator/partner status does not automatically grant access to every CrownThrive property, internal document, campaign, source asset, payout record, or control plane. Access is evaluated by assigned role, agreement, program eligibility, and the permission required by the resource.

If a role appears incorrect, request a role review rather than asking staff to bypass the access check.

## Secrets and sensitive information

Never paste passwords, one-time codes, API secret values, private keys, payment-provider credentials, vault values, or another operator’s confidential information into a support request. If a credential is exposed, rotate/revoke it through its owning system and report the credential type and incident time without reproducing the secret.

## Escalation

Escalate when a creator/partner issue involves active security risk, material financial impact, rights/licensing conflict, production distribution failure, or a time-sensitive campaign that cannot proceed. Preserve the original case/reference so evidence remains connected.

## Support operator rule

Protected creator/partner cases must be handled under least privilege. Staff may view only the information required to diagnose or route the case, and production changes must still go through the governed CrownThrive OS path.
