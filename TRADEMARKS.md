## Symbol discipline

- `™` may be used to identify a claimed brand, mark, product, program, framework, or source identity.
- `SM` may be used for a claimed service mark where appropriate.
- `®` must not be used unless CrownThrive has a current verified registration record applicable to the relevant mark, goods/services, and territory.

A historical source using “patent pending,” “registered,” “official,” “franchise,” or similar status language does not establish current status without current verified evidence.

## Permitted factual reference

A third party may make truthful, limited factual reference to CrownThrive or a CrownThrive product where independently permitted by applicable law, provided the reference:

- does not imply sponsorship, certification, partnership, endorsement, ownership, licensing, or affiliation;
- does not use CrownThrive logos, trade dress, confidential assets, character art, source files, or confusingly similar branding without permission;
- does not alter the name or combine it into a new mark, domain, handle, product, or service;
- does not misstate current capability, pricing, rights, registration, metrics, or legal status;
- does not disparage, counterfeit, impersonate, or create confusion about source.

## No portfolio license by adjacency

Permission to use one CrownThrive name, asset, API, product, license, or platform does not grant permission to use other CrownThrive brands, frameworks, imprints, universes, characters, data, source assets, methods, or marks.

Brand and trademark licenses require a governed written record defining the exact name or mark, approved presentation, goods/services, territory, channels, term, quality controls, review, attribution, economics, sublicensing, suspension, termination, and remedies.

## Domains, social handles, and impersonation

CrownThrive tracks critical domains and social identities through its domain/handle registry. Registration of a confusing domain, handle, account, app, token, marketplace listing, or page does not create rights in a CrownThrive identity.

Suspected impersonation, counterfeit use, misleading affiliation, or domain/handle conflict should be documented with dates, URLs, screenshots, account identifiers, and source evidence, then reported to `contact@crownthrive.com`.
