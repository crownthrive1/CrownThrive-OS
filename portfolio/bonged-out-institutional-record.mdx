# Bonged Out! Institutional Record

This record institutionalizes the **Bonged Out!** source package as a CrownThrive cultural/media imprint while preserving a strict boundary between cultural identity, source-contract terms and currently authorized commercial state.

```yaml

cannabis_relationship: cannabis-adjacent culture; not plant-touching permission
```

The source package positions Bonged Out! as a culture-first media identity built around humor, satire, social commentary, creator individuality and cannabis-adjacent subculture. The press kit explicitly distinguishes the imprint from selling cannabis.

## Cultural thesis

The source material repeatedly describes the imprint through these pillars:

- culture first, commerce second;
- humor as cultural navigation, resistance and connection;
- smart, irreverent, internet-native voice;
- lo-fi editorial grit rather than glossy corporate cannabis aesthetics;
- creator individuality and community signal before monetization;
- satire without punching down;
- cannabis-adjacent identity without clichéd stoner branding.

The press-kit positioning summarizes the concept as a culture/media imprint, not a cannabis product business.

## Voice and narrative rules

Source-defined voice:

- smart;
- comedic;
- irreverent;
- culturally aware;
- conversational;
- bold without becoming hostile;
- internet fluent without corporate imitation of internet culture.

Source red lines include:

- no punching down;
- no try-hard stoner stereotypes;
- no corporate-gloss cannabis posture;
- no hate/discrimination or illegal content;
- no harmful brand association or identity dilution;
- no implication that the brand license authorizes cannabis retail or plant-touching activity.

Named external publications/entertainment properties in source style references are mood references only. They do not create affiliation, endorsement, license or permission to imitate protected trade dress.

## Visual identity

The Style Bible describes a visual system centered on:

- gritty internet-magazine / bootleg-zine energy;
- film grain, archival noise and VHS-like texture;
- black, off-white, faded green and smoke/static cues;
- simple wordmark treatment;
- rough ink, stamp, newspaper and analog-distortion variants;
- reels, shorts, memes, still frames and micro-documentary cards.

The source explicitly rejects cartoon cannabis leaves, psychedelic tie-dye and glossy corporate treatment as default brand language.

## Media formats

Source materials contemplate:

- short-form satire;
- cultural micro-documentaries;
- meme-led editorial posts;
- opinion shorts and street-interview formats;
- independent creator collaborations;
- commentary series;
- potential future FAST/micro-channel, podcast and merchandise extensions.

Future formats are opportunities, not proof of current deployment.

## Operator model

The Operator Handbook describes independent operators as responsible for their own execution, suppliers, pricing, fulfillment, marketing and customer support while staying within the brand license and integrity rules.

It expressly describes the relationship as **not a franchise** and emphasizes operator autonomy rather than mandatory operating procedures or required vendors.

That source position must still be evaluated against the actual commercial relationship and applicable law before a live offering is marketed as definitively outside franchise regulation.

## Historical/source licensing terms

The Licensing Agreement and Lease/Equity Addendum contain specific source terms, including:

- 24-month non-transferable/revocable license language;
- source standard license fee of USD 50,000;
- source equity-path language of USD 40,000 plus 10% equity to CrownThrive;
- source buyout language of USD 150,000 standard or USD 120,000 equity-path;
- CrownThrive ownership of the underlying brand IP until a qualifying buyout;
- operator autonomy;
- brand-integrity controls;
- future CHLOM LEX access language;
- no-franchise / independent-entity language.

**These figures and terms are preserved as source-contract evidence, not automatically current public pricing, checkout, entitlement or live licensing authority.** Before commercial activation, current legal/rights review, ThriveEvergreen economic authority, CHLOM rights state and current signed agreement must reconcile them.

The current CIE framework package itself also remains fail-closed for invented price, checkout, license grant, certification or customer entitlement.

## CIE profile

CIE governs Bonged Out! as an adult cultural imprint with specific constraints:

- audience must remain adult/21+ where required by the context;
- cultural framing must not imply cannabis sales or plant-touching authority;
- satire should not become dehumanizing harassment or punching down;
- counterculture positioning should remain source-authentic rather than generic cannabis branding;
- cross-platform reuse must preserve tone, audience and source attribution;
- partnerships should not create false endorsement or unsafe regulatory association;
- adaptations into merchandise, FAST, podcasts or other channels require new rights/platform/economic checks.

## CHLOM and ThriveEvergreen handoff

```text
CIE cultural identity / audience PASS
→ CHLOM IP + license + operator-rights validation
→ current legal agreement / jurisdiction checks
→ ThriveEvergreen ECAC for economic activation
→ CSS/platform execution where authorized
→ DAIL/evidence + lifecycle monitoring
```

No single stage may substitute for the others.
