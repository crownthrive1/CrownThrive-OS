# Public Brand & Platform Portfolio

<Note>
  This is a public-safe institutional routing layer. It intentionally exposes **identity, state, public domains, internal documentation links and portfolio relationships** while excluding credentials, admin URLs, customer data, restricted evidence, internal infrastructure details, private contracts, trade secrets and unreleased confidential IP.
</Note>

## Permanent portfolio rule

CrownThrive documents an identity because it is part of institutional history or current/future architecture, **not only because it is active today**.

Therefore the portfolio preserves:

- active and production identities;
- development and controlled-test identities;
- reserve/future-build identities;
- legacy/predecessor/successor identities;
- sunset and archived brands;
- research frameworks and unactivated IP;
- merged aliases and renamed platforms;
- historical public brands whose domains remain under CrownThrive custody.

Nothing disappears merely because its lifecycle changes.

## Public portfolio-card contract

Every known identity must eventually expose a public-safe card with:

```yaml
stable_id:
canonical_name:
object_class:
current_disposition:
historical_names: []
parent_or_steward:
corridor:
public_summary:
public_domains: []
internal_docs: []
portfolio_relationships: []
press_room:
public_powered_by_lineage: []
source_refs: []
last_public_scan_at:
last_institutional_review_at:
```

`public_domains` means only domains safe and useful for public routing. Private admin paths, infrastructure hostnames, secret callback URLs and restricted internal endpoints never belong in the public portfolio.

These observations are useful relationship and routing evidence, but public marketing copy cannot override founder adjudications, machine registries, current legal authority or runtime proof.

## Internal-linking contract

Every portfolio page should link, as applicable, to:

- its stable portfolio/register record;
- domain record(s);
- alias/predecessor/successor record;
- platform/corridor page;
- current legal/support coverage;
- current implementation/provider record where public-safe;
- related Canon/Doctrine record;
- press-room/media-kit surface;
- relevant changelog and phase records.

This makes the documentation estate navigable by humans, agents, search and future APIs without exposing internal secrets.

## Press-room relationship

Every material public-facing identity should expose either:

1. a dedicated press/media-kit page; or
2. a portfolio-card press block inherited from the [CrownThrive Press Room](/press/index).

Reserve/sunset identities may use an archive/history press disposition rather than a promotional media kit.

## Phase 3 hard-gate implication

Phase 3 cannot start while material brand/platform/domain documentation remains stale, orphaned or silently missing from the governed estate. The final GATE-008 snapshot must demonstrate that every known identity has a terminal public/internal documentation disposition and that public-safe routing is reconciled.
