## PentaMedia™

PentaMedia™ is CrownThrive's canonical media-operations and maintenance framework. It owns the operational continuity layer for CrownThrive media assets across Melanated TV, MVP, Virality Music, Locticians media, CrownThrive Studios-associated distribution surfaces, and future CrownThrive media networks.

PentaMedia does not replace the underlying brands or networks. Melanated TV remains Melanated TV, MVP remains MVP, Virality Music remains Virality Music, and provider identities such as Viloud remain provider identities. PentaMedia is the governed operating framework that binds, verifies, maintains, documents, monetizes, and reconciles those assets across the CrownThrive media mesh.

## Operating scope

PentaMedia owns the operational framework for:

- media asset ingestion and normalization
- provider binding and provider-state reconciliation
- live-stream identity and playback
- FAST channel operations
- linear channel operations
- syndicated channel operations
- on-demand channel operations
- HLS/M3U8 playback surfaces
- embed/player surfaces
- EPG and schedule readiness
- SCTE-35 advertising markers
- server-side ad insertion (SSAI)
- VAST advertising tags
- AdLuxe Network monetization bindings
- analytics and operational evidence
- public-safe channel directories
- asset continuity, receipts and maintenance state

The maintenance runtime is `integration_control.pentamedia_maintenance_cycle_v1()`.

## Maintenance behavior

Every internal maintenance cycle:

1. discovers or refreshes every registered media asset from the canonical media registry;
2. preserves the network/media family and provider relationship;
3. classifies the asset as FAST, LINEAR, OD, SYND, LIVE, or another registered channel class;
4. evaluates whether a known playback surface exists;
5. evaluates whether a monetization binding exists;
6. identifies unverified playback or unbound FAST monetization as open maintenance work;
7. emits an append-style maintenance receipt; and
8. refreshes the public PentaMedia status surface.

A maintenance open item is not treated as an outage. PentaMedia distinguishes registered, configured, verified and active states instead of manufacturing production claims.

## Advertising and FAST monetization

PentaMedia incorporates the canonical FAST monetization profile:

- 7-day schedule
- 24/7 playout
- weekly repeating grid
- 8 SCTE-35 advertising breaks per hour
- 120 seconds per break
- 16 advertising minutes per hour
- 44 programming minutes per hour
- Viloud SSAI where enabled
- AdLuxe Network as CrownThrive's advertising-network layer
- Adserve Online as the current ad-server transport
- one dedicated AdLuxe zone per production channel

The live seed VAST URL is treated as a template/proving asset, not as permission to reuse one zone across every channel. Production channels must receive their own zone binding.

## Provider boundary

Provider capabilities do not become institutional authority by themselves. PentaMedia may reconcile and operate within certified provider paths, but provider write access must be separately proven and bounded. Secrets, stream keys, API tokens, license codes and private provider credentials remain vault-only and are never published through the public status surface.

## Public-by-design model

PentaMedia supports public transparency through sanitized projections while preserving secure internal control planes. Public users may inspect public channel and framework status surfaces; they do not receive privileged database writes, raw credentials, governance payloads, secret references, or provider-control authority.

## Relationship to Melanated TV Wirein

`Melanated TV Wirein Channels and etc` is now an intake/workflow family operated by PentaMedia. New Viloud channels, embeds, M3U8 links, FAST settings, EPG configuration, AdLuxe zones, VAST tags, analytics snapshots, or future provider media assets should be normalized into the existing PentaMedia mesh rather than creating parallel registries.

## Governance

PentaMedia follows CrownThrive/CHLOM governance:

- no manufactured authority;
- no self-approval;
- D3 remains human-reserved;
- provider success is not authority;
- public claims require evidence;
- secrets remain vault-bound;
- destructive changes require explicit authority;
- provider mutations require certified write adapters;
- maintenance generates evidence rather than silently rewriting history.
