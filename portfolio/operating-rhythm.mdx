## Operating Rhythm and Team Model

The ecosystem needs a repeatable management cadence so that dozens of assets do not collapse back into founder memory and emergency work.

## Human accountability model

The Holdings management plan identifies the following institutional functions, whether filled by employees, contractors, fractional leaders, or founder-supervised teams:

- Founder / Managing Member — vision, culture, capital allocation, portfolio and final governance decisions;
- Portfolio Operations / COO — P0 delivery, accountability, process, vendor and cross-platform execution;
- Platform / Integration Lead — identity, IO/MCP, architecture, deployment, security, standards;
- Revenue & Partnerships — sales, advertisers, publishers, sponsors, affiliates and enterprise relationships;
- Commerce & Fulfillment — catalogs, product QA, vendors, digital entitlements, orders and customer experience;
- Media / Editorial — content supply, programming, rights intake, creator operations and quality;
- Customer Success / Support — knowledge, onboarding, ticketing, escalation and support analytics;
- Finance / Legal / Compliance — books, taxes, contracts, insurance, rights, privacy and audit readiness.

## Operating cadence

**Daily:** incidents, checkout/support failures, transactions, fulfillment exceptions, security alerts and campaign health.

**Weekly:** P0 roadmap, pipeline, releases, support trends, platform reliability, renewals, content supply and cross-corridor blockers.

**Monthly:** P&L by stream, contribution margin, customer cohorts, content economics, rights/license activity and lifecycle decisions.

**Quarterly:** capital allocation, P0/P1 reprioritization, risk register, vendor/license review, domains, impact and strategy reset.

**Annually:** entity/tax/legal review, financial statements, insurance, IP audit, succession, security assessment and strategic-plan refresh.

## Agent augmentation

Agents may prepare, classify, reconcile, monitor, draft, test, and route work. They do not eliminate accountable human ownership. Every agent requires a scope, data sources, permissions, logging, review thresholds, escalation path and rollback.

## Institutional output

Operating reviews should create durable decisions and events rather than disappearing into chat. Material changes must update the relevant registry, ADR, roadmap, support knowledge and DAIL record.
