This record institutionalizes the **Kulture House / Kulture Radio** source package without converting source-era or partner-specific terms into ecosystem-wide defaults.

The source package describes **Kulture House** as the Creative Partner's cultural universe and **Kulture Radio** as its broadcast headquarters. CrownThrive supplies infrastructure, governance, platform integration, distribution rails, analytics, promotion and operating support; the Creative Partner supplies the voice, culture, curation, programming and creative presence.

## Source-defined roles

### Kulture House / Creative Partner

Source materials assign the partner functions including:

- creator, curator, host and storyteller;
- operator/steward of Kulture House and Kulture Radio;
- production of radio, shows, documentaries, listening sessions, live programming, films and commentary;
- community connection and cultural presence;
- project submission, classification and slate management through the Collab Portal.

### CrownThrive

The source package assigns CrownThrive functions including:

- digital, technical, broadcast and distribution infrastructure;
- ecosystem routing into CrownThrive media and platform corridors;
- CHLOM governance/compliance architecture;
- Hybrid Incubator / ThriveLegacy / ThriveAlumni pathways;
- analytics through CrownLytics;
- promotion through CrownPulse, SocialAIly and AdLuxe-related surfaces;
- event/ticketing rails through ThriveTickets.

These are source-defined responsibilities. A provider or platform must still be currently available and authorized before execution.

## Project classification contract

The source package requires projects to be classified before revenue-share treatment is applied.

Recognized source classifications include:

- Exclusive;
- Non-Exclusive;
- Hybrid;
- FAST;
- OD / On Demand;
- SYND / Syndication;
- Live;
- Event;
- PPV / Premium.

The Pocket Playbook and Master Summary state a **no classification, no split** principle and separate internal CrownThrive economics from third-party platform economics.

## Source commercial terms

The source documents include partner-specific economic terms, including:

- Exclusive projects: 80% Partner / 20% CrownThrive when applicable;
- Non-Exclusive projects: 70% Partner / 30% CrownThrive when CrownThrive actively supports the project;
- No support = no split;
- ThriveTickets: 10% ticketing fee in the described source model;
- sponsored live-event support may use separately agreed low splits;
- third-party platform rules pay first, with CrownThrive splits applied only to value CrownThrive actually receives.

These values are **source-contract/business terms for this package**, not automatically the current universal CrownThrive price book. Any live billing, entitlement, invoice, payout or economic activation still resolves through current contractual authority and ThriveEvergreen/commerce governance.

## Broadcast and distribution

The source package distinguishes between events CrownThrive broadcasts and events it does not broadcast.

When CrownThrive broadcasts a qualifying event, source rules describe a time-bounded exclusive broadcast/replay window and possible FAST/OD/SYND/MVTV routing subject to classification, metadata, inventory, rights and technical conditions.

When CrownThrive does not broadcast, the source package leaves the partner free to use its own channels, subject to any separately applicable agreement or platform rule.

## ThriveLegacy source benefits

The project documents describe ThriveLegacy as the highest tier in this specific relationship and list benefits such as broad platform access, service discounts, AdLuxe grant language, CHLOM Business License Tier access, strategy support and a ThriveAlumni pathway.

These are preserved as **relationship-source terms**. They should not be represented as a currently open public program or automatic entitlement for unrelated parties without present authority/evidence.
