# Partner, Vendor and Referral Registry

The **Partner, Vendor and Referral Registry** governs the external services, software vendors, suppliers and infrastructure providers CrownThrive uses to operate the Convergent Ecosystem.

It serves two simultaneous purposes:

1. **operational control** — know what CrownThrive depends on, what it costs, what data it touches, what its limits are and how it can be replaced; and
2. **ethical monetization** — identify legitimate vendor affiliate/referral/partner programs that can create revenue when CrownThrive recommends tools it actually uses or has independently qualified.

The monetization layer must never override security, product fitness, customer interest, licensing restrictions or CrownThrive's vendor-independence doctrine.

## Complete coverage rule

The registry is an **overlay**, not a replacement, for the existing engine/service inventory.

All **85 S100 engine/service source rows (`S100-ENG-001` through `S100-ENG-085`)** are automatically in partner/vendor review scope. Newer infrastructure providers that are not part of that dated 85-row census are added as supplemental partner records rather than rewriting the historical source count.

Therefore:

```text
S100 85-row Engine & Service Seed
             +
current infrastructure/provider discoveries
             ↓
Partner / Vendor / Referral Registry
             ↓
operations • renewal • cost • capacity • referral economics • exit plan
```

## Required partner record

Each partner/vendor record should track:

- stable partner ID;
- legal/provider name and product/service name;
- related S100 engine/service ID where applicable;
- CrownThrive platform(s) and corridor(s) served;
- relationship type: infrastructure, SaaS, licensed source, supplier, manufacturer, marketplace, payments, creative, communications or professional service;
- current verification state;
- contract/license/plan class;
- renewal/billing cycle and known cost where appropriate;
- quota/rate-limit/capacity profile;
- data/security/privacy classification;
- API, webhook, OAuth/OIDC and MCP capability;
- backup/export method;
- replacement/exit candidate;
- support/SLA state;
- affiliate/referral/partner-program state;
- program URL/source authority when verified;
- payout model, cookie/attribution period and restrictions when verified;
- required affiliate disclosures;
- referral revenue received;
- referral customers/conversions where lawful and measurable;
- last verification date and next review.

Never place API secrets, referral private keys, credentials or restricted commercial agreements into the public documentation projection.

## Referral-program lifecycle

Use controlled states:

`research_pending → program_verified → eligible → applied → approved → active → suspended/ended`

or terminal states:

`not_available`, `not_eligible`, `prohibited`, `conflict_of_interest`, `not_economically_material`.

A vendor is **not** represented as having a referral program merely because other websites say it does. Program existence, eligibility and terms require current provider evidence.

## Provider-to-platform separation

A vendor relationship does not merge the CrownThrive platform identities that consume it.

- **Partnero** is the current attribution/affiliate engine for Crown Affiliates and Crown Ambassadors (`S100-ENG-030`). It is not AdLuxe and it is not CrownRewards.
- **Adserver.Online** is the current/revamp ad-serving provider beneath AdLuxe Network (`S100-ENG-020`). It is not Crown Affiliates and it is not CrownRewards.
- **Reward Loyalty / NowSquare** is the self-hosted loyalty engine beneath CrownRewards / My CrownRewards (`S100-ENG-024`). It is not Crown Affiliates and it is not AdLuxe.

Cross-platform events may be linked through CrownThrive ID, IO/MCP, CHLOM, THIVEBASE, CrownLytics and governed event contracts without collapsing provider or platform identity.

## Monetization model for partner relationships

Partner referral economics can become a **secondary Thrive Flywheel revenue lane**:

```text
CrownThrive uses / evaluates a tool
            ↓
operational evidence + training + integration knowledge
            ↓
independent recommendation or implementation pathway
            ↓
verified provider partner/referral link
            ↓
customer conversion
            ↓
commission / referral fee
            ↓
CrownLytics attribution + CHLOM/finance record
            ↓
reinvestment into documentation, support and ecosystem infrastructure
```

Priority commercialization surfaces can include:

- CrownThriveU implementation courses and tool-selection guides;
- CrownThrive Support articles and comparison matrices with clear disclosures;
- CrownThrive IO/MCP integration marketplace listings;
- incubatee/startup infrastructure bundles;
- MM Suites/Suite Pro recommended-tool stack;
- creator, beauty-professional, advertiser and publisher operating kits;
- Crown Affiliates/Ambassadors campaigns where the upstream vendor program permits sub-affiliate or referral distribution;
- managed implementation services paired with a permitted referral commission.

## Disclosure and independence rules

- disclose compensated links where legally/contractually required;
- do not claim a tool is "best" because it pays CrownThrive;
- separate product/security evaluation from referral economics;
- do not route users through a referral relationship if it materially worsens price, rights, privacy or support;
- record referral/affiliate revenue separately from the underlying vendor cost and separately from customer service fees;
- preserve vendor exit plans even when referral revenue is meaningful;
- review anti-kickback, regulated-industry, tax, consumer-protection and contract restrictions for specialized corridors before monetizing a referral.

## Phase integration

**Phase 2 / 2.99:** establish inventory, statuses, known dependencies, program-research queue and disclosure rules.

**Phase 5+:** open a governed CrownThrive Partner Marketplace/Developer Economy where approved providers, integrations and implementation partners can generate referral fees, listing/sponsorship revenue, implementation revenue and API usage while remaining subject to CHLOM and CIE rules.
