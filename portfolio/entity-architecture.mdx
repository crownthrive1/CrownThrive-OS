## Entity and Stewardship Architecture

The current management plan proposes **CrownThrive Holdings** as the future portfolio-stewardship, capital-allocation, governance, and long-term ownership layer above **CrownThrive, LLC**, which remains the operating company and hybrid incubator.

This is an operating design, not a representation that every proposed entity has already been legally formed or that intercompany, tax, securities, franchise, or IP arrangements have been finalized.

## Recommended layers

| Layer | Institutional role | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| CrownThrive Holdings | Portfolio stewardship | Own/control interests, approve lifecycle and capital decisions, support succession |
| CrownThrive, LLC | Operating company / Hybrid Incubator | Operate shared infrastructure, services, support, partnerships, and active portfolio execution |
| CHLOM / IP structure | Rights and licensing governance | Chain of title, permissions, licenses, provenance, splits, restrictions, renewals, and evidence |
| CII / ThriveFund | Impact and reinvestment | Criteria, designated allocations, evidence, milestones, and public accountability; legal form not presumed |
| Project SPVs | Risk-separated ventures | Used selectively for material physical, real-estate, franchise, regulated, or outside-capital risk |

## Holdings mandates

The proposed stewardship layer should govern:

- portfolio lifecycle and priority;
- capital allocation;
- ownership and control records;
- acquisition, licensing, merger, sale, reserve, and sunset decisions;
- risk separation;
- succession and continuity;
- intercompany economics and documentation;
- institutional narrative and partner credibility.

## Legal boundary

Final entity structure, tax treatment, IP transfers, intercompany agreements, capital structure, franchise arrangements, securities implications, and fiduciary obligations require qualified professional review before execution.

## Phase 3 implication

The software should model organizations, assets, ownership/control relationships, responsible operators, and approval authority **without hard-coding a legal conclusion that has not yet been adopted**.
