# Extended Platform and Framework Register

Phase 2.7 expands the institutional inventory beyond the compact Holdings snapshot so long-tail CrownThrive records are not lost.

## Why this register exists

Different source families describe CrownThrive at different moments and with different granularity. A platform count is therefore not a stable measure of institutional identity. One source may count brands, another engines, another frameworks, another domains, another products, and another dormant concepts.

## Phase 2.98 D6 canonical disposition overlay

The [D6 Long-Tail Platform Disposition Register](/knowledge/phase-2-98-d6-long-tail-platform-disposition-register) is now the controlling public-safe overlay for high-confidence long-tail identity decisions. It preserves this recovery list while adding current disposition, alias, predecessor/successor and reactivation-control fields.

Current supported examples include:

- CrownApps / ThriveApps — one alias-controlled asset pending branding ADR;
- ThriveStudio — predecessor to CrownThrive Studios and distinct from AdLuxe Studio;
- CrownThrive Music — Virality umbrella/lineage pending final relationship ADR;
- Ops Oasis — alias of OpsOasis;
- ThriveSeats — plural/legacy lineage for ThriveSeat;
- My CrownOasis / ThriveFoundry — reserve;
- ChainCliques / NFTCliques / StonkCliques — sunset brand reserve;
- ThriveCafe — sunset/repurpose pending and distinct from Melanin Magic Cafe;
- FindCliques — reserve/future rebuild without a false current 2.0 deployment claim;
- SocialAIly — legacy preserved, current implementation unverified;
- Tribes, Melanated Culture & Heritage Museum, Digital Business Academy, NeuralCraft AI Studio, Ilyass.AI / Quantum Initiative, CrownJewel, Storytime, MV VoiceForge and MVP Roku — owner validation or source reconciliation required.

Domain ownership, provider-license history or a legacy Help Center title is not sufficient to promote a reserve, sunset or unresolved record into active production.

## Required disposition

Each record must become one of:

`current-core`, `current-growth`, `incubate`, `reserve`, `research`, `legacy`, `merged`, `renamed`, `successor`, `sunset`, `restricted`, or `needs-validation`.

## Counting rule

Do not publish a single platform count unless the counting methodology is stated. Keep separate counts for legal entities, brands, platforms, services, frameworks, engines, domains, products, and research assets.

## Phase 3 requirement

Stable IDs must survive every classification change. Status is mutable; institutional identity and lineage are not.
