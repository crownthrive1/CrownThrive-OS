# Extended Platform and Framework Register

Phase 2.7 expands the institutional inventory beyond the compact Holdings snapshot so long-tail CrownThrive records are not lost.

## Why this register exists

Different source families describe CrownThrive at different moments and with different granularity. A platform count is therefore not a stable measure of institutional identity. One source may count brands, another engines, another frameworks, another domains, another products, and another dormant concepts.

## Required disposition

Each record must become one of:

`current-core`, `current-growth`, `incubate`, `reserve`, `research`, `legacy`, `merged`, `renamed`, `successor`, `sunset`, `restricted`, or `needs-validation`.

## Counting rule

Do not publish a single platform count unless the counting methodology is stated. Keep separate counts for legal entities, brands, platforms, services, frameworks, engines, domains, products, and research assets.

## Phase 3 requirement

Stable IDs must survive every classification change. Status is mutable; institutional identity and lineage are not.
