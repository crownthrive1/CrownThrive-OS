# Extended Platform and Framework Register

Phase 2.7 expands the institutional inventory beyond the compact Holdings snapshot so long-tail CrownThrive records are not lost. Phase 2.99 now preserves the exact later-v1.0 July 29 source census as the [S103 74-Row Platform/Framework Seed](/knowledge/phase-2-99-workstream-3a-phase-2-7-74-platform-framework-source-seed), with source indexes 0–73 individually addressable. `CT-CON-040` prevents that 74-row version-scoped census from being confused with an earlier same-day v1.0 summary reporting 60 or the later expanded S104 v1.1 registry.

## Why this register exists

Different source families describe CrownThrive at different moments and with different granularity. A platform count is therefore not a stable measure of institutional identity. One source may count brands, another engines, another frameworks, another domains, another products, and another dormant concepts.

## Required disposition

Each record must become one of:

`current-core`, `current-growth`, `incubate`, `reserve`, `research`, `legacy`, `merged`, `renamed`, `successor`, `sunset`, `restricted`, or `needs-validation`.

## Counting rule

Do not publish a single platform count unless the counting methodology is stated. Keep separate counts for legal entities, brands, platforms, services, frameworks, engines, domains, products, and research assets.

## Phase 3 requirement

Stable IDs must survive every classification change. Status is mutable; institutional identity and lineage are not.

All long-tail identities now inherit the public portfolio/domain/press-room documentation contract. Reserve and sunset identities remain documented with archive/history press treatment; current identities receive public-safe portfolio and press routing appropriate to their evidence state.
