# ThriveTools Family Disposition

## Portfolio classification

```yaml
portfolio_parent:
  id: ct.platform.thrivetools
  name: ThriveTools
  source_row: S100-PORT-017
  source_priority: P2
  source_status: Active
  institutional_type: platform_tool_family

child_capabilities:
  - ct.capability.thrivetools.seo
  - ct.capability.thrivetools.opt
```

The two children are capability/application identities under the ThriveTools family. They remain addressable for operations and documentation but do not create additional top-level portfolio rows in canonical institutional counting.

## Why this matters

Older portfolio material may list ThriveTools, SEO and OPT as adjacent named brands/platforms. That historical presentation is retained in source records. Current governance instead distinguishes **identity** from **counting unit**:

- the parent is the portfolio-level platform/tool family;
- SEO and OPT are productized child capabilities;
- each child can have its own host, API, version, provider, data, legal, health and revenue state;
- child maturity cannot be inferred from parent maturity;
- parent maturity cannot be inflated by a child passing one certification gate.

See the [ThriveTools Institutional Registry](/platforms/thrivetools-institutional-registry) and [Domain and Capability Crosswalk](/knowledge/thrivetools-domain-capability-crosswalk).
