# ThriveAlumni Governance Lineage

Older CrownThrive sources describe ThriveAlumni in a much broader civic and governance role than current operating descriptions. Phase 2.7 preserves both states without allowing historical language to silently become current authority.

## Historical doctrine

Recovered Founder Handbook and Help Center materials describe ThriveAlumni as a continuity, civic, contributor, leadership-development, and governance-adjacent layer. Some historical materials use governmental metaphors, committees, boards, alumni councils, or long-term stewardship language.

These records are valuable for institutional history because they explain how CrownThrive thought about continuity, service, accountability, legacy, mentorship, alumni participation, and successor development.

## Current interpretation

Until current governance records explicitly restore a broader authority, ThriveAlumni should be treated as a **verified alumni/contributor continuity network** rather than an independent governmental body with implied legal power over CrownThrive LLC.

## Authority rule

Historical ThriveAlumni documents may inform culture, succession, mentorship, advisory design, and continuity planning. They do not supersede:

- the Managing Member / Founder authority actually in force;
- adopted entity governance documents;
- signed agreements;
- current role and approval registries;
- current CHLOM authority records;
- later explicit founder decisions.

## Phase 3 requirement

Create separate machine-readable records for:

- historical governance model;
- current operational role;
- advisory roles;
- succession/continuity functions;
- committee/board claims requiring current validation;
- any delegated authority, with start/end dates and evidence.

This distinction prevents future agents from mistaking institutional history for present authority.
