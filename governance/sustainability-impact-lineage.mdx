## Sustainability and Impact Lineage

CrownThrive source materials include sustainability, EcoDrive, climate-support, impact, community-reinvestment, ThriveFund, and CII concepts. Phase 2.7 restores these as a separate institutional lineage rather than assuming every historical initiative is active today.

## Historical source themes

Recovered material includes concepts around:

- EcoDrive
- Stripe Climate references
- sustainability and environmental responsibility
- community reinvestment
- impact evidence
- support / stabilization / acceleration concepts associated with ThriveFund
- social and community outcomes
- culturally aligned capital deployment

## Current-state discipline

A historical sustainability or impact statement must not be reused as a current public claim without validating:

- whether the integration/program still exists;
- whether money is actually being allocated;
- the measurement method;
- the reporting period;
- the responsible entity;
- whether the claim is marketing, management reporting, verified evidence, or third-party certification.

## Impact register model

Phase 3 should support records for:

- program
- objective
- beneficiary group
- allocation source
- amount/resource type
- decision authority
- milestone
- outcome metric
- evidence
- reporting period
- public-claim class
- restrictions
- related asset/corridor

## Separation rule

Impact, philanthropy, community support, marketing sponsorship, affiliate rewards, and ordinary operating expenses are different economic categories and should not be merged merely because each may benefit a community.

## Institutional objective

The long-term purpose is to preserve CrownThrive's impact and legacy doctrine while making every public claim traceable to current evidence.
