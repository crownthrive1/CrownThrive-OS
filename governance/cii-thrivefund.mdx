## CII and ThriveFund Governance

The **CrownThrive Impact Institute (CII)** and **ThriveFund** represent CrownThrive's internal impact, capability-building, reinvestment, evidence and continuity functions.

## Founder-adjudicated current model

- **CII is not to be represented as a nonprofit organization by default.**
- **ThriveFund is an internal CrownThrive-aligned impact/reinvestment mechanism**, not presumed to be a legally distinct regulated investment fund, charity, foundation or tax-exempt vehicle.
- ThriveFund is aligned with **MM Suites** and designated corporate/economic allocations intended to fund approved ecosystem objectives.
- **ThriveAlumni** supplies continuity/community alignment and stewardship context around the internal impact/reinvestment function; it does not automatically become a separate corporate governing body.

## Institutional role

CII should govern impact criteria, program standards, evidence, public accountability, capability-building and learning. ThriveFund should govern designated internal allocations, milestone funding, pilot support, stabilization, scholarships/support programs where adopted, integrations and other approved CrownThrive objectives.

## Capital-routing standard

Each allocation should identify source of funds, originating CrownThrive economic lane, approved purpose, authority, recipient/project, milestone conditions, permitted use, payment schedule, reporting/evidence requirements, correction/holdback rules where adopted, outcome metrics and accounting/tax classification.

### MM Suites alignment

MM Suites may designate a corporate share, fee allocation, retained margin, internal reserve, program contribution or other approved economic stream into ThriveFund. The exact percentage or instrument is **not canonical until recorded in the Revenue Registry and approved accounting/legal model**.

This design allows the impact/reinvestment mechanism to be financed internally without claiming nonprofit status.

## Proof over narrative

Public reporting must distinguish amounts allocated, amounts actually transferred, outputs delivered, people served and outcomes. A planned allocation is not a disbursement; a program announcement is not completed impact.

## Holdings and CHLOM relationship

The future/proposed Holdings layer may allocate institutional capital. CrownThrive, LLC remains the operating entity unless formation evidence establishes otherwise. CHLOM governs relevant rights, permissions, eligibility and records. CII/ThriveFund governs designated impact/reinvestment purpose and accountability.

## Legal/accounting boundary

Do not imply nonprofit, charitable, grant-making, securities, investment-fund or tax-advantaged status unless formally established. Final intercompany, reserve, expense, contribution, ownership and tax mechanics require the appropriate legal/accounting implementation.

## Phase 3 implication

Approval, Revenue and DAIL services must support designated internal allocations, milestones, evidence, holds and corrections while preventing agents from autonomously creating regulated financial products or disbursement authority.
