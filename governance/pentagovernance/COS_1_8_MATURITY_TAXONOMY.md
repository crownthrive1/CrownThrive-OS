# CrownThrive OS Maturity Taxonomy — COS 1.8

CrownThrive does not have one global phase label that overrides the observed state of
every corridor, platform, Penta, site, provider, or release. Each governed subject
publishes its own typed maturity.

## Canonical states

- `concept`: named but not implemented
- `incubating`: under governed design or construction
- `implemented`: source exists but production predicates are incomplete
- `candidate`: exact immutable subject exists and is undergoing verification
- `verified`: required tests and independent evidence pass for the exact subject
- `production`: verified, authorized, deployed, and read back
- `degraded`: production subject remains available with declared reduced capability
- `hold`: a required current predicate is missing, stale, or failed
- `retired`: no longer active and preserved for lineage

“Phase 0,” “Phase 3,” and similar program labels are historical or program-level
descriptors. They must not be used as universal claims about the entire ecosystem.
Public claims must identify the subject, maturity type, evidence time, and authority.
