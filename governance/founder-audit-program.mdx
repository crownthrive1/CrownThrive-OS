## Purpose

The program creates a recurring macro-to-micro Founder Audit that measures growth, health, drift, sprawl, evidence continuity and institutional readiness without turning audit automation into sovereign authority.

## Windshield boundaries

1. **Horizon:** mission, portfolio, public claims and institutional purpose.
2. **Windshield:** current phase, systemic blockers and cross-system alignment.
3. **Instrument panel:** timestamped metrics, gates, branches, PRs and runtime health.
4. **Engine bay:** schemas, functions, agents, workflows, capabilities and provider adapters.
5. **Black box:** sources, hashes, votes, logs, versions, receipts and rollback evidence.

Evidence may roll upward only with limitations attached. Provider health is not ecosystem PASS. Green CI is not quorum. A recovered title is not a recovered body. A fingerprint is not a DID. A draft PR is not Canon.

## Independence and anti-capture

- One review per independence domain; cloned labels do not create quorum.
- Implementers, their subagents and their evidence transformers cannot certify the same change.
- Visible GitHub authorship through one shared account is not sufficient proof of independent principals.
- Audit agents cannot modify their own privileges, budgets, probation tests or independence labels.
- Scanner failure, stale data, inaccessible sources and missing evidence resolve to `UNKNOWN` or `BLOCKED`, never PASS.
- Machine advice has zero sovereign vote weight unless a later constitutional amendment is validly accepted.

## Probation

Shadow probation lasts at least 30 calendar days and 20 completed cycles, whichever is later. Promotion to active observer requires zero unauthorized writes or secret exposure, zero false critical PASS on seeded controls, reproducible results, at least 95 percent sampled citation validity, at most 10 percent false positives, adversarial failure testing and explicit human governance approval.

There is no automatic path to sovereign voting. A second 30-day period and ten correct high-severity triages are required before an agent can recommend a hold. Hold activation and release remain separate authorized controls.
