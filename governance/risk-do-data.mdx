# Risk, D&O and Data Governance

The current CrownThrive framework set includes **Risk \+ D&O \+ Data**, linking institutional risk controls, leadership protection/governance defensibility, and data-retention/records discipline.

## Risk register

Institutional risks should be recorded by stable ID with category, affected assets, likelihood, impact, indicators, owner, controls, residual risk, mitigation plan, target date, evidence and review cadence.

Representative categories include:

- key-person and succession risk;
- cyber, privacy and credential risk;
- IP and licensing disputes;
- outdated or conflicting public claims;
- payment/chargeback/fraud risk;
- platform and vendor concentration;
- domain or renewal failure;
- third-party-license restrictions;
- content/cultural/identity harm;
- regulatory and contractual exposure;
- cash-flow and capital-allocation risk;
- physical operations, safety and insurance;
- advertising and creator compliance;
- automation/AI errors or unauthorized actions;
- data loss and institutional-memory loss.

## D&O / leadership protection

Directors-and-officers or comparable management-liability coverage, indemnification, decision records, conflict procedures, professional review and clear entity authority should be evaluated as governance matures. Documentation cannot independently establish insurance coverage or legal protection.

## Records and retention

CrownThrive needs a record-class schedule covering contracts, rights, finance/tax, customer/support, employment/contractor, security, product, source code, content masters, marketing consent, impact evidence and governance decisions. Retention periods should reflect actual legal, operational and privacy requirements rather than one global duration.

## Phase 3 implication

Risk class, data classification, retention and approval requirements should become attributes of registries and workflows so agents can identify a high-risk action before executing it.
