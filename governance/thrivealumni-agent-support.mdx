<Warning>
These agents support administrative workflows. They never simulate approval, officeholder authority, human membership, a vote, or quorum. They are not human members, directors, candidates, executives, advisers, investigators, election officials, fiduciaries, or committee officeholders.
</Warning>

## Publicly observed surfaces

The current public navigation identifies Board Members, Candidates, Executive, Advisory, Judicial Oversight and Special Investigations, Nominations and Elections, Compensation and Honorarium, Member Engagement and Ethics, Innovation and Growth, Strategic Partnerships and External Relations, Infrastructure Security and Risk, DEIA, Education Credentialing and Curriculum, and Legacy Faith and Cultural Heritage.

Each surface has at least one scoped support agent in the committee registry. Those agents may prepare evidence, options, packets, checks, and candidate work orders. They cannot vote, satisfy quorum, appoint or remove people, issue sanctions, sign, spend, change compensation, certify an election, issue a credential, contact an external party, or bind CrownThrive.

## Unresolved public evidence

The suite does not infer current rosters, charters, quorum rules, delegation limits, vote records, authority, or identity-dependent recusals. Every committee row marks those checks `NOT_ASSESSED`; the governing evidence needed to perform them remains `NEED_TO_DO`.

Known public drift remains open:

- DEIA appears in current ThriveAlumni navigation but is absent from one recovered committee list.
- Board “ultimate authority” wording requires reconciliation with CrownThrive LLC institutional-authority wording.
- A homepage member count differs from the visible member cards.
- Public CHLOM fingerprints remain described as coming soon.

## Founder and human authority

Founder and human authority controls remain unchanged. This capability pack does not revive historical councils or committees as present legal authority. Removal, appointments, voting, fiduciary decisions, sanctions, budgets, compensation, and binding commitments remain outside machine authority.
