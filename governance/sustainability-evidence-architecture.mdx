# Sustainability and Impact Evidence Architecture

Sustainability is part of CrownThrive's institutional architecture, not a decorative marketing claim. The current CII policy identifies EcoDrive and Stripe Climate as impact/climate rails that may be used when active, while historical platform materials contain more specific claims that require program/date evidence before reuse.

## P-12 Sustainability & Impact Evidence

The July 2026 CHLOM pallet model explicitly defines **P-12 Sustainability & Impact Evidence** around EcoDrive, Stripe Climate, impact evidence, reporting and claims controls.

## Canonical objects

- impact program;
- partner/provider;
- campaign;
- funding source;
- allocation rule;
- eligible transaction/product;
- contribution event;
- receipt/provider evidence;
- unit of impact;
- methodology;
- reporting period;
- public claim;
- verification status;
- correction/supersession event.

## Claim safety

CrownThrive should distinguish:

- amount allocated;
- amount actually transmitted;
- provider-confirmed activity;
- modeled impact;
- verified delivered impact;
- cumulative historical total;
- current active program.

A percentage or environmental outcome appearing in an older page is not automatically a current universal commitment.

## Ecosystem connections

- **CII** — program standards and public accountability.
- **ThriveFund** — internal approved impact/reinvestment allocations.
- **CHLOM** — rules, evidence, source authority and claim audit.
- **CrownLytics** — measurement and reporting.
- **CrownPulse** — only approved impact signals/public proof.
- **Crown Affiliates / AdLuxe** — sustainability messaging must inherit approved claim wording.
- **Melanin Magic / commerce** — product-specific impact claims require their own effective schedule.
- **MM Suites** — location/operator impact can be tracked separately from corporate commitments.

## API and event model

Potential events include:

- `impact.program.created`
- `impact.allocation.approved`
- `impact.contribution.recorded`
- `impact.provider.confirmed`
- `impact.metric.verified`
- `impact.claim.approved`
- `impact.claim.superseded`

Public dashboards should expose only evidence-backed aggregates and clearly state period/methodology.

## Phase 3 requirement

Create a sustainability/impact registry and event schema before automating environmental claims across portfolio sites. This protects CrownThrive from stale percentages, double counting and unsupported cross-brand copying while preserving sustainability as a real part of the Thrive Flywheel.
