## Trademark, Brand, Domain and Handle Protection

CrownThrive uses many names, imprints, frameworks, universes, products and platform identities. Brand protection requires a governed portfolio and evidence—not simply adding `™` everywhere.

## Brand record

```yaml
brand_id: ct.brand.example
canonical_name: Example
aliases: []
owner_org_id: ct.org.crownthrive-llc
brand_type: platform|imprint|framework|product|program|universe
first_use_claimed_at: null
first_commercial_use_claimed_at: null
use_evidence_refs: []
classes_goods_services: []
territories: [US]
clearance_state: unreviewed
filing_state: not_pursued
application_or_registration_refs: []
domains: []
handles: []
license_profiles: []
conflicts: []
watch_state: active
```

## Filing-state vocabulary

`unreviewed → clearance_search → common_law_use → application_draft → filed_pending → office_action → registered → maintained → expired_or_abandoned → not_pursued`

Only a verified filing/registration record permits corresponding public wording. `™` may indicate claimed brand identity; `®` is used only where a current registration supports it and only for the covered goods/services/territory.

## Evidence of use

Preserve dated public pages, product/service offers, invoices/orders, advertisements, packaging, media, domain history, social profiles, contracts/licenses and source materials. Separate internal concept date from first public/commercial use.

## Naming clearance

Before major launch or investment:

- search CrownThrive's internal aliases/successors;
- inspect relevant public trademark/company/domain/social conflicts;
- assess similarity, goods/services, geography and confusion risk;
- verify domain/handle availability and history;
- record counsel/legal review where needed;
- choose canonical name and fallback;
- update the Master Narrative/alias registry.

A web search is not a legal clearance opinion.

## Domains and handles

Every critical domain/handle record includes registrar/provider, owner, account, MFA/recovery, renewal, auto-renew, DNS, SSL, canonical purpose, redirects, social platform, access roles and recovery/export. Use organization-controlled accounts rather than personal credentials where feasible.

Monitor expiration, DNS change, certificate failure, impersonation, typosquatting and unauthorized pages. Preserve redirects and search continuity when names change.

## Brand architecture

Classify parent/sub-brand/imprint/platform/universe/product relationships. Do not let every feature become an unsupported trademark or public brand. Determine whether a name should be:

- institutional framework;
- public platform;
- internal engine;
- imprint;
- universe/series;
- product/service;
- role/program;
- temporary campaign;
- retired/historical alias.

## Licensing

Brand licenses state exact mark/name/trade dress, approved use, goods/services, territory, term, channels, quality controls, attribution/notice, sublicensing, modifications, approval, records, fees, audit, suspension and termination. A license to one mark does not grant CrownThrive's portfolio.

## AI and generated branding

Record human selection/design and source/provenance of AI-assisted logos, artwork, slogans or naming outputs. Conduct rights/clearance review before adoption. Do not assume a provider output is exclusive or registrable.

## Enforcement and impersonation

Maintain evidence, assess actual rights/scope, preserve communications/pages/account records, send platform notices or route to counsel where appropriate, and avoid overclaiming beyond supported rights. Record outcome, corrections and costs.

## Portfolio prioritization

Because filing every name may be uneconomic, prioritize by revenue, distinctiveness, strategic centrality, licensing/franchise use, public exposure, conflict risk and longevity. Protect source/evidence and common-law use even where filing is deferred.

## Phase 3 and beyond

Phase 3 seeds the brand/domain/handle registry and filing/evidence status. Phase 4 enforces consistent use across platform cohorts. Phase 5 supports external brand/imprint/MM Suites licensing and international protection where justified.
