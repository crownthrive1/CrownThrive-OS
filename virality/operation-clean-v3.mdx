## Operation C.L.E.A.N. v3

Operation C.L.E.A.N. is CrownThrive’s cross-asset governance and commercialization framework. It began as a Virality Music catalog discipline and now applies to music, publishing, visual IP, merchandise assets, software, purchased source packages, websites, domains, product files, services, and other governed creative or operational assets.

> **C.L.E.A.N. does not mean delete.** It means the asset’s identity, custody, provenance, canon/version state, rights status, technical state, commercial disposition, evidence, and next lifecycle action are explicit.

## C — Capture & Custody

Preserve the material source before cleanup or commercialization. Every asset should have a stable identity, source/provider reference, custody record, version, origin/acquisition basis, recovery location, and fingerprint when appropriate.

Deletion is not the default. Potential removals enter quarantine after preservation and dependency review.

## L — Label & Link

Normalize canonical names and connect assets to their brand, universe, work/composition family, versions, aliases, creators, rights/provenance records, products, derivatives, providers, dependencies, and evidence lineage.

Titles may change. Canonical identity must not.

## E — Evaluate & Evidence

Evaluate the asset for:

- provenance and chain of title;
- canon and continuity;
- Cultural Imprint Engine alignment;
- CHLOM identity, authority, rights, consent, and provenance status;
- technical quality and accessibility;
- privacy/security exposure;
- audience/performance evidence;
- creative and commercial value;
- distribution, licensing, merchandise, education, or adaptation fit.

A provider listing, payment, fingerprint, file, or database row is evidence. None alone proves ownership or licensing authority.

## A — Assign & Activate

An asset can be routed to the appropriate lane, including official release, direct commerce, licensing candidacy, Go Flipbooks, Backroad FM programming, SoundCloud testing, merchandise development, education, repair, development, vault, archive, canon hold, rights hold, security hold, or removal quarantine.

Activation is lane-specific. A book may be digitally available while print or adaptation rights remain pending. A song may be public for discovery but not cleared for licensing.

## N — Normalize, Nurture & Monetize

Prepare the metadata, artwork, previews, accessibility, fulfillment package, pricing basis, license classification, manifests, analytics identity, campaign hooks, and derivative-rights records required by the approved commercial lane.

After release, approved assets can move through CrownLytics/CrownPulse measurement, AdLuxe promotion, ThrivePush retention, Crown Rewards, Crown Affiliates, CrownFluence, Crown Ambassadors, Backroad FM, Go Flipbooks, and other applicable Thrive Flywheel corridors.

## Current operating boundaries

- **Official music distribution:** deliberately selected releases only; the catalog is not mass-distributed merely because assets exist.
- **Licensing and commercial rights:** CHLOM-governed Good Shit Only (GSO) workflow.
- **Hosted publishing/reader:** Go Flipbooks.
- **Discovery/testing:** SoundCloud and Backroad FM where appropriate.
- **Promotion:** AdLuxe only after product/release eligibility.
- **Melanated Voices:** not a current Operation C.L.E.A.N. licensing or commerce dependency. Older references are historical unless separately reactivated by current governance.

Payment never substitutes for a rights grant.

## Canon doctrine

Operation C.L.E.A.N. treats canon and identity drift as operational defects. Current public-safe doctrine includes:

- **Fact / Meaning / Permission / Protection**
- **Sequence before opinion**
- **Context before extraction**
- **Consent before expansion**
- **Name before nickname**
- **Attribution before interpretation**
- **Correction without erasure**

Unresolved editions or identity conflicts are preserved and surfaced instead of silently collapsed.

## Phone-catalog mastering and package standard

The canonical Virality Music phone-catalog sublane uses a deterministic **one recording → one package** model. Package completion is evaluated independently from downstream entitlement, public listing, and negotiated licensing execution.

### Per-track package contract

Each dependency-ready unique recording is handled with the following invariants:

- preserve the immutable source file identity and source fingerprint;
- anti-join normalized decoded-audio SHA-256 before creating a new canonical recording identity;
- never deduplicate from title similarity alone;
- keep the original source bytes unchanged;
- label WAV/FLAC outputs derived from lossy sources as derived technical masters, never as recovered lossless originals;
- produce and QC the approved audio delivery set: **24-bit/48 kHz FLAC, 24-bit/48 kHz WAV, 24-bit/44.1 kHz WAV, 16-bit/44.1 kHz WAV, 320 kbps MP3, 256 kbps AAC/M4A, and 192 kbps Opus**;
- package an original-source archive, derived-master set, sale-delivery package, checksums, provenance, technical QC, metadata, rights/license matrix, pricing/Stripe specification, and machine manifest;
- maintain one-track-per-package custody; customer delivery archives do not combine unrelated tracks.

Large package archives may be stored as a deterministic multipart Drive object when a provider transfer boundary prevents a single-object upload. Every multipart package must include the complete ordered part set, per-part hashes, the exact reconstructed archive SHA-256, and a reconstruction receipt. Multipart custody is PASS only after provider readback confirms every expected part and the receipt in the canonical per-track folder.

### Standard licensing posture

The general audio-package license mode is **non-exclusive by default**. The current deterministic planning matrix is:

| Standard audio use class | Planning floor |

| Personal download | \$2 |
| Creator / podcast | \$500 |
| Community | \$750 |
| Brand / small-business organic | \$1,500 |
| Film / episodic | \$3,500 |
| Stage / ticketed event | \$5,000 |
| Game / app | \$6,000 |
| Paid advertising | \$8,500 |
| Broadcast / OTT | \$15,000 |
| Catalog package | \$25,000 |
| National / multi-territory advertising | \$30,000 |
| Trailer / major promotion | \$45,000 |
| Adaptation / universe-development entry | \$75,000 |

**Exclusive licensing starts at \$100,000** and is never an automatic buy-now grant. Exclusivity requires a separately scoped written agreement identifying the exact work/version, field of use, media, territory, term, reserved rights, delivery, consideration, approvals, and execution evidence.

The package can be technically complete while authorship, splits, samples, contributor approvals, AI-generation plan/terms, master/composition control, or other work-level evidence remains explicitly unverified. Those conditions belong in the package evidence and representations fields; missing evidence must not be silently converted into clearance.

## Published reusable skill family

Operation C.L.E.A.N. publishes the phone-catalog workflow as four composable CrownThrive skills rather than one opaque monolith:

- `ct.skill.operation-clean.five-track-package-factory.v1` — advances exactly five dependency-ready unique recordings per cycle, including decoded-audio anti-join, seven-format QC, one-track package construction and cursor continuity.
- `ct.skill.audio-rights-pricing-license-matrix.v1` — assigns the standard non-exclusive rights/license/pricing package, explicit evidence states, governed Stripe metadata and the \$100,000 exclusive-license floor that requires a separately scoped written agreement.
- `ct.skill.multipart-drive-package-custody.v1` — converts provider large-file limits into deterministic multipart Drive custody with ordered parts, per-part hashes, reconstruction receipt, reconstructed archive hash and provider readback.
- `ct.skill.governed-pr-terminalization.v1` — classifies, repairs, restacks, merges or closes CLEAN/source/docs PRs under exact-head governance while preserving predecessor history and separating source convergence from provider-publication readback.

The family composes with PentaGreen, PentaPrice, PentaEntitle/PentaCheckout, Evidence and Ledger Projection, DAIL/PentaContext and the Master IP & Asset Registry. A downstream predicate that remains unresolved does not erase PASS state already earned by another skill on the same asset.

## Lifecycle

```text
DISCOVERED
→ INTAKE_VERIFIED
→ CUSTODY_BOUND
→ IDENTITY_ASSIGNED
→ LABELED_LINKED
→ EVIDENCE_REVIEW
→ CANON_REVIEW / NOT_APPLICABLE
→ RIGHTS_REVIEW
→ TECHNICAL_REVIEW
→ COMMERCIAL_ASSIGNMENT
→ REMEDIATION_REQUIRED / PACKAGE_READY
→ INDEPENDENT_CERTIFICATION when required
→ ACTIVATED / DELIVERED / ARCHIVED / HELD
→ MONITORED
→ RECONCILED
```

A HOLD must identify the missing predicate, the current owner, the evidence needed, and the next executable action.
