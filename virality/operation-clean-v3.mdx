## Operation C.L.E.A.N. v3

Operation C.L.E.A.N. is CrownThrive’s cross-asset governance and commercialization framework. It began as a Virality Music catalog discipline and now applies to music, publishing, visual IP, merchandise assets, software, purchased source packages, websites, domains, product files, services, and other governed creative or operational assets.

> **C.L.E.A.N. does not mean delete.** It means the asset’s identity, custody, provenance, canon/version state, rights status, technical state, commercial disposition, evidence, and next lifecycle action are explicit.

## C — Capture & Custody

Preserve the material source before cleanup or commercialization. Every asset should have a stable identity, source/provider reference, custody record, version, origin/acquisition basis, recovery location, and fingerprint when appropriate.

Deletion is not the default. Potential removals enter quarantine after preservation and dependency review.

## L — Label & Link

Normalize canonical names and connect assets to their brand, universe, work/composition family, versions, aliases, creators, rights/provenance records, products, derivatives, providers, dependencies, and evidence lineage.

Titles may change. Canonical identity must not.

## E — Evaluate & Evidence

Evaluate the asset for:

- provenance and chain of title;
- canon and continuity;
- Cultural Imprint Engine alignment;
- CHLOM identity, authority, rights, consent, and provenance status;
- technical quality and accessibility;
- privacy/security exposure;
- audience/performance evidence;
- creative and commercial value;
- distribution, licensing, merchandise, education, or adaptation fit.

A provider listing, payment, fingerprint, file, or database row is evidence. None alone proves ownership or licensing authority.

## A — Assign & Activate

An asset can be routed to the appropriate lane, including official release, direct commerce, licensing candidacy, Go Flipbooks, Backroad FM programming, SoundCloud testing, merchandise development, education, repair, development, vault, archive, canon hold, rights hold, security hold, or removal quarantine.

Activation is lane-specific. A book may be digitally available while print or adaptation rights remain pending. A song may be public for discovery but not cleared for licensing.

## N — Normalize, Nurture & Monetize

Prepare the metadata, artwork, previews, accessibility, fulfillment package, pricing basis, license classification, manifests, analytics identity, campaign hooks, and derivative-rights records required by the approved commercial lane.

After release, approved assets can move through CrownLytics/CrownPulse measurement, AdLuxe promotion, ThrivePush retention, Crown Rewards, Crown Affiliates, CrownFluence, Crown Ambassadors, Backroad FM, Go Flipbooks, and other applicable Thrive Flywheel corridors.

## Current operating boundaries

- **Official music distribution:** deliberately selected releases only; the catalog is not mass-distributed merely because assets exist.
- **Licensing and commercial rights:** CHLOM-governed Good Shit Only (GSO) workflow.
- **Hosted publishing/reader:** Go Flipbooks.
- **Discovery/testing:** SoundCloud and Backroad FM where appropriate.
- **Promotion:** AdLuxe only after product/release eligibility.
- **Melanated Voices:** not a current Operation C.L.E.A.N. licensing or commerce dependency. Older references are historical unless separately reactivated by current governance.

Payment never substitutes for a rights grant.

## Canon doctrine

Operation C.L.E.A.N. treats canon and identity drift as operational defects. Current public-safe doctrine includes:

- **Fact / Meaning / Permission / Protection**
- **Sequence before opinion**
- **Context before extraction**
- **Consent before expansion**
- **Name before nickname**
- **Attribution before interpretation**
- **Correction without erasure**

Unresolved editions or identity conflicts are preserved and surfaced instead of silently collapsed.

## Lifecycle

```text
DISCOVERED
→ INTAKE_VERIFIED
→ CUSTODY_BOUND
→ IDENTITY_ASSIGNED
→ LABELED_LINKED
→ EVIDENCE_REVIEW
→ CANON_REVIEW / NOT_APPLICABLE
→ RIGHTS_REVIEW
→ TECHNICAL_REVIEW
→ COMMERCIAL_ASSIGNMENT
→ REMEDIATION_REQUIRED / PACKAGE_READY
→ INDEPENDENT_CERTIFICATION when required
→ ACTIVATED / DELIVERED / ARCHIVED / HELD
→ MONITORED
→ RECONCILED
```

A HOLD must identify the missing predicate, the current owner, the evidence needed, and the next executable action.
