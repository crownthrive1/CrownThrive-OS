# Virality Canon & Corrections

Virality Music treats canon as governed production data. Names, dates, characters, family branches, editions, portraits, protected identities, relationships, and chronology cannot be changed because a later asset is easier to market.

The governing rule is **correction without erasure**.

## Source hierarchy

For public canon and derivative products, use the strongest current durable evidence in this order:

1. current verified production and governed registry state;
2. approved final editions and their explicit change/correction records;
3. universe bibles and canon sheets;
4. canonical source masters and edition manifests;
5. governed CrownThrive OS / CHLOM / CIE records;
6. project notes only after reconciliation into durable evidence.

A newer file date does not automatically outrank a governed edition.

## What Really Happened

Two distinct Version 2 collector-edition artifacts are currently preserved as controlled variants. They must not be collapsed merely because one has a different page count, timestamp, filename, or file size.

The book establishes a corrected Receipt Night sequence that distinguishes early phone handling and warning events from the later public confrontation point. The correction changes sequence without automatically changing responsibility.

Until the exact canonical-edition adjudication is complete, derivative products and summaries should preserve the variant status instead of inventing one current master.

## The Third Life Had the Wrong Name

This book establishes a major attribution correction:

- **Trent Maddox** and **Terrence Hollis III / Trey** are different people.
- A shared or similar nickname is not identity evidence.
- Portraits, birthdays, testimony, hotel activity, intimate conduct, reward-account evidence, and derivative merchandise must stay attached to the correct person.

The governing doctrine is:

> **Name before nickname. Attribution before interpretation. Correction without erasure.**

Older misattributed derivative assets should be corrected with a visible change record rather than silently rewritten.

## Fact, Meaning, Permission, Protection

TrapOpera repeatedly separates four questions:

- **Fact** — what the record supports;
- **Meaning** — how a narrator or audience interprets it;
- **Permission** — whether material may be used for a particular purpose;
- **Protection** — what must remain restricted even when its existence is acknowledged.

These four columns are not interchangeable.

Additional operating doctrine includes:

- sequence before opinion;
- context before extraction;
- consent before expansion;
- privacy is not erasure;
- a missing fact is not permission to invent one.

## Willie & Maybelle Jenkins Universe

**Willie and Maybelle remain Jenkins publicly.**

Carter, Jenkens, Jenkins, and Knight may appear as governed ancestral or record variants where supported by canon. That historical complexity does not authorize changing Willie or Maybelle’s established public surname.

**Maybelle was Maybelle Knight before Jenkins.** Her premarital history is her own record and must not be retroactively treated as a story about Willie.

**Willie’s first daughter is acknowledged but protected.** Where the approved books protect her name, image, cause of death, descendants, or private biography, derivative products must respect that boundary. Protection is not erasure and is not permission to invent a face or biography.

## Leonard & Clarence

**Leonard Coffield and Clarence Jenkins are different men.**

Leonard is Willie’s lifelong best friend. Clarence is Willie’s cousin. Comedy, dialogue, products, art, music, and adaptation metadata must not collapse their identities or transfer one man’s conduct to the other.

The governed edition also preserves sealed/private centers of earlier family stories. A sealed reference is not an invitation to fill in the missing material.

## Derivative-product rule

Before a character, quotation, portrait, timeline, shirt, poster, book bundle, audio adaptation, licensing package, or screen asset is activated, Operation C.L.E.A.N. should verify that the derivative points to the current correct person, edition, chronology, permission state, and protected boundary.

If the correct answer is unresolved, use a canon hold instead of filling the gap.
