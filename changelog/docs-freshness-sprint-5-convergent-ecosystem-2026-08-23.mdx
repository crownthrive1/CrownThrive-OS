## Pass B — gap closure and continuity

Pass A exposed nine rows that expected unsupported standalone platform registries: seven CrownLytics records and two Melanated TV records.

Rather than inventing new deployment/provider pages, Pass B binds CrownLytics to the current [Platform Registry](/ecosystem/platform-registry) and applicable current legal controls, while Melanated TV binds to the [Media Federation Institutional Registry](/platforms/media-federation-institutional-registry). The fallback count is therefore reduced from nine to zero using existing current authority.

The explicitly confidential Melanin Magic internal-usage guide remains a `restricted_record` candidate. Lyfted Society investor language, CHLOM LEX exchange language and Virality Music treasury/oracle/valuation research remain review-gated and cannot activate securities, token, exchange, settlement or investment authority.
