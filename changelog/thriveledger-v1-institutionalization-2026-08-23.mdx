The first independent integrity audit returned **PASS on all four implemented checks**:

1. evidence-chain continuity;
2. double-entry balance;
3. legal-entity boundary;
4. reversal integrity.

The HOLD is intentional fail-closed behavior. No journal entries were manufactured to force reconciliation to PASS.
