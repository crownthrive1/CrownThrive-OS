## Sales & Email Operations V2 — August 21, 2026

## Purpose

This pass adds a governed sales and email operating lane to CrownThrive, with two primary functions:

1. inbound lead triage, research, scam screening and nurture;
2. researched one-to-one outreach to owners of claimable Locticians profiles.

## Implemented in this pass

### Private CRM runtime

A restricted, non-public CRM schema now contains governed records for:

- prospects;
- source-backed research evidence;
- outreach sequences;
- category-specific templates;
- outbound/reply events;
- restricted email-vault entries;
- suppression;
- email/tag taxonomy;
- outbound-compliance configuration;
- founder-escalation rules.

The schema is RLS-enabled and not granted to ordinary anonymous/authenticated public access. Service/backend workflows remain the intended access path.

### Outreach content system

The runtime contains eight category-specific first-touch Locticians claim templates plus three general follow-up templates.

The default cadence is:

- Day 0;
- business Day 3;
- business Day 7;
- business Day 14, then stop.

### Email triage and risk

The Sales and Lead Desk classifies legitimate leads, routine support/vendor traffic, founder-priority legal/financial/personal/security matters, and scam/phishing risk.

A current Virality Music licensing thread was converted into a private negative-evaluation case after the sender's follow-up combined malformed template text, unanswered normal commercial questions and redirection to an unexplained external form. Public docs preserve the pattern only; private message IDs/content remain in restricted evidence.

### Mailbox organization

A cross-provider tag taxonomy is now governed for:

- P0 founder/legal/financial/personal priority;
- P1 lead/licensing/partnership/press;
- P2 support/vendor operations;
- lead lifecycle;
- Locticians outreach;
- opt-out/suppression;
- scam/phishing/do-not-reply risk.

Provider-specific tag/category activation continues as the taxonomy is used; it is not necessary to rewrite all historical email merely to satisfy a count.

### Scheduled operation

The inbound Sales and Lead Desk runs as a recurring condition watch. A separate weekday Locticians Claim Outreach workflow researches bounded batches, checks duplicate/suppression/risk/claim state and manages the four-touch sequence.

## Hard cold-send gate

**Unsolicited Locticians marketing email is prepared but not active.**

The private outbound configuration intentionally fails closed until a valid CrownThrive physical postal address is verified. The runtime constraint prevents `cold_outreach_enabled=true` while the address field is empty.

Until that gate opens, the workflow may:

- research profiles;
- source lawful public business contact information;
- score/queue prospects;
- prepare category-specific drafts;
- maintain suppression and lifecycle state.

It may **not send cold commercial email**.

This protects the production estate from silently turning a technically working mail connector into a noncompliant marketing system.

## Collab Portal integration state

The existing Collab Portal Secure API adapter remains the verified integration contract for CRM/project data. This pass adds the sales-record handoff model.

Provider documentation also supports CRM Lead/Prospect/Client roles, Email Marketing/drip sequences, Email Cannons and webhooks. Those are provider capabilities and must not be represented as a live CrownThrive API/MCP send path without CrownThrive execution evidence.

No Collab Portal MCP is certified live by this pass. The current verified architecture remains **Secure API \+ governed webhooks \+ provider-native automations**.

## Email visual boundary

The current direct Outlook automated write surface is plain text. Rich image/logo email is therefore not claimed as active through Outlook. Rich HTML outreach should be activated only through a separately certified sender such as the governed Collab Portal/SuiteDash marketing surface, with lawful asset use, suppression and compliance checks.

## Exit criteria for cold-send activation

Cold Locticians email may advance from HOLD only after:

1. CrownThrive's valid physical postal address is entered into the private outbound configuration;
2. sender identity and opt-out copy are verified;
3. suppression behavior passes;
4. a test profile is researched end-to-end without private-data guessing;
5. a low-volume test message renders correctly;
6. provider/CRM event logging and stop conditions are verified;
7. no legal/privacy/security blocker remains.
