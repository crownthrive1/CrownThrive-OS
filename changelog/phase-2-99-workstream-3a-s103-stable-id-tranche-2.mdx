## Purpose

Advance the S103/S100 identity graph only where the current institutional record already distinguishes the asset's durable identity from its provider, deployment and capability state. This tranche resolves the CrownApps/ThriveApps alias family and the high-confidence media graph without turning management labels, domains or vendor capabilities into production claims.

## Scope

This packet assigns or reuses stable institutional IDs for CrownApps/ThriveApps, Melanated Voices, Melanated Voices Platform, Melanated TV, Melanated Voices TV, Locticians TV, Melanated Vault, Melanated Stock, The TAME Gallery and The Artful Mane Gallery. It updates the S103 crosswalk, S100 Holdings identity seed, media federation, D6/platform-state controls, current-state validation queue, Phase 2.99 plan, Phase 3 readiness gate, ten-phase downstream roadmap and deterministic S103 validation.

## Explicit non-goals

This packet does not:

- decide whether CrownApps or ThriveApps becomes the final public canonical brand;
- resolve `MVP (Roku)` into MVP orchestration, Melanated Voices TV or another channel identity;
- certify Streamit Laravel, Viloud, Appy or another provider as currently installed, entitled or production-active for CrownThrive;
- activate APIs, webhooks, repositories, databases, domains, commerce, rights, licensing or distribution;
- alter legal entity, ownership, economic schedule, intellectual-property or customer-rights state;
- loosen Phase 2.99 or Phase 3 hard gates.

## Evidence baseline

The decision is grounded in the exact S103 74-row recovery seed, the S100 68-row Holdings identity seed, the D6 long-tail disposition register, the Media Federation Institutional Registry, the Platform State Register and provider-verification records. Those sources already separate media orchestration, source/audio, OTT/VOD, channel/distribution, archive, stock, aggregation/intelligence and gallery roles. They also explicitly separate provider capability from CrownThrive account/deployment proof.

## Completion and hard exit

This tranche completes when the S103 crosswalk, applicable S100 rows, media federation, D6/platform-state records, current validation queue, Phase 2.99/Phase 3/ten-phase controls and deterministic validator agree on the stable IDs and CI passes.

## Authority and approval

This is a D1 machine-identity normalization from already adopted/governed evidence. It creates no legal, rights, financial, public-branding or irreversible authority. The public CrownApps-versus-ThriveApps branding decision remains an ADR/owner decision; provider activation and rights/economic decisions remain independently governed.

## Rollback and correction

If stronger evidence proves a merge, split, successor or different parent/child relationship, append a correction/alias/successor record. Preserve S100/S103 source-row identities and never recycle a stable ID for an unrelated asset.

## Downstream roadmap impact

- **Phase 3:** seed import can use the stable identities and later founder-resolved terminal dispositions, but capability activation still requires current provider/domain/repository/API/legal/rights/economic evidence; the former 20-row owner queue no longer supplies ambiguity, while unverified capability remains fail-closed.
- **Phase 4:** media federation must preserve Melanated Voices audio, MVP orchestration, Melanated TV OTT/VOD and channel/distribution identities as separate systems of record; stable IDs support routing without collapsing provenance.
- **Phase 5:** identity resolution cannot be treated as commercial availability, current price, subscriber state or revenue proof.
- **Phase 6:** external licensing/developer exposure still requires rights/API authority and effective agreements; stable identity alone is insufficient.
- **Phase 8:** Holdings reconciliation becomes cleaner across S100 and S103 without forcing count equality; the alias/media IDs survive future vendor or branding changes.
- **Phase 10:** continuity exports now preserve durable media/application identities independently from provider accounts, domains and public names.

No downstream hard gate is relaxed.
