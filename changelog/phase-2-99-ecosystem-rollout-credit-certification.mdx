# Phase 2.99 — Ecosystem Rollout and Credit Certification

**Date:** 2026-08-20  
**Disposition:** `public_projection_repaired / commerce_activation_held`  
**Phase:** 2.99 remains current  
**Phase 3:** blocked

## What changed

CrownThrive established issue-backed rollout and credit-commerce preparation programs beneath the institutional roadmap board. A stable non-voting rollout agent coordinates bounded evidence work.

The public packet now separates doctrine from protected implementation:

- public pages expose certification dimensions, lifecycle states, authority ceilings and a digest;
- exact economic calibration, funding tiers, private canary values and internal runtime topology are held in governed private custody;
- the validator fails if protected economics or topology return to the public projection;
- issue `#131` acceptance remains required for the repaired exact artifact.

## Current public state

- stable identity evidence remains partial;
- certification remains partial / HOLD;
- Store Credits remain inactive;
- exact pricing is not authorized and checkout is disabled;
- assets remain `technical_pass_legal_hold` where technical evidence exists but rights or terms remain open;
- live-provider reversal evidence remains incomplete;
- current security findings require private remediation;
- Phase 3 remains blocked.

Detailed counts and evidence bodies are dynamic private-runtime facts, not timeless public certification claims.

## Store Credits boundary

The unified credit rail remains `ct.credit.store.v1` and is publicly defined only as closed-loop, non-transferable, non-cash-redeemable, non-interest-bearing and non-crypto purchasing value.

Exact conversion, tier and pricing choices are no longer published. The public manifest carries only a deterministic digest of the private governed contract.

Technical tests do not establish legal/tax sufficiency, rights clearance, pricing authorization, fulfillment certification, checkout staging or live commerce.

## Asset-release correction

Package integrity, rights, license terms, delivery, commerce rail and support boundaries remain independent facts. Technical package/delivery evidence can coexist with `technical_pass_legal_hold`; it cannot be simplified to PASS or treated as sellable authority.

## Canary correction

Synthetic testing proves only the internal contract under test. Exact values, private delivery references and execution topology are restricted. Live-provider behavior remains a separate evidence requirement where applicable.

## Migration and founder boundaries

Working production payment, entitlement and webhook rails remain intact until replacement behavior is proven. This packet authorizes no destructive cutover.

Virality preserves the founder override that CrownThrive will not use SoundCloud APIs. Official public embed/widget playback remains separate from prohibited API integration.

## Automation and agents

Governed automation may append evidence and remediation plans. Public documentation does not expose internal schedules, tools or queue identifiers.

The rollout agent and its task-scoped children remain non-voting and cannot self-certify originating work, manufacture independent review or create a sovereign vote.

## Promotion blockers

Promotion remains dependent on:

- accepted parent/current-main lineage;
- exact-head CI after this repair;
- accepted issue `#131` disposition;
- independent security, privacy, legal, tax, rights, operations and accessibility evidence as applicable;
- pricing, fulfillment and checkout authority before commerce activation;
- fresh A/B/C/D/S exact-head decisions including mandatory Agent D;
- the Phase 2.99 hard exit and full-documentation-estate gate.

No credential, customer data, private fingerprint, exact economic calibration, restricted evidence or signed delivery material is published by this repaired projection.
