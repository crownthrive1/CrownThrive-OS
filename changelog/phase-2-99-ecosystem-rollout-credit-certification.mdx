## Store Credits

The unified credit rail remains `ct.credit.store.v1` with 100 credits per USD $1 purchasing-power reference and standardized top-up tiers of 1,000, 2,500, 5,000, 10,000 and 25,000 credits.

Technical evidence proves server-side provider readback, append-only credit accounting, idempotent purchases, time-bounded membership issuance, `CTL-*` license issuance, private delivery and synthetic refund reversal.

**Commercial activation remains HOLD.** The credit program is inactive with `legal_tax_state = specialist_review_required_before_live_activation`. Provider tax-code selection does not establish legal/tax sufficiency, and the packaged developer license/terms remain subject to applicable review.

## Asset-release correction

A prior commerce-centric certification pass demoted package rows while waiting for payment/delivery/reversal canaries. The new model keeps these facts separate:

- package integrity;
- rights;
- license terms;
- delivery canary;
- commerce rail;
- support boundary.

The current downloadable packages have technical package/delivery proof but remain `technical_pass_legal_hold` until rights/license-term authority closes. A payment canary cannot erase independently valid package or delivery evidence.

## Private delivery proof

A dedicated internal canary generated time-limited private signed URLs, fetched each current ZIP immediately and compared exact byte length and SHA-256 with the registered asset version. Six of six passed. Signed URLs were not persisted.

## Refund/reversal proof

A synthetic 1,000-credit funding entry was reversed append-only by −1,000 credits, returning the synthetic balance to zero. Replaying the same reversal idempotency key returned `already_processed` and did not double-debit.

This proves the internal reversal contract. Live-provider refund/dispute behavior remains a separate provider canary requirement.

## Legacy-rail migration rule

Existing KJV/Sermon and Virality production Stripe/webhook rails remain intact until their replacement credit/entitlement/delivery/reversal lifecycle is proved. This packet does not authorize synchronized destructive retirement.

Virality continues to preserve the founder override that SoundCloud APIs are removed from the integration architecture.
