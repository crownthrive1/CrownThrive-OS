## Internal-link continuity rule

The Sprint 1 scope is now machine-tested for a minimum of **two distinct internal documentation routes per substantive page**. Pages may exceed that minimum where stronger continuity is useful.

The validator checks the exact 30-page Pass A scope from `data/documentation/sprint-1-scope.v1.json`. It does not count external links or decorative text as continuity.

## Article rebuild relationship

The **795-title recovered Help Center estate** remains a separate forensic inventory from the current Mintlify page count. This framework does not bulk-generate plausible prose to hit 795.

Each title is rebuilt through:

```text
source recovery
→ authority classification
→ stable identity mapping
→ current/historical/target reconciliation
→ substantive article or explicit non-article disposition
→ continuity links
→ validation
→ versioned publication
→ changelog + machine-seed delta
```

The detailed working states normalize to six terminal classes: `canonical_article`, `merged_successor`, `permanent_redirect`, `restricted_record`, `superseded_history`, or `unresolved_source`.
