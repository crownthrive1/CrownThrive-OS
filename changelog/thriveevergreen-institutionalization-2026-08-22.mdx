## ThriveEvergreen Institutionalization — 2026-08-22

**Program state:** Phase 2 institutional specification/reconciliation<br />**Implementation state:** specification adopted; production runtime remains gated

## Decision

CrownThrive institutionalizes **ThriveEvergreen™** as the mandatory commerce and economic-activation authority across the convergent ecosystem.

```text
asset → rights → product → SKU → license → price → channel → listing
→ advertising → checkout/payment → entitlement → fulfillment
→ revenue routing → refund/dispute → reconciliation → optimization → preservation
```

## CHLOM Metaprotocol

ThriveEvergreen becomes a cross-pallet commerce/economic-execution subprotocol of CHLOM rather than a separate P-13 silo. Its strongest bindings are P-03 Rights & Licensing, P-05 Growth/Advertising/Measurement, P-06 Commerce/Booking/Loyalty, P-10 Operations/Institutional Memory and P-11 Capital/Revenue/Settlement, with other pallets participating by product/corridor context.

The machine activation contract is the **Evergreen Commerce Activation Certificate (`ECAC`)**:

```text
commercial candidate → ThriveEvergreen → ECAC | HOLD | DENY
```

## AdLuxe institutional role

ThriveEvergreen is the commercial/economic operating authority for:

- **AdLuxe Network** — inventory/package economics, pricing/rates/budgets, advertiser payments, invoices/credits, publisher/creator/location earnings, settlement and reconciliation;
- **AdLuxe Studio** — product/offer creative binding, rights/license version, production pricing and commercial-release certification;
- **AdLuxe Places** — place/screen inventory assetization, rate cards/packages, location economics, proof reconciliation and settlement.

AdLuxe remains the specialist advertising layer for creative, campaign, inventory, trafficking, delivery and measurement. Providers such as Adserver.Online or signage software remain replaceable implementation systems.

Advertising inventory itself is now defined as a possible evergreen commercial asset family.

## Autonomous economic doctrine

> **Execute inside signed authority; reconcile immediately afterward; roll back or compensate when evidence fails.**

ThriveEvergreen may execute reversible or compensatable commerce/financial/advertising actions inside its signed authority envelope without synchronous founder intervention. It must perform provider readback, ledger recording and independent reconciliation after execution.

## Supervision

- Top-Level Charter Agent — charter and authority interpretation;
- `ct.agent.agent-a-supervisor` — independent veto, drift detection, quarantine and re-certification;
- CHLOM Rights, Economic, Legal Policy, Security and AdLuxe specialists — domain verdicts;
- independent Commerce Certifier — acceptance evidence.

The Phase 2.99 hourly relay's label `Agent A Orchestrator` is a separate coordination namespace and does not automatically inherit the constitutional supervisor's authority.

## SKU and commerce rules

ThriveEvergreen is the exclusive institutional SKU issuer. SKUs are not reused. Store/payment/provider identifiers remain aliases.

No certified CrownThrive storefront, marketplace, payment adapter, booking system or AdLuxe surface may bypass ThriveEvergreen in the target architecture.

## Adserver evidence classification

Current CrownThrive Adserver.Online account/subscription state is retained as founder-attested operational evidence. Exact plan pricing, quotas/capacity/consumption, billing periods, balances and payment details remain restricted. API eligibility/scopes, deployment mapping and production delivery health still require capability-specific certification.

**Phase 2:** specification, charter, threat model, object/SKU/ECAC ontology, metaprotocol and AdLuxe relationships.

**Phase 3:** executable registry/policy/ledger/reconciliation services, CHLOM/CIE bridges, API/MCP manifests, Stripe/payment and AdLuxe sandbox/canary adapters, capability-scoped credentials and adversarial containment tests.

## Production certification rule

Documentation adoption is not production proof. Certification must attempt to bypass rights, ECAC, SKU uniqueness, price/ad-budget authority, payout controls, credential scopes, audit, entitlements, AdLuxe billing/settlement, sensitive targeting, agent spawning and charter immutability and prove prohibited operations fail.
