## Documentation Wave 7 Quad-Lane Throughput — 2026-08-25

CrownThrive documentation rebuild capacity is extended from a single generalized next-wave posture to **four independently governed work lanes**: substantive successor verification, specialist evidence, historical body recovery, and terminal disposition preparation.

The change begins from the verified Wave 6 baseline of **61 machine-qualified P0 identities and 388 pending P0 identities**. It intentionally does not change those counts. Queue architecture is not counted as substantive completion.

## Quality preservation

The throughput multiplier changes concurrency, not proof standards. Provenance, D2 boundaries, specialist review, parent certification, deduplication, and terminal acceptance separation remain mandatory.
