## Key governance correction

The S100 source mixes management labels such as `Active`, `Core`, `Live`, `Build`, `Revamp`, `Reserve`, `Retired`, `Future Build`, `Planned` and `Recovery Target` with source-recorded license/support fields.

Those are **not** current operational certification.

### `CT-CON-037`

Source management state is preserved independently from provider entitlement, installed version, account state, repository, configuration, API access and production behavior.

### `CT-CON-038`

License and support fields are separated from each other and from deployment state. `Expired` support does not automatically invalidate a license; a recorded license does not prove current entitlement or safe production use; `Listing removed` and `Service failed` are continuity/provider-risk evidence without erasing source lineage.
