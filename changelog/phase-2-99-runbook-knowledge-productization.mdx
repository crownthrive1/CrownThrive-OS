## Protection boundary

This checkpoint does not publish or commercialize secret values, credential fingerprints, private evidence bodies, customer/partner records, personal-case context, unrestricted sovereign-agent prompts, proprietary security findings, private eval corpora, trade-secret calibration, or patent-candidate mechanisms before review.

## Commerce state

The runbooks and eBook are **packaged candidates**. This checkpoint does not authorize an exact price, package license, Stripe Product/Price, checkout, customer entitlement, or live sale. Those remain downstream governed gates.

## Phase boundary

This work is additive Phase 2.99 packaging/protection evidence. It does not authorize Phase 3 entry and does not convert the commercialization catalog into current production or general availability.
