## Phase 3 entry decision

**Controlled Phase 3 engineering is ready to begin after this PR is merged.**

That does not mean CrownThrive is ready for unrestricted autonomous execution. Phase 3 must begin with Sprint 0 controls, then build DAIL, approvals, rights and registry services against the versioned institutional baseline.

## Permanent rule

CrownThrive should never again treat a live website, SaaS account, chat history, single repository, or individual person's memory as the sole source of institutional truth. The institution must remain reconstructable from governed, versioned and portable records.
