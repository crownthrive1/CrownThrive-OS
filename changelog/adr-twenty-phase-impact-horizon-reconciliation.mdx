# ADR — Twenty-Phase Impact Horizon Reconciliation

## Decision

Extend CrownThrive's **roadmap-impact horizon** through Phase 20 while preserving the accepted named Phase 1–10 program and all historical roadmap generations.

The current evidence available to this run does not establish authoritative names, missions or gates for Phases 11–20. Those slots are therefore reserved and machine-addressable, but not semantically invented.

## Why

CrownThrive's current documentation, platform, CHLOM, automation, proprietary-asset, commerce and infrastructure work is creating long-horizon dependencies faster than the older roadmap artifacts captured them. Future-impact preservation must therefore extend beyond the currently named phase set.

This decision ensures that work completed during Phase 2.99 can leave structured downstream notes for later phases without pretending those later phases are already defined or active.

## Required behavior

Every material sprint, reconciliation, factory package, schema migration, phase-gate update or institutionalization packet must now carry a `phase_impact_1_20` vector.

Phases 1–10 use their accepted names and gates. Phases 11–20 accept only:

- `reserved_definition_required`;
- `possible_future_impact` with a structured note;
- later adopted definition references after an authoritative ADR/source exists.

## Non-retroactivity

This ADR does not rename or invalidate the [Ten-Phase Institutional Program Charter](/standards/ten-phase-institutional-program-charter). It adds a future-impact layer. Historical five-phase and ten-phase records remain preserved with their original effective context.

## Relationship to Phase 3

This extension does not unlock Phase 3. It improves the handoff quality by ensuring Phase 3 machine seeds and future service contracts do not erase known long-horizon dependencies.

See the [Phase 3 Readiness Gate](/technology/phase-3-readiness-gate), [Documentation Reconciliation & Continuity Framework](/standards/documentation-reconciliation-continuity-framework), and [Twenty-Phase Horizon Reconciliation](/standards/twenty-phase-horizon-reconciliation).

## Correction rule

When authoritative definitions for Phases 11–20 are recovered or adopted, publish a new ADR or versioned amendment that maps the reserved slots to their accepted definitions. Do not rewrite this decision to make the earlier uncertainty disappear.
