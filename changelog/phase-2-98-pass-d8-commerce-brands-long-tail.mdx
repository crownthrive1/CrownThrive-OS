## Phase 2.98 Pass D8 — Commerce & Brands Long-Tail

## Checkpoint

- **Baseline:** merged Phase 2.98 Pass D7
- **Repository scope:** `crownthrive1/CrownThrive-OS` only
- **Phase:** 2.98
- **Purpose:** prevent long-tail commerce brands from being lost, falsely activated or collapsed into GSO while preparing machine-readable Phase 2.99 seeds.

## Added institutional coverage

Created the [Commerce & Brands Long-Tail Institutional Registry](/platforms/commerce-brands-long-tail-institutional-registry) for:

- The L Market;
- ThriveWick;
- Tears of Defeat;
- ThriveThreads;
- ThriveSip;
- XENThrive / Get Kinetic!;
- MusiqHead Audio.

The pass preserves these as distinct assets/lineage records while explicitly gating current storefront, inventory, price, manufacturing, fulfillment, API and legal claims.

## Governing corrections

- A brand/domain/script/mockup is not a live commerce product.
- GSO may become a commerce surface without becoming owner of every brand or underlying right.
- XENThrive is preserved as historical commerce/IP lineage and recovery source rather than reactivated by implication.
- Product rights, product entitlement and media/content licenses remain separate linked objects.
- Physical, POD, private-label, wholesale, digital and licensed products require separate fulfillment/economic evidence.
- Supplier/provider capability does not prove CrownThrive's current account, contract, inventory or commercial eligibility.

## Phase 2.99 inheritance

Seed stable brand/product/SKU/source/rights/provider/offer/price/inventory/order/fulfillment/refund/support records and explicit lifecycle states for every retained commerce asset. Historical XENThrive records must retain source/version/successor relationships.

## Phase 3 inheritance

The common institutional core should provide reusable product, offer, rights, evidence, event and documentation-impact contracts. No Phase 3 service should silently promote an `incubate`, `reserve`, `historical` or `unverified` brand into active commerce.

## Open work

Current domains, catalogs, providers, costs, pricing, margins, fulfillment, warranties/returns, APIs, customer/order history, analytics, backups and vendor-exit state remain subject to evidence-backed verification.

## Completion rule

D8 is complete for Phase 2.98 when each named brand has a stable identity, present evidence classification, activation gate, Phase 2.99 seed contract and explicit unresolved queue without invented production state.
