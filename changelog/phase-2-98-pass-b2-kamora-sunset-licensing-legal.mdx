## AI-assisted/generated-content expansion

Created **AI-Assisted and Generated Content: Human Authorship, Rights and Disclosure Standard**.

The standard makes four independent questions mandatory:

1. provider commercial permission;
2. statutory copyrightability/human authorship;
3. ownership/control of each right layer;
4. downstream customer/licensee permission.

It incorporates current U.S. Copyright Office AI guidance, work-level Human Contribution Records, provider/account-tier/terms snapshots, source-input authority, version lineage, synthetic voice/likeness controls, training/dataset restrictions, registration disclosure, licensing qualifiers and consumer-protection rules.

Current Suno-specific provider terms are treated as a dated overlay rather than a permanent universal rule. Paid-tier commercial/provider rights do not become a CrownThrive representation that copyright automatically exists.

## Cross-platform disclaimer expansion

Created **Cross-Platform Disclaimer and Disclosure Matrix** covering:

- general information / no professional advice;
- access/purchase versus license scope;
- copyright/trademark/registration status;
- AI-assisted/generated content;
- user-upload/source authority;
- third-party provider/non-affiliation;
- forecasts/business outcomes;
- no securities/franchise/regulated offer by implication;
- AI/automated decisions;
- advertising/sponsorship/affiliate/endorsement disclosure;
- media fiction/testimony/allegation labeling;
- ministry/Scripture/AI boundaries;
- beauty/wellness and medical-claim boundaries;
- education/certificate boundaries;
- marketplace/provider neutrality;
- commerce pre-sale disclosure;
- API/developer limits;
- platform-specific application matrix.

Institutional rule: a disclaimer clarifies a true boundary; it does not cure an unsupported or deceptive statement.
