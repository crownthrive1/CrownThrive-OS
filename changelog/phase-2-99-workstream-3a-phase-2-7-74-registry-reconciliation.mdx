## Purpose

Recover the exact source-row universe behind the Phase 2.7 statement that the July 2026 CrownThrive/CHLOM registry contained **74 platforms/frameworks**, while preserving same-day and later registry-version conflicts instead of treating 74 as a timeless current platform count.

## Scope

This packet:

- registers the later July 29, 2026 v1.0 Platform/Pallet Registry snapshot as `S103`;
- registers the later expanded v1.1 registry as `S104` lineage evidence;
- recovers source indexes 0–73 as `S103-PF-001` through `S103-PF-074`;
- pins the S103 Executive Summary distribution P0=4, P1=20, P2=31, P3=14, P4=4, P5=1;
- preserves an earlier same-name v1.0 snapshot whose Executive Summary reports 60;
- creates `CT-CON-040` to prevent version/count collapse;
- updates count, metrics, public-claim, Phase 2.99 and Phase 3 current-state controls;
- prepares deterministic validation for the source-row identity census.

- declare all 74 rows current platforms;
- normalize source names in-place;
