## Evidence boundary

D6 uses Phase 2.7's explicit unresolved-platform list, later founder adjudications, Holdings domain/engine registry snapshots and existing current institutional registries. An active domain or purchased/expired script remains evidence of asset/lineage, not automatic proof of a current production application.

## Phase 2.99 inheritance

Every long-tail record must enter the machine seed with stable ID, aliases, predecessor/successor relationships, disposition, implementation state, source/domain/engine references, archive/redirect rules, rights custody, reactivation authority and article/changelog mappings. Unknown rows remain machine-readable unknowns.

## Institutional control updates

D6 also updates:

- source and recovery coverage registers;
- extended portfolio and platform-state registers;
- legacy/alias/successor relationships;
- current-state validation and public-correction queues;
- Legal Depot lifecycle/reactivation treatment;
- Phase 2.98, Phase 2.99 and Phase 3 gates;
- articleization/navigation completeness requirements;
- the Long-Tail Disposition and Lineage Agent;
- work-queue classes and adversarial evaluation fixtures.

The pass therefore treats long-tail status as enforceable institutional state rather than a footnote in an old portfolio list.

## Completion rule

D6 does not force every recovered name to become an active platform. It closes what can be closed from evidence, preserves what must remain unresolved, and makes navigation completeness itself a governed validation concern.
