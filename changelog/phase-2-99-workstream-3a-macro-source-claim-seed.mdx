## Contradictions added/resolved

### `CT-CON-032` — MM Suites identity

Recovered Framework OS Doctrine uses `MM Suites` generically for strategic suite groupings. Canonical resolution keeps **MM Suites / Melanin Magic Suites** as the specific beauty/wellness phygital, Suite Pro, location and franchise/regional-license architecture. Generic reusable groupings use capability-pallet/suite/bundle identities.

### `CT-CON-033` — linear hierarchy versus typed architecture

The recovered string `Convergent Ecosystem > CIE > Thrive Flywheel > CHLOM > MM Suites > Corridors > Platforms` is preserved as an ordered governance/application shorthand. It does not create legal ownership, corporate control or database containment. Machine relationships remain typed and cross-cutting.

## Source-authority hardening

The Source Authority Hierarchy now distinguishes:

- a source **declaring itself authoritative**; and
- the source's **effective institutional authority for a specific current claim**.

A manual, handbook, prospectus or paper calling itself authoritative, final, constitutional or a single source of truth does not bypass later founder adjudication, current legal/transactional/operational evidence, accepted ADRs or correction history.
