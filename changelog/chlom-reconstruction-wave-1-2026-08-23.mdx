## Legacy system treatment

Wave 1 preserves XENThrive/Get Kinetic! as historical commerce/brand lineage unless reactivated, ThriveCafe as retired history, Melanin Magic Cafe as separate imprint lineage, ThriveApps/CrownApps alias handling, Studio-family context separation and MVP namespace separation.

## Founder adjudication model

A source-backed article may be written before every contradiction is personally resolved when the conflict is clearly labeled. The Founder may later adjudicate current canonical meaning. The decision creates a dated correction/supersession record and propagates to affected terminology, source, platform, article, changelog and machine records without erasing history.

## Continuity rule

```text
source discovery
→ body reconstruction
→ conflict marking
→ current/historical separation
→ publication
→ founder adjudication when needed
→ correction propagation
→ continuity register
```

No recovered title is counted as a fully reconstructed body merely because it exists in the 795-title census.

## Phase effect

This wave improves documentation completeness and source lineage. It does **not** by itself advance Phase 3, activate historical blockchain/token/DAO architecture, certify legal rights, activate commerce or convert research systems into production.
