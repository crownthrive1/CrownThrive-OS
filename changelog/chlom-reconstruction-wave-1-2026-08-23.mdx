## Founder adjudication model

The recovery program now has an explicit **after-the-fact founder adjudication** model.

A source-backed article may be written before every contradiction is personally resolved when the conflict is clearly labeled. The Founder may later adjudicate the current canonical meaning. That decision creates a dated correction/supersession record and propagates to all affected terminology, source, platform, article, changelog and machine records without erasing history.

## Continuity rule

Future waves inherit the same contract:

```text
source discovery
→ body reconstruction
→ conflict marking
→ current/historical separation
→ publication
→ founder adjudication when needed
→ correction propagation
→ continuity register
```

No recovered title is counted as a fully reconstructed body merely because it exists in the 795-title census.

## Phase effect

This wave improves institutional documentation completeness and source lineage. It does **not** by itself advance Phase 3, activate historical blockchain/token/DAO architecture, certify legal rights, activate commerce, or convert research systems into production.
