## Knowledge-preservation hard rule

The refactor extension does **not** make cleanup destructive.

Automation may not silently delete branches, files, pages, PR history, Canon, Doctrine, evidence, prior current states, or institutional decisions. It may not force-push or silently close PRs. Supersession requires predecessor lineage plus a successor or explicit reason. Material current-page changes retain a version/changelog trail.

Refactoring therefore means **redirect attention, update current owners, stack, extract unique deltas, supersede with lineage, and preserve history**.

## Attention rotation

Controlled-test thresholds:

- 20+ open PRs, or
- 8+ stale main-based PRs, or
- 8+ material `CT-COLL-2+` collisions

cause `CONVERGENCE` mode, which allocates at least 70% of final-review attention to closure/canonicalization while allowing research to continue without promotion pressure.

`INCIDENT` requires an explicit critical-security or founder-priority signal; arbitrary descriptive prose cannot trigger it.

Whole-estate refactor order is:

`canonical main/merge perimeter → Phase 2.99 hard exit/full docs → current-truth docs/source registry → runtime/API/MCP/provider state → agent/framework/federation state → product/platform verticals → future R&D`.
