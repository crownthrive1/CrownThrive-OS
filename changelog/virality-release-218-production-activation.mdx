Release the eight governed companion products and six original wall-art products only after exact protected packages, Stripe mappings, D1 fulfillment rows and unpaid Checkout Session preflights pass. Keep all unrelated gated and non-retail records fail-closed.

Release 218 adds **14 paid products** to the release-214 baseline:

- 4 printable workbook companions;
- 4 editable Canva-ready companion packages;
- 6 original 300-PPI wall-art collections.

The fourteen packages total **40,425,121 bytes**. Every exact SKU/version has one verified protected ZIP, an active live-mode Stripe Product and one-time USD Price, an available D1 commerce row, an active product version and one ready fulfillment file.

## Checkout and delivery verification

Each of the fourteen exact SKUs returned HTTP 201 with a Stripe-hosted checkout URL. D1 independently recorded one pending, unpaid checkout intent for every exact SKU/version. No customer payment was made during this preflight.

The existing shared transaction rail remains responsible for signed-webhook confirmation, account entitlement, account-bound personal license keys, expiring protected downloads, delivery limits, refunds, disputes and revocation. An unpaid checkout preflight does not claim fourteen completed customer purchases.

## Edition-commerce crosswalk

Production D1 now contains **13 linked edition-commerce rows across 12 publishing works**. The 227-page _What Really Happened: Four-Voice Archive_ and the 231-page _Correction Cut_ remain separate edition/SKU mappings under one work.

Legacy source, canon, print, portrait, provider-reader and GSO fields remain unchanged. Retail availability is derived additively from the exact commerce link, product/version state, rights/artifact state and ready-file count.

## Storefront and admin changes

- all fourteen new product pages render publicly with exact titles and checkout controls;
- the SUPER\_ADMIN Edition Commerce view exposes the edition/SKU crosswalk behind server authorization and fresh MFA;
- AdLuxe zones on radio and jukebox surfaces wait for an actual house creative or external fill instead of leaving an empty ad box;
- the temporary upload/finalization route and its production secret were removed after verification.

## Remaining boundary

The **25 AI/music publications** remain gated pending human editorial/legal review, accessibility/native-device verification, protected fulfillment and exact-product checkout/delivery testing. Other provider-specific, inquiry, licensing, blocked and retired records also remain non-retail. Stripe catalog presence alone is never activation proof.
