CrownThrive now has an institutional shared-service architecture instead of requiring every platform to independently rebuild identity, authentication, authorization, billing, licensing, analytics, notifications, CRM, ticketing, search, commerce, routing, rewards and documentation.

## Truth boundaries

- provider capability is not CrownThrive deployment;
- provider success is not CrownThrive economic, licensing, entitlement, reward or settlement truth;
- readiness and control coverage are not legal or professional certification;
- a contract or route plan does not inherit provider-write authority;
- a configured provider is not automatically a certified shared service;
- candidate SLO targets are not customer SLAs;
- derived package candidates do not change the authoritative source-asset count.

## Current holds

No lane is silently declared `shared_service_active`. Current controls still require current evidence, independent verification, negative tests, provider readback, continuity evidence, and operation-specific write canaries where applicable.

The plugin remains uninstalled, unsubmitted, unpublished and commercially inactive. Provider writes, automatic failover, checkout, entitlements, D3 automation, sovereign votes and direct-main merge remain disabled.

## History

Every contract, provider binding, control, route, asset projection, kernel, run, receipt and certification is append-or-supersede. Failed evidence and prior provider states remain preserved.
