## Architecture decision records

Architecture Decision Records preserve why a material decision was made, which alternatives were rejected, and what future operators must understand before changing it.

## When an ADR is required

Create an ADR for decisions involving:

- system-of-record selection
- platform ownership or corridor changes
- commerce-rail assignment
- authentication and authorization
- data classification or retention
- payment and entitlement architecture
- rights, licensing, canon, or provenance architecture
- AI tooling and human approval
- public/private documentation boundaries
- major integrations
- event schemas
- irreversible migrations
- security controls
- deployment and rollback strategy

## ADR format

```markdown
# ADR-0001: Use Stripe webhooks as the fulfillment source of truth

Status: Accepted
Date: 2026-08-17
Owners: Commerce and Platform Operations

## Context
What problem or ambiguity required a decision?

## Decision
What is the approved architecture or rule?

## Alternatives considered
What other approaches were evaluated?

## Consequences
What becomes easier, harder, required, or prohibited?

## Security, privacy, rights, and operational impact
What control or risk changes?

## Implementation and migration
How will the decision be applied safely?

## Verification
What evidence proves the decision is implemented?

## Revisit conditions
What facts or changes would justify reviewing the decision?
```

## Initial Phase 1 decisions

### Public PentaDocs is not the private operating repository

Public documentation explains the system without storing secrets, customer data, protected source assets, private rights records, or confidential implementation details.

### The private registry is the machine-readable source of truth

Phase 2.99 Workstream 1 adopts a federated repository topology with a governed private core. `repo.crownthrive.os` is the logical identity for the private institutional schemas/registries/machine-source boundary, while CHLOM services, adapters/MCPs/SDKs, infrastructure-as-code and private evaluation fixtures receive separate logical repository identities unless a later ADR justifies governed consolidation.

These logical identities are architecture targets, not claims that unconnected repositories already exist. The currently connected `crownthrive1/CrownThrive-OS` repository remains the public-safe institutional/PentaDocs projection (currently rendered through Mintlify) and must not become the store for secrets, raw restricted evidence, customer records or protected source masters.

### Specialized commerce rails remain separate

Stripe Direct, Good Shit Only, Go Flipbooks, subscriptions, services, credits, and Stripe Connect serve different transaction and rights models.

### Signed processor events govern payment fulfillment

A success-page redirect does not create a payment entitlement. Signed, verified, idempotently processed events govern fulfillment.

### Creation and distribution are separate

Virality Vault may contain the full creative inventory. The Distribution Catalog contains only deliberately selected and governed external releases.

### Centralized institutional control with partitioned operational data

Phase 2.99 Workstream 2 selects a portable PostgreSQL-compatible institutional registry for stable identities, authority, versions and institutional relationships; domain-owned operational stores for application state; a separately controlled restricted evidence vault; classified pre-retrieval search; and DAIL/event/workflow contracts that remain portable across providers.

Shared ownership does not authorize unrestricted cross-platform data movement. Cross-domain flows require purpose, fields, authority, retention, failure/replay behavior and policy references. Search indexes, caches and embeddings remain projections rather than sources of truth.

### Agents remain subordinate to registries and approvals

Agent capability does not create authority. Permissions, evidence, approvals, and audit records control production actions.

### Corrections append rather than silently overwrite

Material corrections preserve prior versions, identify affected systems, and create a change record.

## ADR states

- Proposed
- Accepted
- Accepted with conditions
- Superseded
- Deprecated
- Rejected

A superseded ADR should point to its replacement rather than disappearing.

## Review ownership

ADR approval should include the appropriate combination of:

- founder or governing owner
- platform owner
- engineering
- privacy and security
- rights and governance
- commerce or finance
- publishing or content operations

## Public and private ADRs

Public ADRs explain architecture without exposing sensitive implementation details.

Private ADRs may contain protected diagrams, vendor details, migration specifics, security controls, and confidential business information.

<Warning>
  Do not use an ADR to retroactively justify an undocumented production change. Record material decisions before implementation whenever possible.
</Warning>
