# Virality Music Credit-Only Storefront Checkpoint

**Checkpoint date:** August 20, 2026  
**Storefront:** [vm.crownthrive.com](https://vm.crownthrive.com)  
**Status:** production checkpoint deployed from the verified artifact as Sites version 220; Drive fulfillment remains held pending exact parity and private service identity

## Commerce decision

Virality Music catalog purchases use internal **Virality Credits** as their only storefront tender.

Stripe remains the external funding rail for credit topups. A signed Stripe topup event can fund the member ledger, but it does not directly authorize a catalog entitlement. Catalog fulfillment requires a server-resolved SKU and credit price, an atomic ledger debit, an order, and the corresponding entitlement and license record.

Direct Stripe product checkout is retired from the Virality Music catalog-purchase path. Historical payment records and separately governed creator, subscription, or marketplace flows remain preserved.

## Verified credit workflow

The source-controlled integration suite exercised the lifecycle with a synthetic member in an ephemeral D1 test database and stubbed Stripe fixtures:

1. create a $50 topup checkout for 200 purchased credits and 20 promotional credits;
2. process signed completion and replay events idempotently;
3. post a 220-credit balance once;
4. redeem 24 credits for SKU `VM-BR-001`;
5. use the promotional bucket before the purchased bucket;
6. create the order, entitlement, and signed personal-license record;
7. reconcile partial and remainder topup refunds without double posting.

The test passed without charging a live payment method or granting credits to a live person. A live customer grant requires an exact verified recipient and authenticated operator approval.

## Master custody and delivery boundary

The current private Google Drive custody revision contains **236 locally available masters** totaling **147,316,774 source bytes**. The export is split into two book volumes and one printables/templates volume, with a manifest, exact checksums, parity report, integrity tests, and a final custody receipt.

All uploaded custody files were read back as owner-only and not shared. Public Drive links are prohibited.

Google Drive is not yet the active storefront delivery plane. The protected delivery registry contains **144 R2-only exact assets** totaling **424,676,170 bytes** that do not yet have local byte-for-byte matches in the Drive export. Entitlement-gated R2 delivery therefore remains active while Drive-hosted fulfillment stays on **HOLD**.

Drive delivery requires all of the following before activation:

- exact export and checksum parity for every protected object;
- a private server-side OAuth or service-account identity;
- entitlement-gated file resolution;
- download, refund, dispute, and revocation tests;
- permission and readback verification without public sharing.

## THIVEBASE boundary

Supabase/THIVEBASE remains a separate, fail-closed institutional lane. Its credit program is inactive, legal/tax review is pending, and the connected schema does not currently expose an authenticated customer credit route.

Synthetic ledger evidence is test evidence only. It does not authorize production activation, a customer grant, or a change of transaction authority. Any later promotion requires an isolated development branch, Stripe test mode, a purpose-built synthetic Auth user, access-policy review, reconciliation, and governed production acceptance.

## Rights routing

Broader commercial rights route canonically through [CHLOM](https://vm.crownthrive.com/chlom-licensing). The legacy `/gso-licensing` and `/licensing-sync` URLs remain permanent compatibility redirects.

A credit catalog purchase grants only the rights attached to its recorded entitlement. It does not imply adaptation, resale, synchronization, training-data, master-use, manufacturing, or other broader rights.

## Security and governance boundary

Public documentation and GitHub history must not contain:

- API keys or provider credentials;
- webhook or signing secrets;
- private Drive IDs or storage object keys;
- signed delivery URLs;
- customer records;
- raw entitlement or ledger data;
- production authentication material.

Credentials remain runtime-only. Documentation changes remain reviewable until collision checks, automated checks, and the applicable governance approval complete.
