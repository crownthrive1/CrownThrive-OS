# Founder Continuity Layer v1

**Date:** 2026-08-21  
**Public projection state:** governed candidate pending normal repository acceptance  
**Restricted runtime state:** active controlled plane in THIVEBASE

## Decision

CrownThrive introduced the CHLOM Founder Continuity Layer to preserve operational continuity for bounded, reversible `D0`–`D2` / `A0`–`A3` work while keeping `D3` / `A4` authority human-reserved.

The founder directive establishes a 60-minute response window for an override request. Elapsed time does **not** create permission. The authority source is the standing delegation adopted before the individual request. After the response window, the runtime may issue a surrogate authorization only when the action is evidence-supported as **positive and needed** and the current policy's independent-review, evidence, safeguards, exact-snapshot and rollback requirements all pass. Optional, unknown, neutral or negative value/necessity states are not surrogate-eligible.

## Signature and identity boundary

The runtime artifact is labeled `CHLOM_SURROGATE_ATTESTATION`. It is an agent-attributed authorization receipt and must never be represented as the founder's human signature, typed-name attestation or personal act. It does not transfer office, fiduciary responsibility, ownership or CrownThrive sovereign governance authority.

A later explicit founder approval, denial or cancellation supersedes the surrogate result. Revocation, policy pause, stale evidence or failed execution can require rollback or compensation.

## Reserved authority

The layer does not autonomously authorize legal filings, binding contracts, final rights/ownership grants, material money movement, payout-destination changes, credential/secret operations, identity recovery, destructive security changes, irreversible deletion or external human signatures.

## Runtime controls added

The restricted control plane now maintains versioned policy, request, vote, attestation and signal records; exact-snapshot digests; independent reviewer eligibility; positive-and-needed evidence gates; DAIL audit events; expiring surrogate attestations; a fail-closed pause control; execution-state evidence; and rollback triggers.

Security-significant policy fields are version-immutable. Exact request snapshots, vote payloads, attestation payloads and signal payloads are protected by database guards. A surrogate attestation cannot be inserted unless the founder window has elapsed, the request remains policy-eligible, current evidence/rollback gates pass, independent quorum is valid and the recomputed policy, vote-set, rollback, request and attestation digests match.

The timeout evaluator runs through the private scheduler. Existing relay architecture is reused rather than creating a new sovereign voter: the orchestration route handles signaling, while independent eligible reviewers examine the exact request under their own authority.

Founder-action signals use an approved delivery contract with deduplication and delivery evidence. A signal asks for an explicit `APPROVE`, `DENY` or `HOLD` decision; the delivery route may never manufacture the human response itself.

Private scoring calibration, quorum calibration, economic thresholds, reviewer evidence and secret-bearing implementation remain restricted.

## Stress evidence

Fail-closed canaries and boundary tests confirmed that:

- a `D3` / `A4` binding-contract request remained founder-required and created no surrogate attestation;
- a `D1` request classified positive but optional was placed on HOLD / surrogate ineligible;
- direct mutation of the active policy was rejected;
- direct mutation of an exact request snapshot was rejected;
- direct synthetic reviewer-vote injection was rejected; and
- direct surrogate-attestation injection against an ineligible request was rejected.

## Commercialization

A private `CHLOM Founder Continuity` licensing/add-on candidate was registered for pricing review. It is a governance software capability candidate, not a transfer of founder authority. Public pricing is not authorized, checkout is disabled and no Stripe commerce object is activated by this change.

Commercial promotion remains subject to the ordinary CrownThrive lifecycle for rights, pricing, fulfillment, security, support, evidence and checkout readiness.

## Rollback

The layer can be paused fail-closed. Active surrogate attestations are revoked on pause, denial/cancellation supersession, expiration or invalidated prerequisites. If an attestation expires while a covered operation is still in flight, the request becomes rollback-required. If a covered operation has already started, its recorded rollback or compensation path must be invoked and preserved in append-only evidence.
