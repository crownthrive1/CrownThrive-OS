# CHLOM Generation 7 checkpoint

Generation 7 is an additive controlled-test successor overlay to the Gen-6 throughput work. It does not delete Gen-6 history, advance Phase 3, expand the sovereign voter pool or grant D3 automation.

## Material changes

- canonical local monthly request-budget semantics now use `-1` for no CrownThrive local monthly ceiling, `0` for disabled and positive values for local ceilings; provider throttles, billing and plan capacity remain separately authoritative;
- six new CHLOM public-contract/restricted-implementation algorithms support evidence quality, bottleneck burn-down, SEO remediation, governance sufficiency, licensing value and agent-capacity analysis;
- Agents Q/R/T are registered as non-voting D0–D2 parent-subroutes rather than new scheduler slots;
- deterministic score and decision receipts are immutable and DAIL-linked;
- a JWT-protected Gen-7 controlled-test API/MCP surface exposes sanitized scores, status, licensing candidates and remediation queue state without protected weights, thresholds, credentials or private identity IDs;
- fresh dated site-audit evidence created bounded remediation packets for crawlability and accessibility/SEO work;
- CHLOM candidate commercial tiers and pricing research are stored as non-operative pricing/counsel-review state, with checkout and Stripe object creation disabled;
- protected business-plan, moat, pricing, remediation and scoring methods remain encrypted/restricted and are not reproduced in this changelog.

## Identity boundary

Generation 7 can issue provisional internal `did:chlom:` bindings to governed subjects while preserving stable fingerprints and restricted private mappings. These provisional bindings are portability/test references, not proof that a public DID method, public chain, key-backed signature or verifiable credential has been production-certified.

## Promotion boundary

A deterministic score may reduce redundant evidence interpretation; it cannot convert UNKNOWN into PASS, manufacture authority, waive independent verification or make a D3/human-professional decision. Existing A/B/C/D/S governance, mandatory Agent D, disclosure controls, rights/legal/tax/security/accessibility and commerce gates remain intact.
