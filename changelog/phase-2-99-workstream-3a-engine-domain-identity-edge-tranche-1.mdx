## Purpose

Convert the accepted stable identities from S103 tranche 2 into explicit many-to-many source relationships with the recovered S100 85-engine and 82-domain cohorts. The objective is to remove cross-table ambiguity before provider/runtime certification, not to activate integrations.

## Scope and non-goals

This packet covers the CrownApps/ThriveApps alias family and stabilized media identities. It records exact source-engine/domain relationships already present in S100 and preserves source absence where no matching S100 row exists.

It does **not**:

- certify any current provider account, license entitlement or installed version;
- claim a repository, deployment, database, API, webhook or export exists;
- verify registrar ownership, DNS, TLS, HTTP health or canonical routing;
- activate commerce, rights or public availability;
- resolve `MVP (Roku)` by naming similarity;
- create new stable IDs.

## What changed

The new [Engine/Domain Identity Edge Register](/knowledge/phase-2-99-workstream-3a-engine-domain-identity-edge-register) binds ten stable identities to exact S100 source rows and records one blocked identity relationship.

Key invariants:

- CrownApps/ThriveApps maps to both `S100-ENG-046` Appy and `S100-ENG-079` AppMySite, with no S100 domain row inferred;
- Melanated Voices audio owns the exact RKHM/P0dCr8 source-engine family `S100-ENG-008`–`015` and `S100-DOM-062`;
- Melanated Voices Platform orchestration has `S100-DOM-024` but **no exact S100 engine row** and does not inherit TV/audio engines;
- `S100-ENG-025` Viloud remains a shared provider row for Melanated TV, Melanated Voices TV and Locticians TV without merging those identities;
- Melanated TV additionally carries Streamit rows `S100-ENG-026` and `027`;
- Melanated Vault, Melanated Stock and The TAME Gallery now have explicit exact engine/domain edges;
- The Artful Mane Gallery has a source domain edge but no 85-engine row;
- `MVP (Roku)` remains `unresolved_fail_closed` with no effective engine/domain edge.

## Completion criteria

This tranche passes when the first edge manifest validates deterministically, CI remains green, Phase 2.99/Phase 3/roadmap records are synchronized, and no provider/domain relationship has been promoted into current operational certification.

## Hard exit gate

Tranche exit does **not** close Workstream 3A. Full exit still requires remaining stable-ID resolution/freeze, broader identity-edge population, current provider/account/version/deployment/API/export evidence, current domain/DNS/TLS evidence, and accumulated reference/forbidden-promotion validation.

## Authority and rollback

This is D1 source normalization. No founder, counsel, financial or rights approval is required because the packet only records already-supported source relationships and preserves ambiguity. If a source edge is later shown to be incorrect, append a correction/supersession record and update the manifest/validator; never rewrite the original S100/S103 source rows.

## Downstream phase propagation

- **Phase 3:** machine schemas must support many-to-many identity ↔ engine ↔ domain edges and separate source relationship from current certification.
- **Phase 4:** shared provider adapters do not collapse platform identity or system-of-record ownership.
- **Phase 5:** source-engine/domain linkage is not active revenue/customer-routing evidence.
- **Phase 6:** developer/licensing exposure still requires current API/right authority.
- **Phase 8:** Holdings reporting must avoid double-counting shared engines/domains as separate platforms.
- **Phase 10:** continuity exports preserve stable IDs, source edges, shared-provider relations and blocked unresolved edges.

No downstream hard gate is widened.
