**Date:** August 22, 2026<br />**Fleet:** `ct.fleet.chlom-proprietary-asset-factory.100k-plus`<br />**Fleet root:** `b3aba0f74503d3b160503b5b4849bdcb320f6d736b5df5bcbab204051f64d37e`

CrownThrive has activated a bounded first monetization cohort for the CHLOM 100K-plus proprietary asset factory. The cohort sells human-supervised professional services. It does not sell, publish, or license the protected 100,800-asset estate.

## Institutional sequence completed

1. The 48-generation, 100,800-asset public-safe factory projection passed the Documentation Governance, Security Governance, and Governed Merge Gate checks.
2. CrownThrive-OS PR #260 merged, establishing the public-safe 100K-plus documentation baseline.
3. The private cross-system pin, encrypted custody, certification, Stripe reconciliation, and exact offer contract were preserved in governed custody.
4. The private CrownThrive-CIE commercial registry recorded the exact Cohort A offer contracts and Stripe bindings.
5. Two active Stripe Product/Price pairs were created for controlled contact-led Quote or Invoice sales.
