## P0 findings

### Terms

The current Terms already provide a useful master-contract structure, including platform addenda/order of precedence, user content, IP, AI/automation, third-party services, DMCA, termination, disclaimers, liability and electronic assent.

Prospective corrections are required for:

- Kamora360 active-platform examples;
- AI/model-training/data-use separation;
- purpose limits around broad User Content operating/sublicense language;
- IP registration-status precision;
- actual DMCA agent/intake verification;
- registry-driven platform/successor coverage rather than volatile hard-coded platform rosters.

Old accepted versions must remain preserved.

### Legal Center

The March 2025 Legal Center remains useful historical evidence but is too generic to serve as the current comprehensive legal hub. Target state: a Legal Depot landing page that routes to effective Master Terms, Privacy/Cookies, Acceptable Use, Commerce/Refunds, Licensing, AI/Data/Model/Voice, DMCA/IP, Affiliate/Ambassador, Advertising, Developer/API, Education/Ministry, Marketplace, Event, Accessibility, Lifecycle/Migration and program addenda.

### Privacy and public legal accessibility

The current Terms/Membership Policy reference a CrownThrive Privacy Policy. Search-indexed legacy Help Center evidence contains a `CrownThrive, LLC Privacy Policy` plus CCPA/opt-out, cookies/tracking and data-deletion material, but the Phase 2.98 scan did **not conclusively establish the canonical current operative Privacy Policy URL/version**.

Direct public retrieval of the legacy Help Center also returned a sign-in gate during this scan. That is not proof every legacy article is unreachable, but it creates a P0 requirement to verify anonymous pre-assent access for operative policies.

State: `P0 / VERIFY_CANONICAL_URL + VERIFY_ANONYMOUS_ACCESS`.

The future privacy package must map actual data categories, ecosystem handoffs, providers/processors, advertising/analytics, AI inference/evaluation/training state, retention/export/delete, minors/sensitive records and jurisdictional notices rather than relying on generic privacy prose.

### FAQ investment/franchise/equity language

Older/current FAQ content includes `Can I invest? Yes` language and references franchise packets, platform equity models and shared-revenue blueprints.

This is classified `P0 / COUNSEL_REVIEW_REQUIRED / PUBLIC_CORRECTION`. Public explanatory copy must not itself constitute or imply a securities offer, franchise offer, investment advice or automatic ownership/economic right. Any such relationship requires its specific authorized documents and eligibility process.

### FAQ Kamora360 and CHLOM deployment statements

- remove Kamora360 from current/core platform examples;
- map current operational architecture to OpsOasis;
- qualify claims that imply every Suite Pro/franchisee is already fingerprint-licensed or receives automated CHLOM legal/override protection unless supported by current deployment evidence.

### Refunds

Current About/FAQ membership-refund wording must be treated as scope-specific public guidance, not a universal refund policy for every digital product, subscription, event, service, license or jurisdiction.

### DMCA

The current Terms contain a conditional statement about a registered DMCA agent. Phase 2.98 must verify whether a currently registered agent exists before publishing agent details, and inventory the takedown/counter-notice/repeat-infringer workflow.

## Legal-history rule

Never solve legal drift by modifying an old accepted agreement in place.

Use:

`capture → hash/version → preserve accepted history → draft successor → review → publish effective date → obtain applicable assent → redirect/canonicalize explanatory pages → monitor stale fragments`.

## Current public/legal target

The target is not “more policy pages.” The target is one versioned legal control plane with:

- stable policy IDs;
- applicability by role/platform/transaction/jurisdiction/data class;
- effective dates and supersession;
- acceptance/signature evidence;
- Master Terms \+ modular addenda;
- precise rights/licensing;
- precise AI/data/model boundaries;
- proximate disclosures;
- archived historical evidence;
- search/canonical control.
