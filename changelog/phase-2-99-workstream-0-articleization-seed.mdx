## Control decision

Every recovered row begins as a stable seed with `source_recovery_pending` / `reconstruction_required`. No recovered title is automatically treated as a current article, current policy, live platform feature or current economic/legal instruction.

The [Help Center 795-Article Disposition Seed](/support/help-center-795-article-disposition-seed) defines the stable record contract, allowed dispositions, P0/P1/P2 work queues, D0–D3 risk classification, machine invariants and self-healing boundaries.

## Why this matters

The Simplebase recovery problem is not solved by writing 795 plausible pages. It is solved when every recovered institutional identity has:

- durable identity;
- source lineage;
- current/historical/restricted state;
- responsible owner or explicit owner queue;
- evidence-backed body or explicit unresolved status;
- canonical route/redirect/nonpublic disposition;
- correction/version history;
- docs-impact and changelog behavior.

This preserves institutional memory while preventing hallucinated historical reconstruction.
