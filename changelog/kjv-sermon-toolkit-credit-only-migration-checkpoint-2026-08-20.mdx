# KJV Visualized and Sermon Toolkit Credit-Only Migration Checkpoint

**Checkpoint date:** August 20, 2026  
**KJV Visualized:** [kjvsermontoolkit.crownthrive.com](https://kjvsermontoolkit.crownthrive.com)  
**Status:** `partial_unverified` / **HOLD**; this documentation records the target and evidence boundary, not a production conversion

## Commerce decision

First-party KJV Visualized and Sermon Toolkit resource purchases are targeted to accept internal credits only. Stripe remains the external funding rail for credit topups, but a signed topup event may fund only the member credit ledger. It does not directly authorize a resource entitlement.

A credit redemption must resolve an approved resource and edition, atomically debit the ledger, create an order, preserve the governing license and terms, create the entitlement, and route delivery through the protected fulfillment service.

Direct Stripe resource checkout is therefore a disabled target for the converted catalog. It is not yet certified as disabled in production because no source patch was recovered or deployed in this checkpoint.

## Cash lanes that remain separate

The credit-only resource rule does not erase or repurpose other governed cash lanes:

- one historical paid **$7.99** resource entitlement remains preserved and auditable;
- existing subscription records and subscription lifecycle behavior remain preserved;
- Stripe Connect remains the separately governed cash lane for connected sellers;
- internal credits cannot purchase connected-seller products;
- refunds, disputes, reversals, taxes, settlement evidence, and signed webhooks remain attached to their original payment lane.

Historical payment evidence must not be rewritten as a topup or treated as proof that the new credit flow is live.

## Current Stripe readiness

The current Stripe evidence does not expose an active KJV/Sermon Toolkit topup product-and-Price pair. The observed topup products and Prices are inactive, and the successful-topup count is **0**.

Accordingly, topup checkout remains fail-closed. Catalog activation requires an approved active topup mapping, test-mode signed-event and reversal evidence, idempotent credit posting, reconciliation, tax and policy review, and governed production acceptance.

## Private master custody

The private Google Drive custody root and manifest currently account for **13 KJVV4 PDF objects** mapped to **7 logical product codes**.

That inventory is a partial custody record, not certification of the complete KJV Visualized or Sermon Toolkit catalog. Its state remains `partial_unverified` / **HOLD** because catalog-to-file completeness, edition identity, exact checksums, rights, accessibility, and protected-delivery parity have not all been established.

Drive objects remain private and owner-restricted. Google Drive must not become the active storefront delivery plane until each approved edition has:

- one exact master record and checksum;
- a server-side private access identity;
- an entitlement-gated resolver;
- download, refund, dispute, revocation, and audit behavior;
- permission and readback proof without public sharing.

## Production source boundary

Sites version **48**, production commit `aa0e1b659f7a20452aa84f163b71cdfc59b48ada`, remains the authoritative KJV Visualized production baseline.

Production source recovery did not complete, and no storefront source patch was deployed. This checkpoint does not claim that direct resource checkout was removed, that credit topups were activated, or that the live customer workflow passed.

Do not create a replacement Site, force-push, or promote a speculative reconstruction. Recovery must preserve the authoritative baseline and a verified rollback path.

## Supabase / THIVEBASE boundary

The connected Supabase/THIVEBASE evidence remains fail-closed. The observed state contains **0 KJV catalog records** and **0 KJV asset records**, with required master records absent.

Supabase therefore cannot currently serve as KJV catalog, asset, entitlement, or fulfillment authority. Missing records must not be synthesized from product names, Stripe objects, or Drive filenames. Any later materialization requires an isolated development branch, explicit schema and row-level-security review, stable product and edition identifiers, checksum-linked private custody, and governed reconciliation.

## Synthetic test boundary

Credit testing must use an isolated test database, Stripe test-mode fixtures, and a purpose-built synthetic user. A valid synthetic test should cover:

1. signed topup completion and idempotent replay;
2. one ledger posting;
3. an atomic resource redemption;
4. order, license, and entitlement creation;
5. protected delivery authorization;
6. partial and full reversal behavior;
7. preservation of subscriptions, Connect settlement, and the historical paid entitlement.

Synthetic evidence is engineering evidence only. It is not production proof, does not authorize a live customer credit grant, and cannot clear the production HOLD.

## Promotion gates

Promotion remains blocked until all of the following are verified:

- authoritative Sites source recovery and a reviewable patch based on version 48;
- an active, approved Stripe topup Product and Price with signed test-mode event evidence;
- server-resolved credit pricing and atomic, idempotent ledger behavior;
- complete catalog, edition, license, entitlement, and protected-delivery crosswalks;
- exact Drive master coverage and readback-verified private permissions;
- Supabase catalog and asset materialization with least-privilege access controls;
- subscription, historical entitlement, Connect, refund, dispute, and rollback regression tests;
- finance/tax, legal/rights, security/privacy, accessibility/consumer-protection, and operations review.

See [Payment and fulfillment](/commerce/payment-and-fulfillment) for the general entitlement, webhook, delivery, and reconciliation model.
