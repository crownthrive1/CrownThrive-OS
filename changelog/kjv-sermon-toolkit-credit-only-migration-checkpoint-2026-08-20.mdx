## Implemented recovery behavior

The isolated recovery branch converts CrownThrive-owned paid resources to complete internal-credit redemption. Its first-party resource endpoint does not create a Stripe Checkout Session and cannot split a resource order between credits and cash.

A successful redemption now performs one transactional ledger change:

1. resolve the authenticated owner, resource, immutable edition, license, current terms, and private custody gate;
2. reject open account adjustments and stale membership state at commit time;
3. debit only fully governed credits with FIFO grant provenance;
4. create the order, item snapshot, fulfilled entitlement, and encrypted license;
5. write immutable settlement evidence;
6. return the same settled result for an exact replay without a second debit.

A reviewed reversal restores the exact captured grant provenance, restores the wallet, refunds the order and entitlement, revokes the license, and writes administrator actor evidence in the same transaction. Reversal replay is also idempotent.

Legacy formatted USD display fields were removed from the first-party catalog and its generators. Free Scripture and study resources remain free.

## Cash lanes that remain separate

The credit-only first-party resource rule does not erase or repurpose other governed lanes:

- Stripe Checkout remains the approved external rail for later credit topups;
- subscriptions and their existing lifecycle remain preserved;
- Stripe Connect remains the cash-only lane for connected-seller products;
- internal credits cannot purchase connected-seller products;
- historical paid evidence remains preserved in its original lane;
- refunds, disputes, taxes, settlement evidence, and signed webhooks stay attached to the payment path that created them.

Credit topups remain fail-closed. Activation requires an explicit release switch, signed-webhook health evidence, the exact Stripe platform account and mode, a complete canonical Product/Price map, active one-time USD objects with exact metadata, and a reviewed automatic-tax, Product tax-code, and Price tax-behavior decision.
