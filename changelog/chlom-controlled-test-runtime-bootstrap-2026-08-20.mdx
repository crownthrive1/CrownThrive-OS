## Self-healing policy

The newly scheduled watchdogs are evidence checks, not unrestricted repair authority.

```text
Failure detected
      ↓
classify risk
      ↓
D0/D1 deterministic repair where authorized
D2 independent verification / quorum where required
D3 prepare and escalate
      ↓
DAIL evidence
```

No watchdog may heal a failure by weakening rights, security, approval, evidence or legal controls.
