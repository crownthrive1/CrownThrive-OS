# Locticians CLAIMMONTH50 & Scheduled-Agent Architecture — 2026-08-21

## Scope

This pass reconciles two related operating requirements:

1. turn the existing Locticians claimable-profile campaign into a governed offer-aware acquisition workflow; and
2. stop treating scheduled tasks as disconnected prompts by binding active provider tasks to stable agents, skills, authority ceilings and evidence contracts.

## CLAIMMONTH50 evidence

Founder-supplied admin screenshots establish the intended coupon configuration for `CLAIMMONTH50`: 50% percentage discount, Sign Ups & Upgrades, New Sign Ups Only, recurring payments YES, card required when `$0`, unlimited uses, a January 1, 2026 start, October 31, 2028 expiration, all displayed billing cycles and selected Basic/Blogger/Community+/Featured/Premium/Sponsor plans.

Current first-party `/join` public copy independently confirms that `CLAIMMONTH50` exists for claimable listings, but currently names only Community+ and Basic. The broader admin plan scope therefore remains a governed conflict requiring checkout verification before higher-tier eligibility or higher-tier savings are promised externally.

Provider documentation supports the interpretation that recurring `YES` applies the discount to recurring payments, while the coupon expiration date controls new coupon use rather than the order's own end date.

## Sales-system changes

Private runtime changes include:

- a versioned offer registry record `locticians.claimmonth50.v1`;
- founder-attested plan/cycle configuration separated from public plan-scope evidence;
- checkout-verification state;
- category outreach templates upgraded to resolve a current offer block rather than hard-code stale discount copy;
- inbound and outbound sales-agent rules updated to prohibit higher-tier savings claims until eligibility is verified;
- truthful FOMO limited to claimable-profile exclusivity, new-signup eligibility and the finite new-redemption window.

Cold outbound remains fail-closed until the private commercial-email configuration contains the required physical postal address and the outbound gate is explicitly enabled.

## Scheduled-task agent architecture

The private runtime now records a task-to-agent binding layer for active provider automations. The August 21 snapshot contains 20 active bindings across:

- 5 sovereign relay agents;
- 4 non-voting specialists;
- 6 operational watches;
- 2 sales routes;
- 2 governance routes;
- 1 content-governance route.

Provider task identifiers and private routing details remain restricted. The public projection documents stable agent IDs, roles, cadence classes, voting state and authority boundaries.

## Governance

- A/B/C/D/S remain the scheduled sovereign voter pool.
- Four of five approvals remain required for eligible automatic D0-D2 promotion.
- Agent D remains mandatory.
- Specialists, operations agents, sales agents and content/governance agents do not acquire votes by being scheduled.
- D3 remains human-reserved.
- Scheduler state, heartbeat and provider success are evidence, not authority.
