## Scope

This pass reconciles two related operating requirements: turn the existing Locticians claimable-profile campaign into a governed offer-aware acquisition workflow, and bind active provider tasks to stable agents, skills, authority ceilings and evidence contracts.

## CLAIMMONTH50 evidence

Founder-supplied admin screenshots establish the intended coupon configuration for `CLAIMMONTH50`: 50% percentage discount, Sign Ups & Upgrades, New Sign Ups Only, recurring payments YES, card required when `$0`, unlimited uses, a January 1, 2026 start, October 31, 2028 expiration, all displayed billing cycles and selected Basic/Blogger/Community\+/Featured/Premium/Sponsor plans.

Current first-party `/join` public copy independently confirms that `CLAIMMONTH50` exists for claimable listings, but currently names only Community\+ and Basic. The broader admin plan scope therefore remains a governed conflict requiring checkout verification before higher-tier eligibility or higher-tier savings are promised externally.

Provider documentation supports the interpretation that recurring `YES` applies the discount to recurring payments, while the coupon expiration date controls new coupon use rather than the order's own end date.

## Sales-system changes

Private runtime changes include a versioned offer registry, founder-attested plan/cycle configuration separated from public plan-scope evidence, checkout-verification state, offer-aware outreach templates and rules that prohibit higher-tier savings claims until eligibility is verified.

Cold outbound remains fail-closed until the private commercial-email configuration contains the required physical postal address and the outbound gate is explicitly enabled.

## Scheduled-task agent architecture

The private runtime records a task-to-agent binding layer. The August 21 snapshot contains 20 active bindings across five sovereign relay agents, four non-voting specialists, six operational watches, two sales routes, two governance routes and one content-governance route.

Provider task identifiers and private routing details remain restricted. Scheduler state, heartbeat and provider success are evidence, not authority.

## Governance

A/B/C/D/S remain the scheduled sovereign voter pool. Four of five approvals remain required for eligible automatic D0-D2 promotion, Agent D remains mandatory, specialists do not gain votes by being scheduled, and D3 remains human-reserved.

## Reopen triggers

Reopen when coupon checkout behavior, public plan scope, offer dates, prices, Nexus awards, scheduled-task bindings, sovereign-voter rules or cold-outreach compliance state materially change.
