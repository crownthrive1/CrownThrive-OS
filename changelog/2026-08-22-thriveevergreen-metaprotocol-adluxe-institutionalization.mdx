# ThriveEvergreen Metaprotocol & AdLuxe Institutionalization

**Date:** August 22, 2026<br />**Program state:** Phase 2 institutional specification / reconciliation<br />**Implementation state:** adopted specification; full production runtime not yet certified<br />**Founder ratification:** recorded August 22, 2026<br />**Governed source baseline:** PR #265, merged as `84ef5b8655c0b5349d475885e90f77d48cb09316`

## Ratified constitutional doctrine

> **ThriveEvergreen may optimize within authority. It may never manufacture authority.**

This ratification makes the ThriveEvergreen charter, ECAC non-bypass rule, exclusive CrownThrive SKU authority, AdLuxe commercial jurisdiction, provider-evidence reconciliation rule, SAFE\_HOLD model, ex-post reconciliation doctrine and no-self-escalation constraints part of the institutional baseline. The merge record is provenance for the documentation decision; it is not proof of a production runtime.

## Decision

CrownThrive institutionalizes **ThriveEvergreen™** as the mandatory commerce and economic-activation authority for the convergent ecosystem.

The earlier Commerce Agent is expanded into a cross-cutting control plane governing:

```text
asset → rights → product → SKU → license → price → channel → listing →
checkout/payment → entitlement → fulfillment → revenue routing →
advertising → refund/dispute → reconciliation → optimization → preservation
```

## Metaprotocol relationship

ThriveEvergreen is explicitly part of the CHLOM Metaprotocol as a **cross-pallet commerce/economic-execution subprotocol**, not an isolated P-13 silo.

Primary pallet relationships:

- P-03 Rights & Licensing;
- P-05 Growth, Advertising & Measurement;
- P-06 Commerce, Booking & Loyalty;
- P-10 Operations & Institutional Memory;
- P-11 Capital, Revenue & Settlement;
- with P-01/P-02/P-04/P-07/P-08/P-09/P-12 bindings when the commercial object requires them.

The cross-protocol authorization object is the **Evergreen Commerce Activation Certificate (`ECAC`)**.

Target state:

```text
commercial candidate
→ ThriveEvergreen
→ ECAC | HOLD | DENY
```

Certified commerce surfaces must not bypass this gate.

## AdLuxe decision

ThriveEvergreen becomes the commercial/economic operating authority across:

- **AdLuxe Network** — inventory/package economics, budgets/rates, advertiser payment, invoices/credits, publisher/creator/location earnings, settlement and reconciliation;
- **AdLuxe Studio** — product/offer creative binding, license/rights version, production pricing and commercial-release certification;
- **AdLuxe Places** — place/screen inventory assetization, rate cards/packages, location economics, proof reconciliation and settlement.

AdLuxe remains the specialist advertising system. ThriveEvergreen does not replace creative production, trafficking, native delivery measurement, publisher relationships or place operations. It governs the commercial activation, assetization, money and reconciliation layer around them.

Adserver.Online, ADSPOT, AdSpot TV and later providers remain replaceable implementation providers rather than CrownThrive's institutional commercial source of truth.

## Advertising inventory as evergreen assets

Qualified web placements, sponsorship packages, creator packages, media/program sponsorships, events, kiosks/screens/locations and managed campaign services may become governed evergreen commercial assets with stable CrownThrive identities, economic rules, rights/consent conditions and reconciliation requirements.

## SKU authority

ThriveEvergreen is the exclusive institutional issuer of CrownThrive SKUs. Provider/store IDs remain aliases. Retired SKUs are never reused.

## Autonomous economic doctrine

The adopted operating doctrine is:

> **Execute inside signed authority; reconcile immediately afterward; roll back or compensate when evidence fails.**

ThriveEvergreen may perform reversible or compensatable money/e-commerce/advertising actions inside its approved authority envelope without synchronous founder intervention. This delegation does not grant unrestricted authority and does not remove post-action evidence, reconciliation or supervisory control.

## Supervision and containment

- Top-Level Charter Agent — current charter and authority interpretation;
- `ct.agent.agent-a-supervisor` — independent supervisory veto, drift detection, quarantine and re-certification trigger;
- CHLOM Rights, Economic, Legal Policy, Security and AdLuxe specialist agents — independent domain verdicts;
- independent Commerce Certifier — acceptance evidence.

The Phase 2.99 hourly relay's **Agent A Orchestrator** remains a coordination role and is not automatically the same authority as `ct.agent.agent-a-supervisor`.

ThriveEvergreen cannot self-amend its charter, grant itself new permissions, remove supervisors, suppress audit/reconciliation, invent rights, redirect root payout ownership, erase prior evidence or replicate outside approved custody.

## Current Adserver evidence correction

The AdLuxe institutional registry no longer treats the entire current Adserver.Online account/subscription as unknown. Current account/subscription state is founder-attested operational evidence. Exact plan pricing, included/request capacity, consumption, billing-period values, balances and payment details remain restricted and must not be exposed publicly.

Account-specific API eligibility/scopes, deployment mapping and production delivery health still require capability-specific certification.
