## Crown Affiliates and Crown Ambassadors

A dedicated registry separates the referral/performance program from the higher-trust advocacy/representation program. It establishes participant, offer, link/code, attribution, conversion, economic schedule, commission accrual, hold, payout, reversal, training/disclosure and progression records.

Recovered Help Center/Legal/SOP evidence includes affiliate setup, brand master guides, registration, agreement explanation, earnings policy/disclaimer, platform addenda, affiliate payout/tracking and instructor/affiliate identity verification.

Historical MM Suites commission, lifetime override and franchise-referral examples remain historical economic design evidence, not current compensation schedules.

## ThrivePush

A dedicated registry defines ThrivePush as a consent-based re-engagement and permissioned communications layer rather than a source of identity, consent, product, rights or analytics truth.

It establishes contact-point, consent, suppression, audience, template, campaign, provider, delivery, complaint, unsubscribe and conversion records; separates transactional/support/security/legal messaging from promotional messaging; and prohibits sensitive/private data reuse as ordinary marketing targeting.

Current provider, channel, sender-domain/number, campaign and API state remain explicit verification gates.

## CrownThriveU

A dedicated registry defines course, module, lesson, instructor, contributor, enrollment, progress, assessment, credential, offer, license and entitlement records.

Recovered institutional evidence includes CrownThriveU Instructor Onboarding, CrownThriveU platform-specific addendum, CT-SOP-107 Instructor Onboarding & Vetting, CT-SOP-110 CrownThriveU Course Approval & Quality Assurance, CT-SOP-115 Instructor & Affiliate Identity Verification, CT-SOP-117 Instructor Course Takedown & Intellectual Property Dispute, and the CrownThriveU Instructor & Contributor Disclaimer.

The registry distinguishes course completion/certificates from accreditation, professional licensure or regulated certification and makes instructor/content rights explicit.
