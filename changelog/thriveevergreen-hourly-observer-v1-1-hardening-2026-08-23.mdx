## Change

CrownThrive preserved the failed v1 adversarial evidence, applied v1.1.0
hardening, paused and rolled the component back with history intact, then
reapplied the exact activation migration. The policy, agent and schedule are
active only as a production observer; publication target and economic effects
remain zero.

Version 1.1 structurally shares the hourly slot across mutating scopes, provides
a read-only preview, removes publication code, rejects production-write/effect
modes and permits the service to call only the governed wrapper. The already
consumed hour was not bypassed, so a fresh unconsumed-hour persistence canary
remains pending before full observer certification.

## Boundaries preserved

- ThriveEvergreen remains the mandatory `ECAC | HOLD | DENY` boundary.
- The component is non-voting, cannot self-approve and cannot grant D3.
- A Founder directive can set the objective but cannot manufacture independent
  rights, legal/tax, accessibility, security, license, entitlement, quorum or
  authenticated execution evidence.
- Whole-platform Phase 3 status remains NO-GO pending the Phase 2.99 hard exit.

  separately certified component and authority package.
