# Phase 2.98 Pass D5 — Hospitality, Travel & Experiences

## Checkpoint

- **Baseline:** merged Phase 2.98 Pass D4
- **Repository scope:** `crownthrive1/CrownThrive-Support` only
- **Top-level phase:** Phase 2
- **Purpose:** reconcile the remaining hospitality/travel/experiences corridor before Phase 2.99.

## Completed

Created the **Hospitality, Travel & Experiences Institutional Registry** covering:

- ThriveKiln — Holdings P2/Build restaurant and local-hospitality technology role;
- Luxperiences — P2/Revamp premium spaces, venues, rentals and experiences;
- ThriveTravels — P3/Open culturally aligned travel/group experiences;
- ThriveBooking — reserved/reposition cross-platform bookable-services candidate;
- Rooted Noir — P2/Active/Cultural Revamp culturally aligned wedding-planning lane.

The pass preserves supplied domain and engine lineage while refusing to convert it into blanket production claims. Holdings/source records currently map TableTrack to ThriveKiln, Bookapp to ThriveBooking and WedFlow Atelier to Rooted Noir, while current installed versions, repositories, data, APIs and provider configurations remain unverified.

## Controls added

- booking request, payment, provider confirmation and service delivery are separate states;
- third-party venues/travel/hospitality providers require authority, refund, tax/fee, insurance, accessibility, safety, privacy and support records;
- ThriveBooking may route requests but must not duplicate canonical booking/ticket/event systems without an explicit boundary;
- ThriveTravels cannot sell or imply current trips/packages solely because its domain/brand exists;
- precise itinerary/location information remains purpose-limited and is not advertising data by default;
- target API/MCP/event records are clearly labeled as planned adapters until verified.

## Phase 2.99 handoff

Seed provider, destination/location, experience, availability, booking, order/payment, refund, check-in, safety/accessibility, incident and settlement records with source-platform mappings and explicit current verification states.

## Completion rule

D5 does not certify live inventory, current venue/provider agreements, installed application versions, travel-regulatory compliance or production booking APIs. Those remain evidence-gated.
