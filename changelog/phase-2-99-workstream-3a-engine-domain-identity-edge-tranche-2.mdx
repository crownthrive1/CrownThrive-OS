## Purpose

Expand the accepted many-to-many source relationship layer beyond CrownApps/media into current operating-spine, support, beauty/wellness, analytics, messaging and growth/distribution identities so Phase 3 seed generation can distinguish institutional identity from dated provider/domain assignments without rediscovery.

## Scope

The tranche adds effective source relationships for ThriveTools root, ThriveRelay, The Mane Experience, ThriveMaps, Collab Portal, the CrownThrive Support implementation projection, Locticians, ThriveSeat, CrownLytics, CrownPulse, ThrivePush, CrownFluence, Crown Affiliates and Crown Ambassadors, while also recording the abstract ThriveSupport family with no inherited implementation edge.

## Explicit non-goals

This packet does not certify provider accounts, license entitlement, installed versions, repositories, deployments, APIs, exports, registrar custody, DNS/TLS, rights, consent, marketing authority, economic schedules or production state. It does not add SuitePro or Brilliant Directories to the historical 85-engine count merely because current/source notes identify those providers. It does not silently merge ThriveTools SEO or OPT child-capability rows into the root ThriveTools identity.

## What changed

The edge register grows from 10 to **25 resolved identity records**. Likewise, the ThriveSupport knowledge family cannot inherit BeDesk/domain implementation edges from the CrownThrive Support projection by default.
