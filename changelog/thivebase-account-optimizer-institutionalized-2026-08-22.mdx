## Authority and HOLD state

The hourly daemon is **active for observation and runtime-health state only**. This does not activate general production mutation authority.

Target-resource writes remain bounded by the contract's A2/D2, non-destructive, reversible, rollback, exact-pre-state and readback requirements. D3, commercial, sovereign, founder-reserved, irreversible and destructive actions remain outside autonomous authority.

Controller-DID resolution, Vault signing-key proof and independent production certification remain separate verifiable gates. Exact Git-head binding is maintained in THIVEBASE identity/DAIL evidence from the saved PR head instead of being self-asserted inside the same commit. Missing evidence is retained as HOLD rather than converted into PASS.
