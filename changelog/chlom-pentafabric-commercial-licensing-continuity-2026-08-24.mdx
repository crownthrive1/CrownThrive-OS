## CHLOM Pentafabric, Licensing, and Continuous Commercial Projection

## Decision

Founder Kavonte Jones Sr directed CHLOM to be further institutionalized as a continuously developed and monetized CrownThrive metaprotocol, with explicit parent/child repository licensing, public commercial positioning, agent/factory bindings, machine-access terms, website-mesh continuity, and a five-layer architecture that explains the full system.

## Repository correction

The repository relationship is now stated accurately:

- `crownthrive1/CrownThrive-OS` is the **public canonical governance parent** for the managed public-safe CHLOM commercial overlay.
- `crownthrive/chlom-protocol` is the **public commercial child**.
- Both repositories are public.
- Restricted institutional state remains outside public Git and is represented only through bounded public-safe references when appropriate.

This supersedes the earlier inaccurate wording that called the parent GitHub repository private.

## CrownThrive Pentafabric™

The five institutional layers are:

1. Identity & Imprint
2. Rights & Conditions
3. Ledger & Assurance
4. Execution & Interoperability
5. Economy & Distribution

CHLOM is the metaprotocol coordinating the five layers. CIE contributes imprint and cultural context. DAIL preserves evidence-bearing institutional state. Factories and machine interfaces materialize bounded execution. ThriveEvergreen / ECAC governs economic activation.

## Commercial and rights package

The managed child package is expanded to include:

- repository rights notice;
- commercial licensing notice;
- API/MCP/agent/plugin machine-access terms;
- support and voluntary-contribution routing;
- licensing pathways;
- Pentafabric architecture;
- repository/runtime/website-mesh topology;
- agent and factory operating documentation;
- commercial product/service surface;
- existing DAIL and repository-boundary contracts;
- exact upstream provenance.

Public visibility remains non-granting by default. Commercial, OEM, white-label, data/model, certification, production machine, and other rights require explicit applicable authority.

## Continuous agent and factory model

Six bounded candidate agent identities are registered:

- CHLOM R&D Researcher;
- CHLOM Rights & Identity Steward;
- CHLOM Compliance Oracle;
- CHLOM Documentation & Projection Steward;
- CHLOM Commercial Packager;
- CHLOM Mesh Reliability Agent.

They may prepare candidates for the Execution Builder / PentaFactory but may not direct-write main, self-merge, self-certify, manufacture ownership or licenses, or independently activate economics.

## Continuous projection

The CHLOM commercial projection workflow is extended for scheduled continuity. The generated projection receipt is deterministic for an unchanged parent commit so scheduled runs do not create timestamp-only child changes.

When the bounded `CHLOM_PUBLIC_REPO_TOKEN` credential is available and managed child content differs, the factory may open a child candidate pull request. Unmanaged child content remains preserved.

## Website mesh

The public discovery topology is modeled across:

- crownthrive.com;
- crownthrivesupport.com;
- crownthrive.io;
- the CHLOM public child repository;
- the CrownThrive Support canonical parent repository.

Scheduled mesh observation provides availability evidence and total-discovery-outage detection. DNS, CDN, application, database, provider, and API/MCP failover remain deployment-specific and are not falsely asserted as deployed merely because fallback URLs are documented.

- DAIL = Decentralized Autonomous Information Ledger.
- DLA = Dynamic Licensing Asset.
- Licensing Stewardship / Issuer Authority is the current issuer-authority concept.

Historical DAL and historical Decentralized Licensing Authority references remain source lineage only.
