## Change

The previously adopted Sales & Email Operations V2 capabilities are decomposed into six reusable skill contracts: Sales Inbox Triage, Lead Legitimacy & Scam Vetting, Inbound Lead Nurture & Close, Locticians Claimable-Profile Outreach, Follow-Up/Suppression/Opt-Out and Sales Continuity & Evidence.

## Governance

The skills remain nonvoting. D3 remains human-reserved. Public files contain no raw email bodies, private prospect data, credentials, suppression records, private provider message IDs, legal/sensitive evidence or secret configuration.

The Locticians outreach skill preserves the fail-closed commercial-email gate: no unsolicited send when the governed outbound configuration is disabled, the verified physical postal address is absent, the recipient is suppressed, the business contact source is unverified or the listing is no longer claimable.

## Runtime relationship

Mailbox and Locticians scheduled workflows may invoke these contracts, but the skill package itself does not create a new MCP, credential, provider integration, commercial entitlement or sovereign authority.
