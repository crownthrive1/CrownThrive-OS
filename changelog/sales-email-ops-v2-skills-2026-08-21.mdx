# Sales & Email Operations V2 Skills — 2026-08-21

## Change

The previously adopted Sales & Email Operations V2 capabilities are decomposed into six reusable skill contracts:

1. Sales Inbox Triage
2. Lead Legitimacy & Scam Vetting
3. Inbound Lead Nurture & Close
4. Locticians Claimable-Profile Outreach
5. Follow-Up, Suppression & Opt-Out
6. Sales Continuity & Evidence

## Governance

The skills remain nonvoting. D3 remains human-reserved. Public files contain no raw email bodies, private prospect data, credentials, suppression records, private provider message IDs, legal/sensitive evidence, or secret configuration.

The Locticians outreach skill preserves the existing fail-closed commercial-email gate: no unsolicited send when the governed outbound configuration is disabled, the verified physical postal address is absent, the recipient is suppressed, the business contact source is unverified, or the listing is no longer claimable.

## Runtime relationship

The current mailbox and Locticians scheduled workflows may invoke these contracts conceptually, but this repository change does not itself activate a new MCP, credential, provider integration, commercial entitlement, or sovereign authority.
