## Canary disposition

A synthetic preview release proved the bounded adapter/write/readback/rollback sequence. It did **not** prove independent sovereign quorum because the fixture used sovereign-looking identities from one execution context. Quorum acceptance remains HOLD until isolated non-sovereign fixtures and independently bound vote evidence are verified.

A later negative-state canary proved that loss of certification automatically withdrew the publication and quarantined the release. Restoring the same exact release to PASS allowed a new publication attempt while preserving the prior publication and rollback history.

An invalid same-transaction certification design was rejected before adapter certification and replaced with committed provider-visible evidence.

## Persistent automation

Publication dispatch, vote-request routing, adapter recertification and storefront-bootstrap verification are scheduled in THIVEBASE. Exact private cadence, job identifiers, endpoint topology and evidence bodies are intentionally excluded from the public changelog.

## Production Sites status

Developer Marketplace, KJV Visualized / Sermon Toolkit and Virality Music have governed destination and bootstrap records. Their storefront-consumer bootstrap and provider-source mutation evidence remain pending, so production automatic publication remains off.

No public Sites source-write capability was fabricated from project presence or public rendering.

No current commercial product was published by this implementation.
