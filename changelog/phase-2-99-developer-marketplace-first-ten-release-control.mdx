## Two-stage Crown Credits commerce

1. Stripe hosted Checkout funds a fixed top-up.
2. A signed event and provider readback authorize one idempotent Crown Credits ledger entry.
3. A later, separate atomic credit redemption creates the order, exact-version entitlement, and license grant.
4. Protected delivery requires that exact active entitlement and accepted license.

A Stripe redirect or successful top-up creates no product entitlement. The application ledger is the balance authority, and only a later eligible redemption may create an order, exact-version entitlement, and accepted license grant.
