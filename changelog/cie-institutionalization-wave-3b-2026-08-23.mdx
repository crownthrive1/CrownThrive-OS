**Date:** August 23, 2026<br />**Framework:** `ct.framework.cultural-imprint-engine`<br />**Canonical name:** Cultural Imprint Engine<br />**Runtime:** `ct.runtime.cie-chlom.durable.v1`<br />**CIE repository merge:** `2067572bd9674766b4121c904c2042a87e4d5bcb`

## Institutional result

Wave 3B converts the Wave 2 CIE ↔ CHLOM handshake from a deterministic non-mutating contract into a **durable internal controlled-test evidence path** in ThriveBase.

It preserves the separation of authority:

- CIE resolves cultural/narrative state;
- CHLOM resolves rights/licensing/permission/evidence state;
- the durable layer records and correlates both;
- a positive correlation can reach only `COMPOSED_READY_NON_EXECUTING`;
- separately governed systems remain responsible for actual execution, commerce, entitlement, provider mutation and D3 decisions.

## Security result

The durable receipt ledger is forced-RLS and append-only.

At certification readback:

- `anon` and `authenticated` table access were false;
- `anon` and `authenticated` writer execution were false;
- service role could invoke the governed writer;
- direct service-role `INSERT`, `UPDATE` and `DELETE` were false;
- a `BEFORE UPDATE OR DELETE` trigger rejected history mutation.

`rls_enabled_no_policy` remains an intentional security-advisor condition for this private service-function architecture. A permissive policy was not added merely to clear a dashboard warning.

## DAIL result

Wave 3B installation, hardening, canonical exchange receipts, replay conflict, orphan hold and performance hardening all produced restricted DAIL events.

Direct read-only verification confirmed the stored payload digest and global previous-event link for each current Wave 3B DAIL event. The generic DAIL-chain verification call was blocked by the connected SQL tool and is recorded as blocked rather than as PASS.
