### Book Five collision hold

The two supplied _What Really Happened_ PDFs both identify themselves as **TrapOpera: The Family Archive, Book Five** and use the same Version 2 Illustrated Collector Edition label. They are preserved as separate checksum-addressed candidate records. Their per-candidate byte, page and image counts are withheld so public product variants cannot be used to infer a mapping. No title normalization, filename similarity, technical-count comparison or prior commerce crosswalk may merge, overwrite, renumber or declare one authoritative.

The collision remains held until an authorized canon/source review records exact edition identities, provenance, intended supersession or coexistence, and downstream reader/product/entitlement effects. PentaDocs may disclose that the collision exists; it must not publish protected content or adjudicate it by inference.

## Public/private and authority boundaries

This projection includes public URLs, public page and asset observations, stable public IDs, supported summaries, public-safe source labels, cryptographic digests and explicitly qualified status. It excludes:

- protected book, image, audio, video, archive and product binaries;
- extracted manuscript text or full-resolution embedded artwork;
- custody paths, signed links, storage keys, credentials and provider secrets;
- customer, payment, entitlement, download, refund and dispute data;
- unpublished canon, restricted evidence, contract text and chain-of-title material;
- private pricing logic, anti-fraud controls and internal operational details.

Cryptographic identification proves which bytes were observed; it does not prove authorship, ownership, clearance, publication approval, accessibility, commerce activation or legal sufficiency.
