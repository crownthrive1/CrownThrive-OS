## Canonical handling rule

Phase 2.8 does not force a decision where the sources do not support one. Each contradiction must end in one of:

- canonical current state;
- temporal lineage;
- scoped metric distinction;
- successor/alias relationship;
- proposed/research state;
- owner-decision queue;
- legal-review queue;
- evidence-required queue.

## Phase 2.9 handoff

Phase 2.9 will test the institution adversarially. The question changes from **"Do our records agree?"** to **"Could CrownThrive continue operating if people, vendors, systems, credentials, or evidence become unavailable?"**

Phase 3 should not begin until Phase 2.9 identifies and either closes or explicitly defers the continuity failures that could compromise rights, money, identity, governance, support, or institutional recovery.
