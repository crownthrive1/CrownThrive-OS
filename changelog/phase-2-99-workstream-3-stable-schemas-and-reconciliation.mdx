## Purpose

Workstream 3 converts the accepted repository and private-data architecture into stable machine record families **while reopening earlier Phase 2 conclusions to stronger evidence**. It does not begin Phase 3.

The pass follows a macro-to-micro rule:

```text
source/evidence
→ institutional doctrine
→ portfolio/corridor relationship
→ platform/capability state
→ object/field state
→ article/changelog/machine seed
→ validation
```

Historical phase records remain preserved. Effective state may change only through explicit source/version/correction lineage.

## Stable schema contract

The new [Stable Schemas & Seed Contract](/technology/phase-2-99-workstream-3-stable-schemas-seed-contract) defines twenty interoperable record families covering:

- tenants, organizations, actors and service identities;
- roles, authority, delegations, agents and evaluations;
- assets, works, brands, imprints, universes, rooms and characters;
- platforms, corridors, frameworks and CHLOM components;
- versions, corrections, sources, claims and evidence;
- policies, legal applicability, rights, licenses and entitlements;
- prices, commissions, rewards and allocations;
- domains, routes, SEO, APIs, MCPs, webhooks and adapters;
- repositories, deployments, environments, vendors and secret references;
- data flows, DAIL/events, retention, SOPs, incidents and continuity;
- capability/risk/backlog state, approvals, run packets and tests.

The contract requires stable IDs, explicit lifecycle/evidence states, effective periods, contradiction records and fail-closed validation against silent promotion.

## Retroactive Phase 2.0–2.9 lane

The new [Phase 2.0–2.9 Retroactive Reconciliation Program](/knowledge/phase-2-0-through-2-9-retroactive-reconciliation) makes later evidence a formal input to earlier-pass effective state without erasing historical acceptance.

Reconciliation must cover:

- Phase 2.0–2.4 source capture and institutional identity;
- Phase 2.5 CHLOM/MM Suites/Founder/source consolidation;
- Phase 2.6 naming/taxonomy/corridor relationships;
- Phase 2.7 exhaustiveness;
- Phase 2.8 contradiction hunting;
- Phase 2.9 adversarial continuity and recovery.

A newly recovered material source cannot remain merely “added to the library”; its downstream impacts must be classified and propagated before Phase 2.99 exits.

## Phase 3 gate correction

The readiness gate now states the current decision explicitly:

```yaml
phase_3_entry: blocked_pending_phase_2_99_hard_exit
```

New absolute no-go conditions include:

- material recovered/revived/provider evidence whose impacts remain unclassified or unpropagated;
- schema definitions without validated seed population/reference resolution for the applicable entry cohort.

A future controlled Phase 3 entry may be considered only after every Phase 2.99 hard-exit criterion is evaluated, allowed unresolved states are fail-closed, the retroactive Phase 2.0–2.9 lane is clear for all known material evidence, and the exact source/schema/infrastructure/commit snapshot is accepted.
