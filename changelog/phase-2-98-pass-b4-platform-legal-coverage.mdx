## Sunset rule

Sunset does not mean deleting legal obligations. A retiring platform retains the packages needed for responsible closure—typically Lifecycle/Migration, Privacy/Data, Support/Security and IP—plus any continuing contracts, orders, licenses or retention duties.

Kamora360 is the current founder-authorized example: no new commercial/onboarding package is created, but migration/privacy/support/IP obligations remain until closure. OpsOasis receives a new current legal profile and does not automatically inherit old Kamora contracts.

## Uninspected rule

A rediscovered domain/platform defaults to `uninspected`, receives minimum Master Terms/Privacy/IP/Support classification for review purposes, and cannot perform consequential actions until its users, data, commerce, rights, AI, claims and jurisdiction are classified.
