# ADR — Ten-Phase Institutional Roadmap Migration

## Decision

Expand the canonical future institutional-build roadmap to ten top-level phases while preserving all historical phase/release/subphase identifiers and the five-phase/Phases 3–5 charter as versioned evidence of the earlier planning generation.

The canonical future-planning authority is now the [Ten-Phase Institutional Program Charter](/standards/ten-phase-institutional-program-charter).

## Why

Phase 2 recovery has materially increased CrownThrive's institutional resolution. The earlier five-phase generation grouped multiple distinct risk domains into Phase 5: revenue proof, external licensing/developer economy, physical/phygital/regional expansion, holdings/capital scale, advanced CHLOM/interoperability, and long-horizon continuity. Those domains have different source authorities, legal/security/economic gates, validation criteria and rollback requirements.

Separating them makes the roadmap more precise and easier to automate. It also reduces hallucination risk by preventing a single broad phase label from being interpreted as permission for multiple unrelated capabilities.

## Historical preservation rule

This ADR does **not** rename prior work. A document saying "Phase 5" at the time the five-phase model was current retains that historical meaning and effective context. Future migrations may crosswalk that requirement to one or more current phases, but must not alter the original record to make history look cleaner.

## Canonical top-level phases

1. Institutional Mapping
2. Institutional Recovery, Reconciliation & Documentation
3. Executable Institutional Core
4. Federated Ecosystem Activation
5. Revenue & Market Activation
6. Licensing, IP & Developer Economy
7. Physical, Phygital & Regional Expansion
8. Holdings, Capital & Portfolio Scale
9. Advanced CHLOM & Interoperable Infrastructure
10. Generational Continuity, Sovereign Scale & Institutional Permanence

## Requirement inheritance

Requirements in the historical Phases 3–5 charter are not discarded. They are inherited and reclassified prospectively:

- historical Phase 3 requirements primarily map to current Phase 3, with mature commercialization/developer pieces extending into Phase 6 and advanced protocol research into Phase 9;
- historical Phase 4 requirements primarily map to current Phase 4, with revenue proof explicitly graduating into Phase 5 and place-based expansion into Phase 7;
- historical Phase 5 requirements are decomposed across current Phases 5–10 according to their actual risk/operating domain.

No requirement is considered satisfied merely because its old phase number changed position in the current roadmap.

## Fluid downstream propagation rule

Every material pass must perform a roadmap-impact check. When a changed fact affects later objectives, dependencies, gates, checkpoints, acceptance tests, artifacts, risks, sequencing, authority, rollback or handoff criteria, the downstream phase documents must update in the same governed documentation-impact cycle or open an explicit delta.

```text
material change
→ identify invalidated/refined assumptions
→ identify dependent future phases
→ update affected gates/plans/tests
→ preserve superseded version
→ record reason/effective state
→ leave next-run handoff
```

## Hard-gate rule

Each top-level phase has a separate hard exit and the next phase has a separate hard entry. Passing one does not imply the other. Subphases/passes/workstreams may add stricter gates. No automation or agent can infer gate waiver from elapsed time, a roadmap label, a successful unrelated PR or a historical claim.

## Automation impact

The program should increasingly automate D0 and permitted D1 reconciliation, manifest generation, drift detection, roadmap-impact checks, validation, changelog propagation, recovery and next-state handoff. D2/D3 human/specialist authority remains mandatory where defined.

## Immediate effects

- current institutional phase remains Phase 2 / 2.99;
- Phase 3 remains blocked pending the Phase 2.99 hard exit;
- historical Phase 3–5 charter is retained but marked as a historical roadmap generation;
- phase namespace documentation now includes Phases 6–10;
- future Phase 2.99 passes must reconcile downstream implications against ten phases;
- no production system, legal status, license, provider or platform is activated by this ADR.

## Rollback/correction

If a later founder/authority decision changes the roadmap again, publish another ADR and supersession mapping. Do not delete this ADR or rewrite historical phase references.
