## Completed

Created the **Education & Community Long-Tail Institutional Registry** for ThriveArena, QuizNuggets, CrownConsults, ThriveAlumni, CrownThrive Commons and Digital Business Academy.

The pass preserves the Holdings progression architecture while separating course completion, challenge score, CrownThrive credential, professional license, mentor/expert eligibility, alumni/community title, affiliate/ambassador status, CHLOM permission and corporate authority as distinct record types.

## Current evidence controls

- ThriveArena retains the current strategic role of challenges, leaderboards and knowledge proof; exact engine/API/reward implementation remains unverified.
- QuizNuggets retains its quiz creation/publishing/AI/competition role and active-domain evidence; domain presence does not certify its current app/API.
- CrownConsults remains a vetted expert-help lane with explicit regulated-profession scope/qualification gates.
- ThriveAlumni is current alumni/contributor continuity and leadership architecture; historical civic/government metaphors remain history unless explicitly re-adopted. Zaialumni lineage is preserved without claiming current installed software.
- CrownThrive Commons is a private/gated community layer, not CrownThrive ID.
- Digital Business Academy remains `needs_owner_validation` rather than being silently merged into CrownThriveU.

## Institutional control updates

D7 extends the new platform registry across the full institutional control plane:

- source register and recovery-coverage matrix;
- Phase 2.98 extraction plan, Phase 2.99 machine-seed program and Phase 3 readiness gate;
- Platform API/Adapter Matrix and API base-URL/endpoint seed register;
- Legal Depot platform coverage and cross-platform disclaimer matrix;
- articleization/autopublication manifest;
- platform-state, current-validation and public-correction registers;
- Education & Community corridor;
- Education/Community Progression Agent, work queues and adversarial evaluations.

The integrated control layer blocks score/credential/license/role/authority collapse, unsupported professional claims, Commons identity-authority escalation, historical ThriveAlumni government-language reuse and silent Digital Business Academy merger.
