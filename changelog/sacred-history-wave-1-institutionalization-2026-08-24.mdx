## Change

CrownThrive added a private, versioned Sacred History interoperability foundation around the existing KJV Visualized and Sermon Toolkit production property. The foundation separates traditions, texts, sources, claims, evidence, relationships, events, reviews, routes, product candidates, custody, and commerce bindings. It does not replace the KJV reader, migrate payment providers, mint a wallet, issue economic authority, or publish unreviewed Sacred History content.

The first product-planning run generated exactly 1,000 unique, source-linked blueprints across 50 product-enabled topics and 20 formats. These are `sourcing` candidates under commercial `HOLD`; they are not finished products. Strict completion remains zero because no candidate yet has the full evidence, CIE, CHLOM, asset, Drive-readback, license, ThriveEvergreen SKU, entitlement, delivery, and production-test chain.

Current machine accounting therefore records 1,000 blueprints and zero completed SKUs.
