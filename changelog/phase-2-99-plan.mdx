## Mission

Convert the Phase 2.98 platform intelligence and all prior institutional knowledge into a protected, executable seed package so Phase 3.0 can begin without rediscovery, data leakage, vendor lock-in or ambiguous authority.

Phase 2.99 is also the formal **Help Center Articleization & Documentation Projection seed**. Article bodies are already being reconstructed in Phase 2.98; 2.99 makes the recovered article estate machine-addressable, versioned and automatically maintainable before engineering begins.

## Ten-phase roadmap inheritance

The canonical future institutional-build roadmap is now the [Ten-Phase Institutional Program Charter](/standards/ten-phase-institutional-program-charter), adopted through [CT-ADR-ROADMAP-010](/changelog/adr-ten-phase-institutional-roadmap-migration). If new evidence changes future objectives, dependencies, hard entry/exit gates, checkpoints, acceptance tests, authority, risks, automation responsibilities, rollback or handoff criteria, the affected future phase plan must update in the same governed documentation-impact cycle or open an explicit delta. A future roadmap document is not allowed to remain stale merely because it predates the new evidence.
