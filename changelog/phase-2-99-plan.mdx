# Phase 2.99 — Private Core and Machine Seed Program

## Mission

Convert the Phase 2.98 platform intelligence and all prior institutional knowledge into a protected, executable seed package so Phase 3.0 can begin without rediscovery, data leakage, vendor lock-in or ambiguous authority.

Phase 2.99 is also the formal **Help Center Articleization & Documentation Projection seed**. Article bodies are already being reconstructed in Phase 2.98; 2.99 makes the recovered article estate machine-addressable, versioned and automatically maintainable before engineering begins.

## Workstream 0 — Articleization, docs-impact and publication manifests

Produce the documentation seed required by the Documentation Source-of-Truth & Autonomous Governance Standard:

- assign a stable ID, category, platform/corridor, audience, recovery class, source state, authority tier, owner and disposition to every recovered Help Center title;
- reconcile the recovered 795-title inventory against current Mintlify pages without inventing missing bodies;
- require substantive versioned bodies for P0/P1 customer, operator, developer, legal, rights/licensing, commerce and support categories, or an explicit `source_not_recovered`/`owner_input_required` record;
- create article/navigation/redirect/canonical manifests that can be generated and validated;
- preserve prior article versions, redirects and effective periods;
- seed the mandatory `docs_updated | docs_no_change | docs_delta_opened` contract for future code/config/site changes;
- map source-code repositories to documentation targets as those repositories are connected;
- define changelog generation, public-claim refresh and documentation drift queues;
- register `docs.crownthrive.io`, `api.crownthrive.io`, `mcp.crownthrive.io` and `holdings.crownthrive.com` as target hosts with independent deployment evidence states;
- ensure Mintlify remains a replaceable projection service with Git/off-provider recovery.

## Exit criteria

Phase 2.99 is complete when:

- the recovered article estate has a machine-readable article/category/version/disposition manifest and P0/P1 required categories have substantive versioned bodies or explicit unresolved-source states;
- docs-impact and automated changelog/drift contracts are implementation-ready, with source-repository integration gaps explicitly registered;
- Virality rights/readiness/universe/price/GSO seeds, AdLuxe provider/inventory/campaign/reporting seeds and KJV room/source/release/catalog/article/commerce seeds validate without invented production state;
- the two unresolved KJV room identities remain explicit unresolved records unless authoritative evidence is recovered;
- historical Virality pricing and current public starting-point schedules remain separately versioned and cannot both resolve as simultaneously effective defaults;
- provider capability, CrownThrive account entitlement and deployed integration state remain separate fields for AdLuxe and every other vendor-backed platform;
- private/public boundaries are deployed or fully implementation-ready;
- schemas and seed records validate and resolve references;
- current platform/API state imports without invented data;
- secrets/credentials have controlled ownership;
- infrastructure and recovery are reproducible;
- approval/event/correction/evaluation contracts are executable;
- first service and platform-adapter skeletons can be generated from the seed;
- Phase 3.0 run packets, backlog and acceptance tests are ready;
- PRs/commits/deployments and next-run state are exact.
