## Material reconciliations

### DLA

Historical **Decentralized Licensing Authority** is preserved as source-era authority architecture. Current **Dynamic Licensing Asset** remains the machine-readable license/permission object, with licensing stewardship/issuer authority modeled separately.

### DAIL / DAL

Current **DAIL = Decentralized Autonomous Information Ledger** remains the canonical event/evidence/audit/correction lineage. Historical DAL meanings remain source-period records for attestation, adjudication and distribution-related concepts.

### LEX

LEX is reconstructed as a rights-gated licensing lifecycle rather than a generic marketplace. Rights, DLA, listing, payment, agreement, entitlement, fulfillment, settlement and remedies remain separate states.

### TLaaS / CaaS / dS-CaaS

Both recovered TLaaS expansions remain explicit: **Tokenized Licensing as a Service** and **Trust Layer as a Service**. CaaS remains Compliance-as-a-Service. dS-CaaS remains a distinct current governance/runtime label whose exact expansion and relationship must remain source/ADR controlled if conflicting records exist.

### Cases and overrides

Historical adjudication, guardianship, pause and override concepts map to modern cases, holds, appeals, remedies and bounded authority. An override does not erase the control or evidence it overrides.

### Royalties and settlement

Economic calculation, obligation, authorization, provider execution, provider evidence and institutional settlement are separate stages. Historical royalty or split examples do not become current economics through reconstruction.

## 795-title accounting

The recovered Help Center denominator remains **795 titles**. Reconstruction counts increase only when substantive source-grounded bodies are actually written or recovered. Unresolved, restricted and source-not-located records remain explicit rather than filled by inference.

## Founder adjudication

Founder review remains available after publication. A later ruling creates a dated correction/supersession record and propagates to affected terminology, sources, platform/framework records, policies, articles, changelogs and machine manifests while preserving prior source meaning.
