## Decision

Extend CrownThrive's prospective institutional-build roadmap from ten top-level phases to twenty. Preserve every Phase 1–10 identifier, subphase, pass, workstream, acceptance record and historical statement. The migration changes future planning granularity; it does not rewrite institutional history.

## Rationale

The first ten phases remain useful, but the growing institutional program now needs independently governed boundaries for replication, enterprise federation, standards/certification, autonomous operations, knowledge/model sovereignty, treasury/settlement, global IP exchange, civic/institutional partnerships, intergenerational oversight and perpetual continuity.

Separating these concerns prevents a later phase from using broad labels such as "scale" or "continuity" to silently inherit authority that should have its own hard gate.

1. Institutional Mapping
2. Institutional Recovery, Reconciliation & Documentation
3. Executable Institutional Core
4. Federated Ecosystem Activation
5. Revenue & Market Activation
6. Licensing, IP & Developer Economy
7. Physical, Phygital & Regional Expansion
8. Holdings, Capital & Portfolio Scale
9. Advanced CHLOM & Interoperable Infrastructure
10. Generational Continuity, Sovereign Scale & Institutional Permanence
11. Institutional Replication
12. Enterprise Federation
13. Standards & Certification
14. Autonomous Operations
15. Knowledge & Model Sovereignty
16. Treasury & Settlement
17. Global IP Exchange
18. Civic & Institutional Partnerships
19. Intergenerational Oversight
20. Perpetual Institutional Continuity

## Phase 2.99 effect

No Phase 2.99 gate is weakened. Phase 3 remains blocked pending the current hard-exit plus the founder full-documentation, Handbook and brand/domain/portfolio acceptance requirements.

## Phase 20 documentation-domain decision

The default Mintlify deployment domain remains valid for documentation through Phases 2.99–19 unless later governance changes the decision. Full documentation-domain custody, DNS/TLS/provider-exit continuity and intentional custom-domain activation/certification become a Phase 20 hard gate. Historical domain evidence is preserved and absence of a custom Mintlify domain is not falsely marked PASS.

## Change-propagation rule

Every material pass must evaluate downstream effects through Phase 20 and either update affected future phase objectives/gates or open an explicit governed delta. No future phase stays stale merely because it predates new evidence.

## Relationship to prior charter

The [Ten-Phase Institutional Program Charter](/standards/ten-phase-institutional-program-charter) remains a preserved roadmap-generation artifact. The [Twenty-Phase Institutional Program Charter](/standards/twenty-phase-institutional-program-charter) is the current future-planning projection once this ADR packet is accepted through repository governance.

## Rollback

Rollback means restoring `ten_phase_v1` as the current planning projection through a new explicit ADR. It never means deleting this decision or the Phase 11–20 planning history.
