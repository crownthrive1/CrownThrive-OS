## Virality Music Release 214 Storefront Reconciliation

**Release date:** August 20, 2026  
**Production property:** [vm.crownthrive.com](https://vm.crownthrive.com)  
**Documentation issue:** [#162](https://github.com/crownthrive1/CrownThrive-OS/issues/162)

## Decision

Keep the verified **314 paid** and **2 free** Virality Direct products available. Do not mass-activate gated, provider-specific, inquiry, preview, licensing, blocked or retired records. A Stripe catalog object is not sufficient proof of checkout, entitlement or exact protected delivery.

## Verified production snapshot

The release-214 production read reconciled **350 governed commerce records**:

| State | Count | Meaning |
|---|---:|---|
| Paid available | 314 | Exact source-controlled activation evidence and live Stripe Product/Price mapping |
| Free available | 2 | No-charge governed delivery |
| Gated or non-retail | 34 | Provider, rights, inquiry, preview, licensing, blocked, retired or other non-storefront state |
| Total governed | 350 | Complete D1 product-record universe in the release snapshot |

The 314 paid records resolve to:

- 243-product core snapshot;
- 40 Coloring V5 products;
- 5 ZaZa V5 publications;
- 13 approved book editions;
- 4 wall-art tranche-001 products;
- 1 Rosetta 111-page release;
- 8 companion tranche-001 products.

Every available paid row had a Stripe Product and Price mapping in the release read. The fulfillment registry contained **319 active product-version rows** and **827 ready product-file rows**. These counts are dated evidence, not timeless guarantees.

## Release 212 → 214 reconciliation

Release 213 was already deployed after the release-212 handoff. The August 20 custody work registered the supplied source files in governed private storage controls but did not change titles, prices, checkout, entitlements or storefront availability.

Release 214 made public/admin truth corrections:

- removed stale 243- and 306-product activation claims;
- added a read-only SUPER_ADMIN Storefront State module;
- separated the 350-record governed universe from the 316-product available storefront;
- added a fail-closed product-state partition check;
- renamed the empty controlled-commerce doorway to Commerce Activation Archive;
- preserved signed-webhook entitlement, account retrieval, expiring-download, refund/dispute and personal-license controls.

The build, lint and 67 focused commerce/account/admin/license tests passed before deployment.

## Book and edition boundary

The 13 approved retail book products are active with screen PDF and EPUB 3 delivery. They map to 13 edition rows and 12 publishing-work rows because the two *What Really Happened* retail editions share one work.

Legacy edition fields such as `SOURCE_VERIFIED_NOT_ACTIVE` and `NOT_ACTIVE` describe private-source, canon, print, portrait, GSO and provider-reader governance. Checkout does not consume those fields. They must not be overwritten to represent Virality Direct retail state.

The safe Phase 3 model is additive:

1. link each edition to its commerce product and SKU;
2. preserve every existing source/canon/print/provider field;
3. derive `DIGITAL_RETAIL_ACTIVE` only while the linked commerce product, version, rights/artifact state and ready files continue to pass;
4. keep both *What Really Happened* edition links distinct.

## Remaining activation queue

### Companion tranche 002 — 8

All eight Stripe Product/Price mappings exist, but the exact approved workbook/Canva ZIPs and release manifests are not recoverable from the current governed stores. Rebuild or recover the exact packages, upload them to protected storage, create version/file records, attach checksums and activation evidence, then run exact unpaid checkout and fulfillment tests.

### Wall-art tranche 002 — 6

All six Stripe Product/Price mappings exist, but the exact approved retail ZIPs/manifests are not recoverable. Older wall-art and companion-art products are not substitutes. Supported print sizes, 300-DPI claims, checksums, protected storage and exact delivery must be verified before activation.

### AI/music A01–A25 — 25

Technical masters were previously reported as built, but the retail cohort is still gated:

- only A01–A03 exist in Stripe;
- current Library custody is missing A17–A20;
- editorial/legal, accessibility, native-device, protected-fulfillment and exact-product tests remain open.

A03's existing active $54.99 Price is now assigned as its Stripe Product default. Its metadata remains catalog-only with checkout and fulfillment gates open.
