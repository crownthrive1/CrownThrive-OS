## CHLOM commercial parent-child authority — 2026-08-24

## Decision

Founder Kavonte Jones Sr directly authorized the CHLOM repository relationship to be institutionalized as follows:

- `crownthrive1/CrownThrive-OS` is the private CrownThrive parent for institutional product policy, protected implementation custody, commercial release policy and factory controls.
- `crownthrive/chlom-protocol` is the public, monetized commercial CHLOM repository.
- The public child is painted by CrownThrive OS and receives a bounded public-safe commercial overlay from the parent.
- The Framework Factory / Execution Builder pattern is used to materialize and validate the projection.

## Authority boundaries

The Founder-direct instruction authorizes bounded D2 repository wiring, documentation, manifests, validation, projection construction and candidate pull-request creation.

It does not authorize:

- publishing secrets or trade secrets;
- direct write to the public child's main branch;
- force-push;
- self-certification;
- invented pricing or checkout state;
- invented public API state;
- invented token or mainnet activation;
- economic settlement or money movement;
- waiver of child-side verification.
