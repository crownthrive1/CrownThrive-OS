## Native provider bootstrap

```text
pre-state
  -> write and save
  -> exact source/version readback
  -> manifest/feed/peer/build/security checks
  -> rollback to frozen pre-state
  -> rollback verification
  -> exact loader reapply
  -> final provider readback
```

No site/project was deleted, no deletion capability was granted, and current access policies were preserved.

Six projects remain native source-mutation `HOLD`:

- KJV Visualized / Sermon Toolkit: provider source checkout failed before mutation;
- Virality Music: concurrent provider and local source advancement made overwrite unsafe;
- CrownThrive Sites, CrownThrive Holdings, CrownThrive Investor Room, and AdLuxe Network: registered and centrally readable, but source mutation was not attempted while the unsuperseded control-plane certification mismatch remains open.

The four successful provider cycles do not activate their institutional source-bootstrap bindings. All 10 overall results remain `HOLD` until the control mismatch is superseded with evidence and the remaining site-specific predicates pass.
