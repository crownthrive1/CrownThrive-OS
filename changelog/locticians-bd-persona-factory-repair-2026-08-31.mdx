# Locticians BD, Avery & PentaFactory Repair — 2026-08-31

<Note>
  This page records **observed and changed production state** from August 31, 2026. It does not expose API keys or grant new provider, D3, financial, legal, or destructive authority.

## Executive disposition

The Locticians cold-outreach incident was not a PentaMailer transport outage. The initial failure chain was upstream: the planner had no new cold-eligible recipients, research replenishment was starved by a seeder-selection defect, and the Brilliant Directories bulk member-list route was degraded. Avery Cole existed and was approved, but her campaign binding and recipient-production path were not explicit enough to guarantee continuous work production.

The repair restored the production flow while preserving suppression, bounce, complaint, reply, prior-attempt, risk, working-hours, rate, and provider controls.

## Avery / cold-outreach repair

Canonical persona: `ct.persona.locticians.member-success.avery.v1`.

Avery remains a persona/playbook owner, **not** the mail transport. The governed execution path is:

`Locticians prospect sources → eligibility/suppression → Avery campaign assignment → Avery personalization/playbook → outreach schedule → PentaMarketer dispatcher → PentaMail/PentaMailer → provider → acceptance/readback → Avery follow-up state`.

Repairs applied:

- corrected research-queue starvation so the seeder advances past terminal `complete/hold` targets instead of repeatedly selecting them;
- explicitly bound Avery to the Locticians claim campaign while retaining PentaMarketer/PentaMail transport authority;
- restored newly validated cold-eligible contacts without recycling previously attempted recipients;
- preserved four-minute send spacing and all suppression/risk controls;
- extended PentaCrawler with bounded same-domain Contact/Booking/About evidence-page discovery, without guessed URLs or cross-domain crawling;
- corrected the crawler's queue-kind mapping after a canary exposed a schema mismatch; the failed canary rolled back before follow-up rows committed.

Production readback during the repair confirmed new Avery cold sends through the normal scheduler with zero send failures at the observed checkpoint.
