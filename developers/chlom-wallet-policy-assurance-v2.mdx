# CHLOM Wallet Policy Assurance v2

This controlled-test layer converts the Wallet Agent's skills, WalletKit pallets, source evidence, authority ceilings, handoff separation, and hard operational boundaries into deterministic policy decisions. It does **not** create production authority.

## Decision contract

Every evaluated intent resolves to exactly one public-safe disposition:

- `ECAC` — eligible controlled-action candidate only. This is not sovereign approval, commerce activation, a rights grant, a merge authorization, or a phase transition.
- `HOLD` — more evidence, fresher identity/heartbeat state, independent review, rollback evidence, or semantic resolution is required.
- `DENY` — the requested action crosses a hard boundary or exceeds the controlled-test authority envelope.

The source rulepack is `ct.rulepack.chlom-wallet.policy-assurance.v2@2.0.0`. It is limited to A2/D2 and non-production environments.

## AI counsel boundary

AI may provide sanitized advisory signals and rationale. The AI Counsel Firewall always preserves the deterministic disposition. It records disagreement and attempted upgrades, but `accepted_as_final=false` and `ai_final_authority=false` are invariant.

This means an AI suggestion cannot convert `DENY` to `HOLD`, `DENY` to `ECAC`, or `HOLD` to `ECAC`. AI can help explain, prioritize, and identify missing evidence; it cannot manufacture authority.

## Skill-to-pallet closure

The policy crosswalk explicitly maps all 20 Wallet Agent v2 skills plus the three Phase C skills to all 12 WalletKit component pallets. The mapping itself grants no capability. It exists so CI and THIVEBASE can detect orphaned skills, unmapped pallets, invalid bundle references, and accidental authority inheritance.

## Hard boundaries

This tranche does not authorize or perform:

- production credential activation;
- provider writes;
- custody or key export;
- token issuance;
- money movement or settlement;
- production rights grants;
- testnet or mainnet broadcast;
- public effective pricing;
- Stripe Product/Price/Checkout creation;
- merge authorization;
- Phase 3 advancement.

The five independent ERC-4337 reviewer heartbeats and receipts remain outside this tranche and must not be refreshed or fabricated by the Wallet Agent.
