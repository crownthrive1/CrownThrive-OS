## CHLOM Wallet Production Contract v1

**Effective:** August 23, 2026  
**Wallet:** `ct.wallet.system.chlom-wallet-os`  
**Runtime:** Production / active  
**Custody mode:** Non-custodial

CHLOM Wallet is an institutional wallet/economic-evidence control plane. It records governed provider receipts, settlement provenance, value class, reversals, and chain-hashed ledger events. Production activation does **not** convert CHLOM Wallet into a custodian of customer cryptocurrency.

## Stripe adapters

Two Stripe funding classes are distinguished:

- Fiat payment processing: `ct.adapter.stripe.wallet.v1`
- Stablecoin payments: `ct.adapter.stripe.stablecoin-payments.v1`

The stablecoin adapter is deployed in production and fails closed at the provider-enablement boundary.

### Stablecoin tender model

The adapter is modeled for Stripe-supported stablecoin payments including USDC, USDP, and USDG on provider-supported networks. Stripe handles customer-wallet interaction and provider settlement. CHLOM Wallet records the payment as evidence and does not claim custody of the customer token.

When a verified Crown Credits top-up settles, the Crown Credits ledger automatically projects a secret-free settlement event into the CHLOM Wallet append-only evidence ledger. If the Stripe payment method is crypto, the event is tagged with the stablecoin adapter and stablecoin tender provenance.

The projection never creates an entitlement, rights grant, public claim, or on-chain custody by inference.

## Production settlement authority

Production Stripe reconciliation is readback-authoritative. A provider callback or success status is evidence, not final economic truth.

The system independently reads Stripe provider objects before recording a verified settlement. A live webhook may accelerate processing when a provider destination is available, but loss or absence of a dedicated destination does not weaken the readback verification rule.

## Provider capability state

The adapter may be `production` while the provider state is `provider_enablement_pending`. This distinction is mandatory.

Current Stripe platform configuration is machine-read before use. If Stripe reports crypto unavailable, no Checkout flow may pretend crypto is enabled.

## Hard boundaries

- No secret material in responses.
- No provider success → entitlement inference.
- No provider success → rights grant.
- No provider success → public performance claim.
- No stablecoin tender → CHLOM custody inference.
- No Crown Credits → crypto-asset inference.
- No deletion of governed ledger history.
- Provider reversals append compensating evidence; history is not silently rewritten.
