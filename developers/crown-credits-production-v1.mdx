## Crown Credits Production Contract v1

**Effective:** August 23, 2026  
**Program:** `ct.credit.store.v1`  
**Runtime:** `crown-credits-control`  
**State:** Production / active

Crown Credits are CrownThrive LLC closed-loop internal purchasing credits. They are nontransferable, non-cash-redeemable, non-interest-bearing, and are not cryptocurrency or an on-chain asset.

## Production funding rail

Stripe is the production funding provider. The live Crown Credits catalog uses one governed product with five fixed one-time top-up offers. Amounts are resolved server-side from exact governed offer records; clients cannot submit arbitrary funding amounts.

Production settlement uses **provider readback reconciliation** as economic truth. A redirect, browser success page, webhook delivery, or provider-side success label alone cannot issue credits. The backend independently reads the Checkout Session, PaymentIntent, Charge, amount, currency, exact Price/Product mapping, tax state, and idempotency state before the Crown Credits ledger is mutated.

A scheduled reconciliation pass runs every ten minutes for pending live top-ups.

## Stablecoin funding

Crown Credits may be funded through Stripe stablecoin payments when Stripe makes that payment method available on the platform account. Stablecoin is a **tender**, not the stored-value class of Crown Credits.

When a stablecoin payment is used:

1. Stripe handles the customer wallet and stablecoin payment.
2. Stripe settles the completed payment to the Stripe balance in fiat value.
3. CrownThrive independently reads the settled provider objects.
4. Crown Credits are issued only after exact readback validation.
5. CHLOM Wallet records stablecoin tender provenance and settlement evidence.
6. Crown Credits remain closed-loop internal credits and do not become crypto assets.

Current provider enablement is represented explicitly. The CrownThrive runtime may be production-active while the Stripe stablecoin payment method remains `PROVIDER_ENABLEMENT_PENDING`.

## Economic authority

Crown Credits operate under ThriveEvergreen economic governance and CHLOM evidence controls.

Provider evidence is not institutional economic truth by itself. Payment success cannot directly manufacture an entitlement, public performance claim, rights grant, or economic authority.

Canonical MCP operations include:

- `crown_credits.catalog.read`
- `crown_credits.balance.read`
- `crown_credits.topup.create`
- `crown_credits.topup.reconcile`
- `crown_credits.purchase`

Mutation operations are bounded to exact governed offers and idempotent requests.

## Non-negotiable boundaries

- No peer-to-peer credit transfer.
- No cash withdrawal or redemption.
- No interest.
- No crypto-credit representation.
- No arbitrary client-submitted price.
- No entitlement from redirect alone.
- No raw provider secrets in API/MCP output.
- Refund, dispute, or reversal state must reconcile through the institutional ledger.
