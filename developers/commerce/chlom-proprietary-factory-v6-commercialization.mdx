# Generation 6 Commercialization Candidates

Generation 6 stages 40 candidate offers across eight commercial families and five offer forms. These records are commercialization hypotheses, not activated products or revenue guarantees.

## Commercial families

- Institutional Capability OS
- Agent Factory License
- Cultural Intelligence API
- Evidence Certification Service
- Continuity Vault Service
- Ecosystem Wiring Suite
- Monetization Optimizer
- Governed Prompt and Skill Marketplace

## Offer forms

Each family supports Starter, Professional, Enterprise, OEM / White Label, and Strategic Ecosystem License candidates.

Price discovery ranges span low-cost subscriptions through custom multi-year enterprise licensing. Crown Credits equivalents may be bound after the governed pricing, tax, accounting, refund, and entitlement standards are certified.

## Activation gates

Every candidate remains `CANDIDATE_ONLY` with `checkout_enabled=false` and `entitlement_active=false`. Activation requires:

1. rights and licensing clearance;
2. pricing and tax certification;
3. private fulfillment and entitlement testing;
4. security and privacy acceptance;
5. support and service-level commitments;
6. refund and dispute handling;
7. founder or delegated governed release authority.

The current release therefore establishes monetizable inventory and licensing architecture without creating uncontrolled commerce, promises of valuation, or unsupported revenue claims.
