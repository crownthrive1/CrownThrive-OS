7. sidecar classes keep vote false, quorum false, authority inheritance false and external scheduler false;
8. patch candidates default `apply_authorized=false`;

## Stress test

Run `chlom_runtime.agentic_foundry_stress_test_v1()` at least twice without state mutation. Require `PASS` and identical deterministic evidence digests for unchanged state. After a controlled state change, rerun and require the new digest to reflect the change while invariants remain PASS.

## PatchPilot and AssetForge boundaries

A patch candidate is not permission to apply it. Application requires exact snapshot, tests, security review, rollback probe, independent verification and ordinary target-specific authority. D3 is always human-reserved. Provider writes, money movement, rights/entitlements and credential changes retain their narrower contracts.

AssetForge may create and route protected candidates. It may not manufacture ownership, provenance, patent status, publication authority, price, checkout, entitlement or settlement.
