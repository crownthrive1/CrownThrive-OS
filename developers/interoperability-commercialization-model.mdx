# Interoperability Commercialization Model

**State:** research / governed HOLD.  
**Plugin:** `ct.plugin.crownthrive-interoperability-fabric`.  
**Checkout:** disabled.  
**Exact prices:** not authorized.

The commercialization model converts certified interoperability capability into bounded products without selling or exposing CrownThrive's protected implementation.

## Candidate product lanes

| Tier | Intended customer | Candidate value |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Developer Connector SDK | Individual developer or one integration project | public contracts, schema validation, local test adapters and route-planning support |
| Team Interoperability Hub | One organization's integration team | shared registry, mapping workspace, certification queue and observability |
| Enterprise Interoperability Fabric | Private or hybrid enterprise deployment | tenant controls, SSO/RBAC integration, certified adapters, audit exports and priority support |
| Managed Integration & Certification | Organizations requiring implementation support | requirements, adapter build, mapping, negative testing, certification package and handoff |
| Certified Adapter Marketplace — Future | Providers and developers | future submission, certification and licensed distribution under disclosed commercial terms |

## Unit-economics contract

Every tier has a research-only model. Exact pricing cannot be generated until the following inputs are supported by current evidence:

- fixed platform and infrastructure cost;
- expected variable execution cost;
- support hours and support labor cost;
- certification and recertification burden;
- provider/schema change frequency;
- security, continuity and support reserve;
- required gross-margin floor;
- current market comparables across at least two independent sources;
- license scope, rights, fulfillment and update obligations.

The candidate floor formula is conceptually:

`(fixed cost + expected usage + support + certification amortization + risk reserve) / (1 - gross-margin floor)`

The exact model inputs, calibration and commercial decision logic remain restricted.

## Pricing integrity

The following are prohibited:

- invented MSRP or fake discounts;
- treating a competitor's price as proof of CrownThrive's price;
- activating checkout before rights, pricing, fulfillment, security and tax/legal boundaries are resolved;
- hiding usage or overage economics;
- transferring protected algorithms or adapter source merely because capability access is licensed;
- undisclosed paid ranking in a future adapter marketplace.

## Commercial lifecycle

`research -> comparable evidence -> candidate economics -> independent review -> pricing authorization -> fulfillment certification -> checkout staged -> live`

A commercial tier may remain useful and testable while pricing is on HOLD. Technical construction should continue without manufacturing a live offer.
