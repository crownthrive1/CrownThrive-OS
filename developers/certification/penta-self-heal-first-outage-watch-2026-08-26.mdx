## PentaSELF Heal-First Outage Watch Production Proof

## Incident

At `2026-08-26T21:40:00Z`, the State Architecture outage watcher observed one required scheduler gap while `ct-penta-self-v1` was reconciling the same scheduler boundary. The concrete drift was `thivebase_self_diagnostic_v1`: its owned schedule is `3-58/5 * * * *`, while the observed schedule at detection was every minute. PentaSELF healed the drift in the same reconciliation cycle.

The defect was not inability to heal. The defect was notification ordering: the outage watcher could sample the pre-heal state and enqueue an outage before the concurrent PentaSELF repair committed.

## Production repair

Provider migration: `20260826215358_penta_self_heal_first_outage_watch_v1_0_1`.

The production repair:

1. Makes `public.penta_mail_outage_watch_v1()` heal-first for scheduler gaps.
2. Re-reads `public.penta_self_status_v1()` after the bounded PentaSELF reconciliation attempt.
3. Declares `scheduler_gap` active only when the gap remains after reconciliation/readback.
4. Preserves fail-closed behavior for persistent gaps; no authority is manufactured.
5. Staggers `penta-mail-outage-watch-v1` to `1-55/6 * * * *` to reduce same-minute collisions with `ct-penta-self-v1` (`*/2 * * * *`).
6. Adds the outage watcher itself to `penta_self.required_jobs_v1`, making it PentaSELF-owned and automatically repairable.
7. Advances PentaSELF to `1.0.1` and PentaMail to `1.1.0` in the production system registry.

## Controlled production replay

At `2026-08-26T21:54:31Z`, the prior drift was deliberately replayed by changing `thivebase_self_diagnostic_v1` from `3-58/5 * * * *` to every minute and invoking the production outage watcher.

This proves the watcher now invokes healing before outage notification and suppresses the false/transient scheduler-gap alert only after successful readback.
