## ThriveEvergreen Production Authority v2

**Effective:** August 23, 2026  
**Policy:** `ct.policy.thriveevergreen-autonomous-publisher.v2`  
**Runtime mode:** `production_write`  
**Publication activation vocabulary:** `ECAC | HOLD | DENY`

ThriveEvergreen is CrownThrive's Commerce & Economic Activation Authority. It is a cross-platform economic-execution subprotocol of CHLOM.

> ThriveEvergreen may optimize within authority. It may never manufacture authority.

## Production rule

The service is production-active, but production status is not a blanket approval for every candidate.

Every economic/publication candidate still resolves through exact gates. `ECAC` permits only the bounded action covered by the exact authority envelope. `HOLD` preserves state without unauthorized execution. `DENY` blocks execution.

Provider success is evidence, not economic truth. Stripe, fulfillment, delivery, webhook, API, or MCP success cannot directly create an invoice, earning, payout, entitlement, public performance claim, settlement, or rights grant without institutional reconciliation.

## Authority envelope

Production automation remains bounded to D0-D2 operations within explicit founder and institutional authority. D3 authority remains human-reserved.

Production execution retains these hard boundaries:

- no self-approval;
- no delete;
- no autonomous money movement;
- no autonomous production rights grant;
- no credential or secret return;
- exact version/content binding;
- independent evidence/readback where required;
- rollback/compensation support where the adapter allows it.

## Stripe and Crown Credits

Stripe platform/Connect are production providers. Crown Credits are production-active as a closed-loop internal credit program.

The legacy publisher candidate gate continues to fail closed where a candidate-specific Stripe/Drive/authorization requirement is incomplete. This does not place the ThriveEvergreen runtime back into observer mode; it means the production runtime correctly returns HOLD for that exact candidate.

Stablecoin support is represented as an explicit provider capability state. The CHLOM Wallet stablecoin adapter is in production, but Stripe stablecoin payment availability must be confirmed by provider readback before a Crypto payment method is offered.

## API and MCP operations

Canonical production MCP operations include:

- `thriveevergreen.status`
- `thriveevergreen.publish.evaluate`
- `thriveevergreen.publish.dispatch`

`publish.dispatch` cannot bypass an exact HOLD or DENY, cannot invent price/rights authority, and cannot convert provider evidence directly into institutional truth.

## Production status interpretation

`production_write` means the execution engine is active and authorized to perform bounded writes **when exact ECAC gates pass**.

It does not mean:

- all candidates are approved;
- provider limitations are ignored;
- failed health/custody/rights gates are bypassed;
- money movement is automatically authorized;
- every Stripe capability is available.
