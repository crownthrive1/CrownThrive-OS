# SOP and Runbook Registry

The recovered institutional corpus contains operating procedures across platform support, commerce, media, CHLOM, MM Suites, legal/policy administration, partner operations and incident response. These should become a governed **SOP registry**, not isolated documents with unknown currency.

## Required SOP metadata

Each procedure should record:

- stable SOP/runbook ID;
- title and purpose;
- corridor/platform;
- triggering conditions;
- accountable owner and backup owner;
- authorized roles;
- systems/tools used;
- prerequisites and required evidence;
- step sequence;
- stop/escalation conditions;
- approval gates;
- rollback/recovery path;
- outputs and DAIL events;
- related policy/license/security records;
- effective date and review cadence;
- current/superseded/retired status.

## Procedure classes

- routine operations;
- product/content publishing;
- onboarding and offboarding;
- commerce and fulfillment;
- billing/refund/dispute handling;
- support and escalation;
- rights intake and corrections;
- security and incident response;
- deployment and rollback;
- vendor/domain/license renewal;
- data export, backup and restoration;
- platform migration and sunset;
- physical/location operations where applicable.

## Automation standard

A procedure becomes automation-ready only after its inputs, outputs, permissions, failure modes, evidence, rollback and human gates are explicit. Codex or an agent should never infer an irreversible SOP from prose alone.

## Phase 3 implication

The Orchestrator should route work by SOP/workflow IDs and log which procedure version governed each execution.
