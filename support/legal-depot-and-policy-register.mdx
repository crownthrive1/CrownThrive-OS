## Legal Depot and Policy Register

The recovered Help Center contains a substantial **Legal Depot / Legal and Compliance** layer. The institutional system should preserve this as a controlled policy register rather than scattering terms across individual websites.

## Policy families

The register should track at least:

- master/general terms of service;
- privacy and cookie policies;
- acceptable use and community conduct;
- membership and Hybrid Incubator policies;
- program-specific terms and graduation/contribution rules;
- platform addenda;
- subscription, cancellation and refund policies;
- digital-product and download terms;
- marketplace, seller, creator and contributor terms;
- affiliate and ambassador policies;
- advertiser, publisher and campaign terms;
- intellectual-property, DMCA and licensing notices;
- CrownThrive Master Licensing and Rights Architecture plus platform-specific license schedules/addenda;
- AI-assisted/generated-content, human-authorship, provider-terms, synthetic voice/likeness, model-training and data-use policies;
- cross-platform disclaimer/material-disclosure profiles for professional information, AI, forecasts, advertising, affiliates, marketplaces, ministry, wellness, education and regulated relationships;
- CHLOM / CHLOMLex legal and rights notices;
- event and ticketing terms;
- service-level and support policies;
- accessibility and communications policies;
- disclaimers and regulated-category restrictions;
- platform lifecycle, migration and sunset notices;
- data-processing, retention and security-related notices where applicable.

## Policy record

Every policy should have a stable ID, title, scope, entities/brands covered, jurisdictions or audience if relevant, effective date, revision date, superseded version, owner, approval authority, publication URLs, incorporated documents and source evidence.

## Incorporation and precedence

The register should explicitly state which general terms apply ecosystem-wide and which platform/program addenda modify them. A newer product page should not silently override an adopted policy.

## Legal boundary

This documentation inventories CrownThrive policy architecture. It does not independently establish that a clause is legally sufficient, that a regulatory regime applies, or that professional review has occurred. Material legal structures, regulated offerings, privacy obligations, franchise arrangements, securities implications and enforceability require qualified review.

## Phase 3 implication

The Rules Registry should be able to reference adopted policy versions by stable ID and effective date. Agents may identify the relevant policy and flag conflicts, but material legal interpretation and enforcement decisions remain human-governed.
