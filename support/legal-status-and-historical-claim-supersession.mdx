# Legal Status and Historical Claim Supersession

CrownThrive's early prospectuses and technical papers contain ambitious legal, investment, franchise, trademark, patent, token and smart-contract language. They are valuable proof of design history and IP development. They are not automatically accurate statements of current legal or production status.

## Source-preservation rule

Do not rewrite historical sources. Preserve the original file, date, wording, classification and context. Create a current status overlay that tells humans and agents what may be stated or implemented now.

## Historical claims requiring explicit current verification

- patent-pending or provisional-patent status;
- trademark application or registration;
- franchise/FDD readiness or registration;
- active franchisor status;
- regional licenses or territories granted;
- equity, preferred interests, conversion rights or investor participation;
- guaranteed or lifetime overrides;
- assignable/inheritable/resalable licenses;
- securities, tokenized interests or token economics;
- CHLOM smart contracts or blockchain enforcement in production;
- CHLOM.io or other named production services;
- legal-advisor approval;
- operational subsidiaries or HoldCo entities;
- nonprofit, charitable, tax-deductible or investment-fund status;
- specific regulatory registrations or nationwide authorization.

## Current CrownThrive posture

Unless supported by current verified evidence:

- CHLOM is a recovered, cloud-first, multi-tenant, modular, API-first, evidence-driven and human-governed build program; blockchain is optional later-stage infrastructure, not an MVP dependency.
- MM Suites is foundational CrownThrive IP, a phygital operating architecture and franchise/regional-license readiness program. Actual franchise offerings, territory grants, investment interests and regulated relationships remain legal-activation gated.
- Historical patent-pending wording is superseded for current public use because no verified current filing has been established.
- `™` use indicates claimed brand identity, not verified registration.
- CII is a for-profit CrownThrive, LLC initiative, not a nonprofit.
- ThriveFund is an internal impact/reinvestment mechanism, not automatically a regulated investment fund or charity.
- Holdings remains a strategic stewardship architecture until entity formation and intercompany records are verified.

## Legal Depot behavior

The Legal Depot should resolve the correct terms/status by:

- effective date;
- jurisdiction;
- entity;
- platform/program;
- role;
- product/transaction;
- agreement version;
- acceptance evidence;
- supersession and amendment history.

## Agent rule

No agent may convert a historical prospectus statement into a current legal representation, contract, investor solicitation, franchise offer, filing claim or production-status claim. Agents may extract requirements, identify counsel questions, draft controlled internal work product and prepare activation checklists.

## Activation gate

Before a legally consequential model becomes active, require the applicable current package, which may include entity authority, counsel review, registrations/filings, disclosures, signed agreements, accounting/tax treatment, payment/settlement design, privacy/security review, operational support and DAIL evidence.

## Public correction

Where an old public page materially overstates current legal status, create an approved correction or clarification while preserving the historical source in the restricted institutional record.
