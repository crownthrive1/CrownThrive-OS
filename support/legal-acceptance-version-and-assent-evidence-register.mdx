# Legal Acceptance, Version and Assent Evidence Register

## Purpose

A policy existing on a website does not prove a specific person or organization accepted it. CrownThrive therefore treats **publication**, **applicability**, **presentation**, **assent** and **transaction evidence** as separate objects.

This standard applies across accounts, memberships, commerce, licenses, creators, advertisers, affiliates, APIs, media submissions, events, education/ministry, MM Suites and other governed relationships.

Use only evidence collection that is lawful, necessary and proportionate. Device/IP evidence is supporting evidence, not a universal requirement.

## Assent levels

### L0 — notice only

Informational notice; no claim that mere visibility creates a negotiated grant.

Examples: public disclaimer, policy index, archived terms.

### L1 — continued-use notice

Suitable only where the governing relationship and applicable law permit update-by-notice/continued use and the change is not one that requires stronger assent.

Do not use L1 as the default for material new arbitration, ownership, model-training, auto-renewal, franchise, securities, exclusive licensing or other high-consequence rights.

### L2 — clickwrap / affirmative checkbox

User must affirmatively agree to the identified document/version.

Recommended for account Terms, memberships, seller/provider programs, affiliate/ambassador enrollment and ordinary platform addenda.

### L3 — transaction-specific affirmative assent

User confirms terms directly tied to an order, license, subscription, submission or campaign.

Recommended for digital-product licenses, recurring subscriptions, advertiser campaigns, creator/media submissions, event recording permissions and API/SDK terms.

### L4 — electronic signature / separately executed agreement

Recommended or required for negotiated licenses, SOWs, NDAs, material adaptations, exclusive rights, complex enterprise arrangements, voice/likeness/digital-replica rights, operator/regional relationships and other high-consequence transactions.

### L5 — regulated/formal process

Used only where a transaction requires counsel-reviewed disclosures, filings, regulated eligibility, notarization, governmental or third-party process, or another legally prescribed method.

## Required binding-context fields

Every consequential assent resolves:

- contracting CrownThrive entity;
- user/legal organization identity;
- signatory authority where acting for an organization;
- policy/license/agreement stable ID;
- exact version/hash;
- effective date;
- action/transaction/platform;
- product/order/license/campaign/event IDs;
- jurisdiction/locale where relevant;
- material addenda incorporated;
- renewal/term if applicable;
- price/fee schedule if applicable;
- accepted timestamp;
- evidence/receipt.

## Policy updates and reacceptance

Every new policy version receives a change class:

- `typo_or_nonmaterial_clarification`;
- `factual_correction`;
- `provider_or_contact_update`;
- `policy_scope_change`;
- `economic_change`;
- `rights_change`;
- `privacy_or_data_use_change`;
- `dispute_or_liability_change`;
- `regulated_relationship_change`;
- `security_or_abuse_change`.

The change class drives whether notice, reacceptance, new order terms, signature or other review is required.

Do not assume `Last Updated` alone makes a new version binding to every historical user.

## Organization authority

For business/church/school/operator/enterprise relationships record:

- organization ID/legal name;
- signer identity;
- title/role;
- representation of authority;
- domain/email or other verification where appropriate;
- delegated administrators;
- later authority revocation/change.

A platform admin account does not automatically prove legal authority to sign every agreement.

A legal/commerce receipt should resolve:

Do not put secret payment credentials or unnecessary personal data in a public license-verification endpoint.

## Archived policy evidence

For every superseded legal document preserve:

- source body/PDF or immutable snapshot;
- route used at the time;
- exact effective period;
- hash;
- acceptance records;
- successor version;
- reason for change;
- legal hold/retention state.

Historical evidence stays restricted where necessary; a public archive may expose only safe metadata and approved documents.

## Failure states

Block consequential activation when:

- policy version cannot be identified;
- terms were not presented before the action;
- required affirmative assent is missing;
- organization authority is disputed;
- checkout references a stale license;
- acceptance logging is unavailable for a high-consequence transaction;
- the page changed after presentation but the recorded hash does not resolve;
- a material addendum was incorporated only by an inaccessible/gated link;
- a consent was revoked and the action depends on that consent.

## Automation

Agents may calculate applicability, generate presentation bundles, store hashes, verify link availability, detect stale versions and assemble receipts. They may not fabricate assent, backdate acceptance, convert passive browsing into an affirmative signature or mark a relationship legally effective without the required evidence.
