## Complaint families

Do not force every rights complaint into the DMCA process.

- copyright infringement;
- DMCA notice/counter-notice where applicable;
- trademark/brand misuse;
- false affiliation/impersonation;
- name/likeness/publicity rights;
- synthetic voice/digital replica;
- privacy/confidential information;
- trade secret/internal leak;
- counterfeit/unauthorized merchandise;
- music sample/master/composition dispute;
- book/art/character/universe rights dispute;
- license-scope violation;
- API/data scraping or contractual misuse;
- user/provider ownership dispute.

Each complaint routes to the appropriate authority and process.

## Copyright/DMCA workflow

When the DMCA applies, the operating workflow should support:

`notice_received → completeness_review → identity/work/url match → preservation → temporary_hold_or_review → provider/platform_action → uploader_notice → counter_notice_window → counter_notice_review → restoration_or_continued_removal → repeat_infringer_update → close/archive`.

Legal counsel may be required for ambiguous, high-value or contested notices.

## Non-DMCA IP workflow

Trademark, likeness, confidential-information, contract-license and other complaints may use:

`intake → evidence → authority → policy/contract review → risk/safety review → hold → respondent notice where appropriate → decision → remedy → appeal/escalation → archive`.

Do not represent a voluntary platform process as a statutory DMCA procedure when it is not one.

## Platform propagation

A validated rights hold may affect multiple systems:

- CrownThrive.com;
- Virality Music / Backroad FM;
- Go Flipbooks;
- Melanated media;
- CrownThrive Studios;
- KJV/Sermon products;
- Locticians/marketplace listings;
- AdLuxe campaigns;
- CrownThriveU;
- APIs/downloads/entitlements;
- external distribution providers.

A platform removal is not automatically a determination that ownership is legally resolved. Use precise states such as `temporary_hold`, `removed_pending_dispute`, `license_expired`, `provider_takedown`, `rights_dispute`, `final_internal_decision`, or `court_order`.

## Evidence preservation

Before destructive removal, preserve where lawful/appropriate:

- URL/screenshot;
- asset/version/hash;
- metadata/creator/uploader;
- applicable license/terms;
- complaint and timestamps;
- prior revisions;
- distribution endpoints;
- provider notices;
- user notifications;
- decision/authority;
- restoration/takedown event.

Restricted evidence remains private.

## Repeat-infringer and abuse controls

If CrownThrive adopts/operates a repeat-infringer policy, define:

- qualifying events;
- reversed/invalid complaints;
- warnings/strikes if used;
- account/platform scope;
- escalation;
- appeal;
- termination authority;
- record retention;
- anti-abuse protections for fraudulent takedown attempts.

No agent may terminate a user merely because an automated similarity system found a match.

## AI/synthetic-media complaints

Special fields:

- model/provider if known;
- source-input allegation;
- human contribution record;
- voice/likeness identity;
- consent/release;
- synthetic-replica permission;
- similarity evidence;
- training/dataset claim;
- platform/provider policy route.

An AI-origin indicator is not itself proof of infringement, and commercial-use permission from a provider is not complete chain of title.

## Public complaint page requirements
