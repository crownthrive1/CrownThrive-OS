# Recovered SOP Catalog

Phase 2.7 restores the legacy SOP family as an institutional recovery backlog. The recovered Help Center structure identifies SOP records CT-SOP-101 through CT-SOP-119 across operations, support, identity, payments, legal process, content, partners, platform lifecycle, and knowledge publishing.

## Recovered SOP families

The source family includes procedures covering:

- platform onboarding and launch
- DNS/domain configuration
- policy deployment
- brand identity deployment
- abuse, suspension, ban, and enforcement handling
- disputes and escalation
- instructor onboarding
- affiliate/referral payout operations
- refunds, retries, and payment failures
- course/content QA
- platform-specific SOP distribution
- CrownRewards fraud prevention
- emergency and law-enforcement coordination
- partner access and termination
- identity verification
- subpoenas, privacy, and data retention
- course takedowns and IP disputes
- platform retirement/sunset
- Help Center publishing and maintenance

## Recovery states

Each recovered SOP must be marked:

- title recovered
- partial content recovered
- full content recovered
- reconstructed from evidence
- current and adopted
- superseded
- restricted
- requires legal/security review
- needs owner validation

<Warning>
  Do not fabricate verbatim SOP content when only a title or outline survives. Reconstructed procedures must be labeled reconstructed and cite the source/evidence used.
</Warning>

## Minimum SOP record

`SOP_ID`, title, owner, audience, trigger, systems, permissions, prerequisites, steps, evidence, rollback, escalation, legal/privacy class, approval status, effective date, review date, predecessor/successor, source references, and current-state validation.

## Phase 3 rule

An agent may execute only an **adopted/current** SOP within its permission boundary. Recovered or historical SOPs are knowledge sources, not execution authority.
