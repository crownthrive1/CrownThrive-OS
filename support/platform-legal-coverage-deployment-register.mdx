# Platform Legal Coverage and Deployment Register

<Warning>
  This register determines **which legal/policy package must apply** to a CrownThrive platform or transaction. It does not certify legal sufficiency or counsel approval. A package marked required still needs an effective document/version, applicability logic, publication route and acceptance evidence where applicable.
</Warning>

## Goal

Every CrownThrive platform must inherit the correct institutional legal baseline and only add platform-specific terms where necessary. This avoids two failure modes:

1. one generic Terms page pretending to cover every materially different transaction; and
2. dozens of disconnected Terms/Privacy pages that drift from one another.

The architecture is:

```text
Institutional Master Terms
        ↓
shared policy packages
        ↓
platform / role / transaction addenda
        ↓
order / license / membership / agreement
        ↓
acceptance evidence
```

## Status vocabulary

- `existing_public` — a current/reachable operative or explanatory public source exists;
- `existing_fragmented` — coverage exists across multiple older pages/addenda and needs consolidation;
- `architecture_ready` — the institutional package exists but the public effective addendum still needs drafting/deployment;
- `platform_addendum_required` — materially unique platform terms are required;
- `verify_current` — evidence of current operative policy/version is incomplete;
- `sunset_only` — no new commercial/addendum build; lifecycle/migration/history controls only;
- `counsel_gate` — do not activate the regulated relationship without qualified review.

## Sunset / reserve / legacy rule

A sunset property does **not** retain the full active-platform legal burden forever. It must keep the package needed to close responsibly:

`LC + PD + SP + IP` as applicable, plus any continuing contract/order/license obligations.

### Current founder-authorized example

- **Kamora360:** `LC PD SP IP` during migration/retention; no new onboarding/commerce/licensing package.
- **OpsOasis:** receives the new current execution package; it does not inherit old Kamora contracts automatically.

Other retired/reserve properties must follow their own lifecycle/evidence record rather than being assumed equivalent.

## Uninspected platform rule

Any CrownThrive property discovered later or not explicitly listed here receives:

```yaml
coverage_state: uninspected
minimum_packages:
  - MT
  - PD
  - IP
  - SP
consequential_actions_blocked: true
required_next_step: classify platform role, data, users, transactions, rights, AI, public claims and jurisdiction before activation
```

This prevents a newly rediscovered domain or engine from going live without legal classification.

## Phase 2.98 completion requirement

For each active/P0/P1 platform, Phase 2.98 should at minimum determine:

1. required package codes;
2. whether operative public terms exist;
3. whether Privacy/Data coverage is real and reachable;
4. whether commerce/subscription/refund terms apply;
5. whether content/user submissions/AI are involved;
6. whether licensing/third-party rights are involved;
7. whether advertising/affiliate/review disclosures apply;
8. whether minors, wellness, ministry, events, marketplace or regulated gates apply;
9. whether lifecycle/successor history must be preserved;
10. which public page/addendum still needs to be drafted, verified, consolidated or retired.

The goal is not maximum paperwork. The goal is **no ungoverned platform action**.
