# Legal Depot Master Coverage Architecture

The Legal Depot is CrownThrive's versioned legal-policy control system. It preserves historical terms, identifies currently effective terms, composes role/platform/transaction-specific addenda, records acceptance and supports future capabilities without forcing one giant document to govern incompatible activities.

<Warning>
  This architecture organizes legal and policy requirements. It is not legal advice and does not establish that a document is sufficient, filed, enforceable, registered or reviewed by counsel. Regulated and high-consequence structures require qualified current review.
</Warning>

## Phase 2.98 deployment-ready legal controls

Pass B5 adds implementation artifacts that convert the Legal Depot from a coverage map into a controlled deployment substrate:

- **CrownThrive.com Public Legal Deployment Packet** — route-level Terms/Privacy/Legal Center/FAQ/Kamora correction, SEO/canonical, acceptance and rollback controls;

Phase 2.98 also verified the current first-party CrownThrive Privacy Policy at `https://crownthrive.com/page/Privacy-Policy`, Effective/Updated December 13, 2025. The route is canonical/current; the content remains a prospective P0 correction target because it still lists Kamora360 and predates the current AI/data/model-use controls.

These controls remain implementation/drafting architecture until the affected public policy receives the required review, effective version and deployment/assent evidence.

## Phase 2.98 platform-by-platform legal coverage

The **Platform Legal Coverage and Deployment Register** now maps the CrownThrive portfolio to reusable policy-package codes rather than assuming one generic Terms page or duplicating disconnected terms across every property.

Package families include Master Terms, Privacy/Data, IP/DMCA, Commerce/Refunds, Master Licensing, AI/Data/Model/Voice, Community/UGC, Creator/Media, Advertising/Sponsorship, Affiliate/Ambassador, Education/Ministry, Developer/API/MCP, Marketplace/Provider, Beauty/Wellness, Phygital/Operator, Events, Impact, Support/Security, Lifecycle/Sunset, Minors, Accessibility and Regulated-Relationship gates.

Active platforms inherit the required shared packages plus a platform addendum only where their role materially differs. Sunset/reserve properties inherit lifecycle/privacy/support/IP obligations as needed rather than remaining active commercial systems. Uninspected properties are blocked from consequential activation until classified.

## Phase 2.98 licensing, AI and disclosure expansion

The Legal Depot now has three cross-platform control documents that every platform extraction must map:

1. **Master Licensing and Rights Architecture** — asset/right classes, self-serve vs reviewed licensing, work-level scope, term/territory, AI/data/voice rights, performance/adaptation/software/manufacturing and enterprise grants;
2. **AI-Assisted and Generated Content: Human Authorship, Rights and Disclosure Standard** — human-contribution records, provider terms/account timing, copyright-registration disclosure, synthetic voice/likeness, model-training boundaries, licensing claims and consumer-protection controls;
3. **Cross-Platform Disclaimer and Disclosure Matrix** — proximate disclaimers/disclosures for AI, licensing, professional information, ministry, beauty/wellness, advertising, affiliates, marketplaces, events, developer APIs, projections, impact and regulated relationships.

These documents are architecture and drafting controls. They do not convert every template into counsel-approved terms. Each actual platform/order/license must still resolve the effective policy version, transaction, audience, jurisdiction and review state.

### Universal rights principle

No CrownThrive platform may grant more rights than CrownThrive owns/controls, and no payment/access event silently expands the license. For AI-assisted assets, provider commercial-use permission, human copyright authorship, chain of title and customer license scope remain separate evidence questions.

### Universal disclaimer principle

A disclaimer clarifies a true boundary; it does not cure a false or unsupported claim. Material disclosures must appear where the user encounters the relevant offer, media, recommendation, endorsement, AI/synthetic content or professional-information risk.

## Policy hierarchy

1. entity/governance authority and master institutional terms;
2. privacy, cookies, data, security, retention, communications and consent;
3. platform/product/service addenda;
4. role/program terms;
5. transaction-specific order, license, grant, assignment, release, SOW or agreement;
6. jurisdiction/regulated-category addenda;
7. historical/superseded terms retained with effective periods and acceptance evidence.

The most specific applicable valid term controls where consistent with higher authority. Conflicts require adjudication, not silent selection.

## Historical coverage

Every historical policy/agreement retains:

- stable ID;
- exact version and source copy;
- effective period;
- entity/platform/role/territory;
- acceptance/signature evidence;
- amendments and supersession;
- transaction/license relationships;
- retention/legal-hold state.

A current web page does not retroactively prove what terms governed a 2025 user, purchase, license or partner relationship.

## Current-status overlays

Historical prospectuses and papers may describe patent-pending, trademark registration, franchises, FDDs, equity, tokens, smart contracts, assignable licenses or attorney approval. Current agents and public pages use only verified current status overlays. Original sources remain preserved.

## Acceptance and applicability

Phase 3 should resolve applicable policy versions by actor, role, platform, action, product, jurisdiction, data class and effective date. Acceptance requires evidence. No agent may infer acceptance from mere page availability.

## Policy lifecycle

`research → draft → internal_review → counsel_review_if_required → approved → effective → amended → superseded → archived → retained_or_destroyed_per_schedule`

## Automation

Agents may inventory, compare, draft, identify conflicts, prepare redlines, generate applicability matrices and monitor review dates. Reserved legal decisions, filings, regulated offerings, representations and final adoption follow named authority.

## Public/private boundary

Public Legal Center pages contain approved terms and explanations. Legal strategy, privileged communications, confidential agreements, investigations and restricted evidence remain private. The public index may state that a controlled record exists without exposing it.
