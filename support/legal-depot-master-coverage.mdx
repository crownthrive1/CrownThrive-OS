# Legal Depot Master Coverage Architecture

The Legal Depot is CrownThrive's governed policy system. It should preserve historical terms, identify currently effective terms, and support future platform/program addenda without forcing one giant document to govern incompatible activities.

<Warning>
  This architecture organizes legal and policy requirements; it does not substitute for licensed legal review where law, regulation, securities, franchise, employment, privacy, tax, intellectual-property, consumer-protection, or other professional judgment is required.
</Warning>

## Policy hierarchy

1. Entity-level master terms and institutional policies.
2. Privacy, cookies, data retention, security, communications and consent policies.
3. Platform/product/service addenda.
4. Program-specific terms: Hybrid Incubator, MM Suites, affiliates, ambassadors, creators, education, advertising, events, memberships, licensing, marketplaces, developer program, APIs/SDKs, AI features.
5. Transaction-specific licenses, orders, statements of work, grants, assignments, releases, consents and signed agreements.
6. Historical/superseded policies retained with effective dates.

## Required coverage families

- Master Terms / Acceptable Use / Community Conduct.
- Privacy, cookies, data processing, retention, deletion, export, minors, sensitive data, incident notice.
- Accessibility and communications preferences.
- Memberships, subscriptions, cancellations, renewals, trials, billing, taxes, refunds, disputes and chargebacks.
- Digital products, downloads, entitlements, license keys and usage limits.
- Marketplace, seller, buyer, listing, moderation and prohibited-content rules.
- Creator/contributor, media submission, likeness, voice, AI-assisted content, music, publishing and rights warranties.
- CHLOM, DLA, LEX, licensing, sublicensing, rights records, evidence, remedies and disputes.
- Affiliates, ambassadors, referrals, attribution, commissions, earnings disclosures and fraud.
- Advertising/publisher terms, targeting/data rules, brand safety and AdLuxe.
- Developer, API, SDK, MCP, sandbox, rate-limit, security, acceptable automation, data-use and suspension terms.
- AI/ML/LLM feature disclosures, human review boundaries, automated-decision rules, provenance and model/data-use restrictions.
- CrownThriveU/instructor/course/certification policies.
- Events, tickets, releases, venue/provider terms and cancellations.
- MM Suites/Suite Pro/territory/location/franchise-readiness documents with legal activation gates.
- CII/ThriveFund program and allocation rules without unsupported nonprofit/investment-fund representations.
- Support/SLA/incident/abuse/law-enforcement/subpoena processes.
- IP/DMCA/trademark/copyright/counterfeit/reporting procedures.
- Platform migration, deprecation, data portability and sunset.
- Employee/contractor/contributor confidentiality, IP, access and security terms where applicable.

## Historical coverage

Policies must have stable IDs, effective dates, supersession links and source copies. Historical users/orders/licenses should resolve to the terms applicable when the relevant relationship was created unless a lawful later change governs them.

## Future coverage

Every new platform or feature must complete a legal-dependency profile before public launch. The profile identifies applicable policy families, unresolved counsel gates, data classes, commerce model, audience, jurisdictions, rights model and required disclosures.

## Phase 3 role

Phase 3 should make policy identity, version, applicability and acceptance machine-readable. No agent should infer that a person accepted a policy merely because a current page exists.
