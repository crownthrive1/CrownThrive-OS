# Legal Depot Policy and Addendum Matrix

The Legal Depot must operate as a versioned applicability engine, not a folder of disconnected pages. One person may simultaneously be a member, customer, creator, affiliate, developer, licensee, Suite Pro and event attendee; the system must determine the applicable policies for the specific action.

## Master policy families

| Family | Applies to | Examples of dependent addenda |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Institutional Master Terms | all CrownThrive public/account use | platform terms, membership, acceptable use |
| Privacy and Data | all personal/operational data | cookies, analytics, ads, minors, journals, KYC/KYB, retention |
| Commerce and Billing | purchases/subscriptions/services | digital products, refunds, trials, taxes, chargebacks, entitlements |
| Rights and Licensing | assets, content and commercial permissions | CHLOM, DLA, LEX, sync, publishing, character, brand, maker, church |
| Community and Conduct | social/member/community surfaces | Commons, comments, creator conduct, moderation, events |
| Creator and Media | submissions, performances, recordings, likeness | music, podcasts, video, books, UGC, AI assistance, releases |
| Advertising and Distribution | advertisers, publishers, creators | AdLuxe, CrownFluence, sponsorship, tracking, brand safety |
| Affiliate and Ambassador | referral/distribution participants | commission schedules, attribution, disclosures, fraud, payouts |
| Education and Ministry | learners, instructors, churches, schools | CrownThriveU, Sermon Toolkit, KJV Visualized, certificates, minors |
| Developer and Integration | API/MCP/SDK users | sandbox, production keys, data use, security, rate limits, CLA |
| MM Suites and Phygital | Suite Pros, operators, locations, vendors | onboarding, territory, standards, brand license, franchise-readiness |
| CII and ThriveFund | impact programs and allocations | contribution, milestones, evidence, clawback, public reporting |

The policy engine returns the applicable terms, required consent, disclosures, review gates and acceptance evidence.

## Acceptance evidence

Policy acceptance should record:

- actor/organization;
- policy and version;
- action/transaction context;
- effective date;
- presentation locale/language;
- affirmative action;
- timestamp;
- IP/device or other lawful evidence where appropriate;
- order/agreement/license ID;
- later withdrawal or amendment handling.

A current policy page does not prove historical acceptance.

## Role-specific legal packages

### Creator / rights holder

Ownership warranties, contributor releases, AI/provenance disclosure, moral/attribution expectations, distribution authority, payment/split terms, takedown and disputes.

### Developer

API/SDK/MCP terms, scopes, data-use restrictions, security, responsible disclosure, rate limits, compatibility, deprecation, suspension, contributor IP/CLA and marketplace terms.

### Affiliate / ambassador

Approved claims, required disclosure, prohibited self-referral/fraud, attribution window, hold period, commission schedule version, tax/payment information and termination.

### Suite Pro / MM Suites operator

Brand/standards, software access, training, location/territory status, fees/overrides, products, data, customer care, insurance, privacy, dispute/remedy and legal-activation state.

### Church / ministry / school

Authorized users, display/reproduction, distribution, editable files, streaming/public performance, student/minor privacy, theological/editorial responsibility and content boundaries.
