## Allowed terminal dispositions

Every one of the 795 records must terminate in exactly one governed disposition appropriate to its evidence and current role:

- `substantive_current`;
- `substantive_historical`;

- `owner_validation_pending`;

- `redirected_to_successor`;
- `merged_into_canonical`;
- `restricted_projection_only`;
- `retired_history_preserved`;
- `rejected_duplicate_or_unsupported`.

`source_recovery_pending` remains a safe working state while evidence search is genuinely active, but it is not a permanent terminal substitute.

`source_not_recovered` means the historical body was not recovered after sufficient search. It does **not** authorize synthetic historical prose. If the institution still needs the function documented, create a new current artifact from current authoritative evidence and preserve it as a new version/current record.
