## Allowed disposition states

- `substantive_current`;

- `owner_validation_pending`;
- `legal_or_specialist_review_pending`;
- `retired_history_preserved`;

`source_recovery_pending` is the safe initial state. It is not a permanent substitute for reconstruction.

## Priority queues

Workstream 0 processes records in institutional-risk order rather than original numerical order.

### P0 — institution can cause harm if wrong

Prioritize:

- Legal Depot master terms, privacy, data, cookie, IP, DMCA, marketplace, payment, refund, user-generated-content and regulated-role material;
- rights/licensing/DLA/LEX/entitlement articles;
- identity, roles, permissions, verification, SSO, recovery and security;
- payments, payouts, commissions, refunds, disputes and economic schedules;
- CHLOM authority, approvals, DAIL, compliance and remedy records;
- Scripture/theological authority and private spiritual-data boundaries;
- MM Suites franchise/regional-license/economic/legal claims;
- developer/API/MCP authentication and side-effect documentation.

### P1 — customer/operator capability

Prioritize:

- active P0/P1 platform guides;
- onboarding and account workflows;
- products, commerce and fulfillment;
- advertiser/publisher/creator/affiliate/ambassador/instructor/operator workflows;
- media publishing and licensing;
- support/troubleshooting;
- analytics, reports and dashboards;
- current SOPs and runbooks.

### P2 — historical, research and reserve

Process after P0/P1 coverage is controlled:

- historical governance models;
- retired providers and implementations;
- reserve/dormant brands;
- research papers and advanced CHLOM concepts;
- superseded economic examples;
- concepts without current operating state.

P2 content remains valuable institutional IP; lower execution priority does not mean deletion.

## Risk classification

Article risk is independent of platform priority.

| Risk | Typical article | Approval |
| --- | --- | --- |
| D0 | formatting, index, link, metadata | bounded automatic approval |
| D1 | additive support guidance, dated inventory, unresolved-source record | bounded agent approval with evidence |
| D2 | current product/API/provider/eligibility/workflow/public-claim behavior | domain review and authority as required |
| D3 | legal terms, binding licenses, money, privacy/security, Scripture/canon, exclusivity, professional authority, source-history recovery | qualified human authority and specialist gates |

No D2/D3 article becomes current merely because a reconstruction agent produces fluent prose.

## Platform mapping rule

The recovered hierarchy is preserved, but current publication taxonomy may differ.

A recovered article can map to:

- one current platform;
- multiple platforms through a shared institutional policy;
- a successor platform;
- a corridor/framework;
- the institutional core;
- a historical archive;
- a restricted source-only record.

The old Help Center hierarchy remains evidence. It does not force the current Mintlify navigation to recreate every historical folder literally.

## Machine validation invariants

The eventual generated manifest must enforce:

1. exactly 795 source inventory IDs unless a new source-version record supersedes S11;
2. no duplicate `inventory_id`;
3. no duplicate stable `article_id`;
4. every row retains `S11` provenance;
5. every row has one valid disposition;
6. current substantive rows have a canonical route;
7. redirects have a valid destination and cannot loop;
8. merged rows name their canonical article;
9. restricted rows cannot publish through the public projection;
10. D2/D3 rows cannot autopublish without required approvals;
11. original recovered title remains preserved even when current canonical title changes;
12. correction and supersession history remains append-only;
13. no body is labeled historical-original unless that body was actually recovered;
14. every current article has a last-verified date and owner;
15. every material platform change resolves `docs_updated`, `docs_no_change` or `docs_delta_opened`.

## Self-check and self-healing

Safe automated actions:

- regenerate counts from the source inventory;
- detect duplicates or missing IDs;
- detect unlisted substantive MDX pages;
- detect dead internal links and redirect loops;
- suggest candidate mappings from recovered title to current platform article;
- reopen stale reconstruction tasks;
- update dated verification metadata when authoritative evidence is refreshed;
- rebuild generated navigation/search manifests;
- roll back documentation-only releases.

Unsafe autonomous actions:

- invent missing article bodies;
- declare legal terms current;
- infer production capability from a title;
- merge brands/platforms based on naming similarity;
- activate historical economic schedules;
- publish private URLs, credentials or restricted evidence;
- erase historical titles or correction records;
- convert research architecture into deployed architecture without evidence.

### Deterministic manifest generator

The Support repository now includes `scripts/generate_help_center_article_manifest.py`. The generator converts the normalized forensic inventory into stable `ct.article.recovered.0001` through `ct.article.recovered.0795` records, validates required fields, rejects duplicate inventory IDs, refuses a source inventory that does not total exactly 795 records, recomputes section counts, and initializes every record to the safe `source_recovery_pending` / `reconstruction_required` state.

The generated output itself becomes governed evidence only when produced through the repository/source workflow and validated against the registered source/checksum; a locally generated file does not supersede S11.
