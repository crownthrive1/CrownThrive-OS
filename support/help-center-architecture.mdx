## Help Center Architecture

**PentaDocs** is CrownThrive's governed institutional documentation and knowledge system. **ThriveSupport / CrownThrive Support** is the support-facing service and knowledge-delivery surface that draws from PentaDocs so the ecosystem remains teachable, supportable, auditable, and recoverable. Mintlify is the current third-party rendering/search/hosting provider for PentaDocs, not the institutional system name.

The recovered legacy Help Center contains a large structured corpus spanning ecosystem blurbs, platform guides, Hybrid Incubator material, legal/policy families, corporate playbooks, the Thrive Flywheel, Cultural Imprint Engine, MM Suites, developer documentation, CHLOM architecture, media systems, and operating procedures.

## What "institutional knowledge" means here

In CrownThrive OS, **institutional knowledge** is the accumulated, versioned body of decisions, policies, architecture, evidence, definitions, and operating rules that belongs to the institution rather than to any one person, chat log, or vendor account. It has four baseline traits:

- **Governed** — material changes move through bounded authority and review.
- **Sourced** — claims retain evidence classes and provenance through the [Institutional Source Authority Hierarchy](/knowledge/source-authority-hierarchy).
- **Versioned** — corrections and supersessions preserve lineage instead of silently overwriting history.
- **Recoverable** — Git history and off-provider continuity prevent a rendering-provider outage from erasing the institutional record.

In Phase 3, machine-readable institutional knowledge may also drive bounded software behavior, but documentation or historical context never manufactures provider, economic, rights, or D3 authority. First-time readers should start with [New here: what this site is](/start-here/orientation).

## Knowledge layers

The institutional knowledge system should distinguish:

1. **Public knowledge** — customer guides, public frameworks, product usage, policies, status, onboarding and approved technical information.
2. **Gated/operator knowledge** — internal SOPs, partner procedures, admin runbooks and controlled operating detail.
3. **Restricted source evidence** — contracts, credentials, customer records, private financials, raw evidence, confidential methods and protected IP.
4. **Machine-readable registries** — stable IDs, status, rights, roles, events, dependencies, approvals and relationships used by software and agents.

## Recovery classes

Recovered material should be labeled:

- `recovered-verbatim`
- `reconstructed-from-sources`
- `canonical-replacement`
- `archived-historical`
- `missing-owner-input`

Every article or institutional page should eventually carry a stable ID, title, corridor, recovery class, authority tier, source list, status, owner, review date and publication disposition.

## Source-of-truth rule

PentaDocs is the sole **governed institutional documentation source of truth for approved meaning**. That source of truth consists of the versioned institutional record: registries, article/page versions, decisions, source/evidence references, claims, corrections and approval state. ThriveSupport projects approved support knowledge from PentaDocs. Mintlify is the current rendering/search/hosting provider for PentaDocs; Git and off-provider exports preserve the record independently so a Mintlify outage or provider change cannot erase institutional truth.

Operational systems remain authoritative evidence for the runtime facts they originate and must feed the institutional record through verification/reconciliation rather than becoming competing doctrine.

- Phase 2.98 creates substantive P0/P1 articles during active recovery.
- Phase 2.99 assigns every recovered Help Center title a stable ID/disposition/source state and completes the machine-readable article/category/version/navigation/redirect seed; P0/P1 critical categories require substantive bodies or explicit unresolved-source records.
- Phase 3 adds generated/synchronized API, MCP and schema documentation and enforces docs-impact on connected repositories.
- Phases 4–5 continue the same version/update contract for platform onboarding, licensing and external developer changes.

## AI retrieval

Support and documentation agents may search approved knowledge, but retrieval must respect public/gated/restricted boundaries. Restricted evidence should never become globally searchable simply because an AI connector can technically access it.

## Continuity

Every critical knowledge set requires source export, version history and independent backup. The loss of a third-party help-center platform must not be capable of erasing CrownThrive institutional memory again.
