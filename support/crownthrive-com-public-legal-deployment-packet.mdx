## Deployment doctrine

The legal deployment should behave like a migration, not a text overwrite:

`capture → hash/version → archive → draft successor → review → approve → publish → obtain applicable assent → verify links/checkout → monitor → correct`.

For each public legal page, retain:

- prior body/source copy;
- effective/updated dates;
- source hash or repository snapshot;
- route/canonical URL;
- acceptance evidence if any;
- superseded-by relationship;
- reason/change class;
- approvers;
- deployment event;
- rollback copy.

## Terms successor packet

### Preserve

The current Terms already contain useful foundations: CrownThrive as contracting party; ecosystem scope; order of precedence; accounts/security; payment/refund framework; marketplace/ads; user-content ownership; CrownThrive IP; confidentiality; AI accuracy/no professional advice; third-party services; DMCA framework; termination; liability; electronic assent; and platform addenda.

### Correct prospectively

#### Platform roster

Current Terms hard-code Kamora360 among platform families. The successor version should not list Kamora360 as active.

Recommended structural treatment:

> CrownThrive Services include the platforms, brands, programs, applications, tools and successor systems that CrownThrive currently operates or controls, as identified in the applicable Service, order, platform addendum or current ecosystem registry. Historical, retired or migrated services remain governed by the terms applicable during their effective period and any transition notice.

This reduces future legal drift from volatile platform lists while preserving platform-specific addenda.

#### User Content operating license

The service-operation license should remain no broader than reasonably necessary for the stated Service and selected visibility/distribution settings. It should distinguish:

- host/store/transmit;
- format/resize/transcode;
- display/distribute to selected destinations;
- security/moderation;
- backups/retention;
- approved promotion where the user opted into public/promotional participation;
- separately granted commercial/licensing/model/voice/likeness rights.

Do not silently infer merchandise, adaptation, AI-training, reusable voice-model, exclusive ownership or unrelated third-party licensing rights.

#### AI/data/model use

The successor Terms should distinguish:

- AI-assisted features and output limitations;
- inference needed to perform a requested AI feature;
- analytics, abuse/security and product-quality evaluation;
- model/provider data use;
- dataset creation;
- fine-tuning/training;
- synthetic voice/likeness/digital replica;
- user opt-in/consent where applicable;
- provider-specific terms.

Ordinary service use should not be described as unrestricted model-training permission by implication.

#### IP status

Use evidence-safe language such as `CrownThrive owns or controls applicable rights in its original content, software, documentation, brands, frameworks and other proprietary materials, subject to third-party/open-source/provider rights where applicable.`

Do not equate `claimed mark`, `registered trademark`, `copyrightable`, `registered copyright`, `patent pending`, trade secret and contract rights.

#### DMCA

Do not insert a designated-agent identity until registration/authority is verified. The public page may provide an IP complaint intake address/process separately from claiming a registered DMCA agent.

Do not use generic `we do not sell` language as a substitute for reviewing sharing/targeted-ad/data-broker definitions in applicable jurisdictions.

## FAQ correction packet

### Kamora360

Remove Kamora360 from `Core Platforms` and any current operational routing. Replace with OpsOasis only if the public statement accurately matches OpsOasis's verified implementation state. If deployment is not yet publicly verified, use evidence-safe wording such as `OpsOasis is CrownThrive's designated successor operating/execution layer under current institutional architecture.`

### Investment / equity / franchise

Replace casual investment/franchise/equity invitations with counsel-reviewed relationship-intake language. Suggested public-safe structure:

> CrownThrive may evaluate strategic, commercial, licensing, financing, regional or other business relationships through separate eligibility, diligence and documentation processes. Nothing on this FAQ or website is by itself an offer to sell securities, a franchise offer, investment advice, or a grant of equity, ownership or economic rights. Any such relationship exists only under its applicable executed documents and legal requirements.

### CHLOM

Distinguish:

- current governance/licensing doctrine;
- source-defined or in-development automation;
- future DID/ZK/token/oracle/decentralized modules;
- no automatic legal protection simply because a record is described as `fingerprinted`.

### Refunds

FAQ refund statements must point to the applicable product/program refund terms rather than imply one universal policy.
