# Platform Migration and Sunset

CrownThrive has accumulated platforms across multiple implementation eras. Some assets remain active, some are being rebuilt, and others are archived or superseded. **Sunset is an operating state, not deletion of history.**

## Lifecycle decision

Before closing or replacing a platform, record:

- stable platform ID and current status;
- reason for migration/sunset;
- successor platform or destination;
- users/accounts affected;
- content and data inventories;
- rights and license obligations;
- outstanding orders/subscriptions/credits;
- integrations, webhooks and APIs;
- domains, redirects and backlinks;
- support and legal notice requirements;
- export/backup evidence;
- retention/deletion rules;
- final verification and owner approval.

## Migration sequence

```text
Inventory → Freeze risky changes → Export → Validate → Map IDs → Migrate → Reconcile → Redirect → Notify → Observe → Archive
```

## Public history

Where appropriate, keep a concise archived record explaining that a service moved, merged, or retired. Avoid leaving stale pages that still solicit purchases or describe unsupported capabilities.

## Data and rights

Migration must preserve consent, rights, licenses, authorship, attribution, customer entitlements, audit events and required financial records. A technical copy without those relationships is not a complete migration.

## Phase 3 implication

The Portfolio Registry and DAIL should model predecessor/successor relationships so a query for an archived brand can resolve its current destination and historical evidence.
