# Master Licensing and Rights Architecture

<Warning>
  This page is an institutional licensing architecture and drafting/control standard. It is not legal advice, a legal opinion, a promise of registrability, or proof that a specific license form has been reviewed by counsel. High-value, exclusive, cross-border, regulated, franchise, investment, union/guild, publicity/voice, adaptation and disputed-rights transactions require qualified current review.
</Warning>

## Institutional licensing rule

CrownThrive may license only the rights it **owns, controls, administers or is expressly authorized to sublicense**, and only to the extent supported by work-level evidence.

Keep four questions separate:

1. **May CrownThrive commercially use or monetize the asset under source/provider terms?**
2. **Which elements are protectable or otherwise legally controllable?**
3. **Who owns or controls each right layer?**
4. **What exact use is granted to this customer/licensee?**

One answer never substitutes for the others. Platform permission is not copyrightability. Copyrightability is not chain of title. Payment is not a license. Public access is not permission. A license cannot convey rights CrownThrive does not control.

## Two-lane licensing model

### Lane A — standardized self-serve

Self-serve checkout is allowed only when:

- the exact work/version is cleared for the requested use;
- CrownThrive controls all rights needed for that standardized use;
- no exclusivity, unusual edit, character, trademark, voice/likeness, adaptation or source-file right is requested;
- territory, term, user/quantity limits and price are predefined;
- the license text and version are fixed;
- payment, entitlement, certificate and verification can be issued automatically;
- refund/revocation behavior is implemented;
- no counsel/rights-holder approval is required.

### Lane B — reviewed or negotiated

Human/qualified review is required for:

- film, television, advertising, trailer, game or material sync;
- theatrical/performance use;
- streaming, recording, broadcast or capture of a play/event;
- screen option, shopping agreement or adaptation;
- exclusivity;
- character, universe, trademark or brand rights;
- merchandise, manufacturing or material POD quantities;
- large institutional/multi-site audiences;
- cross-border/multi-territory exploitation;
- extensive edits or derivative development;
- culturally or religiously sensitive uses;
- AI training, dataset, model, fine-tuning, voice, likeness or synthetic-replica use;
- third-party contributors, samples or unclear chain of title;
- union, guild, PRO, collective, publisher, label, distributor or provider obligations;
- unusual indemnity, insurance, audit, reporting or security requirements;
- franchise/regional operating rights;
- any regulated or high-consequence transaction.

54. AI/Data/Model Use Addendum

56. Synthetic Voice / Voice Model License

These rights are **opt-in and specifically enumerated**. No public page, stream, purchase, API response or ordinary product license grants training, scraping, dataset creation, embedding extraction, voice cloning, style imitation, fine-tuning or reusable synthetic persona rights by implication.

### Enterprise and regulated relationships

59. Enterprise / Custom License
60. Franchise / Regional-License Agreement — only after legal activation
61. Custom Strategic Partnership / SOW \+ License

Franchise, securities, investment, employment and regulated relationships are never created through generic clickwrap licensing.

## Term architecture

Use scope-dependent terms such as:

- one event;
- one production run;
- one semester/course;
- one campaign;
- 30/90 days;
- 6 months;
- 1 year;
- 3 years;
- 5 years;
- renewable;
- perpetual only when commercially and legally justified.

Track first-use deadline, renewal, reporting, archival period, sell-off/takedown period and surviving clauses.

Capture enough information to determine the right grant:

- buyer legal name and organization;
- project title/type;
- exact work/version requested;
- intended use and context;
- media/platforms;
- territory and term;
- audience, seats, venue/capacity or campuses;
- release/performance dates;
- paid/free access and ticket/product pricing where relevant;
- production/distribution budget where relevant;
- exclusivity;
- edits/derivatives;
- recording/streaming;
- merchandise/manufacturing quantity;
- character/universe/brand elements;
- AI/data/model/voice/likeness use;
- client/reseller/sublicensing request;
- credit language;
- insurance and compliance requirements;
- billing/tax/resale documentation;
- rights-confirmation acknowledgments.

## License lifecycle

```text
asset selected
→ proposed use
→ identity / authority intake
→ rights eligibility
→ chain-of-title / provider / contributor checks
→ standardized checkout OR reviewed quote
→ agreement / acceptance / signature
→ payment or deposit
→ license ID and certificate
→ entitlement / approved asset delivery
→ usage / reporting / royalty obligations
→ amendment / renewal / expiration
→ revocation / remedy / archive
```

No status advances because a Stripe payment exists alone.

## Asset-right families

### Music

Separate composition, lyrics, master sound recording, performance, producer contributions, samples/interpolations, publishing, stems, visual assets, trademarks, synthetic voice and provider/platform rights.

### Books and publishing

Separate manuscript text, illustrations, cover, edition, translation, audiobook, public performance/readings, educational reproduction, derivative/adaptation and merchandise.

### Characters and universes

Separate character identity, likeness/art, names/marks, canon, story-world expression, merchandise and specific adaptation rights. Public canon access is not an adaptation license.

### Media and programming

Separate underlying works from episode/program master, host/guest performance/release, clips, sponsorship, broadcast, VOD, archive and promotional uses.

### Software/API/MCP

Separate account access, API data use, code, SDK, documentation, trademarks, output rights, confidential information, rate limits and redistribution.

### Ministry/education

Separate Scripture/public-domain or third-party source text where applicable from CrownThrive commentary, illustrations, studies, curricula, worksheets, facilitation, recording/streaming and institutional reproduction.

### Brands and products

Separate trademark/source-identifying rights, artwork, packaging, recipes/formulas, confidential know-how, manufacturing, wholesale/distribution and advertising assets.

### MM Suites

Separate brand/system license, Suite Pro rights, territory/location permissions, software services, training/curriculum, products, fees/splits and any franchise/regional relationship. Historical prospectus language is not an active franchise grant.

## AI-assisted assets

For AI-assisted or generated material, license the exact rights supported by the evidence:

- human-authored/protectable elements to the extent CrownThrive controls them;
- provider-granted contractual/commercial-use rights to the extent the provider's applicable terms supply them;
- CrownThrive's original selection, arrangement, editing, performance, lyrics, graphics, metadata, canon and other qualifying contributions;
- no warranty of exclusive statutory copyright in purely AI-generated elements unless a specific reviewed agreement expressly says otherwise.

An asset can be commercially usable under provider terms while some AI-generated elements may lack copyright protection. The license and rights schedule must not blur that distinction.

## Voice, likeness and digital replicas

A performance release is not automatically a voice-model license. Separate:

- performance recording;
- master ownership;
- name/likeness/publicity use;
- promotional use;
- voice transformation;
- synthetic voice generation;
- reusable voice profile/model;
- training/dataset use;
- derivative performance.

Each requires explicit authority, scope, term, compensation and revocation rules where applicable.

## Third-party and provider overlays

Every license may be constrained by:

- contributors and co-owners;
- unions/guilds/collectives;
- PROs/publishers/labels/distributors;
- stock/asset libraries;
- software/open-source licenses;
- AI provider terms;
- app-store/DSP/platform rules;
- trademark/publicity/privacy rights;
- jurisdiction-specific consumer or regulatory rules.

CrownThrive never represents a third-party right as its own merely because the asset is stored in CrownThrive systems.

## Payment, entitlement and verification

Payment records, order records, licenses and entitlements must remain separate but linked.

A public verification surface should reveal only approved fields such as license ID, work, class, licensee display name/organization if authorized, issue date, term, territory summary, media summary, expiration and current status. Never expose payment identifiers, home addresses, confidential economics, bank data, privileged legal notes or private contributor splits.

## Amendments, holds and remedies

Place a license/asset on hold when chain of title, contributor authority, sample/source rights, AI/provider terms, voice/likeness, trademark, territory, canon or safety conflicts are unresolved.

Amendments create new effective records. Do not overwrite the signed grant. Revocation/termination must identify the authority, effective date, takedown/sell-off obligations and surviving terms.

## Legal-status rule

No document in the knowledge base may be described as “attorney approved,” “legally cleared,” “registered,” “copyrighted,” “trademarked,” “patented,” “exclusive,” or “fully enforceable” without the exact evidence supporting that claim.

The institutional target is **defensible precision**: license the exact use, preserve the exact proof, and state uncertainty rather than granting rights by implication.
