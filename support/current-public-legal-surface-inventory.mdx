# Current Public Legal Surface Inventory

<Warning>
  This is a Phase 2.98 evidence and remediation inventory. It does not provide legal advice or declare an existing public contract invalid. Previously effective terms, accepted versions and transaction-specific agreements must be preserved even when newer prospective language is adopted.
</Warning>

## Snapshot

**Review date:** August 17, 2026<br />**Scope:** publicly discoverable CrownThrive.com and legacy Help Center legal/policy surfaces plus current institutional Legal Depot controls.<br />**Current legal architecture:** Master Licensing and Rights Architecture; AI-Assisted and Generated Content Standard; Cross-Platform Disclaimer and Disclosure Matrix; Legal Depot Master Coverage; Policy/Addendum Matrix.

The public legal layer is not empty. CrownThrive already has meaningful terms, program policies, disclaimers and Help Center material. The problem is **version fragmentation and current-state drift**: some public pages predate the current platform registry, successor decisions, AI/human-authorship standard and licensing architecture.

## Decision vocabulary

- `PRESERVE_CURRENT` — substantially consistent; still needs normal counsel/version review;
- `PATCH_PROSPECTIVELY` — retain prior accepted version, publish a corrected successor version;
- `CONSOLIDATE` — useful existing material should become an addendum/module under the Legal Depot rather than remain a disconnected policy;
- `SUPERSEDE` — current public positioning should be replaced while preserving the old version/history;
- `ARCHIVE_ONLY` — no longer current, but retain as evidence;
- `VERIFY_CANONICAL_URL` — the policy concept exists, but the current first-party canonical surface was not conclusively located in this scan;
- `COUNSEL_REVIEW_REQUIRED` — material legal/regulated language needs qualified review before public activation.

## P0 — CrownThrive Terms of Service

### What the current Terms already do well

The December 2025 Terms already contain useful institutional structure:

- CrownThrive, LLC as contracting party;
- ecosystem-wide service scope;
- order of precedence among signed agreement, platform addendum and master terms;
- account/authority/security provisions;
- user-content ownership and operating license;
- marketplace/listing conduct;
- CrownThrive IP and restricted-use provisions;
- AI/automation accuracy and professional-advice disclaimer;
- third-party service limitations;
- copyright complaint/DMCA framework;
- suspension/termination;
- as-is/no-guarantee and limitation-of-liability language;
- electronic communications/signatures;
- platform/addendum concept.

These should be preserved where still accurate rather than discarded.

### P0 prospective corrections

#### 1. Kamora360 must be removed from current active-platform examples

The current Terms expressly list Kamora360 among CrownThrive platform families. Founder adjudication now sunsets Kamora360 and designates OpsOasis as successor.

Required treatment:

- preserve the December 2025 Terms exactly as the old accepted version;
- issue a new effective version that does not present Kamora360 as current;
- optionally identify `successor, renamed, migrated or retired offerings` generically rather than hard-coding volatile rosters into master Terms;
- use the canonical platform registry to generate or reference current platform coverage;
- maintain acceptance/version evidence for users subject to each version.

#### 2. Separate ordinary service improvement from AI/model training permission

The current Terms permit aggregated/de-identified usage data for service improvement, while the broader user-content license covers hosting, operating, promoting, securing and improving the Services.

The new Legal Depot must make a sharper distinction among:

- operational processing necessary to provide the service;
- analytics/security/quality improvement;
- model inference;
- model evaluation;
- dataset creation;
- fine-tuning/training;
- voice/likeness models;
- public or internal generative reuse.

**Default institutional rule:** ordinary account use, upload, streaming, purchase or API access does not grant CrownThrive or another user/model developer unrestricted training, synthetic-voice, dataset or reusable-persona rights by implication. Any CrownThrive provider/data-use practice must be accurately disclosed in the applicable Privacy/AI/Data addendum.

#### 3. User-content sublicense must resolve purpose and platform settings

The current user-content operating license is broad enough to support an ecosystem, but Phase 2.98 should ensure each use has a defined purpose, audience/visibility, platform setting, retention rule, model-use state and withdrawal/archival behavior.

A user-content operating license must not silently become:

- commercial merchandise rights;
- film/TV adaptation rights;
- model-training rights;
- synthetic voice/likeness rights;
- exclusive ownership;
- unrestricted third-party sublicensing.

Those require their own applicable agreement or explicit addendum.

#### 4. IP status language must resolve evidence state

Current master/public policies use broad proprietary/trademark language. Future revisions should preserve ownership claims while separating:

- claimed mark/common-law use;
- filed application;
- registered mark;
- copyrightable work;
- copyright registration;
- confidential trade-secret/process rights;
- patent/provisional status;
- contractual rights.

Do not allow a generic IP paragraph to become evidence that every CrownThrive name is registered or every AI-containing work is copyrightable.

#### 5. DMCA operational record

The current Terms say a registered DMCA agent, if maintained, will be posted in the Help Center/legal pages.

Phase 2.98 must inventory:

- whether CrownThrive currently has a registered designated agent;
- exact agent/contact/effective date if one exists;
- takedown/counter-notice intake path;
- repeat-infringer policy;
- evidence/retention process;
- platform coverage.

Do not publish a fictitious agent record.

## P0 — Public FAQ and marketing legal claims

FAQ copy is explanatory content and must not create a regulated transaction or stronger technical/legal status than operative terms.

### Kamora360

Current FAQ material still lists Kamora360 among Core Platforms. Required status: `PATCH_PROSPECTIVELY` to OpsOasis/current registry language.

### Investment/equity/franchise language

An older FAQ Center says users can invest in CrownThrive and references franchise packets, license options, platform equity models and shared-revenue blueprints.

This is high-risk current public copy. Until a specifically authorized offering/relationship exists, replace with a qualified relationship-intake statement such as:

- CrownThrive may evaluate strategic, licensing, commercial, financing or other relationships through separately documented processes;
- public website copy is not an offer to sell securities, a franchise offer, investment advice or a promise of ownership/economic rights;
- any applicable relationship is governed only by its specific reviewed documents and eligibility process.

`COUNSEL_REVIEW_REQUIRED` before publishing investment/franchise/equity offer language.

### CHLOM / fingerprint / deployed automation claims

FAQ claims that imply every Suite Pro/franchisee is already fingerprint-licensed or that legal/override protection is automatically deployed must be reconciled against current CHLOM implementation evidence. Target architecture remains valuable; current technical/legal status must be qualified.

## P0 — Privacy canonicalization and public accessibility

Phase 2.98 has now verified the current first-party Privacy Policy at:

`https://crownthrive.com/page/Privacy-Policy`

The page identifies itself as **CrownThrive Privacy Policy**, Effective Date **December 13, 2025**, Last Updated **December 13, 2025**. It applies across current/future CrownThrive websites, applications, tools, programs, subdomains, add-ons and successor products and currently hard-codes Kamora360 in its platform examples.

The policy already covers identity/contact/account/support/user-content data, third-party payment processing, device/usage/security logs, cookies/pixels/tags, Google/Facebook connected-account data, CrownLytics-style analytics, marketing/attribution, affiliates/ambassadors, service providers, ecosystem workflows, business transfers, retention, security, user rights, children under 13, cross-border processing and third-party services.

Canonical URL state: `FIRST_PARTY_CANONICAL_VERIFIED`.

Content state: `PATCH_PROSPECTIVELY / P0` because the current page predates the Kamora360→OpsOasis successor decision and the new AI/data/model-use standard.

Direct public retrieval of the legacy Help Center during this Phase 2.98 scan also returned a **sign-in-to-continue gate**. This does not prove that every individual legacy article is inaccessible through every route, but operative policies should rely on intentionally public first-party routes such as the verified CrownThrive.com Privacy page rather than assuming anonymous access to legacy support content.

Required rule: any Terms, Privacy, Cookie, Refund, License, DMCA, Accessibility or other notice that a user must reasonably review before account creation, purchase, submission, licensing or consent must have an intentionally public canonical route appropriate to that transaction. Restricted operator guidance may remain authenticated; operative consumer/partner terms should not be hidden behind an unrelated support login unless the transaction flow independently presents them.

Required Privacy successor corrections include:

- remove Kamora360 from current active examples and resolve successor coverage through the canonical registry;
- distinguish operational processing, analytics/security, AI inference, evaluation, dataset creation, fine-tuning/training and synthetic voice/likeness processing;
- document platform/provider-specific subprocessors and cross-platform handoffs where material;
- preserve the statement that CrownThrive does not sell personal information only if current practices continue to support that representation;
- reconcile cookie/advertising attribution controls with current AdLuxe/CrownLytics/CrownPulse/ThrivePush implementation;
- define retention by material data class instead of leaving all retention solely at the generic `reasonably necessary` level;
- clarify minors/family/ministry-sensitive data where those products are actually offered;
- preserve export/correction/deletion/objection/marketing rights while mapping jurisdiction-specific execution procedures.

The current privacy package should explicitly classify:

- account/identity data;
- transaction/payment references;
- user content/uploads;
- creator/media submissions;
- analytics and cookies;
- advertising/attribution;
- affiliate/ambassador data;
- support/communications;
- device/security logs;
- precise/sensitive data if collected;
- minors/children data where relevant;
- prayer/reflection/private ministry records;
- beauty/wellness records if collected;
- provider/vendor subprocessors;
- cross-platform data sharing;
- AI inference, evaluation, training or no-training states;
- retention/deletion/export;
- international transfers;
- state/jurisdiction rights and notices where applicable;
- business/customer/processor-controller roles where applicable.

No policy should claim data is never shared if the actual service architecture uses necessary processors or ecosystem handoffs. Describe the real flow precisely.

## Do-not-do list

- do not edit historical accepted Terms in place;
- do not backdate a new policy;
- do not describe an internal draft as attorney-approved;
- do not treat a Help Center article as a signed contract automatically;
- do not use a disclaimer to preserve a misleading investment, franchise, copyright or AI claim;
- do not make `continued use` the only acceptance mechanism where a specific transaction or law requires stronger assent;
- do not delete old policy evidence needed to prove what terms applied to a historical event.

## Current conclusion

CrownThrive already possesses a meaningful public legal foundation. Phase 2.98 should **version and converge it**, not start from zero. The immediate P0 corrections are Kamora360 successor language, regulated/equity/franchise FAQ language, AI/data/model-right separation, privacy canonicalization and migration of operative legal terms into the current Legal Depot architecture.
