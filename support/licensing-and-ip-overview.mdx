# CrownThrive Licensing & Intellectual Property

CrownThrive publishes selected institutional documentation so people can understand, evaluate, integrate with, support, and build relationships around the ecosystem. **Public visibility is not a blanket license.**

The controlling repository notice is the root `LICENSE`. This page explains the public-facing licensing posture in plain language; it does not replace a signed agreement or qualified legal review.

## What CrownThrive protects

CrownThrive's protected estate can include, where applicable, original documentation, software, schemas, registries, taxonomies, architecture, workflows, policies, research, training materials, media, music, books, visual assets, characters, universes, brands, marks, product systems, licensing models, platform designs, CHLOM materials, Cultural Imprint Engine materials, Thrive Flywheel materials, MM Suites architecture, ThriveBase public-safe contracts, ThriveEvergreen specifications, AI/ML/agent systems, prompts, evaluation designs, algorithms, datasets, compilations, and confidential methods.

The exact rights attached to an item depend on authorship, chain of title, applicable law, third-party licenses, contracts, registrations, effective versions, and the item's public/private classification.

## Public access does not automatically authorize reuse

Viewing, indexing, linking to, forking, cloning, downloading, searching, citing, embedding, calling a documented API/MCP surface, submitting a contribution, or making a voluntary payment does not by itself grant permission to:

- republish or commercialize CrownThrive material;
- create derivative products or competing replicas;
- train, fine-tune, distill, benchmark, or build datasets/models from protected CrownThrive material beyond rights available under applicable law or an express license;
- extract proprietary taxonomies, knowledge graphs, prompts, evaluation corpora, algorithms, scoring logic, trade-secret methods, or restricted implementation details;
- use CrownThrive names, marks, trade dress, platform identities, characters, universes, or branded assets as though endorsed or affiliated;
- use API keys, access credentials, public endpoints, MCP tools, or provider access outside the exact authorization granted for that interface;
- infer ownership, license, certification, entitlement, partnership, exclusivity, or production status from public documentation.

## Software, documentation, and third-party material are separate rights layers

A repository can contain different rights classes at the same time. CrownThrive-specific documentation and institutional material may be all-rights-reserved while an identified dependency, template, code component, specification, icon, or other third-party work remains governed by its own license.

A file-level license or third-party notice controls that identified material; it does not automatically extend to unrelated CrownThrive content. See `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` in the public repository.

## APIs, MCP, SDKs, agents, and machine-readable interfaces

Documentation of an API, MCP tool, schema, event, SDK, agent contract, or interoperability surface describes an interface. It does not automatically grant a license to the underlying CrownThrive intellectual property, private implementation, data, model, business logic, content, or marks.

Access remains bounded by the applicable credential, role, policy, agreement, rate/cost limit, data class, environment, purpose, and revocation rules. Provider success or technical access cannot manufacture broader institutional authority.

## AI, machine learning, retrieval, and automated extraction

CrownThrive reserves its rights in protected source material used for or exposed through AI/ML/LLM, retrieval, agent, embedding, classification, recommendation, evaluation, and automation systems.

Unless separately authorized or independently permitted by applicable law, public repository access does not grant permission to use CrownThrive Material for commercial model training, fine-tuning, distillation, embedding corpora, synthetic-data generation, competitive evaluation datasets, bulk knowledge extraction, or reconstruction of protected methods.

Public search engines and ordinary discovery/indexing are treated separately from a grant to commercially appropriate the protected corpus. CrownThrive may publish machine-readable discovery surfaces while retaining underlying rights.

## Brands, marks, characters, media, and cultural assets

CrownThrive names, platform identities, claimed marks, logos, trade dress, universes, characters, stories, recordings, compositions, artwork, photographs, videos, curriculum, and other expressive assets remain subject to their applicable rights records.

Use of `™` identifies a claimed brand identity. Use of `®` requires a current verified registration record. Public mention does not grant trademark or merchandising rights.

## Patents, inventions, and confidential methods

Repository access grants no patent license, patent-application rights, invention assignment, or permission to practice protected claims unless an authorized written instrument expressly says so.

Confidential algorithms, security controls, private economic models, calibration, restricted prompts, source assets, credentials, evidence, and other trade-secret or confidential material must remain in authorized custody. Accidental disclosure does not create permission to retain, exploit, redistribute, or commercialize it.

## Commercial licensing

Commercial permission must come from an authorized CrownThrive agreement or other governed rights record defining the relevant asset, scope, use, territory, term, channel, attribution, restrictions, economics, sublicensing rights, approval requirements, termination/remedies, and effective version.

CHLOM governs CrownThrive's rights/evidence relationships. ThriveEvergreen governs economic activation where applicable. Neither a checkout, payment, provider receipt, product page, API call, nor technical delivery independently substitutes for the controlling rights and economic records.

## Contributions

A contribution does not automatically establish ownership, joint authorship, compensation, equity, royalty rights, confidentiality, acceptance, or permission for the contributor to use unrelated CrownThrive material. Material contributions may require a separate contributor, contractor, employment, assignment, NDA, or licensing agreement.

## Enforcement, corrections, and professional review

CrownThrive may correct, supersede, withdraw, restrict, or reclassify public material without waiving rights in prior versions. Failure to enforce a restriction immediately does not necessarily waive it.

Rights are subject to applicable law, non-waivable statutory exceptions, valid third-party rights, and controlling written agreements. The repository notice is intended to preserve CrownThrive's rights and communicate permitted-use boundaries; it is not a substitute for jurisdiction-specific legal advice, registrations, chain-of-title work, or negotiated contracts.
