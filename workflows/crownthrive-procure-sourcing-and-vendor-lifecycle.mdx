# CrownThrive Procure Sourcing & Vendor Lifecycle Workflow

`business need -> requirements -> market research -> candidate providers -> RFQ/RFP -> evidence collection -> comparison -> disclosure/conflict review -> release package -> decision packet -> human award/contract action -> onboarding -> performance evidence -> renewal/exit review`

Each sourcing packet identifies requirements, provider candidates/source dates, evidence class, pricing basis/date/currency where known, data/security/privacy/API/export/support questions, referral/affiliate/sponsorship disclosures, comparison dimensions with uncertainty visible, SOW/acceptance questions and vendor-exit considerations.

Marketplace presence, paid listing, affiliate relationship or vendor response does not prove quality, licensure, insurance, security, certification, availability or fit. Agents may prepare recommendations but may not sign contracts, accept provider terms, move money or manufacture approval.
