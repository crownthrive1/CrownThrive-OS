# CrownThrive Procure Sourcing & Vendor Lifecycle Workflow

## Operating loop

`business need -> requirements -> market/source research -> candidate providers -> RFQ/RFP -> evidence collection -> comparison -> conflict/disclosure review -> decision packet -> human award/contract action -> onboarding -> performance evidence -> renewal/exit review`

## Procurement packet requirements

Each sourcing packet must identify:

- buyer need and budget assumptions if supplied;
- mandatory versus preferred requirements;
- provider candidates and source dates;
- evidence class for every material claim;
- pricing basis and currency/date where known;
- data/security/privacy/API/export/support questions where applicable;
- conflicts, referral/affiliate relationships and sponsorship disclosures;
- weighted comparison dimensions with uncertainty visible;
- contract/SOW questions and acceptance criteria;
- renewal, data export and vendor-exit considerations.

## Provider-state rule

Marketplace presence, a paid listing, an affiliate relationship or a vendor response does not prove quality, licensure, insurance, security, certification, current pricing, availability or fit. These are separate evidence fields.

## Award boundary

Agents may prepare a recommendation packet but may not sign a contract, bind CrownThrive or a customer, accept provider terms, move money or manufacture executive approval. Final vendor selection follows the buyer's applicable human and organizational authority.

## Agent routing

- A: sourcing priority, collision control and ecosystem handoff;
- B: independent evidence and comparison audit;
- C: RFQ/RFP/scorecard/package construction;
- D: independent gate for claims, conflicts and lifecycle state;
- S: provider/security/privacy risk boundary;
- F: public-safe marketplace/provider documentation;
- G: current provider, pricing, version, license and domain evidence;
- H: quote/delivery/webhook communication evidence where integrated;
- E: API/MCP/provider adapter evidence;
- scoped non-voting agent: `ct.agent.crownthrive-procure-vendor-sourcing`.

## Marketplace evolution

Provider profiles, response workspaces, quote routing, paid listings, sponsorships, referral economics, badges and vendor-side tools must each have a separate product/state contract. Paid visibility must be disclosed and cannot alter evidence scores secretly.

## Fail-closed rules

Confidential bids remain restricted. Unknown provider state remains unknown. Procurement recommendations must expose material uncertainty and cannot claim a provider is vetted or certified unless an exact governed verification program supports that designation.
