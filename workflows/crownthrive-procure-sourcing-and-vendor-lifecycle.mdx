# CrownThrive Procure Sourcing & Vendor Lifecycle Workflow

`business need -> requirements -> market research -> candidate providers -> RFQ/RFP -> evidence collection -> comparison -> disclosure/conflict review -> decision packet -> human award/contract action -> onboarding -> performance evidence -> renewal/exit review`

Each sourcing packet identifies mandatory/preferred requirements, provider candidates/source dates, evidence class for material claims, pricing basis/date/currency where known, data/security/privacy/API/export/support questions, referral/affiliate/sponsorship disclosures, comparison dimensions with uncertainty visible, SOW/acceptance questions and vendor-exit considerations.

Marketplace presence, paid listing, affiliate relationship or vendor response does not prove quality, licensure, insurance, security, certification, availability or fit.

Agents may prepare recommendations but may not sign contracts, accept provider terms, move money or manufacture approval.

Agent routing: A prioritizes; B independently audits; C constructs RFQ/RFP/scorecards; D gates claims/conflicts; S reviews provider/security/privacy risk; F documents; G checks current provider/pricing/license state; H validates quote/delivery paths where integrated; E handles API/MCP evidence; `ct.agent.crownthrive-procure-vendor-sourcing` remains non-voting.

Confidential bids remain restricted. Unknown provider state remains unknown. Paid visibility must be disclosed and cannot secretly alter evidence scores.
