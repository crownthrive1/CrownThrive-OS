# Sales lead triage and Locticians claim outreach

This workflow adds a governed **sales-and-email operating lane** to CrownThrive without weakening the existing evidence, privacy, rights, financial, legal or founder-approval controls.

The system has two distinct jobs:

1. **Inbound lead desk** — classify, vet, nurture and route legitimate business-development email.
2. **Researched claim outreach** — identify genuinely claimable Locticians profiles, research the public business identity, contact an appropriate public business address, and help the correct owner claim the profile.

Neither job authorizes agents to sign contracts, grant final rights, alter banking instructions, disclose secrets, make legal determinations or bypass suppression/consent rules.

## Inbound triage pipeline

```text
New message
  ↓
Reason-for-contact classification
  ↓
Identity / legitimacy / risk review
  ↓
Priority tag + lifecycle state
  ↓
┌────────────────────────────┬──────────────────────────────┐
│ Founder / reserved matter  │ Routine legitimate lead      │
│ escalate; no commitment    │ research → reply → nurture  │
└────────────────────────────┴──────────────────────────────┘
  ↓
Archive event + next action + suppression/risk state
```

### Founder-priority classes

Messages are founder-routed when they materially involve:

- court, hearing, subpoena, attorney, filing or legal-rights matters;
- material money movement, wire/bank changes, tax, payment exceptions or high-consequence transactions;
- personal or sensitive matters;
- credentials, secrets, account-security or identity-verification incidents;
- binding contracts, final commercial-rights grants, exclusivity or irreversible commitments.

Routine receipts, ordinary support and already-governed low-risk commerce do not become founder-action items merely because an internal agent still has work to complete.

## Lead legitimacy review

Before an automatic lead reply, evaluate:

- sender and reply-to consistency;
- whether the business/entity can be independently corroborated;
- specificity of the request and relevance to CrownThrive;
- compensation, scope, timing and decision-maker information when applicable;
- links, forms and attachments in context;
- credential, tax, banking, payment or identity-harvest requests;
- malformed placeholders, copied scripts, mass-solicitation artifacts or incoherent formatting;
- whether the sender answers reasonable business questions or attempts to divert the conversation to an unexplained external flow.

A single signal is not enough to declare fraud. Risk classification depends on the combined evidence.

## Locticians claim-outreach qualification

A profile enters outbound research only when all of the following can be supported:

```yaml
locticians_profile_exists: true
claimable_or_unclaimed_state: verified
category: known
location: known
business_identity: corroborated
public_business_email: sourced
email_source_url: recorded
suppressed: false
prior_outreach_checked: true
risk_hold: false
```

Do not guess private email addresses or owner identities. Do not treat a search result, visual similarity, social-media handle or directory record as proof of ownership.

## Claim-outreach message architecture

Each initial message should contain:

1. a specific reason the recipient was selected;
2. the exact Locticians profile URL;
3. a truthful statement that the profile appears claimable/unclaimed;
4. one category-relevant benefit of controlling the profile;
5. one clear next action;
6. required commercial-email disclosures and opt-out language when applicable.

The dominant hooks are:

- **ownership/control** — the profile is already discoverable and the real business should control its information;
- **accuracy** — reduce stale or incorrect service/contact details;
- **trust** — distinguish directory-indexed information from owner-maintained information;
- **discovery** — connect category/location discovery to the right official business;
- **conversion clarity** — make the next contact/claim action obvious without inventing referral or traffic claims.

Do not claim that a business has leads waiting, guaranteed referrals, verified traffic, guaranteed ranking or guaranteed revenue unless a current governed metric explicitly supports that exact claim.

## Four-touch sequence

```yaml
sequence:
  - touch: 1
    due: day_0
    purpose: researched_category_specific_claim_invitation
  - touch: 2
    due: business_day_3
    purpose: concise_profile_reminder
  - touch: 3
    due: business_day_7
    purpose: one_specific_accuracy_or_control_value
  - touch: 4
    due: business_day_14
    purpose: close_the_loop_and_stop
```

### Hard stop conditions

Stop the sequence immediately on:

- reply;
- profile claimed or conversion/won state;
- unsubscribe, opt-out, remove-me or equivalent request;
- wrong-person notification;
- hard bounce;
- suppression record;
- complaint or legal issue;
- scam/phishing/risk hold;
- founder hold.

No later marketing automation may silently re-enroll a suppressed address.

## Reply branching

### Interested

Verify the ownership/claim route, answer supported questions, update the lifecycle and remove unnecessary friction.

### Not now

Do not create an indefinite nurture loop. Ask whether a later reminder is wanted; otherwise stop.

### Wrong contact

Stop outreach to that person. Correct the record and research the appropriate public business contact independently.

### Already claimed

Verify the current listing state, mark claimed/won as appropriate and stop the sequence.

### Opt out

Suppress immediately and honor the request across marketing workflows.

### Complaint, identity dispute or legal threat

Escalate. Do not argue through an automated sales agent.

## Commercial-email compliance gate

Cold outreach is an A3/policy-controlled function, not an always-on permission.

The runtime must verify the current outbound-compliance configuration before any unsolicited commercial email is sent. At minimum the configured sender must have:

- accurate sender/header identity;
- truthful subject lines;
- required commercial/advertising identification;
- a valid physical postal address;
- an easy opt-out mechanism;
- working suppression enforcement.

If the required configuration is incomplete, the system may research and queue prospects but **must not send**.

## Sender and collaboration record

Locticians claim outreach is designed to originate from CrownThrive’s governed business mailbox. Founder and Collab Portal record-copy routing are configured in the private runtime rather than exposed in public documentation.

The Collab Portal handoff is an institutional CRM/project continuity path, not permission to expose sensitive, privileged or credential-bearing messages to a broad project audience.

## Images, logos and rich email

Prospect research may use lawful public visual context to understand a business. That does not create unrestricted reuse rights.

Current direct Outlook automation supports clean plain-text writes. Richly formatted visual outreach should only be activated through a separately certified HTML-capable sender such as the governed Collab Portal/SuiteDash marketing surface, and only after sender, compliance and deliverability gates pass.

## Private CRM contract

The current private runtime uses a restricted sales/email schema with records for:

- prospects;
- research evidence;
- outreach sequences;
- templates;
- outreach events;
- email vault entries;
- suppression;
- tag taxonomy;
- outbound configuration;
- founder-escalation rules.

No private email body, prospect data, suppression record or operational sender credential belongs in this public repository.

## Measurements

Track quality and conversion, not just volume:

- researched profiles;
- qualified claimable profiles;
- valid public business emails sourced;
- messages sent;
- bounce/delivery state;
- reply and positive-reply rates;
- claim-start and claim-completion rates;
- opt-out and complaint rates;
- touches to conversion;
- category/location performance;
- suppression and risk-hold counts;
- time to first meaningful reply.

## Failure rule

The workflow fails closed.

If identity, claim state, public contact source, compliance, suppression, authority or delivery state cannot be verified, **do not send**. Preserve the evidence gap and route it for review instead of manufacturing certainty.
