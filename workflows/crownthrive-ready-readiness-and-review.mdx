# CrownThrive Ready Readiness & Review Workflow

## Operating loop

`readiness objective -> authority/source research -> scope/jurisdiction labeling -> evidence request -> gap analysis -> bounded readiness score -> remediation plan -> specialist boundary -> product/review QA -> delivery -> periodic refresh`

## Readiness packet requirements

Every CrownThrive Ready product must identify:

- exact scope and audience;
- what the product can and cannot establish;
- authoritative sources and effective dates;
- applicable-jurisdiction assumptions or explicit non-jurisdictional scope;
- evidence inputs;
- deterministic checks versus judgment calls;
- remediation recommendations;
- specialist escalation points;
- correction/update triggers;
- customer-facing disclaimers;
- version, manifest and source references.

## Scoring rule

Any automated or assisted readiness score must explain its dimensions, evidence freshness and limitations. A score is a prioritization aid. It is not a legal opinion, audit opinion, certification, accessibility conformance statement, penetration-test result or regulator determination.

## Specialist routing

Legal, tax, accounting, insurance, regulated-industry, formal security assessment, formal accessibility certification and other licensed/professional questions route to qualified human specialists. Agent outputs may prepare the evidence packet but do not impersonate or replace those authorities.

## Agent routing

- A: prioritization and platform handoffs;
- B: source authority/evidence audit;
- C: product/evidence-binder construction;
- D: independent gate and claim-boundary review;
- S: security/privacy/testing boundary;
- F: public-safe guidance/documentation;
- G: current standards/provider-source watch;
- H: notification/delivery proof when recurring monitoring is enabled;
- E: evidence/API adapters where appropriate;
- scoped non-voting agent: `ct.agent.crownthrive-ready-readiness`.

## Recurring monitoring

Readiness Monitor may detect source changes, stale policies, expiring evidence, new vendor versions, missing reviews and reopened risks. It may place a product or customer packet on `REVIEW_REQUIRED` or `HOLD`; it may not auto-declare legal noncompliance or release its own hold.

## Fail-closed rules

Unknown applicability remains unknown. Missing professional review cannot be converted into a PASS by automated scoring, customer preference, payment or founder direction.
