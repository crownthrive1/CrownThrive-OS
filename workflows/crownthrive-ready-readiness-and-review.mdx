# CrownThrive Ready Readiness & Review Workflow

`readiness objective -> authority/source research -> scope/jurisdiction labeling -> evidence request -> gap analysis -> bounded readiness score -> remediation plan -> specialist boundary -> QA -> delivery -> periodic refresh`

Every product identifies exact scope/audience, limitations, authoritative sources/effective dates, jurisdiction assumptions, evidence inputs, deterministic checks versus judgment calls, remediation guidance, specialist escalation points, update triggers, disclaimers and version/source records.

Any score explains its dimensions, evidence freshness and limitations. A score is a prioritization aid, not a legal opinion, audit opinion, certification, accessibility conformance statement, penetration-test result or regulator determination.

Legal, tax, accounting, formal accessibility, formal security and other licensed/professional conclusions route to qualified humans.

Agent routing: A orchestrates; B audits authority/evidence; C builds binders/products; D gates claims; S enforces security/privacy/testing boundaries; F documents; G watches source/standards drift; H validates recurring delivery where applicable; E handles evidence/API adapters; `ct.agent.crownthrive-ready-readiness` remains non-voting.

Readiness Monitor may place records on `REVIEW_REQUIRED` or `HOLD`; it cannot auto-declare legal noncompliance or release its own hold.
