# Locticians Claim Offer Conversion Workflow

## Objective

Help legitimate businesses claim owner-controlled Locticians listings without fake scarcity, unsupported eligibility or savings claims, mass-spam behavior, stale pricing, or unverified recurring-payment language.

```text
claimable profile
→ identity and lawful-contact research
→ suppression and prior-contact check
→ private offer lookup
→ public-source and checkout verification
→ personalized candidate
→ compliance gate
→ send or HOLD
→ claim and conversion readback
```

## Current public observation

The first-party `/join` surface displays `CLAIMMONTH50` as a 50% incentive for claimable Community+ and Basic listings. That observation does not establish provider-admin configuration, broader plan scope, exact checkout acceptance, recurring renewal treatment, future availability, cancellation or refund behavior, or customer entitlement.

The private registry currently classifies the offer as configured, public evidence conflicted and checkout verification pending. Restricted admin configuration and signer evidence must not be copied into this public workflow.

## Economic and customer-copy gate

- Do not quote higher-tier eligibility or savings without exact checkout evidence.
- Do not use “for life,” “lifetime,” “forever,” or equivalent recurring claims without checkout and renewal readback plus accepted consumer/legal review.
- Do not infer renewal behavior from the first charge.
- Until verification closes, direct users to check current eligibility, terms and price at checkout.

## Message sequence

1. Verify the exact claimable profile and lawful business contact.
2. Lead with profile ownership, accuracy and discovery value.
3. Use only verified category-specific facts.
4. Mention a public incentive only within the exact observed public scope.
5. Stop or hold when suppression, compliance, checkout, rights or risk evidence is incomplete.

## Compliance and suppression

Cold outbound remains fail-closed unless the restricted control plane proves enablement, a verified physical postal address, lawful source, claimable state, suppression clearance and available sending capacity. Stop immediately on reply, claim completion, opt-out, wrong person, hard bounce, complaint, legal issue or risk hold.

## Conversion evidence

Capture claim state, selected plan, coupon acceptance, verified checkout amount, billing cycle, renewal readback, provider reference and verification time only in the governed private system. Public records may expose status and non-secret digests only after classification.

## Ownership and review

The Locticians claim-outreach agent is the primary non-voting executor. Supporting sales agents remain non-voting. Legal, financial, identity-dispute, security, exceptional-rights and ungoverned pricing matters leave the sales lane.
