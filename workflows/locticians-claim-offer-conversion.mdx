## Objective

Convert legitimate claimable Locticians profiles into owner-controlled listings and, when there is a genuine fit, eligible paid memberships without using fake scarcity, unsupported savings claims, mass-spam behavior, or stale pricing.

The operating sequence is:

```text
claimable profile
→ identity research
→ lawful business contact source
→ suppression / prior-contact check
→ offer and plan evidence lookup
→ personalized claim message
→ compliance gate
→ send or HOLD
→ reply classification
→ claim support
→ membership fit / coupon verification
→ claimed / won / suppressed / lost / escalated
```

## Governing offer

The private offer registry identifies the founder-configured acquisition offer as:

```yaml
offer_key: locticians.claimmonth50.v1
coupon_code: CLAIMMONTH50
discount: 50 percent
use: Sign Ups & Upgrades
new_signups_only: true
recurring_discount: true
new_redemption_start: 2026-01-01
new_redemption_expiration: 2028-10-31
maximum_uses: unlimited
card_required_when_zero: true
```

Founder-attested admin configuration currently selects Basic, Blogger, Community\+, Featured, Premium and Sponsor membership-plan IDs and all displayed billing-cycle options. CrownRewards Pro, MM Suite Pro and Prestige Member are not selected in the supplied admin configuration.

### Evidence conflict

The current first-party `/join` banner publicly advertises `CLAIMMONTH50` as **50% off Community\+ Member and Basic Plans for claimable listings only**. That narrower public statement conflicts with the broader founder-attested admin selection.

Therefore:

- the offer itself is current public evidence;
- recurring behavior is founder-configured and provider-supported;
- higher-tier plan eligibility remains `checkout_verification_pending` until a real eligible checkout/readback reconciles the scope;
- public-facing copy must not promise Premium, Featured, Sponsor or Blogger eligibility merely because those plans appear checked in an admin screenshot.

## Recurring / “for life” interpretation

The directory-provider coupon contract distinguishes recurring from first-payment discounts:

- recurring `YES` applies the coupon to recurring payments;
- recurring `NO` applies it to the first payment only;
- coupon expiration controls whether a new customer can redeem the coupon after that date; it does not itself set an expiration date on the resulting order.

Customer-safe phrasing after an eligible checkout confirms recurring treatment is:

> 50% off recurring payments for the life of the active eligible membership after redemption.

Do not say `lifetime membership`, `forever regardless of cancellation`, or imply a customer can newly redeem the code after the configured expiration date. Cancellation, plan changes, payment failure, terms, or provider behavior may affect the active membership.

## Current public membership evidence

Dated first-party `/join` pricing currently shows:

| Plan | Public price | 50% arithmetic if eligible | Public Nexus Credit award |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Community\+ | `$4.99/month` | about `$2.50/month` saved | 30 credits |
| Basic | `$9.99/month` or `$99.99/year` | about `$59.94/year` at monthly rate or `$50/year` on annual price | 40 credits |
| Premium | `$19.99/month` or `$199.99/year` | about `$119.94/year` monthly-equivalent or `$100/year` annual | 50 credits |
| Featured | `$99.99/month` or `$999.99/year` | about `$599.94/year` monthly-equivalent or `$500/year` annual | 150 credits |
| Sponsor | `$299.99/month` or `$2,999.99/year` | about `$1,799.94/year` monthly-equivalent or `$1,500/year` annual | 500 credits |

The arithmetic does **not** establish coupon eligibility. Until checkout verifies a higher tier, outbound copy must use `50% off eligible recurring membership payments` rather than quoting those higher-tier savings.

Blogger pricing is not supported by the current `/join` page and must not be invented.

## Value stack

Personalization should lead with the prospect's actual business problem rather than the coupon. Current public Locticians value surfaces include owner-controlled profile data, category/city discovery, applicable direct client communication, ThriveSeat booking paths, reviews/reputation, Premium profile analytics, Featured/Sponsor visibility, CrownThriveU learning, ThrivePeer mentorship, ThriveTickets events, Nexus Credits and CrownRewards as a separate loyalty system.

Do not promise a lead count, search rank, booking volume or return on investment without current evidence for that exact claim.

## FOMO rules

Use urgency only when it is real: a profile is actually claimable, the incentive is valid for the recipient, the redemption window is current, and any savings arithmetic is verified for the selected plan.

Never use fake countdowns, fake scarcity, fabricated demand, invented competitor pressure or unverified high-tier savings.

## Compliance and suppression

Cold outbound remains fail-closed unless the private outbound configuration confirms cold outreach is enabled, a valid physical postal address exists, the recipient is not suppressed, the business email source is verified, the profile remains claimable and daily sending limits are available.

Stop immediately on reply, claim completion, win, opt-out, wrong person, hard bounce, complaint, legal issue, risk hold or suppression.

## Conversion evidence

A conversion record distinguishes claim status, membership selected, coupon entered/accepted, verified plan and billing cycle, recurring-discount message, pre/post-discount amounts, first/recurring payment, provider order reference and verification time. Do not infer recurring economics from a successful first charge alone.

## Ownership and review

Primary executor: `ct.chlom.agent.locticians-claim-outreach`.

Supporting agents include sales follow-up/suppression, sales continuity, inbound nurture/close and lead-risk vetting. Binding legal, financial, identity-dispute, security, exceptional-rights and ungoverned pricing decisions leave the sales lane and enter the appropriate human/governance boundary.
