# CrownThrive Launch Product & Ops Care Workflow

`buyer problem -> research -> product spec -> reusable operating assets -> QA -> rights/policy review -> price evidence -> package -> staged commerce -> fulfillment test -> activation -> managed setup -> Ops Care -> product-learning delta`

Each packet needs a stable SKU, buyer/problem scope, source register, operating procedures, editable templates where applicable, setup instructions, positive/negative tests, acceptance criteria, license placeholder, dependencies, support assumptions, version, manifest/checksums and rollback trigger.

Managed implementation is bounded to an approved product version. Material custom scope routes to bespoke Business Formation & System Ops.

Recurring Ops Care may cover workflow/documentation refresh, KPI/rhythm review and approved platform-integration checks. It cannot become unlimited bespoke consulting or unlicensed legal/tax/accounting advice.

Agent routing: A orchestrates; B audits evidence/differentiation; C builds; D gates; S reviews privacy/security/IP; F documents; G checks provider/dependencies; H certifies delivery where applicable; E handles APIs/automation; `ct.agent.crownthrive-launch-ops-product` remains non-voting.

No exact price, checkout, entitlement, recurring billing, professional conclusion or public certification activates from an agent-generated kit alone.
