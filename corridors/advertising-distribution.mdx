## Advertising, Affiliate and Distribution Corridor

This corridor builds a measurable owned-and-partner distribution network across web, creators, media, affiliates, ambassadors, events, and physical locations.

## PentaGreen commercial operating layer

[**PentaGreen™**](/commerce/pentagreen) is the mandatory economic activation authority for this corridor. AdLuxe, CrownFluence, affiliates, ambassadors and other distribution systems retain their specialist functions, while PentaGreen governs whether the underlying commercial object may be sold, promoted, priced, budgeted, invoiced, credited, commissioned, settled or paid.

Advertising inventory is therefore treated as a governed evergreen commercial asset family. Qualified web placements, sponsorship packages, creator campaigns, media/program sponsorships, events, signage, screens, locations and managed campaign services receive canonical identities and economic rules linked to CHLOM rights and a valid Evergreen Commerce Activation Certificate (`ECAC`) where the offer is active.

The closed loop is:

```text
CHLOM asset / AdLuxe inventory
→ PentaGreen commercial eligibility
→ SKU/service identity + price/rate + ECAC
→ AdLuxe Studio creative
→ AdLuxe Network / Places / CrownFluence distribution
→ CrownLytics attribution and delivery evidence
→ PentaGreen revenue/margin/settlement reconciliation
→ optimize | pause | expand | compensate
```

Provider delivery evidence does not automatically authorize an invoice, payout or public performance claim.

## Architecture

- **AdLuxe Studio** — creative-production layer for campaign assets, copy, product imagery, UGC-style concepts and production workflows.
- **AdLuxe Network** — web advertising / ad-server layer with advertiser, publisher and admin operations.
- **AdLuxe Places** — place-based signage, screens, schedules, campaigns and playback inventory.
- **CrownFluence** — creator campaign exchange, UGC opportunities, influence intelligence and managed relationships.
- **Crown Affiliates** — performance/referral lane.
- **Crown Ambassadors** — qualified advocacy/representation lane with separate standards and progression.
- **CrownLytics** — normalized campaign, attribution, conversion, inventory, creator and revenue reporting.

## AdLuxe evidence and implementation registry

The [AdLuxe Network Institutional Registry](/platforms/adluxe-network-institutional-registry) is the current detailed source for Network, Studio and Places; advertiser/publisher/location/creator roles; inventory; delivery; measurement; API/stats; dashboards; consent; brand safety; economics; provider boundaries; automation and open verification gates.

Adserver.Online or another provider may supply SaaS ad-serving capability. It is not CrownThrive's network identity or institutional source of truth. Provider capability must remain distinct from the exact CrownThrive account, plan, API, credential, version and production state verified through authorized evidence.

The [Crown Affiliates and Ambassadors Institutional Registry](/platforms/crown-affiliates-ambassadors-institutional-registry) governs participant identity, offers, attribution, commission schedules, disclosure/training, payouts and progression. The [ThrivePush Institutional Registry](/platforms/thrivepush-institutional-registry) governs consent, suppression, audiences, templates, delivery events and provider-state verification.

## Creator, affiliate and ambassador separation

The [CrownFluence Institutional Registry](/platforms/crownfluence-institutional-registry) governs creator/influencer profiles, campaign briefs, applications, deliverables, releases, disclosures, approvals, performance evidence and creator compensation.

The [Crown Affiliates and Ambassadors Institutional Registry](/platforms/crown-affiliates-ambassadors-institutional-registry) governs attributable referral economics and higher-trust advocacy/representation as separate roles.

- **AdLuxe** owns approved inventory, delivery, reporting and advertiser/publisher/location operations.
- **CrownFluence** owns creator campaign relationships, deliverables and creator-use permissions.
- **Crown Affiliates** owns offer/link/code attribution and commission eligibility.
- **Crown Ambassadors** owns qualified advocacy, training, representation and progression.

One role never automatically grants another role's authority, data access, economics or rights.

## Role separation

Advertisers, publishers, locations, creators, affiliates and ambassadors are distinct commercial actors. Each requires role-specific contracts, permissions, dashboards, economics, disclosures and performance evidence.

## Inventory integrity

Only real placements, impressions, clicks, conversions, playbacks, creator deliverables and revenue should be reported. Internal testing, mock inventory and illustrative data must be labeled and excluded from commercial performance claims.

## Brand safety and compliance

Campaign systems need content suitability, regulated-category restrictions, audience/privacy controls, claims review, creator disclosure rules, placement exclusions, dispute handling and takedown capability.

## Integration rule

AdLuxe should consume CrownThrive identity, rights, content metadata and analytics rather than creating parallel sources of truth. Campaign and creator permissions should reference CHLOM or the applicable contract/evidence record.

## Phase 3 implication

The event model should support campaign, creative, placement, impression, click, conversion, creator-delivery, approval, invoice and payout events with stable identifiers and auditable source-system references.
