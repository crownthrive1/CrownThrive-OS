# Beauty, Wellness and Phygital Corridor

This corridor compounds an existing professional/community base with products, operations, education, loyalty, media, advertising, and eventually physical nodes. Digital demand and operating economics should validate capital-intensive expansion.

## Core progression

```text
Locticians
→ Melanin Magic
→ ThriveSeat
→ Education / Rewards / Media
→ MM Suites pilot
→ Phygital nodes and regional expansion
```

## Platform roles

- **Locticians** — community, directory, professional discovery and industry network.
- **Melanin Magic** — beauty/wellness product brand spanning consumer, professional and wholesale lanes.
- **ThriveSeat** — booking, service, staff and business operations for beauty/wellness businesses.
- **Locticians TV** — education, tutorials, interviews, events and media inventory.
- **CrownRewards** — loyalty and retention.
- **AdLuxe Places** — place-based advertising inventory.
- **ThriveKiosks / TapStations** — check-in, identity, loyalty, ordering, ads, profiles and local discovery endpoints.
- **MM Suites** — planned physical-suite model that should be validated through an operating pilot before broad franchise/regional scale.

## MM Suites governance

The MM Suites source corpus contains substantial Suite Pro, franchisee, regional-licensee and corporate planning. Those materials are institutional planning evidence, not automatic proof that a franchise program, unit economics, FDD, or legal pathway is currently approved. Physical expansion requires updated underwriting, occupancy and rent assumptions, reserves, insurance, permits, health/safety controls, operating systems, and qualified franchise/licensing counsel.

## Product operations

Melanin Magic's vendor/manufacturer stack and any future in-house or private-label expansion must be recorded by current vendor, manufacturing role, quality standard, margin, fulfillment route, support obligations and rights/claims evidence.

## Phase 3 implication

Rights, product, location, operator, license, revenue-split, partner, equipment and evidence records should be modeled in a way that can support both digital operations now and future physical nodes without requiring a new governance system.
