# Faith, Ministry and Scripture Products

**The Sermon Toolkit** is the umbrella ministry-product platform and **KJV Visualized** is its flagship scripture experience. This is best modeled as a strategic vertical that consumes the wider CrownThrive education, publishing, commerce, media, licensing, AI, support and community spine rather than as an isolated technology stack.

## Current experience model

The live KJV Visualized property currently exposes KJV reading, Daily Devotional, Visual Bible, Open Sanctuary, Reflection, ministry/study rooms, a governed product library/store, partner shop, plans, My Sanctuary, developer/IO surfaces, help, responsible-AI policy and legal/trust pages.

## Core institutional lanes

- **Scripture** — KJV-centered reader, references, people, places, themes, events and journeys.
- **Devotional / study** — devotionals, Open Sanctuary, prayer/reflection, study rooms and discipleship resources.
- **Publishing** — books, printables, guided journals, teaching cards, children/youth resources and visual editions.
- **Ministry operations** — sermon tools, Sunday-to-Sunday conversion workflows, church/teacher resources, archival and training services.
- **Membership** — My Sanctuary, saved materials, reflection/private records, plan entitlements and team/church roles.
- **Commerce / licensing** — personal digital products, church/educator/maker/commercial license classes, partner commerce and governed fulfillment.
- **AI** — assistance for creation, transformation and organization with explicit scripture/source/reviewer boundaries.

## Theological and cultural governance

The content standard is KJV-centered and culturally aware of Black church and Southern Baptist contexts while clearly separating **Scripture**, **commentary**, **illustration**, **application**, and **generated assistance**. No automation should fabricate biblical quotations, historical proof, testimonies, pastoral authority or review.

## Privacy

Private prayer, reflection and pastoral-care information is high-sensitivity data. It must not enter advertising, analytics, logs, public AI training, or general agent memory merely because the product can integrate with those systems.

## Rights / commerce

Digital products intended for physical creation remain digital files plus governed license rights unless a separate physical fulfillment route is explicitly offered. CHLOM should model personal, household, group, church, school, maker, fundraiser, display/streaming, POD and enterprise permissions as separate grants when applicable.

## Phase 3 implication

The platform should consume common identity, product, entitlement, license, evidence, event and approval services while keeping theological content governance specific to the ministry vertical.
