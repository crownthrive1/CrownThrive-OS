# Hospitality, Travel and Experiences

The current portfolio registry contains a working **Hospitality, Travel & Experiences** corridor even though some strategic plans summarize CrownThrive through six or seven broader operating corridors. That discrepancy should be preserved and resolved deliberately rather than silently forcing the assets into another category.

## Representative concepts

Historical and current source material includes **Luxperiences**, Luxperiences Network/imprint materials, curated stays, services, cultural itineraries, events and other experiential concepts.

## Current doctrine

This corridor remains **registry-valid but strategically under review**. Assets can be mapped here while institutional governance decides whether Hospitality/Travel/Experiences remains a permanent top-level corridor, becomes a sub-corridor of Media/Culture or Commerce, or operates as a collection of licensed experience products.

## Activation requirements

Travel, hospitality and experience products require stronger operational diligence because real-world delivery may involve third-party providers, reservations, refunds, insurance, local law, accessibility, consumer protection, safety, taxes and partner performance.

## Phase 3 implication

The data model must not hard-code a fixed number of corridors. Corridor records should have stable IDs, hierarchy, aliases, status and effective dates so organizational taxonomy can evolve without rewriting asset history.
