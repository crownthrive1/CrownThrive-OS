# MM Suites Lineage and Current-State Model

MM Suites appears across prospectuses, operating plans, Help Center materials, corridor maps, and later CrownThrive strategy. Phase 2.7 separates the layers so a historical prospectus is not mistaken for current legal or operational reality.

## Doctrine layer

MM Suites is designed as a beauty/wellness and phygital ecosystem model connecting professional service providers, physical or location-based experiences, booking, education, loyalty, media, commerce, CHLOM governance, and CrownThrive shared infrastructure.

## Operating architecture layer

Source families connect MM Suites with concepts including:

- Suite Pro / SuitePros
- ThriveSeat / ThriveSeats
- Kamora360
- CrownRewards
- CrownThriveU
- affiliates and referrals
- Melanin Magic products/wholesale
- Locticians discovery and professional networks
- CHLOM rights/compliance/governance
- partner onboarding, support, training, and progression

These relationships should be registered independently of whether a physical location or franchise program is active.

## Historical economic/legal research

Prospectus-era materials include concepts involving locations, franchisees, regional licensing, revenue overrides, investor qualification, Suite Pro onboarding, and transferable/structured economic interests.

<Warning>
  Those source records are institutional research/history unless current signed agreements, counsel-reviewed program documents, regulatory compliance, and production evidence establish them as active.
</Warning>

## Required status split

Phase 3 should maintain separate records for:

1. `mm_suites_framework`

4. current digital products/services
5. physical-location concepts

## Current-state rule

Never infer that MM Suites currently operates physical locations, franchises, transferable licenses, investment offerings, or guaranteed revenue merely because historical source material describes those pathways.
