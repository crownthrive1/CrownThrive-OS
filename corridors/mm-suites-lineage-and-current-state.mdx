# MM Suites Lineage and Current-State Model

MM Suites appears across a deep source family including Master Prospectus, Master Plan, Full Business Plan, strategic-growth blueprints, War Room materials, pitch decks, Help Center content and later CrownThrive operating plans. Those documents establish substantial institutional IP and intended operating architecture, but not every legal or physical implementation state.

## Founder-adjudicated direction

The **franchise and regional-license model remains intended**. CrownThrive should continue recovering, modernizing and building the operational infrastructure required to support that model.

The current task is not to erase franchise/regional concepts because they were not fully activated. It is to separate:

1. the IP and operating model CrownThrive owns;
2. software/infrastructure that can be built now;
3. physical/location readiness;
4. franchise/regional legal activation that requires the appropriate agreements, disclosures, registrations or other legal work.

## Doctrine layer

MM Suites is a beauty/wellness and phygital operating model connecting professional service providers, Suite Pros, physical/location experiences, booking, education, loyalty, media, commerce, CHLOM governance and shared CrownThrive infrastructure.

## Confirmed source family

The institutional corpus includes multiple 2025 MM Suites documents, including the **Master Prospectus**, **Master Plan**, **Full Business Plan**, **Revised Final Strategic Growth Blueprint & Investor Prospectus**, **War Room Bundle**, and pitch-deck materials. These are separate source records and should be versioned rather than collapsed into one undated plan.

## Operating architecture

MM Suites source families and current CrownThrive properties connect the model with:

- Suite Pro / SuitePros;
- ThriveSeat / ThriveSeats;
- Locticians;
- Melanin Magic and wholesale/product lanes;
- Kamora360;
- CrownRewards;
- CrownThriveU;
- affiliates/referrals;
- CrownThrive media and distribution;
- CII / ThriveFund internal reinvestment alignment;
- CHLOM rights/compliance/governance;
- partner onboarding, support, training and progression.

The live Virality Music ecosystem also currently routes users into Melanin Magic, Suite Pros and MM Suites as part of its broader cultural/product ecosystem. That is current integration evidence, not proof of physical-franchise activation.

## Build-now layer

Phase 3 and subsequent product work may build:

- Suite Pro identity/role records;
- location/operator registry;
- territory models;
- training/credential requirements;
- booking and customer routing;
- product/wholesale integrations;
- rewards and referral logic;
- rights/policy controls;
- evidence and audit trails;
- operational dashboards;
- support and incident workflows;
- internal ThriveFund allocation records;
- franchise/regional-license readiness data structures.

## Legal-activation layer

Franchise disclosure, registration, territory grants, franchise fees, regional licenses, transferability, equity/investment interests, guaranteed economics and similar legal constructs may activate only after appropriate review and evidence.

Historical economics remain research assumptions until adopted into a current versioned economic schedule.

## Status vocabulary

Every MM Suites component must use a versioned state such as `historical`, `research`, `specified`, `build`, `pilot`, `legal-review`, `ready`, `active`, `suspended`, `retired` or `superseded`.
