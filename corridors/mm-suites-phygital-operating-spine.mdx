# MM Suites Phygital Operating Spine

MM Suites is the place-based and phygital expression of the CrownThrive Convergent Ecosystem. It converts shared digital infrastructure into a governed operating model for professionals, services, products, education, community, licensing, locations and long-term opportunity.

The 2025 prospectus family is foundational IP and design history. Its smart-contract, franchise, equity, token, lifetime-override, filing and production claims require current evidence and legal activation before external use.

## Operating thesis

```text
CrownThrive ID / social identity bridge
        ↓
Suite Pro / operator / customer / partner identity
        ↓
Locticians discovery + ThriveSeat booking
        ↓
Melanin Magic products + licensable imprints
        ↓
CrownThriveU training and credentials
        ↓
Crown Affiliates / Ambassadors / CrownRewards
        ↓
CHLOM rights, rules, roles, revenue, records, remedies
        ↓
OpsOasis / ops workflows / vendor coordination
        ↓
CrownLytics + CrownPulse + ThrivePush
        ↓
CII / ThriveFund evidence and reinvestment
        ↓
location, territory and regional readiness
```

## Kamora360 supersession in the MM Suites architecture

The 2025 MM Suites prospectus family names Kamora360 as the historical back-office CRM/operations hub and describes API/event relationships around it. Those source documents remain preserved exactly as foundational design evidence. Effective August 17, 2026, **OpsOasis supersedes Kamora360 in the current target architecture**.

This is a platform-successor correction only. It does not validate historical claims about CrownTransact, private blockchain deployment, automatic smart-contract allocations, digital wallets, or every described API. Those capabilities retain their own evidence and legal states.

Any MM Suites implementation that previously expected a Kamora360 interface must now map its current operational requirement to OpsOasis, CrownThrive IO, ThriveSeat, CHLOM or another specifically governed system rather than recreating Kamora360.

## Role model

### Founder / Holdings / portfolio

Strategic architecture, IP, capital allocation, territory/brand decisions and reserved governance.

### MM Suites corporate operations

Program standards, location readiness, operator support, product/vendor coordination, training, data, brand and system health.

### Regional operator / licensee candidate

Regional development and support authority only after legal/economic status is explicitly granted.

### Location operator

Local operations, staff/provider coordination, customer experience, facility standards, inventory and reporting.

### Suite Pro

Independent or authorized service professional with defined access, training, brand/use permissions, booking, products, referrals and community participation.

### Customer/member

Booking, purchases, memberships, rewards, consent, support and data rights.

### Vendor/contractor

Buildout, maintenance, products, technology or services under scoped work and access.

### Ambassador/affiliate/mentor/instructor

Distinct roles with separate authority, attribution and economic schedules.

## Capability domains

### Identity and onboarding

Application, verification, role assignment, organization/location link, training prerequisites, agreements, insurance/credential evidence where applicable, access provisioning and periodic review.

### Place and territory

Location/site records, readiness checklist, jurisdiction, leases/permissions by reference, buildout, accessibility, safety, equipment, service categories, territory status and opening/closure state.

### Booking and customer operations

ThriveSeat is the current canonical CrownThrive booking/business-operations platform in this corridor. Phase 2.98 Pass C1 verifies its live public service and **Frezka SaaS/Iqonic implementation lineage**, while the exact installed version, repository, database, API routes and payment configuration remain unverified.

Current division of responsibility:

```text
Locticians
professional discovery / profile / category / location / claim / verification
        ↓ linked provider-business identity
ThriveSeat
service catalog / availability / booking / branch / staff / customer / payment state
        ↓ approved operational work
OpsOasis
internal tasks / incidents / execution / migration work where implemented
        ↓
MM Suites / Suite Pro
program / location / operator / training / license / territory authority
```

Rules:

- Locticians remains source-of-truth for discovery/profile/trust records;
- ThriveSeat remains source-of-truth for appointments/service-business operations unless a later ADR replaces it;
- OpsOasis does not absorb customer bookings merely because it succeeds Kamora360's execution role;
- a ThriveSeat subscription or business record does not create Suite Pro/MM Suites/franchise/license status;
- Frezka is replaceable implementation infrastructure; CrownThrive stable booking/business IDs must survive vendor migration;
- historical MM Suites documents naming `ThriveSeats` and Kamora360 remain preserved but current schema resolves the platform as **ThriveSeat** and the execution successor as **OpsOasis**.

### Products and commerce

Melanin Magic, wholesale, local/small-batch imprints such as Melanin Magic Cafe, GSO, approved third-party products, inventory, commissions, fulfillment and licensing.

### Education and advancement

CrownThriveU onboarding, credential pathways, business/brand training, mentorship, operator development, succession and continuous standards.

### Distribution and Flywheel

Affiliates, ambassadors, referral attribution, ThrivePush, AdLuxe, media, CrownRewards and cross-platform opportunities. The [CrownRewards Institutional Registry](/platforms/crownrewards-institutional-registry) governs points, partner programs, redemptions, reversals and closed-loop value; monetary affiliate/ambassador commissions remain separate economic schedules.

“Lifetime,” permanent, points-to-dollar, override, franchise-referral or similar economic examples from historical MM Suites sources are not current unless a valid adopted schedule explicitly creates them. Historical examples remain preserved for lineage and contradiction analysis rather than silently becoming operating economics.

### Governance and remedies

CHLOM policy/eligibility checks, rights, agreements, DLA/brand permissions, audit events, disputes, holds, corrections, training remediation, suspension, termination and appeal.

### Impact and reinvestment

CII/ThriveFund program criteria, designated corporate allocations, milestones, evidence, public claims and corrections. CII is not a nonprofit and ThriveFund is not automatically an investment fund or charity.

## Economic model

Economic relationships must be separate versioned schedules:

- booking/service platform fees;
- subscription/membership;
- rent/license/space charges;
- wholesale/product margin;
- affiliate/ambassador commission;
- referral reward;
- instructor/mentor compensation;
- operator/location share;
- brand/license fee;
- technology/service fee;
- impact/reinvestment allocation;
- reserves/holdbacks/clawbacks.

No schedule implies equity, ownership or inheritance unless a current signed/legal record explicitly provides it.

## Success measures

Measure verified onboarding, activation time, booking conversion, customer retention, professional income/usage, training completion, product attachment, referral quality, support resolution, standards compliance, operator health, impact evidence and recoverability—not only location count or projected valuation.
