# Commerce, Brands and Product Operations

The commerce corridor converts CrownThrive intellectual property, services, products, licenses, and brand concepts into reliable transactions without creating an uncontrolled inventory burden.

## Mandatory commerce authority

[**PentaGreen™**](/commerce/pentagreen) (legacy alias: ThriveEvergreen™) is the institutional commerce and economic-activation authority for this corridor. Every CrownThrive product, SKU, price, offer, listing, license, checkout, subscription, marketplace transaction, entitlement, fulfillment, refund, commission, royalty, payout, advertising package and other money-moving commercial object must progressively resolve through its governed activation contract.

ThriveEvergreen does not replace CHLOM, CIE, GSO, Go Flipbooks, Stripe, AdLuxe or individual storefronts. It binds them together: CHLOM determines rights and authority; CIE governs cultural alignment; ThriveEvergreen productizes, prices, assigns SKUs, certifies offers, selects channels, operates approved commerce/advertising adapters, reconciles transactions and creates rollback or compensating actions when required.

A commercial object is not considered institutionally active merely because a provider or storefront can transact it. The target state requires a valid Evergreen Commerce Activation Certificate (`ECAC`) linked to the canonical asset, product, SKU, rights, price, tax, fulfillment, entitlement, risk and policy versions.

## Core properties

- **Good Shit Only (GSO)** — broad marketplace and commercialization lane for eligible digital products, services, creative assets, governed licenses, and selected physical/POD goods.
- **Melanin Magic** — focused beauty product brand with consumer, professional and wholesale economics.
- **Go Flipbooks** — digital reader/library and entitlement delivery.
- **CHLOMLex / LEX** — governed commercial surface for qualified brands, domains, licenses, leases, IP and rights opportunities; current implementation status must remain explicit.
- **The Artful Mane Gallery** — art and art-commerce corridor using approved fulfillment/partner infrastructure.
- **Melanated Stock / Vault** — culturally aligned asset-commerce concepts whose current state must be governed by the portfolio registry.
- **Incubated brands** — ThriveWick, Tears of Defeat, ThriveThreads, ThriveSip, XENThrive/Get Kinetic!, MusiqHead Audio, ZaZa-related concepts and other brands remain subject to lifecycle and demand gates.

## Digital-first product standard

A listing card is not a product. Each monetized digital product should have:

- stable SKU/product ID;
- canonical master file or reader;
- preview and metadata;
- rights confirmation;
- format validation;
- price and tax classification;
- license/usage terms;
- tested checkout;
- tested fulfillment/entitlement;
- download/access limits where appropriate;
- support article;
- analytics events;
- refund/dispute behavior;
- version and correction history.

If CrownThrive sells a digital file intended to help the buyer make a physical object—SVG, laser, print, craft, Canva, embroidery, wall art—the listing must distinguish the **digital file/license** from physical fulfillment.

## Product families and versions

Internal versions may remain monetizable where substantively different, but public SEO-facing names should communicate customer value. Product families need explicit upgrade, bundle, legacy-access and duplicate-entitlement rules.

## Phase 3 implication

Phase 3 must implement ThriveEvergreen's machine-readable asset/product/variant/SKU/offer/listing/license/entitlement/order/fulfillment/campaign/economic-event model, ECAC authorization service, ledger, pricing/risk/reconciliation services and provider adapters. Stripe, GSO, Go Flipbooks, AdLuxe and later storefronts remain projections or execution providers; they do not become the canonical source of CrownThrive rights or product identity.

The hard rule is `no-commerce-bypass`: certified commerce-enabled applications must request `ECAC | HOLD | DENY` before economic activation and must emit canonical commerce events for reconciliation.
