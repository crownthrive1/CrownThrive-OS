# Locticians Claimable-Profile Outreach

## Purpose

Research claimable Locticians profiles, verify lawful public business contact paths, prepare evidence-based claim outreach, and remain fail-closed unless compliance, suppression, rights and offer gates pass.

## Required inputs

- exact listing URL and current claimable state;
- verified business category, location and lawful public contact source;
- suppression, prior-contact and risk state;
- current private outbound-compliance record;
- current governed offer and checkout-verification state.

## Allowed

- research and verify public business facts;
- prepare personalized claim-support candidates;
- resolve a current offer from the restricted governed registry;
- record source, claim, suppression and outcome evidence;
- send only when the current private gate explicitly authorizes it.

## Stop or escalate

- do not guess private emails or owner identities;
- do not invent services, traffic, reviews, demand, ranking, savings or results;
- do not copy third-party creative without rights;
- do not publish or repeat restricted admin configuration;
- do not promise plan eligibility, recurring treatment, savings or continued discounts without exact checkout and renewal readback;
- stop on opt-out, wrong person, bounce, complaint, claim completion, legal hold, risk hold or suppression.

## Public offer evidence

The current first-party `/join` page displays `CLAIMMONTH50` as a 50% incentive for claimable Community+ and Basic listings. This is a dated public observation only. It does not prove current checkout eligibility, provider-admin state, recurring behavior, future availability, cancellation or refund behavior, or customer entitlement.

Customer-facing economics must be resolved from current checkout evidence. Until exact checkout verification exists, the only approved public instruction is: **Check current eligibility, terms, and price at checkout.**

## Cold-outreach gate

Outbound send requires current private proof that cold outreach is enabled, a valid physical postal address is configured, the recipient is not suppressed, the business contact source is verified, the listing remains claimable, and all applicable rights and risk gates pass. Until then, research and queue only.

## Output contract

Record the profile URL, category, location, contact source, verified research facts, personalization hook, offer version, checkout-verification state, send or hold state, suppression state, next action and claim outcome. Never place raw customer data or restricted provider evidence in the public repository.

## Verification

A sent message is not evidence of claim completion. A successful first payment is not evidence of recurring treatment. Promotion requires provider readback, applicable specialists, rollback and current-head governance.
