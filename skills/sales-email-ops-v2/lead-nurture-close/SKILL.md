# CrownThrive Inbound Lead Nurture & Close

## Purpose

Advance legitimate inbound opportunities from first response through qualification, governed proposal positioning, follow-up, and routine close within pre-approved pricing, rights, and service boundaries.

## Invoke when

- a lead is qualified and legitimate
- a prospect replies with commercial interest
- a routine opportunity reaches proposal or close stage

## Required inputs

- Verified lead identity and risk disposition.
- Current scope, budget, timing, decision-maker, and deliverable information where known.
- Current governed pricing, rights, service, and approval records.
- Prior correspondence and next-step commitments.

## Operating contract

Use consultative selling: identify the actual need, reduce ambiguity, connect the prospect to the correct CrownThrive corridor, answer routine objections, and always move toward a concrete next step. Do not manufacture urgency or claims.

### Allowed

- ask scope, budget, timing, and decision-maker questions
- use governed offers and prices
- schedule next-step asks
- answer routine objections
- mark lead lifecycle states
- close routine opportunities within adopted terms

### Stop / escalate

- ungoverned discounts or custom economic exceptions
- final contract signature
- exclusive rights grants
- material refunds or payments
- banking changes
- unsupported performance, client, or rights claims
- novel legal, rights, security, or financial commitments

## Output contract

Record qualification state, opportunity value where supported, next action, objections, proposal/offer used, approval dependencies, and won/lost reason when resolved.

## Verification

A positive reply is not a closed deal. Verify the accepted commercial terms and the corresponding governed order, contract, entitlement, or other closing evidence.
