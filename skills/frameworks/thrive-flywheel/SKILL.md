**Framework ID:** `ct.framework.thrive-flywheel`  
**Agent ID:** `ct.framework-agent.thrive-flywheel`  
**Skill ID:** `ct.skill.framework.thrive-flywheel.precompile.v1`  
**State:** candidate precompile / nonvoting  
**Authority ceiling:** D2  
**Activation effect:** none

## Purpose

Translate the Thrive Flywheel into measurable candidate events, state transitions, routing rules, feedback loops, and cross-corridor value hypotheses without creating economic authority.

## Allowed work

- Map create, build, enter, reinvest, reward, measure, distribute, sell, and renewed-create stages to canonical events.
- Define candidate KPIs, denominators, attribution, retention, routing, and feedback contracts.
- Model cross-platform flywheel paths and detect dead ends, duplicated acquisition loops, or missing measurement.
- Generate test fixtures and analytics requirements for CrownLytics, CrownPulse, ThrivePush, CrownRewards, affiliates, and adjacent corridors.

## Hard boundaries

- Do not move money, issue rewards, create commissions, publish pricing, or create economic truth from analytics.
- Do not activate a platform, entitlement, campaign, or provider binding.
- Do not promote the Flywheel before Convergent Ecosystem predecessor evidence is accepted.
- No self-certification, sovereign vote, direct-main merge, silent deletion, or D3 action.

## Promotion handoff

Parallel precompile is allowed. Economic activation routes through ThriveEvergreen/CHLOM and sequence promotion remains gated.

## Completion result

Emit `precompile_ready`, `precompile_hold`, or `precompile_denied` with exact source/head lineage, metric denominators, unresolved evidence, and downstream gate requirements.
