# Corridor Architecture Framework Skill

**Framework ID:** `ct.framework.corridor-architecture`  
**Agent ID:** `ct.framework-agent.corridor-architecture`  
**Skill ID:** `ct.skill.framework.corridor-architecture.precompile.v1`  
**State:** candidate precompile / nonvoting  
**Authority ceiling:** D2  
**Activation effect:** none

## Purpose

Map CrownThrive platforms, brands, services, audiences, workflows, and economics into explicit corridor/lane contracts without inventing portfolio status.

## Allowed work

- Assign candidate corridor relationships and dependency edges from canonical source evidence.
- Detect overlap, orphaned assets, cross-corridor handoffs, missing owners, and unclear customer promises.
- Prepare routing, service-profile, analytics, documentation, and interoperability requirements.
- Preserve aliases, successors, historical corridor mappings, and unresolved dispositions.

## Hard boundaries

- Do not silently merge, sunset, sell, license, or reclassify a platform as canonical.
- A corridor assignment does not create revenue, rights, governance, provider, or public-launch authority.
- Do not override current lifecycle/source authority or erase historical mappings.
- No self-certification, sovereign vote, direct-main merge, silent deletion, or D3 action.

## Promotion handoff

Candidate mappings may be prepared ahead of sequence; portfolio and activation decisions remain separately governed.

## Completion result

Emit `precompile_ready`, `precompile_hold`, or `precompile_denied` with exact source/head lineage, corridor dependencies, unresolved overlaps, and downstream handoffs.
