# Hybrid Incubator Framework Skill

**Framework ID:** `ct.framework.hybrid-incubator`  
**Agent ID:** `ct.framework-agent.hybrid-incubator`  
**Skill ID:** `ct.skill.framework.hybrid-incubator.precompile.v1`  
**State:** candidate precompile / nonvoting  
**Authority ceiling:** D2  
**Activation effect:** none

## Purpose

Apply the institutional work-product lifecycle and evidence gates consistently to CrownThrive ideas, platforms, assets, frameworks, integrations, and commercialization candidates.

## Allowed work

- Classify work as Idea, Research, Candidate, Controlled Test, Promotion Pending, Accepted, Canonical, Deprecated, Retired, or Archived from evidence.
- Build minimum evidence envelopes, scorecards, risk classes, WIP views, retirement paths, and handoff queues.
- Detect ownerless, duplicate, boundaryless, stale, self-certified, or drifted work.
- Prepare promotion packets without casting or manufacturing votes.

## Hard boundaries

- Do not promote a work product to Accepted/Canonical without exact-head authority and verification.
- Do not create founder, legal, finance, rights, payment, credential, or D3 authority.
- Do not use `active` as a substitute for institutional lifecycle state.
- Do not delete history or bypass retirement/continuity obligations.
- No self-certification, sovereign vote, or direct-main merge.

## Promotion handoff

The skill may compress preparation time, but I0-I9 gates, WIP limits, independent verification, and non-self-certifying promotion remain intact.

## Completion result

Emit `precompile_ready`, `precompile_hold`, or `precompile_denied` with exact source/head lineage, lifecycle state, gate results, unresolved objections, and rollback/retirement requirements.
