# CrownThrive Pentafabric Skill

## Purpose

Use CrownThrive Pentafabric™ to analyze, design, reconcile, or package a CrownThrive or licensed system across five distinct layers: Identity & Imprint, Rights & Conditions, Ledger & Assurance, Execution & Interoperability, and Economy & Distribution.

## Required method

1. Identify the subject, asset, actor, organization, repository, agent, or product and its stable identity/provenance context.
2. Identify controlling rights, rules, roles, contracts, conditions, DLA state, issuer authority, restrictions, and remedies.
3. Identify DAIL/evidence requirements, hashes/signatures/attestations, verification, compliance evaluation, correction, and audit state.
4. Identify the implementation surfaces: pallets, modules, frameworks, plugins, skills, APIs, MCPs, agents, factories, portals, dashboards, wireframes, website mesh, and provider adapters.
5. Identify the economic and distribution surface only after authority exists: ThriveEvergreen/ECAC, SKU, access, price candidate, entitlement, payment, revenue routing, licensing class, and channel distribution.

## Invariants

- DAIL = Decentralized Autonomous Information Ledger.
- DLA = Dynamic Licensing Asset.
- Current issuer authority term = Licensing Stewardship / Issuer Authority.
- Execution may not manufacture authority.
- Provider success is not institutional truth.
- A build receipt is not certification.
- Public projection must remain public-safe.
- Private prompts, private scoring, credentials, protected identities, restricted evidence, and private economic logic may remain vault-bounded.
- Economic activation is resolved through ThriveEvergreen / ECAC.

## Output pattern

When producing a candidate, return or materialize:

- stable candidate ID;
- version;
- Pentafabric layer(s);
- source/evidence references;
- authority ceiling;
- dependencies;
- public/private classification;
- required contracts;
- verification requirements;
- rollback/correction path;
- commercialization pathway, if applicable.

## Commercial boundary

This public-safe skill describes the framework method at a high level. Commercial implementation, private execution prompts, restricted datasets, private scoring logic, proprietary automation details, and production machine access may require a separate CrownThrive license or service agreement.
