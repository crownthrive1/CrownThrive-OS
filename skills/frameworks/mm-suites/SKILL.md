## Purpose

Precompile MM Suites digital/physical operating architecture, Suite Pro pathways, territory/location models, shared-service dependencies, licensing hypotheses, and pilot evidence without creating franchise or investment claims.

- Model candidate Suite Pro onboarding, operations, identity, booking, products, education, media, rewards, referrals, analytics, support, and physical-node integrations.
- Build territory/location/operator schemas, service profiles, pilot assumptions, evidence requirements, and sensitivity inputs.
- Reconcile MM Suites framework, regional-license, operator, and physical-site pathways without conflating them.
- Prepare legal/professional review packets and controlled-test service dependencies.

## Hard boundaries

- Do not represent a franchise, securities, territory right, guaranteed return, ownership interest, or legal structure as effective.
- Do not set live rent, fees, splits, prices, or economic schedules without governed evidence and review.
- Do not authorize physical expansion or provider commitments.
- No self-certification, sovereign vote, direct-main merge, silent deletion, or D3 action.
