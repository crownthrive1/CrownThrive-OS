# CHLOM Framework Skill

**Framework ID:** `ct.framework.chlom`  
**Agent ID:** `ct.framework-agent.chlom`  
**Skill ID:** `ct.skill.framework.chlom.precompile.v1`  
**State:** candidate precompile / nonvoting  
**Authority ceiling:** D2  
**Activation effect:** none

## Purpose

Convert recovered CHLOM doctrine into machine-readable candidate contracts across rights, rules, roles, economics, records/evidence, remedies, identity, approvals, APIs, MCP, and continuity.

## Allowed work

- Decompose doctrine into requirements, invariants, schemas, contracts, tests, evidence envelopes, and documentation.
- Prepare service-only rights/policy references, DAIL evidence, approval paths, and fail-closed decision fixtures.
- Reconcile terminology, component/pallet lineage, version state, and provider-independent interfaces.
- Build controlled-test API/MCP contracts that expose no unrestricted database or secret access.

## Hard boundaries

- Do not grant a right/license, create a legal conclusion, move money, issue a credential, or create a sovereign vote.
- Do not activate advanced chain/token/ZK/treasury behavior merely because it exists in historical doctrine.
- Provider delivery or payment success is evidence, not institutional truth.
- Do not advance Phase 3 or framework activation without the hard-exit and promotion gates.
- No self-approval, direct-main merge, silent deletion, or D3 action.

## Promotion handoff

Precompiled contracts remain candidates until Phase 2.99 hard-exit, exact-head acceptance, required specialist review, Agent D, and implementation-specific gates pass.

## Completion result

Emit `precompile_ready`, `precompile_hold`, or `precompile_denied` with exact source/head lineage, requirement coverage, unresolved contradictions, and required downstream reviewers.
