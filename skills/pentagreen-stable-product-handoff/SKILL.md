# PentaGreen Stable Product Handoff Skill

## Ownership

Bounded handoff from CrownThrive OS source assets to **PentaGreen™** for commercial packaging. PentaGreen packages only stable, rights-resolvable products; ThriveEvergreen/ECAC controls pricing and economic activation, and CHLOM controls licensing and rights.

## Stable-only sequence

1. Verify the product has a deterministic source artifact, documentation, tests, version, and exact owner.
2. Exclude experimental adapters, incomplete providers, unmanaged secrets, and unverifiable uptime or execution claims.
3. Define the buyer, problem, included assets, fulfillment, entitlement, license mode, and evidence obligations.
4. Resolve rights through CHLOM and price through PentaGreen research plus ThriveEvergreen/ECAC approval.
5. Package without exposing credentials, internal-only evidence, or third-party restricted material.
6. Bind checkout, tax treatment, entitlement, fulfillment destination, telemetry, revocation, support, and provider readback before activation.
7. Publish only after exact release and provider readback; otherwise retain HOLD with the precise missing gate.

## Hard boundaries

- Stable products only.
- No invented demand, customers, sales, reviews, provider readiness, or revenue.
- No product activation from source tests alone.
- No price manufacture outside PentaGreen/ThriveEvergreen authority.
- No license manufacture outside CHLOM.

## Output

Return product ID, stability proof, commercial viability, target buyer, package contents, price mode, license mode, fulfillment, entitlement, activation state, exact missing gates, and PentaGreen handoff receipt.
