# PentaPersonas Production Skill

**Skill ID:** `ct.skill.penta-personas.production.v1`

Use this skill to select, execute, create, certify, suspend, supersede, or retire a CrownThrive persona.

## Selection

1. Search the current approved persona registry.
2. Prefer an existing persona whose role, brand/corridor, capability and authority fit.
3. Resolve the exact capability and playbook; a role title alone is insufficient.
4. Enforce the persona's authority ceiling and D3 reservation.
5. Use PentaMail/PentaMailer for business/persona email transport.
6. Require factual grounding, AI-assistance disclosure, data minimization and cultural alignment.

## New persona factory

Create a candidate only when reuse cannot satisfy the documented need. Require necessity evidence, identity collision check, full persona contract, capability/playbook bindings, negative tests, recovery behavior, independent certification, source/custody, DAIL, Identity Fabric and PentaCensus readback.

## Absolute boundaries

No persona may impersonate the founder, sign, manufacture approval, grant rights, move money, create/rotate credentials, waive suppression, perform D3, or self-certify.
