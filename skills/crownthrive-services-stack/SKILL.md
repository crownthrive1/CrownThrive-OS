# CrownThrive Services Stack Skill

## Purpose

Use this skill to design, inventory, reconcile, test, and document provider-independent shared services across CrownThrive platforms through CHLOM CaaS.

## Canonical modules

Identity, Authentication, Authorization, Billing, Licensing, Analytics, Notifications, CRM, Ticketing, Search, Commerce, Routing, Rewards, and Documentation.

## Required sequence

1. Resolve the stable CSS service ID.
2. Resolve the exact service contract and version.
3. Resolve platform identity and profile.
4. Resolve tenant, actor, purpose, authority, and data class.
5. Resolve provider bindings without treating provider capability as CSS deployment.
6. Verify least-data, evidence freshness, observability, idempotency, rollback, and provider exit.
7. Run CHLOM CaaS and preserve `ALLOW | HOLD | DENY` reason codes.
8. Route bounded work to an owner and a different verifier.
9. Record DAIL, fingerprints, Vault custody, and version/supersession evidence.
10. Keep professional, legal, payment, rights, D3, public activation, and live commerce separately gated.

## Hard boundaries

- D2 maximum automated authority.
- No self-certification.
- No sovereign vote.
- No direct-main merge.
- No raw credential or private-identity export.
- No provider-write inheritance.
- No legal-compliance or professional-certification claim.
- No billing, payment, rights, entitlement, or settlement from provider success alone.
- No silent deletion.

## Truthful states

Use `specified`, `controlled_test`, `read_verified`, `write_verified`, `HOLD`, `DENY`, `verified contract`, `provider binding candidate`, and `platform profile candidate` precisely.

Never call a provider binding, API key, successful canary, or generated profile a complete platform deployment.
