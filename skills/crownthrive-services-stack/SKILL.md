# CrownThrive Services Stack and CHLOM CaaS Skill

## Purpose

Use this skill to inventory, contract, bind, route, evaluate, certify and continuously govern the fourteen CrownThrive shared-service lanes without duplicating provider-specific infrastructure in each platform.

## Service lanes

Identity, Authentication, Authorization, Billing, Licensing, Analytics, Notifications, CRM, Ticketing, Search, Commerce, Routing, Rewards and Documentation.

## Required sequence

1. Resolve the exact lane ID, service contract and current lifecycle.
2. Inspect existing provider bindings and reuse governed adapters before creating new ones.
3. Verify stable identity, credential/capability state, data classes and provider limits.
4. Map the two foundational CHLOM CaaS controls for the lane.
5. Identify exact evidence, unknowns, stale evidence and specialist-review triggers.
6. Evaluate readiness with the protected system-scoring contract; never score people.
7. For a read route, require contract, mapping, least-data and readback evidence.
8. For a write route, additionally require operation-specific authority, idempotency, rollback/compensation, provider rate policy and read-after-write.
9. Bind an owner and a different independent verifier.
10. Record DAIL, service-stack and custody receipts.
11. Promote only through the exact lifecycle gate.
12. Preserve prior contracts, providers, failures and custody through append or supersession.

## Hard rules

- A2/D2 maximum.
- D3 remains human or qualified-professional reserved.
- Readiness does not equal legal or professional compliance certification.
- Provider capability does not prove CrownThrive deployment.
- Provider success does not create CrownThrive billing, licensing, entitlement, reward or settlement truth.
- Raw credentials and private identity mappings never enter public packages or prompts.
- Provider writes are never inherited from a plan or provider API scope.
- The originator cannot certify its own work.
- No direct-main merge.
- No live checkout or entitlement from a service-contract record.
- Nothing is silently deleted.

## Completion language

Use precise states: `inventory`, `contract_candidate`, `adapter_candidate`, `controlled_test`, `read_verified`, `write_canary`, `write_verified`, `shared_service_active`, `hold`, `blocked`, `superseded`, or `retired`.

Never describe a configured provider as a certified shared service without exact evidence.
