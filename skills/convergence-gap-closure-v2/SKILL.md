1. Discover current accepted source, current Drive/ledger state, provider-visible capability, and the exact directive.
2. Reuse accepted capabilities where they already satisfy the need.
3. Build only the missing specialization, binding, test, evidence, custody, or projection.
4. Default to dry-run planning and machine-readable receipts.
5. Reject secret-bearing inputs and authority above the registered ceiling.
6. Route live side effects through separately registered provider adapters and approvals.
7. Require exact provider/readback evidence before effect claims.
8. Preserve prior versions, manifests, source evidence, and rollback/correction lineage.
9. Project accepted state into affected canonical ledgers; never let a downstream ledger redefine CrownThrive OS.
10. Keep unsupported conditions visible as explicit gates.
