## Purpose

## Deterministic sequence

1. Resolve product/offer version, currency, price basis, taxes/fees, cost categories, commissions, fulfillment, support, acquisition, retention, refund/chargeback, and contribution assumptions.
2. Separate observed values, contracted values, deterministic rules, researched benchmarks, and scenarios.
3. Calculate contribution margin, break-even volume, CAC recovery, lifetime-value scenarios, sensitivity bands, and flywheel reinvestment paths.
4. Detect economically impossible, unsupported, or internally conflicting offers.
5. Generate decision-ready scenarios without activating price or commerce.
6. Route viable stable candidates to PentaGreen and gaps to the appropriate rights/provider/entitlement skill.
