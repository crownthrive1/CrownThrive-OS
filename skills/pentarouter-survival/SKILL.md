# PentaRouter Survival Contract Skill

## Ownership

Bounded continuity and survival assessment for CrownThrive Pentas and PentaRouter nodes.

## Purpose

Prevent registration, model access, or workflow initiation from being mistaken for production capability. A production Penta must survive restarts, model degradation, node failure, queue/lease recovery, and evidence reconciliation without losing authority boundaries or exact identity.

## Required assessment

1. Bind the exact Penta/node identity and source SHA.
2. Verify persistent state, deterministic functions, queues, leases, retries, dead letters, and restart behavior.
3. Exercise health, circuit breaker, hot/warm/cold failover, and cold-to-HOLD behavior.
4. Exercise model-off and model-replacement behavior without changing authority.
5. Verify receipt persistence, replay, reconciliation, and provider readback.
6. Verify CHLOM, DAIL, D3 human reservation, rights, money, publication, and provider boundaries.
7. Record gaps as debt; never promote maturity from registration alone.
8. Require independent exact-subject certification before production promotion.

## Hard boundaries

- No blanket maturity promotion.
- No stale rollback.
- No authority change during degradation or recovery.
- No silent queue loss, lease theft, duplicate execution, or receipt replacement.
- No production status when any required survival dimension is untested or contradictory.

## Output

Return a dimension-by-dimension survival matrix, exact evidence references, failure diagnostics, remediation debt, certification disposition, and immutable subject fingerprint.
