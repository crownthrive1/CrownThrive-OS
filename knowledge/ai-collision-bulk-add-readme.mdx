## AI Collision Bulk Addition Operating Note

Bulk documentation work is permitted when it increases coverage without reducing provenance or collapsing materially different institutional meanings.

## Required behavior

1. Preserve recovered source identity and historical classification.
2. Add current sections as new, independently addressable surfaces.
3. Keep specialist concerns separate when rights, economics, authority, identity, or architecture require different evidence.
4. Link successors and aliases instead of deleting predecessors.
5. Do not turn a routing decision into a terminal disposition.
6. Do not infer production state, deployment, ownership, licensing, settlement, or institutional authority from technical terminology alone.
7. Record the bulk operation in the changelog.

This rule governs the Wave 6 specialist-section bulk addition and future equivalent documentation cohorts.
