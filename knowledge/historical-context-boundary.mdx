# Institutional Archive & Historical Context Portal

CrownThrive preserves its history without allowing history to impersonate present authority. This page is the current PentaDocs portal into the institutional archive and the operating boundary used by **PentaHistorian™** and **PentaScribe™**.

<Warning>
  **Historical, latent, recovered, legacy, archived, superseded, retrospective, source-era, and research-only material is context-only. It has zero implementation authority unless a new current record separately governs its reuse.**
</Warning>

## Current operating model

The active institutional lifecycle is the five-layer PENTA model:

1. **Discover**
2. **Govern**
3. **Execute**
4. **Verify**
5. **Preserve**

The former decimal phase timeline — including Phase 2.5, 2.7, 2.8, 2.9, 2.95, 2.97, 2.98, 2.99 and similar transition labels — is retired as current institution-wide roadmap authority. Those labels remain searchable historical aliases and evidence locators.

The archive policy and retirement map live under [`docs/archive/README.md`](../docs/archive/README.md). Historical filenames, Git tags, release identifiers and source-era phase names are preserved rather than rewritten merely to match current terminology.

## PentaHistorian archive service

PentaHistorian continuously inventories registered source families, compares evidence digests, identifies contradictions/supersession, and hands documentation candidates to PentaScribe. Its source registry includes current GitHub repositories, ThriveBase machine truth, PentaDocs and the recovered Help Center corpus.

PentaHistorian may discover and recommend; it cannot manufacture current truth. A current claim still requires current evidence/readback within the exact scope of that claim.

Current machine records:
- `integration_control.penta_historian_sources_v1`
- `integration_control.penta_historian_observations_v1`
- `integration_control.penta_historian_jobs_v1`

## 795-record substantive rebuild

The recovered 795-title Help Center corpus is not being discarded. It remains a full substantive rebuild program under PentaHistorian + PentaScribe.

For every recovered record:

- preserve the original title/hierarchy/source lineage;
- recover the original body where genuine source evidence exists;
- otherwise create a clearly new current artifact from surviving collateral and current truth;
- never represent reconstructed prose as the unrecovered historical original;
- record successor/current route, archive state and evidence;
- verify current provider/runtime claims before presenting them as current;
- preserve restricted source bodies outside public documentation.

The machine job `help-center-795-substantive-rebuild` stays open until all 795 records reach substantive or explicit evidence-backed terminal coverage.

## The rule

A historical or latent source may be used to understand lineage, terminology, prior assumptions, abandoned designs, unresolved ideas, architectural intent, cultural context, or why a current system exists.

It may **not** by itself authorize, trigger, or justify:

- production implementation or deployment;
- provider writes or configuration changes;
- money movement, billing, settlement, or payouts;
- token minting, transfer, issuance, staking, bridging, or launch;
- governance votes, appointments, removals, or delegated authority;
- license or rights grants, revocations, assignments, or transfers;
- public claims that a capability is currently live;
- security-policy changes or credential handling;
- current legal conclusions; or
- promotion of an archived design into a current contract, registry, workflow, or runtime.

## Context can inform; context cannot command

Historical material can surface a **latent candidate**. A latent candidate is an idea worth preserving or reconsidering. It remains non-executable until a new current-state record is created and approved through the authority, evidence, legal, security, and production gates that apply to that subject.

```text
historical / latent source
        ↓
PentaHistorian discovery + lineage
        ↓
current-state comparison
        ↓
PentaScribe current-record candidate
        ↓
required evidence + governance + tests
        ↓
PentaCertify verification/readback
        ↓
only then: eligible for implementation
```

There is no automatic promotion path from archive to runtime.

## Confidentiality survives archival classification

Calling a document historical does not make a confidential document public. NDAs, agreements, investor materials, private founder packages, private financial models, personal records, restricted technical material, and other protected sources remain subject to their applicable access and retention controls.

Likewise, classifying a contract or legal instrument as a historical source record does **not** amend, terminate, waive, or reinterpret its legal effect. Contractual effect must be determined from the governing agreement, later amendments, authoritative company records, and legal review where required.

## Historical source classes

| Class | What it is | Public treatment | Runtime authority |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `HISTORICAL_CONTEXT` | Prior doctrine, architecture, policy, roadmap, or documentation | Public-safe summary may be published | None |
| `LATENT_CONTEXT` | Unimplemented or dormant concept worth preserving | Describe concept and lineage only | None |
| `RESTRICTED_HISTORICAL` | Confidential legal, commercial, founder, financial, security, or partner material | Metadata/public-safe summary only | None |
| `SUPERSEDED` | Explicitly replaced current-era material | Preserve correction/successor links | None |
| `RETIRED_TIMELINE_ALIAS` | Former institutional timeline/phase label | Searchable historical mapping | None |
| `PRIVATE_CONTEXT` | Personal or non-institutional source retained for context | Do not publish body | None |
| `UNKNOWN_HOLD` | Source whose status cannot yet be resolved | Hold; do not promote | None |

## Current-source requirement

Any future reuse of a historical idea requires a **new current source**. At minimum that record must identify the historical source, specific reused idea, current owner, current legal/security/commercial implications, current evidence/provider state, implementation and rollback requirements, required approval class, and an explicit statement that the new record—not the archive—is current authority.

## Why this exists

CrownThrive has a large historical corpus whose value is institutional memory. Preserving it prevents repeated mistakes, lost inventions, naming drift, undocumented assumptions, and false origin stories. The same corpus becomes dangerous if an old roadmap, token design, prospectus, founder handbook, launch guide, or architecture paper is treated as proof of what is live today.

**PentaDocs preserves the history. PentaHistorian continuously reconciles it. PentaScribe maintains the current record. Current verified evidence determines what CrownThrive may say and do now.**
