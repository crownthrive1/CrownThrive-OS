## Rebuild rule

The recovered structure is **evidence that the knowledge corridor existed**, not automatic proof that every described feature is live today.

Each article must be reconciled against:

1. current governing registry
2. current production evidence
3. current public state
4. historical source body
5. replacement or correction decision

## Target Help Center behavior

A mature CrownThrive Help Center should let a visitor enter through the need they have rather than requiring them to understand the entire ecosystem.

Recommended public pathways include:

- Start here
- Accounts and access
- Products and purchases
- Licensing and rights
- Creators and partners
- Publishing
- Music and media
- Business services
- Community and education
- Billing and refunds
- Privacy and security
- Troubleshooting
- Platform-specific help
- Governance and public documentation

The deeper institutional map should remain searchable underneath those simpler pathways.
