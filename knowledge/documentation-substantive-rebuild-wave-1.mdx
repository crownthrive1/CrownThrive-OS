# Documentation Substantive Rebuild — Wave 1

Candidate mapping is no longer the bottleneck. CrownThrive has a deterministic candidate identity, priority, disposition and continuity destination for all **795 recovered Help Center titles**. The next layer is stricter: determine whether the current successor article is substantive enough to carry the recovered institutional meaning, while refusing to turn missing historical text or high-risk legacy claims into current authority.

Wave 1 applies that standard to a deliberately narrow P0 cohort under the [Documentation Reconciliation & Continuity Framework](/standards/documentation-reconciliation-continuity-framework), [Articleization Contract](/support/articleization-manifest-and-autopublication-contract), [Documentation Gap & Article Rebuild Register](/knowledge/documentation-gap-article-rebuild-register) and [Source Authority Hierarchy](/knowledge/source-authority-hierarchy).

## Pass A — substantive-successor qualification

The completed 795-row candidate map contains **449 P0 candidates**. Wave 1 admits a row only when all of the following are true:

- the candidate priority is P0;
- the candidate disposition is `merged_successor`;
- every candidate continuity route exists;
- the row does not carry a D3/high-risk legal, investment, securities, patent, franchise/license, token/exchange/settlement, restricted or explicitly flagged condition;
- at least one governed canonical anchor route is present;
- that anchor contains at least 1,200 characters of substantive current content; and
- that anchor already carries at least two meaningful internal continuity links.

The deterministic result is:

```yaml
source_universe: 795
p0_candidates: 449
wave_1_machine_qualified: 16
p0_outside_wave_1: 433
terminal_disposition_accepted: false
parent_certification_required: true
wave_sha256: 518a37a3ac0485987c281761012b9411d3f1fbae155bac73ef7a3929fbda15f6
```

The sixteen rows come from three recovered sections:

| Recovered section | Wave 1 rows |
| --- | ---: |
| CrownThrive Legal Depot | 10 |
| Convergent Ecosystem | 5 |
| Hybrid Incubator | 1 |
| **Total** | **16** |

## Pass B — continuity and institutional binding

The selected rows resolve through four existing substantive control articles rather than creating duplicate pages:

| [Security & Privacy Continuity](/technology/security-privacy-continuity) | 9 | current security/private-data/recovery boundaries are substantive, versioned and linked to related controls |
| [Phase 3 Readiness Gate](/technology/phase-3-readiness-gate) | 4 | current entry criteria and blocked-state semantics are explicit and machine-addressable |
| [Ecosystem Status & Resilience](/technology/ecosystem-status-resilience) | 2 | current status/resilience semantics separate observed evidence from unsupported production claims |
| [Documentation Source of Truth & Autonomous Governance](/standards/documentation-source-of-truth-and-autonomous-governance) | 1 | current source, correction, governance and documentation-impact rules are substantive and governed |

This is a substantive-rebuild result because the current successor body is already present and machine-qualified. It is **not** a claim that the historical Help Center article body survived.

## Acceptance boundary

Wave 1 advances the sixteen rows to:

```text
substantive_current_successor_machine_verified_parent_review
```

It does **not** advance them to final terminal acceptance. Parent certification remains required, and D3 authority remains human-reserved. The current framework package remains `controlled_test`; no operational or public activation is created by this documentation classification.

<Warning>
  Historical body recovery and current successor sufficiency are different facts. A current article can substantively replace a lost legacy article without pretending to be the verbatim historical original.
</Warning>

## What Wave 1 intentionally holds out

The machine lane does not auto-adjudicate titles involving investment or securities claims, valuations, prospectuses, patent or provisional-patent status, franchise or regional-license opportunities, confidential/internal operations, token or exchange concepts, settlement/economic authority, or other explicit high-risk flags. Those records remain in governed review lanes rather than being normalized through prose.

The remaining **433 P0 candidates outside Wave 1** are not all necessarily deficient. They are simply outside this exact bounded acceptance policy. The next substantive waves will partition them by evidence and review class instead of weakening the standard to make the count move faster.
