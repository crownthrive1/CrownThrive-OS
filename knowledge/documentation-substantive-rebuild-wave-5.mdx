## AI-family collision finding

Pass A also inspected the remaining `ai_agent_algorithm_reconciliation` P0 family. There are **35** candidates, but the family contains classification collisions: authority/governance records, rights/licensing records, identity records, economic/LEX records, historical-research records, and generic uses of the word `model` that were captured by the historical classifier.

Sprint 11 therefore does **not** broadly qualify that family and does **not** rewrite the historical Sprint 4 crosswalk or its deterministic receipt. Those records need a dedicated reclassification/source-family review before they can enter a substantive machine lane.

## What this does not claim

Wave 5 does not claim that the historical BioLink article body was recovered. The new page is a current evidence-bounded successor. Machine qualification means the current successor is substantive and internally connected enough for parent review; it does not mean the legacy terminal disposition has been accepted.

No provider write, DNS mutation, credential activation, identity proof, rights determination, legal-policy activation, payment, settlement, economic action, interface runtime activation, production deployment, or Phase 3 service was created by this documentation sprint.

The candidate-mapping layer remains complete at **795 / 795**. Historical-body reconstruction, terminal-disposition acceptance, and the remaining P0/P1 substantive rebuild continue as separate work.
