## License and support interpretation

The `license` and `support` fields above are source assertions from the S100 management plan. They require separate current verification.

A record such as:

```yaml
license: Regular
support: Expired
```

means only that the source recorded a Regular license and an expired support/renewal state. It does not prove the license is currently valid, invalid, installed, transferable, secure, supported by the vendor, or used in production.

Likewise:

- `AppSumo redeemed` is source evidence of a redeemed deal, not current account/API entitlement;
- `Owned; details to reconcile` explicitly requires entitlement/source validation;
- `Planned acquisition` is not an owned license;
- `Listing removed` and `Service failed` are provider-continuity signals;
- a future-dated support/renewal field is not a guarantee the account or deployed version remains healthy.

## Provider capability versus CrownThrive deployment

Current provider research from Phase 2.98 may supersede or refine S100 capability assumptions without rewriting the source row. For example, official provider APIs/versions may now be known while the CrownThrive installed version/account entitlement remains unverified.

The correct model is:

```text
source management row
+ provider current capability
+ CrownThrive account entitlement
+ installed version/configuration
+ repository/deployment evidence
+ API/webhook/export tests
= current effective integration state
```

No earlier field can substitute for the complete chain.
