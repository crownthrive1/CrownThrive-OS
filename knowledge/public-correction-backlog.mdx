# Public Correction Backlog

This register does not authorize automatic website edits. It identifies public surfaces that should be reconciled against the approved claim register and current operating evidence.

## P0 corrections

### CrownThrive platform counts

Public surfaces currently contain 50\+, 60\+ and 80\+ ecosystem counts across different publication eras. Replace undated generic counts with either a dated approved metric or wording such as "a growing portfolio of platforms, brands, frameworks and operating assets" until the counting policy is automated.

### One Seat, Multiple Industries

Some FAQ language implies the shared identity experience is already universal. Current canonical wording should state that it is the integration architecture CrownThrive is building toward, with platform-specific integrations available today.

### CHLOM production status

Public pages that describe decentralized licensing, CHLOM LEX, tokenized royalties, DID, treasury, ZK or autonomous governance must distinguish current governance/licensing design from future or research architecture.

### MM Suites franchise wording

Pages describing MM Suites as a franchise blueprint must not be read as proof of an active legally implemented franchise program. Approved wording should distinguish blueprint/model, pilot/validation, and any future legally reviewed franchise or regional-license structure.

### Virality Music legacy portfolio copy

Older CrownThrive portfolio copy frames Virality Music primarily as an artist-distribution, NFT, collaboration and AI-tool platform. Current canonical positioning should reflect the active CrownThrive-owned catalog, label/licensing library, cinematic/media-IP, direct-commerce and governed-rights direction while preserving the older concept in history.

### Sustainability claims

Older recurring claims involving carbon removal, trees, kelp and ocean-plastic cleanup should be reviewed against current vendor/program evidence before reuse. Public language should identify the specific program, eligible activity, measurement period and evidence source.

## P1 corrections

- shared-account / shared-profile claims that exceed verified integration;
- ThriveApps / CrownApps naming;
- CrownThrive Studios / ThriveStudio / AdLuxe Studio role clarity;
- CrownInsights / CrownLytics / CrownPulse role clarity;
- CII / ThriveFund legal-status wording;
- ThriveAlumni governance wording;

- historic token/DAO/investment language;
- archived or successor platform pages that still appear current.

## Correction workflow

1. identify claim ID;
2. capture current public URL and wording;
3. identify authoritative source/evidence;
4. classify as temporal, stale, unsupported, ambiguous or false;
5. obtain required approval;
6. patch the public surface;
7. preserve prior wording in history;
8. verify redirects/SEO where naming changed;
9. record correction event and effective date.

<Note>
  The existence of a backlog item is not itself a determination that the older statement was false when originally published. Many conflicts are normal consequences of a fast-evolving portfolio.
</Note>
