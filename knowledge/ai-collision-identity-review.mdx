## Identity boundary

Review must distinguish canonical platform identity, service identity, agent identity, provider identity, legacy alias, successor relationship, and cryptographic or DID-backed identity where applicable. UUIDs, hashes, fingerprints, SHAs, heartbeats, provider IDs, and deployment names are evidence inputs; none should silently become the canonical identity without reconciliation.

The lane should be read with the [Platform Registry](/ecosystem/platform-registry), [Legacy Alias & Successor Register](/knowledge/legacy-alias-successor-register), [Source Authority Hierarchy](/knowledge/source-authority-hierarchy), and applicable CHLOM identity doctrine.

## Preservation rule

Aliases and historical identifiers remain visible after reconciliation. Where a successor is certified, the predecessor is linked rather than erased. Opaque identifiers that affect compatibility remain stable or receive an explicit migration/alias path.
