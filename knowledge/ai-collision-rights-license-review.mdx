## AI Collision Review — Rights & License

This section holds the **14 records** routed from the historical AI/agent/algorithm family because their primary unresolved concern is rights or licensing rather than generic technical capability.

Review must distinguish ownership, license scope, sublicense authority, territorial or channel restrictions, derivative rights, provider terms, creator or contributor rights, and any CHLOM-governed permission boundary. A working model, automation, API, algorithm, or agent does not establish the right to deploy, distribute, commercialize, train on, transform, or sublicense the underlying asset.

## Required evidence

A record may leave this lane only when the governing source chain establishes the relevant right and the current institutional mapping. Evidence should reconcile the [IP Ownership and Control](/governance/ip-ownership-and-control), [Rights and AI Provenance](/governance/rights-and-ai-provenance), [IP Protection, Chain of Title and Trade Secret](/standards/ip-protection-chain-of-title-and-trade-secret), and [Source Authority Hierarchy](/knowledge/source-authority-hierarchy) requirements.

## Preservation rule

Historical labels remain historical evidence. Current rights conclusions are appended as separately attributable determinations. No title similarity, provider capability, or recovered framework text may silently replace a rights record or manufacture a license.
