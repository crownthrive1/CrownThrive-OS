## Doctrine that remains institutionally strong

Subject to the source-authority hierarchy, these are durable doctrine candidates and should be projected throughout the operating system:

- documentation is institutional and generational insurance;
- no leader should make the institution dependent on undocumented personal memory;
- preserve knowledge so successors do not inherit confusion;
- leadership is stewardship, not possession of a title;
- integrity and accountability outrank short-term convenience;
- community members are people, not commodities;
- decisions should be judged by how they affect the whole convergent ecosystem;
- every material system needs an owner, evidence, controls and a successor path;
- platform/vendor infrastructure is replaceable; CrownThrive-controlled identity, rights, doctrine, canon and evidence are not;
- culture and narrative require governance as seriously as finance, security and operations;
- innovation should move through development, governance and integration rather than becoming unmanaged sprawl;
- the ecosystem should operate through repeatable cadences and measurable feedback loops;
- succession planning begins before the successor is needed;
- **knowledge is never silently destroyed**: corrections and supersessions preserve the prior state and explain why it changed.

## Chat, notebook and founder-memory integration

Founder conversations, notebook material and personal recollection are part of the recovery program, but evidence classes remain explicit:

```text
founder decision in accessible chat -> founder-controlled policy/intent evidence within scope
founder recollection -> preserved recollection until corroborated where stronger proof is required
brainstorm / idea -> proposed or research state until adopted
physical notebook -> source artifact after digitization/indexing/source registration
founder memory alone -> discovery aid, never the only certified recovery copy
```

Material chat decisions must be reconciled into durable institutional records rather than remaining chat-only.
