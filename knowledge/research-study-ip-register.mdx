# Research, Study and IP Register

Phase 2.7 establishes research and study assets as governed institutional property rather than loose files.

## Status classes

Every research asset should be classified as one of:

- current operational reference
- adopted framework
- historical strategy
- research / target architecture
- productizable study
- curriculum asset
- dormant / unused IP
- restricted / confidential
- superseded but preserved
- needs owner validation

<Note>
  A paper, prospectus, model, playbook, or patent draft proves that a source exists. It does not by itself prove that the described system is deployed, legally adopted, commercially available, patented, or financially validated.
</Note>
