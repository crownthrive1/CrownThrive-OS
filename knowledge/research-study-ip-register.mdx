# Research, Study and IP Register

Phase 2.7 establishes research and study assets as governed institutional property rather than loose files.

## Status classes

Every research asset should be classified as one of:

- current operational reference
- adopted framework
- historical strategy
- research / target architecture
- productizable study
- curriculum asset
- dormant / unused IP
- restricted / confidential
- superseded but preserved
- needs owner validation

<Note>
  A paper, prospectus, model, playbook, or patent draft proves that a source exists. It does not by itself prove that the described system is deployed, legally adopted, commercially available, patented, or financially validated.
</Note>

## Enterprise and strategy family

The institutional corpus includes source families such as the CrownThrive Foundational Volume, market and strategic positioning analysis, master operating plans, master business plans, multi-year financial models, the Core Operational Triad, Holdings planning, founder execution benchmarks, infrastructure studies, stakeholder compensation materials, and ambassador earnings playbooks.

## CHLOM technical family

Preserve the CHLOM Institutional Master Compendium, Master Technical Compendium, AI-driven decentralized compliance/licensing whitepaper, implementation briefs, identity/rights/licensing/governance architecture, LEX/DLA/TLaaS materials, historic colored-paper families, protocol/pallet research, privacy/ZKP/DID studies, treasury/tax/capital/yield/oracle studies, and related technical artifacts.

These remain **research or target architecture** unless implementation evidence moves them into current-core status.

## Virality Music research family

Register proprietary or internally developed concepts including Catalog-as-Capital™, catalog valuation/treasury concepts, RSDL, STEROIP, OGN, story-world production bibles, rights/provenance research, participatory-rights architecture, and release/distribution operating studies.

## KJV Visualized / Sermon Toolkit study family

Confirmed study/product families include Abrahamic Covenant, Tabernacle, Servant Leadership, watch-and-study sheets, family watch kits, visual studies, devotionals, curriculum, sermon tools, ministry-room resources, and related printables.

Each study should eventually carry:

`content_id`, `title`, `family`, `edition`, `source`, `audience`, `rights_class`, `status`, `commerce_state`, `license_state`, `master_location`, `related_products`, `related_rooms`, `related_media`, and `revision_history`.

## Cross-corridor operating bibles

Production bibles such as Testimony Aisle Sessions should be registered both as creative IP and as reusable operating patterns when they encode governance, ethics, rights, commerce, media, sponsorship, release QA, and source-of-truth rules.

## Phase 3 implication

Agents must search this register before generating a new framework, study, product, curriculum asset, or technical design. The objective is reuse, modernization, and lineage—not endless duplication.
