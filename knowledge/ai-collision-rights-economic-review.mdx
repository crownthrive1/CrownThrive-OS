## AI Collision Review — Rights + Economic

This section holds the **6 records** whose unresolved state spans both rights/licensing and economic activation. These records require a two-axis review because a commercial mechanism cannot cure a missing right, and a valid right does not by itself establish pricing, entitlement, settlement, payout, or revenue authority.

## Dual gate

The rights side must establish ownership or licensed authority. The economic side must separately establish the permitted commercial state, including SKU or offering identity, entitlement, payment, payout, settlement, or revenue treatment where applicable.

Provider delivery, payment success, storefront state, API success, or model output is evidence of provider behavior, not automatic CrownThrive economic truth. Review therefore aligns with [Rights and AI Provenance](/governance/rights-and-ai-provenance), [Evidence, Claims and Proof](/standards/evidence-claims-and-proof-standard), and the CrownThrive commerce/economic governance layer.

## Preservation rule

Both dimensions remain visible until independently resolved. A terminal economic decision may not silently overwrite a rights hold, and a rights clearance may not silently activate commerce.
