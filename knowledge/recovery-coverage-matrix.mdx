## What remains after the structural pass

The knowledge base still requires article-level recovery and operational verification for hundreds of platform-specific guides, FAQs, policies, developer documents and procedures. Phase 2.99 must disposition every recovered title and provide substantive versioned bodies or explicit unresolved-source records for P0/P1 customer, operator, developer, legal, rights/licensing, commerce and support categories. Lower-risk long-tail bodies may continue in a governed parallel program only when their absence cannot alter authority, rights, money, privacy, security, safety, contractual meaning, source recovery or irreversible automation.

## Definition of sufficient pre–Phase 3 knowledge

Controlled Phase 3.0 engineering can begin only after the Phase 2.7–2.99 gap/conflict/extraction/seed gates are accepted or explicitly deferred and the institution has authoritative machine definitions for identity, assets, rights, rules, roles, approvals, events, products, revenue, evidence, remedies, security classes, source authority, platform lifecycle, corridor relationships, documentation impact and human escalation. The 795-title estate must have a complete disposition manifest, and P0/P1 material articles must exist or carry explicit unresolved-source states. Long-tail customer-help articles may continue in parallel only when their absence cannot alter permissions, rights, money movement, legal claims, privacy, security, safety, source recovery or irreversible automation.
