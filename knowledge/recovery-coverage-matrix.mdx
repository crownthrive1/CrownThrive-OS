## What remains after the structural pass

The knowledge base still requires article-level recovery and operational verification for hundreds of platform-specific guides, FAQs, policies, developer documents and procedures. Those should **not** block Phase 3's first technical sprint if the underlying institutional contracts are complete; instead, they should run as a parallel governed recovery program.

## Definition of sufficient pre–Phase 3 knowledge

Phase 3 can begin only after the Phase 2.7, 2.8 and 2.9 gap/conflict ledgers are either closed or explicitly deferred and the institution has authoritative definitions for identity, assets, rights, rules, roles, approvals, events, products, revenue, evidence, remedies, security classes, source authority, platform lifecycle, corridor relationships and human escalation. Long-tail customer-help articles may continue in parallel only when their absence cannot alter permissions, rights, money movement, legal claims, safety, or irreversible automation.
