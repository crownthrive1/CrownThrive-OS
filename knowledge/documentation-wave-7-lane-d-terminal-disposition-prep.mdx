# Wave 7 Lane D — Terminal Disposition Preparation

Lane D prepares terminal-disposition packets for recovered Help Center identities after source, successor, lineage, and specialist concerns are sufficiently resolved. It does **not** self-accept a terminal outcome.

The only recognized terminal dispositions remain:

1. `canonical_article`
2. `merged_successor`
3. `permanent_redirect`
4. `restricted_record`
5. `superseded_history`
6. `unresolved_source`

## Required packet

```yaml
inventory_id:
legacy_title:
proposed_disposition:
source_refs: []
current_anchor: null
lineage_refs: []
specialist_clearances: []
conflicts_remaining: []
redirect_target: null
restriction_basis: null
quality_checks: []
terminal_acceptance: false
parent_review_required: true
```

A packet must explain why the proposed disposition is more accurate than the five alternatives. `merged_successor` requires a defensible successor relationship; `permanent_redirect` requires a stable target; `restricted_record` requires a documented restriction basis; `superseded_history` must preserve lineage; and `unresolved_source` must not be used merely to clear the queue.

## Separation of preparation and acceptance

Preparation is machine- and specialist-assisted. Acceptance remains governed. This separation prevents throughput pressure from converting recommendations into irreversible institutional truth.

Lane D cross-checks [Source Authority Hierarchy](/knowledge/source-authority-hierarchy), [Legacy Alias & Successor Register](/knowledge/legacy-alias-successor-register), [Versioning, Change Control & Correction](/standards/versioning-change-control-and-correction), and [Documentation Reconciliation & Continuity Framework](/standards/documentation-reconciliation-continuity-framework).

## Exit behavior

Accepted dispositions must be recorded through the appropriate parent/governance process and reflected in the documentation census, rebuild register, redirects or restricted-record index as applicable. Until that occurs, the record remains non-terminal.
