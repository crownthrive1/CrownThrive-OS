## Canonical Adjudication Queue

Phase 2.8 produced a decision queue. On **August 17, 2026**, the Founder resolved a substantial portion of it. Those decisions are now canonical institutional instructions unless later superseded through an ADR.

## Founder-resolved decisions

### CT-ADR-FND-2026-0817-01 — Locticians metric

**Canonical current community figure: 125,000\+**.

Public pages that still show 75,000\+ or 25,000\+ are older snapshots. Until CrownLytics/production telemetry provides an automated canonical figure, the Founder-authorized 125,000\+ figure is the current institutional value.

Counting rule: when two records describe the **same metric definition** but are different snapshots and the Founder has confirmed the larger value is the later/current value, use the larger confirmed value and preserve the lower value as historical. Do not use this rule to merge different metric classes such as members, records, listings, visitors, or accounts.

### CT-ADR-FND-2026-0817-02 — CrownThrive Music / Virality Music

Treat **CrownThrive Music** and **Virality Music** as related but not automatically interchangeable registry strings. Virality Music by CrownThrive is the current active music/catalog/label/licensing/media-IP operating brand. CrownThrive Music remains a lineage/umbrella music record until its exact successor or umbrella relationship is formally versioned.

### CT-ADR-FND-2026-0817-03 — Studios

- **ThriveStudio** is a legacy/alias lineage for **CrownThrive Studios**.
- **CrownThrive Studios** is the CrownThrive-wide production/media studio.
- **AdLuxe Studio** is separate and belongs specifically to **AdLuxe Network**.

Do not merge AdLuxe Studio into CrownThrive Studios.

### CT-ADR-FND-2026-0817-04 — Apps

**CrownApps** and **ThriveApps** are currently interchangeable/alias names pending a final brand decision. The registry must keep one stable asset ID with both aliases until a later ADR settles the public canonical name.

### CT-ADR-FND-2026-0817-05 — Insights and analytics

- **CrownInsights** is its own platform.
- **CrownLytics** is its own platform.
- **CrownPulse** is its own platform.

They may exchange data or signals but must not be collapsed into a single product record.

### CT-ADR-FND-2026-0817-06 — Cafe lineage

- **ThriveCafe** is a retired standalone platform/asset. Preserve its history.
- **Melanin Magic Cafe** is an active/recoverable **Melanin Magic coffee brand and licensable imprint**, designed around local/small-batch production rather than a mass-market centralized cafe chain.
- Melanin Magic Cafe IP should be recovered from XENthrive-era materials and archived conversations when source access permits.

### CT-ADR-FND-2026-0817-07 — Get Kenetic!

Canonical spelling is **Get Kenetic!™**. Historical `Kinetic` references should be treated as misspelling/alias records unless a source specifically represents a separate asset.

### CT-ADR-FND-2026-0817-08 — Shared login / SSO

Current state: several CrownThrive properties rely on external applications and heterogeneous authentication. A broader CrownThrive SSO / CrownThrive ID architecture is **in scope and being built**, but the final identity architecture is not yet fully defined or universally deployed.

Phase 3 must model current authentication coverage per platform and separately version the target SSO architecture.

### CT-ADR-FND-2026-0817-09 — CII / ThriveFund

**CII is not to be represented as a nonprofit organization by default.**

**ThriveFund is an internal CrownThrive-aligned impact/reinvestment mechanism connected to MM Suites and funded through designated corporate/economic allocations, with ThriveAlumni providing an internal continuity/community governance relationship.**

This describes the intended institutional model, not a claim that a legally distinct regulated fund, charity, foundation, or nonprofit entity currently exists. Legal/accounting implementation remains counsel- and structure-gated.

### CT-ADR-FND-2026-0817-10 — CHLOM recovery

CHLOM LEX, DID, ZK, treasury, token, oracle and related decentralized components are **recovery-and-build scope**, not abandoned concepts. Phase 3 and subsequent engineering must modernize them against current technical capability while preserving foundational CHLOM IP, lineage, source evidence, and current governance requirements.

No component may be represented as deployed merely because historical architecture exists.

## Partially resolved / implementation-gated

### Holdings legal formation

Founder direction: reconcile the **new Holdings architecture with the older CrownThrive entity and governance plans** and prepare the structure for eventual implementation.

Current legal fact remains: CrownThrive, LLC is the operating entity unless formation evidence establishes an additional entity. Phase 3 must support the future HoldCo without pretending it already exists.

### MM Suites franchise / regional-license implementation

Founder direction confirms the franchise/regional-license model remains intended and must be reconciled against the full MM Suites source family and current ecosystem implementation, including Virality Music's current MM Suites/Suite Pro routing. The source family includes multiple Master Prospectus, Master Plan, Full Business Plan, War Room, pitch-deck and strategic-growth documents.

Legal implementation, franchise disclosure/registration requirements, territory rights and investment/equity claims remain counsel-gated. Product and systems architecture may be built before those legal structures are activated.

### Patent / provisional strategy

Founder direction: CrownThrive has not pursued the relevant patent/provisional protection to completion and cost/timing are constraints. Historical drafts and patent-strategy materials remain owned research/IP and should be preserved, inventor/ownership records should be retained, and current counsel should determine whether any filing route remains useful.

The institution must not claim an issued or pending patent unless filing evidence exists.

## Required output for each future adjudication

Every decision must produce an ADR or registry event containing decision ID, conflict IDs, canonical statement, effective date, owner/approver, supporting evidence, affected aliases/pages/domains, migration action, historical treatment, and review date.
