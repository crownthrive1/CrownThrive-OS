# Canonical Adjudication Queue

Not every contradiction should be decided by documentation editors or agents. Some require founder authority, legal review, accounting evidence, implementation inspection, or a product decision.

## Decision classes

### Founder / portfolio authority

- CrownThrive Music relationship to Virality Music.
- ThriveStudio relationship to CrownThrive Studios and AdLuxe Studio.
- CrownApps versus ThriveApps canonical naming.
- ThriveCafe versus Melanin Magic Cafe.
- Kinetic versus Kenetic spelling and lineage.
- CrownInsights functional boundary from CrownLytics and CrownPulse.
- whether Hospitality / Travel / Experiences remains a standalone corridor.
- current ThriveAlumni governance/continuity scope.

### Legal / counsel

- whether any MM Suites franchise, regional license, transferability, territory, fee, or override construct is currently legally implemented.
- Holdings entity formation and intercompany structure.
- CII / ThriveFund legal form and any fund-like language.
- any equity, membership-interest, securities, token, DAO, inheritance, or investment-return claim.
- patent/provisional status versus draft strategy.

### Technical / production evidence

- which platforms currently share authentication, profiles, entitlements, events or data.
- current production role of ThriveApps / CrownApps.
- current deployment status of CHLOM LEX, DAIL, DID, ZK, treasury, oracle and token components.
- exact current role of CrownThrive IO versus individual integration adapters.
- current Stripe, webhook and entitlement implementations by platform.

### Metrics / evidence

- Locticians member, verified-record and active-account totals.
- CrownThrive ecosystem member counts.
- platform/brand/framework totals under a defined counting policy.
- live-versus-registered domain counts.
- current Virality Music catalog/release/product metrics.
- any sustainability activity totals or recurring environmental claims.

## Required output for each adjudication

Every decision should produce an ADR or registry event containing:

- decision ID;
- conflict IDs resolved;
- canonical statement;
- effective date;
- owner/approver;
- supporting evidence;
- affected aliases;
- affected pages/domains;
- migration or correction action;
- whether historical wording remains discoverable;
- next review date.

## Phase 3 rule

Unresolved decisions may enter Phase 3 only if their registry state is explicitly `unresolved`, `historical`, `research`, `proposed`, or `restricted` and the affected service refuses to infer a stronger state.

Examples:

- an unresolved franchise structure must not generate franchise agreements;
- an unverified patent record must not produce a public patent claim;
- an unresolved alias must not merge customer or rights records;
- an unverified member count must not be used in investor, press, or advertising outputs.
