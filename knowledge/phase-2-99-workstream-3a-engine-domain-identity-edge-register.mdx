# Phase 2.99 Workstream 3A — Engine/Domain Identity Edge Register

## Purpose

This packet creates the first deterministic **identity → source-engine → source-domain** relationship layer across the recovered S103/S100 registries. It begins with the CrownApps/ThriveApps alias family and the high-confidence media identities normalized in stable-ID tranche 2.

The edges are **source relationships only**. They do not certify provider accounts, current license entitlement, installed versions, repositories, deployments, APIs, exports, DNS, TLS, HTTP health, public availability, rights, or commercial activation.

## Edge rules

- stable IDs come from the accepted S100/S103 identity work; this packet does not invent new identities;
- S100 engine and domain row IDs remain immutable dated source records;
- one engine may serve multiple platform identities and one platform may have multiple engines/domains;
- shared engine assignment never collapses the platform identities it serves;
- an S100 source `Active`, `Live`, `Rebuild`, `Current`, support date, license label, or domain status does not prove current CrownThrive deployment or entitlement;
- absence of an S100 engine/domain row is recorded as absence from that historical source table, not proof that no current implementation/domain exists;
- `MVP (Roku)` remains fail-closed and receives no effective engine/domain edge until its lineage is adjudicated.
