## Phase 2.99 Workstream 3A — Engine/Domain Identity Edge Register

## Purpose

This register is the deterministic **stable identity → S103 source → S100 portfolio → S100 engine/service → S100 domain** relationship layer for Workstream 3A. Tranche 1 established the pattern across CrownApps and the media federation. Tranche 2 expands it across current operating-spine, support, beauty/wellness, analytics, messaging and growth/distribution identities.

The edges are **dated source relationships only**. They do not certify provider accounts, current license entitlement, installed versions, repositories, deployments, APIs, exports, registrar custody, DNS, TLS, HTTP health, public availability, rights, legal authority, commerce activation or revenue state.

## Scope and non-goals

This register preserves the accepted Tranche 2 baseline while allowing later governed adjudications to append current effective relationships. The original tranche did not create new platform identities or adjudicate ThriveTools child-capability identity. The August 21, 2026 founder adjudication now resolves ThriveTools SEO and OPT as child capabilities using `S107`–`S109`; this post-tranche update still does not resolve the remaining S103 unresolved identities, promote the abstract ThriveSupport family into an implementation, infer SuitePro/Brilliant Directories from provider notes into the 85-engine count, or certify unrelated live provider/domain state.

## Evidence baseline and assumptions

The controlling evidence for this register is the accepted S103 74-row recovery/crosswalk, S100 68-row portfolio identity seed, S100 85-row engine/service seed, S100 82-row domain seed, current platform registries, S101/S102 Collab Portal evidence, and the accepted Workstream 3A identity/relationship rules. Source-row presence is historical evidence; current operational truth requires an independent certification chain.

## Edge rules

- stable IDs come from accepted identity work; this register does not invent new identities;
- S100/S103 row IDs remain immutable dated source records;
- one engine may serve multiple platform identities and one platform may have multiple engines/domains;
- shared engine assignment never collapses the platform identities it serves;
- a family/umbrella identity does not automatically inherit implementation-provider edges from one projection;
- a parent platform never inherits child API/version/provider/deployment/economic state merely because a `capability_of` relationship exists; unresolved child relationships remain fail-closed, while resolved child relationships remain independently versioned;
- an S100 source `Active`, `Live`, `Rebuild`, `Current`, support date, license label or domain status does not prove current CrownThrive deployment or entitlement;
- absence of an S100 engine/domain row means only that the dated S100 tables do not contain an exact row for that identity;
- provider references in source notes, such as SuitePro or Brilliant Directories, are not forced into the 85-engine count;
- `MVP (Roku)` remains fail-closed and receives no effective engine/domain edge until its lineage is adjudicated.

## Completion and hard exit for this tranche

This tranche completes only when its manifest, documentation and validator agree on the exact effective and blocked relationships and repository governance CI passes. It does **not** close Workstream 3A or Phase 2.99. Workstream rotation remains prohibited while broader provider/domain/reference verification remains incomplete.

## Validation and rollback

Validation is deterministic and network-free. If any relationship proves wrong, the correction must preserve the source row, append a versioned correction/adjudication, update the machine manifest and validator together, and re-run CI. Unknown current provider/domain state must revert to explicit `unverified` rather than being filled from naming similarity.

## Authority and approval

This tranche is D1 source/identity reconciliation using already accepted evidence. It does not authorize D2/D3 rights, legal, privacy, financial, provider-account, secret, production-mutation or commercial decisions. No authoring agent self-approves those states.
