## Pass A — stale state and gap diagnosis

Sprint 8 correctly left the identity/trust and evidence/audit families open because its then-current anchors did not satisfy every exact admission condition. Sprint 9 preserves that historical result. It does not rewrite the Sprint 8 anchors or retroactively pretend those rows passed earlier.

Instead, Pass A identifies the target-side gap: the recovered identities already point into [CrownThrive ID](/technology/crownthrive-id), [CHLOM Registry Model](/chlom/registry-model), [Source Reconciliation and Authority](/chlom/source-reconciliation), and [Rights Ledger and Evidence Model](/chlom/rights-ledger-and-evidence), but the recovery program needed dedicated current substantive successors that combine those controls into bounded reconstruction contracts.

## Pass B — evidence-backed gap closure

Two dedicated successors close that gap:

- [CHLOM Identity & Trust Reconciliation Contract](/chlom/identity-trust-reconciliation-contract) — substantive successor for **11** P0 identity/trust records;
- [CHLOM Evidence & Audit Reconciliation Contract](/chlom/evidence-audit-reconciliation-contract) — substantive successor for **6** P0 evidence/audit records.

Each successor exceeds the same **1,500-character substantive-body minimum** and **two-internal-link continuity minimum** used by Sprint 8. The new pages explicitly preserve identity-versus-authority, evidence-versus-truth, restricted-source, correction, phase-gate, and no-self-activation boundaries.

## What was not promoted

Wave 3 remains D2 and fail-closed. It does not activate:

- D3 or founder-reserved authority;
- legal-policy conclusions;
- rights or licenses;
- investment, securities, valuation, patent, franchise, regional-license, token, exchange, or settlement authority;
- credentials, private keys, secret material, or restricted evidence exposure;
- provider writes, money movement, checkout, production activation, or broad automation;
- terminal article disposition acceptance.

Historical bodies remain missing unless separately recovered. These are **current substantive successors**, not reconstructions presented as verbatim historical originals.

## Cumulative state

After Wave 3, **52 of 449 P0 candidate identities** have a substantive current successor that has passed the bounded machine qualification lane and is ready for parent review. The remaining **397** are heterogeneous: some need dedicated substantive successor work, while others belong in legal, rights/economic, security, specialist, D3, or other governed review lanes.

The next machine wave must continue to improve the target-side documentation substrate or select another genuinely bounded state family. It must not weaken the admission standard simply to increase the completion count.
