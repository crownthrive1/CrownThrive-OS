# Founder Adjudications — August 17, 2026

This page records founder-authorized institutional decisions made after Phase 2.8 contradiction hunting. These decisions supersede unresolved documentation ambiguity unless later replaced by an ADR.

- **CrownThrive Studios:** current CrownThrive-wide studio; **ThriveStudio** is legacy/alias lineage.

- **CrownApps / ThriveApps:** interchangeable aliases under one stable asset identity until final branding ADR.
- **CrownInsights:** separate platform.
- **CrownLytics:** separate platform.
- **CrownPulse:** separate platform.
- **ThriveCafe:** retired standalone platform.
- **Melanin Magic Cafe:** Melanin Magic coffee brand/licensable imprint using a local/small-batch model; recover XENthrive-era IP when sources are available.
- **Get Kenetic!™:** canonical spelling.
- **CII:** do not represent as a nonprofit by default.
- **ThriveFund:** internal CrownThrive-aligned impact/reinvestment mechanism linked to MM Suites and designated corporate allocations, with ThriveAlumni continuity/community alignment; legal/accounting mechanics remain to be finalized.
- **SSO:** external/heterogeneous authentication exists today; CrownThrive SSO / CrownThrive ID is scoped and actively being built but is not universally defined/deployed.
- **CHLOM:** LEX, DID, ZK, treasury, token, oracle and related modules remain recovery/build scope. Preserve foundational IP, modernize implementations, version every component, and do not claim deployment without evidence.
- **Holdings:** reconcile new and old HoldCo planning; CrownThrive, LLC remains the current operating entity until legal formation evidence says otherwise.
- **MM Suites:** franchise/regional-license architecture remains intended and should be reconstructed from the full source family and current ecosystem integrations; legal activation remains counsel-gated.
- **Kamora360:** sunset in all current institutional, platform, product and operating projections. Preserve historical sources and records, but create no new Kamora360 onboarding, licensing, development or dependencies.
- **OpsOasis:** canonical successor to Kamora360 for CrownThrive operational execution/work-management architecture effective August 17, 2026. The successor decision is authoritative even while exact deployed implementation remains evidence-gated.
- **Kamora domains:** `kamora360.com` and `kamoracrm.com` may remain controlled for migration, redirects, archive and defensive ownership; domain ownership must not be represented as active-platform status.
- **Patent/provisional:** preserve all drafts, inventor/ownership records and technical-moat studies; do not claim pending/issued rights without filing evidence; reassess protection strategy when resources permit.

## Rule

These are institutional decisions, not proof of legal filing, deployment, registration, revenue, or regulatory status. Those require their own evidence classes.
