# Documentation Substantive Rebuild — Wave 6

Sprint 12 continues the substantive-current successor program after candidate mapping reached **795 / 795** and Waves 1–5 machine-qualified **58 / 449** P0 identities for parent review.

This wave deliberately performs classification hygiene before adding another substantive cohort. Sprint 12 converts that finding into a deterministic non-terminal overlay rather than weakening the D2 gate or rewriting the historical Sprint 4 crosswalk.

## Framework versus implementation boundary

The new CIE successor keeps these distinctions explicit:

```text
framework definition ≠ deployed service
channel blueprint ≠ active channel
analytics model ≠ verified metric
integration diagram ≠ live interoperability
provider capability ≠ CrownThrive authority
historical assignment ≠ current platform mapping
CIE review ≠ rights or economic approval
```

That allows recovered framework doctrine to remain useful while current deployment, provider, rights, economic and authority state continue to require their own evidence.

## What Wave 6 does not create

Wave 6 does not recover missing historical bodies and does not accept terminal dispositions. It creates no D3 authority, legal-policy effect, rights or licensing authority, investment or securities claim, patent status, franchise or regional-license activation, identity credential, evidence attestation authority, provider-write permission, payment or settlement authority, secret publication, production deployment or Phase 3 activation.

The 35-row collision overlay remains a routing layer for later specialist work. None of those records is silently converted into a current AI capability merely because the legacy title sounds technical.
