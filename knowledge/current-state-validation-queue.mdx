# Current-State Validation Queue

Phase 2.7 converted ambiguity into an explicit work queue instead of allowing agents to infer current state from old documents. Phase 2.8 now links that queue to the **Institutional Contradiction Ledger** and **Canonical Adjudication Queue** so unresolved conflicts have owners, evidence requirements, and machine-readable blocking states.

## Priority validation topics

### Governance and entity

- Current legal/entity status of any proposed Holdings structure
- Current status of CII / ThriveFund legal form
- Current ThriveAlumni authority, boards, committees, or advisory bodies
- Any C-corporation, share-class, DAO, token, securities, inheritance, or ownership-allocation language found in historical sources

### MM Suites

- Digital framework vs physical-location status
- Suite Pro / SuitePros status
- franchise or regional-license status
- current adopted economics vs historical overrides/models
- any investment or transferable-interest language

### Technology and infrastructure

- CrownThrive ID current implementation scope
- IO/MCP current adapters
- ThriveRelay / Spryn.io current role
- Ops Oasis current role
- CrownInsights vs CrownLytics vs CrownPulse boundaries
- CrownApps / ThriveApps naming
- current sovereign-web or self-hosted infrastructure status

### CHLOM

- current acronym canon for DLA, DAIL and legacy variants
- current vs target pallet/service implementation
- LEX implementation status
- any blockchain, token, DAO, oracle, ZKP, DID, treasury, tax, yield, staking, or bridge claims

### Portfolio

- disposition of long-tail assets in the Extended Register
- CrownThrive Music vs Virality Music
- ThriveStudio vs CrownThrive Studios vs AdLuxe Studio
- ThriveCafe vs Melanin Magic Cafe
- EcoDrive and Stripe Climate status
- Kinetic / Kenetic naming

### Commercial and legal claims

- pricing and compensation rules that remain current
- ambassador/affiliate earnings claims
- patent/provisional filing status
- franchise/license claims
- public platform counts and milestone figures
- Locticians member vs verified-record vs active-account counts
- CrownThrive ecosystem-member methodology and deduplication
- current sustainability-program evidence and qualifying language
- public Virality Music positioning and legacy portfolio-page correction

## Validation evidence classes

Strongest evidence should come from signed governing records, verified current production state, payment/provider state, active domain/deployment state, adopted policies, current registries, or explicit recent owner decisions.

Historical business plans and prospectuses remain valuable evidence of intent, not automatic evidence of present implementation.

## Phase 3 gate

Unresolved items may be deferred only if they are labeled and cannot affect Phase 3 permissions, rights, money movement, public claims, legal structures, or irreversible automation.
