## Validation evidence classes

Strongest evidence should come from signed governing records, verified current production state, payment/provider state, active domain/deployment state, adopted policies, current registries, or explicit recent owner decisions.

Historical business plans and prospectuses remain valuable evidence of intent, not automatic evidence of present implementation.

## Phase 3 gate

Unresolved items may be deferred only if they are labeled and cannot affect Phase 3 permissions, rights, money movement, public claims, legal structures, or irreversible automation.
