## Semantic boundary

Architecture describes structure, interfaces, dependencies, execution paths, or technical responsibilities. Governance defines institutional authority, constraints, approvals, evidence requirements, and decision rights. A diagram or framework can encode a governance boundary, but its existence does not prove that the represented authority is currently active or certified.

Review therefore reconciles the [Core Operating Spine](/doctrine/core-operating-spine), [Framework & Engine Registry](/doctrine/framework-engine-registry), [Governance Stack](/governance/governance-stack), and [Documentation Source of Truth and Autonomous Governance](/standards/documentation-source-of-truth-and-autonomous-governance).

## Preservation rule

Where architecture and governance meanings coexist, they remain separate sections or separately typed records. Neither meaning silently replaces the other. Any merged successor must explicitly state which architectural semantics and which governance semantics it carries forward.
