## Authority boundary

Review must identify who or what is authorized to classify, approve, execute, certify, publish, deploy, spend, settle, license, or represent institutional state. A system may optimize within delegated authority; it may not manufacture a broader mandate from technical capability.

The lane aligns with the [Governance Model](/governance/model), [Autonomy Operating Constitution](/standards/autonomy-operating-constitution), [Human-Agent Hybrid Alignment](/standards/human-agent-hybrid-alignment), and [Evidence, Claims and Proof Standard](/standards/evidence-claims-and-proof-standard).

## Preservation rule

A current governance determination is appended with provenance and scope. It does not erase recovered authority language, competing historical assignments, or narrower delegated mandates. Conflicts remain explicit until evidence supports adjudication.
