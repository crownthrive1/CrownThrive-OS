## Revival Doctrine

Recovered CrownThrive assets must receive one governing disposition:

`ACTIVATE | REVIVE | MERGE | MIGRATE | RESEARCH | RESERVE | LICENSE | ARCHIVE | SUPERSEDE | SUNSET`.

Recovery never implies activation. A domain, provider license, source file, historical Help Center description or detailed technical paper proves lineage/capability evidence only to the extent supported.

Rebuilt current assets may use collateral CrownThrive information, current authoritative research, current technology and asset-type best practice. A rebuilt artifact is a **new current version with source lineage**, not a counterfeit historical original.

Material activation/rebuild decisions require risk-derived specialist review and independent governance/quorum.
