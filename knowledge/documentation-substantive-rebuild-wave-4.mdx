# Documentation Substantive Rebuild — Wave 4

Wave 4 continues the substantive-current successor program after the recovered **795 / 795** title estate reached candidate mapping and Waves 1–3 machine-qualified **52 / 449** P0 identities for parent review.

This wave does not treat every recovered CHLOM component, framework, pallet or engine title as a live implementation. It isolates a much narrower D2 lane: P0 `component_framework_reconciliation` records whose P0 classification is also supported by machine-contract terminology such as API, MCP, SDK, schema, registry, event, endpoint or integration, while high-risk authority, rights, economic, identity, evidence, legal, restricted and secret-bearing records remain held.

## What was deliberately held

The broader `component_framework_reconciliation` family contains records whose priority can originate from other consequential terms. Wave 4 therefore refuses machine promotion when the title or classification carries authority/governance, rights/licensing, evidence/audit, identity/credential, economic/settlement, investment, patent, franchise, restricted, secret or similar high-risk meaning.

Those records are not lost. They remain mapped and must move through the appropriate specialist or later bounded lane rather than inheriting a component-framework disposition simply because the source title also contains a word such as `framework` or `protocol`.

## Current-state contract

The current successor distinguishes stable component identity from deployment state. A recovered module, engine, framework, service, pallet or integration can be institutionally real as lineage while its implementation remains research, specified, controlled-test, blocked, superseded or otherwise non-production.

Machine-facing terminology likewise does not create technical or institutional authority. API reachability is not provider-write permission; a schema does not activate a service; a registry entry does not prove deployment; and a framework name does not grant execution authority. Current machine contracts remain connected to the [CHLOM Registry Model](/chlom/registry-model), [CHLOM Ecosystem Integrations](/chlom/ecosystem-integrations), and [Phase 3 Service Contract](/chlom/phase-3-service-contract).

## Guardrails

Wave 4 does not claim recovery of a missing historical article body and does not accept terminal dispositions. It creates no legal-policy effect, rights/economic authority, identity or credential authority, evidence/attestation authority, investment or securities authority, patent status, franchise/license activation, token/exchange/settlement authority, provider-write expansion, secret publication, money movement or production activation.

Phase 3 remains `blocked_pending_phase_2_99_hard_exit`. The independent [Phase 3 Readiness Gate](/technology/phase-3-readiness-gate) remains controlling, and Phases 11–20 remain `reserved_definition_required`.
