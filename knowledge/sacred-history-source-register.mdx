# Sacred History Wave 1 Source Register

**Stable content ID:** `ct.content.sacred-history`  
**Current institutional generation:** Phase 3  
**Relationship:** additive to KJV Visualized and The Sermon Toolkit; no replacement of the core property  
**Commerce authority:** PentaGreen + CHLOM/DAIL evidence and applicable human/rights gates

This register preserves the first Sacred History source cohort from the August 24, 2026 controlled foundation. It reserves `S124` through `S131`; existing CIE continuation retains `S118` through `S123`. A source entry proves what the identified source says or contains. It never automatically proves a current claim, commercial reuse right, license, product, entitlement, theological conclusion or production deployment.

## Rights and cultural-review codes

- `C`: commercial candidate only under exact item-level public-domain or compatible-license evidence.
- `L`: link/cite/original factual synthesis; no source-asset reproduction in a paid product absent additional authority.
- `H`: reproduction or commercial use held until missing permission/license evidence is recorded.
- `CP`: cultural/community permission or review is required in addition to conventional copyright analysis.

No collection is blanket-cleared by its presence here. Historical verification, CIE cultural review, CHLOM rights/licensing, PentaGreen economic activation, custody/readback and product-specific release certification remain independent.

## Claim classification

Public language must distinguish `DOCUMENTED_FACT`, `PRIMARY_SOURCE_CLAIM`, `TRADITION_SELF_DESCRIPTION`, `SCHOLARLY_CONSENSUS`, `SCHOLARLY_INTERPRETATION`, `CONTESTED_CLAIM`, `DISPUTED_ATTRIBUTION`, `ORAL_TRADITION`, `LATER_TRADITION`, `SCHOLARLY_DATASET_ESTIMATE` and `UNKNOWN` as applicable.

Cross-tradition discovery may use `SHARED_PERSON`, `SHARED_EVENT`, `SHARED_THEME`, `TEXTUAL_RELATIONSHIP`, `TRADITIONAL_PARALLEL`, `HISTORICAL_INFLUENCE` or `DISPUTED_RELATIONSHIP`; none automatically means identity, equivalence or “same passage.”
