# Sacred History Wave 1 Source Register

This page is an **additive continuation of the Institutional Source Register** for the Sacred History first wave. It reserves `S124` through `S131`; the existing CIE continuation keeps `S118` through `S123`. Future normalized projections must preserve these identities rather than renumbering them.

The private working registry contains additional candidate sources and exact-item review metadata. This public page records only the first eight source families needed to govern the initial evidence layer. A source entry proves what the identified source says or contains; it does not automatically prove a current claim, commercial reuse right, license, product, entitlement, or production deployment.

## Rights and cultural-review codes

- `C`: commercial candidate only under the exact item-level public-domain statement or compatible license.
- `L`: link, cite, or use for original factual synthesis; do not reproduce the source asset in a paid product.
- `H`: hold reproduction or commercial use until the missing permission or compatible license is recorded.
- `CP`: cultural or community permission/review is required in addition to conventional copyright analysis.

No collection is blanket-cleared by its presence in this register. Historical verification, CIE review, CHLOM review, Drive certification, and ThriveEvergreen activation remain independent.

## Claim-classification rules

Public language must distinguish `DOCUMENTED_FACT`, `PRIMARY_SOURCE_CLAIM`, `TRADITION_SELF_DESCRIPTION`, `SCHOLARLY_CONSENSUS`, `SCHOLARLY_INTERPRETATION`, `CONTESTED_CLAIM`, `DISPUTED_ATTRIBUTION`, `ORAL_TRADITION`, `LATER_TRADITION`, and `UNKNOWN`.

SlaveVoyages quantities also require `SCHOLARLY_DATASET_ESTIMATE`, dataset/version lineage, relevant variables, retrieval date, methodology, and uncertainty. Cross-tradition discovery may use `SHARED_PERSON`, `SHARED_EVENT`, `SHARED_THEME`, `TEXTUAL_RELATIONSHIP`, `TRADITIONAL_PARALLEL`, `HISTORICAL_INFLUENCE`, or `DISPUTED_RELATIONSHIP`; none of those automatically means “same passage.”

## Current state

The eight public-safe source families are registered. No Sacred History claim, route, product, license, SKU, entitlement, or commercial source reproduction is declared complete by this page. The existing KJV reader and monetized storefront remain outside these localized expansion holds.
