## Review boundary

Economic review must distinguish a capability that *could* support commerce from a CrownThrive-authorized economic state. Provider identifiers, checkout availability, API responses, catalog records, invoices, analytics, advertising delivery, or agent actions do not independently manufacture authority.

Evidence should reconcile the current offering identity, provider aliasing, entitlement path, settlement treatment, rollback/compensation behavior, and applicable governance before any public economic claim is promoted.

## Preservation rule

Existing commercial records remain independently attributable. A newer provider, automation, agent, or economic engine may be added as a successor or execution mechanism only with explicit provenance; it may not silently replace legacy or competing commercial state.
