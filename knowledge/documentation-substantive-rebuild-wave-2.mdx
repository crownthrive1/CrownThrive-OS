## What did not pass the automated lane

The policy also permitted `evidence_audit_reconciliation`, `identity_trust_reconciliation`, and `io_surface_and_machine_contract_reconciliation`. On this exact source state, **no additional row in those families satisfied every Wave 2 admission gate**. They remain open for later reconstruction, stronger evidence, a different qualified canonical anchor, or specialist/parent review.

That distinction matters. A recovered identity or evidence title is not automatically substantive merely because a related current page exists. CrownThrive continues to distinguish historical title recovery from current authoritative meaning through [CrownThrive ID](/technology/crownthrive-id), the [CHLOM Registry Model](/chlom/registry-model), the [Rights Ledger and Evidence Model](/chlom/rights-ledger-and-evidence), and [Institutional Registry Architecture](/technology/institutional-registry-architecture).

## Machine-contract boundary

Wave 2 verifies documentation continuity, not production activation. The current [CHLOM Ecosystem Integrations](/chlom/ecosystem-integrations), [MCP Topology & Tool Boundaries](/technology/mcp-topology-and-tool-boundaries), and [Phase 3 Service Contract](/chlom/phase-3-service-contract) remain the governing current successors for integration and machine-contract semantics.

The machine lane does not grant:

- provider write permission;
- legal or policy authority;
- rights or licensing authority;
- economic, exchange, settlement or payout authority;
- secret or credential exposure;
- production activation;
- Phase 3 entry.
