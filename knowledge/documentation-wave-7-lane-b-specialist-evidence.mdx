# Wave 7 Lane B — Specialist Evidence

Lane B receives records whose semantics require specialized evidence handling. It includes the collision families already separated by the [AI, Agent & Algorithm Collision Routing Register](/knowledge/ai-agent-algorithm-collision-routing-register) and any additional P0 record whose authority surface exceeds Lane A.

## Specialist sublanes

- rights and licensing;
- rights plus economics;
- economics and commerce authority;
- authority and governance;
- identity and credential semantics;
- architecture/governance semantic collisions.

Each sublane may produce an evidence packet, contradiction record, hold recommendation, source-strengthening request, or a recommendation to return the record to Lane A after the specialist concern is resolved.

## Required evidence packet

```yaml
inventory_id:
specialist_lane:
source_refs: []
current_authority_refs: []
conflicts: []
unresolved_questions: []
rights_effect: NONE_UNLESS_PROVEN
economic_effect: NONE_UNLESS_PROVEN
identity_effect: NONE_UNLESS_PROVEN
governance_effect: NONE_UNLESS_PROVEN
recommended_next_state: HOLD
parent_review_required: true
```

The packet is governed by [Rights & AI Provenance](/governance/rights-and-ai-provenance), [IP Ownership & Control](/governance/ip-ownership-and-control), [Source Authority Hierarchy](/knowledge/source-authority-hierarchy), and [Evidence, Claims and Proof](/standards/evidence-claims-and-proof-standard).

## No implicit authority

A legacy title containing terms such as license, royalty, token, validator, identity, exchange, ownership, AI, algorithm, framework, credential, payment, or governance does not create present-day authority. Specialist review must identify the actual controlling evidence.

## Exit behavior

A record exits Lane B only with an explicit evidence-backed recommendation. Terminal acceptance remains outside this lane and is prepared, when appropriate, through [Lane D](/knowledge/documentation-wave-7-lane-d-terminal-disposition-prep).
