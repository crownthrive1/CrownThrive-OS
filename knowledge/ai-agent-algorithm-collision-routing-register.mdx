# AI, Agent & Algorithm Collision Routing Register

This register institutionalizes the **35 P0 records** that remained inside the historical `ai_agent_algorithm_reconciliation` family during Documentation Substantive Rebuild Wave 6. The cohort is preserved as recovered evidence, but **zero records receive generic AI-family machine admission**.

The purpose of this register is routing, not adjudication. It prevents technical-sounding legacy labels from silently becoming present-day CrownThrive authority, deployment claims, ownership claims, payment authority, identity assertions, or production capabilities.

Each specialist section is additive. None replaces the historical crosswalk, the recovered source record, or another current doctrine page. When row-level source identities are independently verified, they may be appended to the appropriate section with provenance and exact disposition evidence.

## Admission boundary

A legacy record containing words such as *AI*, *agent*, *algorithm*, *model*, *automation*, *validator*, *engine*, or *framework* does not by itself prove that CrownThrive currently operates a production AI capability. Classification must resolve the record's dominant institutional concern first: rights, economics, authority, identity, or architecture.

The register therefore sits between recovered history and current institutional doctrine. It is governed alongside [Documentation Substantive Rebuild — Wave 6](/knowledge/documentation-substantive-rebuild-wave-6), the [Source Authority Hierarchy](/knowledge/source-authority-hierarchy), the [Contradiction Ledger](/knowledge/contradiction-ledger), and the [Evidence, Claims and Proof Standard](/standards/evidence-claims-and-proof-standard).

## Non-replacement rule

No specialist section may silently absorb or delete a competing record. Conflicts remain visible until evidence supports a terminal disposition. Where two records describe different institutional functions, both remain independently addressable even if their legacy names overlap.
