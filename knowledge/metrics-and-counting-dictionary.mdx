# Metrics and Counting Dictionary

CrownThrive has accumulated legitimate numbers from different periods and systems. Phase 2.8 establishes the rule that a number without a defined population, time, source and methodology is not an institutional metric.

## Ecosystem inventory metrics

- **platform\_count** — software or operational platforms meeting the current registry definition.
- **brand\_count** — public-facing brands, excluding pure backend engines unless explicitly included.
- **framework\_count** — named governance, operating, cultural, research or methodology frameworks.
- **portfolio\_asset\_count** — all governed portfolio records under a specified inclusion policy.
- **active\_asset\_count** — assets currently marked active/production under the portfolio lifecycle.
- **domain\_count** — governed domain records; must distinguish registered, active, redirected, reserved and expired.
- **engine\_count** — software/service engines tracked independently of brands.

The numbers 50\+, 60\+, 68, 74 and 80\+ found across CrownThrive sources therefore may all be historically meaningful while describing different dates or inclusion rules.

## People and network metrics

Never use these as synonyms:

- **registered\_account**;
- **member**;
- **verified\_member**;
- **professional\_listing**;
- **verified\_record**;
- **active\_member**;
- **monthly\_active\_user**;
- **monthly\_visitor**;
- **subscriber**;
- **customer**;
- **ecosystem\_member**;
- **event attendee**;
- **course learner**.

A Locticians public member count and a larger internal directory/verified-record count can coexist only if each metric declares its population and deduplication method.

### Virality universe/canon counts

Do not conflate:

- **featured\_world\_door\_count** — curated dated homepage/navigation doors;
- **governed\_universe\_count** — top-level universe records meeting the canonical universe definition;
- **series\_count** — series nested under a universe;
- **collection\_or\_lane\_count** — active collections/developing lanes not yet promoted to universe state;
- **room\_count** — listening/experience rooms, which may be nested under a world;
- **character\_dossier\_count** — governed character records;
- **portrait\_count** — assigned visual portraits;
- **no\_likeness\_count** — official characters whose portrait state prohibits or withholds likeness.

The current nine-door Virality homepage snapshot is not the complete governed-universe count.

### KJV room and catalog counts

Do not conflate:

- **public\_declared\_room\_count** — count claimed by current public copy;
- **directly\_named\_room\_count** — canonical names supported by authoritative navigation/source evidence;
- **live\_verified\_room\_count** — rooms whose current route, access, content and lifecycle have been independently verified;
- **unresolved\_room\_record\_count** — stable room placeholders retained because names/source records are missing;
- **catalog\_record\_count** — all governed product/tool records;
- **active\_offer\_count** — records with certified current price/checkout/license/entitlement/fulfillment;
- **source\_master\_count** — records with verified canonical masters;
- **article\_manifest\_count** — required article records, not necessarily published bodies.

A public declaration of sixteen rooms and fourteen recovered names can coexist only with an explicit two-record reconciliation gap.

### Advertising and provider metrics

Do not merge:

- provider-requested impressions;
- served impressions;
- viewable impressions;
- clicks;
- conversions by named definition/window;
- invalid/filtered traffic;
- proof-of-play;
- provider-reported spend or earnings;
- CrownLytics-normalized values;
- advertiser invoices;
- publisher/location accrued earnings;
- settled payouts;
- estimates, tests and demo data.

Every AdLuxe metric identifies provider/account, campaign, placement, definition, attribution window, timezone, filters, effective period and reconciliation state.

### Creator campaign metrics

Do not conflate creator-reported reach, platform-reported views, AdLuxe-delivered impressions, CrownLytics-normalized events, clicks, attributed conversions, approved deliverables, compensation accruals and paid payouts. Each CrownFluence record identifies source, metric definition, attribution window, invalid/filtered activity, reconciliation state and effective period.

### Mentorship and gathering metrics

Do not convert mentor profiles into verified mentors, match proposals into accepted matches, scheduled sessions into completed sessions, room joins into meaningful attendance, registrations into attendance, attendance into recording consent, or feedback into guaranteed outcomes. Program and privacy/safety metrics retain population, period, cohort, provider and evidence definitions.

### Ticket and admission metrics

Do not conflate event capacity, ticket inventory, tickets offered, orders created, payments authorized, paid orders, tickets issued, active tickets, transfers, QR scans, admitted attendees, refunds, disputes, organizer accruals and settled payouts. Every event/ticket metric resolves the event version, ticket type, inventory pool, timezone, processor/order/ticket state and reconciliation timestamp.

### Education, challenge, credential and authority metrics

Track independently:

- course enrollments, active learners, lesson/module completions and course completions;
- quiz/challenge attempts, reviewed attempts, scores, ranks, leaderboard positions and appeals;
- badges, CrownRewards, CrownThrive certificates and institutionally verified credentials;
- current professional/government licenses or board credentials, including jurisdiction and expiration;
- verified mentor/expert eligibility and actual accepted engagements/matches;
- ThriveAlumni members, chapters, committees, local titles and active terms;
- Commons local profiles, groups, memberships, moderation roles and active users;
- CHLOM permissions, delegated institutional roles and corporate/fiduciary authority.

None of these counts or states is interchangeable. A high score is not a course completion; a certificate is not a professional license; a platform-verified expert is not automatically licensed for regulated work; an alumni/Commons title is not corporate or CHLOM authority.

### Expert and community activity metrics

Do not conflate CrownConsults expert profiles, identity-verified providers, credential-verified providers, available providers, booked engagements, completed engagements, deliverables, refunds/disputes and paid provider amounts. Likewise, do not convert Commons group membership into active participation, moderation role into institutional authority, or message volume into healthy community impact.

## Financial metrics

Distinguish:

- gross sales;
- gross payment volume;
- recognized revenue;
- marketplace GMV;
- platform fee revenue;
- creator/partner payout;
- royalty accrual;
- cash received;
- refunds;
- disputes;
- credits issued;
- non-cash rewards;
- forecast/projection;
- valuation estimate.

Historical business-plan projections must never be presented as actual revenue.

## Geography metrics

Claims such as countries served, countries represented, countries with members, countries with customers, and countries with active transactions are different metrics.

## Required metric record

Every consequential metric should carry:

- metric ID;
- canonical name;
- definition;
- population;
- inclusion/exclusion rule;
- source system;
- time window / as-of date;
- deduplication method;
- owner;
- confidence/evidence class;
- public wording;
- prior value history.

<Warning>
  Agents must not transform a record count into a member count, a forecast into revenue, a product record into an available offer, a generated track into a distributed release, a featured world shelf into a total universe count, a declared room count into a verified named-room count, provider counters into reconciled settlement evidence, a score or leaderboard rank into a credential, a CrownThrive certificate into professional licensure, an expert/community/alumni title into institution-wide authority, or a Commons account into CrownThrive ID.
</Warning>
