# Institutional Knowledge Recovery

CrownThrive’s institutional knowledge base is being rebuilt from surviving source files, current production evidence, recovered Help Center structure, project records, and governed current-state decisions.

## Recovery objective

The goal is not to pretend every historical article body survived intact. The goal is to preserve what is recoverable, identify what was reconstructed, and produce a durable source-of-truth architecture that can be maintained going forward.

## Recovery classes

Every recovered or rebuilt knowledge record should be classified as one of:

- **recovered-verbatim** — body recovered directly from a surviving source.
- **reconstructed-from-sources** — rebuilt from multiple supporting sources.
- **canonical-replacement** — new authoritative article replacing obsolete or conflicting material.
- **archived-historical** — retained for lineage but not current guidance.
- **missing-owner-input** — known record that cannot be completed without authoritative input.

## Publication classes

Knowledge may be:

- **public**
- **gated**
- **internal**
- **restricted**

A recovered title does not automatically imply that its content should be public.

## Required record metadata

Each rebuilt article should eventually carry:

- stable article ID
- title
- corridor
- classification
- recovery class
- authority tier
- source list
- status
- owner
- review date
- publication disposition
- superseded article or decision, if applicable

## Scale

The recovered institutional corpus includes the 795-title legacy Help Center estate, current CHLOM institutional recovery materials, and the `S91` Founders Handbook source family. The extended `Founders_Handbook_V1.1.PDF` is 289 pages and approximately 70,352 words, making it a major founder-brain recovery source rather than a short supporting document.

The operating priority is to reconstruct the **spine and authority model first**, then progressively rebuild article bodies and institutional runbooks without losing provenance.

## Founder documentation hard gate

Phase 3 cannot begin while the governed documentation estate remains materially stale. Before Phase 3 entry:

- the recovered 795 records must reach governed terminal dispositions;
- the Founder Handbook deep pass must reconcile material doctrine, canon, runbooks, historical structures and conflicts;
- prior Phase 1–2.x pages must be rescanned against later evidence and founder adjudications;
- every known brand/platform/framework/program identity must have a governed documentation disposition regardless of lifecycle state;
- public-safe domains must be linked where known and useful;
- internal portfolio/platform/domain/alias/source/changelog links must be reconciled;
- relevant public-facing identities must have a press/media-kit or archive press disposition;
- historical pages may remain historical, but stale current conclusions may not remain canonical;
- every material reconstruction must preserve its historical source and clearly identify the new current artifact;
- D2/D3 or otherwise material current reconstructions require applicable specialist review and independent governance/quorum;
- compact page-level changelog/version notes must explain material changes;
- the final Phase-3 snapshot must fail closed while a material current-claim conflict remains unresolved.

## Durable architecture

```text
Governed registries + source lineage
        ↓
Institutional knowledge records
        ↓
Mintlify / Help Center projection
        ↓
Public, gated, internal, or restricted audience view
```

Mintlify is a projection and editing surface. It should not become the only surviving copy of CrownThrive’s institutional memory.
