# Institutional Knowledge Recovery

CrownThrive’s institutional knowledge base is being rebuilt from surviving source files, current production evidence, recovered Help Center structure, project records, and governed current-state decisions.

## Recovery objective

The goal is not to pretend every historical article body survived intact. The goal is to preserve what is recoverable, identify what was reconstructed, and produce a durable source-of-truth architecture that can be maintained going forward.

## Recovery classes

Every recovered or rebuilt knowledge record should be classified as one of:

- **recovered-verbatim** — body recovered directly from a surviving source.
- **reconstructed-from-sources** — rebuilt from multiple supporting sources.
- **canonical-replacement** — new authoritative article replacing obsolete or conflicting material.
- **archived-historical** — retained for lineage but not current guidance.
- **missing-owner-input** — known record that cannot be completed without authoritative input.

## Publication classes

Knowledge may be:

- **public**
- **gated**
- **internal**
- **restricted**

A recovered title does not automatically imply that its content should be public.

## Required record metadata

Each rebuilt article should eventually carry:

- stable article ID
- title
- corridor
- classification
- recovery class
- authority tier
- source list
- status
- owner
- review date
- publication disposition
- superseded article or decision, if applicable

## Scale

The recovered institutional corpus includes a legacy Help Center structure with hundreds of article records and a current CHLOM institutional master compendium. The operating priority is to reconstruct the **spine and authority model first**, then progressively rebuild article bodies without losing provenance.

## Durable architecture

```text
Governed registries + source lineage
        ↓
Institutional knowledge records
        ↓
Mintlify / Help Center projection
        ↓
Public, gated, internal, or restricted audience view
```

Mintlify is a projection and editing surface. It should not become the only surviving copy of CrownThrive’s institutional memory.
