## Open governance gates

## Public/private boundary

The public register includes status, high-level doctrine, stable IDs, exact evidence digests, and correction history. Restricted source bodies, unpublished story facts, protected identities, private Drive identifiers, production recipes, prompt libraries, pricing negotiations, and legal evidence remain in governed private custody.
