## Public/private boundary

The public register includes status, high-level doctrine, stable IDs, exact evidence digests, aggregate cohort counts, recovery dispositions, and correction history. Restricted source bodies, unpublished story facts, protected identities, private Drive identifiers, private rights instruments, production recipes, prompt libraries, pricing negotiations, and legal evidence remain in governed private custody.
