## Duplicate-source handling

Two source families contain duplicate representations:

- the Bonged Out! Style Bible appears twice;
- the Bonged Out! IP Delivery Archive appears twice;
- the CrownThrive Welcome letter is represented in both DOCX and PDF.

These are **duplicate representations of a source family**, not independent corroborating authorities, unless a later comparison identifies a material content difference.

## Commercial-source quarantine

`S122` is intentionally quarantined from live commerce truth.

The following actions require current authority outside this source record:

- publishing live public pricing;
- creating Stripe Product/Price objects;
- enabling checkout;
- issuing a license or entitlement;
- promising or recording an equity grant;
- creating a new buyout right;
- making a definitive franchise/non-franchise legal conclusion for a live relationship.

The required handoff is current contract/legal review → CHLOM rights state → ThriveEvergreen economic authority → authorized execution.

## Current-state rule

A source file proves what the source says. It does not automatically prove present runtime availability, legal enforceability, provider configuration, entitlement, commercial state or production deployment.

That distinction is mandatory across CIE, CHLOM, CSS, ThriveEvergreen and the broader institutional knowledge system.
