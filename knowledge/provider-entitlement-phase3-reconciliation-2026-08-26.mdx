## Adserver and Partnero Entitlement — Phase 3 Reconciliation

This record carries forward the valid founder-attested provider-entitlement facts from the August 21 packet while superseding its Phase‑2.99 control model.

## Adserver.Online

A CrownThrive Adserver.Online account/subscription entitlement was founder-attested. Current AdLuxe source contains stronger later evidence for a bounded **read** adapter and provider-public capability/pricing research.

The entitlement does **not** by itself establish:

- provider-wide API write authority;
- permission for campaign/inventory/delivery mutations;
- settlement, payout or billing authority;
- a specific current account price/capacity/usage figure for public projection;
- a credential scope beyond exact certified evidence.

Current provider writes remain operation-specific and fail closed unless separately certified.

## Partnero

The Crown Affiliates software estate includes a founder-attested **lifetime AppSumo software entitlement** for Partnero. “Lifetime” here describes the software entitlement evidence; it does not create lifetime commission, referral, payout, enrollment, webhook, API, commerce or compensation rights for CrownThrive participants.

Current effective affiliate/ambassador economics require their own adopted schedule, attribution, disclosure, tax/payout, rights and PentaGreen/CHLOM evidence.
