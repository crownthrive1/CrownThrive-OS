## Historical Source Pack — August 26, 2026

<Warning>
  No source in this pack is implementation authority, execution authority, production proof, or current-state proof. Restricted bodies are **not** reproduced here.
</Warning>

## Disposition rules

- `HISTORICAL_CONTEXT` — lineage, doctrine, architecture, or prior operating context only.
- `LATENT_CONTEXT` — contains ideas that may be reconsidered, but cannot be implemented from the historical source.
- `RESTRICTED_HISTORICAL` — body remains private; only public-safe metadata or summaries may be projected.
- `PRIVATE_CONTEXT` — retained privately and excluded from institutional/public operating claims.
- Legal or contractual documents are archived as source records without implying that archival classification changes their legal effect.

## Technical historical treatment

The CHLOM token/mainnet, metaprotocol, and Genesis materials preserve useful concepts—dual-token research, Substrate/EVM sequencing, licensing objects, DLA/LEX concepts, identity, governance, oracle, treasury, and compliance architecture. Those ideas may be studied and compared with the current system, but **none of those source-era instructions may trigger a token launch, chain deployment, contract deployment, bridge, mint, governance action, or production architecture change**.

## Founder Handbook family

The multiple handbook PDFs are retained as separate historical/version evidence. They may explain prior doctrine, terminology, governance intent, succession concepts, ecosystem mappings, and operating philosophy. They do not establish current appointments, current permissions, current platform deployment, or current production state by themselves.

## MM Suites family

The pitch deck, prospectuses, and War Room package remain useful for historical commercial strategy, corridor architecture, franchise-model lineage, stakeholder assumptions, technology-stack intent, and unresolved latent concepts. Their financial projections, operating assumptions, legal positioning, launch claims, and integration claims are not automatically current.

The confidential bodies remain outside public PentaDocs.

## Legal instruments

The NDA and agreement-family records are historical source records for documentation purposes. **This classification is not a waiver, amendment, termination, rescission, or legal interpretation of the instruments themselves.** Their legal effect, if any, is governed by the instrument, later agreements/amendments, authoritative company records, and applicable law.

## Private material

The academic reflection is retained as private source context and is excluded from operational doctrine, current-state claims, and public Help Center publication.

## Promotion rule

A latent concept can re-enter active consideration only through a newly authored current record that names the historical source, defines the exact reused concept, establishes current ownership and evidence, passes required approvals/tests, and explicitly becomes the new authority.
