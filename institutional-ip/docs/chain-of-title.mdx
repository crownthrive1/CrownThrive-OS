# Chain-of-Title Evidence Graph

The graph records creation, contribution, assignment, license, consent, restriction, dispute and termination events. CHLOM remains authoritative for rights. Private agreements are referenced by digest and custody record, not published.

## Fail-closed rule

Unknown, missing, contradictory, superseded or unverified evidence remains `HOLD`. No public page carries private evidence bodies, credentials, fingerprints, protected calibration or counsel work product.
