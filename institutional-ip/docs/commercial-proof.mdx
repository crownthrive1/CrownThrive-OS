# Commercial Proof

Commercial proof advances only from verified external evidence. Internal tests, technical deployments, traffic, synthetic orders, catalog records and unrelated CrownThrive revenue are excluded from customer/revenue proof.

## Fail-closed rule

Unknown, missing, contradictory, superseded or unverified evidence remains `HOLD`. No public page carries private evidence bodies, credentials, fingerprints, protected calibration or counsel work product.
