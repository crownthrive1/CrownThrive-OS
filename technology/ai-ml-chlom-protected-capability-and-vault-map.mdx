## AI, ML and LLM lifecycle boundary

Every candidate system must declare whether it is deterministic automation, a rules engine, predictive ML, anomaly detection, retrieval, generative AI, a tool-using agent, a multi-agent workflow or human-assisted decision support. “AI-powered” is not a sufficient capability record.

The governed lifecycle remains:

```text
idea
→ use-case review
→ data and rights review
→ provider / model / prompt review
→ prototype
→ TEVV and red-team evaluation
→ controlled pilot
→ authorized release decision
→ monitoring and drift review
→ change, suspension or retirement
```

At every transition, model capability remains separate from institutional authority. A model may recommend, retrieve, classify, generate or operate a scoped tool only within the caller's registered role, data class, purpose, approval and autonomy ceiling.

## CHLOM six-function mapping

| CHLOM function | AI/ML coordination responsibility | Fail-closed boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Rights** | Dataset, source, prompt, output, likeness, voice, model and derivative-use provenance | Possession, generation or public availability does not establish permission or commercial rights. |
| **Rules** | Allowed purposes, audiences, data classes, tools, jurisdictions, thresholds and prohibited outputs | Missing policy, consent, scope or applicable review produces HOLD. |
| **Roles** | Owner, model operator, reviewer, cultural steward, security reviewer, rights reviewer and reserved human authority | Technical ability, model confidence or agent consensus cannot create authority. |
| **Revenue** | Candidate fees, royalties, commissions, credits, costs and allocation dependencies | This map activates no pricing, payment, entitlement, payout or economic schedule. |
| **Records** | Use-case records, model cards, dataset lineage, prompt-policy versions, evaluation receipts, incidents and corrections | Public records expose status and public-safe references, not protected payloads or storage details. |
| **Remedies** | Hold, quarantine, correction, rollback, revocation, appeal, re-evaluation and retirement | Failed or drifting systems do not self-promote by weakening the failed control. |

## CIE, Thrive Flywheel and framework boundary

CIE and the Thrive Flywheel are related but do different work:

- **CIE** evaluates whether an artifact, use or context is culturally and narratively aligned. It does not score a person's cultural authenticity and does not replace rights, legal, privacy or security review.
- **The Thrive Flywheel** describes governed movement of people, assets, attention, permissions, signals, revenue and reinvestment. It does not create cross-platform consent, rights or authority merely because a handoff could increase reach.
- **CHLOM** governs the Rights, Rules, Roles, Revenue, Records and Remedies required before, during and after those movements.

## Public-safe protected-vault classes

The word **vault** describes a protected custody class, not one vendor, database, bucket, repository or cryptographic system. Exact providers, object identifiers, paths, endpoints, digests, fingerprints, keys, recovery material, access topology and defensive implementation details remain outside this public page.

| Protected class | Governed contents | Permitted public projection | Never projected here |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Restricted evidence | Contracts, releases, private rights evidence, investigations, source masters and sensitive institutional artifacts | Stable public-safe status, classification and accountable role | Artifact body, locator, access token, private party data or custody topology |
| Model governance | Model cards, provider reviews, dataset lineage, prompt policies, evaluation fixtures, incidents and change history | Use-case category, lifecycle state, applicable gates and non-sensitive limitations | Restricted prompts, private datasets, raw sensitive inputs/outputs or provider credentials |
| Protected algorithm | Proprietary calibration, weights, detection logic, private evaluation corpora and restricted runtime policy | Public doctrine, input/output class, decision boundary and HOLD/PASS semantics when separately approved | Calibration values, executable proprietary logic, private runtime location or reconstruction detail |
| Secrets and credentials | API credentials, signing secrets, OAuth material, encryption keys and privileged service references | Presence of a governed reference class and rotation/owner state | Secret value, recovery value, reusable token, environment mapping or privileged access path |
| Cryptographic custody | Candidate signing, attestation, revocation and recovery material where separately authorized | Research/target capability category and verification purpose | Private keys, seed phrases, key shares, wallet details, signing topology or recovery procedure |
| Continuity archive | Versioned exports, recovery packages and integrity-controlled institutional backups | Recovery class, test state and responsible role | Restricted archive contents, restore credentials or exploitable infrastructure detail |

Public documentation may say that protected evidence or a control record exists only when the underlying authority permits that statement. It must not expose enough detail to retrieve, reconstruct, attack or impersonate the protected system.

## TEVV, model-card and drift gates

Testing, evaluation, verification and validation are release controls, not a marketing score.

### Model-card minimum

A candidate model card should record purpose, owner, system category, provider/model/version, input and output classes, allowed and prohibited data, training/retrieval/evaluation lineage, known limitations, affected audiences, human-review threshold, evaluation scope, monitoring, fallback, incident path and retirement trigger.

### Independent TEVV

Applicable evaluation covers:

- factuality and source faithfulness;
- rights and provenance handling;
- policy, role and authority compliance;
- privacy, secrets and cross-boundary leakage;
- prompt injection, data exfiltration and tool abuse;
- cultural flattening, canon drift and protected-identity handling;
- harmful bias and disproportionate error;
- legal, financial, ministry and other high-consequence overclaim;
- structured-output validity, action verification and rollback;
- accessibility, cost, latency, reliability and audience fit.

The originating builder does not solely approve its own material system. Applicable Security/Privacy, AI/ML/LLM TEVV, IP/Rights/Licensing, CIE/cultural, Operations/SRE, Accessibility/Consumer Protection and reserved-human gates remain independent.

### Red-team and regression gate

Before controlled promotion, use synthetic and appropriately protected fixtures to test instruction collisions, stale or conflicting sources, unauthorized callers, sensitive-data canaries, malicious retrieval, fabricated evidence, unsupported IDs/routes, excessive tool authority, unsafe cross-platform inference, degraded providers and rollback failure.

### Drift and change gate

A change in provider, model, prompt policy, retrieval source, dataset, tool, threshold, policy, audience, data class or intended use may require re-evaluation. Monitoring should detect quality, refusal, correction, bias, cost, latency, incident, policy and outcome drift without logging raw restricted content by default.

Drift produces one of four outcomes: continue under the accepted version, correct and re-evaluate, degrade to read-only/manual operation, or suspend/retire. It never produces silent self-certification.

## Cryptographic and decentralized boundary

Cryptographic digests, attestations, DID/VC-style identity proofs, zero-knowledge methods, smart-contract adapters, chain anchoring, token concepts, treasury logic and poly-chain interoperability remain **research or target architecture unless separately verified and accepted**.

A cryptographic receipt may support evidence that a representation existed or was attested. It does not by itself prove truth, ownership, consent, legal validity, present authority, current applicability or production readiness.

No public-chain component may become the sole copy of CrownThrive institutional truth, private evidence or personal data. Token, cryptocurrency, public-sale, transferable-instrument, wallet, custody and automated-settlement activation remains blocked pending the applicable legal, regulatory, sanctions, tax, accounting, security, privacy, custody, recovery, market-integrity and consumer-protection gates.
