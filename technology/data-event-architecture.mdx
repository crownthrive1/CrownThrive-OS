## Data and Event Architecture

CrownThrive needs a common data language before it can operate as one measurable institution. **CrownLytics** should normalize signals from many operating systems without becoming a substitute for those systems of record.

## Canonical identifiers

Phase 3 registries should normalize stable IDs for at least:

- people and organizations;
- platforms and brands;
- domains and deployments;
- engines and vendors;
- products, SKUs, editions, and bundles;
- content and media assets;
- creators and contributors;
- rights, licenses, entitlements, and evidence;
- campaigns, placements, and creators;
- orders, subscriptions, payouts, and refunds;
- tickets, events, courses, rewards, and support cases.

## Canonical event model

Every event should carry a stable event ID, type, time, actor, subject, source system, correlation ID, environment, status, and privacy classification. Where relevant it should also include approval, rights, financial, and evidence references.

Representative events include:

```text
identity.created
identity.linked
consent.updated
asset.registered
rights.updated
license.issued
license.revoked
product.approved
product.published
order.paid
order.refunded
entitlement.created
content.published
campaign.delivered
reward.earned
support.opened
support.resolved
ticket.scanned
platform.deployed
incident.opened
correction.recorded
```

## Operational data versus analytics

Operational databases remain authoritative for transactional state. CrownLytics consumes normalized events for cross-platform analysis. A pageview analytics tool is not the institutional warehouse, and an analytics dashboard must never be treated as proof that a payment, license, entitlement, or rights state exists.

## Data minimization

Only necessary attributes should cross platform boundaries. Sensitive identity, evidence, health, financial, legal, and protected-person information requires restricted storage, purpose limitation, retention rules, and access review.

## Phase 3 implication

DAIL becomes the durable institutional event/audit layer. CrownLytics becomes the measurement and intelligence layer. The two must be connected but not conflated.
