# Institutional Registry Architecture

Phase 3 should not begin from a handful of CHLOM tables. The broader CrownThrive institution needs a **federated registry architecture** that gives stable identity to the objects agents and platforms must reason about.

## Core institutional registries

### Organization and identity

- organizations / legal entities;
- people and service actors;
- roles and authorities;
- consents and eligibility claims.

### Portfolio

- assets and brands;
- platforms and services;
- frameworks and imprints;
- corridors and hierarchy;
- lifecycle / priority;
- domains and deployments;
- engines / vendors / licenses;
- dependencies and successor relationships.

### Knowledge and governance

- sources;
- policies;
- SOPs/runbooks;
- ADRs and decisions;
- public claims;
- risks;
- approvals;
- signatures / evidence references;
- narrative/canon records.

### CHLOM

- rights;
- rules;
- DLA/licenses;
- roles/authority references;
- revenue/splits;
- evidence;
- remedies/cases;
- DAIL events.

### Commerce

- customers/organizations;
- products and SKUs;
- editions/formats;
- prices and price history;
- orders/payments;
- entitlements/downloads;
- subscriptions;
- refunds/disputes;
- sellers/creators/partners;
- payouts/rewards/credits.

### Content and media

- works and source masters;
- releases;
- books/publications;
- characters/worlds/canon;
- audio/video/images;
- channels/programs;
- contributors;
- distribution windows;
- content-rights relationships.

### Growth and impact

- campaigns/creatives/placements;
- referrals/affiliate links;
- creator deliverables;
- events/tickets;
- courses/credentials;
- impact programs/allocations/outcomes;
- support cases.

## Federated, not monolithic

A registry does not require one giant database. Operational systems can remain authoritative for their transactional state while the institutional layer maintains stable IDs, references, normalized events and governance relationships.

## Registry contract

Each registry needs schema version, stable ID, status vocabulary, source authority, ownership, audit timestamps, correction history, privacy class, export/backup behavior and API contract.

## Phase 3 gate

The first technical sprint should define these IDs and schemas before building agents that would otherwise create incompatible identifiers across different services.
