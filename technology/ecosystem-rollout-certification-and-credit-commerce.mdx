# Ecosystem Rollout, Certification and Credit Commerce

## Governing decision

CrownThrive uses a governed rollout program to move platform cohorts from recovered or current evidence into tested operating state without collapsing identity, provider capability, implementation, rights, commerce, fulfillment or phase authority.

The stable public program identifier is `ct.program.ecosystem-rollout-certification.v1`. Its orchestration agent is non-voting. Issue `#178` coordinates rollout evidence, issue `#179` coordinates credit-commerce preparation, and issue `#100` remains the parent institutional roadmap board.

Issue and project state is coordination evidence. It does not override accepted ADRs, hard gates, specialist authority or the current Phase 2.99 boundary.

## Public/private projection boundary

This page is the public-safe doctrine and interface projection.

- Exact credit conversion, funding tiers, pricing calibration and operative terms remain in restricted private custody.
- Private provider, queue, endpoint, job, canary and evidence topology is not published here.
- The public manifest carries only a deterministic digest for the governed private contract.
- A digest proves byte identity of the referenced private packet; it does not prove legal acceptance, pricing authority, fulfillment certification or live operation.
- Publication remains HOLD until issue `#131` accepts the exact public artifact.

Removing a restricted label without removing protected content is not sanitization. Protected content must be excluded from the public projection.

## Sixteen-dimension certification contract

Every platform is evaluated independently across:

1. stable identity;
2. source/evidence;
3. provider/account/project mapping;
4. authentication and credential boundary;
5. read capability;
6. write capability;
7. design/brand provenance;
8. interaction/accessibility;
9. rights and chain of title;
10. credit-commerce eligibility;
11. fulfillment/delivery;
12. refund/dispute/reversal;
13. observability/DAIL;
14. recovery/provider exit;
15. public documentation/claim state;
16. lifecycle/release/deployment.

A `PASS` in one dimension never silently passes another. `UNKNOWN`, `HOLD`, `BLOCKED`, `DEFERRED`, `PACKAGED`, `CERTIFIED` and `LIVE` retain separate meanings.

## Store Credits public contract

The closed-loop credit program is `ct.credit.store.v1`.

Permanent public boundaries:

- credits are internal purchasing value, not cash, interest-bearing balances, transferable instruments, cryptocurrency or CrownRewards points;
- payment providers fund or reconcile the rail but do not create CrownThrive licenses;
- governed entitlement and license records control access;
- provider configuration is evidence, not legal or tax sufficiency;
- a working funding rail cannot promote a package whose rights, license, pricing or fulfillment gates remain open;
- exact conversion and tier economics require private custody plus independent legal, tax, rights and pricing authority before activation.

The program remains inactive. Exact pricing is not authorized and checkout is disabled.

## Asset-release separation

For every downloadable or account-delivered asset, machine state preserves separate evidence for:

- package integrity;
- rights;
- license terms;
- private delivery;
- commerce rail;
- support boundary;
- final release disposition.

Technical package or delivery evidence may remain valid while the overall state stays `technical_pass_legal_hold`. A canary cannot erase valid evidence, and technical success cannot create rights or commercial authority.

## Canary and provider evidence

Private canaries are append-only and classified by execution mode. Synthetic checks prove only the internal contract under test. They do not substitute for live-provider behavior when provider behavior is the certification target.

Public documentation may state the evidence class and resulting gate. Exact transaction values, private object identities, signed delivery material, provider references and execution topology remain restricted.

## Migration safety

Existing production payment, entitlement or webhook rails remain in place until the replacement lifecycle is proven:

```text
fund/readback → credit → purchase → order → entitlement/license
→ delivery/access → refund/dispute/reversal → recovery
```

Migration is evidence-first, reversible and platform-specific. It is not a synchronized destructive cutover. Virality continues to preserve the founder decision that SoundCloud APIs are outside CrownThrive architecture.

## Agent and specialist routing

The rollout agent coordinates bounded work for identity, evidence, commerce migration, asset release, legacy-rail continuity and canary reconciliation. Internal queue identifiers and execution topology remain private.

Applicable work routes to independent source, proof, design, accessibility, API/MCP, security/privacy, IP/rights/licensing, finance/tax/treasury, legal/regulatory, operations/SRE and regional/localization specialists.

Task-scoped children remain non-voting. Same-family descendants cannot manufacture reviewer independence or sovereign quorum.

## Private interface boundary

The private runtime may expose authenticated read and internal planning contracts. The public repository may expose safe interfaces and digests only.

No current interface may:

- mutate an external provider;
- certify a platform;
- activate Store Credits or checkout;
- publish a product;
- move money;
- grant a sovereign vote;
- enter Phase 3.

## Phase inheritance

- **Phase 2.99:** inventory, reconcile, package, test and preserve explicit HOLDs.
- **Phase 3:** execute only accepted certification, entitlement, license, evidence and bounded-adapter contracts.
- **Phase 4:** federate certified contracts without flattening platform-specific authority.
- **Phase 5:** reconcile attributable revenue, delivery, support and reversals.
- **Phase 6:** externalize rights-cleared assets and services with versioned licenses.
- **Phase 7:** extend appropriate controls to physical, phygital and regional surfaces.
- **Phase 8:** apply Holdings-scale custody, attribution and provider-risk controls.
- **Phase 9:** add advanced interoperability only for validated needs.
- **Phase 10:** prove reconstruction, succession and provider exit.
- **Phases 11–20:** remain prospective until roadmap-v2 is governed.

Phase 3 remains blocked by the Phase 2.99 hard exit and the non-deferrable full-documentation-estate gate.

## Absolute no-go rules

1. Do not publish exact economics or protected runtime topology without accepted issue `#131` disposition.
2. Do not disable a working production rail before replacement proof.
3. Do not call a product live because a price, package, page or provider object exists.
4. Do not grant a CrownThrive license from payment-provider metadata alone.
5. Do not convert Store Credits into transferable, cash or crypto value by drift.
6. Do not use customer data as public ledger identity.
7. Do not treat synthetic evidence as live-provider proof.
8. Do not infer a stable institutional identity from a name.
9. Do not treat private interface deployment as provider-write certification.
10. Do not advance Phase 3 while the hard exit remains blocked.
