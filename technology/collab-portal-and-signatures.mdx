# Collab Portal, Access and Signatures

The **Collab Portal OS** is the controlled execution workspace for partners, contributors, vendors, licensees, clients and selected operators. It complements public support and GitHub/Mintlify by handling relationship-specific documents, approvals and work that should not be globally public.

## Core functions

The portal should support:

- party and organization identity;
- NDA and agreement intake;
- scoped project/workspace access;
- briefs, deliverables and milestone acceptance;
- secure file exchange;
- version history;
- comments and structured approvals;
- digital/electronic signatures where legally appropriate;
- controlled access to sensitive IP;
- license and entitlement references;
- invoice/payment references;
- audit logs and export;
- offboarding and access revocation.

## Signature doctrine

A click, approval event, typed signature, third-party e-signature and cryptographic signature can have different legal and evidentiary properties. The system should record the signature method, document version, signer identity, authority, timestamp, relevant consent and verification evidence.

## Access control

Workspace access follows least privilege and expires when the relationship or project ends unless a continuing role is explicitly approved. Sensitive source material should not be copied into general support or agent context merely because a collaborator has access to it.

## Relationship to CHLOM

The Collab Portal captures execution evidence and approvals. CHLOM records the resulting rights, licenses, restrictions, authority and durable evidence references where relevant.

## Current automation-integration state

The current institutional record does **not** yet contain a verified direct Collab Portal API/connector/write path for project-manager status automation. Until an authenticated endpoint, scopes, platform/service ID, audit semantics, idempotency/retry behavior and support owner are registered and tested, agents must not claim that a status update was posted directly to Collab Portal.

For current project-management runs, the safe continuity path is GitHub/Mintlify as the governed work record plus Gmail project-status messages. Each material pass should include a clearly labeled `Collab Portal Update` section and retain the direct-integration gap in the [Current-State Validation Queue](/knowledge/current-state-validation-queue).

## Phase 3 implication

The Approval Service and DAIL should accept signed/approved artifact references from the Collab Portal while keeping protected document contents in the appropriate restricted store. The future project-status adapter should use the same identity, evidence, least-privilege, idempotency and audit controls rather than becoming an unaudited side channel.
