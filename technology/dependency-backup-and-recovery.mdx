# Dependency, Backup and Recovery Architecture

CrownThrive must be recoverable without depending on one SaaS vendor, one repository, one account, or one person's memory.

## Dependency register

Every P0/P1 platform and institutional service should record:

- source repository;
- deployment host;
- database/storage;
- DNS/domain registrar;
- authentication provider;
- payment provider;
- email/communications provider;
- analytics provider;
- object/media storage;
- external APIs;
- legal/rightsholder dependencies;
- export method;
- replacement option;
- recovery owner.

## Backup classes

### Institutional knowledge

Canonical source should exist in version control, with periodic portable export of Markdown/MDX, registries and navigation/config manifests.

### Restricted source masters

Contracts, signed documents, rights evidence, unreleased masters and confidential research require encrypted controlled backup separate from public Mintlify.

### Service data

Databases need a defined RPO/RTO, backup cadence, retention period, restore owner and test schedule appropriate to their risk.

### Media and IP assets

Masters, stems, artwork, books, source files, metadata and checksum manifests should not live exclusively inside generation, distribution or publishing vendors.

## Secret recovery

Backups must not become a plaintext secret archive. Maintain an inventory of secret names, owners, rotation process, dependent services and recovery method while credentials themselves remain in an approved secret manager.

## Vendor exit packet

Every critical vendor should eventually have:

- export procedure;
- contractual/data-retention considerations;
- migration destination or adapter plan;
- dependency list;
- DNS/routing cutover plan where relevant;
- credential revocation checklist;
- post-exit validation.

## Restore testing

A backup is not verified until restoration is tested. Phase 2.99 GATE-007 must establish the first reproducible recovery evidence before Phase 3 entry; later phases continue recurring synthetic recovery tests for registry data, institutional docs, event/evidence records and selected service databases.

## Scope-based recovery objective

Recovery targets are scoped to impact and founder operating capacity rather than pretending every asset has the same recovery clock.

- **Critical institutional control, current docs, authority/canon/doctrine indexes, security/recovery instructions and minimum read-only operations:** target restoration or independently usable recovery state within **24 hours** where practicable.
- **P0/P1 operational services, database exports, key platform configuration and critical hosted assets:** target **48 hours** where dependencies permit.
- **Broader archives, noncritical historical assets and lower-priority platform estates:** target **72 hours or a documented risk-based schedule**.

These are recovery objectives, not guarantees against provider/legal/physical constraints. The system should self-heal automatically when the repair is D0/D1, reversible, observable and already authorized. Higher-risk repair remains governed and fail-closed.

Minimum Phase-3 recovery acceptance requires that CrownThrive can reconstruct reconciled documentation, Canon/Doctrine, machine registries, source/evidence indexes, critical database state, critical assets and required configuration from independent copies; recover credentials through a separate secure recovery process; and resume at least a governed read-only/degraded mode without depending on undocumented founder memory.

## Current continuity estate — founder inventory, 2026-08-19

Current recoverable/recovery-source lanes include:

- Google Drive and Google Docs institutional masters;
- GitHub repository/history/archive records;
- Gmail operational/governance archive;
- local computer/storage copies;
- individual Verpex cPanel server backups and hosted assets;
- phone-held copies where applicable;
- physical founder notebook records containing canon, doctrine and institutional ideas.

Physical notebooks and phone-held material are source artifacts, not yet complete machine backups. They should be indexed/digitized and entered into the source register. Founder memory is a discovery/reconciliation aid, not a certified backup.

The current Supabase estate must maintain a governed logical export and independent off-site copy until provider-native automated backup/PITR capability is separately available and authorized. Backup existence does not satisfy GATE-007 until restore/reproducibility evidence passes.

## Continuity principle

SaaS is replaceable infrastructure. CrownThrive identity, rights, canon, doctrine, evidence and canonical records must remain institution-controlled and portable. Knowledge is superseded through governed version/correction history, not silently overwritten or destroyed.
