# Phase 3 Readiness Gate

The cumulative recovery program established CrownThrive's doctrine, source lineage, contradictions, continuity, IP, developer architecture and governance standards. Phase 3 may begin only from an executable baseline that distinguishes current fact, historical source, research target, unresolved decision and production evidence.

Phase 2.97 patch release `2.7.9.1` expands the gate. Phase 3 is not a license to skip Phase 2.98 platform extraction or Phase 2.99 private-core/seed preparation.

## Gate A — Phase 2.98 platform extraction

Before Phase 3.0 implementation is considered ready for acceptance, P0/P1 platforms must have:

- stable identity, aliases and portfolio/corridor roles;
- capability-by-capability state;
- domains/routes/robots/sitemaps/SEO profile;
- vendor/engine, repository, deployment and environment inventory;
- identity/roles/SSO readiness;
- products/services/commerce/payment/webhook/entitlement state;
- IP/content/universe/room/product/rights package;
- data entities/classifications/flows/events;
- verified or explicitly unverified API base URLs, endpoints, docs, webhooks, exports and MCP opportunities;
- legal-policy dependencies and historical claim overlays;
- portals/dashboards/role surfaces;
- support/operations/security/backup/vendor-exit;
- claims/metrics and evidence levels;
- dependencies, cleanup candidates, risks and next milestone;
- versioned support/institutional article coverage for material platform capabilities, products, rights/licensing paths and role workflows;
- a documentation-impact map identifying which future code/config/site changes must update which articles, registries and changelogs.

At minimum this includes CrownThrive.com/Support, CHLOM, CrownThrive IO, Virality Music, KJV Visualized/Sermon Toolkit, Locticians/ThriveSeat, AdLuxe, Crown Affiliates/Rewards/Push, CrownLytics/Pulse/Insights, CrownThriveU and MM Suites/Suite Pros.

## Identity and versioning acceptance

- stable IDs survive renames, migrations and vendor changes;
- four-part institutional release, semantic service/API versions and independent policy/rights/economic/content versions coexist;
- corrections append and preserve prior values/effective periods;
- aliases/successors do not create duplicate identity;
- historical prospectus/paper language cannot override current status automatically.

## Explicitly permitted unresolved items

Phase 3 may start with records marked `research`, `proposed`, `legal_review_required`, `unverified`, `implementation_gated` or `source_not_located` if services cannot promote them silently.

Examples:

- HoldCo formation;
- MM Suites franchise/regional legal activation;
- patent/trademark filings not yet pursued or verified;
- universal SSO;
- tokens, DAO, dedicated chain, advanced settlement;
- Purple/Silver/Orange/Yellow papers not yet located;
- long-tail platforms not yet selected for a cohort.

## Absolute no-go conditions

- public repository contains production secrets or restricted evidence;
- no tested backup/restore or rollback for critical records;
- unresolved rights/authority can be treated as permission;
- agents can self-approve material actions;
- economic schedules or money destinations are mutable without version/authority;
- private retrieval depends on post-generation redaction;
- APIs/vendors are represented as integrated without inspection/evidence;
- historical patent/franchise/equity/token/smart-contract language is presented as current;
- cross-platform data moves without purpose, fields, policy and failure behavior;
- checkout can collect money without verified fulfillment/support;
- a material code/config/site/API/product/rights change can be released with neither a synchronized documentation update, a reviewed no-impact declaration nor a governed documentation delta;
- source-code repositories are assumed absent merely because they are not connected to the documentation workflow;
- a deployment/build artifact is reconstructed or force-pushed as authoritative editable source history;
- an unresolved universe, room, character, right, price, provider or account state is filled by naming similarity or arithmetic convenience;
- provider-documented capability is represented as CrownThrive account entitlement or production integration without authenticated evidence;
- historical and current price/license schedules can both resolve as active defaults without an effective-period/supersession decision;
- a platform has no governed changelog identity or documentation-impact owner for material changes;
- recovered audience-equity research becomes current creator ownership/economic rights without an adopted agreement;
- self-described mentor expertise becomes verified licensure or professional authority without evidence;
- private room URLs, access keys or participant records can enter public documentation or tools;
- room attendance can be treated as recording, transcription, clip or replay consent by default;
- payment, ticket issuance, QR validity, admission and organizer settlement collapse into one event state;
- course completion, challenge rank, points, a CrownThrive certificate, mentor/expert status, alumni/community title, CHLOM permission and corporate authority collapse into one credential or role state;
- a self-described CrownConsults expert or Commons/ThriveAlumni role is represented as licensed professional, fiduciary, corporate officer or institution-wide authority without current evidence;
- CrownThrive Commons is treated as CrownThrive ID or as blanket consent for unrelated platform data use;
- Digital Business Academy is silently merged into CrownThriveU or published as active without an adopted relationship and implementation record;
- correction history can be erased.

## Entry decision

Phase 3 is a **go for controlled Phase 3.0 engineering** after the Phase 2.98 and 2.99 deliverables are accepted. Broad autonomous execution remains no-go until the applicable Phase 3A–3D service acceptance tests are proven.

No future discovery should reset these controls. Newly recovered information enters through the versioning/correction/source process.
