# Phase 3 Readiness Gate

The cumulative recovery program established CrownThrive's doctrine, source lineage, contradictions, continuity, IP, developer architecture and governance standards. Phase 3 may begin only from an executable baseline that distinguishes current fact, historical source, research target, unresolved decision and production evidence.

Phase 2.97 patch release `2.7.9.1` expands the gate. Phase 3 is not a license to skip Phase 2.98 platform extraction or Phase 2.99 private-core/seed preparation.

## Gate A — Phase 2.98 platform extraction

Before Phase 3.0 implementation is considered ready for acceptance, P0/P1 platforms must have:

- stable identity, aliases and portfolio/corridor roles;
- capability-by-capability state;
- domains/routes/robots/sitemaps/SEO profile;
- vendor/engine, repository, deployment and environment inventory;
- identity/roles/SSO readiness;
- products/services/commerce/payment/webhook/entitlement state;
- IP/content/universe/room/product/rights package;
- data entities/classifications/flows/events;
- verified or explicitly unverified API base URLs, endpoints, docs, webhooks, exports and MCP opportunities;
- legal-policy dependencies and historical claim overlays;
- portals/dashboards/role surfaces;
- support/operations/security/backup/vendor-exit;
- claims/metrics and evidence levels;
- dependencies, cleanup candidates, risks and next milestone;
- versioned support/institutional article coverage for material platform capabilities, products, rights/licensing paths and role workflows;
- a documentation-impact map identifying which future code/config/site changes must update which articles, registries and changelogs.

At minimum this includes CrownThrive.com/Support, CHLOM, CrownThrive IO, Virality Music, KJV Visualized/Sermon Toolkit, Locticians/ThriveSeat, AdLuxe, Crown Affiliates/Rewards/Push, CrownLytics/Pulse/Insights, CrownThriveU and MM Suites/Suite Pros.

### Pass D platform-specific hard requirements

**Virality Music** must enter Phase 3 with the current featured public shelf separated from the complete governed universe registry; the thirty-class operating license slate mapped to master classes; work-level rights-readiness states; current versus historical price schedules; GSO/Virality Direct order/license/entitlement mappings; and explicit AI/provider/contributor/sample/voice/likeness evidence or holds.

**AdLuxe** must enter with Network, Studio and Places separated; provider capability, CrownThrive account entitlement and deployed integration stored as independent states; advertiser/publisher/location/creator roles; inventory/campaign/creative/reporting/settlement objects; consent/brand-safety/invalid-traffic controls; read/report-first adapter contract; and exact unverified account/API/repository fields rather than assumed integration.

**KJV Visualized / The Sermon Toolkit** must enter with the protected source/release checkpoint, original-repository recovery state, sixteen stable room IDs, fourteen verified names and two unresolved canonical-name records; current route/catalog/license/commerce/article/API states; soft-404 and public-version-title queues; Scripture-versus-authored-content boundaries; and replayable order/entitlement/refund/dispute tests. The two unresolved room names are not a Phase 3 blocker if they remain explicit and cannot be promoted silently.

**CrownThrive Support** must enter with the complete 795-title disposition manifest, stable article/changelog IDs, D0–D3 approval classes, per-platform docs-impact mappings and drift queues defined by the [Articleization Manifest and Autopublication Contract](/support/articleization-manifest-and-autopublication-contract).

Unknown state is permitted only when explicitly labeled `uninspected`, `source_not_located`, `unverified`, `blocked` or `deferred` with owner/reason.

## Identity and versioning acceptance

- stable IDs survive renames, migrations and vendor changes;
- four-part institutional release, semantic service/API versions and independent policy/rights/economic/content versions coexist;
- corrections append and preserve prior values/effective periods;
- aliases/successors do not create duplicate identity;
- historical prospectus/paper language cannot override current status automatically.

## Governance and authority acceptance

- founder/reserved decision registry;
- delegated role and relationship model;
- A0–A4 autonomy classes;
- approval thresholds and expiration;
- no agent self-approval for consequential actions;
- legal/accounting/security/rights/cultural gates;
- break-glass and appeal/override evidence;
- policy applicability by role/platform/action/jurisdiction/version.

## Data, evidence and privacy acceptance

- source authority and E0–E6 evidence levels;
- DAIL envelope and correction/causation/correlation fields;
- public/community/internal/restricted/strategic/machine-only classification;
- pre-retrieval authorization for private knowledge;
- tenant and relationship isolation;
- evidence references/checksums without broad payload exposure;
- retention/deletion/legal hold;
- purpose-limited cross-domain flow records;
- secrets excluded from docs/code/logs.

## Security, supply chain and continuity acceptance

- secret manager/KMS decision and rotation paths;
- dependency lock and software/vendor inventory;
- repository protection/branch/release controls;
- CrownThrive-owned README, agent instructions, brand assets and explicit IP/license scope;
- no Mintlify Starter Kit or unrelated template identity in accepted source/user-facing assets;
- environment separation and production-data restrictions;
- signed/immutable build or equivalent provenance, profiled against SLSA 1.2 where adopted;
- domain/DNS/TLS ownership map, with custom-domain activation required only for the approved public/private launch projection;
- backup, restore and rollback tests;
- vendor-exit records for critical dependencies;
- incident, kill switch, pause, read-only/degraded mode;
- credential compromise drill;
- off-provider copy of critical institutional registries/evidence indexes.

## Commerce, economics and rights acceptance

- payment/webhook/order/entitlement state by active commerce platform;
- signature/idempotency/replay/dead-letter tests;
- protected fulfillment and refund/dispute reconciliation;
- rights-hold and takedown/correction workflow;
- DLA/license/product/entitlement states;
- economic schedules versioned separately from equity/ownership;
- chain of title and AI/provenance fields;
- no historical price or legal offer presented as active without verification.

- API/MCP server/tool registry;

- migration records for earlier MCP revisions and deprecated transport/session behaviors;
- stateless request handling, header-based routing controls, optional discovery, MRTR continuation evidence and cache-staleness tests where the current profile is used;
- verified environment/base URL/auth/scopes where available;
- common request/error/event contracts;
- sandbox/test identities/data/webhooks;
- generated or synchronized docs/SDKs where schemas exist;
- tool side effects/autonomy/approval declarations;
- platform adapter certification and health;
- provider/version drift detection;
- private operations tools separated from public/developer tools;
- contributor/API/SDK/MCP/security terms prepared.

## Search and public-claim acceptance

- canonical host/route/redirect graph;
- production/staging robots controls;
- sitemap generation and validation;
- structured data profiles;
- dynamic claims/metrics sourced from records;
- private/token/admin routes excluded;
- duplicate/thin/programmatic pages controlled;
- accessibility and link validation;

## AI/ML/agent acceptance

- use-case/model/provider/prompt/data/evaluation records;
- provider data-use/privacy/IP review;
- prompt injection and data-exfiltration tests;
- source fidelity, rights, role and cultural evaluations;
- high-consequence human-review thresholds;
- cost/latency/failure monitoring;
- kill switch and fallback;
- no broad autonomous production until at least one lower-risk workflow passes end to end.

## Explicitly permitted unresolved items

Phase 3 may start with records marked `research`, `proposed`, `legal_review_required`, `unverified`, `implementation_gated` or `source_not_located` if services cannot promote them silently.

Examples:

- HoldCo formation;
- MM Suites franchise/regional legal activation;
- patent/trademark filings not yet pursued or verified;
- universal SSO;
- tokens, DAO, dedicated chain, advanced settlement;
- Purple/Silver/Orange/Yellow papers not yet located;
- long-tail platforms not yet selected for a cohort.

## Absolute no-go conditions

- public repository contains production secrets or restricted evidence;
- no tested backup/restore or rollback for critical records;
- unresolved rights/authority can be treated as permission;
- agents can self-approve material actions;
- economic schedules or money destinations are mutable without version/authority;
- private retrieval depends on post-generation redaction;
- APIs/vendors are represented as integrated without inspection/evidence;
- historical patent/franchise/equity/token/smart-contract language is presented as current;
- cross-platform data moves without purpose, fields, policy and failure behavior;
- checkout can collect money without verified fulfillment/support;
- a material code/config/site/API/product/rights change can be released with neither a synchronized documentation update, a reviewed no-impact declaration nor a governed documentation delta;
- source-code repositories are assumed absent merely because they are not connected to the documentation workflow;
- a deployment/build artifact is reconstructed or force-pushed as authoritative editable source history;
- an unresolved universe, room, character, right, price, provider or account state is filled by naming similarity or arithmetic convenience;
- provider-documented capability is represented as CrownThrive account entitlement or production integration without authenticated evidence;
- historical and current price/license schedules can both resolve as active defaults without an effective-period/supersession decision;
- a platform has no governed changelog identity or documentation-impact owner for material changes;
- correction history can be erased.

## Entry decision

Phase 3 is a **go for controlled Phase 3.0 engineering** after the Phase 2.98 and 2.99 deliverables are accepted. Broad autonomous execution remains no-go until the applicable Phase 3A–3D service acceptance tests are proven.

No future discovery should reset these controls. Newly recovered information enters through the versioning/correction/source process.
