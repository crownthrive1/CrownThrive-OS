- remove ownership, attribution, provenance, licensing, rights, or machine-use notices;
- represent a fork, adaptation, integration, model output, or third-party implementation as CrownThrive-approved, CrownThrive-certified, CHLOM-certified, or CIE-certified.

## 3. AI, ML, LLM, retrieval, embedding and agent use

To the maximum extent permitted by applicable law, no permission is granted by public access alone to use protected CrownThrive Material for commercial or research model training, fine-tuning, continued pretraining, distillation, reinforcement learning, synthetic-data generation, prompt-library reconstruction, evaluation-corpus reconstruction, embeddings/vector corpora that substitute for CrownThrive documentation, or systematic extraction of proprietary institutional knowledge.

A separate machine/data license should identify at least: authorized corpus and versions, models/systems, purpose, retention, training status, derivative-output rules, security, attribution, sublicensing, economics, audit, deletion/cessation, and restrictions on reconstruction of protected methods.

Ordinary search indexing, accessibility processing, security research, fair use/fair dealing, and other rights that cannot lawfully be restricted remain subject to applicable law.

## 4. Automated agents and bulk extraction

Automated access does not create broader permission than human access. Unless expressly authorized, no permission is granted for bulk scraping, mirroring, dataset reconstruction, autonomous republication, high-volume harvesting, systematic prompt/schema extraction, or agent workflows designed to clone protected CrownThrive architectures, catalogs, policies, creative corpora, business methods, or institutional systems.

Rate-limit acceptance, successful HTTP responses, public APIs, robots behavior, Git cloning capability, or provider tooling are technical conditions—not a CrownThrive rights grant.

## 5. Reverse engineering and circumvention

Except where applicable law creates a non-waivable right, no permission is granted to bypass access controls, reconstruct confidential methods through systematic behavioral probing, extract private prompts or model policies, decompile protected implementation artifacts, evade usage controls, defeat evidence/licensing gates, bypass provider-write certification, or reconstruct private economic, security, governance, or evaluation logic.
