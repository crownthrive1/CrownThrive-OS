# One ecosystem. One source of truth. Many execution lanes.

CrownThrive Operating System documents how CrownThrive’s brands, platforms, intellectual property, commerce rails, media corridors, governance frameworks, and automation team work together.

It is designed for founders, operators, collaborators, developers, publishing teams, rights managers, support teams, and approved partners who need to understand **what exists, who owns the work, what may move forward, and which approval gates apply**.

<Note>
  This is the public operating map. Secrets, customer records, unpublished source assets, protected identities, private CHLOM logic, confidential partner terms, and production credentials do not belong here.
</Note>

## The operating model

CrownThrive operates through five connected layers:

1. **Experience** — public-facing brands, cultural properties, media, education, community, and story worlds.
2. **Commerce** — Stripe Direct, Good Shit Only, subscriptions, credits, services, sponsorship, and licensing.
3. **Operations** — publishing, fulfillment, product registries, support, analytics, release management, and quality assurance.
4. **Governance** — CHLOM, the Cultural Imprint Engine, rights records, canon, privacy, security, accessibility, and public-claim controls.
5. **Automation** — Codex, Agents SDK, MCP tools, workflow triggers, approvals, evaluations, and audit logs.

Phase 1 establishes the public operating foundation:

- CrownThrive overview and governing principles
- ecosystem and platform map

- commerce rails and product states
- governance and AI-provenance rules
- automation team, permissions, and human approval gates
- initial operational workflows
- initial deployment, payment, and rights-correction runbooks

## Source-of-truth hierarchy

When records disagree, use this order:

1. Signed agreements and governing legal records
2. Rights Ledger, CHLOM, and approved canon records
3. Canonical platform and product registries
4. Verified production state and external processor state
5. Approved public documentation
6. Marketing copy and public summaries

Marketing language never overrides a rights record, payment state, entitlement state, security control, or release gate.
