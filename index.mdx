## Operating model

CrownThrive's public operating model is organized around three connected layers:

- **Culture + meaning:** CIE protects narrative coherence, representation, canon, context, and responsible reuse.
- **Governance + rights:** CHLOM governs rights, rules, roles, evidence, licensing, records, and remedies.
- **Execution + continuity:** Penta coordinates bounded execution, certification, maintenance, release, and continuity.
