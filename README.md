## PENTA: the institutional operating doctrine

The word **penta** derives from the Greek word for **five**. CrownThrive uses it as a deliberate systems doctrine rather than a decorative prefix.

Every institutional capability should ultimately participate in five functions:

### 1. Discover

Establish what exists and what is true: assets, providers, identities, repositories, content, evidence, participants, contracts, schemas, endpoints, dependencies, versions, health, risks, opportunities, and gaps.

Discovery does not create authority. A discovered API is not automatically a certified write path. A discovered document is not automatically current canon. A discovered provider feature is not automatically configured or licensed for CrownThrive.

### 2. Govern

Determine what may happen, under whose authority, with what rights, evidence, credentials, risk ceiling, cultural constraints, licensing conditions, security requirements, and approval rules.

CHLOM is the primary governance architecture for rights, rules, roles, revenue, records, and remedies. CIE governs cultural meaning and imprint coherence within its scope. Vault, identity, certification, and evidence systems preserve the distinction between capability and authority.

### 3. Execute

Perform the authorized work through durable software: PentaRoute, PentaTun, Penta primitives, factories, schedulers, queues, provider-native adapters, internal services, publication systems, media systems, commercial rails, and governed automation.

Execution must be bounded. The existence of PentaDelete does not mean universal delete. The existence of a factory does not mean every generated asset is approved. A deployed endpoint does not automatically gain D3 authority.

### 4. Verify

Prove what happened. Verification uses provider readback, read-after-write checks, receipts, hashes, signatures, canaries, tests, heartbeats, reconciliation, release evidence, security checks, and independent proof surfaces.

A request being sent is not proof that the desired state exists.

### 5. Preserve

Retain enough institutional truth for audit, recovery, succession, portability, seven-generation continuity, rights custody, future automation, historical understanding, and provider exit.

Preservation includes archives, versions, provenance, receipts, stable identities, knowledge, executable runbooks, continuity packages, and the context needed for a future participant to continue the work correctly.

## The canonical five-phase roadmap

CrownThrive's institutional phase model is now aligned to PENTA:

| Phase | PENTA stage | Institutional question | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Phase 1** | **Discover** | What exists, what matters, and what is actually true? | Historical / consolidated |
| **Phase 2** | **Govern** | What may happen, under whose authority, and under what conditions? | Historical / consolidated |
| **Phase 3** | **Execute** | Can CrownThrive perform its work through durable governed software? | **Current** |
| **Phase 4** | **Verify** | Can CrownThrive continuously and independently prove correctness, authority, effect, and resilience? | Future |
| **Phase 5** | **Preserve** | Can CrownThrive survive provider, platform, personnel, repository, and generational change without losing itself? | Future |

This is now the only institution-wide phase taxonomy.

Historical Phase 2.5, 2.7, 2.8, 2.9, 2.95, 2.97, 2.98, 2.99, pre-entry convergence, and similar decimal labels are **retired as current roadmap language**. They remain in archive/history when historically accurate and map into the broader **Phase 2 — Govern** generation.

CrownThrive will not create new decimal institutional phases. Precision belongs in release versions, component versions, maturity states, risk classes, certification states, waves, sprints, cohorts, or milestones.

## Current generation: Phase 3 — Execute

Phase 3 is the era in which CrownThrive converts institutional knowledge and governed capability into durable execution.

The objective is not merely to build more software. It is to make the institution increasingly operable without requiring a founder, a ChatGPT session, or a single SaaS dashboard to manually push every recurring action.

Phase 3 therefore emphasizes:

- autonomous but governed execution through ThriveBase;
- PentaRoute/PentaTun routing and runtime primitives;
- PentaBeata liveness and health;
- PentaFramework Factory and CrownThrive Software Factory production;
- provider-native adapters;
- operation-level bounded-write certification;
- repository federation and state reconciliation;
- media, publishing, commerce, documentation, and continuity automation;
- self-discovery of missing adapters and contracts;
- readback, receipts, rollback, and recovery work generation;
- machine-readable institutional doctrine;
- human-readable documentation through PentaDocs;
- preservation of historical truth without letting stale records govern current work.

Phase 3 completion is evidence-based. It is not achieved because the repository says `v3`, because an endpoint exists, or because an agent generated code. Priority functions must be durably executable, bounded by authority, observable, recoverable, and capable of producing the evidence required for Phase 4.

## Phase 4 — Verify

Phase 4 is the planned assurance-first refactoring generation.

The primary question changes from **"Can it run?"** to **"Can the institution independently prove that it ran correctly, under the right authority, produced the intended effect, and remains recoverable?"**

Expected Phase 4 concerns include cross-provider verification, formal conformance, independent readback, fault injection, adversarial testing, security assurance, provider-exit drills, rollback verification, data integrity, economic reconciliation, rights/license integrity, release provenance, certification renewal, observability quality, and resistance to silent drift.

Phase 4 should progressively reduce the distance between operational claims and independently reproducible proof.

## Phase 5 — Preserve

Phase 5 is the planned continuity and inheritance generation.

Its purpose is not to freeze CrownThrive. It is to ensure CrownThrive can change without losing institutional identity, authority, evidence, culture, rights, or operating knowledge.

Phase 5 centers PentaGeneration, seven-generation continuity, archives, succession, knowledge inheritance, durable identifiers, repository/provider portability, rights custody, dependency replacement, provider exit, data migration, recovery packages, and executable institutional memory.

A mature CrownThrive should be able to replace a provider, lose a platform, change personnel, onboard a successor, or reconstruct a subsystem without losing what the institution is or what it is authorized to do.

## Source-of-truth hierarchy

1. **CrownThrive OS canonical records** — institutional phase, current policy, stable identity, effective governance, version lineage, releases, corrections, and archive dispositions.
2. **OS-bound operational evidence** — ThriveBase state, CHLOM/DAIL evidence, certified provider readbacks, exact Git/release receipts, and other evidence explicitly incorporated into the OS.
3. **Component repositories** — CHLOM, CIE, and other component packages are authoritative within their component scope while remaining subordinate to the OS for institution-wide state.
4. **PentaDocs** — public-safe institutional knowledge projection.
5. **Websites, storefronts, media surfaces, and help centers** — downstream projections that may temporarily lag.
6. **Archive/history** — evidence of what was previously true, planned, attempted, or operated; not current merely because it remains accessible.

A downstream projection can create a reconciliation task. It cannot independently promote, demote, or redefine institutional truth.

## Institutional architecture

### CrownThrive OS

The OS is the institutional control and source-of-truth layer. It distinguishes generation, version, maturity, authority, evidence, current state, historical state, and downstream projection.

### CHLOM

**Compliance Hybrid Licensing and Ownership Model** governs Rights, Rules, Roles, Revenue, Records, and Remedies. CHLOM controls capability and authority boundaries; it does not manufacture cultural truth.

### Cultural Imprint Engine (CIE)

CIE governs cultural meaning, narrative continuity, representation, imprint identity, aesthetics, canon/audience constraints, and responsible reuse. It interoperates with CHLOM but does not inherit CHLOM's rights authority.

### ThriveBase

ThriveBase is the durable operational state, workflow, queue, evidence, registry, scheduling, and automation substrate used by CrownThrive's software fabric.

### PENTA execution family

PENTA provides a common human and machine vocabulary for institutional execution. PentaRoute, PentaTun, PentaBeata and the wider primitive family make routing, fetch, query, search, queueing, retry, binding, vaulting, authentication, signing, auditing, testing, deployment, reconciliation, and similar functions explicitly addressable.

### PentaFramework Factory and CrownThrive Software Factory

Factories generate, test, package, bind, and deploy governed software/framework candidates through provider-native adapters. Generation is not acceptance; acceptance is not provider authority; provider authority is not D3 authority.

### Penta Federation

Penta Federation binds systems, identities, events, proofs, routes, and cross-repository/runtime continuity without requiring every subsystem to maintain an independent copy of canonical state.

### PentaDocs

PentaDocs is CrownThrive's institutional knowledge system. It explains the current architecture for humans while projecting machine-readable doctrine and definitions that can be consumed by agents and runtimes.

### PentaGeneration

PentaGeneration is the long-horizon succession, continuity, inheritance, handoff, and seven-generation stewardship layer and becomes increasingly central as CrownThrive approaches Phase 5.

## Maturity is not phase

Institutional phase and component maturity are separate dimensions.

A component in Phase 3 may still be `CANDIDATE`, `CONTROLLED_TEST`, `ACTIVE`, `PRODUCTION`, `WRITE_VERIFIED`, `RESERVE`, `HISTORICAL`, `SUPERSEDED`, or `RETIRED`.

Similarly, a v1 or v2 API can remain the correct active component contract during OS 3.x. The PENTA phase refactor does not force-renumber every schema, function, API, release, migration, or protocol.

## Authority is not phase

A higher phase never creates more legal, economic, provider, or destructive authority by itself.

No phase label, documentation page, AI output, generated artifact, successful HTTP response, repository merge, deployment, payment link, or provider capability independently creates ownership, licensing rights, D3 authority, entitlement, settlement, or universal mutation authority.
