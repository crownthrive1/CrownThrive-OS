## 🚦 Source promotion is not Mintlify reachability

A source candidate that satisfies its **exact-head required documentation, security, governed-merge, authority, and applicable specialist gates** may proceed through the governed source merge path even if Mintlify is temporarily login-gated, anonymously unreachable, not indexed, awaiting propagation, or missing a custom domain.

Those conditions remain important evidence, but they govern **projection/deployment claims**, not source acceptance. They can block statements such as “publicly deployed,” “indexed,” or “canonical host live.” They do not, by themselves, turn a governance-passed source candidate into a merge HOLD.

This does **not** mean “green CI merges everything.” A separate explicit rights, legal, security, economic, destructive-action, independent-verification, authority, or draft/HOLD gate still controls when applicable.

### CHLOM

CHLOM is CrownThrive's metaprotocol and governance layer for authority, rights, evidence, lifecycle, remediation, machine contracts, licensing, and institutional continuity. Recovered historical CHLOM material is preserved with current-state overlays rather than silently promoted into current doctrine or production state.

### ThriveBase

ThriveBase is CrownThrive's institutional state/control-plane layer. This public repository may document public-safe contracts, IDs, schemas, evidence references, and controlled-test readbacks, but **never** serves as the secret store, private evidence vault, privileged runtime, or credential source.

### ThriveEvergreen™

ThriveEvergreen is the governed Commerce & Economic Activation Authority under CHLOM. It binds products, SKUs, pricing, licensing, Stripe/payment, fulfillment, entitlement, advertising, settlement, and reconciliation into an `ECAC | HOLD | DENY` lifecycle. Provider success is evidence, not automatic economic truth.

### Cultural Imprint Engine

The Cultural Imprint Engine governs cultural/contextual integrity across CrownThrive's platforms, media, imprints, universes, education, community, and commercial expressions. It remains distinct from legal authority, rights ownership, payment state, and provider execution.

## 🤖 AI, ML, agents, APIs, and MCP

CrownThrive treats AI/ML/LLM and agent systems as governed software operators, not as independent institutional authority.

Machine interfaces must preserve:

- stable identity and versioning;
- explicit tool side effects and autonomy class;
- least privilege and audience/data classification;
- source/evidence traceability;
- builder/verifier separation for consequential work;
- DAIL/audit evidence;
- bounded retry, compensation, rollback, quarantine, and fail-closed behavior;
- model/provider/prompt/evaluation lineage;
- public/private projection boundaries.

A model vote, confidence score, workflow success, provider `2xx` response, or generated artifact cannot manufacture rights, legal authority, economic authority, production certification, or founder-reserved permission.
