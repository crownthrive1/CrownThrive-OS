## PentaFactory™ Parallel Precompile

PentaFactory™ may prepare multiple framework packages in parallel when doing so reduces idle time without changing institutional authority.

The durable rule from the predecessor Framework Factory work is retained:

> **Parallelize preparation. Serialize promotion. Never manufacture authority.**

## Work that may proceed in parallel

Within an authorized request/backlog item, PentaFactory/PentaBuild may perform bounded source discovery, scaffolding, contract compilation, skill generation, tests/evals, documentation, packaging, compatibility analysis, and gap detection for multiple candidate frameworks.

A `precompile_ready` result means the candidate preparation packet passed its precompile contract. It does not mean the framework is activated, canonical, economically effective, customer-entitled, provider-write enabled, voting, or production-certified.

## Promotion is current-state driven

The August 23 predecessor packet used a fixed framework activation order and Agent-D-era promotion semantics. Those are historical lineage, not the current Phase-3 authority graph.

Current promotion depends on:

- the exact current artifact/head;
- the active Penta/CHLOM dependency graph;
- PentaRFA/PentaSuite authority where an agent/lease is required;
- PentaCertify exact-scope evidence;
- current directives/policies/contracts/task orders;
- current security, rights, legal, economic, provider, and rollback gates; and
- D3 human involvement where the current contract reserves D3.

A downstream package may be technically precompiled before an upstream package is promoted. It must remain non-operational until its own current promotion predicates are satisfied.

## Historical seed set

The predecessor precompile included Cultural Imprint Engine, Convergent Ecosystem, Thrive Flywheel, CHLOM, Corridor Architecture, Hybrid Incubator, MM Suites, and One Seat, Multiple Industries. Those are preserved as an August 23 seed cohort, not a frozen current roadmap or mandatory activation order.

## Hard boundaries

Parallel precompile does not create a new scheduler slot, vote, self-approval, provider write, money movement, rights grant, checkout activation, or D3 authority. PentaFactory prepares; PentaCertify certifies exact scope; the current governance chain determines whether promotion is allowed.
