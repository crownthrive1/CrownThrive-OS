# CrownThrive Pentafabric™

CrownThrive Pentafabric™ is the five-layer architecture that explains how CrownThrive OS and CHLOM coordinate identity, licensing, evidence, software execution, and economic activation across a heterogeneous ecosystem.

It is a metaprotocol architecture, not a claim that CrownThrive operates one blockchain, one database, one API, or one monolithic application.

## The five layers

### 1. Identity & Imprint

Establishes stable subject and asset identity, DIDs or equivalent references, CHLOM Fingerprint references, provenance, custody, authorship/ownership evidence, organizational identity, agent identity, and Cultural Imprint Engine context.

### 2. Rights & Conditions

Expresses rights, rules, roles, Dynamic Licensing Assets, Licensing Stewardship / Issuer Authority, contracts, conditions, approvals, restrictions, eligibility, field of use, territory, revocation, and remedies.

### 3. Ledger & Assurance

Uses DAIL — Decentralized Autonomous Information Ledger — plus evidence, hashes, signatures, attestations, audit history, compliance oracles, independent verification, disputes, holds, corrections, appeals, and restoration.

### 4. Execution & Interoperability

Materializes governed intent as pallets, modules, containers, components, scaffolds, wireframes, portals, dashboards, widgets, frameworks, plugins, skills, APIs, MCPs, SDKs, webhooks, agents, factories, documentation, website mesh surfaces, and provider adapters.

### 5. Economy & Distribution

Packages already-authorized capabilities through ThriveEvergreen / ECAC into CrownThrive SKUs, access tiers, pricing, subscriptions, entitlements, payments, Crown Credits, royalties, commissions, partner distribution, developer licensing, enterprise licensing, institutional licensing, OEM/white-label arrangements, and revenue reconciliation.

## Architectural invariant

**Execution may optimize within authority. It may never manufacture authority.**

That invariant means a model, provider, agent, workflow, API, MCP tool, repository, payment rail, or deployment can supply evidence or perform a bounded action but cannot independently create ownership, licensing, certification, economic authority, or settlement truth.

## Relationship to CHLOM

CHLOM is the governing metaprotocol across Pentafabric. It provides interoperable contracts for Rights, Rules, Roles, Revenue, Records, and Remedies and coordinates the handoffs between the five layers.

The architecture is deliberately layered so that CrownThrive can replace a database, provider, chain, model, payment rail, website, or implementation framework without losing institutional identity, rights, evidence, or economic control.

## Relationship to CIE

The Cultural Imprint Engine contributes cultural, narrative, lineage, and imprint context primarily at Layer 1 and can influence policy/eligibility interpretation through governed contracts. CIE does not replace CHLOM rights or DAIL evidence.

## Relationship to ThriveEvergreen

ThriveEvergreen is Layer 5 economic activation authority. It converts governed, evidence-backed capabilities into products, SKUs, access, entitlements, and economic execution after required authority exists.

## Public and protected forms

The public Pentafabric specification explains the topology, interfaces, identities, and authority boundaries. Private prompts, protected algorithms, private scoring, credentials, private economic logic, customer data, sensitive evidence, and restricted implementation bodies remain vault-bounded.

## Commercialization

Pentafabric can be packaged as a CrownThrive framework for architecture consulting, integration, licensing systems, compliance/evidence infrastructure, multi-platform orchestration, agent governance, enterprise deployment, and developer tooling. Commercial implementation rights remain separately licensed.
