## Objective and scope

Carry the Phase 4 entry requirements through the existing governed execution system. This runbook explains the inherited G00–G21 gate set; it does not replace the canonical contract or certify Phase 4.

The institutional lifecycle remains Phase 1 Discover, Phase 2 Govern, Phase 3 Execute, Phase 4 Verify and Phase 5 Preserve. Phase 3 remains the current institutional phase until the release evidence establishes otherwise. Earlier phase obligations remain inherited.

The acceptance target is fully hands-off routine operation across the complete frozen estate, with more than 500,000 meaningful verified entries per rolling 24 hours and no degradation against approved, frozen service objectives. A queue threshold, synthetic test or endpoint counter cannot establish sustained production capacity.

## Authority and preconditions

PentaCertifier independently issues no-go findings and their closure predicates. PentaSuper is the sole accountable delegate for Phase 4 remediation. It may draft, build and enlist helper Pentas under unique identities, explicit strict-subset authority, resource ceilings, expiring leases and revocation. A helper cannot inherit a vote, certify its own change or broaden its parent's scope.

PentaDND, including the PentaDnD spelling, is the scoped-maintenance service. It protects the affected branch, workstream or runtime boundary while unrelated authorized work continues. Existing ownership and leases must be resolved before mutation.

Use the current canonical supervisor cursor and existing scheduler routes. A new duty does not justify a duplicate clock or competing supervisor. Routine automation does not create authority for reserved actions.

## Inputs and protected-data boundary

Resolve the exact candidate, dependency graph, full inventory, policy version, frozen service objectives, identity records, maintenance scope, work assignment, rollback plan and authenticated evidence index.

Keep private routing identifiers, credentials, customer records, protected source and internal incident evidence in their authorized stores. Public Support and PentaDocs projections contain approved descriptions and safe access routes. Full private files remain access controlled, with their custody accounted for in the internal inventory.

Preserve stable asset IDs and versions across the master registry, product package, Drive mirror, Support projection and PentaDocs projection. A catalog entry proves discovery; delivery requires its own readback.

## Procedure

1. Read the canonical phase contract, current cursor, exact owner assignment and live maintenance scopes.
2. Reconcile every inherited phase obligation against the complete estate and asset inventory. Missing inventory is a gate failure.
3. Let PentaCertifier record each finding with the affected scope, evidence, exact closure predicate and review role.
4. Route remediation to PentaSuper. Record any helper's scope and ceiling before execution.
5. Apply only the bounded repair, preserve rollback evidence and perform provider read-after-write verification.
6. Reconcile source, deployed effect, registry, custody and public projections for each changed asset.
7. Require independent closure evidence for every pre-soak gate (G00–G18) before recording an exact-candidate proposed PASS; G19–G21 follow in order.
8. Evaluate the subsequent continuous seven-day interval against the frozen policy. Release only after fresh independent evidence and the canonical final GO.

## Verification and readback

Each PASS must resolve to current evidence for the exact candidate and scope. Verify source lineage, provider effect, measurement interval, evidence completeness, trusted timestamps, mirror watermark and reviewer independence.

For capacity, freeze the definition of a meaningful verified entry and deduplication rules. Evaluate the complete rolling 24-hour series across the required interval, including latency, errors, backlog and resource health against the approved objectives. Missing observations hold the gate.

For websites, record every owned site, its canonical source, update adapter, event route, scheduled fallback, freshness objective and public readback. Include Sites-managed properties. Where an API or provider-native scheduler is unavailable, use an already authorized external route with verified execution; an unavailable route remains an explicit gap. Provider reachability and editor access do not prove automatic publication.

For products, check source, rights disposition, formats, exact price, checkout where applicable, webhook, entitlement, delivery and custody. Phase coverage includes historical and current phase material with clear labels.

## Failure and rollback

A relevant regression, missing observation, stale evidence, changed candidate or changed in-scope dependency invalidates the fatigue epoch. Preserve the invalidated epoch and its findings in the daily append-only record; remediate and establish a new proposed PASS when the complete gate set is eligible again.

Unrelated changes outside the frozen dependency scope do not silently rewrite or reset that candidate. Never backdate a proposed PASS or convert synthetic elapsed time into production elapsed time.

On a failed mutation, stop the affected scope, invoke its approved rollback and verify restored behavior. A failed mirror or publication route remains a recorded gap until actual readback closes it.

## Evidence and closure

Bind the proposed PASS to the exact candidate, inventory, policy, service objectives, evidence root and trusted start time. Preserve the decision immutably in DAIL and the daily continuity record.

The elapsed duration must be at least 604,800 continuous seconds after that recorded proposed PASS. Seven dated reports alone are insufficient if coverage has gaps or the candidate has changed.

Closure includes the independent decision, exact source and runtime receipts, all required publication and custody readbacks, economic reconciliation, restoration evidence and the final release authorization. A task completion message or a book about the process does not release the phase.

## Escalation

PentaCertifier routes no-go findings and closure conditions to PentaSuper through the existing governed work system. PentaSuper remains accountable when helpers perform bounded portions of a repair. The canonical supervisor retains continuity and release responsibilities.

Escalate reserved decisions through their established authority route. Record blocked provider capability, unavailable evidence and unresolved ownership as exact findings. Continue unrelated authorized work while the affected gate remains on hold.
