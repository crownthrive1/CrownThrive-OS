# Rights hold and content correction

Use this runbook when ownership, permission, identity, portrait, voice, attribution, chronology, protected information, AI provenance, or public claims may be materially wrong.

## Immediate containment

Depending on severity:

- pause the affected product, listing, page, broadcast, ad, license, or distribution route
- preserve the exact public and internal version
- prevent automated propagation into additional products
- stop new rights grants involving the disputed asset
- preserve transaction and sales evidence when customers or licensees may be affected

Do not delete the disputed record before preserving it.

## Open a correction case

Record:

- correction or hold ID
- reporting party
- affected asset and version
- disputed claim, identity, right, or use
- first known publication
- platforms and products affected
- current commerce and license states
- protected-data implications
- responsible owner
- severity and required approvers

## Four-column review

Separate:

| Column | Question |
| --- | --- |
| Fact | What can be supported by the source record? |
| Meaning | What interpretation or narrative was attached to the fact? |
| Permission | Which uses were actually authorized? |
| Protection | What must remain private, sealed, minimized, or withheld? |

Do not repair one column by silently changing another.

## Evidence preservation

Preserve:

- source files and checksums
- existing product and page versions
- prior captions, metadata, and search snippets
- releases and permission records
- asset and portrait IDs
- contracts and rights records
- affected transactions, licensees, or partners
- relevant system and approval logs

## Review

The Rights and Governance Agent prepares a report addressing:

- canonical identity
- ownership and contributor status
- portrait, voice, or likeness mapping
- AI and source provenance
- chronology and context
- scope of original permission
- affected rights classes
- required public and private corrections
- customer, creator, partner, or descendant impact

Human review is mandatory for material identity, protected-person, rights, legal, or high-value licensing issues.

## Correction decision

Possible decisions:

- no correction required
- metadata correction
- contextual clarification
- attribution correction
- portrait or voice replacement
- rights-scope reduction
- listing or product suspension
- product rebuild
- license amendment
- public correction notice
- recall or deactivation
- permanent rights hold

## Implementation plan

Create a claim-by-claim and system-by-system schedule:

- canonical registry
- source and edition record
- product files
- covers and previews
- product pages
- search and sitemap
- metadata and structured data
- Backroad FM or media assets
- Good Shit Only rights listing
- hosted-reader edition
- customer library and entitlement display
- press and licensing decks
- active licensees or partners
- public correction notice

## Versioning

The corrected version must identify:

- new version
- prior version
- date
- correction scope
- approval
- whether prior copies remain current, legacy, superseded, recalled, or restricted

Do not silently replace the original without a change record.

## Customer and licensee handling

Determine whether affected buyers or licensees require:

- corrected download
- updated reader access
- revised rights terms
- replacement asset
- notice
- refund or credit
- license amendment or termination

The response depends on materiality, contract, product policy, and actual customer impact.

## Public communication

A public correction should be:

- specific about what changed
- careful not to expose protected sources or identities
- clear about what did not change
- dated
- connected to the current version
- free from defensive overstatement

## Re-release gate

Release the hold only when:

- the canonical record is corrected
- required rights and permissions are confirmed
- affected products and platforms are reconciled
- public copy is accurate
- customer and licensee obligations are addressed
- QA passes
- approvals are recorded

## Prevention

Add or improve:

- identity and portrait registry checks
- source and version validation
- rights-scope validation
- contributor and provenance fields
- public-claim tests
- agent evaluations
- cross-platform change propagation
- license and product dependency mapping

<Warning>
  A correction may humanize, contextualize, or narrow a claim without erasing other established responsibility. Correct the specific record; do not convert correction into acquittal or spectacle.
</Warning>
