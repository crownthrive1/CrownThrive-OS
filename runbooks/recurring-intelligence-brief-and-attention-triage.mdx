# Recurring intelligence brief and attention triage

Use this runbook for recurring market, platform, inbox, incident, or ecosystem briefings where the system must identify consequential change without flooding an operator with low-value alerts.

## Objective

Deliver a small number of evidence-backed items that materially affect a decision, risk, deadline, release, provider, customer, license, or operating plan.

## Triage states

- `SUCCESS` - expected state; no human action required;
- `WATCH` - meaningful change or approaching threshold; internal follow-up may be useful;
- `ACTION_NEEDED` - a specific human decision/approval/action is required;
- `FAILURE` - a current failure requires incident handling.

Do not use `ACTION_NEEDED` merely because an internal agent has more work to do.

## Briefing sequence

1. Resolve the reporting window and current baseline.
2. Search approved current sources.
3. Deduplicate repeated reports and recycled headlines.
4. Separate direct evidence from commentary or inference.
5. Rank by consequence, urgency, reversibility, and affected scope.
6. Map each material item to an owner and next safe action.
7. Preserve uncertainty and source unavailability.
8. Produce a concise brief with material changes first.
9. Suppress notification when a condition-watch has no meaningful delta.

## Materiality filters

Prioritize items that change:

- security or provider reliability;
- legal/licensing/rights posture;
- revenue or checkout capability;
- software support/EOL or API compatibility;
- domain/renewal/continuity risk;
- customer fulfillment or entitlement integrity;
- roadmap gate status;
- high-value market opportunity with a concrete operating implication.

Deprioritize:

- duplicated coverage;
- generic industry commentary with no operational effect;
- vanity metrics without a decision implication;
- speculative claims unsupported by current sources.

## Privacy and sensitive-context boundary

A private workflow may use account-specific email, calendar, legal, financial, family, health, customer, or partner context. Commercial runbooks must generalize the method and use synthetic examples. Do not export the source subject matter, names, addresses, attachments, case facts, or personal schedules into the product.

## Output envelope

```yaml
window: daily
status: WATCH
material_changes:
  - claim: example provider version changed
    evidence: first_party
    consequence: compatibility review
    human_action_required: false
unknowns: []
suppressed_noise_count: 12
next_safe_action: open bounded internal review
```

## Verification checklist

- [ ] Reporting window is explicit.
- [ ] Current sources were used.
- [ ] Duplicates/noise were suppressed.
- [ ] Human-facing status reflects actual human action need.
- [ ] Evidence and inference are separated.
- [ ] Sensitive source context is excluded from commercial derivatives.
- [ ] Unknowns remain explicit.
- [ ] Next action is bounded and owner-routed.

## Commercialization state

Public-safe developer pattern candidate. Private inbox content, personal case information, customer/partner data, proprietary alert thresholds, and restricted intelligence sources remain excluded.
