## Email scam, opt-out and escalation runbook

Use this runbook whenever a CrownThrive sales/support mailbox receives a message that may be fraudulent, commercially unsafe, legally material, sensitive, or subject to an opt-out.

## 1. Classify the reason for contact first

Do not start with the sender’s requested action. Determine the actual category of the message:

- lead / licensing / sponsorship / partnership;
- support / vendor / account;
- legal / court;
- financial / transaction;
- personal / sensitive;
- security / credential;
- spam / solicitation;
- suspected scam / phishing.

## 2. Preserve before acting

Preserve the source provider message/thread identifier and material metadata before replying, suppressing or escalating. Private email bodies and sensitive evidence stay in restricted storage, not public docs.

## 3. Scam/phishing review

Check the combined evidence:

- sender and reply-to mismatch;
- non-corroborated business identity;
- generic or copied solicitation language;
- malformed template placeholders;
- unexplained urgency;
- refusal to answer normal business questions;
- unexplained external forms or login pages;
- credential, MFA, tax, identity, bank, wallet or payment-detail requests;
- unexpected attachments;
- unusual payment instruments or overpayment/refund patterns;
- domain/link mismatch;
- request to bypass an established CrownThrive/Stripe/provider workflow.

A single external form or free email provider is not automatically fraudulent. Context and corroboration govern the decision.

### If risk is high

```text
Preserve
→ tag risk
→ suppress automatic reply
→ do not click/open suspicious content
→ archive evidence privately
→ escalate when material
```

Do not send a “prove you are legitimate” message that discloses internal controls, banking information, tax documents, credentials or identity documents.

## 4. Opt-out handling

Any clear request to stop marketing must be treated as an opt-out even when the sender does not use a specific keyword.

Examples:

- unsubscribe;
- opt out;
- remove me;
- stop emailing;
- do not contact me;
- wrong person, when continuing would be unreasonable.

Actions:

1. enter suppression immediately;
2. stop all pending marketing touches;
3. remove the address from active marketing audiences where connected;
4. preserve the event and source;
5. do not re-enroll automatically from another campaign, import or agent.

## 5. Founder-priority escalation

### P0 — Legal / Court

Escalate court notices, subpoenas, hearings, attorney communications, filings, formal demands, deadlines and rights disputes. Agents may summarize and prepare; they do not make legal determinations or send binding legal responses without authority.

### P0 — Financial / Transaction

Escalate material wire/bank changes, unusual payouts, tax issues, disputed payment instructions, high-value exceptions, settlement/payment requests and suspected transaction fraud. Ordinary receipts and already-governed low-risk reconciliation remain operational.

### P0 — Personal / Sensitive

Escalate correspondence whose subject is personal, family, medical, child-related, highly confidential or otherwise inappropriate for routine business automation.

### P0 — Founder Escalation

Use when human action, signature, approval, credential/provider action or other reserved decision is actually required. Do not use ACTION NEEDED merely because internal agents still have work to complete.

## 6. Binding commitment boundary

Agents may qualify, nurture, prepare a proposal, ask commercial questions and negotiate inside already-governed terms.

Agents may not autonomously:

- sign or accept a contract;
- grant final commercial rights;
- agree to exclusivity;
- change material bank/wire instructions;
- disclose production secrets/credentials;
- accept ungoverned economic terms;
- make a legal admission;
- waive a material right;
- delete evidence or suppression history.

## 7. Recovery from false positive

A risk classification is reversible.

If independent evidence later verifies the sender and business purpose:

1. retain the original risk event;
2. append the verification evidence;
3. remove the do-not-reply state only through an authorized review;
4. do not erase the original classification;
5. resume only the next appropriate workflow step.

## 8. Negative evaluation case

The August 21, 2026 Virality Music licensing lead is retained privately as an evaluation case because a follow-up message combined malformed placeholder text, failure to answer normal licensing questions and redirection to an unexplained external form.

Public documentation records only the pattern. Private provider IDs and message content remain outside this repository.
