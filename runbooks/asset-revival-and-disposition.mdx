# Asset Revival & Disposition Runbook

## Mission

Convert historical CrownThrive knowledge into a disciplined portfolio decision without losing history or manufacturing current state.

This runbook is used with the [Revival Doctrine](/doctrine/revival-doctrine), [Asset Resurrection & Disposition Registry](/portfolio/asset-resurrection-disposition-registry), source authority hierarchy, contradiction ledger and current-state validation queue.

## Step 1 — preserve the source

For each asset:

1. register every known source edition;
2. retain original title, effective era, checksum/version when available and source classification;
3. preserve duplicate/corroborating copies without treating them as independent authority;
4. retain raw restricted source outside public Mintlify when required;
5. never rewrite the historical source to match the current interpretation.

## Step 2 — extract the real invention

Classify what the source actually contains:

- brand/name/IP;
- user problem;
- workflow requirement;
- technical component;
- governance/control;
- data/event model;
- content/canon/production format;
- commerce or economic concept;
- legal/rights concept;
- operating cadence/runbook;
- partner/provider dependency;
- research/future architecture.

The goal is to separate **reusable IP** from an obsolete implementation.

## Step 3 — reconcile current successors and overlap

Search current registries, public sites, source repositories, providers and institutional decisions for:

- canonical successor;
- alias/predecessor relationship;
- duplicate capability;
- current domain/route;
- current provider or engine;
- current owner/authority;
- existing current product that already performs the same job.

Do not create a second platform merely because the historical source used a distinct name.

## Step 4 — research the current best use case

When the asset is still useful:

- collect current CrownThrive collateral;
- research the current technology/standard/provider architecture appropriate to the asset type;
- identify security, privacy, legal, rights, accessibility, regional/localization and economic constraints;
- prefer interoperable, recoverable, observable and replaceable implementation patterns;
- preserve public-safe templates/reference code when useful and authorized.

Research does not authorize deployment.

## Step 5 — select exactly one disposition

`ACTIVATE | REVIVE | MERGE | MIGRATE | RESEARCH | RESERVE | LICENSE | ARCHIVE | SUPERSEDE | SUNSET`

Record the reason and reopening trigger. `UNKNOWN` may remain in evidence fields, but the portfolio-management decision itself must be explicit once sufficient authority exists.

## Step 6 — derive the acceptance cell

Examples:

- legal/contract/franchise/regulated-service implications → legal/regulatory specialist;
- rights/IP/creative licensing → IP/rights/licensing specialist;
- money/compensation/tax/settlement → finance/tax/treasury specialist;
- cryptography/blockchain/ZK/token/DAO → blockchain/cryptographic-protocol specialist;
- customer-facing experience → accessibility/consumer-protection specialist;
- international/regional use → localization/regional specialist;
- secrets, auth, data or code → security/privacy/technical specialist.

D2/D3 or otherwise material changes also require independent governance/quorum. The implementing/originating agent does not self-vote.

## Step 7 — execute by disposition

### REVIVE

Build a new current artifact from current evidence and technology. Preserve the historical source as lineage. Do not pretend the new artifact is the old one restored verbatim.

### MERGE

Move reusable requirements/IP into the accepted successor. Preserve alias/predecessor links and redirect/canonical rules where appropriate.

### MIGRATE

Define source → target mapping, data/export/rights/contract continuity, rollback and shutdown criteria.

### RESEARCH

Keep the concept in the R&D register with hypotheses, maturity, evidence gaps and promotion conditions. Do not advertise as deployed capability.

### RESERVE

Preserve name, domain/IP/source and potential reopening trigger without operational burden.

### LICENSE

Define rights, scope, territory, term, restrictions, consideration, enforcement and evidence before any external grant.

### ARCHIVE / SUPERSEDE / SUNSET

Keep historical material and surviving obligations visible. Remove current-product implication; preserve successor, redirects, data/export and legal/support obligations where applicable.

### ACTIVATE

Requires a current implementation plus the appropriate release/operating acceptance packet. A historical source or founder intent alone is insufficient.

## Step 8 — update institutional projections

Patch all affected:

- platform/brand directory;
- domains/routes;
- alias/successor registry;
- public portfolio and press room;
- Help Center/current docs;
- legal/support surfaces;
- machine registries;
- roadmap phase dependencies;
- agent/runbook templates;
- per-page changelog/version notes.

Prior pages remain historically accurate; add current-state overlays instead of falsifying the earlier record.

## Step 9 — exact acceptance

Before the disposition becomes canonical:

1. freeze the exact change/head;
2. run documentation/security/schema/negative checks;
3. obtain required specialist reviews;
4. obtain required independent sovereign quorum;
5. record rejected/remaining conflicts;
6. merge only the accepted exact head;
7. retain rollback/supersession point.

## Special rule — requirements archaeology

The old Help Center may define what CrownThrive wanted a capability to do even when the original body, vendor or implementation is obsolete. Convert such material into current requirements only after source classification and current research. A legacy FAQ must never silently become present policy.

## Special rule — Handbook deep pass

The Founder Handbook is treated as a founder-brain source family. Extract its doctrine, operating rhythms, portfolio relationships, governance intent, continuity rules and asset concepts into durable records. Any material current claim about legal authority, ownership, money, privacy/security, rights or production must be independently reconciled before Phase 3.

## Phase-3 exact-snapshot stop condition

Do not permit Phase 3 transition until all Phase 2.99 hard gates plus the full documentation, Handbook and brand/domain/portfolio hard gates are satisfied on one exact canonical snapshot with exact-head CI, required specialists/quorum, rollback/recovery, no unresolved material current-truth contradictions and the approved `A → B → C` Phase 3 opening sequence.
