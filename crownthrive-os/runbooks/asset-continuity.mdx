# Asset Continuity & Institutional Artifact Operations

CrownThrive treats every durable work product as an **asset candidate**. Documents, spreadsheets, presentations, images, source packages, software, websites, media, canon, doctrine, datasets, templates, licenses and generated artifacts enter one institutional lifecycle so they remain findable, recoverable and understandable beyond the current tools or operators.

<Info>
  The canonical row-level authority is the [CrownThrive Master IP & Asset Registry](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1NH2yk1N6qK49zgcBDTbst08Djvh3ypkbHJN_YRyHQPg/edit). Drive, PentaDocs, source repositories, DAIL, providers and commerce systems are linked custody and execution surfaces; they do not silently replace the registry or one another.
</Info>

## Required lifecycle

Every supported asset progresses through these states:

1. **Discover** — search Drive, Library, chats, email evidence, websites, repositories, databases, providers and documentation.
2. **Identify** — resolve one canonical identity while preserving aliases, editions and historical names.
3. **Preserve** — retain or copy the authoritative source and exact provider identifiers.
4. **Classify** — record asset type, maturity, sensitivity, rights, license and economic role.
5. **Rights** — document who created or controls the asset, supporting evidence, permissions, restrictions and unresolved holds.
6. **Package** — create a dossier, manifest, source hashes, README, rights record and deterministic ZIP when useful.
7. **Identity** — record deterministic identity and a DID candidate; a DID is canonical only after registration and readback.
8. **Evidence** — append material lifecycle events to DAIL and read them back; a pending handoff is not completion.
9. **Metadata** — add tags, SEO title, description, AIO summary, schema types, relationships and public-safe descriptions.
10. **Commerce** — preserve verified pricing or assign an explicitly provisional/NFS state; checkout requires full rights, tax, fulfillment, entitlement and recovery predicates.
11. **Publish** — project only the appropriate public-safe information.
12. **Monitor, version and archive** — preserve predecessors, corrections and recovery paths.
13. **Steward** — assign maintenance, rights, canon, commerce, archive and successor responsibility.

## Artifact completion rule

A newly created artifact is not institutionally complete merely because it appeared in a chat or provider response. Before a sweep closes, verify:

- Drive custody and a direct, resolvable link
- Canonical registry row and ID
- Rights/provenance state or a precise hold
- Version and supersession/archive state
- Source or native Workspace link
- Machine metadata and related-asset relationships
- Manifest/package where materially useful
- Honest DID and DAIL state
- PentaDocs/public projection decision
- Commerce disposition: free, provisional, approved, agreement-only or not for sale

## Rights and credential boundaries

<Warning>
  Do not place passwords, API keys, tokens, private keys, webhook secrets, recovery codes or database credentials in Drive documents, Sheets, ZIPs, manifests, PentaDocs or DAIL. Record only approved Vault aliases or credential-reference IDs.
</Warning>

A rights record is an institutional evidence record and drafting instrument. It does not manufacture copyright ownership, trademark registration, exclusivity, chain of title or an executed license.

## Version and correction discipline

- Add, enrich, supersede and archive; never silently overwrite institutional history.
- Exact duplicates may remain as custody evidence but do not become duplicate rights or entitlements.
- New editions, SKUs, license entitlements and materially distinct derivatives receive their own identities.
- Canon corrections preserve the prior record and link it to the corrected current state.
- Search links should be replaced by direct provider IDs as soon as exact readback is available.

## Seven-generation continuity

The continuity horizon uses the founder generation plus seven successor generations, modeled as 25-year stewardship windows. The system must remain reconstructable across provider failure, technology change, corporate restructuring and leadership transition while preserving CrownThrive's mission, cultural alignment, source lineage and evidence discipline.

Successors must be able to:

- Find a named asset and its authoritative source
- Locate rights, license, version and commerce state
- Verify hashes and evidence
- Understand canon, relationships and dependencies
- Recover the operating minimum without exposing credentials
- Add a new artifact without erasing history
- Record a correction with a preserved predecessor

## Sweep acceptance test

Each recurring sweep must record its run ID, sources scanned, cursor, assets discovered, rows added or improved, duplicate/alias decisions, files copied, rights packets, manifests, hashes, DID/DAIL states, metadata, pricing, PentaDocs changes, holds and exact readback.

A sweep may finish `PASS_WITH_SCOPED_HOLDS` when safe progress is complete and unresolved predicates remain explicitly bounded. It may not manufacture a `PASS` from missing evidence.
