## What makes these assets different

The marketplace packages the controls around implementation:

- authority and approval boundaries;
- evidence and status contracts;
- idempotency and negative tests;
- public-safe knowledge projection;
- version lineage, migration, and rollback;
- explicit checkout, entitlement, and fulfillment gates.

Production credentials, customer data, account identifiers, private incident evidence, and secret calibration are excluded.

## Connected architecture

- **Sites** provides the catalog, product detail, and Field Journal.
- **Stripe** remains the canonical product and price system through Stripe-hosted Checkout and Customer Portal.
- **Supabase** provides the private catalog, event receipt, order, entitlement, delivery, and automation control plane.
- **GitHub** remains the change and release authority where authorized.
- **Mintlify** publishes public-safe implementation guidance through governed candidate branches.
- **Google Drive** preserves knowledge masters and founder implementation records.

## Release language

Use `packaged` when the versioned files and integrity manifest exist. Use `checkout_staged` when product and price records exist but paid fulfillment is not proven. Use `live` only when checkout, signed webhook receipt, idempotent entitlement, authorized private delivery, reversal behavior, monitoring, and visible customer terms are proven together.

## Support and licensing

Launch licenses are designed for one purchasing organization’s internal implementation and client-service outputs. Source redistribution, resale as a competing template, credential sharing, and model-training ingestion are outside the baseline license. Final customer terms accompany each certified release.
