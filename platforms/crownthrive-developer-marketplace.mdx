## Closed-loop credit preview

The marketplace is staged around CrownThrive Store Credits. One US dollar is represented as 100 credits for catalog display. Credits are intended only for eligible CrownThrive digital assets, are not cash, are not transferable, and cannot be withdrawn. Those rules are a product-design draft until qualified counsel approves the final terms.

Stripe is the funding rail for hosted top-up Checkout. Supabase Auth binds the customer identity; the CrownThrive application ledger is the balance authority; Supabase owns product eligibility, entitlements, licenses, and protected delivery.

<Warning>
  The credit program, top-ups, offers, test funding, and live funding are disabled. A public catalog, Stripe Product, successful redirect, synthetic row, or packaged ZIP is not proof of payment or authorization to sell.
</Warning>

## Transaction authority

A client redirect never creates credits. The server retrieves the Checkout Session and verifies environment, payment state, amount, currency, quantity, bound price, customer identity, and metadata before appending a credit entry.

A credit purchase is a second, atomic transaction. It locks the account, validates the exact offer and certified asset, debits credits once, and creates one order, entitlement, and versioned license. Private delivery is authorized at request time and uses a short-lived signed URL.

Refund and dispute handling uses compensating ledger entries. Historical ledger entries are preserved rather than edited.

## Connected architecture

- Sites publishes the catalog, readiness register, Field Journal, and signed-out account/license shells.
- Stripe provides hosted top-up Checkout only; it is not the credit ledger or entitlement authority.
- Supabase provides Auth, private storage, catalog gates, credit accounts and ledger, orders, entitlements, licenses, delivery evidence, and bounded reconciliation.
- Google Drive preserves private release packets, source archives, checksums, knowledge masters, and approval records.
- GitHub preserves public-safe continuity and change records; protected source requires a private repository or the existing private release stores.
- Mintlify holds public-safe implementation guidance on governed candidate branches until publication is approved.
- Existing scheduled agents monitor drift and produce candidate assets, but cannot activate commerce or approve law, tax, licenses, or their own certification.

## Activation gates

Live activation requires current evidence for all of the following:

1. counsel-approved credit, terms, privacy, refund, and license language;
2. specialist-approved digital-product/top-up tax treatment and required registrations;
3. tested Supabase Auth identity, recovery, abuse, and privacy controls;
4. least-privilege Stripe and Supabase secret bindings with explicit mode separation;
5. per-asset provenance, dependency, license, deterministic ZIP, checksum, private readback, and fulfillment certification;
6. test-mode top-up, readback, credit, purchase, replay, cross-identity, download, refund, dispute, expiry, and rollback canaries;
7. explicit owner approval for the exact environment after the evidence packet is reviewed.

## Support and licensing

Each certified offer must identify its license class and exact license version. The current baseline proposal is non-transferable internal implementation and client-service output use, with source redistribution and competing-template resale disabled. This is not operative customer language until counsel approves and the owner publishes a versioned agreement.
