# ThriveTickets Institutional Registry

## Identity

- **Stable platform ID:** `ct.platform.thrivetickets`
- **Canonical name:** ThriveTickets
- **Owner/operator:** CrownThrive, LLC
- **Primary role:** governed event discovery, registration, ticket inventory, admission, attendee communication and event-commerce routing.
- **Corridors:** events, education, media, community, hospitality, sponsorship and phygital experiences.
- **Current evidence state:** meaningful public content was retrieved in the August 2026 domain scan. The recovered Help Center contains a ThriveTickets Brand Master Guide and the Legal Depot contains a ThriveTickets Platform-Specific Addendum. The exact current engine, event inventory, processor, repository, organizer accounts, API and live transaction state remain unverified.

ThriveTickets is not the event itself, the venue, a blanket organizer guarantee or a transfer of recording/participation rights. It is one operating rail inside a larger governed event relationship.

## Event and ticket lifecycle

```text
event/organizer intake
→ venue/schedule/rights/safety review
→ ticket types, inventory, prices and fees
→ public or gated sale configuration
→ checkout/payment
→ order and ticket issuance
→ reminders and updates
→ transfer/resale/reassignment where allowed
→ scan/check-in/admission
→ attendance and incident evidence
→ organizer settlement
→ refund, credit, dispute or archive
```

A payment authorization does not prove ticket issuance; a QR image does not prove current entitlement; a scan does not prove identity unless the event requires and verifies it.

## Inventory, pricing and fees

Every ticket type must state:

- event/version and date/time/time zone;
- venue or virtual access context;
- ticket category and benefits;
- quantity/inventory pool;
- face price, taxes and platform/processing fees;
- recurrence or package/bundle relationship;
- sales window, capacity and waitlist;
- transfer/resale/reassignment rule;
- refund/cancellation/postponement policy;
- age/maturity/accessibility restrictions;
- included/excluded recording, merchandise, food, parking or sponsor benefits;
- organizer/seller identity and support route.

Historical prices, projected event packages or provider defaults do not establish the current effective offer.

## Admission and fraud controls

- unique ticket and barcode/QR identity;
- signed or verifiable payload where selected;
- state transitions preventing duplicate admission;
- offline/partial-connectivity check-in behavior;
- re-entry rules;
- transfer/reissue invalidation;
- staff-device and operator identity;
- suspicious order/velocity/chargeback controls;
- manual override with reason/evidence;
- privacy-safe attendee lookup;
- incident and appeal route.

A screenshot or copied code must not become a permanent source of truth for ticket validity.

## Event changes, refunds and remedies

Cancellation, postponement, venue change, lineup/speaker change, material schedule change, access failure and force-majeure events require versioned notices and current remedy rules.

Refund, credit, exchange, donation or rescheduled admission are different outcomes. Processor activity, CrownThrive order state, ticket status and organizer settlement must reconcile before a case closes.

## Recording, publicity and participant rights

Ticket purchase or attendance does not automatically grant CrownThrive or an organizer unrestricted rights to a participant's name, image, likeness, voice, presentation, submitted materials or private communications.

Event records must identify:

- photography/video/audio notice and release requirements;
- speaker/performer/host releases;
- attendee opt-out/accommodation where applicable;
- minor/guardian controls;
- streaming/replay rights;
- music, slides, artwork and third-party content rights;
- sponsor/advertising disclosure;
- content/maturity warnings;
- credential/certificate eligibility where offered.

## Virtual and hybrid events

ThriveTickets may issue access for ThriveGather or other approved virtual platforms. Ticket issuance, room entitlement, provider invitation and attendance remain separate. Secret room URLs/keys are not stored in public tickets or documentation.

## Agent model

| Agent | Permitted autonomous work | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Event Readiness Agent | validate metadata, venue/schedule, capacity, disclosures and required approvals | cannot activate an event or change terms alone |
| Inventory Agent | calculate remaining inventory and detect oversell/duplication | cannot manufacture seats or bypass capacity |
| Ticket Verification Agent | verify signed/current ticket state and scan rules | cannot override revoked/duplicate state without logged authority |
| Commerce Reconciliation Agent | compare processor, order, ticket, refund and settlement records | cannot move funds or settle disputes autonomously |
| Documentation Agent | update attendee/organizer/refund/access guidance | cannot expose ticket codes, attendee data or unsupported event promises |

## Controlled self-healing

Permitted low-risk recovery includes reprocessing idempotent webhooks, regenerating a ticket from an unchanged approved order, rebuilding check-in indexes, retrying provider status, sending approved transactional notices and opening incidents/deltas.

Agents must not silently create inventory, change prices/fees, admit invalid tickets, alter organizer settlement, publish private attendee data, waive refund terms or overwrite scan/payment history.
