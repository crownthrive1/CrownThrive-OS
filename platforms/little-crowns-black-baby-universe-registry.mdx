## Privacy lock

Never expose through public story, character, product, metadata, analytics, AI or licensing surfaces:

- children’s surnames;
- exact birth dates;
- schools, childcare, teams, addresses, towns or routine locations;
- court, DSS/CPS, foster, placement, visitation, custody, therapy, medical or family-case information;
- private photographs, EXIF, biometric identifiers or source images;
- exact child voice samples or cloned child voices;
- narratives requiring children to carry adult conflict.

Emotional truth may be expressed through love, distance, reassurance, safety, belonging, memory and home without publishing private circumstances.

## Adult-directed children’s commerce and data boundary

The source architecture is **child-suitable content with adult-directed transactions**.

Do not add:

- child accounts or profiles;
- public child comments or direct messaging;
- child photo/voice uploads;
- precise location collection;
- behavioral advertising to children;
- child-level recommendation or behavior scoring;
- pressure-oriented push notifications;
- gamified personal-data collection.

Account, email, review, licensing, purchase and download actions that collect information should sit behind a clear `For grownups` boundary for parents, guardians, educators, librarians, caregivers or other adults.

CrownLytics may receive aggregate adult-directed events; do not create child-level behavioral profiles.

## Reader-rights boundary

The current production masters and upload plan preserve a narrow personal-reading access model unless a separate license says otherwise.

Reader access does not by itself grant:

- redistribution or resale;
- public posting;
- adaptation;
- teaching/classroom facilitation;
- client services;
- commercial framework use;
- public performance or synchronization;
- character/merchandise exploitation;
- model training, dataset use or scraping for replication;
- voice cloning or synthetic child/persona development.

Commercial/institutional rights route to the Master Licensing architecture and a children/family-specific addendum.
