# Kamora360 Sunset and OpsOasis Migration

## Founder-authorized decision

**Effective institutional decision:** August 17, 2026.

- **Kamora360 stable ID:** `ct.platform.kamora360`
- **Canonical state:** `sunset`
- **Successor:** `ct.platform.ops-oasis`
- **Successor display name:** OpsOasis
- **Successor relationship:** `predecessor-of / migrated-into`
- **Public platform posture:** no new Kamora360 positioning, onboarding, product development, licensing, or ecosystem dependency should be created.
- **Historical source posture:** preserve all Kamora360 references in historical prospectuses, agreements, screenshots, invoices, source manifests, accepted policy versions, audit evidence and archival records.

Kamora360 is no longer a current CrownThrive platform destination. OpsOasis becomes the canonical operating/execution successor for the CrownThrive work-management and business-operations lane.

## Why this is a supersession, not deletion

Kamora360 appears throughout earlier CrownThrive and MM Suites source families as a CRM, client/project operations hub and back-office coordination layer. Those records remain evidence of what the institution designed or represented at the time. They must not be rewritten inside archival source files.

Current institutional projections must instead resolve those historical references through this successor record:

```text
historical Kamora360 reference
        ↓
source/effective-date preserved
        ↓
ct.platform.kamora360
        ↓ sunset / predecessor-of
ct.platform.ops-oasis
        ↓
current operational design and implementation
```

## Domain treatment

Historical/current domain records include:

- `kamora360.com` — former primary Kamora360 property;
- `kamoracrm.com` — former alternate/CRM domain.

Domain ownership may remain under CrownThrive for migration, defensive control, evidence preservation and redirects. **A retained domain is not an active platform.**

Target state after migration verification:

`active-marketing → sunset-notice → read-only/export window if required → permanent approved redirect or archival landing → defensive-domain maintenance`.

Do not redirect until authentication, exports, customer records, contractual obligations, support links and any required legal notices are dependency-checked.

## Migration scope

Inventory every current or historical Kamora360 object before shutdown. Potential classes include:

- organizations and businesses;
- users, staff and clients;
- contacts/leads;
- projects and milestones;
- tasks/work items;
- estimates, invoices and payment references;
- subscriptions or recurring records;
- time entries;
- files/attachments;
- tickets/helpdesk records;
- notes/messages;
- custom fields/statuses;
- integrations/webhooks/API credentials;
- reports/exports;
- audit and activity history.

Only migrate classes that actually exist and are required. Unknown objects remain `uninspected`; do not fabricate a migration schema from marketing copy.

## OpsOasis successor responsibilities

OpsOasis becomes the current institutional destination for:

- work items and projects;
- operating run packets;
- task and dependency graphs;
- approvals and decision gates;
- human/agent assignments;
- milestones and delivery state;
- incidents, blockers and risks;
- client/partner operational coordination where appropriate;
- evidence links and completion proof;
- execution dashboards and controlled automation queues.

Collab Portal remains authoritative for agreements, signatures, controlled partner files and sensitive collaboration. ThriveMaps remains authoritative for public/private product feedback, roadmap voting and changelog direction. CrownThrive IO remains the interoperability/API layer. CHLOM remains the rights/rules/roles/revenue/records/remedies governance layer.

## MM Suites migration rule

The 2025 MM Suites prospectus family uses Kamora360 as a back-office CRM and describes event/API relationships built around it. Those statements remain foundational/historical architecture. For the **current target architecture**, references to the operational hub resolve to OpsOasis unless a specific preserved source or contract must be read historically.

This successor mapping does **not** validate historical claims that private blockchain, automated smart-contract allocation, CrownTransact, or every described API was deployed. Each capability keeps its own evidence state.

Phase 2.98 verified that current first-party CrownThrive legal/support surfaces still contain Kamora360 references after the founder sunset decision. Confirmed correction targets include:

- `https://crownthrive.com/page/Terms-and-Conditions` — December 13, 2025 Terms still list Kamora360 among platform families;
- `https://crownthrive.com/page/Privacy-Policy` — December 13, 2025 Privacy Policy still lists Kamora360 among Core Platforms and Tools;
- `https://crownthrive.com/page/FAQs` — current FAQ still lists Kamora360 among Core Platforms;
- older `https://crownthrive.com/page/FAQ-Center` and other historical/platform/portfolio materials may contain additional Kamora-related architecture claims;
- public portfolio/core-platform and MM Suites descriptions require route-by-route reconciliation.

These are current-public correction targets, not authority to modify historical accepted copies in place.

The correction should:

- remove Kamora360 from current/core platform lists;
- identify OpsOasis as the successor only with evidence-safe wording appropriate to OpsOasis's verified implementation state;
- preserve dated archive/source pages when needed for provenance;
- remove or disable new Kamora360 signup/pricing/onboarding paths;
- place a clear sunset/successor notice before redirects where the Kamora route remains reachable;
- update Terms and Privacy prospectively while retaining prior effective/accepted versions;
- update FAQ, portfolio, ecosystem-map and support references;
- inspect inbound links, OAuth callbacks, webhook destinations, API clients, forms and email templates before domain redirect;
- preserve SEO redirects and canonical history rather than creating broken links;
- record the exact deployment timestamp, changed routes, destination routes and rollback state in the migration receipt.

The CrownThrive.com Public Legal Deployment Packet governs the Terms/Privacy/FAQ portion of this migration.

## Migration gates

Kamora360 cannot be marked `fully_retired` until:

1. data/export inventory is complete;
2. legal/contractual retention requirements are classified;
3. active users/accounts and support obligations are identified;
4. current integrations and secrets are inventoried;
5. required records are migrated or archived;
6. OpsOasis destination fields and permissions are verified;
7. redirects and support messaging are tested;
8. old API keys/webhooks/tokens are rotated or revoked;
9. backups and restore evidence exist;
10. public corrections are deployed;
11. a final closure receipt is recorded.

Until then use `sunset_migration_in_progress`.
