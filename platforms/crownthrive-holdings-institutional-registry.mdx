# CrownThrive Holdings institutional registry

CrownThrive Holdings is tracked as **two distinct institutional objects** that must never be collapsed:

1. the **proposed Holdings legal/organizational layer** for long-horizon portfolio stewardship; and

## Proposed Holdings role

The current institutional model keeps long-term stewardship distinct from the operating company:

```text
Proposed CrownThrive Holdings
Portfolio stewardship · capital allocation · long-term ownership · succession
        ↓
CrownThrive, LLC
Operating ecosystem · platform operations · revenue · delivery
        ↓
CHLOM
Rights · rules · records · licensing · remedies
        ↓
CII / ThriveFund / future SPVs
Impact · reinvestment · bounded project/risk structures
```

The [Holdings and Asset Spine](/ecosystem/holdings-and-asset-spine) remains the governing public-safe doctrine for this separation. The Holdings layer remains proposed until the required legal formation, ownership, tax, chain-of-title and intercompany actions are completed and evidenced.

## Website role

The Holdings website is an institutional projection surface. It may explain the proposed stewardship model, portfolio architecture, current operating relationships, evidence boundaries and approved public claims. It does not become a legal source of authority merely because a Sites deployment succeeds.

Current runtime evidence preserves:

- provider-verified Sites connectivity;
- Sites version 4 lineage;
- founder owner/superadmin status;
- a governed Sites Mesh endpoint and ten peer relationships;
- seventeen site-mesh bindings on the recorded surface;
- no deletion and no secret-exposure assertions;
- a pending custom domain and TLS validation state;
- a fail-closed native provider/write cycle.
