# CrownFluence Institutional Registry

## Identity

- **Stable platform ID:** `ct.platform.crownfluence`
- **Canonical name:** CrownFluence
- **Owner/operator:** CrownThrive, LLC
- **Primary role:** governed creator, influencer and user-generated-content campaign exchange plus managed relationship system.
- **Corridor:** advertising, creator distribution, media, commerce and cultural influence.
- **Current evidence state:** meaningful public content was retrieved in the August 2026 domain scan; the exact current engine, provider account, repository, creator/campaign data, API, pricing and payout state remain unverified.

CrownFluence is not equivalent to AdLuxe, Crown Affiliates or Crown Ambassadors. CrownFluence governs creator campaign relationships and deliverables; AdLuxe governs advertising inventory and delivery; Affiliates govern attributable referrals; Ambassadors govern qualified advocacy and representation.

## Campaign lifecycle

```text
brand/campaign intake
→ objective, audience and budget definition
→ brief and rights/disclosure requirements
→ creator discovery or application
→ identity/eligibility/brand-safety review
→ assignment and agreement
→ production and source-file custody
→ claims, rights, likeness, AI and disclosure review
→ approval or required changes
→ publication/AdLuxe handoff
→ evidence collection and attribution
→ compensation, hold, reversal or dispute
→ archive, renewal or correction
```

A creator post, uploaded file or campaign acceptance does not establish approval, payment eligibility or a transferable license by itself.

## Rights and disclosure controls

Every campaign must identify:

- who owns the brief, submitted content and final deliverable;
- the exact campaign-use license granted to CrownThrive/brand partners;
- organic, paid-media, whitelisting, boosting, editing and derivative permissions;
- name, image, likeness, voice and synthetic-media authority;
- music, footage, artwork, product and location permissions;
- AI provider/model and human-contribution records where applicable;
- exclusivity/noncompete/category restrictions;
- territory, term and takedown obligations;
- required material-connection disclosure;
- substantiation for product/performance claims;
- approval, correction and retraction process.

Platform disclosure tools do not replace clear and conspicuous campaign disclosures.

## Economics

Compensation may include fixed creator fees, deliverable fees, usage/whitelisting fees, performance bonuses, affiliate commissions, noncash CrownRewards or negotiated packages. These remain separate economic objects.

No historical blackpaper, prospectus, audience-equity or program concept creates current equity, ownership, perpetual revenue participation or guaranteed earnings without a current authorized agreement and schedule.

## Measurement

CrownLytics should preserve source-specific metrics for deliverables, impressions/reach, views, clicks, conversions, engagement and approved qualitative outcomes. Provider-reported, creator-reported, AdLuxe-delivered and CrownLytics-normalized values remain separate until reconciled.

Do not publish test, estimated, scraped or creator-entered numbers as verified campaign results.

## Agent model

| Agent | Permitted autonomous work | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Creator Eligibility Agent | collect identity/profile evidence and compare approved criteria | cannot infer protected status, licensure or brand authority |
| Deliverable QA Agent | check format, links, metadata, disclosure placement and obvious policy conflicts | cannot approve high-risk claims, rights or likeness use |
| Rights & Release Agent | identify missing releases, source conflicts and AI/voice issues | cannot invent permission or grant expanded rights |
| Performance Reconciliation Agent | compare creator, platform, AdLuxe and CrownLytics reports | cannot issue bonuses or payouts |
| Documentation Agent | update campaign, creator and support articles from approved records | cannot publish private creator contracts or unsupported outcomes |

## Controlled self-healing

Allowed low-risk recovery includes retrying read-only report pulls, regenerating nonauthoritative indexes, quarantining an invalid creative, repairing approved metadata/links and opening incidents or documentation deltas.

Agents must not silently publish a deliverable, alter a creator agreement, expand usage rights, change compensation, remove required disclosures, switch payout destinations or expose private campaign data.
