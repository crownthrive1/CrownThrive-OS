## Four business paths

1. **License through GSO** — music, character, publishing, sync, broadcast, podcast, stage, game, brand or other named rights.
2. **Commission music** — original songs, cues, themes, supervision and cultural music strategy.
3. **Sponsor programming** — Backroad FM, premieres, series, events and approved inventory.
4. **Adapt or option a world** — publishing, screen, stage, game, merchandise and universe development.

Each path requires a separate inquiry/workflow, authority check, rights scope, pricing/economic schedule, agreement, evidence and remedy path.
