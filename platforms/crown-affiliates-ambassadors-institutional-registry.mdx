# Crown Affiliates and Ambassadors Institutional Registry

## Institutional relationship

Crown Affiliates and Crown Ambassadors are connected but distinct CrownThrive distribution roles.

| Program | Stable ID | Primary function |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Crown Affiliates | `ct.platform.crown-affiliates` | governed referral/performance distribution tied to approved offers and attributable conversions |
| Crown Ambassadors | `ct.platform.crown-ambassadors` | higher-trust advocacy/community/representation role with separate eligibility, training, campaign and progression rules |

Neither role creates ownership, employment, partnership, franchise rights or permanent compensation by implication.

## Evidence posture

The recovered Help Center/Legal Depot/SOP estate includes affiliate setup and program guides, Brand Master Guides for Affiliates and Ambassadors, registration and agreement guides, Affiliate/Referral/Earnings master policy and disclaimer, platform-specific addenda, `CT-SOP-108 — Affiliate Payout & Tracking`, and `CT-SOP-115 — Instructor & Affiliate Identity Verification`.

These are recovered title/evidence records. **Provider-license entitlement is now current by founder override:** Kavonte Jones Sr. has adjudicated Crown Affiliates' Partnero entitlement as a lifetime AppSumo license. Purchase codes and private provider-account details remain restricted. This closes provider-license renewal uncertainty only; API credential/Vault binding, authenticated reads, webhook delivery, attribution windows, thresholds, hold periods, payout methods, eligibility and effective program economics remain separately evidence-gated.

Historical MM Suites commission/override/lifetime/franchise-referral examples remain historical economic design evidence. They do not establish current compensation. A **lifetime provider software entitlement** is not a **lifetime commission or referral percentage** and the two concepts must never be conflated.

## Separate economic objects

Attribution record, cash commission, noncash CrownRewards incentive, campaign/creator fee, bonus, refund/reversal and payout remain separate. Equity/ownership is `none` unless a separate agreement expressly creates it. No agent or UI may collapse these into an ambiguous “earnings” balance.

## Program rules

Every effective program/offer must state eligible participant class, eligible products/platforms, attribution method/window, commission/bonus amount or formula, excluded transactions, refund/chargeback reversals, self-referral/fraud restrictions, payout minimum/hold/timing, taxes/reporting obligations, required disclosures, brand/creative permissions, prohibited claims, geographic/age restrictions, effective date and supersession.

Historical “lifetime” commission percentages or overrides cannot be reused without a current adopted schedule and legal/economic review. This restriction does not apply to the separately founder-attested Partnero lifetime AppSumo software entitlement, which is a provider-license fact rather than a compensation schedule.

## Ambassador boundary

Ambassador status does not automatically authorize sub-affiliate/network overrides, franchise referral compensation, contract signature authority, restricted brand assets, public claims on behalf of CrownThrive or private customer-data access.

## Integrations

CrownThrive ID handles identity/role; CrownThriveU handles training; CHLOM handles permissions/economic schedules/disputes/evidence; CrownLytics handles attributable metrics; CrownPulse supports approved signals; ThrivePush handles consent-aware outreach; CrownRewards handles noncash incentives; AdLuxe supports campaign distribution; GSO, Melanin Magic, Virality, events and services provide approved offers; MM Suites/Locticians provide professional/operator pathways.

## Agent boundaries

Attribution agents may deduplicate events and apply an approved schedule/window. Compliance agents may verify disclosure/training metadata. Payout QA may validate readiness. None may change program economics, move money, create permanent bans or grant ownership/contract authority without the applicable approval.
