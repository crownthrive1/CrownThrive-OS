# Commerce & Brands Long-Tail Institutional Registry

## Purpose

This registry governs Commerce & Brands assets that are strategically real in the recovered CrownThrive portfolio but do not yet have enough current evidence to be treated as fully active operating businesses.

The Holdings management plan places these assets inside the **Commerce & Brands** corridor, alongside Good Shit Only and broader product/licensing operations. It also requires portfolio discipline: a brand/domain/script is not automatically a live product, and every active platform must have an owner, customer, revenue model, support path, data events, rights record and lifecycle state before activation.

## Activation standard

An asset may move to `active` only after the applicable controls pass:

1. current owner/custodian and authority;
2. current canonical domain/storefront;
3. source master and product/SKU registry;
4. trademark/brand/content/third-party rights review;
5. manufacturer/provider/license verification;
6. current price, cost, margin and tax/shipping model;
7. order/payment/fulfillment/refund/dispute tests;
8. claims, labeling, safety and regulatory review where applicable;
9. support and correction route;
10. analytics/event definitions;
11. backup/export/vendor-exit path;
12. documentation/changelog outcome.

## The L Market

The L Market should be treated as a marketplace candidate until current evidence establishes its engine, seller model, active catalog, payment flow, fees, terms, moderation, fulfillment responsibilities and support state.

Marketplace activation requires clear separation among seller, CrownThrive operator, payment processor, fulfillment provider and rights authority. A seller listing does not prove ownership or grant CrownThrive broader reuse rights.

## ThriveWick, ThriveThreads and ThriveSip

These product brands may use in-house, private-label, wholesale, print-on-demand or contracted production only when the applicable provider and commercial-use terms are documented.

Minimum records include materials/ingredients/specifications as applicable, labeling, packaging, claims, supplier/manufacturer, batch or source version where relevant, minimum order quantities, unit cost, margin, shipping, returns, quality evidence and insurance/compliance requirements.

Do not infer that a historical product mockup is manufactured inventory.

## Tears of Defeat

Tears of Defeat must retain separation between brand identity, authored/creative assets, merchandise/product rights and any underlying story/content rights. Product activation requires a current source master, permitted merchandise class, provider agreement, quality check, price schedule and fulfillment test.

## XENThrive / Get Kinetic!

XENThrive is preserved primarily as a historical commerce/platform and brand-IP source family. It is a recovery source for older CrownThrive products, imprints, storefront relationships and brand concepts.

Historical XENThrive-era IP should be imported into current registries through stable successor/alias records rather than reactivating XENThrive by default. The current founder adjudication for Melanin Magic Cafe is one example of a concept whose older XENThrive-era source material may need recovery while the modern canonical asset lives elsewhere.

## MusiqHead Audio

MusiqHead Audio requires current classification of whether it is a product brand, audio hardware/accessory line, music-service imprint, licensing lane or a combination of these. Until verified, do not imply active inventory, technical specifications, warranties, manufacturing or endorsement relationships.

If music/content is bundled with a physical or digital product, the product entitlement and media/license rights remain separate linked records.

## Cross-ecosystem handoffs

Qualified products may route through:

- Good Shit Only for broad commerce;
- CHLOM for rights, licensing and permitted-use records;
- Crown Affiliates/Ambassadors for governed attribution;
- CrownRewards for closed-loop incentives;
- AdLuxe for approved campaigns;
- CrownLytics for normalized commerce evidence;
- CrownThrive Support for help, returns, disputes and corrections;
- CrownThriveU for product/business education where appropriate.

No downstream platform becomes the rights authority merely because it displays or sells the product.

## Agent boundaries

| Agent | Allowed autonomous work | Prohibited authority |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Brand Registry Agent | normalize names, aliases, domains and lifecycle evidence | cannot activate a brand from a historical source alone |
| Catalog QA Agent | validate product metadata, files, price mappings and missing fields | cannot invent inventory, cost, claims or clearance |
| Provider Verification Agent | compare supplier/provider records and expiry/version state | cannot accept vendor terms or financial obligations without authority |
| Commerce Reconciliation Agent | compare order, payment, fulfillment and refund evidence | cannot issue refunds, credits or payouts without delegated authority |
| Documentation Agent | maintain current help, product, policy and changelog records | cannot turn unverified products into public offers |

## Phase gates

### Phase 2.98

Recover current/historical brand roles, aliases, domains, product/source/provider evidence, legal boundaries and unresolved operating states.

### Phase 2.99

Seed brand/product/SKU/source/rights/provider/offer/price/inventory/order/fulfillment/refund/support schemas and article dispositions. Preserve historical XENThrive lineage without promoting it as current.

### Phase 3

Implement common product/offer/right/evidence contracts, bounded adapters and documentation-impact events. Long-tail brands remain inactive until their individual activation gates pass.

### Phase 4\+

Qualified brands may enter GSO, affiliate, rewards, AdLuxe, event/phygital and licensing flows only after product economics, quality, fulfillment and support are proven.

## Open verification queue

- current domains and public routes for each brand;
- source masters, logos and trademark/brand ownership evidence;
- current product/SKU catalogs;
- manufacturers, POD/private-label providers and licenses;
- costs, price schedules, margins and taxes;
- inventory and fulfillment state;
- terms/privacy/refund/return/warranty policies;
- active orders/customers and historical data retention;
- APIs/webhooks/exports;
- analytics/events;
- backup/vendor-exit;
- disposition of any XENThrive/Get Kinetic storefront code and remaining brand IP.
