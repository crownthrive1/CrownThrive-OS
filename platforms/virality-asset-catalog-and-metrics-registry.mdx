# Virality Asset Catalog and Metrics Registry

## Purpose

This registry defines the public-safe operating model for Virality Music asset custody, edition lineage, technical validation, provider metrics, and promotion recommendations. It is a method and status page, not a public file index or delivery surface.

**Checkpoint:** August 20, 2026<br />**Platform:** [Virality Music](/platforms/virality-music-institutional-registry)<br />**Rights authority:** CHLOM<br />**Licensing and negotiated commerce:** [Good Shit Only](/platforms/gso-commerce-and-rights-institutional-registry)<br />**Measurement authority:** [CrownLytics](/platforms/crownlytics-institutional-registry)

## Asset state model

| State family | Values | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| rights | `pending_validation`, `cleared`, `restricted`, `disputed`, `blocked` | Whether the asserted use and monetization scope has evidence |
| release | `intake`, `qa`, `approved`, `published`, `blocked`, `archived` | Production and public-release posture |
| canonicality | `candidate`, `canonical`, `variant`, `retired`, `disputed` | Work and edition lineage |
| monetization | `not_eligible`, `eligible`, `listed`, `active`, `suspended` | Commercial readiness, independent of file existence |
| binary custody | `drive_verified`, `supabase_pending`, `dual_verified`, `quarantined`, `rejected` | Exact object-copy status |
| evidence | `verified`, `observed_unverified`, `blocked` | Strength of a metric or provider observation |

An asset may pass structural validation and still remain ineligible for public release or monetization. File presence is never evidence of rights, canon, audience suitability, pricing, fulfillment, or checkout readiness.

## Validation disposition

The current batch passes structural opening and rendering checks. Controlled digital distribution remains gated by rights, audience, accessibility, and commerce evidence.

- The two editable DOCX files are source masters; their paired PDFs are distributions.
- The remaining PDF-only editions are not editable-source substitutes.
- PDFs 01–11 in the private manifest are untagged; tagged PDFs still require PDF/UA certification.
- Print certification remains separate because font embedding, image resolution, trim geometry, bleed, and wrap-cover packages vary.
- Mature-content works require content ratings, age gates, marketplace-policy mapping, and rights clearance.
- The alternate same-title manuscript remains a governed historical/variant record until a canon decision is signed.
- The rebuild-hold edition requires restored editable source plus cover/front-matter remediation before release.

## Custody and projection boundaries

```text
private source or distribution master
  → immutable fingerprint and version record
  → private human custody
  → private operational registry
  → rights, canon, accessibility and commerce gates
  → public catalog projection
  → protected fulfillment or negotiated license
```

Private object identifiers, storage paths, source filenames, provider payloads, credentials, and customer data do not belong in public docs. Public pages may expose a stable catalog identity, approved title, edition, rights-safe description, dated metric, and approved route only after the corresponding gates pass.
