## Editorial boundaries

**Front Porch Sermons!** may draw from Black Southern life, Southern Baptist roots, testimony, family, grief, work, humor, entrepreneurship and gospel/soul/blues/spoken word. Exact KJV text remains distinguishable from commentary/application.

**Built to Outlive Us!** focuses on ownership, systems, knowledge transfer, economics, succession, culture and legacy. Verified outcomes must be separated from goals/estimates/founder claims.

**Receipts & Revelations!** uses explicit evidence classes: fact, attributed claim, opinion, allegation, personal testimony and creative narrative/dramatization. Escalate high-risk publication involving named misconduct, litigation, minors/private people, medical/family information, disputed recordings, copyrighted material and material reputation/employment/business claims.

## Episode lifecycle and provenance

`idea → greenlit → research → preproduction → recorded → edited → reviewed → approved → published → repurposed → archived`.

Recommended file ID:

`MS-[SHOWCODE]-S[SEASON]E[EPISODE]-[YYYYMMDD]-[ASSETTYPE]-v[VERSION]`

Codes: `FPS`, `BTOU`, `RNR`.

Released masters are immutable; corrections create new versions and preserve predecessors.

Each episode packet should include guest release, music provenance, source list, sponsor disclosure, usage restrictions, approved master/transcript metadata and correction/hold history.

## Distribution and derivatives

Publish canonical Virality page first, then approved podcast/audio, screen/video, Backroad radio edit and derivative clips/quotes/newsletter/community assets. MVP/IO may orchestrate discovery later while specialized platforms remain systems of record.

Potential revenue lanes: sponsorship/ads, products, memberships, live experiences, licensing/syndication, music/catalog revenue, affiliate commerce and education/services. Pricing remains versioned management/economic data rather than permanent prose.

Sponsors may not purchase conclusions or obscure disclosure. The network may monetize the program; it may not sell the truth of the program.
