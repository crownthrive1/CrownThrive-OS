# Education & Community Long-Tail Institutional Registry

## Purpose

This registry closes the remaining named education/community roles not already covered by the dedicated CrownThriveU, ThrivePeer and ThriveGather registries.

The August 2026 Holdings plan explicitly defines the education/talent/community progression as:

```text
learn in CrownThriveU
→ prove knowledge in ThriveArena
→ receive mentoring in ThrivePeer
→ qualify as affiliate/ambassador
→ offer paid help through CrownConsults or ThriveBooking
→ teach as a vetted instructor
→ join alumni/leadership structures
→ build using CrownThrive operating tools
```

That progression is strategic architecture. Each platform still requires its own implementation evidence before production/API claims are made.

## QuizNuggets

The Holdings domain registry preserves `quiznuggets.com` as an active domain for quiz creation and publishing. Domain status is not application certification.

Governed objects include quiz, question bank, answer key, explanation/source, embed, competition, attempt, score and moderation/review records.

AI-generated quiz content requires source/review labeling appropriate to the subject. High-consequence education, compliance, Scripture/theology or credentialing assessments need qualified human review and cannot rely solely on model output.

## CrownConsults

CrownConsults is a paid expert-service lane, not a promise that every expert is licensed for regulated professional practice.

Every engagement should resolve:

- expert/provider identity and qualifications;
- service category and scope;
- price/fee schedule;
- booking/availability source;
- prohibited/out-of-scope work;
- required professional-license verification where applicable;
- customer terms and cancellation/refund rules;
- privacy/data classification;
- deliverables and completion evidence;
- dispute/support route.

Medical, legal, financial, mental-health, engineering or other regulated services require current jurisdiction-specific authority where the service crosses into regulated practice.

## CrownThrive Commons

Commons may support groups, gated community, relationships, messages and announcements. It should remain a social/community provider layer connected to CrownThrive ID through explicit account linking rather than becoming the durable institutional identity source of truth.

Community governance requires moderation, conduct, suspension/appeal, privacy, retention, minor safety where applicable and role separation from corporate authority.

## Shared credential and progression doctrine

Keep these record types distinct:

```text
course completion
≠ challenge score
≠ CrownThrive certificate
≠ professional license
≠ mentor/expert eligibility
≠ alumni/community title
≠ affiliate/ambassador status
≠ CHLOM permission
≠ corporate authority
```

Progression may use one record as evidence for another decision, but the later state requires its own rule, approval and effective record.

## Agent boundaries

Education/community agents may validate metadata, retrieve authoritative status, calculate deterministic scores under approved rules, moderate low-risk content under policy and open cases/deltas. They may not fabricate qualifications, issue professional licenses, turn points into authority, suppress appeals or promote unresolved Digital Business Academy lineage into current status.
