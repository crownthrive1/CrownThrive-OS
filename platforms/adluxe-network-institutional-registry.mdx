## Provider boundary

Adserver.Online, ADSPOT, AdSpot TV or any later provider may supply implementation capability. They do not own CrownThrive's institutional advertising relationships.

CrownThrive remains responsible for:

- advertiser and publisher relationships;
- accepted inventory and placement policies;
- campaign approval and brand safety;
- creative rights and disclosures;
- pricing, invoices, credits, commissions and payouts;
- consent and privacy posture;
- CrownThrive-wide identity and role records;
- CHLOM rights, approvals and evidence;
- CrownLytics normalization and approved public claims;
- support, incidents, corrections and vendor-exit continuity.

The provider may remain authoritative for its native delivery objects, counters and logs. CrownThrive owns the governed mapping from those objects to customer, contract, rights, payment and institutional evidence records.

## PentaGreen operating relationship

[**PentaGreen™**](/commerce/pentagreen) is the commercial and economic activation authority across AdLuxe Network, AdLuxe Studio and AdLuxe Places. AdLuxe retains specialist advertising functions; PentaGreen governs the money, commercial-product, SKU, price, licensing, settlement, reconciliation and cross-ecosystem activation layer around them.

| Surface | AdLuxe specialist authority | PentaGreen authority |
| --- | --- | --- |
| AdLuxe Network | advertiser/publisher relationships, inventory, trafficking, delivery, measurement and campaign operations | inventory/package commercial eligibility, service/SKU identity, rate/budget guardrails, payment/invoice/credit rules, publisher economics, settlement, profitability and reconciliation |
| AdLuxe Studio | creative production, source assets, campaign variants, approvals and handoff | offer/product binding, rights/license version, production pricing, commercial-release gate and ECAC linkage |
| AdLuxe Places | locations, screens, schedules, playback and proof-of-play | place-inventory assetization, rate cards/packages, location-partner economics, invoicing, settlement and proof reconciliation |

Advertising inventory itself becomes a governed evergreen commercial asset class. Qualified placements, sponsorships, creator packages, media/program sponsorships, event inventory, screens, locations and managed-campaign services receive stable CrownThrive IDs and, where the product model requires it, canonical service/SKU identities.

Every campaign involving a CrownThrive commercial offer should resolve to the offer's Evergreen Commerce Activation Certificate (`ECAC`) and should emit canonical commerce/advertising events into CrownLytics and the institutional ledger. Provider delivery evidence never automatically becomes an invoice, payout or approved public claim; invalid-traffic, budget, contract and reconciliation rules apply first.

A product can be legal and sellable but still fail the economic threshold for paid distribution. PentaGreen may therefore recommend, activate, limit, pause or decline AdLuxe spend within its signed authority envelope based on expected contribution margin, customer value, rights, audience policy, inventory quality and measured performance.

## Delivery, reporting and source authority

Provider-reported impressions, clicks, conversions and raw events are operational evidence, not automatically approved public claims. CrownLytics should normalize provider data while retaining source lineage.

Provider documentation and live bounded certification now establish the Adserver.Online REST API at `https://api.adsrv.net/v2`, OpenAPI version 2.4.1, Bearer authentication and a provider limit of 100 requests/minute/token. CrownThrive's Vault-only owner credential authenticated successfully for `GET /dict` and a one-day UTC `GET /stats` report. The dictionary response was reduced to 13 top-level structural groups; the statistics response was reduced to one-row/17-field structural evidence. Only status, latency, rate-limit headers, response sizes, SHA-256 digests, row/group counts and field names were retained. No raw provider body or credential entered public documentation.

The permanent `adluxe-api-control` v9 adapter is JWT-protected, separately enforces internal service-role/admin authorization, streams bounded request/response bodies, applies a provider timeout and allowlists only `GET /dict` plus bounded `GET /stats`. Provider writes remain disabled. Account-specific subscription/billing identity, campaign-delivery truth, retention windows and wider read families remain independently gated.

Minimum governed metrics include:

- requested, served and viewable impressions where supported;
- clicks and click-through rate;
- conversions by named definition and attribution window;
- spend, effective CPM/CPC/CPA and pacing;
- invalid or filtered traffic;
- fill rate and no-fill;
- creative, placement, property, zone, campaign and geography dimensions;
- video/signage starts, quartiles, completion and proof-of-play where supported;
- creator deliverables and approved engagement evidence;
- publisher/location earnings, holds and adjustments;
- advertiser invoices, credits and refunds;
- incidents, missed delivery and make-goods.

Never merge provider counters with CrownThrive estimates or third-party analytics without preserving separate sources and reconciliation logic.

## Targeting and privacy

Targeting may use only approved, necessary and supportable attributes. Prohibited behavior includes silently using private spiritual journals, health records, restricted family data, precise location without authority, sensitive traits, private support content or data collected for an unrelated purpose.

Targeting profiles require:

- lawful purpose and consent/notice basis;
- data source and effective period;
- audience construction rule;
- sensitive-data exclusion review;
- geography and age/maturity controls;
- suppression and opt-out rules;
- retention and deletion;
- minimum audience thresholds where needed;
- human review for regulated or high-consequence categories.

## Creative, claim and rights controls

Every creative must identify:

- source files and creator/contributor authority;
- brand and trademark permissions;
- music, image, footage, voice, likeness and synthetic-media rights;
- material claims and substantiation;
- required sponsorship/affiliate/AI disclosures;
- destination URL and offer terms;
- prohibited or restricted categories;
- audience/maturity rating;
- approved versions and expiration;
- takedown and replacement route.

AI-assisted creative follows the CrownThrive AI-assisted/generated-content and human-authorship standard. An AI provider's commercial-use permission does not prove copyright ownership, exclusivity, claim accuracy or likeness authority.

## Self-check and controlled self-healing

Permitted automatic corrections include retrying failed read-only provider pulls, refreshing expired test tokens through approved secret systems, repairing generated docs links, rebuilding nonauthoritative indexes, requeuing idempotent reports and opening documentation deltas.

AdLuxe specialist agents must not silently bypass PentaGreen or their own authority boundaries. PentaGreen itself may perform otherwise-permitted economic actions only through a signed authority envelope, canonical ledger entry and reconciliation workflow.

Agents must not silently:

- alter signed campaign terms outside an approved contract envelope;
- increase budgets or bids outside a certified PentaGreen budget/risk envelope;
- mutate PentaCredits balances or create payouts outside an applicable PentaGreen economic rule;
- change creative claims;
- waive brand-safety rules;
- expose private audience data;
- relabel provider capability as active CrownThrive capability;
- overwrite delivery evidence;
- rebuild an application repository from a deployment artifact.

## Lean implementation order

1. Complete authenticated account/provider/repository inventory.

4. Implement read-only reporting adapter first.

Prefer low-cost/open standards, portable exports, Git-based contracts, scheduled serverless jobs and provider-agnostic schemas. Cheap automation is not permission to remove controls.
