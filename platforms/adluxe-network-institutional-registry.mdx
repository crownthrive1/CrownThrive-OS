# AdLuxe Network Institutional Registry

## Institutional identity

AdLuxe is CrownThrive's governed advertising, sponsorship and campaign-distribution system. It is not one undifferentiated application.

| Surface | Stable ID | Canonical function |
| --- | --- | --- |
| AdLuxe Network | `ct.platform.adluxe-network` | advertiser, publisher, campaign, web-inventory, delivery, reporting and settlement network |
| AdLuxe Studio | `ct.platform.adluxe-studio` | creative production, copy, image/video variants, briefs, approvals, source files and campaign handoff |
| AdLuxe Places | `ct.platform.adluxe-places` | locations, screens, place-based inventory, schedules, playback, proof-of-play and local campaign operations |

**Owner/operator:** CrownThrive, LLC<br />**Primary public host:** `https://adluxe.crownthrive.com`<br />**Related hosts and lanes:** `adluxeplace.com`, `adluxestudio.com`, CrownFluence, Crown Affiliates, Crown Ambassadors, CrownLytics, CrownPulse, ThrivePush, CrownRewards, CrownThrive IO/MCP and CHLOM.

<Warning>
  AdLuxe must separate three evidence layers: CrownThrive's public operating claim, an underlying provider's documented capability, and the exact capability verified in CrownThrive's current account/deployment. Provider documentation never proves that a feature, plan, API key, version, endpoint or integration is active for CrownThrive.
</Warning>

## Provider boundary

Adserver.Online, ADSPOT, AdSpot TV or any later provider may supply implementation capability. They do not own CrownThrive's institutional advertising relationships.

CrownThrive remains responsible for:

- advertiser and publisher relationships;
- accepted inventory and placement policies;
- campaign approval and brand safety;
- creative rights and disclosures;
- pricing, invoices, credits, commissions and payouts;
- consent and privacy posture;
- CrownThrive-wide identity and role records;
- CHLOM rights, approvals and evidence;
- CrownLytics normalization and approved public claims;
- support, incidents, corrections and vendor-exit continuity.

The provider may remain authoritative for its native delivery objects, counters and logs. CrownThrive owns the governed mapping from those objects to customer, contract, rights, payment and institutional evidence records.

## Operating roles and portals

Roles are independent. A person or organization may hold more than one role only through explicit grants.

| Role | Primary capabilities | Required controls |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Super admin | institutional configuration, provider connections, policy, pricing, emergency controls, audit and irreversible actions | founder/authorized executive authority, MFA, dual control for high-risk changes, immutable evidence |
| Admin / campaign operations | advertiser/publisher review, campaign setup, trafficking, approvals, reporting and support | least privilege, change log, separation from settlement approval where feasible |
| Moderator / brand-safety reviewer | creative, destination, claim, category and placement review | documented standards, appeal path, no payment or credential authority |
| Advertiser | organization profile, campaigns, budgets, creatives, targeting, reports, invoices and support | identity/authority, accepted terms, payment verification, claims and rights confirmation |
| Publisher | properties, zones, placements, ad tags, exclusions, reports, earnings and support | property control, traffic-quality review, consent requirements, payment/tax profile |
| Location partner | physical site, screens, schedules, proof-of-play, maintenance and earnings | location authority, device ownership, privacy/signage rules, uptime and content controls |
| Creator / influencer | campaign offers, deliverables, approvals, disclosures and performance | creator agreement, likeness/content rights, FTC disclosure, acceptance and proof |
| Finance / reconciliation | invoices, credits, refunds, commissions, publisher earnings and payouts | scoped financial authority, audit trail, no creative approval by default |
| Analyst | approved aggregate campaign and inventory reporting | data minimization, export controls, claim-registry boundary |
| Support operator | ticket intake, diagnostics, customer communication and escalation | no unrestricted credentials, impersonation log, redaction and incident rules |
| Developer / integration operator | sandbox keys, webhooks, adapters, test events and health | separate environments, scopes, rotation, rate limits and certification |

## Campaign lifecycle

```text
lead or partner intake
→ identity and authority verification
→ advertiser/publisher/location eligibility
→ inventory and audience selection
→ brief and rights intake
→ creative production or upload
→ claim, legal, brand-safety and destination review
→ price/budget/contract approval
→ provider trafficking
→ preflight and sandbox/test impression
→ campaign activation
→ delivery and anomaly monitoring
→ conversion/attribution reconciliation
→ invoice, credit, commission and payout processing
→ final report and evidence package
→ renewal, archive, dispute or remedy
```

No campaign becomes `active` merely because a creative file exists, a provider object was created or payment was attempted.

- version and effective date;
- included inventory and service scope;

- invalid-traffic treatment;
- cancellation and pause terms;
- commission and publisher-share rules;

## Delivery, reporting and source authority

Provider-reported impressions, clicks, conversions and raw events are operational evidence, not automatically approved public claims. CrownLytics should normalize provider data while retaining source lineage.

Provider documentation currently supports the existence of management/reporting/decisioning/JavaScript API families and statistical reporting with campaign, ad, site and zone dimensions. The provider also documents raw conversion reporting. CrownThrive's current provider account/plan entitlement is accepted under the August 21, 2026 founder override, but exact API runtime authentication, base URLs, scopes, retention windows, endpoint versions and permissible export volumes remain unverified until authenticated inspection.

Minimum governed metrics include:

- requested, served and viewable impressions where supported;
- clicks and click-through rate;
- conversions by named definition and attribution window;
- spend, effective CPM/CPC/CPA and pacing;
- invalid or filtered traffic;
- fill rate and no-fill;
- creative, placement, property, zone, campaign and geography dimensions;
- video/signage starts, quartiles, completion and proof-of-play where supported;
- creator deliverables and approved engagement evidence;
- publisher/location earnings, holds and adjustments;
- advertiser invoices, credits and refunds;
- incidents, missed delivery and make-goods.

Never merge provider counters with CrownThrive estimates or third-party analytics without preserving separate sources and reconciliation logic.

## Targeting and privacy

Targeting may use only approved, necessary and supportable attributes. Prohibited behavior includes silently using private spiritual journals, health records, restricted family data, precise location without authority, sensitive traits, private support content or data collected for an unrelated purpose.

Targeting profiles require:

- lawful purpose and consent/notice basis;
- data source and effective period;
- audience construction rule;
- sensitive-data exclusion review;
- geography and age/maturity controls;
- suppression and opt-out rules;
- retention and deletion;
- minimum audience thresholds where needed;
- human review for regulated or high-consequence categories.

## Creative, claim and rights controls

Every creative must identify:

- source files and creator/contributor authority;
- brand and trademark permissions;
- music, image, footage, voice, likeness and synthetic-media rights;
- material claims and substantiation;
- required sponsorship/affiliate/AI disclosures;
- destination URL and offer terms;
- prohibited or restricted categories;
- audience/maturity rating;
- approved versions and expiration;
- takedown and replacement route.

AI-assisted creative follows the CrownThrive AI-assisted/generated-content and human-authorship standard. An AI provider's commercial-use permission does not prove copyright ownership, exclusivity, claim accuracy or likeness authority.

## Agent operating model

| Agent | Permitted autonomous work | Approval boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Inventory Sentinel | crawl configured public placements, verify tags/links, detect missing zones and open deltas | may not create commercial inventory or change pricing |
| Campaign QA Agent | validate metadata, dimensions, destinations, disclosures and obvious policy mismatches | may return changes; cannot approve high-risk claims or regulated categories |
| Delivery Watcher | monitor pacing, no-fill, anomalies and provider health; pause only under preapproved emergency rule | permanent suspension, refunds and public incident claims require authorized human review |
| Documentation Agent | update versioned API, dashboard, inventory and support articles from approved evidence | legal/economic/canon changes require specialist approval |
| Reconciliation Agent | compare provider, Stripe/accounting and CrownLytics totals; open exceptions | cannot issue payouts, credits or write-offs |
| Brand-Safety Agent | classify and queue creatives/placements | final denial/appeal in high-impact cases requires authorized reviewer |

Lower-risk subordinate approvals may advance D0/D1 documentation or QA records only when automated checks pass and no legal, financial, rights, privacy, security or public-claim materiality is present.

## Self-check and controlled self-healing

Permitted automatic corrections include retrying failed read-only provider pulls, refreshing expired test tokens through approved secret systems, repairing generated docs links, rebuilding nonauthoritative indexes, requeuing idempotent reports and opening documentation deltas.

Agents must not silently:

- alter signed campaign terms;
- increase budgets or bids;
- create credits or payouts;
- change creative claims;
- waive brand-safety rules;
- expose private audience data;
- relabel provider capability as active CrownThrive capability;
- overwrite delivery evidence;
- rebuild an application repository from a deployment artifact.
