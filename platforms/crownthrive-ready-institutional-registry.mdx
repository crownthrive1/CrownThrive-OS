# CrownThrive Ready

CrownThrive Ready™ is the productized readiness, trust and evidence-preparation lane for organizations preparing for launch, partnerships, procurement, accessibility review, security review, audit or qualified professional review.

```yaml
platform_id: ct.platform.crownthrive-ready
preferred_route: ready.crownthrive.com
route_state: unverified_target
lifecycle: DESIGN_CONTROLLED_BUILD
checkout: closed
```

## Critical boundary

Ready organizes requirements, identifies gaps, prepares evidence and routes specialist review. It does **not** automatically establish that a buyer is legally compliant, secure, accessible, certified, licensed, accredited or audit-passed.

CHLOM/dS-CaaS may later provide governed policy/evidence infrastructure, but Ready is a customer-facing readiness surface, not a synonym for CHLOM.

- `CT-READY-LAUNCH-001` — Business Launch Readiness Pack

- `CT-READY-SEC-001` — Small Business Security Readiness Pack
- `CT-READY-AI-001` — AI Governance Readiness Pack
- `CT-READY-VENDOR-001` — Third-Party / Vendor Risk Readiness Pack
- `CT-READY-POLICY-001` — Policy Starter Library
- `CT-READY-EVIDENCE-001` — Evidence & Audit Binder Kit
- `CT-READY-IR-001` — Incident Response Readiness Pack
- `CT-READY-TRUST-001` — Customer / Partner Trust Pack

Recurring candidates: `CT-READY-MONITOR-001` Readiness Monitor and `CT-READY-REVIEW-001` Periodic Readiness Review.

## Revenue rails

1. downloadable readiness kits and evidence binders;
2. guided assessments with bounded scoring and explicit limitations;
3. managed remediation preparation/evidence organization;
4. recurring change/watch and renewal reviews;
5. qualified specialist routing;
6. future CHLOM/dS-CaaS implementation after separate gates.

## Ecosystem handoffs

CrownThrive Sites for website remediation, CrownThrive Launch for operating policies, CrownThrive Procure for vendor-risk sourcing, CHLOM for rights/rules/evidence, ThriveSupport for controlled documentation, CrownThriveU for training, CrownLytics for readiness metrics and Collab Portal for remediation work.

## Lifecycle

`concept -> candidate -> packaged -> rights_cleared -> pricing_authorized -> fulfillment_certified -> checkout_staged -> live`

An automated score or generated checklist is not a compliance certificate.

Scoped non-voting execution identity: `ct.agent.crownthrive-ready-readiness`.

## Release gate

Each product requires authoritative sources/effective dates, clear scope/jurisdiction assumptions, professional-review boundaries, rights, QA, security/privacy review, disclaimers, pricing authority, delivery/support and correction/update mechanics.
