# CrownThrive Ready

CrownThrive Ready™ is the productized readiness, trust and evidence-preparation lane for organizations preparing for launch, partnerships, procurement, accessibility review, security review, audit or qualified professional review.

```yaml
platform_id: ct.platform.crownthrive-ready
provider_system: ChatGPT Sites
provider_preview: https://crownthrive-ready.crownthrive.chatgpt.site
preferred_route: ready.crownthrive.com
route_state: unverified_target
lifecycle: DESIGN_CONTROLLED_BUILD
checkout: closed_credit_only
```

## Critical boundary

Ready organizes requirements, identifies gaps, prepares evidence and routes specialist review. It does **not** automatically establish that a buyer is legally compliant, secure, accessible, certified, licensed, accredited or audit-passed.

## Lifecycle

`concept -> candidate -> packaged -> rights_cleared -> pricing_authorized -> fulfillment_certified -> checkout_staged -> publication_staged -> accepted -> live`

The current 10-product cohort is packaged, private, credit-only and HOLD. Automated checks and scores are evidence aids, not professional or legal conclusions.
