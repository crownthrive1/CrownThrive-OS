# CrownThrive Ready

CrownThrive Ready™ is the productized readiness, trust and evidence-preparation lane for organizations that need to become more operationally prepared before launch, procurement, partnership, audit, accessibility review, security review or regulated professional review.

## Stable identity

```yaml
platform_id: ct.platform.crownthrive-ready

preferred_route: ready.crownthrive.com
route_state: unverified_target
lifecycle: DESIGN_CONTROLLED_BUILD
primary_corridor: governance_trust_commerce
checkout: closed
```

The target route remains unverified until provider/DNS/TLS/deployment evidence exists.

## Critical boundary

CrownThrive Ready is a **readiness and preparation product**, not an automatic declaration that a buyer is legally compliant, secure, accessible, certified, licensed, accredited or audit-passed.

It may organize requirements, identify gaps, generate checklists, prepare evidence, document controls and route customers to qualified professionals. Legal conclusions, tax conclusions, regulatory determinations, formal accessibility certification, penetration-test conclusions, audit opinions and other licensed/professional judgments require the appropriate authority.

CHLOM/dS-CaaS may later supply governed policy and evidence infrastructure, but Ready is the customer-facing productized readiness surface rather than a synonym for CHLOM.

## Revenue architecture

1. downloadable readiness kits and evidence binders;
2. guided assessments with bounded scoring and explicit limitations;
3. managed remediation preparation and evidence organization;
4. recurring change/watch and renewal reviews;
5. qualified specialist routing for legal, tax, accessibility, security, accounting or regulated conclusions;
6. enterprise CHLOM/dS-CaaS implementation only after separate authority and implementation gates.

## Ecosystem handoffs

Ready can integrate with CrownThrive Sites for website remediation, CrownThrive Launch for operating policies, CrownThrive Procure for vendor-risk sourcing, CHLOM for rights/rules/evidence, ThriveSupport for controlled documentation, CrownThriveU for training, CrownLytics for readiness metrics and Collab Portal for governed remediation work.

## Product lifecycle

`concept -> candidate -> packaged -> rights_cleared -> pricing_authorized -> fulfillment_certified -> checkout_staged -> live`

A generated checklist, automated score or passing internal test is not a compliance certificate.

## Automation owner

Scoped non-voting execution identity: `ct.agent.crownthrive-ready-readiness`.

It may maintain readiness catalogs, run deterministic evidence checks, identify stale requirements, open remediation deltas and produce public-safe readiness summaries. It may not issue legal opinions, claim conformance/certification, perform unauthorized security testing, decide regulatory applicability, approve its own product or activate commerce.

## Release gates

Each product requires source authority, scope and jurisdiction labeling where relevant, professional-review boundary, version/effective date, rights, accessibility/usability QA, security/privacy review, disclaimers, exact pricing authority, delivery/support and correction/update mechanics.

## Strategic role

CrownThrive Ready turns governance and operational preparation into a repeatable paid product while preserving a hard boundary between **readiness evidence** and **professional certification or legal compliance**.
