## Nexus Credits

Current Locticians public material treats Nexus Credits as an **on-platform promotional credit**, used for items such as leads, boosts, sponsored placement, category visibility, events or upgrades.

Current public summary terms say credits:

- have no cash value;
- are non-transferable;
- may have variable award/redemption values;
- may be forfeited for fraud/spam/policy violations;
- operate under changeable program rules with effective-date updates.

The institutional system must not conflate Locticians Nexus Credits with CrownRewards points, closed-loop CrownThrive Credits, cash, equity, royalties or transferable stored value.
