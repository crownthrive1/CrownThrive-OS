# CrownThrive Sites

**CrownThrive Sites™** is the proposed self-service and managed website-commerce lane for CrownThrive. It closes the gap between high-touch **Digital Products & Platform Dev** services and the broader GSO / Developer Marketplace / CHLOMLex commerce surfaces by turning repeatable website work into packaged products with governed fulfillment, maintenance and upgrade paths.

<Warning>
  Current state is `DESIGN / CONTROLLED_BUILD`. The preferred public route is `sites.crownthrive.com`, but DNS, provider project identity, deployment URL and live checkout remain `UNVERIFIED` until direct provider evidence exists. This page does not claim a production site or active offer.
</Warning>

## Market gap

CrownThrive already describes custom platform development, workflow/SOP buildout, ecosystem tooling and premium resource access. The missing commercial layer is a **dedicated website-as-a-product storefront** where a buyer can purchase a ready-to-use site system, launch asset, upgrade pack or maintenance plan without first entering a bespoke consulting engagement.

CrownThrive Sites therefore productizes work that otherwise remains trapped in one-off service delivery.

## Primary buyers

- solopreneurs and local service businesses;
- beauty and wellness professionals;
- churches and ministry teams;
- creators, authors, artists and podcasters;
- coaches, consultants and educators;
- small ecommerce and digital-product sellers;
- early-stage founders who need a credible launch surface before a larger platform build.

## Revenue architecture

CrownThrive Sites should support four distinct rails:

1. **Digital site products** — templates, page packs, component packs, launch kits, SEO/accessibility packs, automation recipes and documentation.
2. **Managed implementation** — fixed-scope build packages that configure an approved kit for the customer.
3. **Recurring maintenance** — updates, uptime/route checks, content changes, backups, performance/accessibility checks and governed support.
4. **Ecosystem upgrades** — optional CrownThrive IO, BrandCards, CrownLytics, CrownPulse, ThrivePush, AdLuxe, CrownRewards, ThriveTickets, ThriveSeat, GSO and other appropriate adapters.

The website product and the managed service must remain separate order objects even when sold together.

## Pricing model

Use evidence-backed pricing bands until exact offers are independently authorized:

- page/template packs: low-ticket self-service;
- complete vertical site kits: mid-ticket digital product;
- configured launch packages: fixed-scope service tier;
- managed custom builds: higher-ticket implementation;
- maintenance: monthly or annual recurring service;
- ecosystem adapters: bundled or separately priced add-ons.

Exact prices must be supported by current comparables, cost-to-serve, license scope and the applicable CrownThrive economic approval path before Stripe activation.

## “Built & Maintained by CrownThrive” attribution

A completed customer site may optionally display a public attribution such as **Built & Maintained by CrownThrive** or **Powered by CrownThrive** when the customer agreement permits it.

The attribution should:

- link to the CrownThrive Sites public surface;
- never be hidden or deceptive;
- be removable where the purchased license/plan permits;
- never imply CrownThrive owns customer content, trademarks or business operations;
- function as a referral/discovery loop rather than an SEO manipulation scheme;
- be versioned as part of the applicable site license or managed-service agreement.

This creates a compounding acquisition loop: customer site → CrownThrive Sites → new site buyer → ecosystem upgrade.

## Commerce and fulfillment contract

Digital products inherit the CrownThrive product-state model:

`concept -> candidate -> packaged -> rights_cleared -> pricing_authorized -> fulfillment_certified -> checkout_staged -> live`

A valid digital purchase must bind:

- exact SKU and immutable release version;
- exact license/version accepted by the buyer;
- exact Price/amount/currency;
- payment result;
- entitlement or protected download state;
- checksum/release manifest;
- support/update scope;
- refund/dispute/revocation state;
- customer identity using the approved account/guest model;
- CHLOM rights/provenance record where applicable.

## Managed-site lifecycle

`lead_or_self_select -> requirements -> scope/kit selection -> order -> intake -> build -> staging -> customer review -> accessibility/security/SEO QA -> production release -> readback -> maintenance -> change requests -> renewal_or_exit`

Maintenance does not authorize unlimited redesign. Scope, response expectations, included change volume, excluded work and escalation must be explicit per plan.

## Automation and agent ownership

### CrownThrive Sites Product & Maintenance Agent

Proposed ID: `ct.agent.crownthrive-sites-product-maintenance`

Primary responsibilities:

- maintain kit/SKU/version/catalog records;
- prepare site-build packets from approved templates;
- reconcile customer-approved content against scope;
- run deterministic SEO, route, link, performance and accessibility checks;
- prepare deployment/readback/rollback evidence;
- maintain maintenance queues and recurring health evidence;
- open docs/support deltas when repeated customer issues expose a product gap;
- route usage and conversion aggregates to CrownLytics without copying private customer content into public documentation.

This agent is non-sovereign. It cannot approve its own product, choose tax treatment, bind customer legal terms, expose credentials, publish a customer site without the required release authority, or perform unrestricted Stripe mutations.

### Specialist subagents

The existing relay may dispatch bounded subagents for:

- vertical template research and differentiation;
- visual/responsive QA;
- accessibility evidence;
- SEO/search metadata;
- conversion/CTA review;
- CHLOM license/provenance checks;
- Stripe/entitlement reconciliation;
- uptime/link/maintenance monitoring;
- customer-support pattern extraction;
- security/privacy scanning.

Subagents remain non-voting and inherit the existing A/B/C/D/S governance model.
