# CrownThrive Launch

CrownThrive Launch™ is the productized business-operating-system lane between self-guided founders and CrownThrive's bespoke Business Formation & System Ops engagements.

```yaml
platform_id: ct.platform.crownthrive-launch
preferred_route: launch.crownthrive.com
route_state: unverified_target
lifecycle: DESIGN_CONTROLLED_BUILD
checkout: closed
```

## Buyer and boundary

Launch serves founders, service businesses, creators, beauty professionals, churches/ministry teams, coaches/consultants and digital sellers that need repeatable business systems without a bespoke transformation engagement.

It is not a law firm, accounting service, tax preparer, accreditation body or automatic business-formation authority. Regulated/professional determinations remain separately gated.

## Revenue rails

1. self-service digital operating kits;
2. fixed-scope managed implementation;
3. recurring Ops Care;
4. ecosystem integration upgrades;
5. bespoke Business Formation & System Ops when productized scope is insufficient.

Exact pricing remains evidence-backed and separately authorized.

## Ecosystem handoffs

CrownThrive Sites, CrownThrive IO, BrandCards, Collab Portal, CrownLytics, CrownPulse, ThrivePush, CrownRewards, CrownThriveU and CHLOM can be attached where appropriate. Handoffs never transfer authority, rights or private customer data automatically.

## Lifecycle

`concept -> candidate -> packaged -> rights_cleared -> pricing_authorized -> fulfillment_certified -> checkout_staged -> live`

Scoped non-voting execution identity: `ct.agent.crownthrive-launch-ops-product`.

## Release gate

Before `live`: immutable package/version identity, source-master alignment, rights/IP disposition, customer license/terms, privacy/data alignment, QA, security review, evidence-backed pricing, applicable tax classification, payment/webhook/entitlement proof, refund/dispute behavior, delivery/rollback evidence and support scope.
