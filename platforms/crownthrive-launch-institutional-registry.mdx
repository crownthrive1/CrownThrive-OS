# CrownThrive Launch

CrownThrive Launch™ is the productized business-operating-system lane between self-guided founders and CrownThrive's bespoke Business Formation & System Ops engagements.

```yaml
checkout: closed_credit_only
```

## Buyer and boundary

Launch serves founders, service businesses, creators, beauty professionals, churches/ministry teams, coaches/consultants and digital sellers that need repeatable business systems without a bespoke transformation engagement.

It is not a law firm, accounting service, tax preparer, accreditation body or automatic business-formation authority. Regulated and professional determinations remain separately gated.
