# ThriveSeat Institutional Registry

## Identity

- **Stable platform ID:** `ct.platform.thriveseat`
- **Canonical name:** ThriveSeat
- **Owner/operator:** CrownThrive, LLC
- **Corridor:** Beauty, Wellness & Phygital
- **Primary role:** booking, staff/provider operations, customer management, payments/appointments, service-business administration and MM Suites operating support
- **Current public state:** `public_host_reachable / production-capability-visible / implementation-reconciliation-required`
- **Implementation lineage:** Frezka SaaS / Frezka Flutter \+ Laravel family from Iqonic/Envato evidence

ThriveSeat is the canonical CrownThrive platform identity. The underlying Frezka software is an implementation/vendor lineage and must not replace the institutional platform identity in public architecture, rights records, APIs, event schemas, documentation or MM Suites contracts.

## Naming and schema resolution

`ThriveSeat` is the platform. Historical `ThriveSeats` references are retained as legacy/plural wording unless a source explicitly describes a physical seat/unit object. `Suite Pro` is a separate MM Suites professional/operator role and must never be modeled as another name for ThriveSeat.

## Current capability surface

The current public property presents a multi-tenant salon/spa/business-management product with scheduling/booking, staff and service management, customer operations, plans/subscriptions and other SaaS functions inherited from the underlying implementation family. Public capability is evidence of available product behavior; it does not by itself verify every module, payment flow, mobile build, API, webhook, tenant configuration or production entitlement in CrownThrive’s installed version.

## Locticians handoff

Locticians owns professional/consumer discovery, profiles, verification, community and matching. ThriveSeat owns service-business operations and booking workflows.

Target governed flow:

`Locticians discovery/profile → provider identity/link → ThriveSeat availability/service → booking/payment/appointment → completion/support → CrownRewards/analytics → Locticians reputation or follow-up where policy permits`.

Profile verification on Locticians does not automatically equal appointment completion, licensing status, insurance status or service quality. Those claims require their own evidence classes.

## MM Suites handoff

ThriveSeat is the primary booking/operating candidate for MM Suites and Suite Pros, but MM Suites must remain vendor-independent.

Required MM Suites adapter domains include locations and suites/workspaces, Suite Pro/provider identities, services and pricing, calendars/availability, bookings/appointments, deposits/payments/refunds, customer records and consent, staff/provider schedules, booking status and completion events, cancellations/no-shows, loyalty/rewards events, and operational exports/reconciliation.

No franchise, regional-license or Suite Pro agreement may make the Frezka implementation itself the permanent institutional source of truth.

## Payments and commercial controls

Before treating ThriveSeat commerce as fully production-certified, verify payment processor and merchant-of-record relationship, subscription/plan configuration, provider/location payout behavior, deposits and cancellation/refund rules, taxes/fees, chargeback/dispute handling, booking/payment webhook coverage, order/booking reconciliation, privacy/consent treatment, and vendor-license compliance.

Public plan or pricing displays are dated product evidence and may not be treated as permanent economic terms.

Recoverable assets must include tenant/location/provider records, customers and consent state, services/pricing, appointments/calendars, payment references, plan/subscription records, configuration/branding, files/media, audit/support history, source-code/license evidence, database/schema backups and migration mappings.
