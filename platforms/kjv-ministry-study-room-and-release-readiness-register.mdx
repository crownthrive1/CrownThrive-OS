## Source-recovery runbook

1. Ask the source-hosting service to repair or repack the original repository through revision `aa0e1b659f7a20452aa84f163b71cdfc59b48ada`.

3. Verify exact `HEAD`, branch protections and clean worktree.

## Controlled self-healing

Permitted low-risk recovery includes retrying read-only repository checks, rebuilding public route inventories, fixing generated documentation links, refreshing nonauthoritative search indexes, rerunning idempotent test events in sandbox and opening docs/incident deltas.

Self-healing must stop when a proposed action would alter source history, Scripture, canon, signed terms, prices, paid entitlements, private spiritual records, production credentials or public legal/theological claims.
