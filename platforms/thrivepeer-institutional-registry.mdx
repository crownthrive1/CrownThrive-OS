## Program lifecycle

```text
program definition
→ participant/mentor intake
→ identity, role and scope verification
→ consent/privacy/safety configuration
→ matching criteria and availability
→ proposed match
→ acceptance
→ session/check-in activity
→ goals/resources/referrals
→ feedback and evidence
→ continuation, rematch, completion or closure
→ correction, dispute or archive
```

No participant is automatically matched because of profile similarity, points, course completion or affiliate/ambassador status.

## Matching and authority rules

Matching may consider declared goals, expertise, availability, language, geography, maturity/age, accessibility needs, conflict/exclusion rules and organization context. Sensitive traits must not be inferred or exposed without an approved purpose and authority.

Mentor authority must be described precisely:

- lived-experience peer;
- trained CrownThrive mentor;
- verified subject-matter expert;
- licensed professional, only where exact current credential evidence exists;
- volunteer, contractor, partner or employee status, as separately documented.

The platform must not transform a self-description into verified professional authority.

## Privacy and safety

Private session notes, goals, direct messages, contact information, personal disclosures and safety cases require relationship-scoped access and retention controls.

- no private mentoring content in advertising or ordinary analytics;
- no automated clinical, legal, financial or crisis determination;
- clear emergency and safeguarding boundaries;
- additional age, guardian and communication controls for minors where allowed;
- block/report/escalation and appeal paths;
- export/delete and program-record retention rules;
- no silent sharing with affiliates, advertisers or unrelated platforms.

## Economics and rewards

Potential value forms include volunteer service, paid mentor sessions, program stipends, service fees, organization seats, CrownRewards incentives or verified expert referrals. These are distinct objects.

Payment, rewards or participation do not guarantee results, employment, business growth, funding, licensing, promotion or advancement.

## Agent model

| Agent | Permitted autonomous work | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Match Preparation Agent | calculate candidates from approved criteria and exclusions | cannot final-match high-risk/minor/safeguarding cases alone |
| Mentor Verification Agent | collect identity, experience and credential evidence | cannot infer licensure or authority from claims alone |
| Session Operations Agent | schedule, remind and reconcile attendance | cannot access private content beyond assigned scope |
| Safety Sentinel | detect reports/rule triggers and open cases | cannot provide crisis care or conceal incidents |
| Documentation Agent | update program, mentor and participant guidance | cannot expose private mentoring records or guarantee outcomes |

## Controlled self-healing

Permitted low-risk recovery includes retrying schedule synchronization, rebuilding derived availability, quarantining invalid resources, repairing approved article links and opening incidents/deltas.

Agents must not silently rematch participants, expose private notes, change mentor authority, alter payment schedules, close safety cases or represent an automated recommendation as professional advice.
