## Curriculum and rights

Each course must distinguish:

- CrownThrive-owned curriculum;
- licensed third-party materials;
- instructor/contributor materials;
- public-domain materials;
- AI-assisted/generated content;
- media/music/visual assets;
- trademarks/brand assets;
- learner submissions/private data.

Instructor onboarding does not automatically assign all instructor IP to CrownThrive. The effective agreement must define ownership, platform license, edits, recording, promotion, compensation, reuse, termination and post-termination access.

## Quality and credential integrity

Quality controls should cover:

- source accuracy;
- learning objectives and outcomes;
- instructor competence/identity;
- plagiarism/rights review;
- factual and claims review;
- accessibility;
- assessment validity;
- grading/completion logic;
- certificate/credential criteria;
- course version and corrections;
- learner support;
- refund/cancellation behavior.

A certificate represents only the completion/assessment standard explicitly defined by its credential record. It must not imply a professional license, government accreditation or regulated certification without evidence.

## Commerce and entitlement

Courses may use one-time, subscription, cohort, organization, bundled or licensed access models. Every offer must resolve current price, seat/user limits, term, refund, access/expiry, download, credential and organization rights.

Payment does not prove enrollment completion or credential issuance.

## Agent model

| Agent | Autonomous work | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Course QA Agent | metadata, source references, accessibility and deterministic QA checks | cannot approve professional/regulated claims alone |
| Instructor Intake Agent | collect identity, expertise and agreement prerequisites | cannot determine legal licensure from assertion alone |
| Curriculum Rights Agent | detect missing source/permission/AI disclosures and open holds | cannot invent chain of title |
| Credential Integrity Agent | verify requirements before issuance and flag mismatches | cannot fabricate accreditation or license status |
| Documentation Agent | rebuild/update learner/instructor/course articles | cannot publish restricted learner or instructor data |
