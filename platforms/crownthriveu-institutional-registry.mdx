# CrownThriveU Institutional Registry

## Identity

- **Stable platform ID:** `ct.platform.crownthrive-u`
- **Canonical name:** CrownThriveU
- **Owner/operator:** CrownThrive, LLC
- **Corridors:** education, talent, workforce, community, licensing and creator/partner enablement

→ curriculum_build

→ QA/assessment review
→ price/license/entitlement review

→ course_active

No course is `active` because a file, lesson list or historical title exists.

## Curriculum and rights

Each course must distinguish CrownThrive-owned curriculum, licensed third-party materials, instructor/contributor materials, public-domain materials, AI-assisted/generated content, media/music/visual assets, trademarks/brand assets and learner submissions/private data.

Instructor onboarding does not automatically assign all instructor IP to CrownThrive. Effective agreements define ownership, platform license, edits, recording, promotion, compensation, reuse, termination and post-termination access.

## Quality and credential integrity

Quality controls include source accuracy, learning objectives, instructor competence/identity, plagiarism/rights review, factual/claims review, accessibility, assessment validity, grading/completion logic, certificate criteria, version/corrections, learner support and refund/cancellation behavior.

A certificate represents only the completion/assessment standard explicitly defined by its credential record. It must not imply professional licensure, government accreditation or regulated certification without evidence.

## Affiliate Academy and internal workforce education

CrownThriveU is the natural training layer for affiliate disclosure/offer education, ambassador standards, Suite Pro/operator education, product/professional education, developer/integration education, platform operator/support training and creator/instructor progression. Training completion remains separate from program eligibility, compensation, employment, licensure or ownership.

## Commerce and integrations

Course offers may use one-time, subscription, cohort, organization, bundled or licensed access models. Payment does not prove completion or credential issuance.

CrownThrive ID supplies identity/roles; CHLOM supplies rights/agreements/evidence; GSO supports eligible commerce; Affiliates/Ambassadors use training and qualified offers; CrownRewards supports approved incentives; ThrivePush supports consent-aware learning messages; CrownLytics/Pulse provide evidence; Go Flipbooks may deliver licensed readers; KJV/Sermon and beauty/MM Suites pathways may supply governed curriculum.

## Agent boundaries

Course QA may run deterministic checks; Instructor Intake may gather prerequisites; Curriculum Rights may detect missing permissions/AI disclosures; Credential Integrity may verify configured criteria. No agent may fabricate accreditation, infer licensure/rights from assertion/upload or issue a credential when recorded requirements are unmet.
