## Scripture and authored-content boundary

The KJV biblical text must remain separately labeled from:

- CrownThrive commentary;
- historical context;
- denominational/traditional interpretation;
- teaching application;
- devotional writing;
- visual reconstruction;
- testimony/memory;
- fictional or dramatized narrative;
- AI-generated or AI-assisted material.

The institutional rule is: **the image opens the door; the text remains the judge.** No illustration, generated explanation or sermon tool may override or silently alter the KJV text.

- additional safeguards for minors;
- no representation that AI is a pastor, clinician or emergency service.
