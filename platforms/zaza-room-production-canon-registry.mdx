## Governing promise

**Album law:** _Never write about smoking. Always write about what happened after._

The world uses cannabis-context comedy as a pressure environment around ordinary adult responsibilities: relationships, money, work, family, art, law, ownership, land, culture, consequence and repair.

## Twelve canon laws

1. **Adults remain adults** — every principal is at least 21; no youth-coded marketing or consumption pressure.
2. **Double OG is fictional** — an original in-universe story property; no cannabis/seeds/starts/delivery/gifts/potency-testing retail transaction is offered by the story world.
3. **Virginia law supplies tension—not a loophole** — the fiction may recognize legal-state context but cannot turn unlawful sale/gifting/public-consumption behavior into a clever workaround.
4. **Cannabis opens a door—not every answer** — altered perception does not make a character automatically wise, healed, productive or consequence-free.
5. **Black stoners are whole people** — work, family, faith, money, land, politics, ambition, disability, desire, grief, style and community remain present.
6. **The wheel stays sober** — impaired driving and unsafe vehicle conduct are not aspirational; Route 420's designated driver remains sober.
7. **No medical dosage or cultivation instruction** — fiction/culture, not treatment claims, dosage, grow steps, testing guidance or legal advice.
8. **Consent governs every room** — no consumption requirement; private moments, devices, stories, plants, voices, likenesses and works do not become communal property.
9. **The room returns to earth** — surreal sequences land in choices, boundaries, consequences, tasks or changed understanding.
10. **Culture is not set dressing** — Black Southern language, food, hair, church memory, red-clay land, neighborhood life, work, politics and humor remain specific and protected from caricature.
11. **Comedy punches through the system** — hypocrisy, extraction and foolishness may be targets; addiction, incarceration, health risk and harmed communities are not disposable jokes.
12. **Every right remains named** — music, page, character, portrait, voice, performance, screen, animation, merchandise, hospitality, strain identity, sequel, training and synthetic use remain separate unless a signed scope joins them.
