# Virality Music Licensing and Rights Register

## 2026-08-20 authority correction

CHLOM is Virality Music's sole active licensing and release-fingerprint authority. The public licensing routes now resolve to `/chlom-licensing`. GSO names, IDs, receipts and prior routes remain immutable historical lineage, but GSO does not issue new Virality licenses.

Personal digital products use Virality Credits and receive an account-bound entitlement plus signed personal-use license. Payment/top-up, order, entitlement and license remain separate linked records. Private Google Drive is canonical custody; customer delivery continues through the authenticated private R2 broker until a production server-side Drive identity is implemented and verified.


## Purpose

Virality Music is a music, publishing and story-world institution. Its commercial model must distinguish listening access, personal products, commissioned services, sponsorship, standardized licensing and negotiated rights transactions.

This register is a platform-specific projection of the CrownThrive **Master Licensing and Rights Architecture**. It does not replace the master license object, CHLOM evidence, signed terms, payment/order, entitlement or work-level rights record.

<Warning>
  A public track, book, character page, price card, SoundCloud stream, hosted reader, inquiry form or Stripe payment does not grant commercial rights. Every grant is work-, version-, use-, territory-, term- and licensee-specific.
</Warning>

## Asset and rights layers

### Music and audio

Separate at minimum:

- composition and melody;
- lyrics and literary text;
- master sound recording;
- producer, performer and contributor interests;
- samples, interpolations and third-party source material;
- publishing administration;
- neighboring/public-performance/collective obligations where applicable;
- stems, instrumental, acapella and multitrack source files;
- artwork, video, title treatment and metadata;
- voice, likeness and synthetic-replica rights;
- AI-provider or software terms;
- trademark, brand and universe associations.

### Books, poems and publishing

Separate:

- manuscript/poem text;
- edition and editorial arrangement;
- cover, illustrations and page design;
- audiobook/spoken performance;
- translation;
- excerpt/reprint;
- educational reproduction;
- public reading/performance;
- stage/screen/audio adaptation;
- merchandise and character elements;
- Go Flipbooks access entitlement from upstream publication rights.

### Characters and universes

Separate:

- character identity and protectable expression;
- name/mark and source-identifying use;
- approved likeness/art and no-likeness restrictions;
- canon and continuity access;
- specific source works and editions;
- merchandise/product category;
- stage, screen, audio, game and interactive rights;
- sequel, prequel, spinoff and derivative scope;
- territorial, language and platform rights;
- approvals, reserved rights, reversion and participation.

Public canon access never grants adaptation rights. A universe partnership never automatically includes every track, composition, master, book, character, mark, future work or derivative.

## Commissioned work

Commissioning requires a service SOW plus a rights schedule. It must define:

- brief, creative scope and exclusions;
- references and prohibited imitation;
- human and AI-assisted contribution record;
- revisions and acceptance;
- delivery formats/source files;
- ownership versus license;
- exclusivity and reserved portfolio rights;
- master/composition/publishing treatment;
- voice/likeness and synthetic-media boundaries;
- credits and portfolio/case-study permission;
- fees, deposits, milestones, cancellation and kill fee;
- third-party clearances;
- release/distribution responsibilities;
- dispute and correction route.

“Custom song” does not inherently mean work made for hire, full buyout, exclusive publishing or transferable source files.

## Sponsorship and programming

Sponsorship is governed jointly by Virality/Backroad FM content standards and AdLuxe campaign controls. Records must name:

- program/room/channel and inventory;
- dates, frequency and placement;
- host-read, audio, visual, page, email or event deliverables;
- sponsor creative and claim approvals;
- music/content rights;
- audience and measurement source;
- disclosures and category restrictions;
- pricing, make-goods and cancellation;
- editorial independence;
- reporting and evidence package.

Sponsorship does not grant ownership of the program, universe, catalog or audience data.

## AI-assisted catalog boundary

For AI-assisted or generated material, Virality may license only the rights supported by evidence, including CrownThrive-controlled human-authored, selected, arranged, edited, performed, designed, branded, canonical and contractual elements.

Provider commercial-use permission may support use under provider terms. It does not by itself prove exclusive statutory copyright, chain of title, absence of similarity/conflict, publishing control, voice/likeness authority or an unrestricted right to sublicense model outputs.

No ordinary stream, download, product, API response or license grants model training, scraping, dataset creation, embedding extraction, style imitation, voice cloning, fine-tuning or reusable synthetic-persona rights by implication.
