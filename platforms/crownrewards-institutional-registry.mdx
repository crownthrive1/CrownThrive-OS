# CrownRewards Institutional Registry

## Identity and current rule

- **Stable platform ID:** `ct.platform.crownrewards`
- **Canonical name:** CrownRewards
- **Historical/public alias:** My CrownRewards
- **Owner/operator:** CrownThrive, LLC
- **Corridor:** retention, incentives, loyalty, distribution and measurable progression

CrownRewards is governed as a **closed-loop loyalty and promotional-value system by default**. Points, credits, perks and member benefits are not cash, cryptocurrency, securities, investment interests, deposits, transferable stored value or ownership rights unless a future specifically reviewed program establishes a different legal/economic structure.

## Historical architecture boundary

Older CHLOM materials explored token-like, transferable and programmable reward concepts, including gifting, burn/redeem mechanics and cross-platform reward rights. Those sources remain protected research and design history. They are **not current production proof** and do not authorize tokenization, cash equivalence, transferability or immutable ownership representations.

Historical MM Suites prospectuses also contain illustrative affiliate/reward percentages, lifetime overrides and example points-to-dollar values. Those examples remain historical planning assumptions until a current effective economic/reward schedule explicitly adopts a value.

## Program rule schedule

Every program/partner schedule should define:

- eligible users/roles;
- earn events and rates;
- excluded/refunded/invalid transactions;
- caps and minimums;
- redemption catalog and required balance;
- expiration/inactivity policy;
- reversal/refund behavior;
- promotional-value treatment;
- transfer/gift permission, normally `false` unless explicitly enabled;
- cash-out permission, normally `false`;
- partner funding/economic owner;
- fraud/abuse controls;
- effective date and supersession;
- notice requirements.

Do not hard-code a historic `points = dollars` statement as a universal conversion rate.

## Partner model

Partners need:

- organization/authority verification;
- program configuration rights;
- approved earn/redemption rules;
- staff roles;
- funding/settlement responsibility where applicable;
- customer support/escalation;
- QR/member-card configuration;
- analytics/reporting scope;
- privacy and data-use terms;
- suspension/termination and post-termination treatment.

A partner may not create rights, cash-equivalent value or promotional claims outside the approved schedule.

## Member experience

Members should be able to:

- see current balance and pending/reversed/expired activity;
- understand exactly how points were earned;
- see current rules and expiration;
- redeem eligible rewards;
- view linked offers and restrictions;
- report errors;
- manage account/security/privacy;
- export applicable account data;
- receive clear notice when rules materially change.

## Attribution versus reward versus commission

These remain distinct records:

- referral attribution — who influenced a conversion;
- affiliate/ambassador commission — contractual monetary compensation;
- CrownRewards points/perk — closed-loop/promotional incentive;
- discount/credit — transaction-specific price reduction;
- ownership/equity — never implied by the above.

A single transaction may generate more than one record, but none should be merged into an ambiguous “earnings” total.

## Fraud and integrity

Controls should detect:

- duplicate/self-referral abuse;
- repeated refunded purchases;
- suspicious QR scanning;
- synthetic/duplicate identities;
- staff-created unauthorized adjustments;
- velocity anomalies;
- expired/replayed requests;
- partner configuration drift;
- negative-balance/reversal errors.

Fraud flags create holds/cases rather than deleting evidence.

## Accounting and liability

The system must maintain a current finance policy for promotional value, partner-funded rewards, expiration/breakage, credits, reversals and outstanding obligations. Historical token/treasury descriptions do not establish current accounting treatment.

## Agent model

| Agent | Autonomous work | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rewards Reconciliation Agent | compare source events, ledger and balances; open exceptions | cannot alter balances without governed adjustment event |
| Fraud Sentinel | detect anomalies and open holds for preapproved patterns | cannot confiscate value or terminate accounts autonomously |
| Rule Drift Agent | compare active schedules against docs/config and open deltas | cannot publish new economic rates |
| Partner QA Agent | validate configuration completeness and permissions | cannot approve financial/legal exceptions |
| Documentation Agent | update partner/member/troubleshooting articles | cannot imply cash/crypto/investment characteristics |

## Controlled self-healing

May reprocess idempotent source events, rebuild derived balances/indexes, retry failed reports, repair documentation links and quarantine inconsistent program configuration.

May not silently change historical ledger events, expire/revoke rewards outside an effective policy, change conversion values, enable transfers/cash-out, or alter partner economics.
