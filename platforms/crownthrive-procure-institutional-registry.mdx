# CrownThrive Procure

CrownThrive Procure™ is the B2B procurement and vendor-sourcing lane for founders, small organizations and CrownThrive ecosystem participants that need structured supplier discovery, comparison, RFQ/RFP preparation, due diligence and recurring vendor-management support.

```yaml
platform_id: ct.platform.crownthrive-procure
preferred_route: procure.crownthrive.com
route_state: unverified_target
lifecycle: DESIGN_CONTROLLED_BUILD
checkout: closed
```

## Boundary

Procure is not GSO and is not CHLOMLex. GSO governs broad digital-product and commercial-rights marketplace commerce. CHLOMLex is the governed licensing/exchange concept. Procure focuses on **business purchasing and supplier selection**.

A provider cannot be described as vetted, certified, approved, insured, licensed or guaranteed without the exact evidence and authority for that statement.

## Revenue rails

1. procurement templates and digital decision kits;
2. paid sourcing/research engagements;
3. managed RFQ/RFP preparation;
4. recurring vendor watch, renewal and procurement concierge;
5. disclosed referral/affiliate revenue where appropriate;
6. vendor-side listing/response/sponsorship products after marketplace/legal/economic review.

## Ecosystem handoffs

CrownPulse for market signals, CrownLytics for sourcing/renewal metrics, BrandCards for provider identity projection, CrownThrive Ready for vendor-risk readiness, CHLOM for rights/rules/evidence, Crown Affiliates for disclosed attribution, AdLuxe for sponsored discovery, Collab Portal for sourcing work and CrownThrive IO for approved utilities.

## Lifecycle

`concept -> candidate -> packaged -> rights_cleared -> pricing_authorized -> fulfillment_certified -> checkout_staged -> live`

Supplier presence, payment or sponsorship never equals endorsement or certification.

Scoped non-voting execution identity: `ct.agent.crownthrive-procure-vendor-sourcing`.

## Release gate

Require exact product version, current-source citations, claim/evidence classes, disclosure rules, confidentiality/privacy controls, conflict-of-interest handling, pricing authority, delivery/support, refund/dispute mechanics and appropriate marketplace/provider terms before paid activation.
