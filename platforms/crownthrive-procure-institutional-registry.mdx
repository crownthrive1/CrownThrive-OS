# CrownThrive Procure

CrownThrive Procure™ is the B2B procurement and vendor-sourcing lane for founders, small organizations and CrownThrive ecosystem participants who need structured supplier discovery, comparison, RFQ/RFP preparation, due diligence and recurring vendor-management support.

## Stable identity

```yaml
platform_id: ct.platform.crownthrive-procure
canonical_name: CrownThrive Procure
preferred_route: procure.crownthrive.com
route_state: unverified_target
lifecycle: DESIGN_CONTROLLED_BUILD
primary_corridor: procurement_commerce_operations
checkout: closed
```

The target route remains unverified until provider/DNS/TLS/deployment evidence exists.

## Boundary with existing commerce platforms

CrownThrive Procure is not Good Shit Only and is not CHLOMLex. GSO governs broad digital-product and commercial-rights marketplace commerce; CHLOMLex is the governed licensing/exchange concept. Procure focuses on **business purchasing and supplier selection**: sourcing services/software/providers, organizing quotes, comparing evidence and managing supplier lifecycle.

It must not describe a provider as vetted, certified, approved, insured, licensed or guaranteed unless the exact evidence and authority for that statement exists.

## Future marketplace layers

After evidence and commerce gates mature, Procure may support provider profiles, quote-request routing, category sponsorships, referral/affiliate attribution, vendor response workspaces, verified evidence badges and managed procurement engagements. Sponsored or affiliate relationships must be disclosed. Payment, seller payout or marketplace economics require separate commerce architecture and authority.

## Revenue architecture

1. procurement templates and digital decision kits;
2. paid sourcing/research engagements;
3. managed RFQ/RFP preparation;
4. recurring vendor watch, renewal and procurement concierge;
5. disclosed referral/affiliate revenue where appropriate;
6. vendor-side listing, response or sponsorship products only after marketplace/legal/economic review.

## Ecosystem handoffs

Procure can use CrownPulse for market signals, CrownLytics for sourcing/renewal metrics, BrandCards for provider identity projection, CrownThrive Ready for vendor-risk readiness, CHLOM for rights/rules/evidence, Crown Affiliates for disclosed attribution, AdLuxe for sponsored discovery, CrownRewards for eligible closed-loop incentives, Collab Portal for sourcing work and CrownThrive IO for approved business utilities.

## Product lifecycle

`concept -> candidate -> packaged -> rights_cleared -> pricing_authorized -> fulfillment_certified -> checkout_staged -> live`

Supplier presence or payment never equals endorsement, certification or procurement approval.

## Automation owner

Scoped non-voting execution identity: `ct.agent.crownthrive-procure-vendor-sourcing`.

It may maintain supplier-category records, research public provider evidence, prepare comparison packets, detect stale provider claims and open sourcing/renewal deltas. It may not bind CrownThrive to a supplier, sign agreements, move funds, misrepresent vendor verification, choose legal/tax treatment, expose confidential bids or self-certify marketplace status.

## Release gates

Require exact product version, current-source citations, claim/evidence classes, disclosure rules, privacy/confidentiality controls, vendor-response boundaries, conflict-of-interest handling, pricing authority, delivery/support, refund/dispute mechanics and appropriate marketplace/provider terms before paid activation.

## Strategic role

CrownThrive Procure monetizes a recurring pain point after business launch: choosing, comparing, buying and replacing providers without repeating research from zero. It also generates governed supplier intelligence that can improve CrownThrive's own vendor decisions without turning internal restricted evidence into public claims.
