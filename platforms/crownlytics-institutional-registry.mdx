## Measurement and privacy

CrownLytics can calculate analytics, but CrownThrive's governed claim registry decides which metrics may become approved institutional/public claims. Visitor/session/replay/heatmap data requires explicit data-class, retention, access, export/delete and privacy controls.

## Relationships

```text
platform events → CrownLytics evidence → CrownPulse governed signal → ThrivePush only where consent/purpose permits
```

No downstream service may manufacture, broaden or reinterpret analytics evidence outside an adopted rule.
