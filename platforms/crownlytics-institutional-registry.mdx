# CrownLytics Institutional Registry

## Identity

- **Stable platform ID:** `ct.platform.crownlytics`
- **Role:** CrownThrive measurement, evidence-state, attribution, experimentation, and governed recommendation authority
- **Relationship to Virality:** measures approved catalog, provider, route, conversion, and campaign signals without owning rights, canon, offers, or checkout
- **Operating posture:** evidence-first; recommendations begin in shadow mode

CrownLytics is the measurement authority for Virality Music. CrownPulse may transport or surface approved signals, but it does not supersede the metric dictionary, evidence ledger, or attribution rules.

## Metric evidence model

Every metric record requires:

```yaml
metric_id:
entity_id:
provider:
snapshot_at:
measurement_window:
value:
unit:
definition_version:
evidence_state: verified|observed_unverified|blocked
source_digest:
collected_at:
freshness_expires_at:
```

Evidence precedence is source-specific and must follow the active governance for that provider. In the general case, accepted first-party/provider evidence outranks a public observation, and unavailable evidence remains unavailable rather than being inferred.

For SoundCloud, the August 20, 2026 founder override removes SoundCloud API usage from the architecture. CrownThrive must not request, store, configure, certify, invoke, or depend on SoundCloud API credentials or authenticated API reads/writes. SoundCloud metrics can be promoted only by permitted non-API evidence whose provenance and verification policy support that stronger state.

Lower-strength evidence may extend the audit trail but may not silently overwrite a stronger accepted snapshot.

## Virality daily signal families

- account and track plays;
- likes, reposts, comments, downloads, and followers when supplied by the provider;
- track count and catalog coverage;
- freshness and source/provenance completeness;
- public-route availability and broken-route state;
- rights, release, canon, audience, and commerce eligibility;
- qualified licensing inquiries, checkout conversions, refunds, disputes, and entitlement state when lawfully connected;
- campaign and placement exposure with explicit attribution windows.

## Recommendation contract

The first algorithm is a bounded ranking and placement recommender, not an autonomous publisher.

Eligibility is binary and evaluated before scoring:

```text
eligible = rights-cleared
        ∧ release-approved
        ∧ canon-approved
        ∧ audience-compatible
        ∧ commerce-or-license-route-ready
        ∧ rollback-available
```

An eligible asset may receive a shadow score derived from normalized momentum, engagement quality, freshness, catalog coverage, conversion evidence, strategic priority, fatigue, and world-balance controls. Score components, weights, evidence windows, and definition versions must be recorded with the recommendation.

Safeguards include:

- minimum sample size and freshness;
- cooldowns and frequency caps;
- no protected-class targeting or sensitive-trait inference;
- no causal claim from correlation alone;
- no use of unverified metrics to trigger public writes;
- world and catalog-balance constraints so a single high-volume world does not erase the rest of the archive;
- explicit human approval for consequential placement, pricing, license, or entitlement changes;
- immutable decision and rollback events.

## Authority boundaries

| Authority | Owns | Does not own |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Virality Music | work identity, canon, release readiness, public catalog | metric definitions, rights adjudication, checkout |
| CHLOM | rights and provenance | ranking or merchandising |
| GSO | offers, licenses, checkout and negotiated commerce | canon or metric truth |
| CrownLytics | definitions, snapshots, attribution, experiments, recommendation evidence | provider writes, rights, price, license grant, entitlement |
| Growth & Analytics Agent | analysis and shadow recommendations | execution without approval |
| Virality Rights/Canon Agent | rights/canon checks and blocks | commerce activation |

## Promotion lifecycle

`proposed → approved|rejected|expired → executing → succeeded|failed|revoked`

The current implementation supports `proposed` records only. Execution is a separate D3 capability and is not enabled. A recommendation row never authorizes a live mutation by itself.
