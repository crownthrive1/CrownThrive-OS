# Good Shit Only Commerce and Rights Institutional Registry

## Identity

- **Stable platform ID:** `ct.platform.gso`
- **Canonical name:** Good Shit Only
- **Alias:** GSO
- **Owner/operator:** CrownThrive, LLC
- **Corridors:** commerce/brands, digital products, creator commerce, licensing/rights routing
- **Current evidence state:** institutional role and cross-platform routing documented; exact current engine, repository, database, API and full transaction state remain uninspected unless separately verified.

GSO is a commerce and transaction surface. **CHLOM remains the institutional source for rights, permission, authority, license state, evidence and remedies.** GSO may present an approved offer or collect a payment; it does not create rights merely because an item appears in a marketplace.

## Canonical role

Current CrownThrive management architecture positions GSO as the broad marketplace for:

- digital products;
- books, printables, templates and creative assets;
- selected services;
- bundles and hosted-reader offers;
- approved physical/POD goods;
- approved creator/brand collections;
- standardized license offers;
- reviewed commercial-rights inquiry routing.

Virality currently preserves a `License Through GSO` public route and historical GSO Rights Desk lineage. That routing does not collapse Virality, CHLOM, Go Flipbooks or GSO into one system.

## Non-equivalences

- product card ≠ source master;
- source master ≠ released offer;
- Stripe Product/Price ≠ functioning checkout;
- payment ≠ order completion;
- order completion ≠ entitlement;
- entitlement ≠ commercial license;
- personal access ≠ reproduction/resale/adaptation/manufacturing rights;
- hosted reader ≠ source-IP ownership;
- seller listing ≠ verified seller authority;
- GSO offer ≠ CHLOM rights clearance.

## Offer classes

### Personal digital access

Books, printables, journals, guides, templates, readers and digital bundles. Requires current file/reader, preview, price, terms, delivery and refund behavior.

### Creator/professional resources

Tools, worksheets, editable or production-oriented resources. Rights must state whether client work, editing, production or commercial use is actually allowed.

### Physical/POD

Only where a verified provider, inventory/production method, shipping, tax, quality, refund and support model exists. Digital listings must not imply a physical shipment.

### Standardized licenses

Only rights-cleared assets and predefined license classes may use self-serve checkout. License version, scope, territory, term, quantity/seat/location limits and verification must be linked.

### Reviewed rights transactions

Adaptation, exclusivity, characters/universes, manufacturing, source files, broad institutional use, AI/data/model/voice, high-value enterprise or uncertain chain-of-title requests route to reviewed/negotiated handling.

## Seller/brand model

A seller/brand record needs:

- legal/operating identity;
- authority to list the asset;
- payout/tax profile where applicable;
- applicable marketplace/seller agreement;
- product/source/rights relationships;
- content/claims compliance;
- support and fulfillment owner;
- dispute/takedown route;
- suspension/termination state.

Marketplace participation does not transfer IP to GSO or CrownThrive unless a separate assignment/license says so.

## Payment, order, license and entitlement separation

```text
Stripe/provider payment
        ↓
CrownThrive order
        ↓
product access entitlement
        ↓
license, only when an applicable grant is actually issued
        ↓
protected delivery / reader access
        ↓
usage / download / support / refund / dispute evidence
```

Refund or dispute handling must reconcile both access and license state according to the effective terms. Revocation must not destroy historical evidence.

## Agent model

| Agent | Autonomous work | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Catalog Agent | metadata/file/preview/price/license mapping checks | cannot activate missing/uncleared products |
| Rights Router | preliminary standardized eligibility and inquiry routing | cannot issue negotiated/exclusive/adaptation rights |
| Commerce Reconciliation Agent | compare payment/order/entitlement/fulfillment/refund evidence | cannot move money or waive policy without authority |
| Seller QA Agent | validate onboarding completeness and listing requirements | cannot establish ownership from seller assertion alone |
| Documentation Agent | update product, seller, license and support articles | cannot publish restricted economics/rights evidence |

## Controlled self-healing

May retry idempotent webhook/reconciliation jobs in test or approved replay paths, rebuild catalog indexes, repair generated links, requeue failed fulfillment checks and open missing documentation deltas.

May not silently issue/refund funds, grant/revoke licenses, modify prices/commissions, waive rights holds, change signed seller terms or expose protected files.
