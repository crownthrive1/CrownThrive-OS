# Good Shit Only Historical Commerce Registry

## Identity and present status

- **Stable platform ID:** `ct.platform.gso`
- **Canonical name:** Good Shit Only
- **Alias:** GSO
- **Owner/operator:** CrownThrive, LLC
- **Current Virality role:** historical marketplace and rights-routing provenance
- **Active Virality licensing authority:** CHLOM
- **Activation state:** no active GSO license issuance is asserted by this registry

GSO remains a valid CrownThrive identity and may have marketplace history outside Virality. For Virality, its former licensing route is preserved as historical evidence only. Do not delete the GSO identity or its source fingerprint; mark the Virality-to-GSO route historical.

## Current Virality boundary

Virality first-party digital products use:

- Virality Credits as the customer tender;
- signed payment-provider events only for approved credit top-ups and reversals;
- CHLOM for license state, authority, fingerprints, holds, and remedies;
- private Google Drive custody for canonical sources;
- protected, integrity-checked runtime delivery.

GSO must not issue or expand a Virality license, activate a product, or turn a payment into permission.

## Historical object model

Historical GSO records may include seller, product, edition, offer, price, order, payment, entitlement, license, fulfillment, refund, payout, and evidence identifiers. Preserve these as provenance. A legacy field name such as `gso_license_id` may remain for backward compatibility but does not establish current authority.

## Non-equivalences

- historical GSO route ≠ active Virality licensing authority;
- product card ≠ approved release;
- payment ≠ entitlement;
- entitlement ≠ commercial license;
- personal access ≠ reproduction, resale, adaptation, manufacturing, performance, or training rights;
- Drive custody ≠ public file access;
- marketplace identity ≠ verified seller authority.

## CHLOM handoff

All current Virality rights decisions route to CHLOM.

```text
Virality order
  → entitlement
  → CHLOM license and fingerprint evidence
  → protected delivery
  → usage, refund, dispute, hold, and remedy evidence
```

A compatibility integration may expose a read-only `gso.chlom_handoff.get_status` operation. It must not issue, revoke, or alter a CHLOM license.

## Historical lifecycle

Historical records may describe:

```text
listed → payment observed → order recorded → entitlement recorded
       → legacy rights route observed → fulfilled|refunded|disputed|archived
```

These states are evidentiary, not proof that the old rail is currently operational.

## Controlled automation

Allowed:

- reconcile historical identifiers;
- preserve and verify source fingerprints;
- map legacy records to CHLOM authority;
- rebuild read-only indexes;
- open documentation or support deltas.

Not allowed:

- silently issue or broaden rights;
- mark a product active from a historical listing;
- move money, refund, or revoke without an approved transaction path;
- expose protected files;
- rewrite historical evidence.
