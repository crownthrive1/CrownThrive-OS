# BrandCards Institutional Registry

## Institutional identity

BrandCards is a CrownThrive profile/share/contact surface intended to make a person, creator, professional, brand, partner or organization portable across digital and phygital touchpoints without replacing the durable institutional identity layer.

- **Stable platform ID:** `ct.platform.brandcards`
- **Canonical identity dependency:** CrownThrive ID
- **Primary corridor:** Technology & Operating Spine / Education, Talent & Community / Growth & Distribution
- **Phase:** 2.98 Pass D4
- **Current implementation state:** `recovered_role_current_deployment_unverified`

The recovered portfolio and Phase 2.7 materials establish BrandCards as a distinct platform record requiring explicit disposition. The Holdings operating plan also uses BrandCards as part of the progression/business-building path. Current engine, source repository, domain, provider account, live profile count, QR/NFC implementation, pricing and API state remain verification-gated.

<Warning>
  BrandCards is not CrownThrive ID. A public profile or QR code is a projection/handoff surface; identity authority, roles, consent, entitlements and sensitive account records remain in their governing systems.
</Warning>

## Data-class separation

Public cards may expose only explicitly published fields. Restricted data remains in CrownThrive ID or the relevant source platform.

### Public-safe examples

- display name;
- public brand/business name;
- public bio/headline;
- approved photo/logo;
- public social/profile links;
- public business contact channel;
- public services/offers;
- approved verification badge;
- public location or service area when intentionally disclosed.

### Restricted examples

- legal identity documents;
- home address unless explicitly published for a business purpose;
- private email/phone;
- payment/bank/tax data;
- internal role/permission details;
- private support or legal records;
- private health/spiritual/family data;
- raw analytics identifiers;
- credentials or secrets.

## CrownThrive ID relationship

```text
CrownThrive ID
→ durable actor/organization identity
→ role/consent/assurance
→ BrandCard public projection
→ QR/NFC/link share
→ target platform or action
```

Changing a BrandCard must not mutate legal identity or institutional roles by implication. A verification badge must resolve to a governed verification record and expiry/revocation state.

## QR/NFC and routing controls

Every encoded/shareable payload should be versioned and minimally scoped.

```yaml
share_payload_id:
brandcard_id:
payload_type: url|deep_link|signed_reference|contact_card
version:
created_at:
expires_at:
redirect_policy_id:
analytics_allowed:
restricted_parameters_present: false
```

Do not embed access tokens, private identifiers, secrets, payment credentials or privileged role assertions in QR/NFC payloads.

Redirects must preserve anti-phishing controls, canonical-domain verification and an incident/takedown path.

## Verification and badges

Potential badges may represent identity, provider status, program completion, membership, partner status or other approved facts. Badge labels must identify their authority and effective period.


A CrownThriveU certificate, Locticians verification, Crown Ambassador role and CHLOM permission are separate record types and should not be collapsed into one “verified” status.

## Analytics boundary

Minimum public/profile metrics may include views, QR scans, link clicks and downstream conversion events where consent and platform rules permit.

CrownLytics should normalize events without turning a BrandCard into a surveillance profile. Sensitive cross-platform behavior must not be joined merely because the same CrownThrive ID exists.

## Safety, abuse and correction

Required controls include:

- impersonation reporting;
- trademark/name conflict handling;
- malicious-link scanning or review;
- prohibited content and fraud policy;
- badge revocation;
- privacy/contact suppression;
- correction history;
- archived/retired redirect policy;
- child/minor profile restrictions where applicable.

No agent may create a public profile for a person from scraped/private data without authority.

## Agent boundaries

| Agent | Autonomous work | Prohibited authority |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Profile QA Agent | validate links, metadata, image dimensions, duplicate routes and public-field schema | cannot publish private data or assert identity verification |
| Badge Verification Agent | compare badge state to authoritative record and remove stale display after approved revocation event | cannot create a credential or professional license |
| Link Safety Agent | detect broken/malicious destinations and pause unsafe links under preapproved policy | cannot alter commercial destination/economics without owner authority |
| Documentation Agent | update current card fields, APIs, support articles and changelog | cannot reveal restricted profiles or analytics identifiers |

## Controlled self-healing

Permitted: repair generated QR assets, refresh public-safe badges from authoritative state, flag broken links, rebuild search indexes, restore known-good card rendering and open correction/incident deltas.

Prohibited silent actions: invent profile data, change public identity, switch affiliate ownership, alter paid offers, expose restricted data or override a takedown/appeal.
