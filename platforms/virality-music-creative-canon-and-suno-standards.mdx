## Virality Music Creative Canon and Production Standards

Virality Music treats songs, albums, books, characters, voices, artwork, playlists, and adaptations as versioned cultural assets. The current Music Maker canon is governed through source records, stable IDs, corrections, privacy boundaries, and work-specific rights evidence.

## Current production contract

- Suno v5.5 is the default production target.
- Generation-ready delivery separates Title, Style, Lyrics, and Advanced Exclude fields.
- Style remains under 1,000 characters; Lyrics remains under 5,000 characters, including spaces, tags, punctuation, symbols, and line breaks.
- Prompts use concrete audible behavior and avoid the prohibited production term recorded in the restricted prompt linter.
- ReMi supports narrow concept and critique passes; human direction, revision, cultural judgment, selection, and final approval remain authoritative.
- Named-artist imitation, celebrity voice imitation, and unauthorized recordings, voices, stems, samples, or catalogs are prohibited.

## Story-world model

Every universe records its thesis, audience, geography, eras, characters, relationships, timeline, voice map, music DNA, works, rights, privacy, source generations, and corrections. A public playlist or page is a distribution surface, not proof of canon acceptance or licensing authority.

Current governed families include Willie & Maybelle Jenkins, TrapOpera: The Family Archive, The ZaZa Room, PACIFIC PRESSURE, Word House, Little Crowns/Black Baby Universe, Tendernism, and Bless the House Creator Systems, with other production lanes retained according to their independent source state.

## Voice custody

A fictional voice is a behavior map: register, rhythm, diction, grain, timing, emotional defense, contradiction, and silence. A real human voice requires explicit consent for the exact synthetic, training, release, adaptation, term, territory, and revocation scope.

Willie & Maybelle music preserves equal narrative authority, rotating leads and duets, Black Southern specificity, grown-folks humor, and a three-layer structure: comic surface, human middle, honest bottom.

## Correction without erasure

Current work uses Jenkins for Willie and Maybelle. Historical Carter references remain preserved as source history where applicable. Current editions keep Maybelle's James Earle history separate from Willie's Rosetta history. TrapOpera keeps Trent Maddox and Terrence Hollis III as separate people under the controlling correction record.

Conflicting editions are preserved as separate generations until an authorized decision assigns canonical, corrected, superseded, alternate-continuity, or archived state.

## Catalog and history

SoundCloud remains a development library, archive, and audience surface; it is not connected through the prohibited SoundCloud API. External DSP distribution remains a deliberately selected commercial catalog. Dated metrics and playlist orders are historical snapshots and never become timeless claims.

The current verified SoundCloud baseline is dated August 13, 2026: 1,114,510 plays, 4,335 tracks, 20,484 minutes, 11,216 likes, 637 comments, 274 reposts, and 3 downloads. Later or earlier observations remain separately dated and classified.

## Rights boundary

Platform commercial permission is not a copyright determination. Every release or license must identify composition, master, human contribution, AI/tool provenance, voice and likeness consent, samples, artwork, contributors, media, territory, term, exclusivity, restrictions, and signed authority.

Public documentation does not reproduce unpublished lyrics, masters, protected identities, private prompt grammar, scoring weights, internal benchmarks, contracts, or restricted rights evidence.
