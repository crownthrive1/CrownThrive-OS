# Virality Music Creative Canon and Production Standards

Virality Music treats songs, albums, books, characters, voices, artwork, playlists, and adaptations as versioned cultural assets. The current Music Maker canon is governed through source records, stable IDs, corrections, privacy boundaries, and work-specific rights evidence.

**Current policy observation:** August 24, 2026

## Current production contract

- Suno v5.5 is the default production target.
- Generation-ready delivery separates Title, Style, Lyrics, and Advanced Exclude fields.
- Style remains under 1,000 characters; Lyrics remains under 5,000 characters, including spaces, tags, punctuation, symbols, and line breaks.
- Prompts use concrete audible behavior and avoid the prohibited production term recorded in the restricted prompt linter.
- ReMi supports narrow concept and critique passes; human direction, revision, cultural judgment, selection, and final approval remain authoritative.
- Named-artist imitation, celebrity voice imitation, and unauthorized recordings, voices, stems, samples, or catalogs are prohibited.

## Suno terms gate effective September 3, 2026

Suno’s [terms revised August 10, 2026](https://suno.com/terms-september-2026) take effect September 3, 2026. The [download-policy FAQ](https://help.suno.com/en/articles/13614785) is treated as a provider constraint, not as a CrownThrive customer license.

A work may pass the Suno provider-commercialization gate only when all of the following are evidenced:

1. the output was generated while the account was on a paid tier;
2. the exact output was obtained through a permitted Suno-approved download channel;
3. the output is not a remix;
4. every input and contributor was authorized for the intended use; and
5. the work-level provenance and human-contribution record is complete.

Basic/free outputs and remixes remain personal and noncommercial under these terms. Stream-ripping or recording playback is not treated as a permitted download. Provider fingerprints, watermarks, and metadata must remain intact.

The observed plan allowances are 20 downloads per month for Pro, 60 per month for Premier, and seven lifetime noncommercial downloads for Free, with no rollover. These are dated provider facts and must be reverified before use.

<Warning>
  Passing the Suno provider gate does not prove copyright, exclusivity, chain of title, absence of conflict, publishing control, voice or likeness authority, or a right to grant a broader downstream license. CrownThrive customer terms must remain within both the provider constraint and the rights CrownThrive can evidence work by work.
</Warning>

## Required work-level provenance

Each candidate records the provider and terms version, output ID, generation date and tier, permitted-download date/channel/tier, download-receipt fingerprint, remix status, commercial basis, input and contributor evidence, human contribution, distribution release ID, and retained terms snapshot.

Missing evidence returns **HOLD**. A complete record returns only **PASS\_PROVIDER\_GATE**; a customer-facing license, release approval, and commerce activation remain separate decisions.

Public machine-readable policy: [https://vm.crownthrive.com/api/governance/suno-commercialization](https://vm.crownthrive.com/api/governance/suno-commercialization)

## Rights boundary

Platform commercial permission is not a copyright determination. Every release or license must identify composition, master, human contribution, AI/tool provenance, voice and likeness consent, samples, artwork, contributors, media, territory, term, exclusivity, restrictions, and signed authority.

Public documentation does not reproduce unpublished lyrics, masters, protected identities, private prompt grammar, scoring weights, internal benchmarks, contracts, or restricted rights evidence.
