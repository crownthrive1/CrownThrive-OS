# ThriveGather Institutional Registry

## Identity

- **Stable platform ID:** `ct.platform.thrivegather`
- **Canonical name:** ThriveGather™
- **Owner/operator:** CrownThrive, LLC
- **Primary role:** governed virtual gathering, cohort, workshop, co-working, sponsor and community-event environment.
- **Recovered provider lineage:** Help Center titles identify ThriveGather as historically **powered by GoBrunch**.
- **Current evidence state:** a large recovered Help Center family establishes intended roles and operations; current GoBrunch account, workspaces, plan, URLs, data, recordings, API/export and active-event state remain unverified.

A provider room existing does not prove the event is approved, recorded, licensed, accessible or monetized.

## Access and moderation

Every room/event must define:

- audience and maturity/age restrictions;
- public, member, invite-only, organization, paid, sponsor or private access;
- capacity and waitlist rules;
- host/moderator authority;
- conduct and removal rules;
- chat, microphone, camera, screen-share, file and breakout privileges;
- report/appeal path;
- accessibility/accommodation route;
- regional/time-zone presentation;
- retention of chat, attendance, files and recordings.

Moderators may manage live privileges within delegated scope. They do not automatically gain access to private member profiles, payment data, sponsor contracts or restricted recordings.

## Recording and reuse

Recording, transcription, clips, screenshots, chat export and replay access require explicit event-level rules.

- participant notice/consent where required;
- speaker/host/guest releases;
- music, slides, images and third-party content rights;
- minor/sensitive-session restrictions;
- sponsor and promotional use;
- editing/derivative/clip permissions;
- retention, download and deletion;
- who may access or purchase a replay;
- corrections and takedowns.

Room attendance does not automatically transfer a participant's voice, likeness, presentation or private statements to CrownThrive.

## Sponsorship and monetization

ThriveGather sponsor packages may include room branding, named sessions, approved announcements, creative placements, hosted segments, attendee-facing resources and aggregate reporting. Sponsorship must remain labeled and governed through AdLuxe/CHLOM controls.

Potential economics include memberships, event registration, sponsor packages, premium rooms, organization access, replay products, host services or bundles. Historical help titles about monetizing tiers do not prove current prices or provider billing.

## Agent model

| Agent | Permitted autonomous work | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Access Preflight Agent | compare entitlement, event, tier, time and capacity state | cannot reveal private keys or override denied access |
| Event Operations Agent | prepare schedules, reminders, room metadata and checklists | cannot activate unapproved sponsor/recording terms |
| Moderator Support Agent | surface rules, reports and live operational guidance | cannot silently ban, impersonate or access unrelated private records |
| Recording Rights Agent | verify notices/releases/source materials and replay permissions | cannot infer consent from attendance alone |
| Documentation Agent | reconstruct member/host/sponsor/troubleshooting articles | cannot republish old workspace URLs, secret keys or unsupported provider instructions |

## Controlled self-healing

Permitted low-risk recovery includes retrying read-only room status, rebuilding schedules, repairing approved embeds/links, reissuing an entitlement through the governing system, switching to read-only/event-cancelled messaging under a documented outage rule and opening incidents/deltas.

Agents must not silently publish recordings, expand room access, reveal secret keys, change membership prices, alter sponsor obligations or represent provider availability as guaranteed.
