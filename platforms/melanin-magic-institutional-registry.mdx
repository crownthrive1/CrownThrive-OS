## Product and claims governance

Every actively sold product should resolve:

- exact product/variant/SKU;
- manufacturer/vendor and current authorization;
- formulation/specification source;
- ingredient declaration and allergen/sensitivity warnings where applicable;
- packaging/label version;
- quantity/net contents;
- instructions and intended use;
- claim inventory and substantiation state;
- current price and taxes/discount rules;
- stock/availability state;
- lot/batch or traceability method where relevant;
- fulfillment/shipping/return/refund policy;
- consumer support path;
- professional/wholesale terms where applicable;
- product-photo/media rights;
- current provider and vendor-exit record.

No agent may convert historical marketing language into a medical, therapeutic, disease-treatment, guaranteed-growth, guaranteed-result or other regulated/high-consequence claim without current substantiation and review.

## Consumer, professional and wholesale separation

### Consumer

Retail purchase, product education, routine building, fulfillment, support and retention.

### Professional / Stylepreneur

Professional product use, education, referral/affiliate participation, service-business handoffs and approved promotional materials. Professional participation does not itself create a wholesale or MM Suites right.

### Wholesale / retailer

Business verification, terms, approved SKUs, quantities/minimums, resale permissions, territory/channel rules, pricing schedule, order/fulfillment, tax/resale documentation, marketing standards and suspension/revocation.

Wholesale access does not transfer trademarks, formulas, product-development rights or manufacturing authority.

## Agent model

| Agent | Autonomous work | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Product Metadata Agent | normalize SKUs, files, labels, images, offers and provider mappings | cannot invent formulas, claims, compliance or stock |
| Claims Sentinel | detect unsupported/medical/high-risk language and open review | cannot approve regulated claims alone |
| Wholesale QA Agent | validate applicant completeness, terms version, price/quantity metadata | cannot bind or terminate strategic accounts without authority |
| Commerce Reconciliation Agent | compare checkout, order, fulfillment, refund and inventory evidence | cannot issue unapproved refunds/write-offs |
| Documentation Agent | update product/wholesale/support articles from approved records | cannot publish restricted formulas or unreviewed claims |
| Vendor Drift Agent | monitor provider docs/export/availability and open migration tasks | cannot switch providers or expose credentials autonomously |

## Self-check and controlled self-healing

Permitted low-risk automation includes rebuilding product indexes, repairing broken product/article links, retrying idempotent provider reads, refreshing dated provider/offer snapshots, flagging stale inventory and reopening failed documentation tasks.

Self-healing stops before changing formulas, labels, ingredient declarations, claims, prices, refunds, vendor contracts, wholesale terms, credentials or production inventory without the applicable authority.
