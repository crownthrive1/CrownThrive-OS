## Institutional role

PentaAds Placement OS is CrownThrive's governed, source-neutral placement and
installation layer. It can produce placement discovery, preservation,
deduplication, installation and public-readback evidence. Independent
governance determines whether that evidence is accepted.

PentaAds does not replace:

- the verified source/write authority of the target site;
- AdLuxe campaign, inventory, provider or commercial authority;
- consent, privacy, rights or brand-safety controls;
- independent review or provider/runtime readback.

## Authority and impact boundary

The source binding has a D2 ceiling and creates no autonomous D3 authority.
It grants no spend, contract-change, rights-change or credential-change
authority and does not authorize mutation of `melanatedvoices.com`.

Operational, economic and privacy impact remain `UNKNOWN` until independently
assessed for the exact deployment. That limitation is separate from whether a
display placement renders successfully.
