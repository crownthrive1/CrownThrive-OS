# CII, ThriveFund & Sustainability Institutional Registry

## Institutional identity

This registry governs the current CrownThrive impact/reinvestment architecture without silently adopting historical charity, fund, equity, token or securities language.

| Surface | Stable ID | Current institutional function |
| --- | --- | --- |
| CrownThrive Impact Institute | `ct.platform.cii` | impact criteria, capability-building, program standards, evidence and public-accountability framework |
| ThriveFund | `ct.program.thrivefund` | internal CrownThrive-aligned reinvestment/impact allocation mechanism |
| EcoDrive | `ct.program.ecodrive` | historical/current-reconciliation sustainability concept; provider/operating state not presumed |

**Owner/operator:** CrownThrive, LLC unless later legal/entity evidence establishes another owner.<br />**Phase:** 2.98 Pass D4<br />**Evidence posture:** current public policy \+ founder adjudication \+ Holdings planning \+ Phase 2.7 recovery requirements.

<Warning>
  CII is not presumed to be a nonprofit. ThriveFund is not presumed to be a regulated investment fund, charity, foundation, securities vehicle or tax-advantaged account. EcoDrive/Stripe Climate or any outside provider relationship must be verified separately before current public claims are made.
</Warning>

## Purpose and operating relationship

CII governs **why** an impact/reinvestment program exists, what qualifies, what evidence is required and how public accountability works.

ThriveFund governs **which designated internal value is allocated**, under what rule, for what approved purpose, subject to what milestones/holds/corrections and with what accounting/evidence trail.

CHLOM governs applicable rights, rules, eligibility, approvals, agreements and audit references. CrownLytics governs normalized measurement. CrownPulse may surface approved signals. CrownThrive Support preserves current policy/help records and correction history.

## MM Suites and corridor alignment

MM Suites may route an approved corporate share, fee allocation, retained margin, reserve, program contribution or other defined economic stream into ThriveFund. The exact instrument, percentage, accounting treatment and effective date remain noncanonical until recorded in the Revenue Registry and approved through current legal/accounting governance.

The impact mechanism should be able to support approved pilots, stabilization, capability-building, education, accessibility, community outcomes, sustainability or other adopted objectives without converting an operating payment into an investment or charitable contribution by implication.

## Sustainability evidence model

Sustainability claims must follow evidence rather than branding.

```text
program approved
→ eligible economic/environmental trigger
→ allocation approved
→ amount actually transmitted
→ provider/recipient receipt
→ impact/output evidence
→ normalized CrownLytics record
→ claim review
→ public statement
→ correction/version history
```

Potential evidence classes include:

- provider receipts or certificates;
- verified purchase/retirement/offset records where applicable;
- project milestone evidence;
- invoices and transaction records;
- participant/service counts with definitions;
- accessibility or community-delivery records;
- sustainability-provider status;
- public methodology and limitations.

No agent may convert a marketing promise, planned allocation, provider dashboard screenshot or historical partnership into a current impact result without dated evidence.

## Public claim taxonomy

Approved public statements should identify what is being claimed:

- amount allocated;
- amount transmitted;
- program funded;
- people/accounts/locations served under a defined method;
- output delivered;
- independently verified result where applicable;
- provider-supported sustainability activity;
- percentage of a defined economic lane allocated under an effective schedule.

Do not use “donation,” “charitable,” “tax deductible,” “investment,” “fund return,” “guaranteed impact,” “equity,” or equivalent regulated language unless the applicable legal and evidence state supports it.

## Program and participant roles

| Role | Authority |
| --- | --- |
| Founder/authorized executive | adopts programs, economic schedules and high-consequence public claims |
| CII program steward | prepares eligibility, milestones, evidence standards and operating reviews |
| ThriveFund allocation operator | prepares/resolves allocation records within approved schedules |
| Finance/reconciliation | verifies source transactions, transmitted value, receipts and accounting classification |
| Impact/evidence reviewer | validates outputs/outcomes and methodology |
| Legal/compliance reviewer | reviews regulated-language, entity, tax, consumer, securities, franchise and agreement implications |

No single agent should originate, approve, transmit and publicly certify a material allocation.

## Agent boundaries and self-healing

Permitted autonomous work includes:

- detect missing receipts/evidence;
- reconcile internal IDs and program versions;
- compare approved schedules against recorded allocations;
- refresh public aggregate reports after authoritative records are approved;
- flag stale provider claims;
- open holds/deltas;
- repair documentation metadata and links.

Agents must not silently:

- create or move money;
- change an allocation percentage;
- convert a program into equity/investment/charity;
- guarantee outcomes;
- delete prior corrections;
- represent planned value as transmitted value;
- publish protected recipient information;
- infer EcoDrive/Stripe Climate provider status from historical references.
