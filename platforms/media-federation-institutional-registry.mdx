# CrownThrive Media Federation Institutional Registry

## Mission

CrownThrive's media architecture must behave as a federation of specialized systems, not a forced monolith. The governing Holdings/media sources explicitly position the **Melanated Voices Platform (MVP)** as the long-term discovery/orchestration experience while preserving specialized publishing, streaming, radio, music, reader and production systems of record.

**Federation rule:** normalize metadata once, route authorized playback/read/watch links through CrownThrive IO/MCP, preserve the authoritative source system, and never migrate content merely to make the architecture look centralized.

## MVP — orchestration, not replacement

MVP should eventually unify:

- discovery/search;
- CrownThrive identity handoff;
- cross-media library;
- entitlement awareness;
- recommendations;
- audio/video/music/article/book/live-experience discovery;
- creator/profile relationships.

MVP should **not recreate** Virality Music, Go Flipbooks, Melanated TV/Streamit, channel systems such as Viloud, or the Melanated Voices audio engine. Those specialized systems remain source systems until a governed migration explicitly changes that state.

## Melanated Voices

Historical/current CrownThrive sources describe Melanated Voices as the audio/podcast/spoken-media lane. Prior Gmail evidence confirms past operational account activity and creator-content notices, establishing historical operational use.

Current direct-host retrieval was not sufficiently verified in this Pass B web sweep. Therefore:

- platform identity and historical operations: `verified_history`;
- current public-host state: `unverified_current`;
- current engine/vendor/API state: `unverified_current`;
- archival/user/content migration state: `requires_inventory`.

Older public descriptions also mention broader music, radio, audiobook, AI-podcast, NFT and revenue-sharing capabilities. Those remain `public_claims_requiring_current_reconciliation`, not automatically current functionality.

## Melanated TV

The current public `melanatedtv.com` property is reachable and populated with film/video/music/cultural programming and CrownThrive branding.

Historical/management engine records associate the property with:

- Streamit Laravel for OTT/VOD;
- Streamit Short Drama add-on;
- Viloud for programmed/linear channels and cross-channel distribution.

These provider assignments are management/source evidence and require current admin/license verification before being labeled active production dependencies.

Melanated TV should own video-program metadata, playback/source references, series/season/episode relationships, content suitability, captions/accessibility, rights windows, subscriptions/paywalls where applicable, and programmed-channel relationships.

## Melanated Voices TV and Locticians TV

These should be modeled as distinct channel/distribution manifestations, not aliases for their source communities.

### Melanated Voices TV

- preserves Roku/video manifestation and historical channel identity;
- receives approved content from Melanated Voices and the wider federation;
- must retain old channel/app/store identifiers during rebuild/sunset work;
- cannot imply ownership of underlying source IP merely because it distributes it.

### Locticians TV

- beauty/wellness tutorials, interviews, events, professional education and community programming;
- receives rights-cleared beauty/professional content from Locticians and partners;
- provides approved AdLuxe inventory where suitable;
- must keep professional/community claims distinct from provider-quality guarantees.

## The Mane Experience and TAME

### The Mane Experience

Participatory culture-first media: articles, shorts, music, polls, quizzes, creator/community participation and discovery. It is an editorial/community destination rather than a universal source-of-truth database.

### The TAME Gallery

Aggregation/intelligence layer for feeds, deduplication, trend detection and editorial handoff. It must not auto-publish scraped or aggregated content as if CrownThrive owns the underlying rights. Every downstream reuse needs source attribution, license/permission state and transformation purpose.

## Federation content-supply lifecycle

1. recruit creators, artists, filmmakers, educators, podcasters, brands and institutional partners;
2. verify identity, chain of title, releases, permitted uses, territories, duration, monetization and revenue terms through CHLOM-compatible records;
3. perform technical, editorial, accessibility, brand/cultural and safety review;
4. assign approved content to source catalogs/channels/collections/episodes/events/social packages/ad inventory;
5. syndicate metadata and authorized links through MVP/CrownThrive IO rather than duplicating masters indiscriminately;
6. distribute to the appropriate specialized systems;
7. measure watch/read/listen/conversion/retention/ad-delivery/purchase/subscription/creator performance in CrownLytics;
8. preserve corrections, takedowns, expiration and rights changes across all projections.

## Media identity and versioning

Every media object needs:

- stable media/work/edition/episode IDs;
- source platform and source record;
- creator/contributor identities;
- master/source location reference;
- rights profile and license windows;
- audience/content controls;
- transcript/caption/subtitle state;
- derivative relationships;
- distribution endpoints;
- publication/current/superseded state;
- analytics/event references;
- correction/takedown history.

A `published` state on one endpoint does not mean every other endpoint is authorized or current.

## Cross-platform distribution contract

CrownThrive IO/MCP should provide adapter contracts for:

- source-system discovery;
- metadata normalization;
- entitlement verification;
- authorized playback/reader URLs;
- publish/schedule where explicitly supported;
- channel/program synchronization;
- takedown/hold propagation;
- analytics event normalization.

No adapter should receive more credentials or data than required. Restricted partner feeds and unreleased masters stay outside public search/MCP projections.

## Commercialization

Media monetization may include:

- AdLuxe inventory and sponsorship;
- premium memberships/paywalls where the source platform supports them;
- direct products/books/printables/courses/tickets;
- content licensing/syndication;
- brand integration and creator campaigns;
- production services;
- transparent affiliate/commerce routing;
- CrownRewards/ThrivePerks retention incentives.

Every transaction must resolve the correct source rights. A distribution platform's checkout cannot manufacture rights it does not hold.

## Vendor independence

Known or historical engines such as RKHM-family products, Streamit, Viloud, Riverside, Radio.co and other SaaS providers are implementation records—not institutional identities. Each needs:

- provider/product/version;
- account owner;
- license/support state;
- export path;
- APIs/webhooks;
- data ownership;
- replacement path;
- last verified timestamp.

## Phase 2.99 / Phase 3 handoff

Pass B should seed the media federation with stable platform IDs, source-system relationships, normalized media/content schemas, rights windows, provider/adapter registry, entitlement contracts, source-link routing, syndication rules, corrections/takedown propagation and analytics events.

**Current conclusion:** the CrownThrive media lane is a federated architecture with multiple distinct systems of record. MVP is the future experience/orchestration layer—not a license to collapse those systems or erase their provenance.
