## CrownThrive Media Federation Institutional Registry

## Mission

CrownThrive media should operate as a federation of specialized systems, not a forced monolith. The Holdings/media architecture positions **Melanated Voices Platform (MVP)** as a future cross-media discovery/orchestration experience while preserving specialized systems of record.

**Federation rule:** normalize metadata and route authorized playback/read/watch links through CrownThrive IO/MCP without erasing the authoritative source system or rights lineage.

## MVP — orchestration, not replacement

MVP may unify discovery/search, identity handoff, cross-media library, entitlement awareness and recommendations. It should not recreate or silently replace Virality Music, Go Flipbooks, Melanated TV, channel systems or the Melanated Voices audio engine.

Every media item should retain a canonical source platform/record, rights profile, playback/reader URL, audience class, availability window and source version.

## Media property boundaries

Melanated Voices historically owns the audio/spoken-media lane. Melanated TV owns video/OTT/channel presentation. Melanated Voices TV and Locticians TV are distribution/channel manifestations and do not own underlying source IP merely because they carry it.

The Mane Experience is an editorial/community destination. TAME is an aggregation/intelligence layer and must not auto-publish aggregated third-party material as owned CrownThrive content.

## Content-supply lifecycle

1. recruit creators/partners;
2. verify identity, rights, releases, territories, duration, monetization and revenue terms;
3. perform technical/editorial/accessibility/cultural review;
4. assign approved content to source catalogs/channels;
5. normalize metadata and authorized links through IO/MVP;
6. distribute through the correct specialized system;
7. measure activity in CrownLytics;
8. propagate corrections, holds, takedowns and expiry across projections.

## Required media record

Every media object needs stable work/edition/episode IDs, source system, creators/contributors, source/master reference, rights window, audience controls, accessibility state, derivatives, distribution endpoints, publication state, analytics references and correction/takedown history.

A `published` state on one endpoint does not authorize every other endpoint.
