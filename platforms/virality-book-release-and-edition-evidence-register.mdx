## Release evidence dimensions

No title should be assigned one blanket `ready` state. Resolve these dimensions independently:

```text
catalog/source record
→ authoritative source/master artifact
→ source provenance + checksum
→ edition/version identity
→ technical QA
→ creator/publisher approval
→ rights approval
→ canon approval
→ accessibility/device/print evidence
→ reader/platform upload
→ reader/live-route verification
→ product/price/Stripe mapping
→ checkout
→ entitlement/delivery
→ returning access
→ refund/revocation/expiry
→ live offer
→ monitoring/correction/supersession
```

A later stage does not automatically prove an earlier stage unless the evidence packet explicitly contains it.

## Audit scope rule

The August 14 release audit says it is an **evidence-backed snapshot of local repository and scratch artifacts only**. Therefore findings such as `binary not present` mean `not found inside the audited local scope`, not `the asset does not exist anywhere in CrownThrive's source estate`.

Later File Library recovery can supersede an artifact-existence finding without erasing the original audit's validity for its stated scope.

### Upload-plan packaging correction

The later Go Flipbooks plan marks Books 09–12 **`Fix rear cover, then upload`**. It requires replacing the incomplete rear-cover sentence ending in `voice`, rerendering and visually inspecting every page before upload.

Therefore the correct progression is:

## Catalog-claim-only cohort

Expansion/attached-V4/creator-system rows may say `PDF complete`, `file complete`, or `Go Flipbooks upload pending` while the audited workspace contains no checksumed master binary.

Canonical rule:

> A catalog completion claim is metadata evidence, not a deliverable master.

Before activation:

- recover or rebuild the asserted master;
- hash and provenance it;
- obtain current creator/publisher/rights/canon approval;
- run PDF/EPUB/print/accessibility QA as applicable;
- map the exact product/price/license;
- test reader/checkout/fulfillment.

## Go Flipbooks standalone upload controls

The upload plan's non-negotiable rules include:

1. standalone titles rather than hidden bundle substitution;
2. upload the exact approved PDF without recompression or silent renaming;
3. paid reader `noindex`; public product page indexable/canonical;
4. downloads/printing off unless product-specific approval grants them;
5. never publish a URL, price, inventory or availability claim before real testing.

The post-upload verification log is the stronger platform-activation evidence. A row remains unverified until it contains the actual reader/product IDs/URLs, tested price, upload date, mobile/desktop/page-count/age-gate/checkout/returning-access/noindex evidence and owner approval.

## Stripe evidence

## Rights/canon/economic discipline

A title may be technically valid while still held for:

- creator/publisher approval;
- chain of title;
- AI/provider/source provenance;
- likeness/voice/character permission;
- canon conflict;
- maturity/age-gate;
- price approval;
- accessibility;
- reader/platform verification;
- commerce/legal review.

Current pricing is versioned economic data. Proposed prices from a handoff spreadsheet remain proposals until the governing authority approves and the live product/price mapping is verified.
