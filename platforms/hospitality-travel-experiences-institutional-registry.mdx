## Booking and reservation boundaries

A reservation request, ticket purchase, payment, provider confirmation and actual service delivery are different states.

```text
experience/service discovered
→ availability requested
→ price/terms resolved
→ booking/order created
→ payment attempted
→ provider confirmation
→ customer confirmation
→ delivery/check-in
→ completion
→ refund/dispute/reconciliation if needed
```

No agent may represent an unconfirmed provider reservation as booked.

## Travel and third-party provider controls

Travel and hospitality may involve hotels, venues, restaurants, transportation providers, independent hosts, guides and other third parties. Every external relationship should identify:

- provider legal/business identity;
- service scope;
- cancellation/refund rules;
- taxes/fees;
- accessibility and accommodation information;
- insurance/liability allocation;
- safety/emergency contacts;
- licenses/permits where relevant;
- customer-data sharing;
- payment/settlement model;
- support/escalation route;
- evidence of confirmation and completion.

CrownThrive must not imply it owns, operates, insures or guarantees a third-party service unless the relationship actually supports that claim.

## Rooted Noir boundary

Rooted Noir may orchestrate culturally aligned wedding-planning workflows, vendors, schedules, checklists, budgets and content. It must distinguish planning/support from regulated professional advice, venue/vendor contracts, officiant/licensing requirements, insurance and actual vendor performance.

## ThriveKiln boundary

ThriveKiln's source-defined role is restaurant/local-hospitality operations. Candidate capabilities may include table/reservation, menu/order, customer, venue, staff, payment and reporting workflows depending on the actual TableTrack implementation. Vendor documentation or a purchased license does not prove those capabilities are configured in CrownThrive production.

## ThriveBooking boundary

The current registry treats ThriveBooking as a reserved/reposition ecosystem booking marketplace candidate. It must not duplicate ThriveSeat, ThriveTickets, ThriveGather, CrownConsults or third-party booking systems without a clear source-of-truth boundary.

Potential role:

```text
cross-platform discovery / request routing
→ canonical source platform
→ booking or quote
→ payment/entitlement where applicable
→ support/reconciliation
```

## Luxperiences boundary

Luxperiences is preserved as the premium spaces, venues, rentals and experiences lane with historical Luxperiences Network/imprint lineage. Before activation, each offer must establish inventory authority, provider/host identity, location rights, pricing, deposit/refund behavior, safety/accessibility, insurance and event/recording/content rights.

## ThriveTravels boundary

ThriveTravels remains an open/P3 travel concept. No destination, itinerary, group trip, travel package or travel-provider relationship is considered current merely because the brand/domain exists. Travel-specific regulatory, seller-of-travel, package-travel, insurance and jurisdiction requirements must be evaluated before customer sales where applicable.

## Data and privacy

Hospitality/travel records may include sensitive itinerary, location, accessibility, emergency-contact or identity information. Use minimum necessary data, purpose limitation, retention rules and relationship-scoped access. Do not feed precise travel/location data into advertising without explicit authority.

## Agent boundaries

Hospitality agents may validate inventory metadata, request availability, reconcile provider confirmations and prepare customer/support records. They may not invent availability, bind CrownThrive to unapproved venue/vendor contracts, waive cancellation terms, issue regulated travel advice or suppress safety incidents.
