# ThrivePush Institutional Registry

## Identity

- **Stable platform ID:** `ct.platform.thrivepush`
- **Canonical name:** ThrivePush
- **Owner/operator:** CrownThrive, LLC
- **Primary role:** consent-based re-engagement, release reminders, abandoned-interest recovery and permissioned communications across approved ecosystem lanes.
- **Current evidence state:** portfolio role is documented; exact provider/engine, connected channels, sender infrastructure, API and live campaign state remain subject to verification.

ThrivePush is a communications/orchestration layer. It must not become an independent source of truth for identity, consent, product state, rights, rewards or analytics.

## Canonical responsibilities

Resolve approved audiences from authorized source systems; honor channel-specific consent and suppression; render approved templates from current data; send through verified providers; receive delivery/bounce/click/unsubscribe events; normalize evidence into CrownLytics; use CrownPulse signals only when purpose and consent permit; and trigger CrownRewards/affiliate outcomes only through explicit current rules.

## Consent and suppression rules

A person appearing in a customer, member, directory, event, rewards or affiliate record does not automatically create permission to message through every channel.

Every send must resolve purpose, source of the contact point, consent/notice state, channel, organization/platform context, suppression state, age/maturity and sensitive-data restrictions where applicable, frequency rules, current template/version and opt-out path.

Private spiritual journals, health data, confidential support records, child/minor profiles, restricted rights evidence and unrelated sensitive attributes cannot be repurposed into ordinary marketing audiences.

## Campaign lifecycle

```text
→ template/version resolution
→ content/offer/rights checks
→ test/preflight
→ send
→ delivery/bounce/complaint/unsubscribe events
→ CrownLytics normalization
→ conversion/retention evidence

```

## Message classes

Transactional/order/access, security/account, support/service, subscription/member, product/content release, abandoned-interest/re-engagement, affiliate/ambassador program, event/ticket, marketing/promotional and required policy/legal messages remain separate. Transactional purpose is not blanket marketing consent.

## Integrations

CrownThrive ID supplies account/contact context; CrownLytics normalizes evidence; CrownPulse supplies approved signals but not consent; CrownRewards supports approved lifecycle notices; Affiliates/Ambassadors use program-scoped communications; AdLuxe provides campaign handoffs; GSO/Melanin Magic/Virality/CrownThriveU/events provide governed triggers; Support transactional notices remain distinct from marketing.

## Agent and self-healing boundaries

Consent agents may block inconsistent sends; campaign QA may validate links/templates/offers; delivery watchers may pause under preapproved complaint/bounce thresholds and open incidents. They may not manufacture consent, expand audiences, expose credentials or silently switch provider/sender. Recovery may retry idempotent status pulls, rebuild previews or quarantine invalid templates; production should not auto-resume after privacy/consent/deliverability incidents without review.
