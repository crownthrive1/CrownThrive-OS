# Phases 3–5 Institutional Program Charter

This charter supersedes narrower pre-recovery descriptions of later phases while preserving their compatible requirements.

## Phase 3 — Executable Institutional Core & Developer Substrate

**Mission:** convert CrownThrive institutional knowledge, IP, governance, identity, APIs and CHLOM architecture into versioned machine-readable infrastructure.

### 3.0 Institutional Bootstrap

Stable IDs; schemas; source authority; platform/API/MCP registries; paper/IP/component/pallet registries; roles; approvals; data classes; environments; versioning; dependency/backup records; synthetic fixtures and evals.

### 3A Trust Core

DAIL/event/evidence service; Approval Service; actor/authority registry; source/evidence authority; correction and audit model.

### 3B Rights & Policy Core

Rights Registry; DLA; Rules/Policy Engine; roles/permissions; provenance; eligibility; policy applicability.

### 3C Economic & Remedy Core

Revenue/split/commission/royalty registries; entitlement state; cases; holds; disputes; remedies; corrections.

### 3D Developer & Integration Core

CrownThrive ID; social identity bridge; CrownThrive IO/MCP; API federation; SDKs; webhooks; sandbox; adapters; developer credentials; platform integration certification.

### 3E Advanced CHLOM Laboratory

LEX; DID/VC; ZK; oracles; advanced treasury/settlement; cryptographic provenance; decentralized components; token/programmatic-value research only where justified.

## Phase 4 — Federated Ecosystem Activation & Thrive Flywheel

**Mission:** prove that specialized CrownThrive platforms can operate as one institution through shared identity, governance, events, attribution, entitlements, analytics and distribution.

Core outputs: phased platform onboarding; shared SSO/account linking; adapter certification; cross-platform entitlements; Crown Affiliates/Ambassadors attribution; CrownRewards; ThrivePush; CrownLytics/CrownPulse/CrownInsights measurement; MM Suites/Suite Pro operating pilots; AdLuxe integration; education/community pathways; support/legal integration; sustainability evidence; role portals and dashboards.

Phase 4 onboards in cohorts, beginning with representative P0/P1 platforms rather than attempting every portfolio record simultaneously.

## Phase 5 — Institutional Scale, Licensing & Sovereign Expansion

**Mission:** scale CrownThrive IP, methods, licensing, developer economy and institutional operating system beyond internal platform integration.

Core outputs may include mature LEX/external licensing; certified developer ecosystem; external APIs/SDK marketplace; Holdings maturity where justified; MM Suites legal/franchise/regional activation when counsel-ready; advanced CHLOM capabilities that prove value; interoperable rights/provenance; selective decentralized architecture; institutional succession; global partner/licensing expansion; and self-sustaining operating automation.

## Permanent phase rule

A phase number must always specify its namespace. Ecosystem maturity, institutional engineering phase, asset lifecycle, product release, migration stage and historical incubator phases are separate dimensions.

## Entry rule

Each phase must inherit the machine-readable state, unresolved-risk register, tests, evidence and recovery controls of the phase before it. No phase begins by discarding the previous phase's institutional memory.
