# Phases 3–5 Institutional Program Charter

This charter supersedes narrower pre-recovery descriptions of later phases while preserving compatible requirements. It is adopted under Phase 2.97 patch release `2.7.9.1` and governs subsequent planning until changed through a documented correction or architecture decision.

## Program doctrine

- Phase 3 makes CrownThrive machine-readable and governable.
- Phase 4 makes the ecosystem operate through the shared spine.
- Phase 5 makes mature CrownThrive capabilities externally scalable and selectively sovereign.
- CHLOM is the rights, policy, authority, economics, record and remedy center—not the entirety of CrownThrive OS.
- MM Suites is the phygital proving ground connecting digital infrastructure to people, place and operator economics.
- every phase inherits stable IDs, versions, evidence, unresolved risks, tests, recovery and public/private boundaries;
- no phase number is a substitute for capability-specific production evidence.

# Phase 3 — Executable Institutional Core and Developer Substrate

## Mission

Convert CrownThrive institutional knowledge, IP, governance, identity, APIs and CHLOM architecture into versioned machine-readable services that platforms, operators, developers and agents can safely use.

## Phase 3.0 — Institutional Bootstrap Release

### Outputs

- private engineering/storage boundary and public projection contract;
- stable ID and record schemas;
- source, paper/IP, framework, component/pallet and platform registries;
- alias, predecessor/successor and version/correction graph;
- organizations, actors, roles, authority and agent registry;
- data classifications, tenant/relationship model and purpose-limited flows;
- API/MCP/domain/repository/deployment/vendor/secret-reference registries;
- environment/IaC/CI/CD/observability/backup baseline;
- Legal Depot policy/applicability/acceptance schemas;
- claims, metrics and evidence levels;
- run packets, evaluations, synthetic fixtures and emergency controls;
- P0/P1 platform state and integration profiles.

### Exit criteria

Schemas validate; seed records are referentially complete; public/restricted isolation is tested; source/claim/rights uncertainty is machine-readable; restore/rollback is demonstrated; no production secret enters public docs or code.

## Phase 3A — Trust Core

### Outputs

- DAIL append-oriented event/audit service;
- Approval Service;
- actor/authority/delegation service;
- evidence-object/reference service;
- correction/supersession/retraction trail;
- holds, emergency pause and break-glass evidence;
- source authority and claim decision support;
- first agent-control-plane integration.

### Exit criteria

Every material mutation emits an attributable event; duplicate/replayed actions are safe; approvals actually block; corrections preserve history; restricted evidence is not exposed through public roles; pause/rollback works.

## Phase 3B — Rights and Policy Core

### Outputs

- Asset/Rights Registry;
- Dynamic Licensing Asset service;
- Rules/Policy and eligibility service;
- role/relationship authorization;
- provenance and chain-of-title graph;
- agreement/license/offer/entitlement states;
- Cultural Imprint Engine and canon-policy hooks;
- policy applicability and acceptance records.

### Exit criteria

Rights cannot silently diverge from DLA/license state; expired/revoked grants fail verification; accepted terms are immutable and amendable only through linked records; unauthorized cross-tenant access fails; source/evidence uncertainty produces hold rather than invented authority.

## Phase 3C — Economic and Remedy Core

### Outputs

- fee, royalty, split, commission, reward, reserve and allocation schedules;
- revenue/settlement/reconciliation registry;
- order/payment/entitlement adapters;
- usage statements and audit;
- cases, disputes, appeals, corrections, revocation and reinstatement;
- refund/chargeback/hold/clawback governance;
- CII/ThriveFund and sustainability allocation/evidence primitives.

### Exit criteria

Economic schedules are versioned by effective period; arithmetic reconciles; retries do not duplicate value; compensation is not confused with ownership; holds propagate; case outcomes and appeals are evidenced; agents cannot reroute funds without authority.

## Phase 3D — Developer, Identity and Integration Core

### Outputs

- CrownThrive ID and social/community identity bridge;
- OAuth/OIDC account linking and assurance;
- CrownThrive IO/API gateway and MCP federation;
- platform API/adapter registry;
- OpenAPI/event contracts, SDKs and webhook libraries;
- sandbox/test keys/synthetic tenants;
- developer identity, terms, scopes, certification and contribution controls;
- first platform MCPs and common role/widget library;
- SEO/search fleet control plane;
- automation control plane/work queues.

### Exit criteria

Identity is not vendor-locked; least-privilege scopes work; platform adapters pass sandbox/integration certification; MCP tools disclose side effects and enforce approval; SDKs match source contracts; vendor drift/health is monitored; one approved cross-platform workflow works end to end.

## Phase 3E — Advanced CHLOM Laboratory

### Scope

LEX, DID/verifiable credentials, ZK proofs, oracles/scribes, cryptographic provenance, multiparty treasury/settlement, public proof anchoring, bridge/interoperability and token/programmatic-value research.

### Rules

- isolated research environment;
- no production authority inherited from a prototype;
- no wallet/token/public-chain dependency for core services;
- legal, privacy, security, economic and market validation before pilot;
- cloud and decentralized adapters share stable institutional contracts;
- private evidence and PII do not become public immutable payloads.

### Exit criteria

Each activated capability proves a real problem, threat model, privacy model, operational owner, recovery path, legal posture, interoperability value and measurable advantage over conventional infrastructure.

## Phase 3 completion definition

Phase 3 is complete when CrownThrive can register an organization and asset, preserve evidence, resolve roles/authority, evaluate rules, create a governed DLA/license offer, complete eligibility/agreement/payment, issue a verifiable entitlement, record usage/economics, handle disputes/corrections/remedies, expose controlled APIs/MCP tools and prove the full event trail across at least two materially different platform pilots.

# Phase 4 — Federated Ecosystem Activation and Thrive Flywheel

## Mission

Prove that specialized CrownThrive platforms can operate as one institution through shared identity, policies, events, rights, entitlements, attribution, analytics, support and distribution without collapsing their distinct roles or data stores.

## Cohort strategy

### Cohort 1 — institutional proof

CrownThrive.com/Support, CHLOM, CrownThrive ID, CrownThrive IO/MCP, CrownLytics/CrownPulse and automation control plane.

### Cohort 2 — media, publishing and ministry

Virality Music, Backroad FM, CrownThrive Studios, Go Flipbooks, Melanated media/MVP, KJV Visualized and The Sermon Toolkit.

### Cohort 3 — beauty, commerce and phygital

Locticians, ThriveSeat, Melanin Magic, GSO, MM Suites/Suite Pros, CrownRewards and CrownThriveU.

### Cohort 4 — distribution and experience

AdLuxe, Crown Affiliates, Crown Ambassadors, CrownFluence, ThrivePush, ThriveTickets, ThriveGather, BrandCards/ThriveKiosks and approved long-tail platforms.

Cohorts may overlap through shared infrastructure but each has entry/exit tests.

## Core outputs

- progressive SSO/account linking;
- certified platform adapters and MCPs;
- cross-platform entitlements and saved identity/profile references;
- normalized event taxonomy and CrownLytics measurement;
- CrownPulse signal/alert workflows;
- Crown Affiliates/Ambassadors attribution and versioned commissions;
- CrownRewards retention/progression;
- ThrivePush permissioned communications;
- AdLuxe inventory/campaign evidence;
- support/legal/policy enforcement;
- automated SEO/search fleet;
- role portals and shared widgets;
- MM Suites digital/Suite Pro/location-pilot operating proof;
- CII/ThriveFund and sustainability evidence;
- platform lifecycle and migration automation.

## Flywheel proof

At least one user/organization should be able to discover through one platform, authenticate/link identity, complete a governed action, receive an entitlement/reward, enter an adjacent relevant pathway, generate attributable value and receive support—all with rights/policy/event/economic evidence.

## Phase 4 exit criteria

- multiple cohorts meet reliability/security/privacy/support objectives;
- identity and entitlements work without manual reconciliation as the normal path;
- commission/reward/advertising measurements reconcile;
- platform failures degrade safely and recover;
- MM Suites phygital pilot has current legal/operating status and measured outcomes;
- public claims flow from approved registries;
- the institution can onboard another platform through the standard adapter/extraction/certification process.

# Phase 5 — Institutional Scale, Licensing and Sovereign Expansion

## Mission

Turn mature CrownThrive IP, methods, platform capabilities and CHLOM services into externally scalable licensing, certified development, place-based expansion and selectively sovereign infrastructure.

## Core programs

### LEX and external licensing

Governed offers for brands, content, universes, characters, methods, curricula, software, APIs, datasets, location models and other defined rights; licensee onboarding, agreements, payment/settlement, entitlements, usage, statements, renewal and remedies.

### Developer and partner economy

External developer accounts, sandbox, SDKs/MCPs, app/adapter certification, component marketplace, contribution/IP terms, usage metering, revenue share and security governance.

### MM Suites regional/franchise/license activation

Only after current counsel-reviewed entity, disclosure, agreement, jurisdiction, finance, insurance, support and operating packages are ready. Foundational prospectus language does not itself authorize offers.

### Holdings and portfolio maturity

Evaluate HoldCo, IP stewardship, SPVs/subsidiaries and intercompany arrangements based on proven operations, risk, capital and licensing needs—not old diagrams alone.

### Advanced CHLOM activation

DID/VC, ZK, oracles, cryptographic provenance, multiparty settlement/governance and selective decentralized infrastructure that proved value in 3E/Phase 4.

### Global and sovereign operations

Cross-border licensing, multilingual/legal localization, regional partners, data-residency strategies, portable/self-hostable components, independent key custody, institutional succession and long-horizon continuity.

## Phase 5 exit criteria

- external licensees/developers/partners operate under verified agreements and support;
- LEX transactions reconcile rights, payments, entitlements, usage and remedies;
- advanced infrastructure has independent security/operational evidence where appropriate;
- legal entities and regional structures match current records;
- succession and vendor-exit tests prove the institution is not dependent on one founder, SaaS, cloud or model;
- CrownThrive's operating system is commercially and operationally self-sustaining enough to maintain its own controls.

# Permanent program rules

1. phase numbers always specify namespace;
2. capability state is independent from phase label;
3. no phase deletes source/history;
4. every phase has entry, exit, rollback, evidence and unresolved-risk records;
5. public, internal and machine projections share IDs/versions but not unrestricted data;
6. later decentralization preserves correction, privacy, appeal and legal authority;
7. each run updates the inherited baseline rather than restarting discovery;
8. exceptions require a documented decision and effective version.
