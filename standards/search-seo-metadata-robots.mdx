# Search, SEO, AIO, Metadata, Robots and Sitemap Standard

Search is shared institutional infrastructure. Every public CrownThrive platform should be discoverable, accurate, canonical, accessible and machine-understandable without exposing restricted records or creating low-value automated pages.

## Governing principles

1. one canonical public URL per resource and audience purpose;
2. metadata resolves from governed records;
3. dynamic numbers/status resolve from claim/metric records;
4. private or tokenized routes are protected by authentication, not only crawler directives;
5. structured data reflects visible, supported content;
6. search scale never justifies fabricated pages or claims;
7. migrations preserve lineage, links and search equity;
8. each platform has its own SEO profile under a central fleet standard.

## Required page metadata

Where applicable:

- unique human-readable title;
- unique description;
- canonical URL;
- robots/indexability state;
- Open Graph and social metadata;
- stable entity/content ID in structured records;
- language/locale;
- published and modified timestamps from real source versions;
- organization/imprint/author/contributor;
- primary image, dimensions and alt text;
- breadcrumbs and parent relationships;
- audience/content rating;
- appropriate structured data;
- current claim references;
- previous/successor route relationships.

Internal release/version codes stay out of public titles unless the version is meaningful to the customer.

## Canonical and duplication rules

- aliases and legacy routes redirect to the canonical route;
- edition routes identify work/edition relationships and avoid identical competing copies;
- syndication pages reference the canonical owner while providing real audience-specific value;
- MVP, Go Flipbooks, Virality Music, Melanated TV and CrownThrive.com must not publish indistinguishable copies without canonical strategy;
- filters/search results/account pages generally do not become indexable landing pages without review;
- URL parameters, print views, preview tokens and expired routes must not create duplicate indexes.

## Robots rules

Every host/environment has a versioned intentional `robots.txt`.

### Production

Allow approved public routes. Disallow crawler access to paths where appropriate, but use authentication and `noindex` for private routes.

### Staging/test/preview

Require access control and site-wide noindex; robots is supplemental.

### Always protected/excluded

- admin, super-admin and ops;
- account/profile/security/billing;
- checkout sessions/callbacks;
- signed/expiring downloads;
- private evidence and journals;
- internal APIs/docs;
- webhook endpoints;
- search/admin indexes;
- debug/health detail;
- draft/review routes.

A deployment should fail or alert if production is accidentally blocked or a private environment becomes indexable.

## Sitemap architecture

Use sitemap indexes when volume warrants it. Candidate partitions include:

- static/public pages;
- platforms/brands;
- articles/help;
- products/offers;
- books/editions;
- music/releases/tracks;
- universes/worlds;
- characters;
- radio programs;
- rooms/studies;
- people/places/events/themes;
- courses/resources;
- videos/media.

Sitemaps exclude redirects, noncanonical duplicates, private routes, expired tokens, soft 404s, unapproved drafts and suspended/removed pages unless an archive route is intentionally public.

`lastmod` must come from a meaningful content/version change, not every build.

## Structured data policy

Potential types include Organization, WebSite, BreadcrumbList, Article, Book, MusicRecording, MusicAlbum, Product, Offer, Event, Course, VideoObject, FAQPage, SoftwareApplication and appropriate creative-work relationships.

Do not:

- mark fictional/testimonial content as real-person evidence;
- mark historical catalog prices as current offers;
- publish aggregate ratings without real compliant review data;
- claim availability or price not visible/current;
- use FAQ schema for hidden keyword blocks;
- publish legal/medical/financial authority schema that is not supported.

## Entity and relationship graph

Stable records should express:

- platform → brand/imprint;
- universe → works/characters/rooms;
- work → editions/releases/products;
- product → offer/license/entitlement;
- KJV passage → people/places/events/themes/studies;
- station → signals/programs/tracks;
- organization → services/platforms;
- current route → predecessor/successor.

This graph improves internal search, public structured data, recommendations and AI retrieval.

## Programmatic SEO safety

Generated pages require:

- governed source data;
- meaningful differentiated user value;
- verified location/identity where applicable;
- human-readable content;
- canonical and duplicate controls;
- minimum completeness/quality threshold;
- rights and public-claim approval;
- lifecycle/refresh/sunset policy.

Prohibited: fake city/location pages, fabricated professionals, fake reviews, empty category matrices, mass-spun copy, unsupported statistics and doorway pages.

## AIO and machine-discovery outputs

Public machine outputs may include structured data, feeds, public APIs, documentation and controlled `llms.txt`/AI-discovery files. They must:

- use approved public facts;
- state canonical sources;
- exclude private/restricted content;
- distinguish current/historical/planned;
- provide update timestamp/version;
- never grant training or reuse rights merely by being crawlable.

## Platform-specific standards

### Virality Music

Model universes, characters, books, music, radio, products, rooms and rights relationships. Preserve audience ratings and avoid indexing private ops, checkout tokens or protected Evidence Room source files.

### KJV Visualized / Sermon Toolkit

Model Scripture, people, places, events, themes, rooms, studies, products and organizations. Avoid thin verse/commentary duplication; keep private reflection/prayer content out of indexes; coordinate canonical domains.

### Locticians / directories

Only publish real, supported listings and locations. Distinguish member/listing/verified-record counts and manage stale/closed profiles.

### AdLuxe / affiliates

Keep campaign/admin/conversion detail private; public pages need required advertising/affiliate disclosures and approved claims.

## Redirect and migration standard

A migration plan includes old/new route map, status codes, canonical updates, internal links, sitemap change, analytics annotations, search-console actions where supported, monitoring and rollback. Avoid redirect chains and mass redirecting unrelated content to the homepage.

## Automated fleet checks

- crawlability/indexability;
- title/description/canonical uniqueness;
- status/redirect chain;
- broken internal/external links;
- orphan pages;
- robots/sitemap validity;
- structured-data validity;
- image/alt/accessibility;
- claim freshness;
- duplicate/thin content;
- page performance;
- accidental private data;
- environment leakage;
- domain/SSL/DNS health.

## Change authority

Derived sitemap/robots fixes may be A2 when policy-defined and reversible. Canonical ownership, public claims, legal wording, large route deletion, domain migration and programmatic page generation require the applicable A3/A4 approval.

## Evidence

Each fleet run creates a run ID, platform/profile version, findings, automatic changes, held items, verification and DAIL/CrownLytics events. Search health becomes part of platform state.
