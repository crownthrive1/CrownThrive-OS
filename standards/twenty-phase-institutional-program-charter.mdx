  This charter extends the prior ten-phase roadmap; it does **not** erase or renumber historical Phase 1–10 evidence. CrownThrive remains in **Phase 2 / subphase 2.99** until its exact hard-exit and documentation gates pass.

## Program invariants

1. Later phases never waive earlier hard gates.
2. Historical phase records are append-only institutional history.
3. Current state, historical source, research target, reserve/sunset disposition and production evidence remain separate.
4. Knowledge is never silently destroyed or overwritten.
5. Material documentation changes preserve prior state and include a compact changelog/version record.
6. Material rebuilt artifacts use collateral evidence, current research/current technology and asset-type best practice, then receive applicable specialists and independent governance.
7. Source discovery, domain custody or provider capability never equals active deployment.
8. Each phase inherits the applicable Canon, Doctrine, Revival Doctrine, security, rights, legal, economic, accessibility, localization and recovery controls from prior phases.
9. Every material Phase 2.99 change propagates into all affected future Phase 3–20 plans.
10. No Phase 3 entry occurs except from the accepted exact canonical snapshot.

## Phase 1 — Institutional Mapping

Preserve and map the ecosystem's origin, identities, corridors, source hierarchy, naming/relationship rules and early governance architecture.

**Historical state:** completed baseline; later corrections preserve original evidence.

## Phase 2 — Institutional Recovery, Reconciliation & Documentation

**ACTIVE. Current subphase: 2.99.**

Objectives include recovery/classification of institutional knowledge; reconciliation of the 68/82/85/74 source universes; terminal disposition of the complete 795 Help Center estate; reconciliation of material current documentation from Phase 1 forward; preservation of Founder Handbook/Canon/Doctrine history; external-state/deferral classification; restricted-source/export/recovery/provider-exit proof; and the exact Phase 3 entry packet.

Phase 3 remains blocked until every required Phase 2.99 hard gate and the founder full-documentation/Handbook/brand-domain-portfolio hard gates are accepted.

## Phase 3 — Executable Institutional Core

Founder-approved opening sequence, once entry is lawful:

```text
A. CHLOM + THIVEBASE Institutional Core
→ B. CrownThrive ID / identity + authority
→ C. one bounded platform-adapter pilot
```

Core concerns: identity, authority, evidence, Canon/Doctrine, DAIL, policy, rights/licensing, remedies, event contracts, API/MCP contracts, audit and bounded pilots.

## Phase 4 — Federated Ecosystem Activation

Federate accepted CrownThrive platforms through shared identity, entitlements, events, approved data contracts and certified adapters. Prepare point-of-use provider integrations without expanding authority by convenience.

## Phase 5 — Revenue & Market Activation

Activate attributable, supportable revenue rails only after product, price, rights, customer-protection, fulfillment, refund/dispute, commission, tax/accounting and provider controls are accepted.

## Phase 6 — Licensing, IP & Developer Economy

Operationalize external licensing, developer identities, SDK/API/MCP access, usage metering, rights/remedies, template/package distribution and controlled commercialization of reusable CrownThrive/CHLOM components.

## Phase 7 — Physical, Phygital & Regional Expansion

Activate physical/phygital lanes such as MM Suites, kiosks/TapStations, places, events and regional operating models only after safety, accessibility, insurance, legal/regulatory, rights, identity and continuity requirements pass.

## Phase 8 — Holdings, Capital & Portfolio Scale

Formalize evidence-backed portfolio stewardship, legal/entity/IP custody, risk isolation, capital allocation, portfolio reporting and controlled asset revival/sunset economics. Planning documents do not prove entity formation or investment authority.

## Phase 9 — Advanced CHLOM & Interoperable Infrastructure

Research and selectively implement advanced cryptographic/interoperable components where justified: verifiable credentials, provenance, ZK, distributed ledgers or other advanced architecture. Research papers remain research until promoted through evidence and governance.

## Phase 10 — Generational Continuity, Sovereign Scale & Institutional Permanence

Prove succession, archives, provider exit, disaster recovery, durable stewardship, knowledge transfer and long-horizon operating continuity.

## Phase 11 — Institutional Replication

Package accepted CrownThrive institutional patterns so they can be reproduced across organizations, regions and controlled operating cells without flattening local context, culture, rights or authority.

## Phase 12 — Enterprise Federation

Federate mature capabilities with enterprise partners under explicit multi-organization identity, data, authority, liability, licensing and operating boundaries.

## Phase 13 — Standards & Certification

Formalize CrownThrive/CHLOM standards, certification, conformance, assurance, qualified implementation and independent-review ecosystems. Certification binds to testable requirements and evidence.

## Phase 14 — Autonomous Operations

Expand governed automation, self-healing and agentic execution only after lower-phase authority, observability, rollback, evaluation and escalation controls prove reliable. Autonomy remains bounded by risk and authority.

## Phase 15 — Knowledge & Model Sovereignty

Protect institutional knowledge, Canon/Doctrine, model/runtime portability, retrieval boundaries, evaluation sets, provider independence and recovery so CrownThrive does not depend on a single model, provider, SaaS or conversation.

## Phase 16 — Treasury & Settlement

Mature governed treasury, settlement, reconciliation, economic routing and programmable financial controls under legal, accounting, tax, security and rights governance. Historical Treasury/token models do not become current money movement by roadmap placement.

## Phase 17 — Global IP Exchange

Scale rights, licensing and IP exchange under provenance, jurisdiction-aware contracts, cross-border requirements, creator/developer participation, audit and remedies. CHLOM LEX concepts activate only to the extent evidence and law permit.

## Phase 18 — Civic & Institutional Partnerships

Govern partnerships with civic, educational, cultural, community and institutional bodies under explicit authority, public-interest, data, accessibility, communications and accountability boundaries.

## Phase 19 — Intergenerational Oversight

Formalize long-horizon stewardship, succession, independent oversight, cultural continuity, knowledge transfer and intergenerational governance mechanisms without converting historical metaphors into unreviewed legal authority.

## Phase 20 — Perpetual Institutional Continuity

Prove CrownThrive can survive leadership, model, cloud, vendor, repository, domain, jurisdiction and generation changes while preserving mission, identity, rights, evidence, Canon/Doctrine, governance and recoverability.

### Phase 20 documentation-domain continuity hard gate

CrownThrive uses the default Mintlify deployment domain during Phases 2.99–19 unless a later explicit decision changes that. Full documentation-domain custody, DNS/TLS continuity, provider-exit recovery and **intentional custom-domain activation/certification** are a Phase 20 hard gate. Prior domain research/history remains preserved; absence of a custom Mintlify domain today is not a Phase 2.99 failure.

## Revival Doctrine across the roadmap

The [Revival Doctrine](/doctrine/revival-doctrine) applies to all 20 phases. Every recovered asset receives one governing disposition:

`ACTIVATE | REVIVE | MERGE | MIGRATE | RESEARCH | RESERVE | LICENSE | ARCHIVE | SUPERSEDE | SUNSET`.

An asset activates in the phase appropriate to its risk/dependency graph, not merely because it was rediscovered during Phase 2.

## Exact Phase 3 transition rule

Phase 3 may open only from a single exact canonical snapshot where:

- all Phase 2.99 hard gates pass or receive the specifically permitted non-pass governed treatment without violating a non-deferrable gate;
- the full-documentation, Founder-Handbook and brand/domain/portfolio hard gates are accepted;
- exact main SHA plus schema/infrastructure/source versions are frozen;
- exact-head CI and required specialists pass;
- required independent sovereign quorum approves;
- no unresolved material current-truth contradiction remains;
- rollback/recovery is defined;
- the approved A → B → C Phase 3 sequence is frozen.
