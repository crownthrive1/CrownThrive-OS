# CrownThrive Non-Negotiables

These rules apply across CrownThrive, LLC, its platforms, imprints, frameworks, programs, operators, agents, vendors, developers and future entities. A platform may add stricter rules; it may not silently weaken these.

## 1. Institutional identity is stable

Every consequential organization, actor, asset, platform, brand, imprint, framework, universe, character, room, product, policy, API, MCP, component, paper, right, license, claim, event and decision receives a stable ID independent of display name, vendor, domain, version or implementation.

## 2. History is preserved

Renames, migrations, corrections, retractions, mergers, retirements and replacements create lineage. No material record is silently overwritten or deleted merely because the current model changed.

## 3. Current state must be proven

A source saying that something was planned, designed, “patent-pending,” tokenized, franchised, registered, integrated, production-ready or legally reviewed does not prove current state. Production, legal, filing, rights, financial, impact and metric claims require the applicable evidence class.

## 4. No unsupported public claims

Never fabricate users, plays, products, countries, revenue, impact, rights clearance, customer proof, partnerships, legal status, API capability, platform readiness or AI performance. Dated historical metrics remain historical snapshots.

## 5. Rights before reuse

No asset is distributed, licensed, adapted, trained on, commercialized, published or transferred without a rights/provenance record appropriate to the use. Possession of a file does not prove ownership or authority.

## 6. Scripture, commentary and creative material remain distinguishable

KJV text, CrownThrive commentary, historical context, denominational tradition, devotional application, testimony, dramatization, visual reconstruction and AI-assisted content must remain separately labeled. No tool may present generated material as Scripture or pastoral authority.

## 7. Canon and cultural identity are governed

Virality Music universes, characters, timelines, imprints, ministry rooms and crossovers require versioned canon and Cultural Imprint Engine review. Similar names or themes do not grant permission to merge identities.

## 8. Privacy and security are architectural

Restricted data must be protected by authentication, authorization, purpose limitation, minimum access, encryption, logging, retention controls and tested recovery. `robots.txt`, obscurity and a confidentiality footer are not access controls.

## 9. No public private-evidence exposure

Contracts, customer data, credentials, investigations, rights evidence, unpublished masters, sensitive journals, security details, private economics and trade secrets remain behind the correct role/data boundary. Public summaries reference protected evidence without reproducing it.

## 10. No secrets in documentation or source history

Documentation and code contain secret-manager references, never live credentials. Suspected exposure triggers incident evidence and dependency review. Provider credentials remain long-lived server-side assets and are changed only with explicit founder authorization.

## 11. Human authority remains explicit

Agents may prepare and execute within adopted policies. They may not independently authorize reserved founder, legal, securities, franchise, tax, ownership, irreversible security or material capital decisions.

## 12. No agent self-approval

Material actions require separation of proposer, executor and verifier according to risk. The same agent cannot be the only source, decision-maker, executor and auditor.

## 13. Automation must be observable and reversible

Every consequential automated action requires actor/agent identity, source snapshot, policy/approval basis, correlation ID, outcome, affected records, verification and rollback or compensating action.

## 14. No autonomous legal determinations

AI may identify issues, classify source language, draft work product and prepare review packets. It may not represent that legal sufficiency, regulatory status, enforceability, tax treatment, franchise status, securities status or filing status has been established without current authority/evidence.

## 15. No undocumented money movement

Pricing, fees, commissions, royalties, splits, overrides, impact allocations, reserves and clawbacks require versioned economic schedules, effective windows, authority and reconciliation. Compensation, rewards, royalties and ownership are distinct instruments.

## 16. No forced token, wallet, chain or DAO dependency

Core CrownThrive/CHLOM services must be useful through cloud/API infrastructure. Decentralized components may be added when they improve verifiability, resilience, interoperability, settlement or market structure. They do not replace privacy, appeals, legal authority or operational accountability.

## 17. APIs and integrations must have exit paths

No critical vendor integration enters production without owner, documentation, scopes, version, security, incident, export, replacement and deprecation records. Supported APIs are preferred over brittle automation.

## 18. One control plane does not mean one unsafe database

CrownThrive centralizes stable identity, source authority, policy, platform/API state and orchestration. Operational data remains partitioned by domain, tenant, purpose and classification.

## 19. Every interface has an audience and role

Public pages, member areas, customer portals, creator dashboards, affiliate tools, developer consoles, operator centers, admin surfaces and machine APIs must declare who they are for and what authority they grant. Audience alone never grants authority.

## 20. Search cannot leak or distort the institution

Canonical URLs, metadata, structured data, robots, sitemaps and AI-discovery outputs must resolve to governed records. Private/admin/token routes are never indexed; programmatic SEO cannot fabricate value or claims.

## 21. Accessibility and cultural alignment are release gates

Products and platforms must meet applicable accessibility, readability, caption/transcript, alt-text and interaction standards. Cultural specificity, Black/Southern context and community representation must be handled through CIE rather than generic cosmetic language.

## 22. The customer receives what was promised

Payment success is not fulfillment. Orders, webhooks, entitlements, files, download limits, refunds, disputes, licenses and support must reconcile. No checkout is activated without a verified end-to-end path.

## 23. Historical IP is not discarded because it is not yet built

Whitepapers, Blackpapers, Gold Papers, Blue/Red/Green papers, Genesis, LEX, MM Suites, old platform concepts and advanced CHLOM designs remain governed research/IP. Their state is explicit; they are neither falsely live nor casually abandoned.

## 24. Cleanup requires dependency proof

Old bridges, routes, credentials, vendors, repositories and scaffolding are inventoried before removal. Preserve source, evidence, inbound links, users, entitlements, licenses and rollback where applicable.

## 25. Every run strengthens the next

Every governed run ends with versioned records, changed/deferred items, tests, evidence, unresolved risks, migration/rollback notes and an exact next-run packet. No phase begins by rediscovering settled institutional facts.

## 26. Documentation and changelog are part of the release

Every material property, code, configuration, product, price, license, API, content, canon, policy, role, provider or state change resolves `docs_updated`, `docs_no_change` or `docs_delta_opened` and updates the applicable platform changelog. A passing build with stale institutional meaning is not complete.

## 27. Provider capability is not CrownThrive deployment

Vendor documentation, CrownThrive account entitlement and deployed CrownThrive integration are separate states. No agent or public page may collapse them to fill an API, feature, plan, campaign, delivery or reporting gap.

## 28. A deployment artifact is not editable source history

A public site or build/TAR may prove output and support rollback. Only an integrity-verified editable repository/source master proves editable source history. Do not reconstruct or force-push build artifacts as the original source lineage.

## 29. Unresolved records remain unresolved

Missing room names, universe relationships, rights, prices, providers, repositories, versions or current states must carry explicit unresolved/blocked/unverified fields. Arithmetic convenience, naming similarity and plausible inference do not close an institutional record.

## 30. The homepage is a governed control surface

The Mintlify homepage must reflect the current accepted phase/gate posture, source-of-truth model and primary institutional navigation. It is not exempt from documentation-impact rules because it is visually prominent or marketing-facing. If a merged PR, source pull, provider verification, domain/API state change, roadmap decision or correction materially changes a homepage claim, the homepage must be updated in the same governed propagation cycle or carry an explicit risk-appropriate delta.

## 31. Pull requests propagate institutional meaning

A pull request is not complete when only its directly edited files are correct. Material changes must be reconciled through every affected macro, corridor, platform, object, article, changelog, machine-seed, homepage and phase-gate record. The required outcome remains `docs_updated`, `docs_no_change` or `docs_delta_opened`; high-risk false or stale public claims cannot be deferred merely to keep a merge moving.

## Enforcement

Violations create the appropriate hold, incident, correction, case, remedy or access review. These rules become executable through Phase 3 registries, policies, approvals, DAIL events, API/MCP scopes and platform adapters.
