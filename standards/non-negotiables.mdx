# CrownThrive Non-Negotiables

These rules are mandatory across the CrownThrive Convergent Ecosystem and are the default constraints for humans, agents, AI systems, developers, vendors, workflows, and platforms.

## Institutional truth

- Stable institutional IDs survive renames, migrations, vendors, domains, and restructures.
- Current, historical, proposed, research, restricted, superseded, and unverified states must never be collapsed.
- Older source material is preserved; it is not silently rewritten to fit current architecture.
- Dynamic claims require an effective date, evidence source, and last-verified timestamp.
- Unknown state is represented as unknown; agents may not promote uncertainty into fact.

## Rights and ownership

- CrownThrive intellectual property, frameworks, source material, masters, policies, schemas, code, media, studies, papers, brands, imprints, universes, curricula, and derivative assets must have traceable ownership/control records.
- AI assistance does not erase human authorship, provenance, source history, rights restrictions, or disclosure obligations.
- No license, sublicensing right, franchise right, territory, assignment, transfer, derivative right, data-use right, or training right is inferred from adjacency.
- Restricted algorithms, evidence, contracts, secrets, credentials, legal files, private identity data, and protected implementation details remain access-controlled.

## Human and machine authority

- Automation operates from delegated authority, never presumed authority.
- Material legal, ownership, irreversible financial, destructive, credential, public-claim, and governance actions require the applicable approval rule.
- Agents must emit evidence for consequential actions and preserve correction history.
- Human override, emergency stop, rollback, and review paths must exist for consequential automation.

## Architecture

- CrownThrive is federated: platforms may retain specialized backends while connecting through common IDs, contracts, APIs, events, and governance.
- No single SaaS vendor, website, repository, chat, account, social platform, payment processor, or founder memory may be the sole institutional source of truth.
- Public documentation is not the storage location for secrets or restricted operational data.
- Shared services must be versioned, observable, recoverable, and replaceable.

## Culture and ecosystem alignment

- Every asset must map to a corridor, role, audience, purpose, owner, lifecycle state, and Flywheel relationship.
- Cultural Imprint Engine alignment is required where narrative, representation, community, media, education, culture, or imprint identity is involved.
- Cross-platform integration must create value rather than superficial cross-linking.
- Compensation, ownership, royalties, commissions, rewards, referrals, grants, allocations, and equity are separate economic/legal concepts.

## Continuity

- Every critical dependency requires an owner, export path, recovery path, replacement strategy, and documented state.
- Backups are not considered valid until restoration is tested.
- Every run should leave the system more machine-readable, versioned, recoverable, and ready for the next run.

## Phase rule

These non-negotiables govern Phase 3, Phase 4, Phase 5, and subsequent work unless an explicit founder-approved ADR supersedes a rule.
