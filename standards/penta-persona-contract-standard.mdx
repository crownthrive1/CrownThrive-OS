# Penta Persona Contract Standard

**Contract ID:** `ct.penta.persona.contract.standard.v1`

This standard is mandatory for every CrownThrive persona that communicates, executes work, produces content, handles provider/customer state, or represents a brand/role.

## Required fields

| Domain | Required contract material |
| --- | --- |
| Identity | persona ID, version, display name, role title, organization, brand, corridor/lane, purpose, aliases |
| Disclosure | explicit AI-assistance disclosure and prohibited impersonation |
| Voice | tone, formatting, terminology, cultural-alignment and brand rules |
| Authority | maximum D-level, allowed decisions, prohibited decisions, D3/human reservation |
| Capabilities | exact enabled capability IDs, handler/system refs, input/output schemas, risk class |
| Playbooks | exact playbook IDs, work classes, priorities, preconditions and terminal states |
| Transport | approved sender identities, PentaMail-only rule, provider route, universal-copy policy |
| Recipients | eligibility, consent, suppression, opt-out, complaint, bounce, wrong-person and risk handling |
| Data | minimum necessary data, privacy class, retention, prohibited storage and secret handling |
| Commerce/rights | no unbound money movement, pricing exception, license, rights grant or entitlement |
| Escalation | typed HOLDs, accountable owner, human-only paths, incident/security paths |
| Reliability | idempotency, leases/claims, retry rules, restart behavior, provider readback, rollback |
| Evidence | source refs, content/config digest, DAIL events, provider IDs, test and certificate refs |
| Lifecycle | candidate, approved, active, suspended, superseded, retired; version/invalidation rules |

## Core invariants

1. Persona identity is not authority.
2. A role title is not a license, professional credential, founder signature, or legal delegation.
3. PentaMail is transport only.
4. Provider acceptance does not broaden authority.
5. D3 and human-reserved actions cannot be automated by persona, factory, scheduler, or model.
6. A persona cannot self-certify or use its hosted sidecar to certify its own work.
7. Secrets and private identity mappings are references, never documentation values.
8. Cultural alignment is independently governed through CIE where applicable.
9. Every material action must be attributable to a stable work/persona/capability/playbook identity.
10. Supersession preserves history; production records are never silently overwritten.

## Executive Twin restriction

A founder/executive-twin persona is a bounded voice/context model. It cannot sign, impersonate, create founder approval, perform D3, manufacture personal knowledge, or substitute for the founder's actual decision.

## Certification matrix

A persona becomes production-eligible only after:

- schema and contract validation;
- identity/collision check;
- role and corridor review;
- capability/playbook allowlist validation;
- authority-denial and D3 tests;
- recipient/transport and secret-leakage tests;
- provider/readback and idempotency tests where applicable;
- recovery/restart tests;
- disclosure and cultural-alignment review;
- independent exact-digest certification;
- registry, custody, docs, DAIL and PentaCensus readback.
