# Evidence, Claims and Proof Standard

CrownThrive claims must resolve to evidence, a defined measurement method and an approved public wording. Narrative ambition, old prospectuses, generated copy and live-page text are not automatically proof.

## Evidence classes

| Class | Description | Typical use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `E0_unverified` | assertion located but not corroborated | recovery queue only |
| `E1_source_exists` | dated source shows the statement was made or designed | historical doctrine/research |
| `E2_owner_attested` | explicit current founder/authorized-owner decision | canonical direction subject to contrary legal/technical evidence |
| `E3_operational` | current system, provider, repository, database or production behavior supports claim | capability/status |
| `E4_transactional` | payment, agreement, signed record, entitlement, ledger or provider event supports claim | commerce/rights/economic proof |
| `E5_independent` | qualified external source, registration, audit, court/provider/partner confirmation | legal/registration/third-party validation |
| `E6_reconciled` | multiple sources, method and reviewer agree; conflicts resolved | high-confidence institutional/public claim |

## Claim record

Every material claim should contain:

```yaml
claim_id: ct.claim.01J...
subject_id: ct.platform.example
claim_class: production_status
statement: "Example platform is publicly reachable"
approved_wording: "Publicly accessible; deeper feature state varies"
evidence_level: E3_operational
source_refs: []
as_of: "2026-08-17"
measurement_method: null
owner: ct.role.platform-owner
reviewer: ct.role.claim-reviewer
status: current
supersedes: null
next_review_at: "2026-09-17"
```

## Metrics

No number is reusable without:

- population definition;
- inclusion/exclusion rule;
- source system;
- as-of date or period;
- deduplication method;
- whether actual, estimate, projection, scenario or historical snapshot;
- owner and last verification.

The Founder-authorized Locticians figure is **125,000\+**. Older lower public numbers are historical/stale snapshots and belong in the correction backlog; they must not silently replace the current institutional value.

## Capability claims

A route appearing in navigation is not proof that its full workflow is operational. Classify separately:

- public page reachable;
- interface present;
- data connected;
- authentication working;
- action working;
- payment working;
- webhook working;
- entitlement fulfilled;
- admin/ops working;
- monitoring/recovery working.

## Legal and registration claims

Do not claim entity formation, nonprofit status, franchise activation, securities compliance, trademark registration, patent filing, tax status, attorney review, FDD readiness or regulatory approval without the applicable evidence. Historical MM Suites and CHLOM source documents may prove foundational intent and IP architecture while their legal/production claims remain historical or implementation-gated.

## AI/ML claims

Specify whether a function is rules-based, predictive ML, generative AI, retrieval, human-assisted, automated or merely planned. “AI-powered” is not a substitute for model/provider, use case, evaluation, data boundary and human-review records.

## Sustainability and impact

Separate:

- amount allocated;
- amount transmitted;
- provider acknowledgment;
- modeled impact;
- independently verified impact;
- people reached;
- output delivered;
- outcome observed;
- long-term impact.

A partner badge or intention is not proof of environmental or community impact.

## Rights and provenance

A file in CrownThrive storage is not automatically owned, cleared or licensable. Rights claims require chain-of-title evidence, contributor identity, source/provenance and scope.

## Claim automation

Agents may draft or update claims only from approved record classes. Dynamic website statistics should be retrieved from the applicable current claim/metric registry, not copied manually between sites. Conflicting or stale evidence must place the claim on hold.

## Correction rule

When evidence changes, preserve the prior claim, mark its effective period, issue the applicable correction and propagate the new approved wording through websites, structured data, APIs, sitemaps, media kits, investor materials and agent retrieval indexes.
