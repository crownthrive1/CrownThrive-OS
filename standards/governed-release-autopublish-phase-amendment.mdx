# Governed Release Auto-Publish Phase Amendment

This amendment extends the Ecosystem Rollout & Certification program with the governed automatic-publication contract. It does not waive any Phase 2.99 hard-exit requirement and does not authorize Phase 3 entry.

## Phase 2.99 — register, quarantine, prove

Phase 2.99 must establish:

- stable release, product and destination identities;
- exact version/hash release snapshots;
- specialist certification dimensions;
- independent vote eligibility and quorum rules;
- D3 human-reserved approval rules;
- surface-specific publish routes;
- adapter state separated from provider account/project presence;
- idempotent jobs and append-only attempts;
- automatic rollback on authorization loss;
- dynamic-feed canary proof;
- Sites project bootstrap packets; and
- explicit `HOLD`/`UNKNOWN` quarantine behavior.

A dynamic feed can be technically certified while a production site consumer remains unverified. That is not production auto-publish PASS.

## Phase 3 — executable institutional core

After the Phase 2.99 hard exit independently passes, Phase 3 inherits:

- executable release decision contracts;
- exact vote/certification identity binding;
- entitlement/license authority binding;
- production adapter certification;
- rollback and read-after-write conformance; and
- bounded release-publisher execution roles.

Phase 3 may not treat a scheduler or provider project as authority.

## Phase 4 — federated activation

Federated activation must preserve platform-specific destinations and rights while allowing shared release/vote/evidence primitives. A CrownThrive-wide product factory must not flatten KJV, Virality, AdLuxe, Locticians, education or other brand-specific governance.

## Phase 5 — revenue and market activation

Revenue activation requires attributable published offers, functioning credit/entitlement paths, refund/dispute behavior and support evidence. Publication alone does not prove revenue readiness.

## Phase 6 — licensing, IP and developer economy

External developer products, runbooks, templates, APIs/MCPs and design/site-control assets must use exact license/version release snapshots and CHLOM/THIVEBASE entitlement authority.

## Phase 7 — physical/phygital/regional

The same decision/rollback model extends to governed digital configuration pushed into kiosks, screens, MM Suites and location-aware surfaces, with physical safety and regional controls added where applicable.

## Phase 8 — Holdings and portfolio scale

Portfolio-scale publication adds entity/IP custody, provider concentration, cost/revenue attribution, rights segregation and portfolio rollback/exit requirements.

## Phase 9 — advanced interoperability

Cryptographic or interoperable credential mechanisms may strengthen release provenance where justified. A machine-readable release digest does not turn Store Credits or publication records into crypto assets.

## Phase 10 — continuity

Continuity must reconstruct:

- current and prior published releases;
- votes and certifications;
- exact asset/catalog versions;
- destination mappings;
- adapter certifications;
- publication attempts;
- rollback/supersession lineage; and
- provider exit/bootstrap requirements.

## Phase 11 — replication

The accepted release/publisher pattern becomes a reusable deployment template only after lower-phase evidence demonstrates that it is safe to replicate.

## Phase 12 — enterprise federation

Multi-organization publication requires explicit authority boundaries so one organization's voters, adapters or entitlements cannot authorize another organization's release.

## Phase 13 — standards and certification

CrownThrive may formalize release, vote, adapter, feed-consumer and rollback conformance profiles after sufficient operating proof.

## Phase 14 — autonomous operations

Broader autonomous publication is allowed only after anomaly handling, rollback, adapter loss, vote revocation, supersession and recovery are proven. Phase 14 cannot redefine automation as permission to bypass upstream authority.

## Phase 15 — knowledge/model sovereignty

Release authorization must remain reconstructable without dependence on one model/vendor. Model output does not itself create a vote or certification.

## Phase 16 — treasury and settlement

Published commerce must reconcile Store Credits and payment-provider settlement under the separate treasury/accounting controls. Publication does not change Store Credits into cash-equivalent or transferable instruments.

## Phase 17 — global IP exchange

Cross-border publication adds jurisdiction-aware rights/license and consumer requirements before release PASS.

## Phase 18 — civic and institutional partnerships

Partner publication requires explicit partner authority, claims, privacy, accessibility and public-interest boundaries.

## Phase 19 — intergenerational oversight

Long-horizon publication authority must survive personnel and agent turnover through recorded identities, votes, evidence and succession controls.

## Phase 20 — perpetual continuity

CrownThrive must be able to recreate the release/publish/rollback system after provider, model, cloud, repository or leadership change without losing rights, authorization or release lineage.

## Hard gate

`auto_publish_if_release_pass=true` means **publish after release PASS**. It must never be interpreted as `publish_if_built`, `publish_if_packaged`, `publish_if_provider_exists`, `publish_if_technical_canary_passed`, or `publish_if_price_exists`.

Phase 3 remains blocked until the existing Phase 2.99 hard exit and full documentation-estate gate are independently accepted.
