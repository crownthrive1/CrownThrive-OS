# CHLOM Agentic Oracle & Adjudication Standard

CHLOM's Generation-6 adjudication layer exists to prevent two failure modes at once: unbounded agent deadlock and ungoverned agent authority expansion.

An oracle is a **decision-support evidence aggregator**. It does not manufacture truth, legal authority, ownership, certification, governance votes or professional judgment.

## Agentic operating mode

Eligible D0–D2 agents may operate in agentic mode for internal, reversible and evidence-backed work. They are expected to inspect their own operational state before and after a packet:

- current capability and authority ceiling;
- source/evidence freshness;
- packet dependencies and write-set collision risk;
- previous failures/retries and whether the current method is still efficient;
- expected work versus actual progress;
- whether the packet should continue, split, hand off, reassign, enter verification, block or escalate.

Operational self-awareness here means **state/capability introspection**, not consciousness.

## Bounded self-healing

Agents may autonomously repair deterministic D0/D1 defects within their accepted capability. D2 repair requires the pre-existing independent-verification, rollback and governance predicates applicable to the artifact.

Permitted adaptive behavior can include:

- re-planning a bounded packet;
- splitting a packet into non-colliding work units;
- assigning a better eligible owner or independent verifier;
- suppressing an unchanged retry;
- switching to another already-accepted skill or recovery path;
- reconciling stale branches/current-main state;
- opening or closing internal evidence-backed lifecycle transitions;
- proposing WIP/cadence changes.

Self-healing cannot weaken a gate, promote UNKNOWN/HOLD without evidence, certify originating work, create authority, create sovereign votes, perform D3, move money, grant rights or substitute for a qualified professional.

## Multidimensional execution

An eligible agent may perform several compatible functions in one bounded run when every function is inside its authority envelope and combining them reduces handoff latency. For example, one permitted run may reconcile evidence, perform deterministic validation, repair a safe defect, rerun the failed check, update a candidate document and create the exact handoff.

Separation of duties still applies. The originating agent cannot become the independent verifier merely because the work occurred in one run.

## Packet lifecycle and telemetry

Generation-6 packet lifecycle is:

`planned -> running -> verifying -> done | blocked`

`cancelled` and `superseded` are explicit terminal alternatives.

Every live invocation must identify the cycle, packet, agent, role, state, progress and evidence. Material runs record:

- start/heartbeat/handoff/completion timestamps;
- normalized blocker count before and after;
- blocker codes and unlock condition;
- retries;
- collision and no-op suppressions;
- work units completed;
- review wait, verification and total cycle time;
- owner/verifier throughput;
- exact PR/head where applicable.

Canary/replay telemetry is segregated from live throughput metrics.

## Oracle adjudication lifecycle

A disagreement enters:

`open -> collecting -> candidate -> resolved_auto | resolved_governed`

or a fail-closed outcome:

`founder_required | professional_required | blocked | expired`

The standard bounded path is:

1. normalize the question and authority class;
2. collect independently attributable positions and evidence;
3. calculate consensus confidence and disagreement;
4. emit a disposition candidate;
5. enforce the authority matrix before any state change;
6. record the disposition and lineage.

A low-risk D0/D1 dispute may resolve automatically when at least two independently attributable positions agree at the governed confidence threshold and existing policy authorizes the operation.

D2 consensus is a candidate only until the required independent verifier/governance path accepts it.

D3 is human-reserved. A material legal, tax, rights, sanctions, payment, binding-contract or other qualified-professional question does not become machine-resolvable because several agents agree.

## Deadlock and founder escalation

The default adjudication round limit is bounded. If the required agents/oracles cannot converge after the accepted number of rounds, the system stops repeated redispatch and escalates.

A founder escalation packet must contain:

- one precise question;
- competing positions;
- sources/evidence for each position;
- authority/risk classification;
- reversible options where possible;
- consequence of deferral;
- the machine-recommended bounded disposition;
- the exact authority needed from the founder.

`ACTION NEEDED` is not created merely because normal backlog work remains open.

## Founder override

A founder override is scoped D3 evidence. It must identify the signer, exact statement/subject, authority scope, time and deterministic Fingerprint ID. A typed-name attestation records institutional intent but is not cryptographic nonrepudiation.

An override does not silently broaden unrelated scopes. Professional/legal/tax/rights/payment decisions remain independently bounded unless the human action requested is itself the authorized decision and applicable policy permits it.

## Protected algorithms

PDIS, HRDS, ACBS and SNRS are public-contract/restricted-implementation algorithms. Public documentation may expose names, inputs, outputs, invariants, reason-code classes, lifecycle and digests. Exact weights, calibration, private features and protected CIE calibration remain restricted.

A Vault-bound bundle may enter controlled testing. Production requires separately accepted integrity, security, disclosure and authority evidence.

## Testability

The restricted runtime provides controlled functions for packet transitions, agent heartbeat/run telemetry, cycle metric roll-up and oracle positions/evaluation. A sanitized authenticated aggregate snapshot may be used for test/readback. Test access never grants mutation authority beyond the caller's existing envelope and does not expose Vault content or protected implementation.
