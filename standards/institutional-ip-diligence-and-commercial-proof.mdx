# Master IP, Title, Valuation, Supply-Chain and Commercial-Proof Standard

A CrownThrive asset is not diligence-ready merely because a source file, idea, brand, Git commit or revenue hypothesis exists. Diligence readiness requires five connected records: invention and authorship, chain of title, identifiable asset and valuation inputs, exact software release provenance, and external commercial proof.

## Non-negotiables

1. Do not infer inventorship from repository authorship, executive role or prompt origin.
2. Do not infer assignment, work-for-hire validity or licensing authority from file possession.
3. Do not state a filing, registration, novelty, patentability or legal conclusion without verified evidence.
4. Do not publish agreements, personal data, customer identity, payment evidence, counsel work product, credentials, fingerprints or protected implementation.
5. Do not produce a monetary valuation from missing or unverified inputs.
6. Do not call an automated scenario an independent appraisal.
7. Do not treat an SBOM or unsigned provenance file as release certification.
8. Do not treat interviews, internal tests, synthetic orders or unrelated revenue as paid product proof.
9. Do not create generic anonymous or authenticated database access for private IP records.
10. Do not treat generated registries, CI, task runs or comments as sovereign approval.

## Evidence classes

Public records may store `PUBLIC_REFERENCE`, `PRIVATE_REFERENCE`, `RESTRICTED_REFERENCE`, `LEGAL_REFERENCE`, `FINANCIAL_REFERENCE` or `PROVIDER_REFERENCE`, each with a SHA-256 digest and `body_in_public_record=false` for non-public evidence.

## Diligence transition

The integrated gate remains HOLD when any invention lacks signed inventorship evidence, any material title link is unresolved, valuation readiness is incomplete, release provenance or vulnerability evidence is missing, or the product lacks verified external commercial proof. HOLD protects the portfolio from false claims and can coexist with continued product development.
