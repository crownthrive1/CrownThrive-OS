# Master IP, Title, Valuation, Supply-Chain and Commercial-Proof Standard

A CrownThrive asset is not diligence-ready merely because a source file, idea, brand, Git commit, registry row or revenue hypothesis exists. Diligence readiness requires connected invention, title, valuation, release and external commercial evidence.

## Authority boundaries

- CHLOM remains authoritative for rights and remedies.
- ThriveEvergreen remains authoritative for SKU, offer, price, ECAC, checkout, entitlement and economic activation.
- Framework Factory may create candidate records but not title, patentability, valuation, certification or commerce authority.
- Repository-federation transport identities remain non-voting and require OIDC for accepted writes.
- Agent D remains mandatory for applicable institutional acceptance; D3 remains human-reserved.

## Public/private boundary

Public records may store safe states, identifiers, formulas, schemas, hard-blocks and evidence references/digests. Signed disclosures, agreements, counsel work product, customer identity/payment evidence, private valuation inputs, credentials, fingerprints, protected algorithms, private eval corpora, calibration, weights and defensive rules remain private.

## Claims safety

Do not infer inventorship, title, filing, novelty, patentability, value, customers, revenue, certification or commercial activation. Missing or contradictory evidence is `HOLD`, not zero or PASS.
