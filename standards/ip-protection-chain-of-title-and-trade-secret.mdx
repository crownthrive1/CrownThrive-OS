# IP Protection, Chain of Title and Trade-Secret Standard

CrownThrive must operate as though every valuable method, universe, character, workflow, data model, paper, source file, brand, imprint, API contract and operating pattern could be copied, misattributed or separated from its evidence. Protection starts with inventory and chain of title, not with a symbol on a page.

## Protected IP classes

- company and platform names;
- trademarks, service marks, slogans and trade dress;
- frameworks and operating methods;
- CHLOM papers, algorithms, components, pallets, schemas and policy logic;
- Cultural Imprint Engine methods and imprint governance;
- Thrive Flywheel, Convergent Ecosystem, Hybrid Incubator and MM Suites architecture;
- source code, infrastructure code, SDKs, APIs, MCP contracts and adapters;
- databases, taxonomies, field models, mappings and curated datasets;
- music, lyrics, recordings, masters, stems, books, scripts, visual art, design systems and products;
- Virality Music universes, characters, canon, dossiers and evidence formats;
- KJV Visualized/Sermon Toolkit original commentary, studies, visual systems, curricula and room architecture, while preserving that KJV Scripture itself is distinct from CrownThrive-authored material;
- prompts, evaluation sets, agent policies, model-routing logic and automation workflows;
- confidential economic schedules, licensing logic, territory models, override structures and partner terms;
- domains, social handles and reputation assets.

## Rights-status vocabulary

Never describe a mark, patent or filing more strongly than verified evidence supports.

| State | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `owned_documented` | CrownThrive control/ownership supported by records |
| `licensed_in` | third-party rights received under a license |
| `assigned` | written assignment exists |
| `work_made_for_hire_claimed` | agreement contains applicable language; legal sufficiency may require review |
| `common_law_mark` | mark used in commerce but no verified registration |
| `application_draft` | filing materials drafted but not filed |
| `filed_pending` | filing receipt/serial number verified |
| `registered` | current registration verified |
| `expired_or_abandoned` | filing/registration no longer active |
| `not_pursued` | protection considered but not filed |
| `trade_secret_candidate` | valuable confidential information being evaluated for controls |
| `trade_secret_controlled` | secrecy measures, access controls and evidence are active |
| `public_domain_or_unowned` | CrownThrive does not claim exclusive ownership in the underlying material |
| `rights_uncertain` | evidence incomplete or contradictory |

The `™` symbol indicates a claim of brand identity; it is not proof of federal registration. Historical “patent-pending” language is not a current claim unless a filing receipt is verified.

## Minimum chain-of-title record

Every material asset should carry:

- stable asset/work ID;
- title and aliases;
- creator/contributor identities;
- creation dates and source files;
- ownership basis;
- assignments/releases/licenses;
- human-authorship contribution;
- AI/model/provider provenance where material;
- embedded or third-party material;
- restrictions and excluded rights;
- territories, media, term and sublicensing authority;
- related versions/derivatives;
- registration/filing status;
- evidence locations and hashes;
- confidentiality class;
- public-claim wording;
- dispute/hold status.

## Trade-secret controls

A trade secret is protected through reasonable secrecy measures, not through a confidentiality label alone. CrownThrive should apply:

1. need-to-know role access;
2. separate restricted repositories/storage;
3. NDA/confidentiality and contributor terms where appropriate;
4. access logging and periodic review;
5. controlled exports and watermarking for sensitive distributions;
6. secret scanning and data-loss-prevention checks;
7. public-safe projections rather than source publication;
8. offboarding and credential revocation;
9. incident response and evidence preservation;
10. records showing why the information has value and how secrecy was maintained.

## Source preservation

For high-value IP, retain the earliest recoverable source, working files, revisions, exports, correspondence, approvals and publication evidence. A final PDF alone is not a sufficient institutional master when editable or machine-readable sources exist.

## AI and model protection

Do not place restricted CrownThrive IP into external model providers unless the applicable data-use, retention, training, confidentiality and access rules permit it. Record the provider, model, workspace/account, policy status, source classification, prompt-policy version and output destination.

## Theft, leakage and misattribution response

When suspected misuse occurs:

- freeze affected licenses or access where authorized;
- preserve URLs, files, timestamps, hashes, communications and account records;
- identify the exact asset/right rather than asserting ownership broadly;
- compare source lineage and access history;
- classify as mistake, contract breach, impersonation, infringement, unauthorized derivative, data theft or trade-secret incident;
- route to counsel or platform notice procedures when appropriate;
- preserve corrections and outcomes in CHLOM/DAIL.

## Licensing rule

Commercialization must identify exactly what is being licensed: brand, content, character, master, composition, template, method, dataset, API, software, territory, location model, service package, or derivative right. Access to one CrownThrive asset never implies access to adjacent ecosystem IP.

## Pre-publication IP disclosure gate

CrownThrive must **not publish first and classify later**. Before a materially detailed technical, agentic, CHLOM, policy, software, research, security, economic or operating artifact is published, distributed, licensed, open-sourced, supplied to a partner or materially disclosed outside its existing approved audience, the exact artifact or disclosure must receive an IP-disclosure disposition.

Use the controlling machine policy `developers/manifests/ip-disclosure-commercialization-policy.v1.json` and the reusable review contract `developers/templates/ip-disclosure-review-template.v1.yaml`.

The required projection boundary is:

1. **public specification / public doctrine** — approved public-safe description of what a system does;
2. **copyright/licensed implementation** — approved implementation material distributed only under the applicable license;
3. **restricted institutional implementation** — CrownThrive-specific configuration and private authority/evidence details;
4. **trade-secret kernel** — high-value confidential policy, economic, evaluation, orchestration, security or implementation know-how not generally distributed.

Potential patent candidates, trade-secret candidates, restricted institutional detail, unknown rights, security-sensitive detail, private economic calibration, proprietary evaluation corpora, private DAIL/evidence bodies and production credentials remain fail-closed until the applicable disposition is recorded. Removing the classification label is not a valid way to cure protected content.

A `PATENT_CANDIDATE`, `TRADE_SECRET_CANDIDATE`, `TRADE_SECRET_CONTROLLED`, `RESTRICTED_INSTITUTIONAL` or `RIGHTS_REVIEW` classification is not a registration or legal-sufficiency claim. It is a routing and protection state that prevents premature disclosure.

### Commercialization separation

Sellability does not erase protection. CrownThrive may license sanitized interfaces, implementation rights, capability packages, software/service access, conformance, education, support and professional services while retaining ownership/control of the core architecture and trade-secret kernel.

Every offer separately moves through `concept → candidate → packaged → rights_cleared → pricing_authorized → fulfillment_certified → checkout_staged → live`. A repository manifest, product page or Stripe object does not skip these states.

No live product/price/checkout is authorized by the IP policy itself. Exact pricing, package license, tax/accounting, fulfillment, entitlement, support, refund/dispute and applicable specialist gates remain independent controls.

## Phase 3 requirement

Phase 3 must seed an IP registry and rights-evidence graph before broad agentic creation, licensing, developer access or cross-platform reuse. The pre-publication IP gate is already mandatory during Phase 2.99 and is inherited by every later phase.
