# Institutional Incubator Operating Standard

## Lifecycle

Every institutional work product occupies one primary lifecycle state:

`idea → research → candidate → controlled_test → promotion_pending → accepted → canonical → deprecated → retired → archived`

Provider `active` state never substitutes for institutional lifecycle state.

## Minimum evidence envelope

Every candidate must have a stable ID, owner, boundary, lifecycle and maturity state, source IDs, authority, classification, rights/license/commercial status, repository/provider/Drive references, outcome metric and denominator, risk class, dependencies, rollback, retirement/succession path, exact-head tests, votes and unresolved objections.

## Gates

| Gate | Required proof |
|---|---|
| I0 Intake | Unique stable ID, owner and boundary |
| I1 Evidence | Claims linked to adequate sources |
| I2 Rights | Build, publication, privacy and monetization authority |
| I3 Design | Corridor, spine and authority fit; no unresolved overlap |
| I4 Build | Bounded, reversible and observable implementation |
| I5 TEVV | Independent test, evaluation, verification and validation |
| I6 Promotion | Valid exact-head vote and no active block |
| I7 Parity | GitHub, Mintlify, THIVEBASE, Drive and public surface aligned |
| I8 Operate | Steward, SLO, incident, cost and review evidence |
| I9 Retire | Redirects, custody, obligations, history and succession preserved |

## Trial WIP budgets

- Seven active governed promotion PRs; three simultaneous authority/governance PRs.
- Nine permanent audit principals; six ephemeral specialists with 72-hour TTL.
- One open D3 work item ecosystem-wide; eight open D2 items ecosystem-wide and three per corridor.
- Five production migrations per day and 15 per rolling week before Architecture override.
- Ten new application tables and three new Edge Functions per rolling week.
- Twenty-five documentation pages per PR unless a recovery-batch exception is declared.
- Twenty-five new R&D reconciliation scopes with 30-day TTL.
- Ten canonical scope promotions per governed decision.
- One promotion-pending version per stable institutional ID.
- Every branch older than 14 days requires a retention reason; at 30 days it requires a close, rebase or supersede decision.

## Living-document contract

Accepted institutional pages carry stable ID, version, state, owner, authority, effective/review date, sources, supersession links, machine-manifest reference and downstream impacts. Material change creates a version and changelog entry; it does not silently overwrite history.

Automation may open a finding, recommend HOLD or prepare a draft. It may not rewrite controlled claims, delete evidence, grant itself authority or declare acceptance.

## Vote and review

IIOS-1 becomes effective only after a valid exact-head vote. It expires into review after the initial 60-day probation unless renewed, amended or superseded by an accepted decision.
