# CrownThrive Licensed Software Commercialization Authority

Use the governed ThriveBase authority ledger before opening any licensing-related commercialization hold.

## Rule
An active record in `public.ct_software_commercialization_authority_v1` authorizes CrownThrive to monetize its own branded product, service, hosting, implementation, automation, production, delivery, advertising, analytics, support, licensing package, or managed service within the recorded scope. The resolver is `public.ct_resolve_software_commercialization_authority_v1`.

Do not block merely because a dependency is licensed, purchased, subscribed, API-backed, provider-backed, private, or not publicly attributed. Preserve provider truth internally and present the CrownThrive product publicly.

## Separate gates
Customer-content rights, payment, entitlement, provider health, explicit current restrictions, security, quality, accessibility, delivery, adverse payment events, and exact release evidence remain independent.

## New holds
A later hold requires exact current evidence: revocation, expiration, provider suspension, proven unresolved scope contradiction, unauthorized source, failed access/readback, missing customer rights, binding legal prohibition, security incident, or another specific failed operation.

## Routing
Master Registry and PentaCensus locate authority; ThriveBase resolves it; private repositories and Drive preserve source evidence; PentaGreen monetizes; PentaAssure certifies; PentaDocs projects the reusable rule. Preserve prior versions and record supersession rather than deleting history.
