## Route-state rules

- `XML-sitemap-discoverable` means a route appeared as a unique public `<loc>` after overlapping XML category maps were deduplicated.
- `rendered-directory-linked` means a same-origin link appeared in `/sitemap`; the 1,825-link rendered directory is not exhaustive.
- `sitemap-image occurrence` counts every XML reference, while `unique image URL` deduplicates URL identity; neither is a unique-work or rights count.
- `reachable` requires a separate successful route check at the stated time.
- `indexable` depends on canonical, robots, metadata, status, and search-engine behavior; sitemap presence alone does not prove it.
- `current` requires the exact route to resolve to the intended current record rather than an archive, stale alias, or redirect.
- `commerce-active` requires exact product, price, checkout, webhook, entitlement, delivery, and readback evidence. Current paid state is `SAFE_HOLD`.
- `rights-available` requires an exact rights profile and approval; a public route never establishes it alone.
- `archived` remains visible for lineage where appropriate and must not be presented as the current edition or offer.
