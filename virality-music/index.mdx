## Virality Music portal

This portal translates its public website into an audience-friendly PentaDocs doorway while preserving the difference between a public record, a protected source asset, a commercial offer, and an authorized rights grant.

It records **355 products**, **0 redeemable paid products**, **353 production-ready paid records held from redemption**, **2 free records**, and **9 bundles**. Browse and inquiry links are available; this documentation does not activate checkout, delivery, entitlement, or licensing rights.

## How to read a record

- **Public route** means the page was exposed by the first-party property during the observation. It does not prove every linked provider action completed.
- **Governed record** means an institutional identity or lifecycle record exists. It is not automatically a live product, released edition, or cleared license.
- **Production-ready held** means preparation evidence exists while redemption remains intentionally unavailable under `SAFE_HOLD`.
- **Free** describes the catalog's price class; users should still follow the exact route and terms presented at access time.
- **Inquiry path** collects a request. It is not an approval, quote, contract, option, license, or assignment.
- **Protected source** may inform this public portal without being copied into it.
