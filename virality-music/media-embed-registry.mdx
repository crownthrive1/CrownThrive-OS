## Rights and evidence boundary

`EMBEDDABLE` is a provider/presentation state. It does **not** establish master, composition, publishing, public-performance, sync, adaptation, sublicensing, AI/data, merchandising, or other commercial rights. Distributor evidence and rights evidence remain separate.

The public Topic release groups Jungle Nights, Baby I Do, High Times, Deep End, Toe Tag, and Garden of Eden remain `PUBLIC_DISTRIBUTOR_EVIDENCE / NEEDS_RIGHTS_AND_METADATA_RECONCILIATION` unless stronger governed evidence resolves them.
