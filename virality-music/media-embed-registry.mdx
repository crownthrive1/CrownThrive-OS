## Rights and evidence boundary

`EMBEDDABLE` means the provider player was observed as usable on the audited public surface. It does not mean CrownThrive has established master, composition, publishing, performance, synchronization, adaptation, sublicensing, AI/data, or other commercial rights for that asset. Public distribution evidence and rights evidence remain separate.

The additional current Topic release groups **Jungle Nights, Baby I Do, High Times, Deep End, Toe Tag, and Garden of Eden** remain `PUBLIC_DISTRIBUTOR_EVIDENCE / NEEDS_RIGHTS_AND_METADATA_RECONCILIATION` unless stronger governed evidence resolves them.
