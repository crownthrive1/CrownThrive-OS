## Licensing and business

Virality Music supports four public business paths: license an eligible existing work, commission new work, sponsor approved programming, or explore an adaptation/option. Each path begins with discovery and intake. None becomes a grant until the exact rights, scope, terms, approvals, agreement, and evidence exist.

<Warning>
  A public track, book, character, universe, price, product card, form submission, email, stream, download, payment attempt, or documentation page does **not** grant commercial rights. This portal routes inquiries only.
</Warning>

### Standard phone-catalog audio package policy

For the governed Virality Music phone-catalog package factory, the standard license posture is **non-exclusive by default**. The currently assigned per-track planning matrix is Personal \$2; Creator/Podcast \$500; Community \$750; Brand \$1,500; Film \$3,500; Stage \$5,000; Game \$6,000; Ads \$8,500; Broadcast/OTT \$15,000; Catalog \$25,000; National/Multi-Territory Advertising \$30,000; Trailer/Major Promotion \$45,000; and Adaptation \$75,000.

**Exclusive licensing begins at \$100,000.** The \$100,000 figure is a floor for scoped negotiation, not an automatic purchase or grant. Any exclusive transaction requires a separate written agreement defining the exact work/version, field of use, media, term, territory, reserved rights, consideration, delivery, approvals, representations, and execution evidence.

A complete package contains the standard license templates, track rider, pricing specification, rights/evidence fields, approved delivery formats, checksums, provenance, QC and machine manifest. Package completion does not silently convert unresolved authorship, splits, samples, contributor approvals, AI-plan terms, master/composition control, or conflicting-grant questions into cleared rights.

## Four paths, four transaction types

| Public path | Typical object | What must remain explicit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| License existing work | a named track, master, composition, poem, book, illustration, character, or approved asset | exact version, use, media, territory, term, edits, exclusivity, attribution, delivery, and reserved rights |
| Commission new work | service scope plus a rights schedule | brief, references, revisions, acceptance, human/AI contribution, source files, ownership vs. license, fees, cancellation, credits, and third-party clearance |
| Sponsor programming | approved inventory and campaign package | program/room, dates, frequency, creative, claims, disclosures, rights, audience evidence, measurement, cancellation, and make-goods |
| Adapt / option a world | underlying work and development rights | option structure, exact works/elements, exclusivity, term, territory, approvals, credits, reserved rights, participation, reversion, canon, culture, voice/likeness, and AI restrictions |

## Rights layers that may be involved

### Music

- composition and melody;
- lyrics and literary text;
- master sound recording;
- performer, producer, contributor, publishing, and collective interests;
- samples, interpolations, source audio, stems, and multitracks;
- artwork, video, metadata, title treatment, marks, voice, likeness, and synthetic-replica rights;
- provider, model, software, distribution, and territory terms.

### Books and poetry

- text, edition, arrangement, cover, illustrations, typography, and page design;
- excerpt, reprint, translation, educational, public-reading, performance, audio, and adaptation rights;
- character, universe, merchandise, and source-file rights;
- reader access and entitlement, which do not replace upstream publication rights.

### Characters and universes

- character expression, name/mark, approved likeness, continuity, canon, source works, and edition scope;
- stage, screen, audio, game, interactive, merchandise, sequel, prequel, spinoff, and derivative rights;
- approvals, territory, language, platform, exclusivity, reserved rights, participation, and reversion.

## Intake checklist

Bring enough detail to route the request without exposing unnecessary private information:

- requesting person and organization;
- project title and project type;
- exact work, edition, track, character, or universe requested;
- intended use and context;
- media/platforms, territory, term, audience, and release dates;
- paid/free access, distribution size, venue/capacity, or campaign scope where relevant;
- edits, excerpts, remixes, samples, derivatives, stems, or source-file needs;
- broadcast, streaming, recording, manufacturing, merchandise, resale, or sublicensing needs;
- exclusivity, credits, insurance, reporting, and compliance requirements;
- any AI, data, model, voice, likeness, or synthetic-media use.

Do not send credentials, private customer records, unreleased third-party material, or privileged documents through a general public form.

## Controlled outcomes

An inquiry may resolve to a state such as:

- `INFORMATION_REQUIRED`;
- `HUMAN_REVIEW_REQUIRED`;
- `RIGHTS_CLEARANCE_REQUIRED`;
- `CONTRIBUTOR_APPROVAL_REQUIRED`;
- `CANON_OR_CULTURAL_REVIEW_REQUIRED`;
- `CUSTOM_QUOTE`;
- `NOT_AVAILABLE`;
- `HELD`;
- `ELIGIBLE_FOR_EXACT_STANDARDIZED_SCOPE`;
- `APPROVED_UNDER_SIGNED_TERMS`.

Only the last state, tied to the exact authoritative agreement and evidence, supports a rights grant. Even then, the grant is no broader than its terms.

## AI and machine-use boundary

Ordinary listening, viewing, reading, linking, product access, or a conventional commercial license does not silently grant rights to scrape, train, fine-tune, distill, embed, benchmark, imitate style, clone voice, create reusable synthetic personas, construct datasets, or reconstruct protected methods. Any such use requires an exact written scope when it is not independently permitted by law.
