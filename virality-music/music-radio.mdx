## Backroad FM signal architecture

The current public architecture describes six governed programming signals:

1. **Front Porch Radio** — Southern conversation, character, and story-led listening.
2. **Sanctuary Signal** — faith, testimony, reflection, and Gospel Soul context.
3. **After Dark** — adult and late-night programming under appropriate audience controls.
4. **TrapOpera Radio** — music and audio-cinema relationships inside the adult story world.
5. **Diaspora Exchange** — cross-location cultural listening with source and context discipline.
6. **Shadow Frequency** — suspense, darker atmosphere, and shadow-story programming.

These are program identities inside one radio universe. A signal name does not prove a particular schedule slot, sponsorship inventory, track clearance, or continuous provider uptime unless current schedule and provider evidence say so.

## Accessibility and listener control

- Audio should not begin on a visitor's first page load without an intentional user action.
- Play, pause, volume, close, request, and navigation controls need keyboard access and visible focus.
- Now-playing text should be available independently of cover art, animation, waveform, or color.
- Images need concise alt text when they communicate release, program, character, or location identity; decorative visualizers should use empty alt text.
- Transcripts, captions, lyric text, and audio description should be included or tracked where required by the exact format and audience.
- Explicit, adult, or potentially distressing material needs clear content labeling before playback or entry.
- Motion and animation should respect reduced-motion preferences.

## Rights-aware listening

| Public action | What it permits | What it does not establish |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Open a track/release link | listen under the destination provider's terms | download, reuse, sync, sample, remix, redistribute, train, or sublicense |
| Play Backroad FM | receive the public stream under its presented terms | public-performance, rebroadcast, venue, capture, or commercial-use rights |
| Submit a Jukebox request | ask for programming consideration | guaranteed play, payment, endorsement, or license |
| View lyrics/poetry | read the approved public projection | reproduction, anthology, training, performance, adaptation, or merchandise rights |
| Watch a public video | view the public presentation | clip extraction, rebroadcast, paid-media use, or derivative rights |
