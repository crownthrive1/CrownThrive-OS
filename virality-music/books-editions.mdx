## The edition evidence chain

```text
work identity
→ edition and version identity
→ source provenance and checksum
→ canon and audience review
→ rights and contributor review
→ technical, accessibility, device, and print QA
→ reader upload and live-route readback
→ product, price, and license mapping
→ checkout and webhook evidence
→ entitlement and protected delivery
→ refund, revocation, expiry, and returning access
→ correction, supersession, or archive
```

No later arrow manufactures an earlier one. A technically valid PDF can still be held for rights, canon, accessibility, platform, commerce, or approval reasons.

## Source and accessibility boundary

- A final PDF is not automatically the source master when editable text, layout, illustration, or audio sources exist.
- PentaDocs may name an approved public work and link to its canonical public route without copying protected binaries.
- A cover image needs alt text that communicates title, edition, and meaningful visual context; do not transcribe decorative texture.
- A reader needs navigable headings, logical reading order, meaningful link labels, adequate contrast, keyboard access, and format-specific QA.
- Page count, preview length, edition name, format, and archive/supersession notices must resolve to the exact edition.
- An archived reader is preserved as history and should not be presented as the current edition.
