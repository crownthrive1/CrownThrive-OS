## Worlds and universes

Virality Music uses worlds to connect sound, characters, books, locations, rooms, visual identity, products, and adaptation possibilities. The nine doors below are the **featured public shelf observed August 26, 2026**. They are not a claim that only nine governed worlds, series, collections, or archives exist.

## Complete public top-level directory

The deployed top-level registry contains **66 public records**: 11 flagship-canon records, 10 official universe cards, 2 official flagship corridors, 23 developed universes, 7 production frameworks, and 13 album worlds or series. Sixty-five routes resolved directly and Southern Soul Jazz resolved through one intentional redirect during the August 26, 2026 observation.

  “Rights band” is the site’s public planning label. It is not a price, active offer, clearance, license, adaptation grant, or proof of ownership. </Note>

## Cultural and accessibility controls

- Label fictional, dramatized, documentary, historical, testimonial, and AI-assisted material accurately.
- Keep adult worlds out of child/family/ministry bundles and recommendations unless an explicit audience review allows the exact item.
- Do not infer a real person's biography from a fictional dossier or cultural setting.
- Give every meaningful world, cover, room, and character image concise alt text that communicates purpose and context.
- Use empty alt text for decorative texture, not for covers, portraits, maps, timelines, or product previews.
- Preserve no-likeness decisions; never generate a substitute face merely to complete a visual grid.
- Treat culture as living context with local and community stewardship—not as a style pack available for unrestricted reuse.
