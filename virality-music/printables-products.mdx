## Printables and products

Virality Music product records connect books, character dossiers, journals, posters, workbooks, templates, family packs, wall art, listening tools, companion designs, and bundles. The catalog is visible for discovery, but paid redemption remains deliberately unavailable in the current evidence state.

<Warning>
  **Do not treat this as a live paid storefront.** As of August 26, 2026, the observed catalog state is `SAFE_HOLD`: **355 total product records**, **353 production-ready paid records held**, **0 redeemable paid records**, **2 free records**, and **9 bundles**. The bundle count is a product classification and may overlap the 355-record inventory rather than adding nine more rows.
</Warning>

## What must be true before a paid record becomes redeemable

The exact product must pass the applicable controls. No one success promotes an entire catalog.

```text
stable product + version identity
→ approved source package and checksum
→ rights, canon, audience, and cultural clearance
→ accessibility and format QA
→ approved public description, price, and terms
→ exact payment-provider Product/Price readback
→ checkout canary
→ signed webhook and duplicate protection
→ order and entitlement issuance
→ protected delivery and returning access
→ refund, dispute, expiry, and revocation behavior
→ monitoring, support, and reconciliation
```

`Production-ready` is not the same as `redeemable`. A catalog row, historical price, payment-provider object, file, checkout button, or successful HTTP response does not independently prove the complete path.

## Customer-facing state language

Use only wording supported by the exact record:

| If the exact state is… | Public wording |
| --- | --- |
| `free` with verified access | “Open the free record” or the exact approved access label |
| `production_ready` + `SAFE_HOLD` | “Review product record” or “Notify me / inquire,” never “Buy now” |
| `inquiry_only` | “Request information” or the exact business-path label |
| `archived` | “Archived record,” with a successor link if one exists |
| `unknown` | “Availability not verified” |

## License and delivery boundary

- A personal-use product receipt does not grant commercial, adaptation, broadcast, manufacturing, training, or resale rights.
- A product preview or mockup is not the deliverable master.
- A visible price may be historical, proposed, or current; its label and effective evidence must travel with it.
- A free item does not waive copyright, mark, privacy, or machine-use restrictions.
- Customer, payment, download-token, refund, dispute, and entitlement records remain private.
- Protected files should be delivered through the approved entitlement path, never by publishing master URLs in PentaDocs.
