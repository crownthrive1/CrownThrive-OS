## State and price interpretation

- `AVAILABLE_FREE_OBSERVED` identifies the two zero-credit public records; the exact route and displayed access terms still control.
- `PRODUCTION_READY_HELD` identifies preparation evidence while paid redemption remains unavailable.
- Configured credits are retained only as an aggregate distribution on the overview page; per-record values are excluded to prevent reconstruction of private pricing logic.
- Historical dollar values on the books page are separate shop observations and must not be reconciled to this printables registry by title alone.
- `source_status: available` in the machine source cannot override `redemption_available: false` or the global `SAFE_HOLD`.
