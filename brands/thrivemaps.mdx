# ThriveMaps — Help Shape What Comes Next

ThriveMaps is CrownThrive's product-feedback, roadmap and changelog layer. It gives communities a structured place to submit ideas, vote on priorities, follow product direction and see what changed without turning support tickets into an unorganized feature-request inbox.

## What this helps you do

- Submit a product suggestion with enough context to evaluate it.
- Vote or react to existing ideas instead of duplicating them.
- Follow a public roadmap where the product makes one available.
- Read release and changelog updates.
- Separate a feature request from an outage, bug or account-support issue.

## Availability and eligibility

ThriveMaps is a current CrownThrive feedback/roadmap identity, but public boards may be product-specific rather than centralized on one universal URL. CrownThrive properties have historically exposed roadmap, update and ProductLift-style routes from their own navigation. Use the roadmap link published by the specific product whenever available.

<Warning>
  A roadmap card is not a promise of delivery, date, pricing or scope. It expresses product direction and feedback state. Current production behavior must be verified in the product itself or its Help Center.
</Warning>

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Search before submitting">
    Look for an existing request that already captures the problem or desired outcome.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe the outcome, not only the feature">
    Explain what you are trying to accomplish, who is affected and what currently blocks you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Vote and follow">
    Use reactions or votes to show demand and subscribe to updates when the board supports notifications.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Watch the changelog">
    A feature should be treated as available when the product and current release information confirm it, not merely when a roadmap item changes labels.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Feedback versus support

Use **ThriveMaps** for ideas and product direction. Use **crownthrivesupport.com** for troubleshooting, billing, account access, policies or incidents. Use the **Network Status** surface for outages and maintenance. Keeping those lanes separate helps the team respond faster and prevents a bug from being buried in a product-ideas board.

## Roadmap status language

Roadmap labels can vary by board, but the practical meanings should remain clear:

- **Idea / Under review:** feedback exists; no delivery commitment.
- **Planned:** the product intends to work on the item, but scope and timing can change.
- **In progress:** work has begun; this still does not mean the feature is usable in production.
- **Released:** verify the current product and release notes for actual availability.

## Ecosystem routes

ThriveMaps receives feedback from users across products such as Locticians, CrownThrive IO, CrownPulse, CrownLytics, ThrivePush, ThriveSeat and other CrownThrive surfaces. PentaDocs can document the released behavior after it is verified; ThriveMaps remains the public direction-and-feedback layer.

## Pricing and access

No standalone public ThriveMaps pricing schedule is established in the source set used for this guide. Individual product roadmaps can be publicly viewable even when the product itself has paid plans.

## Privacy, conduct and moderation

Do not post passwords, API keys, personal records, private customer data, unpublished contract terms or security vulnerabilities on a public roadmap board. Product feedback may be public. Sensitive bugs or security reports belong in the appropriate support/private reporting path.

## Troubleshooting

**I submitted an idea and cannot find it.** It may be pending moderation, merged with an existing request or associated with a different product board.

**A roadmap says planned, but the feature is not visible.** Planned does not mean released. Check the current product and Help Center.

**I need immediate help with a broken feature.** Use CrownThrive Support rather than the roadmap.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can voting guarantee a feature will be built?

No. Votes are one input into prioritization, not a contractual commitment.

### Is ThriveMaps the same as Network Status?

No. ThriveMaps handles feedback and roadmaps. Network Status communicates operational incidents and maintenance.

### Can I submit an integration idea?

Yes, provided you explain the actual workflow and expected user outcome rather than only naming another vendor.
