# Crown Affiliates — Earn From Approved Referrals, Not Ambiguous Promises

Crown Affiliates is CrownThrive's referral and performance-distribution program. It uses approved links, codes and offer rules to attribute qualifying conversions and calculate eligible commissions under the current program schedule.

## What this helps you do

- Join an eligible affiliate program.
- Receive governed links or codes for approved offers.
- Understand what counts as a qualifying conversion.
- Track attribution and eligible commission activity.
- Follow disclosure and promotional rules.
- Progress into other CrownThrive opportunities through separate eligibility paths.

## Availability and eligibility

Crown Affiliates has a current public route at [**https://affiliates.crownthrive.com**](https://affiliates.crownthrive.com). Enrollment, approved offers, commission percentages, cookie/attribution windows, payout minimums, hold periods and payment methods can change by program/version. Use the live affiliate dashboard and current agreement for actual economics.

## How it works

## Economics and pricing

Joining an affiliate program is not the same as buying a product plan, so there is no single “Crown Affiliates subscription price” established in the source set used for this guide.

A valid current affiliate schedule should specify:

- Eligible products/platforms.
- Commission amount or formula.
- Attribution method and window.
- Excluded transactions.
- Self-referral/fraud restrictions.
- Refund and chargeback reversals.
- Payout minimum, timing and hold period.
- Tax/reporting responsibilities.
- Geographic or age restrictions.
- Effective date and supersession.

<Warning>
  Historical lifetime percentages, permanent overrides or franchise-referral examples do not create current compensation. Use the effective current program schedule.
</Warning>

## Crown Affiliates versus Crown Ambassadors

Affiliates are primarily a **performance/referral** role. Crown Ambassadors are a higher-trust **advocacy/representation** role with separate eligibility, campaign, training and brand-use rules. One role does not automatically grant the other.

## Crown Affiliates versus CrownFluence

CrownFluence manages creator/influencer campaign deliverables and rights. An affiliate can promote an approved offer without becoming a contracted campaign creator; a creator can participate in a campaign without receiving affiliate commission unless separately enrolled.

## Thrive Flywheel mapping

Affiliates support acquisition and distributed growth. Approved offers can come from CrownThrive brands such as Melanin Magic, Virality Music, education, events or other current programs. CrownLytics supports attribution evidence, CrownThriveU can handle required training, My CrownRewards can provide separate noncash incentives and CHLOM can govern rights/economic schedules according to implemented capability.

## Privacy, disclosure and conduct

Do not buy or fabricate traffic, create fake accounts, hide disclosures, misrepresent prices, impersonate CrownThrive or claim endorsement/authority beyond the affiliate role. Customer personal data remains with the approved commerce/analytics systems; affiliates do not receive private customer information merely because they generated a referral.

## Troubleshooting

**A conversion is missing.** Check the current attribution window, link/code, excluded transactions, refund state and whether the purchase completed through the eligible path.

**My commission was reversed.** Refunds, chargebacks, fraud findings or ineligible transactions can reverse an accrual according to the current program.

**I want a different commission rate.** Program economics are governed by the current schedule. Do not assume a historical rate or negotiate by publishing your own percentage.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does affiliate status make me a CrownThrive employee or partner?

No. It does not create employment, ownership, franchise or partnership rights by implication.

### Can I use CrownThrive logos?

Only according to the approved brand/creative permissions for the program.

### Are CrownRewards points the same as cash affiliate commissions?

No. Noncash loyalty incentives and monetary commissions are separate economic objects.
