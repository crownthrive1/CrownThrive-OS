# ThriveAlumni — Stay Connected After the Program Ends

ThriveAlumni is CrownThrive's graduate and alumni community for continuing relationships after a course, cohort, incubator program or other eligible learning/progression experience ends. The purpose is continuity: networking, benefits, career opportunities, ongoing education, mentoring, private events and pathways to give back.

## What this helps you do

- Stay connected to peers after a program ends.
- Find continuing-learning and mentorship opportunities.
- Discover eligible events, career or collaboration opportunities.
- Preserve earned program identity or credentials without treating alumni status as a new professional license.
- Progress from participant to mentor, instructor, ambassador or other eligible role through separate qualification paths.

## Availability and eligibility

ThriveAlumni is a current CrownThrive identity. Public navigation has referenced [**https://thrivealumni.com**](https://thrivealumni.com). Exact eligibility depends on the program you completed; not every CrownThrive account or purchase automatically creates alumni status.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Complete an eligible program or pathway">
    Alumni status begins from a program or relationship that actually grants it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Keep your profile current">
    Update contact, interests, skills and availability so opportunities can reach the right people.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use alumni benefits intentionally">
    Join relevant events, mentoring, learning or career opportunities rather than treating alumni access as an automatic entitlement to every CrownThrive program.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Progress into new roles separately">
    Becoming a mentor, instructor, consultant, affiliate or ambassador requires the rules of that role.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Benefits and opportunities

Potential ThriveAlumni benefits can include networking, job/career opportunity boards, private events, continuing education, credential visibility, member perks and leadership opportunities. The live program or current benefit page controls exact eligibility and availability.

## Pricing and access

No single current public ThriveAlumni price table is present in the source set used for this guide. Alumni access can be included with an eligible program or offered through a separate membership/benefit structure. Do not infer a fee from CrownThriveU, ThrivePeer or another platform.

## Ecosystem routes

ThriveAlumni can connect graduates back to **CrownThriveU** for continuing education, **ThrivePeer** for mentoring, **ThriveGather** and **ThriveTickets** for events, **CrownConsult** for separately scoped expertise opportunities, **Crown Affiliates/Ambassadors** for approved distribution roles and **My CrownRewards** for eligible loyalty benefits.

## Privacy and professional identity

Alumni directories should not expose private contact, employment or education details without appropriate visibility settings. Alumni status can show a relationship to a program, but it should not be presented as a credential, license or endorsement beyond what the underlying program actually granted.

## Troubleshooting

**I completed a course but do not see alumni access.** Check whether that specific course or cohort grants ThriveAlumni eligibility. Completion alone does not automatically guarantee every alumni program.

**An old alumni benefit is no longer available.** Benefits can change. Use the current program page rather than an archived promotion.

**I want to mentor other members.** Use the ThrivePeer mentor/role process. Alumni status can support eligibility but does not automatically create the mentor role.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is ThriveAlumni the same as CrownThriveU?

No. CrownThriveU is the learning platform; ThriveAlumni supports post-program community and continuity.

### Can alumni receive job opportunities?

Career and opportunity pathways are part of the intended alumni model, but a listing is not a guarantee of employment.

### Does alumni status expire?

That depends on the current program rules. Use the live alumni policy for your specific cohort or benefit.
