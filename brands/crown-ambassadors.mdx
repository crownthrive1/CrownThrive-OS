# Crown Ambassadors — Advocacy With Standards

Crown Ambassadors is CrownThrive's higher-trust advocacy and representation program. Ambassadors can participate in selected campaigns, launches, community activations and brand storytelling under a current program agreement, training standard and scope of authority.

## What this helps you do

- Understand what Ambassador status does and does not authorize.
- Participate in approved campaigns and brand activations.
- Use approved assets, talking points and disclosure language.
- Progress through training and eligibility requirements.
- Receive campaign or program benefits according to current schedules.

## Availability and eligibility

Crown Ambassadors is an invite-only or qualified program under current CrownThrive governance. Public legal materials describe a month-to-month Ambassador structure, but exact current commission rates, exclusivity rules, campaign benefits, qualification thresholds and active provider state must be checked against the current program.

Ambassador status does not automatically create employment, ownership, franchise, signature authority, sub-affiliate/network override rights or permanent compensation.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Meet the program eligibility requirements">
    Complete any required identity, disclosure, conduct or product training before representing the program.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Accept a current campaign or assignment">
    Review the approved message, offer, creative rights, dates and any exclusivity or disclosure requirement.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Represent the brand within scope">
    Use approved claims and assets. Do not improvise medical, financial, performance or partnership claims on CrownThrive's behalf.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Provide campaign evidence">
    Submit the required links, content or performance information according to the campaign rules.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Receive eligible benefits under the current schedule">
    Monetary commission, creator fees, noncash perks and CrownRewards are separate benefit types and should not be combined into one ambiguous earnings balance.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Ambassador versus Affiliate

**Crown Affiliates** focuses on attributable referral/performance distribution. **Crown Ambassadors** focuses on advocacy, community representation and higher-trust participation. An Ambassador can also be an Affiliate only when separately enrolled under the applicable program rules.

## Ambassador versus CrownFluence

CrownFluence is a creator-campaign exchange built around briefs and deliverables. Ambassadors can participate in creator campaigns, but Ambassador status alone does not create a content-production contract or paid-media license.

## Compensation and benefits

No single current Crown Ambassador public compensation table is established in the source set used for this guide. A valid program or campaign should state:

- Eligible offer or campaign.
- Commission, fee, bonus or perk.
- Attribution or performance condition where applicable.
- Payout/hold/reversal rules.
- Exclusivity or category restrictions.
- Required disclosures.
- Brand-asset permissions.
- Effective term and termination rules.

<Warning>
  Do not reuse historical “lifetime,” override or franchise-referral percentages as current Ambassador compensation without a current authorized schedule.
</Warning>

## Thrive Flywheel mapping

Ambassadors can strengthen discovery, culture and trust around CrownThrive launches. CrownThriveU can support required training, CrownFluence can manage creator campaigns, AdLuxe can handle paid media, My CrownRewards can provide separate noncash perks, CrownLytics can support performance evidence and Crown Affiliates can manage separate referral economics.

## Conduct, privacy and disclosures

Ambassadors should disclose material relationships clearly. They do not receive private customer data merely because they promote a product. Private contracts, payout information and unreleased campaigns should not be exposed publicly.

## Troubleshooting

**I am an Ambassador but cannot access an Affiliate link.** The programs are separate. Confirm whether that campaign also requires affiliate enrollment.

**A campaign ended but the content is still live.** Review current post-campaign usage, takedown, disclosure and paid-media rights. Some content may remain live while boosted/paid rights expire.

**I am being asked to make a claim that seems unsupported.** Pause and request clarification. Ambassador status is not authority to invent or repeat unsupported claims.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can anyone become a Crown Ambassador automatically?

No. The program uses separate eligibility and approval rules.

### Does Ambassador status let me sign contracts for CrownThrive?

No. Representation in a campaign is not general contract-signature authority.

### Can Ambassadors receive free products?

That can be a program benefit, but any gift or material connection still requires appropriate disclosure.
