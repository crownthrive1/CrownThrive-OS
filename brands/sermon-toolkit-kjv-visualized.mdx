# The Sermon Toolkit & KJV Visualized — Discover, Understand, Prepare and Teach

**The Sermon Toolkit™** is CrownThrive's umbrella ministry, pastoral and teaching platform. **KJV Visualized™** is the flagship visual-scripture publishing and reader experience. The current relationship is explicit:

**KJV Visualized: DISCOVER · UNDERSTAND → The Sermon Toolkit: PREPARE · STRUCTURE → CrownThrive: PUBLISH · DISTRIBUTE**

The two systems share institutional identity, rights and entitlements without becoming duplicate products.

## Scripture and authored-content boundary

The governing rule is simple:

> **The image opens the door; the text remains the judge.**

KJV Scripture must remain separately labeled from:

- CrownThrive commentary.
- Historical context.
- Denominational/traditional interpretation.
- Teaching application.
- Devotional writing.
- Visual reconstruction.
- Testimony or memory.
- Fiction/dramatization.
- AI-generated or AI-assisted material.

No illustration, sermon aid or AI explanation may silently alter or override the Scripture text.

## My Sanctuary and private records

My Sanctuary is intended to converge devotional, reflection, prayer, reading plans, saved Scripture, sermons/notes, purchases/licenses, downloads, courses/certificates, church/team, rewards, billing, profile, privacy/security and data export/deletion.

Prayer journals and reflection records should not flow into advertising, ordinary analytics or public AI prompts. AI analysis must be opt-in and privacy-safe.

## Sunday-to-Sunday Discipleship Engine

The approved architecture can transform a properly authorized sermon into structured derivatives such as transcript/manuscript, outline, notes, devotional, small-group/youth/family material, bulletin, email/SMS/social content, quote cards/slides, Go-Flipbooks reader and archive records. Upload rights, malware scanning, Scripture verification, review and no-public-release-without-authorization remain required.

## AI \+ AIDA

## Frequently asked questions

No. They are tightly integrated but distinct: KJV Visualized is the visual-scripture/reader experience; Sermon Toolkit is the ministry preparation/teaching umbrella.

No. Source/review/rights gates remain separate from route planning.
