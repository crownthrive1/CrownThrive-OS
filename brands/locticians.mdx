# Locticians — Hair. Health. Culture. Your Global Natural Lifestyle Network.

Locticians is a culture-first natural lifestyle network connecting people with professionals, brands, educators and community across beauty, grooming, wellness, culture, fashion, education and entertainment. The current public platform highlights **75,000\+ members**, Verified Pros, Get Matched, community content, discovery and ecosystem-powered professional growth.

## What this helps you do

### Members and clients

- Search for professionals by service, specialty, category or location.
- Use Get Matched when you want a more guided introduction.
- Review profiles, portfolios, services, reviews and policies.
- Book supported professionals through ThriveSeat links.
- Participate in community discussions, articles, events and rewards where available.

### Professionals and creators

- Publish a rich profile and services.
- Build reviews and reputation.
- Appear in category/city discovery and Get Matched results according to current rules.
- Publish events, education and community content.
- Use Nexus Credits for eligible Locticians visibility tools.
- Connect to learning, mentorship, media, loyalty and advertising across CrownThrive.

## Get Matched

Get Matched is a guided request service for people and organizations that want help finding aligned professionals.

### Individuals and families

Share texture, style history, scalp or skin considerations, sensitivities, timing, city and budget.

### Salons, barbershops and suites

Use the request as a talent pipeline for roles, schedules and staffing needs.

### Brands, studios and productions

Submit dates, locations, deliverables, team requirements and cultural/continuity needs for shoots, tours or projects.

### Educators and program leads

Find professionals for workshops, trainings, schools, churches, community programs and wellness organizations.

## How Get Matched works

<Steps>

## Verified Pro

“Verified Pro” is a trust signal, not a guarantee of every outcome. Profiles can include reviews, portfolios, category tags and policies. Customers should still review the specific service and professional before booking.

## Nexus Credits versus CrownRewards

**Nexus Credits** are Locticians internal credits for visibility and promotion such as eligible featured spots, boosts, coupon unlocks and event amplification.

**My CrownRewards** is the separate ecosystem loyalty platform at mycrownrewards.com. CrownRewards points do not convert into Nexus Credits and do not determine Locticians search placement.

## Privacy and safety

Get Matched requests can contain texture, sensitivities, schedule and contact information. Share only what is needed for matching. Service professionals remain responsible for their own service policies, licensing/credentials where applicable, sanitation and customer safety.

## Troubleshooting

**I need a professional quickly.** Use Get Matched and include location, timing, budget and the actual service need.

**I joined but cannot see professional features.** Check the current membership tier and whether the feature is a community, professional or paid visibility feature.

**My Nexus balance is not the same as CrownRewards.** That is expected. They are separate systems.

**A booking problem happens after I leave Locticians.** If the appointment is being managed by ThriveSeat, use the booking/provider support route for that transaction while keeping the Locticians profile context.
