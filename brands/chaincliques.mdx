# ChainCliques — Preserved Web3 Community Identity, Not a Live Production Claim

ChainCliques is a CrownThrive community brand historically associated with blockchain-native discovery, wallet identity and token-gated group concepts. The shared TrendyGroup implementation used by the Cliques family is sunset, while the brand is preserved for history and possible future reuse.

## What this helps you understand

- Why ChainCliques still appears in historical and legal documentation.
- Why a preserved domain does not prove a current Web3 product.
- Why legacy wallet/community concepts are not current production capabilities by implication.

## Availability and eligibility

Current disposition: **sunset brand reserve**. No current product launch, wallet integration, token-gating engine, API or membership program is established by the sources used for this guide.

## Historical role

Historical material framed ChainCliques around chain-native communities, wallets, token-gated groups and reputation. Those concepts remain useful lineage, but any future implementation must use current identity, security, moderation, privacy and rights standards.

## Pricing

There is no current ChainCliques pricing table because there is no verified current production service. Historical plans, token requirements or third-party engine pricing are not current CrownThrive offers.

## Security and privacy

Do not connect a wallet, sign a transaction or provide recovery phrases based on an old ChainCliques page or message. CrownThrive will never use historical community branding as authority to request a seed phrase or private key.

## Current alternatives

Use current CrownThrive community tools such as Locticians, CrownThrive Commons, ThriveGather or ThrivePeer depending on the goal.

## Troubleshooting

**I found a ChainCliques login or wallet prompt.** Treat it as unverified. Use CrownThrive Support before connecting an account or signing anything.

**Can ChainCliques be reactivated later?** The brand is preserved, but a reactivation would require an explicit current product/governance record.
