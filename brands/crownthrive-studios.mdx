# CrownThrive Studios — Make the Asset Once, Preserve the Rights Forever

CrownThrive Studios is CrownThrive's current creative and media production operating system for audio, visual, screen, campaign, brand and AI-assisted production workflows. Its purpose is not only to make content look good; it is to preserve the project, source assets, contributors, approvals, rights and downstream destinations so work can be reused intelligently without losing provenance.

## Availability and lineage

The current public host is [**https://crownthrivestudios.com**](https://crownthrivestudios.com). CrownThrive Studios is the canonical current identity. Historical **ThriveStudio™** references are preserved as predecessor/alias lineage. **AdLuxe Studio is separate** and remains the advertising-creative lane under AdLuxe Network.

## What this helps you do

- Develop photo, video, audio, campaign and brand assets.
- Use AI-assisted tools without losing source/rights context.
- Organize projects, versions, approvals and asset libraries.
- Prepare content for Virality Music, Melanated media, Locticians TV, The Mane Experience, AdLuxe, CrownThriveU and other approved destinations.
- Preserve creator, likeness, voice, music and third-party rights information.

## Production objects

A production-ready asset should preserve:

- Project and asset IDs.
- Operator/contributor identity.
- Source files and references.
- Provider/model/version where AI is used.
- Revision history.
- Ownership/license state.
- Voice/likeness permissions.
- Approval/review state.
- Intended destinations.
- Correction/archive history.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open a scoped production project">
    Define the asset, audience, destination, deliverables, rights and deadline before generating files.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Collect source and release evidence">
    Gather approved logos, footage, music, scripts, photos, releases and client materials.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Produce and version">
    Create drafts, keep revision history and avoid overwriting the approved master silently.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review rights, brand and accessibility">
    Check the actual destination requirements before approval.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Hand off to the receiving platform">
    The receiving platform's editorial, rights, theological, accessibility or commerce rules remain authoritative.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## AI-assisted production

Current public Studios material includes AI-assisted creative workflows. AI does not erase source obligations. A provider granting commercial use is not automatically proof of copyright ownership, third-party clearance, voice/likeness permission or factual accuracy.

CHLOM protection/integration is presented as future/coming-soon in current Studios evidence. Do not claim automatic production enforcement or rights clearance until the specific implementation is verified.

## Pricing and project scope

No universal public CrownThrive Studios service price table is present in the current source set used for this guide. Production can be priced per asset, session, project, package, campaign, retainer or managed program. A current quote should state:

- Deliverables and formats.
- Number of revisions.
- Source/master-file treatment.
- Rights and usage term.
- Talent, music or third-party costs.
- Turnaround and approval milestones.
- Storage/archive expectations.
- Downstream distribution included or excluded.

Do not infer Studios pricing from AdLuxe Studio or a historical ThriveStudio document.

## CrownThrive Studios versus Melanated Studios

**Melanated Studios** is an original-programming/show-franchise imprint inside Virality Music. CrownThrive Studios can provide production support, packaging and standards, but it does not automatically own the show franchises or replace that imprint.

## Thrive Flywheel mapping

Studios supplies production assets across the ecosystem: Virality Music, Melanated media, Locticians TV, The Mane Experience, AdLuxe, Sermon Toolkit/KJV Visualized and CrownThriveU. CrownLytics can measure published performance; PentaDocs can preserve product/brand instructions; CHLOM can support future rights governance according to implemented scope.

## Privacy, rights and safety

Keep private client source material, unreleased media, access credentials and contributor records out of public galleries. Minors, health information, sensitive locations and private likeness/voice material require extra handling. Do not publish an AI-generated representation as a real event or endorsement without disclosure/rights review.

## Troubleshooting

**An old project says ThriveStudio. Is it invalid?** No. Preserve the lineage and route the current operating identity to CrownThrive Studios.

**I need an ad banner, not a full production project.** AdLuxe Studio may be the better campaign-creative lane.

**The receiving platform rejected an approved Studios asset.** Studio approval does not override the destination platform's rules. Fix the destination-specific issue and preserve the revision history.
