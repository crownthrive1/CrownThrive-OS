# KineticReleaf — Recovery Support for People Who Keep Moving

KineticReleaf is CrownThrive's recovery and wellness product line for topical products, recovery kits and lifestyle-focused post-activity routines. The brand is designed around everyday recovery support, not as a substitute for medical evaluation or treatment.

## What this helps you understand

- The intended role of KineticReleaf in CrownThrive's wellness corridor.
- What product information should be checked before use.
- Why “recovery” language does not automatically mean a product treats an injury or disease.
- How KineticReleaf can participate in retail, events, service businesses and loyalty programs when a current offer is verified.

## Availability and eligibility

KineticReleaf is a CrownThrive brand identity used for recovery/wellness products. Exact active SKUs, ingredients, supplier/manufacturer, inventory, price and fulfillment must be verified through the current product offer before purchase.

<Warning>
  If you have a significant injury, unexplained pain, swelling, numbness, weakness or another medical concern, use appropriate medical care. A consumer wellness product is not a diagnosis or treatment plan.
</Warning>

## Product standards

A current KineticReleaf item should identify:

- Exact product and variant.
- Ingredient list and relevant warnings.
- Intended use and directions.
- Quantity/net contents.
- Manufacturer or approved provider.
- Packaging and label version.
- Current price and inventory.
- Shipping, returns and support.
- Claims that are appropriate for the product category.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is KineticReleaf a prescription medical product?

This Help Center guide does not establish that. Treat it as a consumer recovery/wellness line unless a specific current product is lawfully classified and labeled otherwise.

### Can KineticReleaf guarantee recovery after exercise?

No. Individual experiences differ, and a wellness product should not be represented as a guaranteed recovery outcome.

### Is KineticReleaf the same as Get Kenetic!?

No. KineticReleaf is the recovery/wellness product line; Get Kenetic! is a separate performance/supplement brand identity.
