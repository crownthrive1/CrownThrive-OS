# Melanated Vault — Preserve the Record Without Exposing What Should Stay Protected

Melanated Vault is CrownThrive's archive/media identity for preserving cultural works, historical material, masters, source records and curated media that should remain recoverable across provider or platform changes. A vault is not simply a public download folder: assets can be public, member-only, licensed, restricted or internal depending on their rights and privacy state.

## What this helps you do

- Understand why CrownThrive preserves source masters and historical media.
- Discover public or approved archival materials when a current route exists.
- Keep archive identity separate from current publication or commerce.
- Preserve provenance, rights, corrections and takedown history.

## Availability and eligibility

Melanated Vault is a current media/archive identity in CrownThrive's stable-ID architecture. Provider/deployment verification remains open. Exact public browsing, membership access, search, API and current collection state must be verified from the live deployment.

## Access classes

A preserved asset can be:

- Public.
- Free but account-gated.
- Paid/entitled.
- Licensed to a defined user or organization.
- Restricted for rights, privacy or legal reasons.
- Internal preservation only.

Archive preservation does not override those access classes.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Preserve the source and metadata">
    Keep stable work/asset IDs, creators, dates, source files, rights and historical context.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign an access class">
    Public discovery and source preservation are different decisions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish only the approved derivative">
    A preview, reader or public copy can be different from the protected master.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Propagate corrections and restrictions">
    A takedown, rights expiry or privacy correction should update downstream access without deleting the institutional history.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Pricing and access

No universal Melanated Vault price table is established in the current source set. Some archival items may be public; others can be part of memberships, purchases, licenses or research/partner programs. The current asset/collection offer controls.

## Rights and privacy

A historical photograph, recording or document can contain privacy, likeness, copyright, community or family restrictions even if it is old. Preserve source evidence without assuming age equals public-domain status.

## Thrive Flywheel mapping

Melanated Vault can preserve source material used by Melanated media, Virality Music, CrownThrive Studios, CrownThriveU and historical/cultural programming. MVP can later surface authorized discovery metadata without becoming the owner or exposing protected masters.

## Troubleshooting

**I found a Vault title but cannot access the file.** Discovery and access are separate. Check the current rights/entitlement state.

**Can I reuse an archived image in my project?** Only when the current rights/license permits it.

**Why preserve content that is no longer public?** Institutional history, provenance, corrections and rights evidence can remain important after public availability ends.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Melanated Vault a public-domain library?

No.

### Does preservation mean permanent public access?

No. Preservation and public availability are distinct.

### Can MVP index Vault items later?

That is an intended federation use when authorized metadata and access rules permit it.
