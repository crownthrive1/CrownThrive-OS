# Melanin Magic Cafe — Bold Brews, Culture and Everyday Ritual

Melanin Magic Cafe is the coffee-focused lifestyle brand within the Melanin Magic family. Its role is to extend Melanin Magic beyond hair and beauty products into a culturally grounded everyday ritual that can support retail, events, gifting and community experiences.

## What this helps you understand

- How Melanin Magic Cafe differs from ThriveSip and the retired ThriveCafe platform identity.
- What information a current coffee offer should provide.
- How Cafe products can participate in Melanin Magic, MM Suites and event experiences.

## Availability and eligibility

The current CrownThrive architecture recognizes Melanin Magic Cafe as a modern product/imprint concept, including lineage recovered from older commerce sources. Exact active coffee SKUs, roast partners, inventory, subscriptions and pricing require current product evidence before a specific item should be represented as available.

## Product standards

A current Cafe offer should identify the product or blend, roast/format, net weight or count, ingredients where applicable, caffeine/decaf status, supplier/roaster, packaging, preparation guidance, price, shipping and return/support terms.

## Ecosystem routes

Melanin Magic Cafe can pair with Melanin Magic, MM Suites, ThriveKiosks, TapStations, ThriveTickets and culture/media activations. My CrownRewards can support eligible loyalty. Broader impact claims should route through the CrownThrive Impact Institute at [**https://crownthrive.org**](https://crownthrive.org).

## Safety and claims

Coffee and beverage offers should state ingredients and caffeine information where relevant. Lifestyle language is not medical advice. Customers with health concerns or caffeine sensitivities should use appropriate professional guidance.

## Troubleshooting

**I found a Melanin Magic Cafe mention but no product page.** The brand can exist as a current identity while individual product offers remain unverified or not yet active.

**Is Melanin Magic Cafe the same as ThriveCafe?** No. ThriveCafe is preserved as a separate sunset/repurpose-pending platform identity. Melanin Magic Cafe is the coffee brand.

**Is it the same as ThriveSip?** No. ThriveSip is a broader beverage/lifestyle brand lane; Melanin Magic Cafe is specifically the Melanin Magic coffee imprint.

## Frequently asked questions

### Will Melanin Magic Cafe be sold inside MM Suites locations?

That can be an ecosystem use case, but a location should only represent products that are actually approved, stocked and permitted for that location.

### Can events use the Cafe brand?

Only through an approved current event or product arrangement.
