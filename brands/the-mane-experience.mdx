# The Mane Experience — Where Culture Becomes Community Media

The Mane Experience, often shortened to **T.M.E.**, is CrownThrive's participatory culture-first editorial and community media destination. It brings together beauty, lifestyle, entrepreneurship, creator spotlights, community stories and user-contributed media while maintaining editorial, moderation and rights standards.

## What this helps you do

- Read culture, beauty, wellness and entrepreneurship features.
- Discover creators, professionals and community stories.
- Submit or participate in editorial/community content when intake is open.
- Understand contributor credits, moderation and content-use boundaries.
- Move from a story or spotlight into the relevant CrownThrive profile, event, product or program.

## Availability and eligibility

The Mane Experience is a current editorial/community identity. Public route lineage includes [**https://themaneexperience.com**](https://themaneexperience.com). Exact CMS, contributor workflow, monetization and live submission state should be verified from the current site.

## The Mane Experience versus The TAME Gallery

- **The Mane Experience:** editorial/community destination and participatory storytelling.
- **The TAME Gallery:** aggregation, deduplication, trend intelligence and editorial handoff layer.

TAME can surface signals; The Mane Experience makes editorial choices. Aggregated third-party material is not automatically CrownThrive-owned content.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Discover or pitch a story">

  </Step>

  </Step>
</Steps>

## Pricing, credits and contributor economics

No universal current The Mane Experience subscription or contributor-pay table is present in the source set used for this guide. Content can be editorial, contributor-submitted, sponsored or campaign-based. Payment, credits, CrownRewards, contributor fees and sponsorship economics are separate arrangements and should be stated in the current agreement.

## Sponsored and branded content

AdLuxe can govern approved sponsored inventory. Sponsored editorial should be labeled clearly and should not rewrite community/editorial standards simply because a brand paid for placement.

## Rights and user submissions

Submitting text, images or video should not be interpreted as an unlimited transfer of every underlying right unless the current contributor terms explicitly say so. Contributor identity, source files, permissions, corrections and takedown state should remain recoverable.

## Thrive Flywheel mapping

The Mane Experience can create discovery for Locticians, Melanin Magic, Virality Music, CrownThriveU, events and creators. AdLuxe can support paid amplification, CrownFluence can support approved creator campaigns, CrownLytics can measure traffic and My CrownRewards can support separate loyalty programs.

## Privacy, conduct and accessibility

Do not publish private messages, customer records, minors' information or sensitive personal stories without appropriate consent. Use alt text, readable contrast and accessible media where practical. Community comments remain subject to moderation.

## Troubleshooting

**A featured profile has outdated booking information.** Use the linked current Locticians/ThriveSeat profile for live service details.

**I submitted a story but it was not published.** Submission does not guarantee editorial acceptance. Check the current submission terms and status.

**A sponsored feature was mistaken for independent editorial.** Use the disclosure/correction path so commercial relationships remain clear.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is The Mane Experience the same as Locticians?

No. Locticians is a network/directory; T.M.E. is editorial/community media.

### Is TAME the short name for The Mane Experience?

No. The TAME Gallery is a separate aggregation/intelligence identity.

### Can brands sponsor content?

Yes where an approved current AdLuxe/editorial package exists and sponsorship is disclosed.
