# CrownInsights — See the System More Clearly

CrownInsights is CrownThrive's visual support and business-insight surface. It is designed to turn complicated product, workflow and performance information into clearer guides, visual explanations and action-oriented views.

## What this helps you do

- Understand a CrownThrive workflow visually before taking action.
- Review product guidance without digging through deep technical documentation.
- Translate business signals into plain-language next steps.
- Connect learning and support material to the underlying platform that performs the task.

## Availability and eligibility

Current CrownThrive support surfaces reference CrownInsights at [**https://insight.crownthrive.com**](https://insight.crownthrive.com). The institutional registry preserves CrownInsights as a current visual-guidance/learning-support identity while provider, hosting and feature details remain separately verified. If a specific dashboard or tool is not visible on the public site, do not assume the capability is available merely because it is described historically.

## How CrownInsights differs from nearby products

- **CrownInsights** explains and visualizes.
- **CrownLytics** measures web behavior, traffic, heatmaps and replays.
- **CrownThrive Support / PentaDocs** is the governed knowledge and Help Center layer.
- **CrownThriveU** provides structured courses and learning experiences.

These surfaces can cross-link, but they serve different jobs.

## How it works

## Ecosystem routes

CrownInsights can point users toward CrownThrive IO, CrownLytics, ThriveSeat, Locticians, CrownThriveU, PentaDocs and other product-specific guides. It is especially useful at the **understand and decide** stage of the Thrive Flywheel, before a user moves into an operational product.

## Pricing and access

No standalone CrownInsights pricing schedule is established in the current public source set used for this guide. Treat CrownInsights as a CrownThrive guidance/insight surface unless a current checkout or plan page explicitly states otherwise.

## Privacy and accessibility

Visual guides should avoid exposing account-specific secrets, personal records or unredacted operational screenshots. Use readable labels, alt text and explanatory text so the guidance remains useful for people who cannot rely on the visual alone.

## Troubleshooting

**A screenshot does not match the current product.** Use the linked product documentation and current interface as the controlling user experience. Visual guides can lag behind fast-moving products.

**I need raw analytics, not an explanation.** Use CrownLytics for web analytics and behavior data.

**I need a step-by-step support article.** Use CrownThrive Support at crownthrivesupport.com.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is CrownInsights the same as CrownLytics?

No. CrownInsights is a visual knowledge and decision-support surface. CrownLytics is the analytics platform.

### Is CrownInsights a course platform?

No. CrownThriveU is the structured learning platform; CrownInsights can support learning with visual guidance.

### Does CrownInsights have its own paid plan?

No current public pricing source used for this article establishes one.
