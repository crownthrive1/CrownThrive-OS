# CHLOM — Make Authority, Rights and Compliance Explicit

**CHLOM™** is CrownThrive's compliance, licensing, rights/ownership and governance framework. It is designed to help platforms answer difficult questions consistently: **Who has authority? What right exists? What evidence supports it? What conditions apply? What changes if the transaction is refunded, revoked, disputed or superseded?**

CHLOM is not one generic blockchain feature and it is not permission to claim that every envisioned engine, pallet, token or automated compliance path is live today.

## What this helps you understand

- How CrownThrive separates identity, roles, authority, rights, entitlements and evidence.
- Why payment does not automatically equal ownership or commercial rights.
- How licenses can be issued, conditioned, suspended, revoked or superseded under governed rules.
- Why privacy-preserving verification can prove a condition without publishing unnecessary private data.
- How CHLOM can connect to commerce, media, education, events and professional programs while preserving source authority.

## Current public role

CHLOM is a current CrownThrive institutional framework with active governance, documentation and implementation work. Some runtime, provider, token, smart-contract, cross-chain, AI/ML, DLA/LEX, ZK, tax, treasury, staking and other technical capabilities are implemented, piloted, planned or held at different states. Public documentation should describe only the capability actually verified for the relevant surface.

</Warning>

## Core public concepts

### Authority and roles

CHLOM separates a person's identity from the authority attached to a role. A user account, employee title, moderator role, licensee role and governance voter are different relationships.

### Licenses and entitlements

A **license** expresses a governed right or permission. An **entitlement** expresses access to a product/service/content experience. They can be related without being identical.

### DLA — Decentralized Licensing Authority

DLA is the licensing-governance concept for issuing, amending, suspending and revoking eligible license relationships according to current authority and evidence.

### LEX — License Exchange

LEX is the governed license-market concept for approved license classes and rights offers. A listing cannot manufacture rights the seller does not actually have.

### Privacy-preserving verification

CHLOM architecture can use attestations and privacy-preserving proofs so a condition can be verified without exposing more underlying personal/business data than necessary.

### Evidence and auditability

Important decisions should preserve the source, version, actor, date, evidence and correction/supersession history so later systems do not turn an old claim into current authority.

## How it works conceptually

<Steps>
  <Step title="Identify the actor and role">
    Determine who is acting and which current role is relevant.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the authority and conditions">
    Confirm the license, agreement, policy, jurisdiction, product or governance rule that actually permits the action.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Evaluate required evidence">
    Use the minimum evidence needed for the decision and protect private supporting data.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Record the resulting state">
    Preserve the decision, rights/entitlement state, evidence references and effective version.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Handle changes explicitly">
    Refunds, expiration, suspension, revocation, correction and supersession should change current state without deleting history.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## CHLOM AI, ML and automation

CrownThrive uses CHLOM AI/ML concepts for decision support, policy analysis, risk/anomaly detection and governance assistance. High-consequence decisions still require the applicable current authority, evidence, review and appeal process. AI output does not become authority merely because it is generated inside a CHLOM workflow.

## CHLOM DID and Fingerprint concepts

CHLOM identity architecture includes decentralized/verifiable identity and internal fingerprint/anchor concepts for linking governed evidence. Public-facing identifiers should protect privacy and should not expose raw private biometric or secret data. “Fingerprint” is an integrity/identity concept and must not be misrepresented as a promise that every public user is being biometrically fingerprinted.

## Dual DAO governance

CHLOM governance architecture includes multi-body/dual-governance concepts so high-risk decisions are not reduced to one actor or one simple token vote. Exact quorum, delegation, risk class and authority rules belong to the current governance version and can change through governed amendment.

## CHM / CLOM and token caution

Internal and technical CHLOM materials include dual-token architecture concepts such as **CHM** and **CLOM**. This Help Center page does **not** announce a public token sale, investment offering, guaranteed value, exchange listing, yield product or public-mainnet availability.

## Pricing and commercial access

There is no universal public CHLOM subscription or token price table established in the current source set used for this guide. CHLOM can appear as governance infrastructure inside other products or as separately scoped enterprise/licensing/integration work.

A current commercial CHLOM engagement should identify:

- Service/integration scope.
- Environment and supported capability.
- Data/evidence classes.
- Roles/authority.
- Implementation and support obligations.
- Security/compliance requirements.
- Current price/fees.
- Effective legal and license terms.

Do not extrapolate enterprise pricing or token value from historical whitepapers, draft architecture or provider costs.

## Patent, trademark and IP language

Public CrownThrive materials have used proprietary and patent-pending language around CHLOM. A contractual or public claim of ownership is not the same as a government registration or issued patent. Current legal/IP status should be represented by the applicable official filing/record, not assumed from marketing copy.

## Privacy and high-risk decisions

CHLOM is intended to reduce unnecessary exposure, not centralize every private record. Health, family, child, legal, prayer/reflection and other restricted data should remain segregated and used only where the relevant workflow has authority. High-risk licensing, governance, money movement and provider changes require stricter review than a normal content recommendation.

## Troubleshooting

**A platform says “CHLOM enabled,” but I cannot see a blockchain transaction.** CHLOM can govern policies, roles, evidence and entitlements at different implementation layers. Do not assume every integration is on-chain.

**Someone says I need to buy CHM/CLOM to use a CrownThrive service.** Verify the current product and official terms. This public guide does not establish a universal token requirement.

**A historical CHLOM paper describes a feature that is missing.** Architecture, research and target-state papers are not automatically current production evidence.

**Can CHLOM automatically override a human legal or regulatory authority?** No. It must operate within the actual authority and law applicable to the decision.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is CHLOM a cryptocurrency?

No. CHLOM is the broader compliance, licensing, rights and governance framework. Token concepts are only one possible subsystem.

### Is CHLOM a blockchain?

It includes ledger/blockchain architecture concepts and implementations, but the institutional framework is broader than one chain.

### Does CHLOM replace contracts or regulators?

No. It can encode, verify and govern relationships according to current authority; it does not manufacture legal authority.

### Can I see the confidential technical design?

Detailed enabling architecture is restricted and shared through controlled technical/legal channels when appropriate.
