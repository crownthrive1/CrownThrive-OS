# BrandCards — Your Identity, Made Smarter

BrandCards is built around one job: **make it easier for another person to understand who you are, save your information and take the next action**. It turns a traditional business card into a mobile-ready smart profile that can hold contact information, services, testimonials, links, forms, products, appointments and brand elements.

## What this helps you do

- Replace printed contact information that becomes outdated.
- Create a branded, shareable professional profile.
- Let visitors save your contact details directly to their phone.
- Share through QR, NFC-compatible links, email, text and social profiles.
- Add services, products, appointments, testimonials and inquiry forms.
- Apply a custom domain, CSS or JavaScript where the plan supports it.
- Maintain several vCards for teams, roles or business lines.

## Availability and eligibility

BrandCards is available at [**https://brandcards.co**](https://brandcards.co). Plan limits determine how many vCards and how much storage an account can use. Some exact content blocks differ by plan, so use the live storefront as the final check before purchasing for a specific feature.

## BrandCards versus CrownThrive IO and ThriveKiosks

**BrandCards is identity-first.** It is optimized for people, roles, contact and professional presentation.

**CrownThrive IO is routing and digital-utility-first.** It handles bio pages, links, QR, static sites, file/event/vCard links, analytics, pixels and developer utilities.

**ThriveKiosks is service/product interaction-first.** It is designed around digital kiosks, service menus, products, categories, booking and phygital interactions.

The products can connect, but one does not need to impersonate the others.

## Thrive Flywheel mapping

BrandCards often sits at the **identity and trust** stage of a journey. A BrandCard can route to ThriveSeat for booking, ThriveTickets for an event, ThriveKiosks for a product/service menu, CrownThrive IO for campaign links, CrownRewards for loyalty or CrownLytics for broader measurement.

## Policies and rights

BrandCards maintains its own legal surface, including Terms & Conditions, Privacy Policy, Refund & Cancellation Policy, Shipping & Delivery Policy and an Imprint page. This separation matters because BrandCards should not inherit another product's commerce or privacy terms merely because both sit inside CrownThrive.

Only publish names, logos, photographs, testimonials and business claims you are authorized to use. A smart profile is public-facing identity infrastructure; accuracy matters.

## Troubleshooting

**A QR code does not scan reliably.** Increase size and contrast, avoid glare and test the printed version rather than only the screen preview.

**A custom domain is not resolving.** Verify the DNS values shown by BrandCards and allow propagation time before opening a support request.

**An inquiry form is not arriving.** Check the destination email, spam filtering and any custom mail configuration.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is BrandCards just another link-in-bio tool?

No. It can contain links, but its core use is structured identity, contact and professional presentation.

### Can I use BrandCards with NFC?

BrandCards supports NFC-ready links. Physical NFC products or deployment services may be offered separately; always verify the current hardware policy before purchase.

### Can I create cards for a team?

Yes. Higher plans provide enough vCard capacity for team and role deployments.

### Does BrandCards replace ThriveSeat?

No. BrandCards can link to booking, while ThriveSeat manages scheduling and service operations.
