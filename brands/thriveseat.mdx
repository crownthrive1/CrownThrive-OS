# ThriveSeat — One Platform. Total Control. Built to Thrive.

ThriveSeat combines booking, payments, staff management, client records, memberships and analytics in one system for appointment-driven businesses. It is designed to let a solo professional start with one chair or calendar and grow into a team or multi-location operation without replacing the core booking infrastructure every time the business expands.

## What this helps you do

- Accept online appointments 24/7.
- Collect deposits and payments.
- Send reminders and confirmations.
- Manage clients, notes and service history.
- Coordinate staff availability and shifts.
- Offer memberships and recurring service plans.
- Track booking, revenue, service and retention metrics.
- Manage several branches on higher plans.

## Availability and eligibility

ThriveSeat is available at [**https://thriveseat.com**](https://thriveseat.com) and currently promotes a 30-day free trial. The platform serves beauty and grooming, wellness and fitness, creative services, education/coaching and other appointment-driven businesses.

## Core capabilities

### Appointment booking

Publish services, availability and booking rules. Clients can choose a service and time without waiting for manual back-and-forth.

### Payments and deposits

Collect deposits or payment according to your service policy. The business remains responsible for clearly communicating cancellation, refund and no-show terms.

### Client management

Maintain client profiles, notes, service history and review-capture workflows so repeat service does not depend on memory or scattered messages.

### Memberships and packages

Create tiered service subscriptions such as monthly retwist plans, wash plans, VIP packages or other recurring-value arrangements supported by your business model.

### Staff and branch management

Set staff availability, coordinate shifts, track time off and review performance across eligible locations.

### Analytics and reporting

Review bookings, service trends, earnings, client retention and staff metrics. Higher tiers include deeper financial and commission tools.

## Privacy, payment and accessibility

Appointment systems can contain sensitive contact, scheduling and service-history information. Give staff only the access they need, keep client notes professional and relevant, and do not use free-text notes as a place for unnecessary sensitive personal information. Payment handling must follow the configured processor and published business refund/cancellation policies.

## Troubleshooting

**Clients can see a time that should be unavailable.** Review staff availability, branch assignment, buffers, service duration and time-off settings.

**A client did not receive a reminder.** Check the contact information, reminder settings, delivery status and whether the appointment met the configured notification window.

**My team is double-booked.** Verify each service's staff mapping and check whether group/family booking or overlapping availability was enabled intentionally.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is ThriveSeat only for salons?

No. Its public positioning covers beauty, wellness, fitness, coaching, creative and other service businesses.

### Can I take deposits?

Yes. Deposit and payment collection are part of the current feature set.

### Can I create memberships?

Yes. Tiered subscription plans and membership management are supported, with deeper controls on higher tiers.

### Does ThriveSeat replace Locticians?

No. Locticians handles discovery/community; ThriveSeat handles scheduling and service operations.
