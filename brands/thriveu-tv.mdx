# ThriveU TV — Learn by Watching, Continue by Doing

ThriveU TV is the video learning extension of CrownThriveU. It is designed for workshops, course-aligned videos, replays, instructional series and educational programming that benefits from a streaming/video experience rather than a traditional lesson page.

## What this helps you do

- Watch educational programming and workshop replays.
- Discover video content connected to CrownThriveU topics.
- Follow credential-aligned learning when the underlying course explicitly includes the video.
- Separate watching a video from completing a course assessment or earning a credential.

## Availability and eligibility

ThriveU TV is preserved as a current CrownThrive education/media identity with a platform-specific addendum. Exact current catalog, provider, public URL, subscriptions and viewing entitlements require current verification. Use CrownThriveU or the current CrownThrive media navigation for live access paths.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the current program or video">
    Use the active course, event or ThriveU TV catalog rather than an archived media reference.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check whether it is part of a course">
    A video can be public, member-only, paid, replay-only or required learning depending on the current program.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Watch with accessibility options where available">
    Use captions, transcripts or other available support when provided.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Return to CrownThriveU for course completion">
    If the video belongs to a credentialed course, the assessment and completion rules remain in CrownThriveU.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Pricing and access

No universal current ThriveU TV standalone pricing table is present in the source set used for this guide. Access may be public, included with a CrownThriveU course, event-based, member-only or part of another offer. Use the live content page for price and entitlement.

## Content rights and moderation

Video availability does not create download, rebroadcast, clipping or commercial-use rights. Instructors and contributors retain rights according to their applicable agreements. Learner comments or submissions remain subject to moderation and privacy rules.

## Ecosystem routes

ThriveU TV can connect course content from CrownThriveU, live or registered events from ThriveTickets/ThriveGather, mentoring discussions from ThrivePeer and broader media distribution where the rights permit. It remains an education-focused media surface rather than a replacement for Melanated Voices or Locticians TV.

## Troubleshooting

**A video plays but my course still shows incomplete.** Watching and course completion are separate. Return to CrownThriveU and check the actual lesson/assessment requirements.

**A replay is unavailable.** Replays can have time-limited access or rights restrictions. Check the current event/course offer.

**Can I download the video?** Only when the current content page explicitly grants a download.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is ThriveU TV the same as CrownThriveU?

No. CrownThriveU is the learning system; ThriveU TV is a video-learning extension.

### Does watching a video earn a certificate?

Not automatically. Credential criteria are defined by the underlying CrownThriveU course or program.

### Can instructors submit videos?

Contributor/instructor submissions must follow the current onboarding and content-rights process.
