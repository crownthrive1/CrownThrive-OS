# Go-Flipbooks — Turn Approved Documents Into Controlled Reading Experiences

Go-Flipbooks is CrownThrive's digital library, interactive reader, preview and licensed-content delivery layer. It can present books, courses, catalogs, guides and visual resources without becoming the owner of the source IP simply because it renders the pages.

## What this helps you do

- Read approved books and documents in an interactive reader.
- Preview selected pages without exposing the full source file.
- Receive full-reader access when the current entitlement grants it.
- Understand whether download or print is permitted.
- Keep edition/version history intact when a publication is corrected or superseded.
- Deliver Virality Music or ministry/education publications through a controlled reader instead of a raw public PDF.

## Availability and eligibility

Go-Flipbooks is a current CrownThrive institutional platform with an Active-Expand management state in the August 2026 registry. Current exact direct host/vendor/API implementation remains verification-gated. Historical API material and FlipLink usage can inform lineage, but the public Help Center should use the current verified reader route for each publication.

## The reader lifecycle

A publication can be finished without being reader-ready. The governed lifecycle separates:

`master complete → checksum/manifest complete → reader upload pending → reader verified → price verified → entitlement verified → offer active`

That separation matters because a completed PDF, an uploaded reader and a paid customer entitlement are three different states.

## Commerce and entitlements

A current product can involve separate product, price, payment, terms/license, entitlement, reader grant, access event and revocation/expiration records. Technical ability to open a reader does not create commercial rights. A payment receipt does not grant reproduction, resale or adaptation beyond the current terms.

## Pricing

Go-Flipbooks does not use one universal customer price because pricing belongs to the publication/product/license being delivered. A free sample, a paid personal reader, a course reader and a commercial licensed document can all use the same reader infrastructure with different economics.

The current product offer should show:

- Publication/edition.
- Price and currency.
- Access term.
- Preview/full access.
- Download/print rights.
- Refund/support rules.
- Commercial rights, if any.

Do not infer price from page count or an old FlipLink account.

## Virality, education and ministry routes

Virality Music remains the source institution for its books/worlds/catalog IP. CrownThriveU can use readers for course materials. KJV Visualized/Sermon Toolkit can use approved readers for studies and ministry resources while preserving Scripture/commentary/license boundaries. MVP and CrownThrive IO should route to the authorized reader rather than copying protected PDFs into a generic content store.

## Accessibility and privacy

Reader experiences should preserve readable text, meaningful navigation and accessible alternatives where the source supports them. Do not expose private entitlement data, protected source files or customer/order information in public reader URLs.

## Troubleshooting

**I paid but the reader says I do not have access.** Payment and entitlement can fail independently. Use support with the order/payment and publication reference.

**I can read online but cannot download.** Download rights are a separate permission. The current reader terms control.

**A publication looks different from an older version.** Check the edition/version. A corrected reader can supersede an earlier edition without erasing its historical record.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does Go-Flipbooks own the books it displays?

No. Source ownership stays with the relevant rightsholder/platform.

### Is a reader link the same as a license?

No. Reader access is a technical entitlement; commercial-use rights require the applicable license.

### Can every reader be printed?

No. Print permission is publication-specific.

### Is there a public API?

The institutional registry has not yet verified the current vendor/API implementation.
