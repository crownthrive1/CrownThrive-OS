# ThrivePeer — Learn With People, Not Just Content

ThrivePeer is CrownThrive's mentorship and accountability platform for structured peer support, mentor matching, goals, sessions, resources and professional progression. It is intended to complement learning and community systems, not replace therapy, crisis care, professional licensure or guaranteed consulting outcomes.

## What this helps you do

- Join a mentoring or accountability program when one is available.
- Define goals and the type of support you need.
- Review a mentor's verified scope rather than relying only on a title.
- Schedule or participate in structured sessions/check-ins.
- Track progress, feedback and program completion.
- Move between CrownThriveU learning, ThriveAlumni leadership and approved expert/mentor pathways without confusing the roles.

## Availability and eligibility

ThrivePeer is a current CrownThrive institutional identity with meaningful public content observed in the August 2026 domain scan. The registry still requires current verification of exact engine/provider, active mentor pools, payment/stipend schedules, scheduling behavior and APIs. Specific programs can have their own eligibility rules.

## Roles

- Participant or mentee.
- Peer/accountability partner.
- Mentor.
- Subject-matter guide.
- Program or cohort manager.
- Organization/team administrator.
- Safety/moderation reviewer.

A mentor label does not automatically prove licensure or professional authority. Where a program requires a licensed professional, the credential must be separately verified.

## How matching works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Define the goal">
    State what you want help with, preferred format, availability and relevant boundaries.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the mentor's scope">
    Look at the verified experience and program role. Do not assume clinical, legal or financial authority from a self-description.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Accept the match">
    Matching should be an explicit relationship, not something silently created because two profiles looked similar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use sessions and check-ins">
    Work toward agreed goals, record appropriate progress and close or rematch when the relationship is not a fit.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## ThrivePeer versus nearby products

- **CrownThriveU:** structured courses and curriculum.
- **ThrivePeer:** mentorship, accountability and relational progression.
- **CrownConsult:** separately scoped expert/consultant engagements.
- **ThriveAlumni:** post-program community and leadership pathways.
- **ThriveGather:** rooms, cohorts and live-session environments.

## Pricing and value exchange

The current source set does not establish one universal ThrivePeer public pricing table. Programs can use volunteer mentoring, paid sessions, organization seats, stipends, service fees or CrownRewards incentives as separate arrangements. A reward or paid session does not guarantee employment, funding, business success or professional certification.

Use the live ThrivePeer program or current CrownThrive offer for any actual fee, session price, organization seat or compensation schedule.

## Privacy and safety

Mentoring can involve private goals, direct messages and sensitive personal disclosures. Keep access relationship-scoped. ThrivePeer is not a crisis service; urgent safety or medical concerns require the appropriate emergency or professional resource.

Minors, safeguarding cases and high-risk professional advice require additional controls and should not be auto-matched or handled solely by an AI recommendation.

## Thrive Flywheel mapping

A learner can develop skills through CrownThriveU, gain accountability through ThrivePeer, join community or events through ThriveGather/ThriveTickets, move into ThriveAlumni, and later serve as a mentor or other qualified role. CrownRewards can support approved incentives, while CrownLytics should use aggregate program evidence rather than private session content.

## Troubleshooting

**My mentor is giving advice outside the stated program scope.** Pause and use the program/support route. A mentor title does not create unlimited professional authority.

**I need to change mentors.** Use the program's rematch or support process. A match should be closable without fabricating misconduct.

**I missed a session.** Review the program's scheduling and cancellation rules; payment/refund terms can vary by program.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is ThrivePeer therapy?

No. Mentorship and peer support are not therapy or crisis care unless a separately identified licensed service is explicitly provided.

### Does CrownThrive guarantee mentor outcomes?

No. Mentoring can support progress, but outcomes depend on the participant, mentor, program and external conditions.

### Can CrownThriveU graduates become mentors automatically?

Course completion can support progression, but it does not automatically grant a mentor role or professional credential.
