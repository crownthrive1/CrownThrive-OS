# CrownConsults — Get Help From the Right Person for the Right Scope

CrownConsults is CrownThrive's vetted expert-help lane for paid, scoped engagements with qualified providers. The product is designed to connect a customer with an expert whose verified experience matches the task, while keeping service scope, qualifications, price, privacy, deliverables and dispute rules explicit.

## What this helps you do

- Find an expert for a clearly defined business or professional need.
- Review the expert's stated and verified qualifications.
- Understand the exact service scope before booking.
- See the current price, cancellation/refund rules and expected deliverable.
- Keep regulated professional services separate from general business guidance.

## Availability and eligibility

CrownConsults is a current strategic CrownThrive role. Historical Help Center material used **CrownConsult** for an embedded consultant widget; current institutional records use **CrownConsults** for the expert-service lane. The exact provider roster, booking engine, payments, API and live pricing require current verification.

## Expert verification and scope

Every active expert engagement should identify:

- Expert/provider identity.
- Relevant experience and qualifications.
- Current professional license where the service requires one.
- Service category and explicit scope.
- Price or fee schedule.
- Availability/booking source.
- What the expert will deliver.
- Cancellation and refund rules.
- Confidentiality/data classification.
- Conflict or prohibited-work boundaries.
- Support and dispute route.

A self-written profile or platform “verified” badge does not substitute for jurisdiction-specific licensure when regulated practice is involved.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Define the problem">
    Describe the result you need, the context and what you have already tried.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the expert and scope">
    Confirm the provider's actual qualifications and whether the engagement is advisory, technical, creative or regulated professional work.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review price and terms">
    Check the current fee, time/session unit, deliverables, cancellation and refund rules before paying.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete the engagement">

  </Step>
  <Step title="Use support if the scope or outcome is disputed">
    A disappointing result, scope dispute, missed session and regulated-practice complaint are different cases and should be handled accordingly.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Pricing

No verified universal CrownConsults public price table is present in the source set used for this guide. Expert services can be priced per session, per hour, per deliverable, fixed project or organization package. A current booking should show:

- Expert and service.
- Price and currency.
- Session duration or deliverable.
- Taxes/fees if applicable.
- Cancellation/refund terms.
- Whether follow-up is included.
- Any limits on document review, revisions or async support.

Do not infer a consultant's price from another expert or from a historical AppSumo/provider account.

## Regulated-service boundary

Medical, legal, financial, mental-health, engineering and other regulated services require the applicable current professional authority. CrownConsults can route a customer to a qualified expert, but the platform itself does not manufacture a license or guarantee a professional outcome.

## Thrive Flywheel mapping

CrownThriveU can build skills, ThrivePeer can provide mentoring/accountability, ThriveAlumni can support continuity and CrownConsults can provide a separately scoped paid expert engagement. These stages are intentionally different so a mentor is not automatically sold as a licensed consultant.

## Privacy and confidentiality

Share only the information needed for the engagement. Highly sensitive customer, financial, legal, medical or security material should use an approved secure route rather than ordinary chat or public comments. Expert access ends with the scope unless another engagement explicitly continues it.

## Troubleshooting

**An expert's profile does not show the license I need.** Do not assume it exists. Ask support or use another verified provider.

**I paid but the expert did not attend.** Use the current cancellation/refund and support path with the booking reference.

**The engagement produced advice outside the agreed scope.** Document the issue and open a support case. Scope and professional authority matter.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does CrownConsults guarantee results?

No. The platform can structure a qualified engagement, but outcomes are not guaranteed.

### Is CrownConsults the same as ThrivePeer?

No. ThrivePeer is mentorship/accountability; CrownConsults is a paid expert-service lane.

### Is every CrownConsults provider licensed?

Not every service requires a professional license. When regulated work does require one, the current credential should be verified for that jurisdiction.
