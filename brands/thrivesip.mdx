# ThriveSip — Brew, Blend and Build the Ritual

ThriveSip is CrownThrive's beverage and lifestyle brand lane for coffee, tea and functional-blend concepts. The brand is designed around everyday ritual, subscription-friendly products and community-oriented lifestyle storytelling.

## What this helps you do

- Understand the intended ThriveSip product lane.
- Know which product details must be verified before purchase.
- Separate beverage/lifestyle marketing from medical or disease claims.
- Understand how ThriveSip can connect to CrownThrive media, commerce, rewards and physical activations.

## Availability and eligibility

The current institutional registry classifies ThriveSip as an incubated brand whose active manufacturing, current catalog, provider, labeling and claims state still require verification. A recovered product concept or historical listing is not current inventory by itself.

## Product standards

A current ThriveSip product should identify:

- Exact coffee, tea or blend name.
- Ingredient list and allergens where applicable.
- Net weight or serving size.
- Supplier/manufacturer and current production authorization.
- Preparation instructions.
- Caffeine or functional-ingredient information where relevant.
- Current price, shipping and subscription terms.
- Label and packaging version.
- Claims profile and support route.

## Pricing

No verified current ThriveSip price table is present in the source set used for this guide. Coffee, tea, subscriptions, gift sets and specialty blends can have different economics. Use the current storefront at the time of purchase rather than an old mockup or historical price.

## Ecosystem routes

ThriveSip can support event activations, Kulture House-style cultural programming, Good Shit Only commerce, ThriveKiosks menus, TapStations physical touchpoints, My CrownRewards loyalty and AdLuxe campaigns after a product offer is current and verified.

## Claims, safety and rights

Do not treat terms such as “wellness,” “functional,” “energy” or “focus” as medical guarantees. Any health-related claim must match the actual product, ingredients and applicable substantiation. Food/beverage labeling, allergens and production requirements remain product-specific.

## Troubleshooting

**I found ThriveSip content but no checkout.** That can mean the brand identity is preserved while the current offer is not active. Do not assume inventory.

**Can I subscribe automatically?** Only when a current ThriveSip offer explicitly includes a recurring subscription and its cancellation terms.

**Can an event use ThriveSip branding?** Only with the applicable brand permission and a verified product/service arrangement.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is ThriveSip the same as Melanin Magic Cafe?

No. They are related beverage/lifestyle concepts but remain distinct brand identities.

### Is every ThriveSip blend a supplement?

No. Product classification depends on the actual product. Do not infer supplement status from brand language alone.

### Where will current pricing appear?

On the current authorized product offer when the specific product is live.
