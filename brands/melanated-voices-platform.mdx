# Melanated Voices Platform — One Discovery Layer, Many Source Systems

Melanated Voices Platform, shortened to **MVP**, is CrownThrive's future cross-media discovery and orchestration identity. Its intended role is to help people find and move among audio, video, books, radio, creator media and other culturally aligned content while keeping the underlying source system, rights and entitlements intact.

## What this helps you understand

- Why MVP should not duplicate Virality Music, Go-Flipbooks or Melanated TV.
- How one discovery experience can route to the correct authoritative source.
- Why content ownership stays with the source/rightsholder rather than transferring to MVP because it appears in a library.
- Why “MVP (Roku)” historical lineage remains separate and unresolved.

## Availability and eligibility

MVP is currently a **target / long-term orchestration build** in the media federation. It has a stable identity, but the full cross-media discovery, library, identity and entitlement layer is implementation-gated. Do not represent every envisioned MVP feature as currently deployed.

## Intended capabilities

Future/current MVP architecture can include:

- Cross-media search and discovery.
- Library views across approved content.
- Identity handoff to the source platform.
- Entitlement awareness without copying protected content into an uncontrolled system.
- Recommendations based on authorized metadata and user preferences.
- Playback/read/watch routing through the correct source endpoint.

## How it should work

<Steps>
  <Step title="Discover the content">
    MVP indexes or presents authorized metadata for a work, episode, book, channel or media object.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Preserve the source identity">
    The media object keeps its source platform, stable ID, rights profile, audience class and current availability.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check access">
    Public, purchased, member-only and restricted content require different entitlement states.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Route to the approved endpoint">
    MVP should hand off to the authorized playback, reader or platform experience rather than inventing a parallel copy.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Propagate corrections and takedowns">
    Source changes must flow to the discovery layer.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## MVP versus MVP (Roku)

The media registry assigns a stable identity to **Melanated Voices Platform (MVP)** as cross-media orchestration. A separate historical `MVP (Roku)` record remains unresolved and must not be silently merged into MVP or Melanated Voices TV until the Roku/video lineage is reconciled.

## Pricing and access

No authoritative current standalone MVP pricing table is present in the source set used for this guide. A future MVP experience may aggregate free, purchased, subscription or separately licensed content without making those source products share one price.

## Rights and entitlements

MVP discovery does not create rights. A user may be able to discover a work without being entitled to download, stream, reproduce or commercially use it. Entitlement checks belong to the authoritative source/commerce system.

## Thrive Flywheel mapping

MVP can strengthen media discovery and recirculation across Virality Music, Backroad FM, Melanated Voices, Melanated TV, Locticians TV, Go-Flipbooks and CrownThrive Studios. CrownThrive IO can provide governed links; CrownLytics can measure normalized discovery paths; CrownRewards can support approved loyalty without becoming media ownership.

## Privacy and recommendations

Cross-media recommendations should use approved user preferences and behavior with appropriate consent. Private support, health, legal, child or restricted data should not become recommendation inputs merely because systems are connected.

## Troubleshooting

**A title appears in MVP but will not play.** Discovery and entitlement are separate. Check the source platform and current access state.

**I purchased content elsewhere in CrownThrive. Should MVP automatically know?** That is the intended entitlement-awareness direction, but the current implementation must actually support the integration before it is assumed.

**Is MVP a Roku channel?** Not by default. The historical MVP (Roku) lineage remains separately unresolved.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does MVP replace Melanated TV?

No. Melanated TV remains a specialized video/OTT system.

### Does MVP own the content it indexes?

No. The source platform and rights records remain authoritative.

### Is the full MVP experience live now?

The federation currently treats MVP as a future/target orchestration build with implementation gates.
