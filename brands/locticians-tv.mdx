# Locticians TV — Watch the Craft, Culture and Business Move

Locticians TV is the video channel for beauty, wellness, natural-lifestyle education, tutorials, interviews, events and professional storytelling connected to the Locticians network. It gives professionals and community voices a media lane without turning the directory itself into a video platform.

## What this helps you do

- Watch tutorials, interviews and community programming.
- Discover professionals and topics connected to the Locticians network.
- Understand contributor and clip/rebroadcast rights.
- Route current professional education into CrownThriveU or ThrivePeer where appropriate.
- Keep video/channel distribution separate from the underlying creator's ownership and Locticians profile.

## Availability and eligibility

Locticians TV is a current channel identity with historical/active program lineage. The media federation records [**https://tv.locticians.com/**](https://tv.locticians.com/) as a public route in current ecosystem materials, while exact Viloud/provider account IDs and full technical deployment remain verification-gated.

## Pricing and access

No universal standalone Locticians TV subscription price is established in the current source set used for this guide. Programming can be public, sponsored, event-linked, course-linked or otherwise entitled. Use the live title or program page for access terms.

## Contributor rights

A contributor should know whether the content is live, recorded, clipped, syndicated, sponsored or reused in ads. Video appearance does not automatically transfer all likeness, voice, tutorial, product or course rights.

## Thrive Flywheel mapping

Locticians TV can create discovery for Locticians profiles, route bookings through ThriveSeat, support learning in CrownThriveU and ThrivePeer, amplify events through ThriveTickets, use AdLuxe for labeled sponsorship and connect loyalty through My CrownRewards where a current program applies.

## Privacy and safety

Before publishing demonstrations involving clients, minors or private services, obtain the appropriate consent and avoid exposing sensitive service/health information. Educational content should clearly distinguish general instruction from individualized professional/medical advice.

## Troubleshooting

**A video references a service or price that changed.** Use the current Locticians profile or booking page for live services/pricing.

**Can I use a tutorial in my own course?** Only when the current content/license terms allow reuse.

**An old Locticians TV link does not load.** Provider and channel routes can change; start from the current Locticians network or Help Center.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Locticians TV the same as Locticians?

No. Locticians is the Natural Lifestyle Network/Directory; Locticians TV is the related video channel.

### Can professionals be featured automatically by joining Locticians?

No. Media features and directory membership are separate editorial/program states.

### Does Viloud own Locticians TV?

No. Viloud is provider lineage, not the CrownThrive brand or content owner.
