# Melanin Magic Wholesale — Grow With the Brand, Without Blurring the Boundaries

Melanin Magic Wholesale is the business-to-business distribution lane for eligible retailers and professionals that want to purchase approved Melanin Magic products for resale or professional business use under the program's current terms.

## What this helps you do

- Understand how wholesale differs from ordinary retail purchasing.
- Prepare the business and resale information required for an application.
- Purchase approved wholesale SKUs and quantities after approval.
- Follow current brand, resale, marketing and product-claim standards.
- Keep wholesale access separate from trademark ownership, manufacturing rights, formulas and MM Suites rights.

## Availability and eligibility

The public wholesale information page is [**https://shopmelaninmagic.com/about-wholesale-program**](https://shopmelaninmagic.com/about-wholesale-program). Wholesale access is business-facing and can require verification, resale/tax documentation, approved products, quantity or minimum rules, channel restrictions and current terms.

The institutional registry identifies **Candid Wholesale** as a founder-attested current provider relationship as of August 21, 2026, but also records an unresolved Melanin Magic catalog crosswalk because an observed Candid catalog surface was branded for Locticians. That means **provider relationship does not automatically prove which Melanin Magic SKUs, prices or terms are currently available through that provider**.

## Wholesale versus related lanes

### Consumer retail

Retail customers purchase products for personal use through the Melanin Magic storefront. Retail bulk sizes do not automatically grant resale permission.

### Professional / Stylepreneur

Professional participation can include product use, education, approved referrals and service-business handoffs. It does not automatically create wholesale status.

### Wholesale / retailer

Wholesale is a verified business relationship involving approved SKUs, pricing schedules, quantities, resale permissions, fulfillment and program standards.

### MM Suites

MM Suites is a separate phygital program and operating model. Wholesale approval does not create a Suite Pro, territory, franchise, location or MM Suites operating right.

## Pricing and ordering

No single fixed wholesale price table is authoritative in the current public source set used for this guide. Wholesale economics depend on the approved catalog, current price schedule, quantities, provider/fulfillment arrangement, taxes and business terms.

<Warning>
  Do not estimate wholesale discounts by subtracting a percentage from the consumer storefront. Retail price, wholesale price, resale terms and professional-use pricing are separate records.
</Warning>

A current wholesale account should be able to identify:

- Approved product/SKU and variant.
- Current wholesale price.
- Required quantity or minimum where applicable.
- Tax/resale-document treatment.
- Fulfillment/shipping terms.
- Return/refund rules.
- Current resale permissions and restricted channels, if any.
- Marketing and product-claim requirements.

## Provider and fulfillment boundary

The registry separates the **Melanin Magic brand and product identity** from replaceable providers. Management evidence identifies Candid Wholesale as a current provider relationship, Genesis Private Label as a core manufacturer lane, Selfnamed as a selective skincare/body-care lane and additional in-house/POD possibilities. None of those provider names transfers product ownership, wholesale authority or customer rights to the provider.

## Product and claims standards

Every active wholesale product should resolve to a current SKU, label, ingredient declaration, provider/manufacturer, quantity, price, intended use, support path and claims profile. Historical brochures or recovered Help Center titles are not sufficient to prove current inventory or regulatory status.

Wholesale partners must not convert marketing language into disease-treatment, medical, therapeutic or guaranteed-result claims without appropriate substantiation and authorization.

## Privacy, payments and rights

Business verification can involve tax, payment and contact information. Use only the approved provider or CrownThrive intake path and do not send sensitive business documents through public comments or ordinary social messages. Wholesale access does not transfer formulas, trademarks, product-development rights or manufacturing authority.

## Troubleshooting

**I can see retail products but not a wholesale SKU.** Consumer availability does not guarantee wholesale availability. Use the current wholesale catalog tied to your approved account.

**A wholesale provider shows Locticians-branded items.** Do not assume those items are the Melanin Magic wholesale catalog. The current institutional record explicitly keeps that crosswalk unresolved until verified.

**I need a large quantity for one event.** Large retail quantity and wholesale resale are different use cases. Contact the wholesale program to determine the appropriate lane.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does wholesale access let me manufacture Melanin Magic products?

No. Wholesale is a distribution/resale relationship, not manufacturing or formula ownership.

### Does a gallon-size retail product count as wholesale?

Not automatically. Wholesale is defined by the business program, price schedule and resale terms rather than container size alone.

### Does wholesale approval make me a Suite Pro?

No. MM Suites and Suite Pro participation are separate programs.

### Where can I see the current wholesale terms?

Use the live wholesale program and approved provider/account materials. Do not rely on an old price sheet.
