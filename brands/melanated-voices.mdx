# Melanated Voices — Stories That Sound Like the People Living Them

Melanated Voices is CrownThrive's audio, podcast, spoken-media and community-voice platform. Its role is to amplify culturally grounded storytelling, interviews, commentary, podcasts, voice notes and creator-led audio without collapsing those works into the video/channel systems that may later distribute or promote them.

## What this helps you do

- Discover approved spoken-media and audio programming.
- Submit or contribute audio where current contributor intake is open.
- Understand how source ownership differs from distribution.
- Follow corrections, moderation and takedown rules.
- Route approved audio into broader CrownThrive media and discovery experiences.

## Availability and eligibility

Melanated Voices is a current CrownThrive media identity with historical operational evidence. Current exact host/vendor, account, API, active catalog and public route state remain verification items. Use current CrownThrive/Melanated navigation rather than an archived player or provider URL.

## Melanated Voices versus nearby media identities

- **Melanated Voices:** audio, podcasts and spoken-media source lane.
- **Melanated TV:** OTT/video programming and video distribution.
- **Melanated Voices TV:** video/Roku manifestation tied to the Voices corridor; rebuild/verification remains open.
- **MVP / Melanated Voices Platform:** future cross-media orchestration/discovery layer, not the source owner of everything it indexes.

## How it works

  <Step title="Create or submit the source work">

  </Step>

    A correction at the source should flow to projections rather than leaving stale copies in downstream channels.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Pricing and creator economics

No universal current Melanated Voices subscription, contributor fee or creator revenue-share table is present in the source set used for this guide. Current media offers can be free, sponsored, licensed, commissioned or part of another CrownThrive program. Use the current content/campaign agreement for compensation and rights.

## Rights and contributor releases

A creator submission should identify source ownership, contributors, music/clip permissions, guest releases, territory, monetization rights, duration and any restrictions. Uploading a podcast or voice recording does not automatically grant CrownThrive unlimited adaptation or AI voice rights.

## Thrive Flywheel mapping

Melanated Voices can create discovery and trust through storytelling, route viewers/listeners into Locticians, Virality Music, CrownThriveU, events or campaigns, and distribute approved video derivatives through Melanated TV/Voices TV. AdLuxe can manage labeled sponsorship inventory; CrownLytics can normalize performance evidence.

## Privacy and safety

Stories can include real people, trauma, community conflict or sensitive lived experience. Do not publish private records, non-consensual recordings or identifying details simply because the story is compelling. Corrections and takedowns should preserve evidence without repeating harmful material unnecessarily.

## Troubleshooting

**I found a historical episode but cannot play it.** The source may be archived, migrated or awaiting current provider verification. Use support with the title/source context.

**Can I reuse a Melanated Voices clip in my own project?** Only with the rights provided by the current content/license terms.

**Does appearing on Melanated Voices mean I am endorsing all CrownThrive products?** No. A media contribution does not create blanket endorsement or partnership rights.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Melanated Voices the same as MVP?

No. Melanated Voices is the spoken/audio media identity; MVP is the future cross-media orchestration/discovery identity.

### Is Melanated Voices TV the same platform?

It is a related video/channel manifestation, but the systems retain distinct roles and rights lineage.

### Can submitted audio be used to train AI automatically?

No such blanket right is established by this guide. AI/data/model/voice uses require explicit rights and current terms.
