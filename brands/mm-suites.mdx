# MM Suites — Where Digital Infrastructure Meets Place

**MM Suites** is CrownThrive's phygital operating model for connecting independent professionals, products, education, bookings, community, locations and long-term business opportunity. Historical materials also use **Melanin Magic Suites**; current documentation treats the MM Suites framework and platform identity as one governed family rather than two unrelated active systems.

## What this helps you understand

- What a Suite Pro role is intended to represent.
- How Locticians, ThriveSeat and Melanin Magic support the professional journey.
- Why joining a digital product does not automatically create an MM Suites license, territory or ownership right.
- How future physical locations, suites, operators and territories are expected to be governed.
- Which historical franchise, token, equity or lifetime-economic concepts are not current by default.

## Availability and eligibility

MM Suites is a **current adopted CrownThrive framework and platform identity with implementation gates**. The 2025 prospectus family remains foundational IP and design history, but historical claims about smart contracts, franchise rights, equity, tokens, lifetime overrides, filings or production operations require current evidence and legal activation before they can be offered externally.

<Warning>
  A ThriveSeat subscription, Locticians profile, Melanin Magic wholesale account or other CrownThrive membership **does not automatically create Suite Pro, MM Suites, franchise, license, territory or ownership status**.
</Warning>

## The current operating spine

The present architecture uses distinct systems for distinct responsibilities:

- **Locticians:** professional discovery, profile, category, location and trust signals.
- **ThriveSeat:** service catalog, availability, booking, branch, staff, customer and payment operations.
- **Melanin Magic:** consumer, professional and wholesale product lanes.
- **CrownThriveU:** education and credential pathways.
- **Crown Affiliates / Ambassadors / My CrownRewards:** separately governed distribution, referral and loyalty programs.
- **OpsOasis:** current operations/execution successor where older documents referenced Kamora360.
- **CrownLytics, CrownPulse and ThrivePush:** measurement, engagement and permissioned communication.
- **CrownThrive Impact Institute / ThriveFund records:** impact and reinvestment programs where explicitly adopted.
- **CHLOM:** future and current governance/rights controls according to the capability actually implemented and authorized.

## Who MM Suites is designed to serve

### Suite Pros

Independent or authorized service professionals operating under defined access, training, booking, product, brand-use and community rules.

### Location operators

Operators responsible for local facility standards, professional coordination, inventory, customer experience and reporting under a current agreement.

### Regional/operator candidates

Potential development or support roles that receive authority only after the relevant legal and economic status is granted.

### Customers and members

People using services, memberships, products, rewards and support at participating locations or digital experiences.

### Vendors and contractors

Scoped providers supporting buildout, maintenance, products, technology or other services without inheriting broader platform authority.

## Economic structure

MM Suites is not governed by one universal “split.” Current economic relationships must be defined in separate schedules, which can include:

- Booking/service fees.
- Subscription or membership fees.
- Rent, license or space charges.
- Wholesale/product margin.
- Affiliate or ambassador commissions.
- Referral rewards.
- Instructor or mentor compensation.
- Operator/location shares.
- Brand or license fees.
- Technology/service fees.
- Impact/reinvestment allocations.
- Reserves, holdbacks or clawbacks where applicable.

<Warning>
  Historical examples involving “lifetime,” permanent overrides, points-to-dollar values, franchise-referral economics, equity or inheritance are not current merely because they appear in an older prospectus. A current adopted schedule and, where required, signed legal record must create the right.
</Warning>

## Pricing and program costs

There is **no single current public MM Suites price table** in the source set used for this guide. Costs can differ by role and can include software subscriptions, products, training, location/space economics, operator agreements or other separate schedules.

Do not use historical prospectus projections as a current quote. A current offer must identify the actual role, location, services, fees, term, support, deposit/payment terms and rights granted.

## Digital-first and location activation

The current legal/operational progression is intentionally staged:

`foundational IP → requirements reconciled → digital operating pilot → location pilot ready → legal package draft → counsel review → jurisdiction ready → offer authorized → active relationship`

The point of that sequence is to prevent a historical concept document from becoming a live franchise or territory offer without the required legal and operational work.

## Thrive Flywheel mapping

MM Suites is a place-based expression of the CrownThrive ecosystem. Locticians can create discovery, ThriveSeat handles appointments, Melanin Magic supplies approved products, CrownThriveU supports education, loyalty/referral programs can support retention and distribution, and CrownLytics/CrownPulse/ThrivePush can support learning and communication. Physical and digital participation should reinforce one another without collapsing their authority boundaries.

## Impact and sustainability

Impact programs are coordinated through the **CrownThrive Impact Institute at [https://crownthrive.org](https://crownthrive.org)**. CII is not automatically a nonprofit, and ThriveFund is not automatically an investment fund or charity. Any public impact or reinvestment claim should identify the current program and evidence rather than borrowing numbers from historical MM Suites projections.

## Privacy, safety and accessibility

Physical locations introduce responsibilities that software-only platforms do not: accessibility, facility safety, local licensing, insurance, sanitation, equipment, staff/provider requirements and emergency procedures. These obligations depend on location and service category and must be addressed before a location is represented as ready.

## Troubleshooting

**I joined Locticians. Am I automatically a Suite Pro?** No. Locticians membership and MM Suites status are separate.

**I use ThriveSeat. Does that make my business an MM Suites location?** No. ThriveSeat is the booking/business-operations system, not a license or location-authority grant.

**An old document lists a franchise fee or lifetime override. Is it current?** Not unless a current authorized offer and applicable legal/economic schedule confirm it.

**Which system replaced Kamora360 in the current architecture?** OpsOasis is the current operations/execution successor for the relevant role. Historical documents remain preserved for lineage.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is MM Suites the same as Melanin Magic Suites?

They are preserved as the same current framework/platform family with historical naming lineage, not two separate active systems.

### Can a professional participate digitally before a physical location exists?

The current architecture explicitly supports digital and controlled pilot pathways, subject to the requirements of the specific program role.

### Does MM Suites sell equity or token rights?

This public guide does not establish any such current offer. Historical equity/token concepts require separate current legal and operational authorization before external use.

### Where should a professional start?

Start with the current CrownThrive product relevant to your immediate need, such as Locticians for discovery, ThriveSeat for booking or CrownThriveU for education. Use a formal MM Suites program route only when it is published as available.
