# NFTCliques — Preserved NFT Community Lineage

NFTCliques is a CrownThrive brand historically focused on NFT community discovery, wallet-based engagement, drops and gated experiences. The shared TrendyGroup implementation is sunset. The brand remains preserved so its history and IP are not lost, but it is not a current-production NFT marketplace or community platform under the present institutional record.

## What this helps you understand

- Why NFTCliques appears in older ecosystem and legal documentation.
- Why a visible domain or historical listing does not prove current minting, trading or wallet functionality.
- How CrownThrive preserves the brand without silently reactivating old Web3 behavior.

## Availability and eligibility

Current disposition: **sunset brand reserve**. No current marketplace, minting, wallet-login, token-gated membership, trading or payment state is established by the source set used for this guide.

Never provide a seed phrase, private key or remote wallet-control access to someone claiming to “restore” an old NFTCliques account. Any future official wallet integration would be published through current CrownThrive channels.
