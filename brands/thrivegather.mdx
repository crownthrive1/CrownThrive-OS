# ThriveGather — Make the Room Matter

ThriveGather is CrownThrive's virtual gathering environment for workshops, cohorts, community rooms, co-working, sponsor experiences and live programming. Historical Help Center sources identify GoBrunch provider lineage, but CrownThrive treats the provider as replaceable infrastructure rather than the identity of ThriveGather itself.

## What this helps you do

- Join approved virtual events or member rooms.
- Run cohorts, workshops or community sessions.
- Use access tiers for public, member, invite-only, sponsor or private experiences.
- Coordinate hosts and moderators.
- Manage recordings and replay access according to event-specific rights.
- Run sponsor experiences without hiding that content is sponsored.

## Availability and eligibility

ThriveGather is a current CrownThrive platform identity with a recovered Help Center article family. The exact current GoBrunch or successor account, workspaces, plan, room URLs, membership tiers, prices, recordings and API/export state still require current verification. Use the current event/program link provided by the organizer rather than an old room URL.

## Roles and access

Potential roles include public guest, community member, Pro member, VIP/Legacy member, sponsor, registrant, host, facilitator and moderator. These are separate roles. A room link or secret key is not sufficient authorization if the event also requires a ticket, membership or explicit invite.

## Sponsorship and monetization

ThriveGather can support room branding, named sessions, sponsor messages, approved resources and aggregate sponsor reporting. Sponsorship should remain clearly labeled and governed through the relevant AdLuxe/rights controls.

Potential value models include memberships, paid registrations, sponsor packages, premium rooms, organization access, replay products and hosted programs. Historical article titles about monetization do **not** establish current pricing.

## Pricing and access

No authoritative current ThriveGather plan/room pricing table is present in the source set used for this guide. A current event or program should show its own access model: free registration, paid ticket, membership, organization seat, sponsor access or invitation.

Do not pay based on an old room URL or historical tier description. Use the current CrownThrive event, ThriveTickets or program offer.

## Recordings and privacy

Recording, transcription, screenshots, clips, chat export and replay use require event-level rules. Attendance does not automatically transfer a participant's voice, likeness, presentation or private statements to CrownThrive. Hosts should disclose recording before or at entry where required and secure appropriate rights for slides, music and other media.

## Ecosystem routes

- **ThriveTickets:** registration and paid admission.
- **CrownThriveU:** classes, workshops and cohorts.
- **ThrivePeer:** mentor/accountability sessions.
- **AdLuxe / CrownFluence:** approved sponsor and creator campaigns.
- **ThrivePush:** permissioned reminders and follow-up.
- **My CrownRewards / Affiliates:** approved incentives and referrals.
- **CrownLytics:** aggregate attendance/engagement evidence.
- **CrownThrive IO:** governed links, embeds and QR access.

## Accessibility and safety

Provide accessible event information, time zones, captioning/transcription where available, accommodation routes and a clear way to report conduct problems. Private rooms are not a substitute for safeguarding rules.

## Troubleshooting

**The room link says I do not have access.** Check the event time, registration, membership tier, capacity and whether you are using the current link.

**Audio or video does not work.** Check browser permissions, device selection and network quality before changing event access settings.

**I missed the session. Is there always a replay?** No. Recording and replay access depend on the event's rights and offer.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is ThriveGather the same as ThriveTickets?

No. ThriveTickets handles event/ticket operations; ThriveGather is the virtual gathering environment.

### Can sponsors automatically access participant data?

No. Sponsorship does not create unlimited access to private member or attendee data.

### Is GoBrunch the CrownThrive product name?

No. GoBrunch is historical provider lineage; ThriveGather is the CrownThrive platform identity.
