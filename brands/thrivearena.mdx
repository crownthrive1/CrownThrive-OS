# ThriveArena — Prove What You Know, Build Evidence of Progress

ThriveArena is CrownThrive's competitive-learning and challenge layer. It is designed for knowledge challenges, skill demonstrations, branded competitions, leaderboards and achievement evidence that can support learning and progression without pretending a score is automatically a professional credential.

## What this helps you do

- Join a current challenge when you meet the eligibility rules.
- Submit an attempt, answer set, project or other approved evidence.
- Understand the published scoring rules before competing.
- See leaderboard or completion status where enabled.
- Receive a reward or achievement only when the current challenge schedule grants it.
- Use strong challenge performance as evidence for a later program decision without treating it as automatic authority.

## Availability and eligibility

ThriveArena is a current CrownThrive strategic role. Current exact domain, engine, provider, live challenge catalog, scoring implementation, API and reward state remain verification items. A historical challenge, score or leaderboard screenshot is not a current active competition.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open a current challenge">
    Review the challenge owner, dates, eligibility, submission format and reward before entering.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the scoring rules">
    Know whether scoring is deterministic, human reviewed, rubric based or mixed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit within the active window">
    Use the allowed format and preserve source/citation evidence when the challenge requires it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the score and any appeal path">
    Leaderboard position and final score should use the challenge's effective version and rules.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use the result accurately">
    A challenge can demonstrate learning or skill, but a later credential, job, mentor role or CHLOM permission requires its own decision.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Pricing, entry fees and rewards

No universal current ThriveArena price table is established in the source set used for this guide. Challenges can be free, member-only, sponsored, ticketed or organization-based. The current challenge must identify any entry fee, eligibility, refund rule and reward schedule.

Potential rewards can include recognition, CrownRewards incentives, program qualification evidence or other published benefits. A leaderboard or points balance does not create equity, employment, ownership or permanent compensation.

## Scoring integrity

Each challenge should identify its scoring rule, version, tie-breakers, review mode, eligibility and appeal process. AI-generated or high-consequence assessments need appropriate source and human-review controls rather than treating model output as authoritative scoring by default.

## Thrive Flywheel mapping

CrownThriveU supports learning; ThriveArena can provide challenge evidence; ThrivePeer can provide mentoring; Crown Affiliates/Ambassadors or instructor/expert programs can use verified evidence as one eligibility input; ThriveAlumni can support longer-term progression. None of those transitions is automatic.

## Privacy and conduct

Do not submit another person's work, private records, unauthorized media or confidential employer/client materials unless the challenge explicitly permits and protects them. Anti-cheating rules, moderation and appeals should be clear for competitive challenges.

## Troubleshooting

**My score changed after review.** Check whether the challenge allows human review, corrections, disqualification or rule-based rescoring.

**I won a challenge but did not receive a professional credential.** A challenge result and a credential are separate records unless the current challenge explicitly grants one.

**A challenge page is visible after the end date.** Archive visibility does not mean submissions or rewards remain open.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is ThriveArena a credentialing authority?

No. It can provide achievement or qualifying evidence, but professional licenses and formal credentials require separate authority.

### Can CrownRewards be tied to challenges?

Yes, when the current challenge has an effective reward schedule.

### Does a leaderboard guarantee job or program selection?

No. Later selection decisions use their own criteria and approvals.
