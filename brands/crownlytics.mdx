# CrownLytics — Data-Driven Decisions, Privacy Assured

CrownLytics is an all-in-one web analytics platform for understanding how visitors move through your website. It combines lightweight traffic analytics with anonymized visitor history, realtime activity, heatmaps, privacy-filtered session replay, outbound-click tracking, goals, exports, team access, custom domains and a developer API.

## What this helps you do

- Understand which pages and sources attract useful traffic.
- See where people click and where layouts create friction.
- Replay representative sessions while masking private details.
- Track conversions through custom goals and events.
- Measure external-link activity and partner handoffs.
- Share analytics with teams and export results.
- Build a measurement loop with CrownPulse, ThrivePush, AdLuxe and other CrownThrive tools.

## Availability and eligibility

CrownLytics is available at [**https://crownlytics.com**](https://crownlytics.com) with Free, Starter, Professional, Agency and custom Enterprise plans. Setup is designed around a lightweight tracking script. Site owners remain responsible for their own privacy disclosures and consent requirements.

## Key features

### Anonymous visitor history

Review return visits and activity without turning the product into an identity-surveillance system. CrownLytics emphasizes anonymized history rather than unnecessary personal data.

### Realtime analytics

See active countries, devices and pages as activity happens. This is useful during launches, events and paid campaigns when you need immediate operational signal.

### Heatmaps

Collect privacy-friendly click data and compare full-page heatmaps across desktop, tablet and mobile layouts.

### Session recording and replay

Watch a visual reconstruction of user journeys with private details automatically hidden. Use replays for usability debugging, not invasive profiling.

### Outbound-click tracking

Measure which links and domains send visitors away from your site and identify the most common outbound destinations.

### Goals and custom parameters

Create conversion goals and pass controlled custom parameters when your analysis needs context beyond the page URL.

### Teams, exports and reports

Invite teammates, receive scheduled email reports and export supported data in CSV, JSON or PDF formats depending on plan.

### API and custom domains

Developers can work with websites, statistics and custom-domain capabilities through the API. Custom domains can also provide a branded tracking-script URL.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add your website">
    Create a tracked website and copy the CrownLytics tracking code.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Install the script">
    Add the lightweight script to the pages you want measured, directly or through your site-management tooling.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Define your goals">
    Start with a small set of meaningful conversions such as a booking click, form completion, checkout handoff or newsletter signup.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Collect enough signal">

  </Step>
  <Step title="Improve and re-measure">
    Fix a specific friction point, then compare the next period against the prior behavior.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Privacy, security and accessibility

CrownLytics states alignment with GDPR, CCPA and PECR. Private details are automatically hidden in replays, and the product emphasizes data ownership and deletion controls. Use goals and custom parameters carefully: do not place sensitive personal or regulated data in tracking parameters merely because the platform technically accepts custom values.

## Troubleshooting

**No data is appearing.** Check that the script is installed on the correct pages and not blocked by your own content-security policy or tag-manager conditions.

**Heatmaps look sparse.** Wait for enough traffic and make sure you are comparing the correct device version.

**A replay appears incomplete.** Some interactions, external embeds or browser restrictions can limit what is reconstructable. Use replay alongside events and page analytics rather than treating video alone as complete evidence.

**Counts differ from another analytics platform.** Compare definitions, bot handling, privacy blocking, event definitions and time zones before concluding one system is wrong.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does CrownLytics track personal identities by default?

The platform is designed around anonymized visitor behavior. Avoid adding personal identifiers through custom parameters unless you have a lawful, documented reason and appropriate consent.

### Are session replays privacy filtered?

Yes. The public feature description states that private details are automatically hidden.

### Can I export my data?

Paid plan capabilities include supported export formats such as CSV, JSON and PDF, with exact availability depending on plan.

### Is there an API?

Yes. CrownLytics publishes a developer-ready API for websites, statistics and custom-domain workflows.

### Can CrownLytics work without other CrownThrive products?

Yes. Ecosystem connections are optional.
