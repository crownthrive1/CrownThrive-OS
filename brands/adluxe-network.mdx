# AdLuxe Network — Put Paid Reach Behind Offers That Are Ready

AdLuxe Network is CrownThrive's governed advertising, sponsorship and campaign-distribution network. It connects approved advertisers with CrownThrive-owned and approved partner inventory while keeping creative rights, audience policy, delivery evidence, invoices, publisher/location economics and reporting under explicit controls.

## What this helps you do

### Advertisers

- Submit a campaign, creative and destination for review.
- Select eligible web, media, creator, event or place-based inventory.
- Set budget, timing and approved targeting.
- Review delivery and normalized reporting.
- Resolve credits, missed delivery or support cases through the current campaign terms.

### Publishers and partners

- Register eligible properties and placements.
- Define inventory, exclusions and audience context.
- Review delivery and earnings evidence.
- Keep advertising distinct from editorial, canon and rights authority.

## Availability and eligibility

AdLuxe Network is a current CrownThrive platform. The primary public host is recorded as [**https://adluxe.crownthrive.com**](https://adluxe.crownthrive.com). The underlying ad-serving implementation uses Adserver.Online provider infrastructure for selected functions, but provider documentation or provider plan names do not automatically describe the exact CrownThrive customer-facing AdLuxe offer.

Current bounded read-only provider API access has been verified for selected reporting/dictionary endpoints. Provider writes remain closed until separately certified.

## The AdLuxe family

- **AdLuxe Network:** advertiser, publisher, inventory, campaign, delivery and reporting network.
- **AdLuxe Studio:** creative-production and campaign-asset service lane.
- **AdLuxe Places:** physical/place-based advertising inventory and proof-of-play lane.

These are connected but distinct products/functions.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start with a current offer and objective">
    Define what you are promoting, the destination, budget, audience and measurable outcome.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit creative and rights evidence">
    Creative, claims, music, images, likenesses, trademarks and landing-page terms must be appropriate for the campaign.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose eligible inventory">

  </Step>
  <Step title="Measure, reconcile and improve">
    Provider delivery evidence, CrownLytics measurement and commercial invoices/settlements remain separate records until reconciled.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Inventory families

AdLuxe can govern CrownThrive-owned web placements, approved partner websites, Virality Music/Backroad FM sponsorship, Melanated media inventory, Locticians placements, CrownFluence creator campaigns, event/ticket/kiosk/screen placements and approved branded-content packages.

A platform being part of CrownThrive does not automatically make every page or user surface available for advertising. Inventory must be explicitly activated.

## Pricing and commercial models

AdLuxe customer-facing campaign pricing can use CPM, CPC, CPA/CPL, flat sponsorship, revenue share, production fees, managed-service fees, creator fees, place-based rates or negotiated packages. **No one universal AdLuxe customer rate card is authoritative in the source set used for this guide.** Use the current AdLuxe quote/package for the actual campaign.

A commercial schedule should state:

- Included inventory and services.
- Budget or minimum.
- Pricing model and effective rate.
- Campaign dates and pacing.
- Included creative/production, if any.
- Measurement definition and attribution window.
- Overage or scope-change rules.
- Cancellation/pause rules.
- Invalid-traffic treatment.
- Credit/make-good policy.
- Reporting cadence.
- Taxes, payment terms and approval authority.

### Underlying provider public pricing — planning reference only

- Promo Starter — **\$29/month**, 1M included ad requests.

<Warning>

</Warning>

## Reporting and source authority

Provider-reported impressions, clicks and conversions are operational evidence. CrownLytics can normalize or compare that evidence, but source lineage must remain visible. A provider counter is not automatically a customer invoice, publisher payout or approved public performance claim.

## Privacy and targeting

Targeting must use approved, necessary attributes and lawful consent/notice. CrownThrive does not permit unrelated private support data, health records, spiritual journals, precise location without authority or other sensitive information to be silently converted into advertising audiences.

## Brand safety and creative rights

Every creative should resolve source rights, trademarks, music/footage/likeness permissions, material claims, disclosure requirements, destination URL and approved version. AI-assisted creative still requires rights and claim review.

## Thrive Flywheel mapping

AdLuxe strengthens the **acquisition and amplification** portion of the Thrive Flywheel. Use ThriveFoundry or CrownThrive IO for a focused destination, CrownPulse for onsite proof, CrownLytics for measurement, ThrivePush for permissioned return traffic, CrownFluence for creator campaigns and My CrownRewards for separately governed loyalty.

## Troubleshooting

**My campaign was paid for but is not live.** Payment is not the same as campaign activation. Creative, inventory, timing and approval gates may still be open.

**Provider stats and CrownLytics do not match exactly.** Compare measurement definitions, time zones, filtering, invalid traffic and attribution windows before assuming one system is wrong.

**A publisher expected a different payout.** Use the current commercial schedule and reconciled delivery evidence. Provider counters alone do not establish settlement.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is AdLuxe Network the same as Adserver.Online?

No. Adserver.Online is replaceable provider infrastructure. AdLuxe Network is CrownThrive's institutional advertising relationship and product layer.

### Is AdLuxe Studio the same as CrownThrive Studios?

No. AdLuxe Studio is the advertising-creative lane under AdLuxe. CrownThrive Studios is the broader media/creative production studio identity.

### Can every CrownThrive page carry ads?

No. Inventory must be explicitly approved and activated.

### Can I buy AdLuxe at the provider's $$29/$$89/$$199/$$599 rate?

No. Those are provider infrastructure prices, not CrownThrive customer campaign rates.
