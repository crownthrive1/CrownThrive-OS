# ThriveWick Candles — Build the Atmosphere

ThriveWick is CrownThrive's aromatics and ambiance brand lane for candles, diffusers, ritual sets and related home-fragrance concepts. It is designed for gifting, lifestyle merchandising, events and curated physical environments while maintaining product-safety and sourcing boundaries.

## What this helps you do

- Understand the ThriveWick brand and intended product classes.
- Know what a current candle or fragrance offer should disclose.
- Separate a fragrance concept or mockup from actual manufactured inventory.
- Understand how ThriveWick can participate in events, MM Suites or gift programs when current products are verified.

## Availability and eligibility

The institutional registry classifies ThriveWick as an incubated brand whose current products, providers and operational state still need verification. Purchase only through a current CrownThrive-authorized offer.

## Product standards

A current ThriveWick offer should identify fragrance, wax/material composition, vessel, net weight or burn quantity, wick/material information where relevant, safety instructions, provider/manufacturer, current price, fulfillment and return policy.

## Pricing

No verified current ThriveWick price schedule is present in the source set used for this guide. Price can vary by vessel, fragrance, size, packaging, provider and collection. The live offer controls.

## Ecosystem routes

ThriveWick can support lifestyle bundles, Melanin Magic experiences, MM Suites ambiance, event gifting, ThriveKiosks merchandise surfaces and Good Shit Only commerce after the specific product is verified. My CrownRewards and affiliate programs can participate only where a current campaign explicitly includes the product.

## Safety and claims

Home-fragrance products require practical fire, skin-contact and environmental-use warnings depending on product class. Do not make therapeutic, health or air-purification claims unless the exact product has appropriate substantiation.

## Troubleshooting

**I found a ThriveWick product image but no checkout.** Treat it as brand/source material, not proof of current inventory.

**Can I use a ThriveWick candle in a business location?** Only if the product and location safety policy allow it. Some facilities prohibit open flame.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does ThriveWick manufacture all products in-house?

The current registry does not establish one universal production model. In-house, contracted or private-label production requires current provider verification.

### Are all fragrance materials “natural”?

Do not assume that from the brand name. Use the actual ingredient/material disclosure for the current item.
