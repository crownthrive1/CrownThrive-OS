# FindCliques — The Community Discovery Brand Is Preserved, Not Pretended Live

FindCliques is CrownThrive's community-discovery brand for connecting people around groups, interests, communities and shared activity. Its historic TrendyGroup implementation was sunset, while the brand and findcliques.com domain were deliberately preserved for a future rebuild.

## What this helps you understand

- Why FindCliques still appears in CrownThrive's ecosystem map.
- Why an active/renewed domain does not prove a live application.
- Why historical profiles, groups or articles should not be represented as a current FindCliques 2.0 launch.
- Where to use current CrownThrive community products while FindCliques is reserved.

## Availability and eligibility

The current disposition is **reserve / future rebuild**. Domain registry notes referencing “Future FindCliques 2.0” are planning evidence, not release evidence. A future rebuild must receive a current product, privacy, moderation, data, provider/engine and legal activation record before it is marketed as live.

## Historical product role

FindCliques was conceived around interest-based community discovery and relationship infrastructure. That strategic role can inform a future rebuild, but old engine features do not automatically define the future product.

## Pricing

There is no current FindCliques pricing table because the platform is not currently established as a live production offer. Any historical subscription or TrendyGroup pricing should be treated as obsolete unless a future launch publishes a new plan.

## Data and privacy

If a future rebuild uses historical accounts or community data, CrownThrive must establish lawful migration, consent, retention and user-control rules. The existence of an archive does not automatically authorize reactivating old member profiles.

## Current alternatives

Depending on your goal:

- **Locticians** for natural-lifestyle professional/community discovery.
- **CrownThrive Commons** for current gated community/group roles.
- **ThriveGather** for live rooms, cohorts and workshops.
- **ThrivePeer** for mentorship and accountability.

## Troubleshooting

**I can reach findcliques.com. Does that mean the platform is live?** No. Domain reachability and application production state are different.

**I found an old FindCliques account or screenshot.** Treat it as historical evidence unless the current product explicitly imports or recognizes it.

**When is FindCliques 2.0 launching?** No authorized public launch date is established in the current source set.
