# Melanin Magic — Celebrate Melanin-Rich Excellence

**Beauty. Culture. Empowerment.** Melanin Magic is a lifestyle brand built around natural hair care, beauty, wellness and resources that celebrate melanin-rich communities. The product experience combines physical care products with educational and lifestyle resources so customers can build a routine rather than shop disconnected items.

## What this helps you do

- Build a wash, condition, repair, treatment and styling routine.
- Understand ingredient and safety information before purchasing.
- Shop individual products, bundles, wellness items and digital resources.
- Find professional/community guidance through Locticians.
- Access wholesale information when purchasing for a business.
- Participate in the brand's sustainability and impact commitments.

## Availability and eligibility

The retail storefront is [**https://shopmelaninmagic.com**](https://shopmelaninmagic.com). Product availability, inventory messages, sizes and pricing can change, so the live product page controls at checkout. Wholesale information is published separately at [**https://shopmelaninmagic.com/about-wholesale-program**](https://shopmelaninmagic.com/about-wholesale-program).

## Brand principles

### Pledge for safety

Melanin Magic publicly emphasizes formulas designed for hair, scalp and skin care with a focus on quality and gentleness. The brand states that products are made in the USA. Cosmetic products can still cause individual sensitivities, so patch testing remains important.

### Effective ingredients

The brand positions its small-batch product range around nature-inspired ingredients and practical goals such as cleansing, hydration, strength, shine, hold and manageability.

### Ethical sourcing and sustainability

Melanin Magic describes ethical sourcing as part of the brand and states that **1% of purchases** supports climate and environmental initiatives through Stripe Climate and EcoDrive. Broader CrownThrive impact governance is coordinated through the **CrownThrive Impact Institute at [https://crownthrive.org](https://crownthrive.org)**.

## Safety, claims and privacy

Melanin Magic's public disclaimer states that products are intended for cosmetic purposes, individual results vary, and customers should patch test new hair or skin products. If irritation occurs, discontinue use. General website information is not a substitute for medical advice.

Only rely on a product page for claims that are actually made for that product. “Natural” does not mean allergy-free or medically therapeutic.

## Troubleshooting

**A product says “Only a Few Left.”** Treat inventory messaging as a storefront snapshot. Availability can change between page load and checkout.

**I am unsure which routine stage I need.** Start with your current hair/scalp goal and review the ingredient/product guidance. For individualized hair-service advice, use Locticians.

**I need a business-size purchase.** Check the Wholesale program rather than assuming a consumer gallon option includes wholesale pricing or resale rights.

**A sale price differs from this article.** The live product page controls. Help Center pricing snapshots are explanatory, not a price guarantee.
