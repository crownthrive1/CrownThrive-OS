# CrownThriveU — Learn It, Prove It, Put It to Work

CrownThriveU is CrownThrive's education platform for courses, instructors, learning paths, assessments, credentials and organization-based training. Its job is to turn CrownThrive knowledge and partner expertise into structured learning without confusing a course certificate with professional licensure or guaranteed business outcomes.

## What this helps you do

### Learners

- Enroll in eligible courses or cohorts.
- Work through structured lessons and resources.
- Complete assessments or requirements where the course uses them.
- Receive completion records or credentials when the configured criteria are met.
- Continue into ThrivePeer mentorship or ThriveAlumni community where eligible.

### Instructors and contributors

- Propose and build courses under current instructor/contributor terms.
- Provide curriculum, media and assessments with clear rights.
- Participate in review and corrections before a course is represented as approved.
- Teach without automatically giving away every underlying piece of instructor IP unless the agreement says so.

## Availability and eligibility

CrownThriveU is available at [**https://crownthriveu.com**](https://crownthriveu.com). The institutional record establishes its education-platform role while keeping exact current LMS/engine, active catalog, enrollment, payment, credential and API state subject to current verification.

## Course lifecycle

A course should move through a real release process:

`proposal → source/rights intake → instructor verification → curriculum build → content/claims/accessibility review → QA/assessment review → offer/entitlement review → pilot → active course → monitored/corrected/retired`

A PDF, course title or old lesson list is not an active course by itself.

## Learning areas

CrownThriveU can support education across professional beauty, MM Suites/Suite Pro operations, affiliates/ambassadors, creator and media workflows, developer/integration learning, product education, ministry/Sermon Toolkit pathways and broader business/community education when current courses are released.

## Instructor and contributor rights

A course can contain CrownThrive-owned material, instructor material, licensed third-party content, public-domain material, AI-assisted content, learner submissions and protected media. Those categories need different permissions.

Instructor onboarding does not automatically assign every instructor-created asset to CrownThrive. The current agreement controls ownership, platform license, edits, recordings, promotion, compensation, reuse and post-termination treatment.

## Pricing and access

No single authoritative current CrownThriveU platform-wide pricing table is present in the source set used for this guide. Courses can use one-time, subscription, cohort, organization, bundle or licensed-access models. The **live course offer controls its current price, access term, refund rules and included credential**.

Do not infer one course's price from another or treat payment as proof of completion.

## Credentials and certificates

A credential should identify the course/version, learner, completion or assessment criteria and issue state. A certificate can prove that the stated CrownThriveU requirements were met; it does not automatically mean professional licensure, government accreditation or regulated certification.

## Ecosystem routes

- **ThrivePeer:** mentorship and accountability after or alongside learning.
- **ThriveAlumni:** post-program community and continuing opportunity.
- **Crown Affiliates/Ambassadors:** training before eligible promotional roles.
- **MM Suites / Locticians / Melanin Magic:** professional and product education where programs are active.
- **Go-Flipbooks:** licensed reader or course-resource delivery.
- **CrownRewards:** approved learning incentives.
- **ThrivePush:** permissioned reminders.
- **CrownLytics/CrownPulse:** aggregate learning evidence and engagement where appropriate.

## Privacy, accessibility and academic integrity

Learner submissions, grades and private feedback should remain scoped to the learning relationship. Courses should use accessible content formats, captions/transcripts where appropriate and readable learning materials. Plagiarism, fabricated credentials and unauthorized reuse of instructor or learner work can trigger correction or removal.

## Troubleshooting

**A course appears in an old article but not the catalog.** The course may be unreleased, retired or awaiting reconstruction. Use the current catalog.

**I paid but do not see a certificate.** Payment creates access, not automatic completion. Verify the course criteria and completion state.

**I am an instructor and see my material used incorrectly.** Use the instructor/IP dispute route and provide the course, asset and current agreement context.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does CrownThriveU guarantee a job after a course?

No. Learning can support progression, but employment and income outcomes are not guaranteed.

### Are CrownThriveU certificates professional licenses?

Not unless the specific current credential explicitly has that externally recognized status.

### Can I become an instructor automatically after graduating?

No. Instructor onboarding and approval are separate.

### Can organizations purchase seats?

The institutional model supports organization/team access, but current pricing and seat rules must come from a live offer.
