# TapStations — Physical Touchpoints for Digital Action

TapStations is CrownThrive's **hardware and deployment product line** for physical QR and NFC touchpoints. It is designed to bring a ThriveKiosks-powered digital experience into salons, venues, pop-ups, corridors, events, campuses and other physical environments.

<Warning>
  **TapStations is coming soon at [https://tapstations.com](https://tapstations.com).** Projected hardware, rental and managed-service pricing in this guide is planning information, not a live purchase commitment. Final launch pricing controls.
</Warning>

## The three-part architecture

- **TapStations = the hardware and deployment brand.**
- **ThriveKiosks = the software engine underneath the station experience.**
- **Collab Portal = the control room for anything managed, deployed, rented, governed or supported as a managed service.**

This keeps ThriveKiosks focused on software while TapStations owns the physical product experience.

## What this helps you do

- Place tap-to-open or scan-to-open experiences in physical spaces.
- Route visitors to bookings, tickets, offers, menus, forms or other approved destinations.
- Use a Self-Serve hardware kit without turning routine ownership into a managed-services project.
- Choose a Managed lane when deployment, rotations, rental operations or ongoing changes require CrownThrive involvement.

## Availability and eligibility

TapStations is a **coming-soon** product line. A ThriveKiosks subscription is expected to remain the software requirement for station experiences. Managed plans and rentals use Collab Portal at [**https://portal.crownthrive.com**](https://portal.crownthrive.com).

## Self-Serve lane

Self-Serve is for customers who want to purchase hardware, follow a setup flow and operate the station themselves.

- Buy the approved hardware kit.
- Receive a Quick Start and setup-wizard style flow.
- Connect the station to a ThriveKiosks experience.
- Handle routine content and destination changes through the software.
- Use Help Center support for standard questions.
- Collab Portal is not required for day-to-day use unless a paid support add-on or managed request is purchased.

## Managed lane

Managed is for deployments that involve CrownThrive operational work.

Examples include:

- Initial configuration and setup services.
- Rentals or event deployments.
- Multi-location installation.
- Corridor governance.
- Sponsor rotations.
- Ongoing content or campaign updates.
- Management subscriptions.
- Formal support SLAs.

For these uses, **Collab Portal is mandatory** so station profiles, provisioning, QA signoff, change requests, inventory and evidence stay in one managed workspace.

## What lives where

### TapStations.com

The TapStations storefront is intended to hold hardware SKUs, bundles, rentals, management plans, setup packages, policies, hardware specifications, outcomes and use cases.

### ThriveKiosks.com

ThriveKiosks remains the software site for digital kiosk plans, templates, features, categories, products, PWAs and demos.

### Collab Portal

Managed station profiles, provisioning, QA, changes, rentals, inventory, sponsor rotations, corridor onboarding, SLAs and evidence logs belong in Collab Portal.

## Thrive Flywheel mapping

TapStations extends the Flywheel into the physical world. A person can tap or scan at a location, open a ThriveKiosks experience, book with ThriveSeat, buy tickets through ThriveTickets, join a My CrownRewards program or move into another CrownThrive destination. CrownLytics can support deeper measurement when the linked experience is instrumented appropriately.

## Privacy, accessibility and physical placement

Physical placement matters. Keep QR and NFC touchpoints reachable, readable and easy to understand. Do not place stations where tapping or scanning creates a safety hazard. If the experience collects personal information, the digital destination must provide the appropriate notices and consent path.

## Troubleshooting

**The NFC tap does not work.** Confirm the device supports NFC, identify the correct tap location on the phone and test the QR fallback.

**The QR opens an outdated experience.** Review the mapping and the ThriveKiosks destination. Managed customers should use Collab Portal for governed changes.

**I bought Self-Serve but now need help across several locations.** Move the support request into a managed scope through Collab Portal rather than trying to coordinate a multi-site deployment through ad hoc email.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is TapStations the same as ThriveKiosks?

No. TapStations is the physical product/deployment line. ThriveKiosks is the software.

### Is tapstations.com live for purchasing now?

TapStations is currently documented as coming soon. Do not treat projected SKUs or prices as a live catalog until the storefront launches.

### Do Self-Serve customers have to use Collab Portal?

Not for routine day-to-day operation. Collab Portal becomes the official path when paid support or managed work is involved.

### Are managed deployments optional?

Yes. The two-lane structure exists specifically so simple Self-Serve users are not forced into managed operations.
