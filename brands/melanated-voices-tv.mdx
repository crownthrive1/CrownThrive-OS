# Melanated Voices TV — The Video Channel for the Voices Corridor

Melanated Voices TV is the video and channel-distribution manifestation of the Melanated Voices corridor. It carries culturally grounded video programming while keeping the underlying creator, source work, rights and Melanated Voices identity intact.

## Availability and current state

The current institutional state is **channel/distribution identity with rebuild and provider verification still open**. Holdings records preserve melanatedvoicestv.com as an active domain and Viloud as shared provider lineage for channel-style distribution, but domain/provider evidence does not prove a complete current Roku/video deployment.

A separate historical `MVP (Roku)` lineage record remains unresolved. Do not silently equate that record with Melanated Voices TV or the future MVP orchestration layer.

## What this helps you do

- Understand the role of Melanated Voices TV relative to Melanated Voices and Melanated TV.
- Follow current channel/programming access when published.
- Understand that channel distribution does not transfer source ownership.
- Know why old Roku/channel references require current verification.

## Provider and distribution boundary

Viloud is provider infrastructure, not the CrownThrive brand. The current provider documentation supports delivery/channel/schedule/EPG capabilities at the provider layer, while actual CrownThrive account/channel IDs and current configuration remain separately verified.

## Thrive Flywheel mapping

## Troubleshooting

**I found an old Roku channel reference. Is it still active?** Not automatically. Use the current channel route and Help Center.

**Is Melanated Voices TV the same as MVP?** No. MVP is cross-media orchestration; Melanated Voices TV is a video/channel distribution identity.

**Does the channel own submitted content?** Distribution alone does not transfer ownership. Use the current contributor/license agreement.
