# Wearable Art — When a Gallery Work Becomes Something You Can Wear

CrownThrive Wearable Art turns approved gallery artwork into fashion and artwear editions through current product/manufacturing relationships, including Le Galeriste lineage. The garment is a product edition of the art; buying it does not transfer the artist's copyright or merchandising rights.

## What this helps you do

- Shop current wearable-art editions when available.
- Understand the artist and collection behind a garment.
- Review size, material, care and fulfillment before buying.
- Know what rights come with owning the physical product.
- Understand the provider/manufacturer relationship without confusing it with CrownThrive brand ownership.

## Availability and eligibility

Wearable Art is a current CrownThrive/Melanin Magic/Artful Mane product identity with Le Galeriste provider lineage. Exact current garments, price, size availability, provider terms, fulfillment and return policies must be verified from the live product offer.

## How it works

  <Step title="Choose a current wearable edition">

## Pricing

No universal Wearable Art price table is established in the source set used for this guide. Prices vary by garment, collection, material, print method, artist agreement, provider and shipping. The current product page controls.

## Artist royalties and rights

An artist's compensation can be a fee, royalty or other current arrangement according to the contribution/product agreement. A product being “artist royalty” aligned does not imply one permanent percentage for every collection.

## Le Galeriste provider boundary

Le Galeriste can provide wearable-art production/fulfillment infrastructure. CrownThrive must preserve the source art, artist rights, collection identity, product mapping, customer support and alternative-provider path outside a replaceable vendor.

## Sustainability

Material, production and sustainability claims must match the current product/provider evidence. Broader impact programs are coordinated through the **CrownThrive Impact Institute at [https://crownthrive.org](https://crownthrive.org)**.

## Troubleshooting

**The size I want is unavailable.** Use the current product/provider inventory. Archived collection images do not guarantee size stock.

**Can I resell the garment?** Ordinary resale of a lawfully purchased physical item and reproducing/creating new merchandise are different rights. Do not reproduce the art without permission.

**The provider page and CrownThrive page disagree on care instructions.** Use the current product specification and contact support before washing or altering the item.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Wearable Art the same as The Artful Mane Gallery?

Wearable Art is a product expression of approved gallery art; the Gallery is the curation/art identity.

### Does Le Galeriste own the artwork?

Not by implication. Provider manufacturing/fulfillment does not create source-art ownership.

### Is every product a limited edition?

Only when the current product offer explicitly says so.
