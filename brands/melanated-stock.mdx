# Melanated Stock — Stock Media With Cultural Context

Melanated Stock is CrownThrive's stock-media identity for culturally relevant photography, video and creative assets. Its role is to make better representation easier to license without treating people, culture or community imagery as generic visual inventory with no provenance.

## What this helps you do

- Discover stock assets when the current catalog is available.
- Understand contributor, model/property release and license requirements.
- Use approved media in brand, editorial, educational or campaign work according to the current license.
- Keep stock-asset licensing distinct from source ownership and broader character/universe rights.

## Availability and eligibility

Melanated Stock is a current stable media identity with provider/deployment verification still open. Public-host evidence has existed, but the current catalog, search, licensing engine, pricing, contributor program and API require current verification.

## Asset requirements

A licensable asset should identify creator/contributor, source file, model/property releases where relevant, editorial/commercial class, allowed uses, prohibited uses, territory/term where applicable, download variant and correction/takedown state.

## Representation and sensitive use

Even a properly licensed stock image can be used harmfully. Do not use identifiable people to imply sensitive medical, criminal, political, sexual or other personal attributes they did not actually represent. Model releases and platform licenses do not authorize defamatory or deceptive context.

## Thrive Flywheel mapping

Melanated Stock can supply approved creative to CrownThrive Studios, AdLuxe Studio, ThriveFoundry, CrownThriveU, The Mane Experience and other media products. CrownLytics can measure published performance without becoming the rights authority for the asset.

## Troubleshooting

**I downloaded an image earlier. Can I use it for a new campaign?** Check the license you received and whether the new use fits its scope.

**Can I resell a stock asset by itself?** Usually not unless the current license explicitly grants that right.

**A contributor asks for a takedown.** Use the rights/support process and preserve the source, license and release history while reviewing the request.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does Melanated Stock own every image in the library?

Not necessarily. Contributors can license assets through the platform while retaining ownership according to the current contributor agreement.

### Is every asset cleared for commercial advertising?

No. Check the individual asset/license class.

### Can stock media be used to train AI models?

Only if the applicable contributor/source and license terms explicitly allow that use.
