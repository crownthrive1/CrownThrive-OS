# Luxperiences — Discover Premium Spaces Without Guessing What Is Booked

Luxperiences is CrownThrive's premium spaces, venues, rentals and curated-experiences lane. It is intended to help people discover elevated physical experiences while keeping provider identity, availability, booking, deposits, refunds, safety and accessibility explicit.

## Availability and eligibility

The current Help Center legal addendum describes **browsing-only access while booking functionality remains disabled until official launch**. The institutional registry also preserves Luxperiences as a P2/Revamp platform whose current domain, engine/provider and live inventory still require verification.

<Warning>
  A visible venue, space or experience is **not a confirmed reservation**. Do not make travel, event or payment commitments based on a listing unless the provider and current booking flow explicitly confirm availability.
</Warning>

## What this helps you do

- Discover premium venues, rentals or experiences when listings are published.
- Understand what information a future bookable offer must provide.
- Separate inspiration from confirmed inventory.
- Know where provider responsibility, safety and refund terms belong.

## What a bookable offer must establish

Before Luxperiences presents a live booking, the offer should resolve:

- Provider or host identity.
- Exact property, space or experience.
- Location and date/time availability.
- Price, fees, taxes and deposit requirements.
- Capacity and restrictions.
- Cancellation/refund terms.
- Accessibility information.
- Safety and emergency contacts.
- Insurance/liability allocation where relevant.
- Customer-data sharing.
- Confirmation and support route.

No verified current Luxperiences customer price catalog is present because current Help Center policy treats the product as browsing-only pending launch. Future pricing will be offer-specific: premium experiences cannot responsibly use one universal price because venues, dates, capacity, providers and services differ.

## Thrive Flywheel mapping

Luxperiences can eventually connect discovery, ThriveTickets events, ThriveKiosks/TapStations onsite experiences, CrownRewards, media storytelling and AdLuxe promotion. Those routes become operational only where the actual venue/provider and booking state are current.

## Privacy, accessibility and safety

Hospitality experiences can involve location, itinerary, accessibility and emergency information. Collect only what is necessary for the booking or experience. Accessibility details should describe the actual venue rather than generic brand promises. Safety incidents must be escalated rather than hidden to protect a listing.

## Troubleshooting

**I see a listing but cannot book it.** That is consistent with the current browsing-only policy. Wait for an official bookable flow.

**Can support manually guarantee a venue date?** Not without provider confirmation and an approved booking process.

**I am a venue owner and want to list.** Use CrownThrive partner intake and provide the current inventory, rights, pricing, accessibility, safety and support information required for review.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Luxperiences currently a full booking marketplace?

No. Current Help Center policy describes browsing-only access until official booking launch.

### Does CrownThrive own every listed venue?

No such assumption should be made. Third-party provider/host relationships must be identified for the actual offer.

### Will prices be the same year-round?

Do not assume that. Future offer pricing can vary by provider, date, capacity and services.
