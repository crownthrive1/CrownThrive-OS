# My CrownRewards — Built on Culture. Powered by Loyalty.

My CrownRewards is CrownThrive's customer loyalty platform for digital reward programs, points, perks and stamp cards. Members join free and can participate in eligible partner programs; partners configure loyalty experiences; staff use a dedicated operational portal for awarding or redeeming eligible activity.

## What this helps you do

### Members

- Join participating reward programs without a membership fee.
- Collect points, stamps or other published program benefits.
- See available rewards and redemption rules.
- Use one My CrownRewards identity across participating programs where supported.

### Partners

- Launch digital loyalty cards and stamp programs.
- Define reward, point and redemption logic.
- Generate QR codes for in-person earning or redemption.
- Give staff a dedicated operational interface.
- Use promotions and tier structures to encourage repeat engagement.

## Digital stamp cards

Stamp cards are designed for repeat-visit programs such as visit-based rewards. Each stamp should correspond to the published program rules. A staff member should not award, reverse or redeem benefits outside the authorized program merely because they have operational access.

## CrownRewards versus Nexus Credits

My CrownRewards points and rewards are **loyalty benefits**. **Nexus Credits** are Locticians-specific on-platform credits used for visibility and promotion inside Locticians. CrownRewards points do not convert to Nexus Credits, and a loyalty balance should not be treated as advertising placement currency.

## Partner pricing

### CrownRewards Partner Add-On — \$300/month

For participating businesses that want to operate My CrownRewards loyalty programs.

Current supplied partner benefits include:

- Unlimited access to create and manage digital loyalty cards.
- Partner Portal control of rewards, points and redemptions.
- Staff Portal for scanning, rewarding and redeeming.
- Member Portal access for customers participating in eligible programs.
- Optional discounted access to the Locticians Community & Directory.
- Optional CrownRewards Pro badge and featured listing opportunities, with extra cost after launch where applicable.
- Custom QR-code generation for earning and redemption.
- Tiered rewards and promotional tools.
- Automated customer alerts for balances, unlocks and expirations.
- Broader ecosystem linking described as a Phase 3 expansion item.

**Best for:** businesses that want a managed loyalty program without building a separate rewards platform.

### CrownRewards Partner Add-On — \$3,000/year

The annual option provides the same core partner program model at the supplied annual price.

- Effective published saving compared with twelve monthly \$300 payments.
- Better fit for partners committing to year-round loyalty rather than a short test.
- Managed multi-location, corridor, sponsorship or custom operational work remains separately scoped through Collab Portal.

### Members — Free

Members join participating programs without a membership charge from My CrownRewards. Individual partner offers can still have purchase or eligibility requirements; “free to join” does not mean every reward is earned without a qualifying action.

## Thrive Flywheel mapping

My CrownRewards is a **retention and recirculation** layer. Locticians, ThriveSeat, ThriveTickets, ThriveKiosks, BrandCards or other brands can create qualifying customer interactions; My CrownRewards can reinforce repeat behavior; ThrivePush can support permissioned reminders; CrownLytics can support program analysis; and AdLuxe can promote a loyalty campaign when appropriate.

## Impact

Impact campaigns should route through the **CrownThrive Impact Institute at [https://crownthrive.org](https://crownthrive.org)**. Individual CrownThrive brands may participate in EcoDrive or Stripe Climate initiatives, but My CrownRewards should not imply that every point or redemption automatically creates a specific environmental outcome unless the active campaign explicitly says so.

## Privacy, fairness and program integrity

- Partners must publish clear earning and redemption rules.
- Staff access should be role-limited.
- Refunds or reversed purchases can affect eligible rewards according to program rules.
- Partners should not fabricate scarcity, points or reward activity.
- Members should not create duplicate/fraudulent activity to manufacture points or stamps.

## Troubleshooting

**My points or stamp did not appear.** Confirm you were signed into the correct member account, the activity qualified under the partner's rules and the staff/partner transaction completed successfully.

**A reward cannot be redeemed.** Review eligibility, expiry, location and balance requirements. If the rules are satisfied, contact the program or support with the program name and transaction details.

**I am a partner and need multi-location help.** Use Collab Portal for managed operational work instead of trying to coordinate a governed rollout through ordinary member support.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is My CrownRewards free for members?

Yes. The current public positioning is “Join free. Earn free. Thrive free.” Qualifying partner actions still determine whether a specific reward is earned.

### Can any business create a partner program instantly?

Partner onboarding is routed through CrownThrive. Use the Partner Portal after the business has the appropriate access.

### Is CrownRewards a cryptocurrency or investment product?

The current Help Center guide treats it as a loyalty and closed-loop incentive system, not an investment claim.

### Where do managed partner projects live?

Managed operations use Collab Portal at [https://portal.crownthrive.com](https://portal.crownthrive.com).
