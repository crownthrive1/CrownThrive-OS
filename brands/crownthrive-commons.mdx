# CrownThrive Commons — A Place for Community That Still Has Boundaries

CrownThrive Commons is the private or gated community layer for groups, member relationships, messaging, announcements and community participation across CrownThrive programs. It is designed to support real connection without turning a social profile or moderator badge into the source of institutional identity or corporate authority.

## What this helps you do

- Join eligible CrownThrive groups or community spaces.
- Participate in discussions and announcements.
- Build member relationships within the current visibility rules.
- Message or collaborate where the group allows it.
- Understand how moderation, suspension and appeals should work.

## Availability and eligibility

CrownThrive Commons is a current strategic role. Exact current engine, version, auth, data model, moderation tools and public URL remain verification items. Access can be program-, cohort-, membership- or invitation-based.

## Commons versus CrownThrive ID

CrownThrive Commons can hold social/community account information, but it is **not the durable identity authority for CrownThrive**. Account linking should connect the community experience to CrownThrive's broader identity system rather than letting a replaceable community provider become the institutional source of truth.

## Pricing and access

No standalone CrownThrive Commons pricing table is established in the current source set used for this guide. Access may be included with a CrownThrive program, course, membership, alumni relationship or other current offer. Use the specific program for any live fee or access term.

## Moderation and safety

Community systems need content standards, anti-harassment controls, privacy, suspension/appeal, minor-safety measures where relevant and separation between community moderation and corporate/legal authority.

A moderator can manage the community space within delegated scope; the role does not automatically grant billing, CHLOM, employment or executive authority.

## Thrive Flywheel mapping

Commons can strengthen retention and community continuity around CrownThriveU, ThrivePeer, ThriveAlumni, ThriveGather, Locticians and other member programs. Public campaigns still belong in the appropriate advertising/media products rather than turning private community data into an uncontrolled marketing audience.

## Troubleshooting

**I can access CrownThrive elsewhere but not a Commons group.** Group membership is a separate permission. Confirm the current program or invitation.

**I was suspended and disagree with the decision.** Use the available moderation appeal route rather than creating duplicate accounts to bypass the restriction.

**Can a group admin see everything in my CrownThrive account?** They should only receive the data and controls required for the community role, not unrestricted CrownThrive-wide information.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is CrownThrive Commons a public social network?

Its institutional role is private/gated community rather than a universal public network.

### Does joining Commons make me a CrownThrive member in every program?

No. Program and platform eligibility remain separate.

### Is a moderator a CrownThrive executive?

No. Moderation is a scoped community role unless another authority is separately granted.
