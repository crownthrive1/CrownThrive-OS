# ThriveKiosks — Power Your Brand With a Professional Digital Kiosk

ThriveKiosks gives creators, entrepreneurs and small businesses a mobile-first hub where a brand can present contact information, services, products, media, bookings and clear customer actions in one place. It is designed to be shared by personalized link, QR, NFC, social media, text or email and can operate as an always-on digital business station.

## What this helps you do

- Build a professional mobile kiosk without a full custom website project.
- Present services, products, pricing and payment methods.
- Show galleries, testimonials, hours, maps and YouTube content.
- Accept inquiries and route visitors into appointments or service bookings.
- Give visitors a one-tap way to save contact information.
- Share the kiosk through a personalized link, QR or NFC-compatible path.
- Use a Progressive Web App option on supported plans.

## Availability and eligibility

ThriveKiosks is available at [**https://thrivekiosks.com**](https://thrivekiosks.com). The current product includes vCard and kiosk functionality in the same plan structure. **TapStations is a separate, coming-soon physical hardware/deployment product line at [https://tapstations.com](https://tapstations.com) and should not be treated as the ThriveKiosks software storefront.**

Deeper ADE revenue routing and CHLOM policy/licensing integrations are coming soon unless a specific feature is explicitly marked live.

## Core features

### Photo galleries

Show portfolio, product or service imagery in mobile-friendly layouts.

### Services and bookings

List services with descriptions, prices and inquiry/booking options. ThriveSeat can be used as the downstream scheduling system when that integration is appropriate.

### Products and categories

Paid plans add kiosk-specific product catalogs and category capacity, making ThriveKiosks more than a basic contact card.

### Contact and messaging

Publish business information, social profiles, contact forms and WhatsApp links. Visitors can save supported contact information to their phones.

### Business information

Display hours, accepted payment methods, location and Google Maps information.

### Media and social

Embed YouTube content, link social channels and use galleries/testimonials as trust-building elements.

### Branding and PWA

Eligible plans support custom domains, personalized links, advanced settings, Progressive Web App features and platform-brand suppression.

### NFC and QR

ThriveKiosks supports QR and NFC-oriented sharing. Physical NFC-card policies are published separately, and larger physical deployment programs belong to TapStations.

## ThriveKiosks versus BrandCards, CrownThrive IO and TapStations

- **ThriveKiosks:** customer-facing digital business station for services, products, media and action.
- **BrandCards:** identity-first smart vCard/profile.
- **CrownThrive IO:** broader link, QR, hosting, routing, analytics and web-tool platform.
- **TapStations:** physical hardware and managed deployment line, powered by ThriveKiosks.

## Policies, privacy and accessibility

ThriveKiosks publishes separate NFC Card, NFC A&H, Acceptable Use & Apps, UGC, Terms, Privacy and Refund policies. A kiosk can display products or claims, but the platform does not make those claims true. Businesses remain responsible for accurate prices, service descriptions, rights and disclosures.

## Troubleshooting

**My kiosk opens, but a booking button fails.** Test the downstream booking URL separately. ThriveKiosks can route the visitor, but it cannot make an unavailable external booking destination work.

**My NFC tag opens the wrong place.** Check the mapped URL and follow the NFC Card Policy before reprogramming or replacing physical media.

**The kiosk feels crowded.** Reduce categories and place the most important action first. A digital kiosk should reduce friction, not recreate a large website menu on a small screen.
