# CrownThrive Services Stack Control Plugin

**Plugin ID:** `ct.plugin.crownthrive-services-stack`  
**Version:** `0.1.0`  
**State:** controlled test / governed HOLD  
**Stack ID:** `ct.stack.crownthrive-services-stack`  
**CHLOM CaaS platform:** `ct.platform.chlom-caas`

The plugin exposes the public-safe control surface for CrownThrive's shared-service architecture. It governs fourteen reusable lanes—Identity, Authentication, Authorization, Billing, Licensing, Analytics, Notifications, CRM, Ticketing, Search, Commerce, Routing, Rewards, and Documentation—without making each platform rebuild those capabilities independently.

## What it controls

- stable shared-service identities and exact contract versions;
- replaceable provider bindings and fallback candidates;
- CHLOM CaaS controls, evidence requirements, gaps, and remediation state;
- least-data route plans, idempotency, retry, rollback, compensation, and readback;
- candidate SLO, capacity, cost, recovery, and continuity policies;
- derived Asset Fabric packages, protected kernels, and productization candidates;
- independent certification receipts and append-only lifecycle history.

## Tool surface

The controlled-test MCP surface contains fourteen tools:

`css.status`, `css.services.list`, `css.services.get`, `css.providers.list`, `css.contracts.list`, `css.controls.list`, `css.routes.list`, `css.readiness.evaluate`, `css.controls.map`, `css.gaps.scan`, `css.bind.plan`, `css.failover.plan`, `css.certification.submit`, and `css.receipts.list`.

Readiness and control coverage are system/evidence states. They are not legal, regulatory, accounting, tax, accessibility, cybersecurity, insurance, or other professional certification.

## Hard boundaries

- no D3 automation;
- no sovereign-vote effect;
- no direct-main merge;
- no originator self-certification;
- no raw secret, private identity, or protected implementation export;
- no provider-write inheritance;
- no automatic failover execution;
- no provider response creating CrownThrive billing, license, entitlement, reward, or settlement truth;
- no checkout or entitlement activation;
- no silent deletion.

## Current promotion state

The package is not installed, submitted, published, commercially live, or a customer entitlement. Current work is limited to contract, control, provider-binding, route, evidence, continuity, and independent-verification construction.
