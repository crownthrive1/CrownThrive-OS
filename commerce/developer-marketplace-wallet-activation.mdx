## Identity and data boundary

1. Sites dispatch verifies the signed-in ChatGPT viewer.
2. The server normalizes the viewer email and converts it to a one-way SHA-256 identity fingerprint.
3. Only that fingerprint and a fixed action are sent through the private server-to-server gateway.
4. Supabase creates or reads the owner-scoped wallet and remains authoritative for balance, purchases, entitlements, licenses, reversals, and delivery.
5. Raw email, wallet fingerprints, provider object IDs, secrets, and storage paths must never appear in public markup or catalog feeds.

A redirect, checkout session identifier, email field, or browser claim is never accepted as payment, entitlement, license, or delivery evidence.

## Funding activation runbook

Complete these steps in order:

1. Roll/delete the historically exposed Stripe key and review Workbench activity.
2. Create a least-privilege restricted runtime key and install it through Supabase secret management.
3. Register the active credit-commerce webhook for the required event set and install its signing secret through secret management.
4. Correct and attest the Stripe merchant business profile and remediate the payout bank error.
5. Complete jurisdiction/tax classification and registration facts.
6. Make customer terms operative and capture versioned acceptance.
7. Complete rights, accessibility, package-integrity, independent-review, protected-delivery, refund, dispute, reversal, and rollback evidence for an exact release.
8. Approve the exact offer-release binding.
9. Run a bounded test-mode canary and reconcile provider readback.
10. Run one bounded live canary only after the preceding gates pass.
11. Activate one approved offer, observe it, and expand in controlled tranches.

Never place secret values in chat, source, GitHub, Mintlify, Google Drive, or public Site configuration.

## Authority and continuity

Google Drive is the human master and recovery record. Supabase/THIVEBASE is the authority for ledger state, metadata, lineage, rights, queues, checksums, and evidence. CHLOM may fingerprint an exact version and its integrity only; it must not assert authorship, ownership, legal sufficiency, access, or license validity. The agent that produced an asset cannot certify its own release.
