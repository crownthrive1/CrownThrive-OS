# ThriveEvergreen™ Commerce & Economic Activation Authority

**Canonical platform:** `ct.platform.thriveevergreen`<br />**Canonical asset class:** `ct.assetclass.evergreen`<br />**Steward agent:** `ct.agent.thriveevergreen-steward`<br />**Constitutional supervisor:** `ct.agent.agent-a-supervisor`<br />**Governance parent:** CHLOM<br />**Cultural authority:** CIE<br />**Metaprotocol role:** cross-pallet commerce/economic-execution subprotocol<br />**Current state:** `specified_phase_2`

<Info>
  ThriveEvergreen is CrownThrive's mandatory commercial activation authority. It does not replace CHLOM, AdLuxe, Stripe, CrownLytics, storefronts, fulfillment providers or individual platforms. It binds their commercial states into one governed lifecycle.
</Info>

## Constitutional invariants

> **ThriveEvergreen may optimize within authority. It may never manufacture authority.**

The following are institutional invariants rather than implementation suggestions:

- every certified CrownThrive commerce, booking, marketplace, payment, advertising, storefront, API, MCP, plugin or agent surface that can activate economic state must resolve through `ECAC | HOLD | DENY`;
- a provider identifier is an alias, never CrownThrive's canonical product, SKU, rights, offer or settlement identity;
- provider evidence is **not** economic truth: delivery, payment, fulfillment or API success cannot directly create an invoice, earning, payout, entitlement, public performance claim or settlement without the applicable institutional reconciliation;
- execution authority is bounded, signed, versioned and revocable; execution does not eliminate accountability;
- material commercial configuration changes supersede or re-evaluate the affected ECAC rather than silently mutating its historical state;
- completed adverse events, corrections, rollbacks and compensating actions remain append-only evidence and cannot be erased to make the ledger appear clean;
- restricted algorithms, prompts, credentials, private economics and root-control material remain outside the public documentation projection.

This specification is institutionally adopted in Phase 2. It is **not** a claim that the Phase 3 runtime, adapters, ledgers, APIs/MCPs, Vault boundaries or adversarial certification are already in production.

## Mandatory lifecycle

```text
asset → provenance → rights → CIE → product → variant → SKU → license
→ cost/margin → price → promotion → tax → channel → listing → advertising
→ checkout/payment → entitlement → fulfillment → invoice → revenue routing
→ payout/settlement → refund/dispute → reconciliation → optimization
→ renewal/supersession → preservation/succession
```

> **No CrownThrive commercial object becomes economically active without ThriveEvergreen authorization.**

The target authorization interface is:

```text
commercial candidate → ThriveEvergreen → ECAC | HOLD | DENY
```

`ECAC` means **Evergreen Commerce Activation Certificate**. It binds the exact asset/version, product/variant, SKU, offer, rights evidence, price, tax class, fulfillment, entitlement, refund, risk, advertising, territory, channel and policy hashes that were approved.

## Authority doctrine

ThriveEvergreen may execute reversible or compensatable commercial/financial actions inside a current signed authority envelope and reconcile immediately afterward.

It may autonomously:

- productize eligible CHLOM assets;
- assign permanent SKUs;
- build variants, bundles and store projections;
- price and promote inside approved limits;
- activate already-certified offers;
- operate approved Stripe/payment, storefront, AdLuxe and fulfillment adapters;
- execute approved standard non-exclusive licenses;
- process bounded policy-compliant refunds;
- route approved commissions, royalties, fees and settlement rules;
- pause products/campaigns when rights, economics, risk or fulfillment fail;
- perform provider readback and reconciliation;
- roll back reversible actions or create compensating actions when literal reversal is impossible.

It may never manufacture authority. It cannot self-amend its charter, grant itself new secrets/scopes, remove Charter/Agent A supervision, invent rights/consent, disable audit/reconciliation, redirect root payout ownership controls, erase adverse events or replicate outside approved CrownThrive custody.

CrownThrive governance retains `FREEZE`, `QUARANTINE`, `REVOKE_CREDENTIALS`, `ROLLBACK`, `RESTORE` and `DECOMMISSION` powers.

## Pricing and promotion intelligence

Pricing evaluates cost, provider/payment expense, tax, fulfillment, support, refund/dispute risk, royalties, commissions, margin, demand, scarcity, license breadth, seats/users, term, territory, exclusivity and strategic value.

It maintains at least:

- floor price;
- sustainable price;
- target price;
- premium price;
- negotiated range;
- promotion floor;
- walk-away price.

Promotions may use bundles, volume, wholesale, introductory, affiliate/reward, seasonal, clearance or premium strategies only when contractual and economic constraints remain satisfied.

## Rights and legal-policy boundary

Technical capability to sell is not authority to sell. Payment, provider commercial-use terms, possession of a file, AI generation or a storefront listing do not prove chain of title or sublicensing rights.

An autonomously executable agreement must fit an approved contract envelope. Ownership assignment, material exclusivity, perpetual sublicensing, material source-code transfer, equity/securities, unusually broad guarantees/indemnities, franchise/regional structures and other high-consequence obligations use the authority/professional review required by governance and law.

## Stripe and payment architecture

Stripe is a payment/commerce adapter, not the CrownThrive source of product or rights truth.

Certified ThriveEvergreen adapters may manage Products, Prices, Checkout, Billing, Invoicing, Customer Portal, tax integrations, promotions, refunds, disputes, Connect, transfers, application fees, webhooks, Quotes and revenue recovery.

Every provider object maps to canonical `asset_id`, `product_id`, `sku`, `offer_id`, `license_policy_id`, `pricing_decision_id` and `ecac_id`.

Credential promotion follows:

```text
READ → TEST_WRITE → CANARY_LIVE_WRITE → BOUNDED_PRODUCTION_WRITE
```

Root account ownership, payout bank destinations, unrestricted credential scopes, tax identity and platform-liability configuration remain separately governed.

## Commerce topology

Before payment activation ThriveEvergreen determines whether the transaction is a direct CrownThrive sale, marketplace, SaaS merchant transaction, single/multi-vendor sale, immediate/held settlement, commission-bearing transaction, subscription/usage model and domestic/cross-border relationship.

Merchant-of-record, risk, loss liability, disputes, onboarding and settlement must match the topology.

## Crown Credits

ThriveEvergreen governs credit issuance, purchase, promotional credits, redemption, refund-to-credit, expiration, stacking, restricted uses, fraud controls, cross-platform use and ledger treatment. Credits must not silently become cash, deposits, securities or unrestricted transferable value without the appropriate determination.

## Entitlement and fulfillment

```text

```

Digital fulfillment covers signed/expiring URLs, download limits, libraries, versioned files, license certificates and protected-master separation.

Physical fulfillment covers suppliers/manufacturers, production rights, inventory, COGS, packaging, shipping, returns, quality, recalls where applicable, MOQ/wholesale and geography. Providers remain replaceable infrastructure.

## Subscription and usage commerce

Subscription state includes trial/freemium, billing, plan changes, proration, renewals, cancellation/pause, revenue recovery, grace periods, entitlement suspension/reactivation and churn recovery.

Usage-based products require a governed meter, unit, aggregation rule, measurement source, interval, included usage, overage, caps, tiers and billable evidence.
