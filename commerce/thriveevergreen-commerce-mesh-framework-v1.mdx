# ThriveEvergreen Commerce Mesh Framework

**Framework ID:** `ct.framework.thriveevergreen-commerce-mesh.v1`  
**Authority:** Founder override `ct-founder-override-checkout-credit-production-20260824-v1`  
**State:** Production-enabled framework; execution remains evidence-bound and customer-initiated where payment is involved.

## Institutional purpose

ThriveEvergreen is CrownThrive's Commerce & Economic Activation Authority and cross-pallet commerce subprotocol. This framework turns that authority into a reusable production mesh that can discover, bind, package, license, route, publish, sell, fulfill, reconcile, and continuously verify monetizable CrownThrive products across the ecosystem without turning any single website, repository, processor, or provider into the system of record.

The framework is designed for **redundancy, interoperability, exact-version evidence, bounded elasticity, and reversible production execution**.

## Core production contract

The mesh MUST maintain these linked layers for every monetizable product or service:

1. canonical product identity and SKU;
2. exact version and source/custody fingerprint;
3. rights and license package;
4. price and Crown Credits price where applicable;
5. Stripe Product/Price binding for direct checkout where applicable;
6. wallet/credit rail binding where applicable;
7. checkout and credit-checkout routes;
8. entitlement and fulfillment route;
9. support and refund/revocation behavior;
10. analytics and DAIL evidence;
11. site-mesh and repository/documentation bindings;
12. rollback and provider reconciliation path.

A product card or database row alone is not a finished product. A production offer must resolve to a real deliverable, exact entitlement, or defined human-supervised service.

## Product factory standard

ThriveEvergreen may continuously convert qualified CrownThrive source assets into product candidates, including but not limited to:

- printable PDFs and workbooks;
- editable templates where license permits;
- checklists, planners, scorecards, journals, trackers, cards, posters, wall art, learning kits, operating kits, runbooks, SOP packs, implementation kits, business systems, creator kits, ministry kits, beauty-professional kits, procurement packs, readiness packs, data sheets, dashboards, slide kits, spreadsheet tools, licensed media companions, digital craft files, and cross-format bundles;
- PDF + DOCX/ODT + XLSX/CSV + PPTX + EPUB + PNG/JPG/SVG packages where the source and product type support those formats;
- software access, memberships, API/MCP access, implementation services, licensing opportunities, and governed bundles.

Each candidate must have a **value proof**: useful customer outcome, complete content, coherent design, quality-control evidence, appropriate format set, clear scope, and a price/value rationale. The engine MUST NOT manufacture rights, credentials, customer demand, tax facts, provider success, or unsupported valuation claims.

## Pallet projection

ThriveEvergreen remains a cross-pallet subprotocol, not a standalone P-13 pallet. The mesh projects commerce capabilities through the existing CHLOM pallet system, including identity, governance, rights/licensing, media/IP, growth/advertising, commerce/booking/loyalty, community/events, education/incubator/impact, and infrastructure/interoperability.

The production mesh adds reusable component pallets for:

- product qualification;
- package design and format expansion;
- license assignment;
- pricing and economic fit;
- Stripe publication;
- Crown Credits binding;
- checkout routing;
- entitlement/fulfillment;
- procurement sourcing;
- distribution/affiliate readiness;
- repository/documentation convergence;
- evidence, certification, reconciliation, and rollback.

## Redundant agent topology

The engine uses paired independent execution roles rather than a single self-certifying agent:

- **Product Architect A / B** — independently shape and score product packages.
- **Rights & License Binder A / B** — independently validate source-rights/license mappings.
- **Commerce Binder A / B** — independently validate pricing, SKU, Stripe, Crown Credits, checkout and entitlement bindings.
- **Route Mesh Agent A / B** — independently discover and reconcile missing site, API, repository and documentation routes.
- **Fulfillment QA A / B** — independently validate files, formats, delivery, checksum and customer experience.
- **Certification Agent A / B** — compare outputs and issue PASS/HOLD/CONFLICT evidence; neither originating agent may self-certify.
- **Continuity/Recovery Agent A / B** — preserve rollback, custody, provider reconciliation and stale-route remediation.

Agents are non-voting unless a separately governed contract explicitly grants vote eligibility. D3 remains human-reserved.

### Bounded elasticity

The mesh supports controlled replication of worker instances based on demand. It is **not uncontrolled self-replication**. Replica counts are derived from queue depth, age, SLA, failure rate and product volume, bounded by a configured minimum and maximum. Every replica inherits the same immutable authority ceiling, tool scope, no-self-approval rule, kill switch, heartbeat requirement, idempotency contract, evidence contract and rollback requirement.

Replicas may process additional work; they may not expand their own permissions, create new authority, modify signed terms, grant themselves credentials, or change the maximum replica ceiling.

## Wallet and checkout interoperability

Crown Credits and direct Stripe checkout are interoperable but distinct rails:

- **Direct checkout** charges the exact Stripe Price for an eligible offer and issues the mapped entitlement only after verified payment success.
- **Credit checkout** debits the internal Crown Credits ledger and issues the same governed entitlement through the exact offer/release binding.
- **Credit top-up** is the only Crown Credits action that creates a Stripe Checkout Session; purchased credits remain closed-loop under the operative credit terms.
- Every rail resolves to the same canonical SKU, release binding, license, fulfillment package, analytics identity and DAIL trail.

## Procurement and licensing

The ThriveEvergreen procurement lane may source design inputs, packaging inputs, production services, or provider capabilities, but provider evidence is never economic truth. Every sourced component receives provenance, rights scope, permitted derivative use, territory/term where applicable, replacement/exit metadata, cost evidence, and a supplier/provider alias.

CHLOM remains the source of truth for ownership, permitted use, licensing, restrictions and provenance. ThriveEvergreen is the exclusive CrownThrive SKU issuer for offers created through this engine.

## Drive custody

Every released product family must have a canonical private Drive custody location under the ThriveEvergreen product estate with:

- source/master package;
- production exports;
- preview assets;
- license/terms package;
- checksum/manifest;
- provider binding receipt;
- fulfillment test receipt;
- rollback/recovery note.

Public repositories and Mintlify contain public-safe manifests and documentation only. Protected masters and secrets remain in restricted custody.

## Mesh convergence rule

Every active product must be discoverable from the institutional mesh. The reconciliation engine continuously compares:

- catalog offers;
- site-mesh bindings and peer links;
- interop route registry;
- wallet/credit routes;
- Stripe Product/Price bindings;
- entitlement and fulfillment routes;
- repository agent bindings;
- Drive custody bindings;
- Mintlify documentation nodes;
- CrownLytics/DAIL event identities.

Missing or stale routes create repair work packets. Safe mechanical repair can execute automatically inside the agent's existing authority. Provider writes, licensing effects, public economic claims, or high-consequence state changes require the authority already granted for that exact action.

## Commercial quality bar

Production products must be worth the stated price. The quality score combines usefulness, completeness, design coherence, format coverage, editability where licensed, documentation, support clarity, rights confidence, fulfillment reliability, discoverability, and comparative economic fit. Low-score packages are routed back to production rather than published merely to increase catalog count.

## Production cycle

`discover -> qualify -> design -> format -> hash -> rights/license bind -> price -> SKU -> provider/credit bind -> route -> dual QA -> dual certify -> publish -> customer-initiated checkout -> verified settlement -> entitlement -> fulfill -> reconcile -> measure -> improve`

Every cycle is idempotent. Re-running a completed step must read provider/institutional state before attempting another write.

## Persistent desired state

For offers and routes covered by the Founder production override, **ENABLED is the desired state**. Temporary fail-closed behavior is allowed only for a confirmed exploit-critical security defect, payment/entitlement integrity failure, corrupt deliverable, invalid rights state, or unreconcilable provider mismatch. Remediation must preserve the intent to restore the affected route to ENABLED once the defect is corrected.

## Evidence and rollback

All material actions emit DAIL-compatible evidence with the exact subject, version, content hash, authority basis, acting agent, independent verifier, provider readback, result state and rollback reference. Provider success alone never creates an institutional entitlement, payout, license, performance claim, or settlement without reconciliation.
