# ThriveEvergreen Production Automation Fabric

The ThriveEvergreen Production Automation Fabric extends the canonical `thriveevergreen_autonomous_publisher_v2` runtime without replacing it. The v2 engine remains CrownThrive's production commerce/economic activation engine. The Fabric supplies bounded specialist agents, reusable skills, plugin adapters, P‑01 through P‑12 commerce projections, deterministic algorithms, advisory ML, schedules, evidence contracts, and Virality Music integration contracts around that engine.

> **ThriveEvergreen may optimize within authority. It may never manufacture authority.**

## Runtime boundary

```text
canonical engine: ct.agent.thriveevergreen-autonomous-publisher.v2
engine mode: production_write
publication activation: ECAC
D3: human-reserved
self approval: forbidden
provider success -> authority: forbidden
ML -> authority: forbidden
generalized external dispatch: separately gated
```

## Specialist agent family

The production fabric defines eight non-voting A2/D2 specialist agents under the v2 publisher:

1. Candidate Intake — normalizes exact-version product/economic candidates.
2. Rights & Economic Gate — reconciles CHLOM rights, CIE state and commercial prerequisites.
3. Offer & Pricing — computes bounded pricing/margin envelopes; does not publish effective prices.
4. Provider Reconciler — compares provider state with institutional expectations.
5. Entitlement & Fulfillment — derives proposed entitlement/fulfillment state from reconciled evidence.
6. Settlement & Reconciliation — computes allocation/reconciliation deltas without autonomously moving value.
7. Risk & Anomaly — combines deterministic controls and advisory anomaly scores.
8. Continuity Optimizer — detects drift, stale mappings and continuity failures; proposes bounded corrections.

No specialist agent is an independent certifier of its own work. Agent D and existing independent certification lanes remain external to the originating agent family.

## Skills

The Fabric defines twelve reusable skills:

- candidate intake normalization;
- SKU proposal materialization;
- CHLOM rights gate reconciliation;
- CIE commerce-profile reconciliation;
- merchant/topology classification;
- pricing-envelope computation;
- tax/payment readiness;
- provider readback reconciliation;
- entitlement/fulfillment state reduction;
- refund/dispute reconciliation;
- settlement/revenue-route reconciliation;

The CHLOM Wallet skill registry intentionally records these definitions as `CONTROLLED_TEST`; that registry's constitutional constraints prohibit provider writes, credential access, money movement, rights grants and chain broadcast. Production execution remains in the separately certified v2 engine.

## CHLOM pallet projections

ThriveEvergreen does **not** create a P‑13. It projects commercial/economic rules across all twelve CHLOM pallets:

- P‑01 Identity & Access → customer/seller/operator identity and wallet/auth context.
- P‑02 Governance & Compliance → ECAC, risk, emergency controls and audit.
- P‑03 Rights & Licensing → chain of title, license policy, term/territory/revocation.
- P‑04 Media & IP → music, books, video, art, curriculum, software and derivatives.
- P‑05 Growth, Advertising & Measurement → AdLuxe, promotion eligibility and measurement.
- P‑06 Commerce, Booking & Loyalty → products, SKUs, orders, subscriptions, credits and rewards.
- P‑07 Community, Directory & Events → seller/provider/event commerce.
- P‑08 Education, Incubator & Impact → courses, credentials, services and incubated products.
- P‑09 Physical Suites & Place-Based → MM Suites, kiosks, screens and place-based commerce.
- P‑10 Operations & Institutional Memory → evidence, support, documentation and recovery.
- P‑11 Capital, Revenue & Settlement → pricing, commissions, royalties, payouts and reconciliation.
- P‑12 Sustainability & Impact Evidence → governed impact allocation/evidence.

Pallet projections are specifications, not offers, prices, provider writes or money movement.

The algorithms produce evidence or proposed state. They cannot grant authority.

## Advisory ML pack

Three advisory models are defined:

- Demand Score v1 — governed logistic score over normalized demand features.
- Price Elasticity Advisor v1 — estimates observed demand sensitivity; cannot publish price.
- Anomaly Risk v1 — combines refund, dispute, price-drift, provider-mismatch and fulfillment-latency features.

ML is **advisory only**. Model output cannot issue ECAC, grant rights, publish price, write providers, move money, create payout truth or activate entitlements.

## Automation topology

The Fabric binds to existing production schedulers rather than duplicating them:

- 10 bounded ThriveEvergreen v2 publisher slots per hour;
- Crown Credits reconciliation every 10 minutes;
- Stripe stablecoin effective-capability reconciliation every 6 hours.

It adds only missing cycles:

- daily economic/provider/rights drift scan;
- weekly advisory-model calibration and feature-drift review.

Every mutation-capable automation must be idempotent, perform read-after-write, preserve exact-version evidence and have rollback or compensating-action semantics.

## Virality Music convergence

Virality Music consumes the Fabric through existing production control surfaces rather than becoming a second source of commercial truth.

```text
VM catalog / product experience
  -> Virality Credits presentation
  -> Crown Credits canonical ledger
  -> Stripe / Connect / stablecoin / wallet corridors
  -> CHLOM rights + wallet evidence
  -> ThriveEvergreen exact candidate evaluation
  -> ECAC | HOLD | DENY
  -> entitlement / fulfillment only after qualifying reconciliation
```

The existing rule remains:

```text
1 Virality Credit = 25 Crown Credits
```

Native ChatGPT Sites source mutation remains separately gated. The Fabric may compile a VM integration manifest and installation plan without claiming that native site source has been modified.
