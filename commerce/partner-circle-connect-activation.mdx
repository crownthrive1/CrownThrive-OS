# Partner Circle, Crown Credits, and Stripe Connect Activation

This runbook defines the production gate for Partner Circle referrals, Crown Credits, creator seller commerce, and Stripe Connect. These are separate value rails and must never be treated as interchangeable.

<Warning>
  Current state as of August 22, 2026: the Stripe platform account, KJV Sanctuary Plan catalog, and required KJV direct/Connect webhook event coverage are present, and a provider-side recurring Checkout construction canary passes. The authoritative signed-in KJV Sites plan action still requires source reconciliation because its stale direct-billing path performs an unnecessary Account/KYC preflight before Checkout. Seller settlement and paid Crown Credit top-ups remain on hold until their lifecycle, legal/tax, source, and recovery gates below are complete.
</Warning>

## Value-rail boundary

| Rail | Purpose | Settlement | System of record | Allowed today |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Partner Circle referrals | Reward approved referrals and attributable promotion | Promotional, noncash Crown Credits | Partner application and credit ledgers | Review-gated |
| Crown Credit top-up | Buy first-party closed-loop value | Ordinary platform Checkout; no Connect transfer | Supabase credit ledger | Inactive pending tax and lifecycle proof |
| Partner Circle seller commerce | Pay one creator or seller per marketplace order | Stripe Connect destination charge | Stripe plus immutable settlement ledger | Control path prepared; live order settlement gated |
| CrownThrive IO | Link, QR, analytics, and integration services | No implicit commerce authority | CrownThrive IO provider and audit projection | Read-verified |
| CHLOM / CIE | Rights, rules, roles, evidence, and remedies | No payment authority by itself | Governed evidence registry | Controlled test |

Crown Credits are first-party, closed-loop, nontransferable, noncash, and non-cash-out. They must not be used to fund third-party partner payouts without separate legal and payment-regulatory approval.

## Approved Connect model

The approved CrownThrive marketplace tuple is:

- Accounts v2 connected-account creation;
- Express dashboard access;
- platform-owned pricing and negative-balance liability;
- recipient configuration with transfers capability requested;
- destination charges for one seller per order;
- Stripe-hosted onboarding and KYC;
- no on-behalf-of parameter;
- a disclosed platform fee; and
- a hard checkout gate until recovery workflows pass.

A full-dashboard account with Stripe-owned pricing or loss liability belongs to a direct-charge model and must not be silently reused for this destination-charge rail.

For direct KJV memberships, Account/KYC lookup is not a checkout prerequisite. The governed direct-commerce controller must use the exact bound Product/Price mapping, first-party terms acceptance, idempotency, signed webhook reconciliation, and controlled public errors without exposing provider diagnostics. Account/KYC and capability reads belong to the Connect seller-control lane, not the direct membership lane.

## Safe founder test

The controlled test identity is the founder account already registered in the KJV/Sermon property. Testing must bind by immutable user ID, not by email search.

1. Reconcile the exact authoritative KJV Sites source and bind the plan action to the governed commerce controller.
2. Prove authenticated direct membership Checkout creation against the exact plan Price without an Account/KYC preflight.
3. Create or select an isolated Stripe sandbox fixture for seller-commerce lifecycle tests.
4. Create one sandbox customer and one Accounts v2 Express recipient account for the immutable founder user.
5. Complete hosted onboarding; verify transfers capability.
6. Issue a fixed, one-time promotional QA credit grant. Do not initiate a real charge.
7. Replay the grant request and confirm the balance does not change.
8. After tax approval, run the lowest sandbox credit top-up and verify the canonical total-credit field.
9. Run one destination charge and reconcile its fee and transfer.
10. Refund it with transfer reversal and application-fee refund.
11. Simulate a dispute and verify explicit recovery.
12. Record creation, webhook receipt, ledger post, entitlement, transfer, and recovery timestamps.

A promotional founder QA grant must be fixed, capped, idempotent, auditable, nontransferable, and separately revocable. It must not weaken the general prohibition on arbitrary self-grants.

## Super-admin control surface

The superior dashboard must show one readiness matrix instead of scattered flags. At minimum it must include:

- canonical identity bindings and collision warnings;
- Partner Circle application and review status;
- Crown Credit balances, grants, revocations, and replay keys;
- Stripe product, price, tax, webhook, and event-mode status;
- Connect onboarding, capability, fee, transfer, payout, refund, dispute, and recovery state;
- direct membership Checkout source binding and stale-handler detection;
- a recovery queue with owner, age, next action, and evidence;
- CrownThrive IO health, quota, last successful readback, and mutation gates;
- CHLOM/CIE certification state, quorum status, evidence hashes, and heartbeat freshness;
- GitHub branch, pull request, required-check, Sites deployment, and Mintlify deployment state;
- observed MFA state for privileged operators; and
- append-only audit events for every administrative mutation.

Checkout controls must fail closed. A green provider account does not make a commerce rail green. A provider-side Checkout construction pass does not certify the site button, webhook delivery, entitlement, or settlement lifecycle.

## Production release sequence

1. Reconcile the exact live KJV/Sermon source before application code deployment.
2. Route direct membership Checkout to the governed direct-commerce controller and remove the stale Account/KYC preflight.
3. Verify controlled customer-facing errors contain no restricted-key prefix, provider account identifier, permission diagnostic, or Dashboard remediation URL.
4. Prove authenticated Checkout creation and signed membership webhook reconciliation.
5. Implement provider-backed refund, application-fee refund, and transfer-reversal recovery.
6. Add route-level integration tests against isolated sandbox data.
7. Add the founder QA credit operation with fixed limits and audit evidence.
8. Complete the full sandbox matrix.
9. Keep webhook subscriptions limited to the required direct and Connect lifecycle events and read them back after change.
10. Activate only the lowest credit tier after specialist legal/tax approval and signed settlement/reversal proof.
11. Run a controlled live canary.
12. Enable seller checkout only after independent readback confirms every artifact.
13. Publish the evidence packet and keep rollback controls available.

<Note>
  Partner Circle referral rewards, Crown Credit top-ups, and Connect seller settlement are deliberately separate. Any future design that combines them requires a new architecture decision and legal review.
</Note>
