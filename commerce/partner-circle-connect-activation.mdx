# Partner Circle and Commerce Safety Gate

Partner Circle, Crown Credits, creator commerce, CrownThrive IO, CHLOM, and CIE are governed as separate capabilities. Documentation or candidate packaging does not activate payments, transfers, credits, entitlements, provider writes, or customer access.

<Warning>
  These services remain governed candidates unless the exact release has passed its required legal, tax, security, privacy, rights, accessibility, operational, rollback, and sovereign-approval gates.
</Warning>

## Public operating boundary

- Crown Credits remain a first-party, closed-loop capability and do not create cash, withdrawal, transfer, or third-party settlement rights.
- Referral rewards do not establish seller-payment authority.
- CHLOM and CIE provide governed rights, evidence, and decision-support contracts; they do not independently authorize payments or publication.
- Provider account availability, documentation, or green CI does not prove production readiness.
- D3 actions remain human-reserved.
- A/B/C/D/S remain the sovereign voter pool, with Agent D mandatory under the current constitution.

## Required activation evidence

Before any live commerce or entitlement release, the governed private evidence packet must prove:

1. immutable user and tenant authorization;
2. least-privilege provider and administrative access;
3. secure secret and signing-key custody;
4. isolated test and production environments;
5. bounded, authenticated, and idempotent requests;
6. verified event authenticity, replay protection, and out-of-order handling;
7. correct ledger, entitlement, refund, dispute, and recovery behavior;
8. legal, tax, privacy, accessibility, consumer-protection, and rights disposition;
9. independent read-after-write verification;
10. rollback, incident response, audit retention, and human override;
11. exact-artifact IP and disclosure clearance; and
12. fresh governed approval with mandatory Agent D and no blocking finding.

A missing or unavailable predicate remains HOLD or NOT-PASS. It must never be inferred from an adjacent provider status.

## Public and restricted evidence

Public documentation may expose approved service descriptions, safe interfaces, non-sensitive status summaries, and contract digests. It must not expose credentials, private identities, internal authority mappings, provider coordinates, tenant or customer data, administrative field inventories, live remediation details, proprietary tests, protected algorithms, or restricted security architecture.

Detailed implementation, testing, recovery, provider, and control evidence remains in governed private storage with sanitized stable references.

## Release discipline

Any implementation packet must use a review branch, pass the unchanged control families, receive applicable specialist review, preserve rollback evidence, and complete A/B/C/D/S review before promotion. A public page is documentation only; it is not checkout, entitlement, settlement, certification, or production authority.
