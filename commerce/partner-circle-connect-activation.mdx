## Partner Circle and Commerce Safety Gate

Partner Circle, Crown Credits, connected seller commerce, CrownThrive IO, CHLOM, and related economic systems are **separate governed capabilities**. A provider account, credential, API route, product record, test result, dashboard state, documentation page, or green CI run does not activate commerce and does not manufacture economic authority.

<Warning>
  Public documentation intentionally exposes the safety contract rather than detailed provider coordinates, administrative topology, private test identities, settlement configuration, recovery internals, or restricted economic evidence. Current activation truth is determined by governed private evidence and exact-scope readback.
</Warning>

## Value-class boundary

| Capability | Public-safe purpose | Authority boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Partner Circle | governed referral/partner participation | does not create payout or seller-settlement authority |
| Crown Credits | first-party closed-loop value/reward accounting | does not create cash, withdrawal, transfer, or third-party settlement rights |
| Connected seller commerce | separately governed seller transaction/settlement lane | requires exact provider, finance/tax, recovery, rights, and customer-protection authority |
| CrownThrive IO | integration/routing/portal services | technical connectivity does not create commerce authority |
| CHLOM | policy, rights, rules, evidence, and remedies | does not independently authorize payment or settlement |
| PentaPay™ | governed compensation/payment eligibility and receipt state | records do not move external money |
| PentaCost™ | budgets, commitments, limits, and cost evidence | budget records do not authorize unrelated spend |

Crown Credits and referral rewards must remain distinct from cash settlement unless a separately governed current policy expressly defines another relationship.

## Phase 3 economic control chain

Economic execution follows the applicable Penta authority and provider gates:

```text
current directive / policy / contract
          ↓
PentaManager bounded task authority
          ↓
PentaCost budget / hard-limit check where applicable
          ↓
PentaCredentials provider binding
          ↓
PentaBuild integration artifact
          ↓
PentaCertify exact operation certification
          ↓
independent legal / finance / rights / security checks when required
          ↓
authorized external operation
          ↓
provider + ledger + entitlement readback
          ↓
PentaNurture health / drift / recovery monitoring
```

Any missing gate yields `HOLD`, `readback_required`, or another explicit non-PASS state. An adjacent provider PASS cannot satisfy a missing economic, rights, customer, tax, recovery, or authority gate.

## Required activation evidence

Before any live payment, seller settlement, credit purchase, entitlement issuance, or related economic release, the governed evidence packet must prove the applicable subset of:

1. stable institutional subject and tenant identity;
2. current authority instrument and decision class;
3. least-privilege provider binding;
4. server-side secret/signing-key custody without public disclosure;
5. isolated test/production boundaries where required;
6. bounded, authenticated, idempotent operation contracts;
7. provider event authenticity, replay protection, and failure handling;
8. ledger and entitlement conservation/reconciliation;
9. refund, reversal, dispute, and unknown-outcome recovery behavior;
10. legal, tax, finance/treasury, rights, privacy, accessibility, and consumer-protection disposition where applicable;
11. independent read-after-write or equivalent effect verification;
12. rollback/incident evidence and human override for reserved decisions;
13. exact-artifact disclosure/IP classification; and
14. current release certification for the exact operation being activated.

A missing predicate stays missing. Penta systems may route or remediate the gap; they may not synthesize a PASS.

## Public versus restricted evidence

Public-safe documentation may expose:

- product/capability purpose;
- value-class separation;
- high-level control sequence;
- non-sensitive lifecycle state;
- public-safe contract/version identifiers;
- generalized reasons for HOLD;
- aggregate verification status where approved.

Public documentation must not expose:

- credentials, tokens, signing material, or secret references that reveal protected infrastructure;
- private customer, partner, seller, or test identities;
- provider account coordinates or privileged administrative identifiers;
- internal field inventories that materially aid privileged mutation;
- detailed settlement/recovery topology;
- restricted fraud, abuse, security, tax, or rights decision logic;
- private evidence bodies, DAIL coordinates, or protected economic schedules;
- remediation details that would weaken the control surface.

Detailed implementation and certification evidence remains in governed private systems with public-safe references where appropriate.

## Self-approval and money movement

No worker, agent, beneficiary, or Penta subsystem may self-create economic authority.

- PentaPay self-approval is prohibited where the approving subject is the beneficiary.
- PentaCost hard limits fail closed.
- A compensation or payment record is not proof that external money moved.
- External movement requires a separately authorized provider/financial rail and readback.
- D3/reserved-human decisions remain reserved even when every technical test passes.

## Provider and checkout rule

A green provider account does not make a commerce rail green. A successful API call does not prove customer-facing checkout, signed event delivery, entitlement issuance, settlement, reversal, dispute recovery, or lawful activation.

Each side-effecting provider operation is certified and authorized independently. Read certification does not grant write authority, and one write operation does not authorize another.

## Current-state projection

Public current state should use only bounded labels such as:

- `candidate`;
- `controlled_test`;
- `provider_read_verified`;
- `operation_certified`;
- `hold`;
- `readback_required`;
- `production_monitored`;
- `superseded`.

Exact private provider/economic details are not required to explain why a public state remains held.

## Release discipline

Any activation packet must use current-main source, exact-head checks, applicable Penta governance, specialist evidence, provider certification, independent readback, documentation reconciliation, and rollback/recovery evidence.

A public page is documentation only. It is not checkout, settlement, a rights grant, customer entitlement, tax approval, legal approval, provider permission, or production authority.
