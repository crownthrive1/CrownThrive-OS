## Fail-closed release path

A candidate can move toward sale only after all required evidence is independently reviewed and the controlling release gates are passed:

1. Complete editorial and substantive quality review.
2. Establish rights provenance, third-party notices, and an approved operative license contract.
3. Complete legal, refund, tax, pricing, and public-exposure review.
4. Independently certify the exact package intended for fulfillment.
5. Bind the offer to that exact immutable edition and asset version.
6. Verify customer identity, least-privilege access, secrets, signed webhooks, and fulfillment behavior.
7. Pass payment, refund, dispute, replay, concurrency, delivery, and revocation canaries.
8. Receive explicit owner approval for activation.

## Exact-release control

The database purchase function, entitlement/library projection, and protected delivery path now require one approved immutable offer-to-release binding. The exact binding, asset version, asset hash, license template, and terms version are snapshotted into the purchase records; asset version identity and stored bytes cannot be silently overwritten or deleted.

This closes the mutable-`latest` design defect. It does **not** activate any candidate: there are still zero approved factory bindings, zero operative factory licenses, zero complete independent gate-evidence sets, and zero live offers.

Publication requires eight evidence gates and at least two independent reviewers. The originating agent cannot certify its own work. Minimum internal thresholds are 95/100 overall, editorial, design, usability, and accessibility-evidence dimensions; executable assets require 100/100 validation. Stable targets are EPUB 3.3, EPUB Accessibility 1.1, and applicable WCAG 2.2 AA without an asserted conformance claim. OEM/white-label remains disabled until Phase 20.

A priced offer also requires at least three relevant comparables from two independent sources. No licensed product or production entitlement may be free; the minimum transaction price is 400 credits.

## Identity and licensing boundary

CHLOM fingerprints may identify an exact generated version. A fingerprint is not proof of ownership, rights clearance, truth, authorship, or a decentralized identity credential. Commercial rights arise only from an approved operative license and a valid entitlement tied to an exact certified release.
