# PentaCredits™ Economic Value System

**Canonical system:** `PentaCredits™`<br />**Stable contract:** `ct.pentagreen.credits.v1`<br />**Parent authority:** [PentaGreen™](/commerce/pentagreen)<br />**Legacy aliases:** Crown Credits; legacy `credit_*` runtime identifiers where required for compatibility

PentaCredits is CrownThrive's canonical governed credits and economic-value subsystem. It does not create independent monetary authority; it operates inside PentaGreen's exact authority envelope and the applicable CHLOM rights, policy, finance, legal, evidence, and reconciliation controls.

## Scope

PentaCredits governs:

- credit programs and program rules;
- account balances and ledger entries;
- purchases and qualifying top-ups;
- promotional or restricted credits;
- reversals, corrections, and refund-to-credit flows;
- usage restrictions, expiration, caps, and eligibility where authorized;
- fraud and anomaly controls;
- cross-platform use only where an exact program contract permits it;
- reconciliation between provider/payment evidence and institutional economic state.

## Economic boundary

```text
qualifying authority/event
→ PentaGreen ECAC/HOLD/DENY
→ PentaCredits program policy
→ ledger mutation through a certified path
→ receipt/readback
→ reconciliation
→ corrected institutional balance
```

A provider response is not sufficient by itself to create or erase credit value. Failed or ambiguous reconciliation remains fail-closed.

## Runtime compatibility

The current production data plane retains the existing governed `developer_commerce.credit_*` primitives for continuity. PentaCredits exposes canonical compatibility views and wrappers over those proven primitives rather than destructively renaming historical tables and ledger evidence.

Canonical wrappers include balance, purchase, and reconciliation surfaces under the `pentacredits_*` namespace. Existing exact legacy identifiers remain valid when referenced by immutable evidence, prior releases, migrations, or provider integrations.

## Safety rules

PentaCredits may not silently convert a credit program into cash, deposits, securities, unrestricted transferable value, or a new regulated financial instrument merely because a technical provider can move value. Any expansion of economic characteristics requires the applicable institutional and professional review before activation.

## Relationship to other PentaGreen components

- **PentaSKU** identifies the exact commercial object.
- **PentaPrice** determines authorized terms.
- **PentaCheckout** creates bounded purchase intent.
- **PentaReceipt** records evidence.
- **PentaReconcile** resolves evidence into institutional truth.
- **PentaLedger** stores the reconciled economic projection.
- **PentaHold** contains uncertain or unauthorized state.
- **PentaCompensate** handles reversals and compensating actions.

> **PentaCredits can execute only inside PentaGreen authority. It cannot manufacture authority.**
