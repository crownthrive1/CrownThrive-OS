## Members

### PentaSurgeon

`penta.surgeon` owns bounded hard-repair execution through the existing PentaSELF repair contract. It remains fail-closed where required and does not gain direct-main, provider, credential, financial, D3, certification, or dispatch authority from subfamily membership.

### PentaChart

`penta.chart` records structured repair facts and evidence through the PentaScribe repair-chart path. Its charting terminology is operational; it does not represent medical or clinical authority.

### PentaRounds

`penta.rounds` performs independent post-repair verification through the existing PentaDnD hard-repair contract test path. It does not self-certify the originating repair.

## Production certification

The pre-existing Surgical Care production certification is preserved. The topology overlay does not replace or downgrade its runtime evidence. It only distinguishes **runtime organization** from **constitutional representation**.

Current bounded controls include:

- one immediate retry ceiling;
- rollback only for a repair-caused regression;
- independent post-repair verification;
- append-only repair/chart evidence;
- D3 conditions remain human-reserved;
- no new scheduler clock created by the topology overlay;
- no authority expansion.

## Constitutional boundary

The topology contract explicitly prevents the operational subfamily from becoming a constitutional Family by implication. `SURGICAL_CARE` has no row in the 15-Family constitutional crosswalk and no constitutional PentaMocracy Family row.

Accordingly:

- its production software remains live;
- its certification history remains immutable;
- its three member identities remain system-known;
- its canonical parent remains unresolved;
- it receives no electoral or representational weight;
- it cannot inherit authority from a future parent merely by routing relationship.
