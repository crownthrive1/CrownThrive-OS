# Penta Surgical Care Family

The **Penta Surgical Care Family** is CrownThrive’s production family for high-importance repairs that must be executed, verified, charted, certified, and closed without granting PentaSELF unlimited repair authority.

## Family members

### PentaSurgeon

Executes exact registered D0–D2 repair handlers inside bounded transactional surgery. PentaSurgeon cannot run arbitrary SQL, move money, manufacture authority, mutate secrets, or write directly to `main`.

### PentaChart

Creates structured operational charts for the repair lifecycle. Charts are redacted, hash-addressed, linked to the repair case, and preserved through PentaScribe and PentaDocs.

### PentaRounds

Performs post-operation readback, determines whether a regression exists, tests whether the repair caused it, verifies compensation, verifies the one bounded retry, and determines discharge readiness.

## Collaborating Pentas

| Penta | Responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| PentaSELF | Owns the bounded repair case and coordinates the lifecycle |
| PentaScribe | Preserves structured institutional narrative and lineage |
| PentaDocs | Maintains internal records and redacted public reports |
| PentaDnD | Isolates and independently tests the exact repair generation |
| PentaCertify | Provides the second independent exact-generation certification |
| PentaPR | Classifies the exact pull-request head and terminal action |
| PentaMerge | Executes the governed provider merge after all gates pass |
| PentaCloser | Confirms provider readback, documentation, and final closure |

## Disposition states

A repair may be held, compensated, retried, certified, merged, or discharged. A rollback is never used merely because a post-check is unhealthy. The system must prove both a regression and causal attribution to the surgery before compensation is permitted.

## Production certification

The family entered production after a full canary proved:

- bounded mutation;
- detected surgery-caused regression;
- verified compensation;
- one immediate post-compensation retry;
- healthy post-operation readback;
- independent PentaDnD and PentaCertify passes;
- structured PentaChart records;
- PentaDocs internal/public projection; and
- no authority expansion or direct-main write.

See [PentaSELF Hard Repairs](/pentaself/hard-repairs) for the operating contract.
