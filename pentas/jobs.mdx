## Penta Jobs & Functions

| Job | Pentas | Purpose |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| [Architect](/pentas/jobs/architect) | **25** | Define topology, boundaries, contracts and system structure. |
| [Route](/pentas/jobs/route) | **101** | Route work, data, events or authority to the correct destination. |
| [Retrieve](/pentas/jobs/retrieve) | **126** | Fetch, read, list, query, search or resolve governed information. |
| [Transform](/pentas/jobs/transform) | **104** | Parse, normalize, transform, validate or reconcile data and artifacts. |
| [Orchestrate](/pentas/jobs/orchestrate) | **39** | Coordinate workflows, agents, queues, schedules and multi-system execution. |
| [Build](/pentas/jobs/build) | **112** | Create software, frameworks, artifacts, assets or infrastructure. |
| [Test & Certify](/pentas/jobs/test-certify) | **69** | Test, assure, certify, gate and produce readiness evidence. |
| [Secure](/pentas/jobs/secure) | **72** | Enforce identity, credentials, trust, privacy, compliance and security controls. |
| [Observe](/pentas/jobs/observe) | **134** | Measure health, telemetry, logs, metrics, status and evidence freshness. |
| [Recover](/pentas/jobs/recover) | **56** | Retry, heal, restore, rollback, reconcile and maintain continuity. |
| [Govern](/pentas/jobs/govern) | **123** | Apply authority, policy, legal, licensing and institutional governance constraints. |
| [Document](/pentas/jobs/document) | **46** | Record, explain, index, preserve and project institutional knowledge. |
| [Publish](/pentas/jobs/publish) | **85** | Package, release, distribute or syndicate approved content and artifacts. |
| [Communicate & Grow](/pentas/jobs/communicate-grow) | **85** | Market, message, nurture, support, sell and grow relationships or audiences. |
| [Analyze & Research](/pentas/jobs/analyze-research) | **54** | Research, model, score, forecast, measure and derive intelligence. |
| [Manage People & Community](/pentas/jobs/manage-people-community) | **23** | Coordinate people, cohorts, training, talent, community and service ownership. |
| [Transact & Economic](/pentas/jobs/transact-economic) | **37** | Price, meter, bill, entitle, pay, settle, reconcile and route economic activity. |
| [Support & Operate](/pentas/jobs/support-operate) | **166** | Run day-to-day service operations, support, incident response and accountable handoffs. |
