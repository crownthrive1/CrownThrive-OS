# PentaMocracy — CrownThrive Internal Government

PentaMocracy is CrownThrive's internal constitutional and institutional operating model. It uses a U.S.-federal-government analogy to organize all registered Pentas and related systems without manufacturing sovereign, legal, provider, financial, rights, or D3 authority.

## Constitutional branches

| Branch | Institutional function |
| --- | --- |
| **Penta Executive** | Executes bounded D0–D2 policy through registered departments, agencies, offices, systems, and certified runtimes. |
| **Penta Legislative** | Maintains internal rules, standards, charters, appropriations proposals, family representation, system representation, and ratification records. |
| **Penta Judicial** | Performs due-process review, recusal-aware adjudication, appeals, exact-evidence review, and separation-of-duties enforcement. |

The three branches remain independently checked through CHLOM, DAIL, PentaAssure, PentaAudit, PentaDnD, PentaCertify, and human-reserved authority.

## Bicameral Congress

### Penta House of Families

The House represents the fifteen canonical constitutional Penta families. Operational subfamilies, including the Penta Surgical Care Family, remain nonvoting until separately constitutionalized.

### Penta Senate of Systems

The Senate represents registered CrownThrive systems by exact system identity. A production-capable system may receive an internal D0–D2 voting membership, but machine votes can never satisfy a required human ratification floor.

## Executive departments

Each constitutional Penta family has a department or independent establishment. Departments contain axis bureaus for truth, authority, execution, interoperation, continuity, coordination, orchestration, translation, and unresolved/unclassified work.

Every non-family Penta identity has a production office or agency under its accountable family department and axis bureau.

## Judicial organization

The judicial branch includes:

- Penta Supreme Court;
- Penta Court of Appeals;
- Penta District Courts; and
- independent assurance and recusal-aware review lanes.

No Penta may judge, certify, approve, merge, or close its own high-consequence action without the independent checks required by the governing contract.

## Independent establishments

Independent establishments include census, archives and records, inspector-general, comptroller, reserve, sanctions observation, research, intelligence, security, trust, observability, resilience, and continuity functions.

These bodies create no independent sovereign or legal authority. They observe, preserve, investigate, reconcile, certify, hold, or recommend within their registered ceiling.

## Membership and authority

PentaMocracy currently uses workforce and institutional-representation memberships. One membership maps to one internal vote only where voting is explicitly eligible.

<Warning>
  Membership does not create legal authority, provider credentials, money-movement permission, publication rights, licensing rights, destructive authority, or D3 authority. Required human ratification remains binding.
</Warning>

## Production semantics

All current registered Penta identities and systems have a production-governed citizen runtime for status, description, government assignment, readiness, help, documentation, and governed routing.

That does **not** mean every specialist function has been promoted to autonomous execution. Source maturity is preserved:

- **specialist execution** — exact runtime and certification evidence exists;
- **governed coordination** — production routing and institutional presence are active while specialist execution remains gated;
- **candidate fail-closed** — production presence, records, government assignment, and build/certification routing are active, but independent specialist execution is forbidden.
