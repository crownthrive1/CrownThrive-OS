## Mission

PentaDiscovery is the systemwide discovery coordinator and discovery metaprotocol. Its recorded operating loop is:

`Observe → Fingerprint → Resolve → Classify → Register → Package → Price Route → Dispatch → Receipt`

It receives Penta discovery hand-raises and observed signals, produces fingerprinted evidence, classifies/registers discoverable objects, attaches governed route/economic context and hands the resulting work to PentaCensus, PentaCrawler, PentaHelp or the appropriate governance/execution lane.

It does **not** manufacture canonical state, D3 authority, credential authority, destructive provider-write authority or inherited provider money-movement authority.
