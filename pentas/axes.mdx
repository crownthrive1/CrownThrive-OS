## Pentagonal Axes

| Axis | Core components | Responsibility |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| [Truth](/pentas/axes/truth) | **8** | Identity, architecture, contracts, schemas, semantics, provenance and verified state representations. |
| [Authority](/pentas/axes/authority) | **5** | Decision rights, policy, consent, rights, security, legal/economic/risk bounds and capability constraints. |
| [Execution](/pentas/axes/execution) | **21** | Bounded work performed by software, agents, workflows, factories, tools, models and operational services. |
| [Interoperation](/pentas/axes/interoperation) | **10** | Routes, protocols, bindings, transports, events, adapters, dependencies and governed handoffs. |
| [Continuity](/pentas/axes/continuity) | **4** | Evidence, lineage, reconciliation, recovery, supersession, succession, history and exact-baseline inheritance. |

Axis membership is primary architectural responsibility, not permission/readiness/certification/production. Use [families](/pentas/families), [layers](/pentas/layers), [jobs](/pentas/jobs), [lifecycle](/pentas/lifecycle) and [audiences](/pentas/audiences) for complementary operating coordinates.
