# PentaRounds

PentaRounds is the post-operation verification member of the Penta Surgical Care Family.

## Responsibilities

PentaRounds reads current production truth after a repair and determines:

1. whether the repaired target is healthy;
2. whether a regression exists;
3. whether the repair caused that regression;
4. whether compensation restored the prior safe state;
5. whether the single bounded retry produced a healthy non-regressed state; and
6. whether the repair is ready for independent certification and discharge.

## Independence

PentaRounds is separate from PentaSurgeon. It does not certify on behalf of the originator. The exact repair generation must also pass PentaDnD isolation and PentaCertify assurance.

## Rollback rule

An unhealthy post-check alone does not authorize rollback. Compensation requires affirmative evidence of both regression and repair causation. Without that proof, the case is held for newer evidence or a newer repair generation.

## Follow-up

Discharged repairs remain represented in PentaSELF’s permanent-repair state and PentaDocs record. New evidence may open a newer generation, but an older snapshot cannot silently roll the system backward.
