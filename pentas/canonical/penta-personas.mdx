# PentaPersonas

PentaPersonas is the institutional persona layer spanning two live canonical runtimes:

- [PentaPersona Execution](/pentas/canonical/penta-persona-execution) — capability- and playbook-bound work execution;
- [PentaPersona Factory](/pentas/canonical/penta-persona-factory) — reuse-first persona discovery, proposal, testing, and certification workflow.

It is not the same thing as the candidate-only `penta.candidate.persona.engine` specification.

## Persona contract invariant

Every production persona must bind:

- immutable persona identity and version;
- display identity, role, organization, brand, corridor/lane, and purpose;
- explicit AI-assistance disclosure;
- voice/tone rules and culturally aligned conduct;
- exact enabled capabilities and playbooks;
- authority ceiling and prohibited authorities;
- approved sender/transport binding;
- input/output/data-minimization contract;
- escalation and human-reserved decisions;
- provider, money, rights, credential, and legal boundaries;
- tests, certification, evidence, custody, lifecycle, supersession, and invalidation conditions.

The governing schema is [Penta Persona Contract Standard](/standards/penta-persona-contract-standard). The safe production roster is stored at `data/penta/personas/production-persona-registry.v1.json`.

## System boundary

A persona may interpret and act only within its exact capability/playbook and authority contract. PentaMail transports authorized messages but does not grant persona authority. Provider success is evidence, not permission. D3, binding legal/rights commitments, material money movement, credential creation/rotation, and founder-only decisions remain reserved.

## Factory doctrine

Reuse an existing qualified persona first. A new persona is a last resort and requires necessity evidence, collision checks, contract completion, negative tests, independent certification, registration, and versioned custody. Factory output is a candidate until every required gate passes.
