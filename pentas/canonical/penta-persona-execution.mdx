## Runtime contract

Persona execution resolves an approved persona, exact capability, exact playbook, authority ceiling, prerequisites, current provider/transport state, idempotency, and evidence target before work is claimed.

### Required state machine

`proposed -> eligible -> claimed -> executing -> provider_pending/readback_pending -> completed`

or a typed `HOLD`, `DENIED`, `FAILED`, `CANCELLED`, or `SUPERSEDED` state.

## Mandatory checks

1. Persona identity is approved and current.
2. Capability and playbook are enabled and production-certified.
3. Requested work matches role/corridor and input schema.
4. Authority remains at or below both persona and capability ceilings.
5. D3/human-reserved conditions are routed, never executed.
6. PentaMail/PentaMailer is used for business/persona email transport.
7. Provider write requires a current verified binding and read-after-write.
8. Money, rights, legal, credential, privacy, and safety gates remain independent.
9. Material outcomes append/read back DAIL.

## Recovery

Execution state must survive worker/model restart. Stale claims are reconciled using exact work identity, lease/claim state, provider observations, and idempotency before another action. Ambiguous external outcomes remain HOLD until readback resolves them.
