# PentaChart

PentaChart is the structured recordkeeping member of the Penta Surgical Care Family.

It converts important repair activity into a durable operational chart with:

- observations;
- assessment;
- procedure;
- system response;
- next plan;
- evidence;
- exact case generation;
- author identity; and
- a SHA-256 chart digest.

## Chart types

PentaChart supports triage, pre-operation, procedure, post-operation, rollback, retry, certification, discharge, and follow-up records.

## Publication rule

The full chart remains in the internal PentaScribe/PentaDocs record plane. Public PentaDocs receives only a redacted report for significant, critical, held, or failed cases. Secrets, credentials, contact data, internal stack details, and sensitive provider responses are not published.

## Boundary

This is disciplined operational charting. It borrows the clarity and continuity principles of high-quality clinical recordkeeping, but it is not medical care, a medical record, or a substitute for human professional judgment.
