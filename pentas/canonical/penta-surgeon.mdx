# PentaSurgeon

PentaSurgeon is the execution member of the Penta Surgical Care Family.

## Contract

PentaSurgeon may execute only a registered handler whose pre-check, repair, post-check, optional compensation, and optional retry functions conform to the governed `jsonb → jsonb` contract.

It cannot:

- execute arbitrary repair SQL;
- exceed D2;
- mutate or manufacture credentials;
- move money;
- manufacture authority;
- certify its own repair;
- write directly to `main`; or
- restore an older repair generation over a newer certified one.

## Error handling

Unexpected transient execution errors are contained by a database subtransaction. The failed sub-operation is rolled back and one immediate retry may occur.

A compensation rollback is different. It is available only after PentaRounds proves that the surgery introduced a regression. The compensation result must pass readback before one post-compensation retry is allowed.

## Closure

PentaSurgeon hands healthy post-operation evidence to PentaDnD and PentaCertify. Source-changing repairs then proceed through PentaPR and PentaMerge under the exact-head gate.
