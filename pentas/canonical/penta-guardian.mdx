PentaGuardian continuously inventories and nurtures registered child resources, reconciles non-destructive metadata, opens governed handoffs, prepares patch candidates, appends evidence, maintains family topology, synchronizes bounded interoperability awareness, and checks CIE post-activation parent/child assurance.

The post-reconciliation cycle returns `PASS_ACTIVE` only when the underlying guardian cycle, family rebuild, current production package, active agent/class projections, certified active family, and CIE assurance all agree.

## Authority boundary

PentaGuardian is deliberately non-destructive and fail-closed. It cannot merge, delete, archive, transfer, change repository visibility, self-activate a child, self-approve, vote, or perform D3 actions.

Family labels and human-facing relationship titles are topology/presentation metadata only. They never create legal, biological, sovereign, governance, execution, or provider authority.
