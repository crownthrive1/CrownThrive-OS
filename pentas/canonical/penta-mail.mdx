# PentaMail

PentaMail is the canonical institutional communications transport for CrownThrive. It accepts already-authorized communication work, applies transport policy, queues and dispatches through governed providers, records provider outcomes, and closes material delivery state through CHLOM/DAIL.

> **Boundary:** PentaMail transports approved intent. It does not invent business intent, choose a legal position, grant rights, move money, create credentials, approve a contract, or perform D3/human-reserved action.

## PentaMailer relationship

**PentaMailer is the transport/pooling/provider-execution subcomponent of PentaMail, not a competing peer authority.** The aliases `penta.mailer`, `pentamailer`, and `ct.penta.mailer.v1` resolve to `penta.mail` with a non-peer subcomponent classification. See [PentaMailer](/pentas/canonical/penta-mailer).

## Canonical topology

`Gmail/Outlook acquisition -> Communications Watch -> normalized work/evidence -> PentaMarketer/persona -> PentaMail/PentaMailer -> governed provider -> provider readback -> CHLOM/DAIL`

Gmail and Outlook are acquisition and thread-context surfaces. They are not a direct-send fallback for CrownThrive business or persona communications.

## Traffic classes

1. `system_internal`
2. `support_transactional`
3. `marketing_locticians`
4. `marketing_other`

Support and transactional traffic must not be stranded behind marketing quotas. Every class remains subject to recipient eligibility, suppression, opt-out, complaint, bounce, risk, working-hours, campaign, and provider constraints.

## Required send contract

A material outbound result is complete only when all applicable predicates pass:

- canonical work/message identity;
- authorized business owner and persona;
- recipient-policy and suppression checks;
- stable idempotency key;
- traffic class and applicable campaign window/caps;
- PentaMail/PentaMailer ownership;
- provider acceptance and exact provider message identity;
- persisted outbox/work terminal state;
- universal-copy policy where required;
- applicable CHLOM/DAIL append and readback.

## Survival footprint

PentaMail persists identities, queues, attempts, outcomes, rate reservations, suppression state, receipt chains, recovery state, and authority/readback evidence. Model-assisted composition may degrade without a model; deterministic queueing, policy enforcement, provider dispatch, idempotency, terminal-state reconciliation, evidence, and restart behavior must remain.

## Certification posture

Production runtime is evidenced. This documentation and its source commit do not independently certify future provider routes, changed policy, new sender identities, changed credentials, or changed delivery logic. Exact-head and provider-current certification remains mandatory.
