## Lifecycle

`discover need -> search/reuse -> collision check -> contract -> capability/playbook binding -> negative tests -> independent certification -> registration -> activation -> monitoring -> supersede/retire`

The factory may propose and prepare candidates. It cannot self-certify, manufacture necessity, bypass identity collision, grant D3, create credentials, expand provider authority, or convert a candidate into production without exact evidence.

## Candidate acceptance

A persona candidate must include a complete [Penta Persona Contract Standard](/standards/penta-persona-contract-standard) packet, conflict analysis, data and disclosure policy, authority/non-authority matrix, transport binding, test results, recovery behavior, source/custody refs, and independent certificate for the exact candidate digest.
