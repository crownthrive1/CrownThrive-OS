## Penta Glossary

For the complete generated corpus, use the [Penta Dictionary](/pentas/dictionary).

## Agent ingestion

Deterministic loading of Pentagonal reference metadata, Penta routing records, constraints and target contracts before routing/execution.

**Machine rule:** Load machine manifests first, then resolve live authority/readiness/bindings.

## Architectural layer

Cross-family technical/operational stack placement.

**Machine rule:** Use for architecture-first discovery; layer does not promote state.

## Audience

The intended machine/human consumer of a Penta contract or guide.

**Machine rule:** Audience changes presentation, not authority.

## Authority

Scope-specific permission and decision-right context governing whether an actor/component may perform an action.

**Machine rule:** Ability is not permission; resolve current authority immediately before consequential execution.

## Authority axis

Decision rights, policy, consent, rights, security, legal/economic/risk bounds and capability constraints.

**Machine rule:** Axis membership is architectural context, not authority/readiness/production.

## Candidate Penta

A preserved noncanonical reference awaiting canonicalization, aliasing, supersession, primitive/subcomponent classification or retirement.

**Machine rule:** Remain fail-closed for independent runtime/provider writes.

## Canonical Penta

A Penta identity accepted into the governed canonical namespace.

**Machine rule:** Canonical identity is required before execution can be considered, but is not sufficient authorization.

## Canonicalization

Governed resolution of a discovered/candidate identity into the institutional namespace without manufacturing role, maturity or authority.

**Machine rule:** Preserve the candidate until a governed disposition exists.

## CERTIFIED

Status used only with an applicable authorized certification record for the exact asserted scope.

**Machine rule:** Do not infer certification from test success or docs completeness.

## CHLOM

CrownThrive authority, rights, consent and bounded-execution control boundary in the institutional source hierarchy.

**Machine rule:** Documentation does not override applicable CHLOM/current authority.

## Continuity axis

Evidence, lineage, reconciliation, recovery, supersession, succession, history and exact-baseline inheritance.

**Machine rule:** Axis membership is architectural context, not authority/readiness/production.

## D3

Human-reserved/sovereign consequence class unless governance explicitly changes the boundary.

**Machine rule:** Agents must not self-create or self-approve D3 authority.

## DAIL

Append-only evidence/receipt/lineage boundary for material actions, corrections and traceability.

**Machine rule:** Preserve material readback/receipts as DAIL-compatible evidence.

## Evidence

A source, receipt, readback or verified record supporting a bounded claim.

**Machine rule:** Carry subject, scope, time, source and assertion; never generalize one event into system-wide proof.

## Exact-head

Discipline binding approval/tests/release to one exact commit or artifact, not a moving branch assumption.

**Machine rule:** If the head moves, re-evaluate the new bytes.

## Execution axis

Bounded work performed by software, agents, workflows, factories, tools, models and operational services.

**Machine rule:** Axis membership is architectural context, not authority/readiness/production.

## Execution eligibility

A predicate that allows a canonical identity to enter an execution decision path; necessary but never sufficient for material action.

**Machine rule:** Require eligibility plus current readiness, authority, dependencies/bindings and evidence target.

## Fail closed

Withhold/stop action when required authority, evidence, security, dependency, rights, recovery or rollback predicates are unresolved.

**Machine rule:** Unknown high-consequence predicates block execution.

## Handoff envelope

Task/correlation, origin/target, job, authority, dependency, idempotency, expected readback and evidence context carried between Pentas.

**Machine rule:** Never strip authority/evidence/correlation context during routing.

## HOLD

Named unresolved predicate prohibiting a specific action until required conditions are satisfied.

**Machine rule:** Never turn HOLD into PASS merely because time passed or docs changed.

## Idempotency

Contract allowing repeated delivery/retry without unintended duplicate material effects.

**Machine rule:** Retry writes only when replay safety is known; otherwise read back/reconcile first.

## Interoperation axis

Routes, protocols, bindings, transports, events, adapters, dependencies and governed handoffs.

**Machine rule:** Axis membership is architectural context, not authority/readiness/production.

## Job/function

Normalized work class used for task-first routing.

**Machine rule:** Match intent to job IDs, then apply identity/readiness/authority/binding gates.

## Lifecycle stage

When a Penta participates across discover, design, build, verify, release, operate, observe, recover, govern and evolve.

**Machine rule:** Participation is not maturity or production.

## PASS

Successful bounded assertion tied to exact tested scope, environment, timestamp and evidence.

**Machine rule:** PASS never silently generalizes to other state dimensions.

## Penta

A Penta is a stable, named, contract-bounded institutional capability in PentaOS. It may be a service, control, registry, agent layer, workflow, tool, adapter, factory, evidence/knowledge component or governed composite. The name identifies an institutional role; it does not by itself grant execution, provider, legal, rights, financial, release or D3 authority. A Penta is not synonymous with an LLM or autonomous agent.

**Machine rule:** Resolve stable identity and canonical registry state before treating a Penta name as a target.

## Penta family

The institutional home of a Penta, orthogonal to layer, job, lifecycle and audience.

**Machine rule:** Use family for institutional grouping, not task authority.

## Pentagonal Architecture

The five-axis Penta architecture: truth, authority, execution, interoperation and continuity.

**Machine rule:** Use axes as architecture responsibilities, never as permission or production claims.

## PRODUCTION

Runtime/deployment state supported by applicable release/deployment/provider readback evidence.

**Machine rule:** A rendered page or merged PR does not independently prove production.

## Readback

Post-action observation from the target runtime/provider verifying what actually occurred.

**Machine rule:** Read back material writes before claiming convergence or retrying ambiguity.

## Readiness

Evidence-backed current ability of a capability and dependencies to perform its bounded role.

**Machine rule:** Re-read readiness; do not treat it as permanent.

## Source of truth

Controlling institutional record for a defined state dimension under CrownThrive source precedence.

**Machine rule:** Resolve governing records before accepting projections as authority.

## Stable identity

Durable institutional identity preserved across renames, providers, routes, versions, migrations and archive transitions.

**Machine rule:** Bind software/evidence to stable IDs or machine keys, not display names.

## Truth axis

Identity, architecture, contracts, schemas, semantics, provenance and verified state representations.

**Machine rule:** Axis membership is architectural context, not authority/readiness/production.
